## Goal
Answer, on the same tickets, whether the owner's real fills cost more than the paper account's did, by how much, where (entry, exit, charges, missed tickets) and with what certainty; show the distribution of live slippage and the execution-gap waterfall by strategy and month. Every number has its n; below the minimum it says so.

## Scope
**In:** Domain `Domain/LiveTrading/Pairing`: `TicketPair`, `PairedShortfall` (pure), `PairingPolicy`, `ShortfallStats` (mean, sd, two-sided and one-sided intervals), `NonInferiority` (verdict), `SlippageHistogram`, `ExecutionGapBuilder` (group sums and the identity), `ExcludedReason`; Application: `GetLiveVsPaperQuery`, `GetExecutionGapQuery` and handlers over ports `ILivePairSource` (live side: `fill_grades`, `live_executions`, `live_ticket_outcomes`, `journal_entries`) and `IPaperTradeSource` (paper side: `paper.entries` + `paper.exits`; a port so P10's final shape is isolated); Api endpoints; the **Live vs paper** tab of `/live`; tests; ADR.
**Out:** any table or migration; the ramp and the phase verdict (P11-04, P11-06 read this report's numbers through the same handler, never a second computation); backtest-versus-paper (P10-04); the P8 catalogue entries R14 and R32 (a one-line follow-up in P8's catalogue to link here); account comparison across several live accounts; per-instrument tables.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a live round trip and a paper trade for the same `plan_item_id`, when paired, then the shortfall in R on the common risk per share rho (the plan's |entry - stop|) is s = paper R - live R (positive = live worse), and it splits exactly into entry difference d x (live entry - paper entry)/rho plus exit difference d x (paper exit - live exit)/rho: with live entry 1,250.475 / exit 1,262.00, paper entry 1,250.30 / exit 1,263.00, rho 10, d = +1: entry 0.0175, exit 0.1000, s 0.1175 (paper 1.2700, live 1.1525).
2. Given ten pairs with s = [0.02, 0.05, -0.01, 0.08, 0.03, 0.00, 0.06, 0.04, 0.01, 0.02], then mean 0.0300, sd 0.0279, se 0.00882, two-sided 95 % interval 0.0101 to 0.0499 (t(9) = 2.262), one-sided 95 % upper bound 0.0462 (t = 1.833); and the verdict is `insufficient` because the non-inferiority verdict needs >= 30 pairs.
3. Given 30 or more pairs, then the verdict is `within` when the one-sided 95 % upper bound of the mean shortfall is <= 0.10 R, `not_within` when the one-sided 95 % **lower** bound is > 0.10 R, otherwise `inconclusive` (the interval is too wide to say); thresholds come from `Live:Report:NonInferiorityR` (0.10) and `Live:Report:MinPairs` (30).
4. Given tickets that cannot be paired, then each is listed with exactly one reason and counted: `live_only` (paper has no entry: a paper skip code is shown), `paper_only` (the live ticket was missed, rejected or not taken: its outcome class is shown), `live_open`, `paper_open`, `live_uncertain` (P7-14 `uncertain` trip: no R), `no_plan_levels`, `different_quantity_basis` is NOT a reason (R is per share); the exclusion counts are in the Evidence block.
5. Given charges, then the net comparison is shown only for pairs where the live charges are known and the paper charges come from a stored sheet (`paper.exits.charges` not null); otherwise the gross comparison is the report and says so; gross and net are never mixed in one mean.
6. Given the live account has several paper accounts to pair with and no `Live:Report:PaperAccountId` is set, then 422 `live.paper_account_required`; with exactly one paper account it is used and named in the Evidence block; paper epochs are never pooled (the current epoch of P10-01 only, named).
7. Given the slippage histogram, then the bins are fixed in basis points (< -10, -10 to -5, -5 to -2, -2 to 0, 0 to 2, 2 to 5, 5 to 10, 10 to 20, >= 20), edges inclusive on the lower side, one bar per bin with its count, and the observations are the live total entry slippages of P11-02 of one reference kind; a bin with 0 is drawn as 0 count, never hidden; fewer than 10 observations is `insufficient` with n and no chart.
8. Given the execution gap by `strategy` or `month`, then each group's rows are: plan R (sum over approved live tickets whose replay graded), missed triggers (minus the plan R of `missed_no_order`, `missed_unfilled`, `rejected`), entry slippage, exit difference, charges difference (minus), result = actual net R of the group's graded round trips, and the identity plan - missed - entry - exit - charges = actual holds exactly on the fixture; a row whose input is unavailable is `unavailable`, not 0; the "overrides" row is `unavailable` ("measured by the decisions report of P8-04").
9. Given a group with fewer than 5 tickets, then its sums are shown with n and its per-ticket mean is `insufficient`; groups are listed in a fixed order (key ascending); the total row sums only groups that are values.
10. Given the equity overlay, then three cumulative-R lines (plan prices, paper, live) are drawn over the **paired set only**, in exit-date order, with n beside each point's final value; a day without a closed pair adds no point (no zero fill); a non-value is never a flat line.
11. Every response has the Evidence block (basis gross or net, n, date range, reference kinds, paper account and epoch, pair rule, exclusion counts, limits) and the six states; no secret or account number in a response or log; the same stored data gives byte-identical responses.
12. The tab refuses to render a response without its Evidence block, imports no mutation and issues no non-GET call (a test).

## Test plan
- **Unit (Domain):** the pair split of criterion 1; `ShortfallStats` against the ten-pair table (mean, sd, se, both intervals, one-sided bound) and against brute force; the verdict boundaries (upper exactly 0.10 is `within`; lower exactly 0.10 is `inconclusive`); histogram edges at each boundary; the identity of the execution gap as a property over random tickets with a **negative control** (doctor one exit price in the live side: the identity against the stored actual must fail on at least 10 seeds); order independence of pairs; adding a pair never changes another group.
- **Unit (Application, fakes):** every exclusion reason once; paper account selection (none, one, several); gross/net rule; empty stores; a paper source that is `unavailable` gives `unavailable`, not an empty report.
- **Integration (Testcontainers):** both endpoints on a seeded week (three tickets: filled both, live missed with paper filled, paper skipped with live filled), empty database, 422 cases, determinism (two calls byte-identical).
- **Frontend (MSW):** every state; Evidence block required; interval text; no digit for a non-value; chart table fallback; the tab imports no mutation.
- **Fixture (hand-worked):** pair A = criterion 1 (s 0.1175). Execution-gap group "ORB / 2026-11": tickets T1 (filled, plan R 1.4000, entry slip 0.0475, exit difference 0.2000, charges 0.0960, actual net 1.0565), T3 (INFY, `missed_no_order`, plan R 1.2000), T2 (rejected, plan R 0.8000): plan sum 3.4000, missed -(1.2000 + 0.8000) = -2.0000, entry -0.0475, exit -0.2000, charges -0.0960, result 1.0565 = 3.4000 - 2.0000 - 0.0475 - 0.2000 - 0.0960 (rounded to 4 places); n = 3 so the per-ticket mean is `insufficient`.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **Q: What is the comparison unit?** A: The same ticket (paired). Why: pairing cancels the market's noise, so tens of pairs can answer the execution question while independent samples cannot (the sd of R is about 1, the sd of the paired shortfall about 0.1 to 0.2); doc 09 §12 already uses paper shadow for the same reason. Rejected: live mean R versus backtest mean R (variance of both runs adds; no power at n < 200).
2. **Q: Which R basis?** A: The plan's rho for both sides, gross first. Why: R per share on a common denominator makes the difference pure price; live and paper then differ only in fills (and charges, shown apart). Rejected: each side's own risk per share (mixes fill-dependent denominators).
3. **Q: Non-inferiority margin 0.10 R, one-sided 95 %?** A: Yes. Why: it is two thirds of the +0.15 R expectancy gate (doc 09 §7), above the modelled slippage (1 tick + 5 % of range, ADR 0038), and a one-sided bound is the standard form of "not worse than" (a two-sided interval wastes half its alpha). Rejected: 0.05 R (needs a negative mean at n = 30 with sd 0.15: upper 0.05 requires mean about -0.01), no margin (tests equality, never passes).
4. **Q: Minimum pairs?** A: 30 for a verdict, 10 for any interval. Why: below 30 the t interval is unstable for skewed shortfalls; 10 is the floor used in P11-02 for any interval. Rejected: a verdict at n = 10.
5. **Q: How are tickets missing on one side treated?** A: Listed and costed, never dropped. Why: the missed tickets are the main way live differs from paper (doc 09 §9 "missed / late triggers"); excluding them would flatter live. Their plan R appears in the execution-gap waterfall. Rejected: pairing only tickets filled on both sides without disclosure.
6. **Q: Chart for the distribution?** A: A fixed-bin histogram, not a smoothed density. Why: smoothing invents mass at n = 20; fixed bins are checkable by hand. Rejected: kernel density.
7. **Q: Where do R14 and R32 live?** A: In the `/live` tab for now. Why: the P8 catalogue shells for real-fills reports are `not_evaluable` placeholders (P8-00 scope: "Out"); linking them here avoids a hard dependency on P8-08. Rejected: building in `/reports` (couples two slices).

## Risks and honest limits
Paired tickets are a selected sample (taken by the owner and filled on paper); the report prints how many were left out and why. The paper fill is itself modelled (official 1-minute bars with the standard slippage), so "live minus paper" is live minus a model, not live minus a market. Paper and live can differ in quantity and in the minute of entry; R per share removes the first, not the second. About 40 to 80 pairs in eight weeks give an interval of roughly +-0.05 R on a sd of 0.15; narrower claims are not available. The overlay is cumulative R over the paired set, not an account curve (no sizing, no compounding). Charges: live are Dhan's recorded lines, paper are the stored sheet's model.
