## Goal
Every simulated trade can say what it cost. A trade gets a quantity from a simulated capital and a risk profile, its two orders are charged by a charge sheet, line by line, on the rates in force on the date of each order, and the trade carries gross R (`RExact`), filled R (`RFilled`) and net R (`RNet = RFilled − CostR`) side by side, with the lines of the charge visible so that it can be recomputed by hand to the paisa. A trade that cannot be sized (under one share) or whose cost is too large for its risk (`cost_R` above `max_cost_R`) is skipped and counted, never hidden. A rate that is missing makes a sheet impossible to build (fail closed), and the run record, its key, its labels and its limits say what was charged. This slice builds all of that as pure library code and proves it on a TEST sheet; **no request can ask for it yet** (P3-03f), so every run through the handler is gross and says `costs: none`.

## Definitions (decisions the tests pin)
Money is rupees; a *paisa* is 0.01. A *leg* is one order: the entry or the exit of a trade; its *side* is the side of the order (a BUY trade enters with a buy order and leaves with a sell order; a SELL trade the reverse). Dates are India dates. Percent fields are in percent (0.03 means 0.03 %), as in doc 16.

**D1 — Where costs are applied.** In the replayer, by a pure Domain function, after the walk. `InstrumentReplayer.Scan` takes the trades of a segment after the flagged-day drop, the window cut and (P3-03d) the discontinuity exclusion, and before the fates are counted, and calls `TradeCostApplier.Apply` for every trade (`Trades` and `OpenAtEnd` alike); a skipped trade leaves its list and is counted as a fate. Against the alternatives: (a) **in the walker**: it would take a sheet and a profile, its golden file and hand-calculated cases would change, and the two-stage seam (P4 replays the same entries under other exits) would carry costs it must not know; (b) **a post-pass in `Simulator.Run`**: the `Simulator` has no window, so it would cost trades before `From` (which a sheet effective from `From` cannot price) and would hand the replayer a result whose counters no longer add up. In the replayer the window is known, the costing is a function of one `SimTrade` and a `CostContext`, `TradeWalker.cs`, `Simulator.cs` and the Scanning namespace are not in the diff, `XDrishti.Simulation` stays pure, and the gross result of `Simulator.Run` stays what it says (`SimulationHeader` says `costs: none` for the result it describes; a run's labels come from the run record, D15). A gross run (no cost context) calls nothing and the trades are those of the walker. A trade skipped by costing is not a walker no-trade: `WalkCounters` is the walker's and does not change (its `ExitsStop`, `ExitsTarget` and the other exit counts still include the cost-skipped trades, as they include the outcomes of flagged days, so **the totals of `Walk` are not the kept trades; `Fates` are**); the identity is in D14.

**D2 — The seam to the request.** This slice adds `CostContext? Costs = null` as the last member of `InstrumentReplayInput` and `CostRecord? Costs = null` as the last member of `RunHeader` (and of `SimulationRun`). `RunSimulationHandler`, `SimulationRequest`, `ISimulationEngine` and the adapter are **not changed**: the handler builds the header without `Costs` and the input without a context, so a run through the handler is gross, `Costs` is null, the key line is `costs=none` and the labels say `costs: none`. P3-03f adds the request member and the sheet read. The costed path is exercised by tests that call `InstrumentReplayer` with a context and `RunAssembler` with a header that carries a `CostRecord`. Reason for not giving the request a member here: a member that selects a sheet needs a source of sheets, and a source needs storage (P3-03f); a request member with no source would be a way to ask for something that cannot exist.

**D3 — Types and units.** All money is `decimal` in rupees; ratios (`CostR`, `RNet`) are `double`, like `RExact`. Reasons: the exit check is "equal to the paisa", and a sum of eight rounded lines misrounds on exact halves in binary doubles. **Measured in C# on .NET 10 with a throwaway program (outside the repository), computing each line in `double` and rounding with `Math.Round(x, 2, MidpointRounding.AwayFromZero)`:** the exit-leg SEBI of W1 (255,000 × 50 / 10,000,000 = 1.275) gives **1.27** where the decimal gives 1.28; so does the exit-leg exchange charge of W6 (25,500 × 0.005 / 100 = 1.275); the entry-leg SEBI of W7 (201,000 × 50 / 10,000,000 = 1.005) gives **1.00** where the decimal gives 1.01. Every other line of W1 to W8 agrees between `double` and `decimal` in that measurement; `Math.Round(5.005, 2, AwayFromZero)` is 5.01 (W6's stamp duty is a *half-away versus half-even* case, not a `double` trap: the decimal under `half_even` gives 5.00). (Python's `round()` is not what .NET does and is not the evidence.) The walker already keeps prices and R in decimal and converts at the end (`D(...)`). The inputs of the costing are the `SimTrade`'s own fields: prices converted from the double fields with the conversion of ADR 0038 T3 (`Math.Round((decimal)x, 8)`), so the costing is a function of the stored record and can be recomputed from it. `Quantity` is `long`. Percent and per-crore rates are decimals with at most 6 decimal places (more is refused as probably a unit slip). **How costs enter the record:** the money values are `decimal` fields of `TradeCosting`; `CostR` is the decimal quotient converted once with the walker's `D` (zero is always +0); `RNet = RFilled − CostR` is one subtraction of two doubles, exact under IEEE and deterministic.

**D4 — The shape of the sheet.** `CostSheet(Id, Label, Source, Checked, Schedules)`:
- `Id`: lower-case `[a-z0-9][a-z0-9._-]{0,39}`. **An id that starts with `test-` is a TEST sheet** (`IsTest` is derived from the id, there is no `test` field): the ids with that prefix are reserved for synthetic sheets, `CostSheetJson.Parse` and `Create` accept them (a TEST sheet round-trips like any other), and P3-03f's store and loader refuse to keep one. `Label` ≤ 80 characters, `Source` ≤ 200. `Checked`: optional, ≤ 200, what the sheet was checked against; **absent means unchecked and every label says so** (D15).
- `Schedules`: 1 to 12 `ChargeSchedule`, strictly ascending by `EffectiveFrom` (years 2000 to 2099). A `ChargeSchedule(EffectiveFrom, GstPct, Rounding, IntradayEquity, Delivery)`; the rates in force on a date are those of the last schedule whose `EffectiveFrom` is on or before it. A date before the first schedule has no rates: `costs.no_rates_for_date`.
- `ChargeRates` (one per segment, **every field present, a zero written as 0, never null**): `BrokeragePct`, `BrokerageCap` (rupees per order), `BrokerageMin` (rupees per order; 0 means none), `SttBuyPct`, `SttSellPct`, `ExchangeTxnPct`, `SebiPerCrore` (rupees per 1,00,00,000 of turnover), `StampBuyPct`, `OtherPct` (the "Oth. Charges" column; 0 if none) and `DpChargePerSell` (rupees per delivery sell order; 0 for the intraday segment).
- **The brokerage rule is closed** (a cap of 0 must never mean "no cap"): if `BrokerageCap` is 0 then `BrokeragePct` and `BrokerageMin` must both be 0 (brokerage is 0); if `BrokerageCap` is above 0 then `BrokerageMin` ≤ `BrokerageCap`. A flat fee per order is written `pct 0, min = cap = fee`. A broker with a percent and no cap is not expressible in this sheet (it would be a new field, not a zero); the owner is asked in P3-03f.
- Validation, each a field error with a path (`schedules[1].delivery_equity.stt_sell_pct`) and a code (`required`, `not_a_number`, `negative`, `too_large`, `too_precise`, `not_ascending`, `duplicate`, `duplicate_property`, `brokerage_cap_required`, `min_above_cap`, `unknown_field`): percent lines at most 5, `GstPct` at most 100, `BrokerageCap` and `BrokerageMin` at most 10,000, `SebiPerCrore` at most 1,000, `DpChargePerSell` at most 1,000. The bounds are nets against a slipped unit, not guards (0.0003 for 0.03 passes), which is why a sheet says `unchecked` until it is checked against bills (P3-03f). **A property name that appears twice in one object is refused** (`System.Text.Json` keeps the last value silently; the parser rejects duplicates, through the reader's own option if .NET 10 offers it, otherwise by a token walk: UNVERIFIED which). An unknown field is refused. **The input text is at most 16,000 characters before parsing** and the canonical text at most 8,000 (the limit that P3-03f's migration gives the settings value; `costs.sheet_too_long`).
- **Per segment, not per instrument:** every instrument is charged as an NSE cash-market equity (limit `cash_equity_only`).

**D5 — The canonical forms and what is hashed.** `CostSheet.ToCanonicalJson()` writes `id`, `label`, `source`, `checked` (only when present) and `schedules` in the field order of D4 and the layout of P3-03f, snake_case keys, no white space, numbers by `CanonicalText.Number(decimal)` (no trailing zeros, invariant culture, `0` for zero), dates as `yyyy-MM-dd`. **`RatesJson()` is the same text restricted to `id` and `schedules`, and `RatesSha256` is `CanonicalText.Sha256Hex` of it:** the label, the source and `checked` describe the sheet and change no trade, so writing `checked` after a successful check changes neither the hash nor the run key. `Parse(ToCanonicalJson(s))` equals `s` for every valid sheet, a TEST sheet included, and the hash does not depend on the spelling of the input (`0.030` and `0.03`; key order; white space).

**D6 — Rounding.** Each charge line of a leg is computed in decimal from the exact turnover and rounded **to the paisa, once, with the schedule's rule**: `half_away_from_zero` (the TEST sheet's), `half_even` or `down`. GST is computed from the **already rounded** brokerage, exchange and SEBI lines of the same leg and rounded once. A leg's total is the sum of its eight rounded lines; a trade's total is the sum of its two legs. Whether Dhan rounds per order, per trade or per bill, and half up or half even, is UNVERIFIED. **Only the rounding mode is a field of the sheet, so only it can be settled by the owner's bills without a code change;** the GST base (brokerage, exchange, SEBI), the brokerage per order and the level at which rounding happens (per leg) are fixed in D8 and D6 and would change in code and ADR if the check (P3-03f) shows otherwise.

**D7 — Effective dates, per leg.** Each leg uses the schedule in force on its own India date: the entry leg on the trade date, the exit leg on the date of the exit minute. For an intraday trade both are the same date; for a swing trade (P3-03d) they may fall on either side of a date at which a rate changed, and each leg pays its own rates (L1).

**D8 — The charge of one leg.** For a leg of turnover `T` (quantity × the **filled** price: `EntryFilledPrice` or `ExitFilledPrice`, equal to the exact prices when the fills are exact), side `s` and segment `g`, with the rates `r` of `g` on the leg's date, each line rounded as in D6:

| Line | Formula | Applies to | Source of the form |
|------|---------|-----------|---------------------|
| Brokerage | `0` if `r.BrokerageCap` is 0, else `min(r.BrokerageCap, max(r.BrokerageMin, T × r.BrokeragePct / 100))` | every order, both sides; one order per leg | doc 16 `brokerage`: "pct, max" (a minimum and "per executed order" are UNVERIFIED) |
| STT | `T × r.SttBuyPct / 100` on a buy, `T × r.SttSellPct / 100` on a sell | the sheet says which side pays: intraday sell only in doc 16 (`eq_intraday_sell_pct`); delivery sides UNVERIFIED (doc 16 has one number) | doc 16 `stt_ctt` |
| Exchange transaction | `T × r.ExchangeTxnPct / 100` | both sides | doc 16 `exchange_txn_pct` |
| SEBI fee | `T × r.SebiPerCrore / 10,000,000` | both sides | doc 16 `sebi_fee_per_cr` |
| Stamp duty | `T × r.StampBuyPct / 100` | **buy orders only** | doc 16 `stamp_duty_buy_pct` |
| Other | `T × r.OtherPct / 100` | both sides | the "Oth. Charges" column of the bill (a bill may carry a negative rounding line there; the model's line is a percent of turnover and is never negative) |
| GST | `GstPct / 100 × (brokerage + exchange + SEBI)` of the same leg, **not** on STT, stamp duty, other or DP | both sides | doc 16 `gst_pct`; the base is UNVERIFIED and is checked against bills |
| DP charge | `r.DpChargePerSell`, once | **delivery sell orders only**; no GST added (whether the billed figure includes GST is UNVERIFIED) | the depository report (local note): the real lines are per ISIN per day and not uniform, so this is one flat figure per delivery sale (limit `dp_charge_is_flat`) |

`CostModel.Leg` returns `LegCharges(Brokerage, Stt, ExchangeTxn, SebiFee, StampDuty, OtherCharges, DpCharge, Gst)` and `Total`. **Every line is ≥ 0, the total is monotone non-decreasing in `T`, and a line that the sheet sets to 0 is 0.**

**D9 — What a charge is not.** It is not a tax computation on trading income, not an account-level fee (ADR 0026), not a margin or funding cost. Its percentages are applied to the turnover of simulated orders at the filled price; it says nothing about edge.

**D10 — The sizing profile.** `SizingProfile(Name, RiskPerTradePct, MaxCapitalPerTradePct)`, built by `Create` (risk in (0, 2], the bound of `RiskLimits.MaxRiskPerTradePct`; max capital in (0, 100]). `Standard` = 0.5 and 25 (doc 16; the seeded `Standard` row); `Conservative` = 0.25 and 25. A test in the database project reads the two seeded rows and requires equality with the code defaults, so that a change of the seed or of the constants is noticed (the owner's edits to the stored rows do not reach a run in P3; the run stores the numbers it used). The profile has no other field: the entries per day, open positions, heat and loss limits are portfolio limits (doc 08 §7), not simulated.

**D11 — The quantity.** With `C` the simulated capital, `p` the profile, `entry` = `SimTrade.EntryPrice` (the **exact** entry), `stop` = `SimTrade.StopPrice` and `risk = abs(entry − stop)` per share (the unit of R, ADR 0038), all in decimal:
```
riskAmount  = C × p.RiskPerTradePct / 100
maxCapital  = C × p.MaxCapitalPerTradePct / 100
quantity    = floor( min( riskAmount / risk , maxCapital / entry ) )          # long; lot size 1 (equity)
skip if quantity < 1                                                          # TradeSkip.QuantityBelowOne
```
`Sizing.Quantity` returns `Result<long>`: **a `capital`, `entry` or `risk` that is not above 0 is `costs.sizing_input_invalid`** (never a divide by zero); the walker guarantees `risk > 0` (`EntryThroughStop` is no trade), so in the replay the error is a caller error (`InvalidOperationException`). Decisions with reasons: **the row's `fill` is read as the exact entry**, not the filled price: the quantity must not depend on slippage or on the grid (ADR 0038 S4), the unit of R is the exact ticket, and a limit order is sized before its fill price is known; the filled entry is a few ticks above at most, so the notional can exceed the cap by well under one percent (limit `no_margin_or_leverage`). **`floor`, not round**, so that the risk taken never exceeds the budget. **The minimum is 1 share**; a zero is a counted skip, not a rounding up. **Capital is fixed**: every trade is sized from the same `C` (no compounding), and trades of different instruments overlap without a cap on exposure (limit `fixed_capital`; label `portfolio limits: not simulated`). **Leverage and margin are out**; doc 08's liquidity cap is not applied (limit `no_liquidity_cap`); the lot rounding for futures does not apply. `RiskTaken = quantity × risk`; **1R of the trade is `RiskTaken`** (doc 08 §1), below `riskAmount` by the part the floor cut.

**D12 — The segment.** `IntradayEquity` for a strategy that does not hold overnight, `Delivery` for one that does, decided in `XDrishti.Simulation.Replay` (a Domain function cannot name `Style`, a `Strategies` type) from the prepared strategy: P3-03d's `Simulator.HoldsOvernight` (UNVERIFIED until it is merged) or the style, and handed to `Apply` as a `CostSegment`. A swing trade that exits on its entry day (N = 1) is still `Delivery`; how a broker bills it is UNVERIFIED. Today `Simulator.Create` refuses every style but intraday, so only `IntradayEquity` is reached until P3-03d; the delivery segment is tested at the level of one leg (L1) and by a hand-made swing-shaped `SimTrade` (exit date after entry date).

**D13 — Costing a trade, and net R.** `TradeCostApplier.Apply(trade, context, segment)` returns a **`CostApplication`**, a sealed record built only by `Kept(trade)` and `Skipped(skip)` (exactly one of them is set; a private constructor):
1. `Sizing.Quantity` (D11). Below 1: `Skipped(QuantityBelowOne)`.
2. `EntryTurnover = quantity × EntryFilledPrice`, `ExitTurnover = quantity × ExitFilledPrice`; the entry leg has the side of the entry order, the exit leg the other, each on its own date (D7); `CostModel.Leg` for each.
3. `TotalCost` = entry leg total + exit leg total (a multiple of 0.01). `CostR = TotalCost / RiskTaken` (decimal).
4. **`CostR > MaxCostR` is compared in decimal**, before any conversion: `Skipped(CostAboveMax)` (doc 08 §6; quantity is checked first, then cost). A trade whose `CostR` equals `MaxCostR` is kept (W1b).
5. Otherwise `Kept(trade with { Costs = CostBasis.Charged, Costing = new TradeCosting(...) })`; **every other field is the walker's, byte for byte.**
**Net R.** `RNet = RFilled − CostR`: net R starts from `RFilled`, the result at the prices an order would have got (slippage is already in it, so it is never charged twice), not from `RExact`. `RExact`, `RFilled` and `RNet` are stored together: `RNet ≤ RFilled ≤ RExact`, with `RNet = RFilled` only when `TotalCost = 0`. With `SlippageConfig.None`, `RFilled = RExact`. An `EndOfData` trade is costed like any other and stays in `OpenAtEnd`.

**D14 — The record.**
- **`SimTrade` schema 4.** `CurrentSchemaVersion` is 4 (P3-03d: 3). One new **last** positional parameter `TradeCosting? Costing = null` (the 33rd; a default value, so that the walker's construction call needs no change); `CostBasis` gains `Charged`; **`Costs == Charged` if and only if `Costing` is not null**. One value and not a dozen columns, so that "all or nothing" is one invariant and one pin; the exporter flattens it. **Nothing of schema 2 or 3 is byte-compatible with schema 4** (the four golden files, the run key and `sim-4` all change; no reader of an older record exists; runs recorded before cannot be compared with new ones by key).
- **`TradeCosting`**: `Segment`, `SheetId`, `Quantity` (`long`), `RiskTaken` (`decimal`), `EntryTurnover`, `ExitTurnover`, `EntryLeg`, `ExitLeg` (`LegCharges`, eight lines each, so the 16 lines of a trade are visible), `TotalCost` (`decimal`), `CostR` (`double`), `RNet` (`double`). **D15 — Labels, limits, wording.**

- `CostLimits.NotModelled` (the `FillLimit(Code, Text)` pair, in this order): `fixed_capital` (every trade is sized from the same starting capital: no compounding and no equity curve; trades of different instruments overlap and their combined exposure is not capped) · `no_margin_or_leverage` (intraday margin, leverage, funding and interest are not modelled; the cap per trade is a cap on notional, which the filled entry may exceed by a few ticks) · `no_liquidity_cap` (doc 08's liquidity cap is not applied) · `one_order_per_leg` (each leg is one order for the brokerage cap; partial fills and order splitting are not modelled) · `cash_equity_only` (every instrument is charged at the sheet's NSE cash-equity figures; ETFs, other series, BSE, futures and options are not told apart) · `no_trading_tax` (tax on trading income and account-level fees such as annual maintenance are outside the charge) · `rates_as_sheet` (the figures are the sheet's and exactly as right as the sheet; an unchecked sheet is labelled so) · `dp_charge_is_flat` (the depository charge is one flat figure per delivery sale; the depository's own lines vary and some are 0) · `no_flat_order_fees` (a flat fee per order other than the brokerage, such as an auto square-off fee, cannot be expressed; "other" is a share of turnover) · `swing_gate_before_costing` (a swing position that costing skipped still blocks the later candidates that the position gate of P3-03d refused; D17). The scan is **word-bounded** (`gain` and `edge` occur inside other words; `rates` is not `rate`). The field is `RNet`, "R after the sheet's charges"; the cost is "a charge on a simulated order". **D17 — A named limit: the position gate of P3-03d counts a trade that costing skips (decided by the coordinator after the P3-03d review; the owner may reverse, question 2).** P3-03d's gate (its D11) runs in `Simulator.Run` before the walk: a swing candidate is blocked as `PositionOpen` while the last accepted trade of the instrument is open (an `EndOfData` trade blocks every later candidate). Costing runs afterwards, in the replayer. So **a swing trade that costing skips (quantity under 1 or `cost_R` above `max_cost_R`) still counts as the strategy's position for the gate, and the later candidates that it blocks stay `PositionOpen`**, although a sized account would have taken no position and could have taken them.
- **Why it is kept.** The gate models the position that the *signal* opens; sizing and cost skips are accounting applied afterwards to the trades that exist. The replay must also stay window-independent (P3-04): trades before `From` cannot be costed, because the sheet has no rates before its first schedule, so a gate that waited for costing would give a different state at `From` than a longer run does. The alternative (decide the gate after costing) is a change of P3-03d's `Simulator.Run` and of the replay's window rule and is left to a later slice if the owner wants it.
- **What is pinned.** The limit is `swing_gate_before_costing` in `CostLimits` (D15), printed by the banner. A test (criterion 19) builds a swing-shaped candidate list in which the first trade is skipped by cost and the second candidate would have been taken, and asserts the resulting fates: the first is `SkippedCost` 1, the second is `PositionOpen` 1 (not a trade), `Fates.Total` equals the candidates, and the same list with the first trade kept has the same `PositionOpen`. Intraday is unaffected (not gated; P3-03d pins `PositionOpen` 0 on every intraday run, and so does a test here).

## Acceptance criteria
Numbered, each testable. Domain and replay parts in `XDrishti.Domain.Tests` and `XDrishti.Simulation.Tests`; the seeded-profile comparison in `XDrishti.Api.IntegrationTests` (Testcontainers); the rules in `XDrishti.Architecture.Tests`.

**The sheet**
1. **Validation fails closed.** Given each of: a missing line (every field of D4 omitted in turn), a JSON `null`, a number given as a string, a negative number, NaN or an infinity, a line above its bound, a rate with more than 6 decimals, a duplicate or descending `effective_from`, a date outside 2000 to 2099, zero or 13 schedules, an unknown field, **a property name repeated in one object (the second value would otherwise win)**, an id outside the pattern, **`brokerage_cap` 0 with `brokerage_pct` above 0 or `brokerage_min` above 0 (`brokerage_cap_required`)**, `brokerage_min` above a non-zero cap (`min_above_cap`), an input text over 16,000 characters, a canonical text over 8,000, when `CostSheetJson.Parse` or `CostSheet.Create` is called then it returns `costs.sheet_invalid` (or `costs.sheet_too_long`) with a `FieldErrors` entry whose path and code are those of D4, at most 20; and the same sheet with that one problem repaired parses. A flat fee (`pct 0, min = cap = 20`) parses and costs 20 on any turnover. **A null rate never reaches the model.**
2. **Canonical forms and hashes.** Given `test-synthetic-1` when `ToCanonicalJson` is parsed back then the sheet is equal (a TEST sheet round-trips; the id prefix makes `IsTest` true); `RatesSha256` is equal for the same sheet spelt with trailing zeros, another key order and white space, differs for one changed digit or a changed id, and **does not change when `label`, `source` or `checked` change** while `ToCanonicalJson` does; the text is identical under the cloned-invariant culture with German separators (P3-03b AC11); `0` is never written `-0`.
3. **Effective dates.** `RatesOn` for 2025-12-31 is `costs.no_rates_for_date`; 2026-01-01 and 2026-03-02 give schedule A; 2026-03-03 and 2099-12-31 give B.

**The model**
4. **The lines, one by one.** Given the legs of W1, W2, W3, W5, W6, W7, W8 and L1 when `CostModel.Leg` is called then every line, `Total` and the group sums equal the printed figures to the paisa, each line asserted separately, including the buy-only stamp duty, the sell-only DP charge, the STT side per segment and the GST base (brokerage, exchange, SEBI; not STT, stamp, other or DP).
5. **Rounding.** Given W1's exit SEBI 1.275, W6's stamp duty 5.005 and W7's entry SEBI 1.005 then they round to 1.28 / 5.01 / 1.01 under `half_away_from_zero`, 1.28 / 5.00 / 1.00 under `half_even` and 1.27 / 5.00 / 1.00 under `down`; a test computes the same lines in `double` with `Math.Round(x, 2, MidpointRounding.AwayFromZero)` and shows that it returns **1.27 for W1's SEBI and 1.00 for W7's** (the trap named) and 5.01 for W6's stamp duty (no trap); GST is computed from the rounded lines (a case where it differs by a paisa from GST on the unrounded lines is pinned).
6. **Brokerage branches.** Given turnovers below, at and above `cap / (pct / 100)`, a `brokerage_min` above the percent amount, a flat fee, and a sheet whose cap and percent are both 0 then the brokerage is the percent amount, the cap, the cap, the minimum, the fee and 0; the boundary turnover itself gives the cap; **a cap of 0 with a percent above 0 cannot be built (criterion 1), so the model never sees an "uncapped" case.**
7. **Properties of the model** (a grid and 2,000 seeded legs, both segments, both sides, both schedules): every line and the total are ≥ 0; the total is monotone non-decreasing in turnover (so in quantity at a fixed price and in price at a fixed quantity); `total(2T) ≤ 2 × total(T) + 0.10` (the cap and the DP charge do not double; each rounded line adds at most a paisa and GST at most two); a sheet with every rate 0 gives 0; a line set to 0 in the sheet is 0 in the leg.

**Sizing**
8. **The quantity.** Given W1, W2, W4 and W7 then 2,500, 1,251, 0 and 2,000; a `byCap` of exactly 1.0 gives 1 and 0.99999999 gives 0; `floor`, never rounding (`byRisk` 2.9999 gives 2). The quantity uses `EntryPrice`: the same trade with `SlippageConfig.None` and with the defaults has the same quantity. **A capital, entry or risk that is 0, negative or not a number is `costs.sizing_input_invalid`, never an exception from a division.**
9. **Properties of the quantity** (500 seeded days, random capitals, prices 20 to 5,000, stops 0.2 % to 3 %): `quantity × risk ≤ riskAmount` and `quantity × entry ≤ maxCapital`; non-decreasing in capital and in `RiskPerTradePct`; **doubling the capital gives `2q` or `2q + 1`**; the quantity is 0 exactly when `min(byRisk, byCap) < 1`; a tighter stop never gives a smaller `byRisk`.
10. **The profile.** `SizingProfile.Create` refuses 0, negative, NaN, an infinity, risk above 2 and max capital above 100; `Standard` is 0.5 and 25, `Conservative` 0.25 and 25; the integration test reads `accounts.risk_profiles` and requires the seeded `Standard` and `Conservative` rows to equal them. `CostContext.Create` refuses a null sheet or profile (`ArgumentNullException`), a capital outside (0, 10,000,000,000] and a `MaxCostR` outside (0, 100].

**Costing a trade**
11. **The worked trades.** Given W1 to W8 through `TradeCostApplier.Apply` then quantity, `RiskTaken`, both turnovers, both legs' eight lines, `TotalCost`, `CostR` and `RNet` equal the figures (money to the paisa, ratios to 1e-9 against the decimal quotient); W3, W6-at-default are `Skipped(CostAboveMax)`, W4 `Skipped(QuantityBelowOne)`; W5 differs from W1 by exactly 12.75; W1b is kept at 0.09282 and skipped at 0.09281; W7 and W8 are also produced by **a replay of the F1 and F2 invented days through `InstrumentReplayer`** (not only by the applier), and the costed golden file agrees with the hand figures line by line.
12. **Net R properties** (500 seeded days from P3-03b's generator, both sides, ticks 0.01 / 0.05 / 0.10, `None` and default slippage, capitals 10,000 to 1,00,00,000, both schedules): `RNet ≤ RFilled ≤ RExact` on every kept trade, with `RNet < RFilled` whenever `TotalCost > 0`; `RNet = RFilled − CostR` exactly; `CostR = TotalCost / RiskTaken` to 1e-12; `CostR ≤ MaxCostR` on every kept trade; kept plus `SkippedQuantity` plus `SkippedCost` is exactly the walker's trades (none lost or invented); and **the walker's fields are untouched**: `costed with { Costing = null, Costs = None }` equals the gross trade of the same run field for field.
13. **A SELL is charged on its own sides.** Given W2 the entry leg has STT and no stamp duty and the exit leg stamp duty and no STT; a BUY the reverse. Given a hand-made swing-shaped trade (segment `Delivery`, entry date before a boundary, exit date after) the entry leg uses the earlier schedule and the exit leg the later (L1), and the DP charge is on the sell leg only.
14. **Skips are counted and ordered.** Given a seeded run in which some trades fail quantity and some cost then each skipped trade is counted exactly once, quantity before cost (a trade that fails both is `SkippedQuantity`), the identity of D14 holds on every fixture, and the skip fates are 0 in a gross run; at a fixed capital raising `MaxCostR` never increases `SkippedCost`, and raising the capital never increases `SkippedQuantity`.
15. **The same risk as the walker's.** SimTrade has no risk field, and the walker and the costing both derive it from 8-decimal conversions; for every trade of the 500 seeded days `d × (ExitPrice − EntryPrice) / abs(EntryPrice − StopPrice)` computed in decimal from the double fields equals `RExact` to 1e-9, so `RiskTaken / Quantity` is the walker's `risk`.

**The record**
16. **`SimTrade` schema 4.** `CurrentSchemaVersion` is 4 and the positional properties are exactly the 32 of schema 3 followed by `Costing` as the 33rd (the pin test is revised on purpose); `Costs == Charged` iff `Costing` is not null on every trade of every fixture; `TotalCost` equals the sum of the 16 lines, `CostR` ≥ 0, `Quantity` ≥ 1. The four golden files are regenerated with the ADR and a costed one is added (paired with W7 and W8, criterion 11).
17. **The run record and the key.** Through `RunAssembler` with a header that carries a `CostRecord` and replays made with a context: the trades differ from the gross run's only in `Costs` and `Costing` (minus the skipped ones); `CostRecord` carries the id, the rates hash, the canonical text without `checked`, `Checked`, the capital, the profile and `max_cost_R`; the key differs when each of sheet id, rates, capital, risk percent, max-capital percent, `max_cost_R` and gross-versus-sheet changes, and is equal across two runs of the same input, across two spellings of the sheet text and when only `label`, `source` or `checked` change; `SimulationRun.CurrentSchemaVersion` is 4 and `SimulationEngine.Version` is `sim-4`, the pin tests say so; a gross run's key text has the line `costs=none`.
18. **Fail closed in the replay.** Given a trade dated before the first schedule (a caller error that P3-03f's request check prevents) `Apply` returns `costs.no_rates_for_date` and the replay throws `InvalidOperationException`, never a zero-cost trade.
19. **The position gate counts a skipped swing trade (D17; the pinned limit).** Given a hand-made swing-shaped candidate list in which the first trade is skipped by cost and the second candidate follows inside its hold, then the first is `SkippedCost` 1 and the second is `PositionOpen` (not walked, not a trade); given the same list with the first trade kept the `PositionOpen` is the same; `Fates.Total` equals the candidates in both; `PositionOpen` is 0 on every intraday run of the 500 seeded days with the costing on; `CostLimits.NotModelled` holds `swing_gate_before_costing`.
20. **The handler is unchanged.** Through `RunSimulationHandler` (P3-04a's tests, unchanged except the pins and goldens) a run has `Costs` null, trades with `Costs = None` and `Costing = null`, the key line `costs=none`, the third label `costs: none`; the engine port, the adapter and `SimulationRequest` are not in the change list.

**Labels, limits, wording**
21. **Labels.** `CostDescription.Label` returns `costs: none`, `costs: sheet dhan-x (not checked against bills)`, `costs: sheet dhan-x` (with `Checked` set), and `costs: sheet test-synthetic-1 (TEST sheet: synthetic rates, not the broker's) (not checked against bills)`; **`SimulationHeader.LabelsOf(rules, costs)` returns the five labels in the order fills, entry limit, costs, portfolio limits, sample for a gross run, a sheet run and a TEST run**, and `LabelsOf(rules)` equals the overload with null; `SizingLabel` returns the line of D15 with `1000000`, `standard`, `0.5`, `25` and `0.1`; `CostLimits.NotModelled` holds exactly the ten codes of D15 in that order, each with a non-empty English text; the word-bounded scan of D15 finds nothing in any string the slice produces, a sentinel string with `profit` and one with `total` must be caught, and `rates` and `window` must not be.

## Non-functional
- **Performance:** costing is O(1) per trade, a few dozen decimal operations; negligible next to the walk and the read. An explicit benchmark prints trades costed per second; no asserted number.
- **Determinism and fail closed:** no clock, `Random` or culture dependence; a missing or null rate cannot be constructed; a date without rates refuses; nothing falls back to a default or to zero.
- **Security and privacy:** the sheet is not a secret; tests use the TEST sheet and invented data; nothing is read from `data/`.
- **Observability:** the skip fates and the sizing/cost record are the explanation; no logging in the pure library.
- **Compatibility:** nothing of schema 2 or 3 is byte-compatible with schema 4 (D14).

## Where the roadmap row and the code disagree
1. **"One validated JSON row in `platform.app_settings`"** — `AppSetting.MaxValueLength` is 500 ("a short text value") and one schedule of two segments is about 600 characters: handled in P3-03f (migration), but the canonical limit of 8,000 is fixed here (D4).
2. **"A run without a sheet is refused, never zero-cost"** against "until the sheet exists every run is gross and says so" (ISS-25, Wave text, doc 19): after the split, this slice leaves every run gross and labelled; the refusal is made a type-level fact in P3-03f (the request has a required cost choice with an explicit gross).
3. **"A trade is skipped, and counted"** — the code has no counter for a skip after the walk (`WalkCounters` is the walker's; `RunFates` has no skip fate). Two fates are added, which changes the run record, its key and `SimulationRun`'s schema as well as `SimTrade`'s; the row names `SimTrade` only. The totals of `Walk` still include the skipped trades.
4. **`max_cost_R`** is under `selection:` in doc 16 (the nightly plan), not in the risk profile; the value is 0.10 in doc 16 and doc 10. It is a field of `CostContext`.
5. **"Cost in R"** is undefined in the row; the code's unit is `risk` per share from the exact entry, and the floor makes the rupee risk taken smaller than the budget. `CostR` divides by `RiskTaken` (doc 08 §1), not by the budget. **"`fill`"** in `max capital / fill` is read as the exact entry (D11).
6. **"Intraday and delivery"** — `Simulator.Create` refuses every style but intraday, so the delivery segment is unreachable by a run until P3-03d merges; tested at the level of a leg.
7. **Doc 16's `costs` shape** (`stt_ctt` with a futures field, `brokerage.delivery: 0` without a unit, no minimum, "other", DP charge or effective dates) is not the shape the model needs.
8. **ISS-25's "derive the rates from the owner's own imported bills"**: the importers store one charge total per bill, so rates per line cannot be derived from the stored data; the original files hold the lines (P3-03f).
9. **The charge list of the row has no DP charge**; the depository report shows one per delivery sale (local note, UNVERIFIED), so it is an explicit line.
10. **The position gate of P3-03d** runs before the walk and costing after it, so a swing trade skipped by costing still holds the gate (D17, a named limit).
11. **The size**: the row says 2; this half is 2.3 and the pair 3.8.
12. **P3-04b1 (Draft)** fixes the label `costs: none` as a constant and `costs` as `none` in `trades.csv`; both change with this slice (hand-over above).

## Open questions
Questions 1 and 2 are for the owner (the split; the named limit of D17); the third is a shape decision of this slice that the owner confirms with the spec. The charge sheet itself, its dates and the DP figure are questions of [P3-03f](P3-03f-the-cost-choice-the-stored-charge-sheet-the-verbs-and-the-bill-check.md), which needs them; this slice does not.
1. **Is the split accepted?** **Recommendation:** yes (recommended, the owner confirms): this slice is the pure half at about 2.3 agent-days and P3-03f the half that needs the owner's sheet at about 1.6, together 3.8 against the 3.6 of the first draft (+0.2: the bill check reads the original files line by line, and the verbs join P3-04b2's dispatch); the specs README allows one slice 1 to 3 days. 2. **Accept the named limit that a swing trade skipped for quantity or cost still counts as the strategy's position for P3-03d's gate (D17), or move the gate after costing in a later slice?** **Recommendation:** accept it for P3: the gate models the signal's position, the skip is accounting, the replay stays window-independent (pre-window trades cannot be costed), and the limit is printed in every run's banner; it concerns swing runs only (none before P3-06, which may slide) and intraday is unaffected. Moving the gate after costing would change P3-03d's `Simulator.Run` and the replay's window rule.
3. **Does the sheet carry a DP charge field for delivery sales?** **Recommendation:** yes, as `dp_charge_per_sell` (0 allowed): costs are always on and a swing exit pays a depository charge every time; the figure is one flat amount per sale (the real lines vary, limit `dp_charge_is_flat`). The amount is the owner's, asked in P3-03f.

## Risks
- **The formulas are from the field names of doc 16 and are UNVERIFIED** (which side pays STT in delivery, the GST base, brokerage per order, whether the DP figure includes GST, per-leg rounding). A wrong form would pass the worked examples and be wrong on a real bill. Mitigation: each is a field of the sheet or the single place of D8; every label says `not checked against bills` until P3-03f's check and the owner's examples have passed; the negative controls pin the forms we commit to.
- **A slipped unit** (0.0003 for 0.03): the bounds are nets; the bill check is the guard.
- **Merge conflicts with P3-03d** in `SimTrade`, `RunFates`, `RunAssembler`, the record and the replayer. Mitigation: merge order; `Costing` appended last with a default; fates appended at the end with defaults; version constants set at the rebase; the fates identity test is the net.
- **The gate interaction (D17)** biases a swing trade set when costing skips trades: a named limit, printed in the banner, pinned by a test; reversible by a later slice.
- **Skipping by `max_cost_R` shapes the trade set** (it drops tight-stop trades) and the floor leaves risk unused: counted, labelled; a result is never quoted without them.
- **Fixed capital and unconstrained overlap** overstate what one account could run at once (`fixed_capital`).
- **Quantity from the exact entry** can exceed the cap by a few ticks (`no_margin_or_leverage`).
- **Charges as a share of risk dominate small stops**: that is what the slice exists to show; no figure is quoted outside its run folder before P3-08 is green.
- **ETFs and other series** are charged as cash equity (`cash_equity_only`).
- **A golden file written by the code** is not evidence: the paired hand figures (W7, W8) are.
