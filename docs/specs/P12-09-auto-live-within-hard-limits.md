# P12-09 — `auto_live` within hard limits: the caps, the guard, the always-ask list and "every action notified"

- **Needs (build order of the group: P12-07, P12-11, P12-08, P12-09, P12-10, P12-12):** P12-11 (readiness, which the guard reads), P12-07 (modes, `ExecutionSettings`, risk-direction rule), P12-08 (`ActionClass`, tickets for always-ask actions, `day_unlocks`), P12-01 (submit path calls the ledger), P12-03 (pre-trade checks; its registry already holds the global `exec.max_order_value_inr`, `exec.max_orders_per_day`, `exec.entry_window_*` and `exec.max_orders_per_second`), the intent creator (P12-08), P12-04 (broker-side stop), P12-05 (halts, kill switch), P12-06 (reconciliation state), P12-11 (readiness). Feeds P12-10, P12-12.
- **Size:** 3 agent-days (estimate).
- **Safety:** yes. It lets software send orders without a tap. Default: no account is in `auto_live`; `auto_live_enabled` is OFF; the limits sheet is empty, and an empty sheet allows nothing; the spec being Draft holds the build.

## Assumptions
1. **`auto_live` is the automatic form of the tap** (CLAUDE.md setting rule): its switch `auto_live_enabled` in `execution.settings` defaults OFF, only the owner turns it on (password, cooling-off, P12-07), every automatic send is recorded as automatic with the settings and limits snapshot ids, and `unavailable` inputs never count as a pass.
2. **It never starts a position the owner did not approve the evening before.** A ticket must be Approved by the owner (kind `owner`); an `auto` approval (P7-12) is accepted only if `exec.auto_live_allow_auto_approved` is ON (default OFF). With both auto switches ON the chain "rule approves, software sends" is possible and the owner chose it twice.
3. **Hard limits apply to every system-sent entry**, in `semi_auto` too (one limits sheet per account), so a tap cannot exceed what `auto_live` could. The effective limit is always the stricter of the account's sheet and P12-01's global registry value (order value, orders a day, entry window); the sheet can only narrow the global ones, which stay compiled ceilings.
4. **Rates.** Our ceiling is P12-01's `exec.max_orders_per_second` (default 2, compiled ceiling 5; doc 16, doc 19 §5), which this spec does not change. Dhan allows 10 per second, 250 per minute, 1,000 per hour, 7,000 per day for order APIs (<https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/>, read 2026-10-24); the SEBI/NSE threshold at which an own algo must be registered is 10 orders per second per exchange (NSE implementation standards of 5 May 2025 as reported by <https://community.upstox.com/t/interpretation-of-sebi-circular-titled-safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading/8020>, <https://www.sahi.com/blogs/sebi-algo-trading-rules-2026-what-every-retail-trader-must-know-before-april>, read 2026-10-24; secondary sources, the circular text was not readable: UNVERIFIED, re-checked by P11-05 and P12-11). The default sits at one fifth of that threshold and the compiled ceiling at half.
5. **Futures are out of `auto_live`.** Lots, margins and the swing-short rule (futures only, doc 19 §2) are not covered by the pre-trade checks of this phase; the only product is `EQ_MIS` (Dhan `INTRADAY`; P12-02 criterion 4: CNC, MTF, CO, BO cannot be expressed), and entries are BUY only until P12-13. A ticket in any other product or side is refused `product.not_allowed` (in `semi_auto` it would be refused the same way by P12-03).

## Goal
With the owner's per-account limits sheet complete and the day unlocked, software may place the entry of an owner-approved ticket, tighten its stop, take its planned exits and cancel its own pending orders, and nothing else: it refuses and says why whenever a cap, the clock, the feed, the token, the broker state or the owner's presence cannot be shown to be fine.

## Scope
**In:** Domain `Execution/Limits` (`ExecutionLimits`, `LimitChange` risk classification, `AutoLiveGuard`, `LossCap`, `AlwaysAskList`); Application (get/change limits, `ReserveEntry` used by the submit path, auto action recording), ports `ILimitsStore`, `ILedger`, `IActionLog`, `IOwnerPresence`, `IFeedHealth`; Infrastructure; migration `AddAutoLiveLimits`; endpoints `GET/PUT /api/execution/limits`; settings keys; tests; ADR.
**Out:** the submit path itself (P12-01), pre-trade checks (P12-03), stops (P12-04), halts and the kill switch (P12-05; this slice only calls them), screens (P12-10), futures.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given no limits row, or one with any cap unset, or an empty allow-list, then every entry is refused `limits.incomplete` (an empty sheet allows nothing). *`AutoLiveGuardTests`.*
2. Hand example (sheet: 6 orders a day, Rs 60,000 per order, Rs 150,000 per day, Rs 1,500 loss, 3 open positions, window 09:20 to 14:45, allow-list TATAPOWER, INFY, SBIN, products `EQ_MIS`): three entries of Rs 49,482.00 (120 x 412.35) are accepted (running notional 49,482.00, 98,964.00, 148,446.00); a fourth of Rs 49,482.00 is refused `cap.notional_day`; a fourth of Rs 1,554.00 is accepted (reaching exactly 150,000.00) and one of Rs 1,554.01 refused; the seventh order of any size is refused `cap.orders_day`; Rs 60,000.00 in one order passes and Rs 60,000.01 is refused `cap.notional_order`. *`AutoLiveGuardTests`, `LedgerDatabaseTests`.*
3. Loss cap (worst case, not mark to market): refuse an entry when `max(0, -realised today) + open risk to stops + new risk > max_loss_per_day`; a profit gives no room. Realised -600, open risk 870, new risk 30 → 1,500 allowed (boundary), new risk 100 → 1,570 `cap.loss_day`; realised +400 counts as 0 loss. An unavailable open-risk or realised figure refuses `cap.loss_unavailable`. *`LossCapTests`.*
4. Day halt: the halt itself is P12-05's trigger `loss_cap` (evaluated from its `day_marks`); this slice supplies the number through `ILossCapSource` (the account's `max_loss_per_day` from the limits row in force, or none when the sheet is incomplete) and `exit_all_on_loss_halt` (default OFF, so the level is `entries`; ON makes it `exit_all`, which P12-05 gates by its release rules). When realised plus unrealised mark-to-market is at or below minus the cap, no new entries are sent for the day and the stops stay at the broker; an unavailable mark does not halt, it refuses new entries (`cap.loss_unavailable`) and notifies. *`LossCapTests` with a fake `IHaltRequests`.*
5. Window and instrument: an entry at 09:19:59 IST is refused `window.closed`, at 09:20:00 allowed, at 14:44:59.9 allowed, at 14:45:00 refused (end exclusive, India time from the database clock); a symbol not on the allow-list is refused `instrument.not_allowed`; a product other than `EQ_MIS`, a futures ticket or a SELL entry is refused `product.not_allowed`. Window limits in settings: start not before 09:16, end not after 15:00. *`AutoLiveGuardTests`.*
6. The guard also refuses (each a code in the action log, never silent; the guard runs only for an `auto_live` account with both switches ON, otherwise `mode.not_auto` and no row, criterion 12): ticket not owner-approved while the auto-approval switch is OFF (`approval.not_owner`); plan marked provisional or listing an unavailable basis input such as "market gate unavailable" (`plan.provisional`); quantity above the plan's allocation (`qty.above_plan`, and it never rounds up); an open position or open order already in the instrument (`exposure.duplicate`); a strategy version never used live by this account (`strategy.first_use`, the entry is routed to a ticket as in `semi_auto`: always-ask `new_strategy_live`); pre-trade checks not all pass or any unavailable (`pretrade.failed`); no day unlock while `exec.auto_live_require_day_unlock` is ON (default ON); owner not attended (below); readiness not `ready`; feed price older than 15 s or missing (`feed.stale`); an unresolved reconciliation break today (`recon.break`); halt or kill switch active; max open positions reached. *`AutoLiveGuardTests` (one case per code, plus the all-pass case).*
7. Attended: with `exec.auto_live_require_attended` ON (default), new entries need an owner session seen within `exec.auto_live_attended_min` (default 10) by the session's `last_seen_at` (`auth.sessions`, verified column `LastSeenAt`) and not locked; otherwise `owner.not_attended` and a notice; stops, exits and cancels continue. An open browser tab is not proof of attention (limit). *`AutoLiveGuardTests`, `OwnerPresenceTests`.*
8. Always-ask. `StopLoosen`, `AddToPosition` and `ReEntry` are `Refused` in every mode until P12-13 (P12-07 routing); raising any cap, widening the window, adding to the allow-list, switching a requirement off, accepting auto-approved tickets, enabling a mode: owner act with password and the cooling-off, never automatic; a client-id change, role removal or archive demotes the account (P12-07). A table `AlwaysAskList` maps each of doc 19 §4's four items (`risk_increase`, `new_strategy_live`, `enable_execution`, `account_changes`) to its enforcement point; a test fails if one has none. *`AlwaysAskListTests`.*
9. Limits change: `PUT /api/execution/limits/{accountId}` appends a snapshot row; if any cap goes up, the window widens, the allow-list gains a symbol or a switch loosens (a mixed change counts as a raise), the password is required and `effective_from` = 09:00 IST of the next trading day after one full closed session; a pure lowering needs only the session and takes effect at once; the response shows the row in force and the pending row. *`LimitChangeTests`, `LimitsDatabaseTests`.*
10. The database refuses an entry ledger row that exceeds the limits row in force: orders, notional per order, notional per day, outside the window, symbol not on the allow-list, product not allowed, mode not `semi_auto`/`auto_live`, `execution_enabled` OFF, or without its `action_log` row; two concurrent entries for one account serialise (advisory lock) and cannot both pass the last slot. *`LedgerDatabaseTests`.*
11. Every automatic send writes one `action_log` row (kind `auto_entry`), in the same transaction as the ledger entry, carrying the settings id, the limits id and the plain-language text ("Placed BUY 120 TATAPOWER at LIMIT 412.35 with stop 405.10 and target 425.80 because ticket T approved by you on 23 Oct was triggered at 09:46; day total Rs 49,482 of Rs 150,000, order 1 of 6"); every refusal writes `auto_refused` with its codes; every automatic stop tightening, exit and cancel writes its kind. No action without a row (a test over the submit port). *`ActionLogTests`.*
12. With `auto_live_enabled` OFF (or no settings row), or `execution_enabled` OFF, an `auto_live` account's entry is refused `mode.not_auto` after reading the two switches and nothing else: it is routed to no tap (the OFF switch means no automatic send and `auto_live` is not a manual path), no ledger or action-log row is written and no notice is raised; the Today tab shows "auto_live is off". Criterion 6's "never silent" applies once the guard runs. *`AutoLiveGuardTests`, `ExecutionSettingsDatabaseTests`.*
13. Rate: the submit path never emits more than `exec.max_orders_per_second` order calls per second (default 2; token bucket in the adapter, P12-02); a test proves the guard does not rely on it for the daily caps. *`LedgerDatabaseTests`.*
14. Log ids 6720 to 6729 unique; `scripts/gen-api.sh` run; `scripts/check.sh` green; Architecture tests unchanged.

## Contract
- **API:** `GET /api/execution/limits?accountId=` → `LimitsView { inForce?: LimitsRow, pending?: LimitsRow, complete: bool, missing[], history[latest 20], ceilings }`; `PUT /api/execution/limits/{accountId}` body `{ maxOrdersPerDay, maxNotionalPerOrder, maxNotionalPerDay, maxLossPerDay, maxOpenPositions, allowList[], windowStart, windowEnd, products[], exitAllOnLossHalt, reason, password? }`; problems `execution.values_invalid` (400, field named), `execution.password_required` (400), `auth.invalid_credentials` (401), `auth.locked` (423), `execution.owner_only` (403), `execution.stale` (409), `execution.disabled` (409).
- **Ceilings** (code constants, not settings; the owner cannot exceed them): orders a day 20, open positions 10, allow-list 50 symbols, window 09:16 to 15:00 IST; the rate is P12-01's registry value.
- **Data** (schema `execution`; migration `AddAutoLiveLimits`): `limits` (insert-only complete snapshots per account): `id`, `account_id`, `max_orders_per_day int check 1..20`, `max_notional_per_order numeric(18,2) > 0`, `max_notional_per_day numeric(18,2) >= max_notional_per_order`, `max_loss_per_day numeric(18,2) > 0`, `max_open_positions int check 1..10`, `allow_list jsonb` (array, at most 50, each an NSE trading symbol), `window_start time`, `window_end time` (check start >= 09:16 and end <= 15:00 and start < end), `products text[]` (check: contained in `{EQ_MIS}`), `exit_all_on_loss_halt bool default false`, `actor owner:%`, `reason`, `changed_at`, `effective_from`, `snapshot_hash`. `action_log` (insert-only; created here, read by P12-10): `id bigserial`, `account_id null`, `session_date`, `kind varchar(24)`, `by varchar(8) check in ('auto','tap','system','owner')`, `intent_public_id null`, `ticket_id null`, `text`, `codes jsonb`, `settings_id null`, `limits_id null`, `at`. `ledger_entries` (insert-only): `id`, `account_id`, `session_date`, `intent_public_id uuid unique`, `symbol`, `strategy_hash`, `product`, `side`, `quantity`, `notional numeric(18,2)`, `risk_at_stop numeric(18,2)`, `mode`, `limits_id fk`, `settings_id fk`, `action_log_id fk not null`, `sent_at` (stamped). Trigger `execution.check_ledger_entry` (advisory lock per account and date) enforces criterion 10; the loss cap, open positions and exposure checks stay in the application (stated in limits). **Grants** (D10 of P12-07): `xd_app` INSERT on `limits` (after the password check); `xd_exec` INSERT on `ledger_entries` and `action_log`; SELECT for both; nothing else. Settings keys (P12-01 registry, risk direction): `exec.auto_live_allow_auto_approved` false [true riskier], `exec.auto_live_require_day_unlock` true [false riskier], `exec.auto_live_require_attended` true [false riskier], `exec.auto_live_attended_min` 10 (1 to 30) [higher riskier].
- **UI:** none here (limits form and the action feed are drawn by P12-10).
- **Events/jobs:** `AutoLiveGuard` runs inside P12-01's intent handling (no loop of its own); the loss-cap evaluator runs on each position or price update P12-06/feed provides and at least every 15 s while positions are open.

## Non-functional
Guard evaluation under 5 ms (pure, inputs prefetched), ledger insert one transaction. Logs 6720 to 6729: codes, counts, short hashes; never symbol-level P&L text beyond ids, never the reason text. Security: limits changes need the session, CSRF, and the password when raising (verified `Elevate: false`); the actor from the session.

## Test plan
Unit: guard (one test per refusal code and the all-pass case), caps arithmetic above, loss formula table (realised -600/+400/0, open risk 0/870, new risks 30/100, unavailable), window boundaries, limit-change classification (each field up, down, mixed), always-ask mapping. Integration: ledger trigger refusals and the concurrent last slot (20 parallel reserves, 1 passes), grants as `xd_app`, settings OFF touching nothing. Property: for any random sequence of entries, the sum accepted never exceeds the day caps and each refusal names a rule. Edge: a limits raise pending while a lower sheet is in force (in force wins); symbol case and suffix (`TATAPOWER` vs `TATAPOWER-EQ`: exact match on the master's trading symbol only).

## Decisions (researched)
- **D1. Worst-case loss cap plus a mark-to-market halt.** Question: how should "max loss per day" bite? Answer: refuse an entry that could take the day's worst case past the cap (stops fill at their price), and halt when the mark-to-market itself reaches it. Reason: a cap that only reacts after the loss is a cap on the second loss; doc 20 lists "daily loss" as a kill-switch trigger, and doc 19 §2 requires a daily loss limit for extreme days. Rejected: realised-only (ignores open positions); mark-to-market only (a gap past all stops). Gaps beyond stops remain a stated limit.
- **D2. Dhan's own kill switch and P&L exit are not the primary guard.** Dhan's kill switch can be activated only when all positions are closed and there are no pending orders, and then disables trading for the day; its P&L exit (`/v2/pnlExit`, profit/loss value, optional kill switch) is configured per day and exits applicable positions (<https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/traders-control/>, read 2026-10-24). So our caps and halts (P12-05) are primary; the broker-side P&L exit is a possible second line (P12-05 decides), not a substitute.
- **D3. An empty sheet allows nothing; ceilings are code.** Reason: fail closed; a default of "reasonable numbers" would be a limit the owner never chose. Ceilings keep a typing slip (Rs 5,00,000 for 50,000) from being legal beyond the stated maximums where a ceiling applies (orders, positions, window); money caps have no code ceiling by design (they depend on capital) but a raise waits for cooling-off.
- **D4. The database checks the countable caps; the application checks the rest.** Counts and notionals are sums over insert-only rows, a trigger can prove them (ADR 0091's approach for auto approvals); the loss cap needs live positions and prices, which only the application has. Both are listed; the DB cannot be asked to know P&L.
- **D5. Attendance is the owner's session, not an extra heartbeat.** `auth.sessions.last_seen_at` exists and is updated at most once a minute by authenticated activity (doc 04 §2); no new endpoint or channel. An out-of-app channel (a text or push message) would send trading data out of the machine (SAFETY, P8-21 not built) and is a follow-up. Rejected: unattended `auto_live` in the first release.
- **D6. Owner-approved tickets only by default.** The evening approval is the human decision; `auto_live` removes the tap, not the review. Source: doc 04 §4.1, ADR 0091/0095.
- **D7. Strategy first-use tap.** The first live entry of a strategy version hash for an account needs a ticket even in `auto_live` (doc 19 §4 `new_strategy_live`). Reason: a new version has no live history; the ledger already records `strategy_hash`, so no extra table.
- **D8. No re-entry after a stop-out the same day, and no averaging.** They are `ReEntry`/`AddToPosition`, refused until P12-13 gives them a ticket. Reason: the cheapest way to turn a controlled loss into an uncontrolled one; both are on the always-ask list as `risk_increase`.
- **D9. Notify every action individually, in the app only.** Doc 20 says "every action notified"; the in-app bell is the channel that exists (derived notices, ADR 0027). A batch summary would hide a runaway sequence. See P12-10.

## Risks and honest limits
- Stops fill at their price only in an orderly market; a gap or a halted stock can lose more than `risk_at_stop`; the loss cap is a worst case under that assumption.
- `auto_live` trusts the plan: the P7/P10 evidence is replayed history of about 2.1 years with modelled fills and no hold-out, plans are provisional until a cell is Active (P10); a `provisional` plan is refused, so until cells are Active the guard sends nothing, honestly.
- Presence is a browser session, not a person; a lock screen or a quiet tab can pass it. The attended window and the daily unlock narrow, not close, that gap.
- The 15 s feed freshness uses the app's feed, not the exchange's tape; the order band check is P12-03's.
- Until P7-06 (correlation) and P15 (results dates, F&O ban, surveillance) give the four Soft checks a source, P12-03 reads them `unavailable` and `auto_live` clears nothing; this is intended and means the first `auto_live` day cannot come before those inputs exist (the ladder gate would not be reached anyway).
- The caps are per account; two accounts trading the same signal double the exposure (the portfolio-level cap of the plan, P7-02, is the only joint limit).
- Every threshold here (15 s, 10 min, 09:20 to 14:45) is judgement and a printed limit until live evidence (P12-12) exists.
