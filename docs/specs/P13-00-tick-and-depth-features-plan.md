## Goal
Find out, with a protocol that cannot flatter itself, whether order-book and tick features add out-of-sample value to the entries the system already takes, and whether a depth-based fill estimate is closer to reality than the simulator's fixed slippage rule. **Kill criterion (the roadmap's, made exact):** the microstructure feature set `md-1` is kept (as a shadow challenger, then in scoring only by the owner's act) **only if** the paired out-of-sample comparison of section "Evaluation protocol" shows a lower bound of the improvement in net R per taken candidate above the margin, with at least 6 test folds and the minimum n; otherwise it is dropped: the recorder is stopped by the owner, the files are removed on the owner's say, the feature code stays in the repo as unused. No verdict is a failure of the system: `NOT_EVALUABLE` and `INCONCLUSIVE` are legitimate answers and are printed as such.

## What P13 takes from P7-16 (spec now on disk, checked 2026-10-24)
Recorded: `market.depth_snapshots` (hypertable; six int4 arrays: bid/ask price, quantity, orders), whole-book snapshots **at most once a second, change-only, receipt time** (`Depth:SampleMs` 1000), up to 50 instruments a day (`Depth:MaxInstruments`), set per day = armed tickets (≤ 20), then `Depth:PinnedSymbols`, then the most liquid by 20-session turnover, stored in `market.depth_session_instruments` with reason and rank; `market.depth_sessions.gap_seconds`; budget `Depth:MaxStoredGb` 40. ## Evaluation protocol (what decides keep or drop)
1. **Population.** Candidates of all stored intraday strategies in the cells the champion model covers, on instrument-sessions with ≥ 85 % coverage whose recording reason is **pinned or liquid** (primary population); candidates on names recorded only because they were armed are a secondary report (D3), from the first recorded session. Hold-out: the last 2 months are reserved and used once at the phase check (`HoldOut.Reserve`, `validation.holdout_touched` stays). Daily-and-longer swing candidates are out (D7).
2. **Models.** A = the champion recipe on `tf-1` features (ADR 0068), refitted per fold; B = the same recipe on `tf-1` + `md-1`. Same rows, folds, seeds, calibration slice. Nothing else differs.
3. **Folds.** Expanding, 2 months of initial training, **monthly test windows**; purge = label horizon + one bar (ADR 0067), embargo 3 sessions (`extensions.embargo_sessions`; the 7-day embargo of P6-05 would cost a quarter of a monthly fold). Fewer than 6 usable test folds can never give KEEP (the rule of P5-03/P6-05).
4. **Multiple testing.** Every (feature subset, model configuration, label, take-policy) evaluated is a trial appended to the ledger family `ext:md-1` **before** its result is read; N = distinct trial hashes; all margins are multiplied by `MultipleTesting.Factor(N)`. The primary comparison is *all of `md-1` against none* (one trial); subsets are secondary and counted.
5. **Metrics.** Primary: Δ net R per taken candidate (B minus A) under the ADR 0075 take policy with the threshold chosen only from earlier folds, at least half the candidates kept; net R only if the stored charge sheet is the owner's (ISS-25), otherwise gross and labelled. Secondary: Δ log loss, Δ Brier, calibration error in pp (≤ 5 pp), per-fold sign.
6. **Interval.** Paired **day-block bootstrap** (resample sessions, 10,000 draws, fixed seed) of the pooled ΔR; reported with the 90 % one-sided lower bound.
7. **KEEP (shadow) requires all:** ≥ 6 test folds; pooled paired OOS candidates ≥ 2,000 and ≥ 300 candidates whose take decision differs between A and B; lower bound of ΔR ≥ `0.02 R × Factor(N)` (a choice, printed, tunable); ΔR > 0 in ≥ 4 of 6 folds (`ceil(2/3)`); Δ log loss below `−0.005 × Factor(N)` pooled and Brier below `−0.002 × Factor(N)` (the P6-05 margins); calibration error of B ≤ 5 pp; hold-out untouched until the phase check; then once on the hold-out with the same sign and no worse than A on log loss.
8. **DROP:** ≥ 6 folds and upper 90 % bound of ΔR < 0.02 R, or 12 months of recording with no KEEP. **INCONCLUSIVE:** otherwise (keep recording). **NOT_EVALUABLE:** < 6 folds or < 2,000 paired candidates or coverage rule failed; it names the missing quantity.
9. **Power (hand-worked, printed with the result).** ΔR is the mean over all n candidates of `(take_B − take_A) · R`; with a flip share f and `E[R²] ≈ 1.2` (UNVERIFIED; measured and printed), `SE ≈ sqrt(f · 1.2 / n)`. For f = 0.15: n = 1,000 → SE 0.0134, minimum detectable effect (one-sided 5 %, 80 % power, `2.49 × SE`) 0.033 R; n = 2,000 → 0.0095, 0.024 R; n = 4,000 → 0.0067, 0.017 R. Candidates on one day are correlated, so the real SE is larger (the bootstrap measures it). A margin of 0.02 R × Factor(N) is therefore detectable only from about 2,000 candidates, which is why that is the minimum.
10. **Slippage verdict (P13-04).** The depth estimate is *better* only if, on P11 live fills (when they exist, n ≥ 60) or on the owner's real fills, its mean absolute error against realised slippage in ticks is lower than the ADR 0038 rule's by a bootstrap lower bound > 0. Until then its status is `unvalidated`, and it stays a sensitivity.
11. **After KEEP:** the shadow period of P13-07 (≥ 60 paper trades) must not show a calibration error above 5 pp or negative ΔR before any ON.

## Scope
**In:** P13-01 to P13-10 above; the evaluation protocol; the extension side table; the report. **Out:** the recorder (P7-16) and a tick recorder unless P7-16 is amended, 200-level depth (D4), strategies that trade the book itself, HFT (doc 20: not pursued), live use before P12.

## Acceptance criteria (of the phase; each slice carries its own list)
1. Given a recorded fixture of two instruments and three sessions, when `AsOf(t, lag)` is called, then the snapshot returned has receipt time ≤ `t − lag`, and replacing every snapshot after `t` with extremes changes no feature (property test with a control that a snapshot before `t` matters).
2. Given a book walk of the hand-worked example (below), then the fill price, slippage in ticks and in bps equal the hand values; a quantity above the visible 20 levels gives `unavailable` (never a worse-than-last-level fill).
3. Given the evaluation of a fixture where B has no skill (labels independent of `md-1`), when run with N = 1 and N = 16, then the verdict is not KEEP; given a fixture where `md-1` carries the label (cheating control), then KEEP at ≥ 6 folds and INCONCLUSIVE at 5.
4. Given fewer than 6 folds or fewer than 2,000 candidates, then the verdict is `NOT_EVALUABLE` with the missing quantity named, and no number is rendered as a digit for a non-value (P8-00 D3).
5. Given the same inputs in any row order, then the evaluation record (and its id) is identical (determinism, fixed seed).
6. Given a KEEP, when the owner has not turned `learning.extensions.md1_in_scoring` ON, then no score changes; given the setting ON without password re-entry, then it is refused.
7. Given a DROP, then a suggestion appears and nothing is stopped or deleted; given a day with < 85 % coverage, then it contributes no candidate and is counted in the evidence block.

## Test plan
Unit: order book (crossed, locked, empty side, stale), each feature on hand books, book walk, ledger count, margins, verdict table. Properties: no leak, determinism, row order. Integration (Testcontainers): insert-only grants, read of a compressed chunk, record round trip. Frontend (MSW): each state. Architecture: core in Domain, adapters in Infrastructure (ADR 0039). **Hand-worked numbers:** book bid 99.95 × 1,200, ask 100.00 × 800 → `imb_l1 = (1200 − 800) / 2000 = +0.20`; microprice `(99.95·800 + 100.00·1200) / 2000 = 99.98`, mid 99.975, deviation +0.005 = +0.5 bps. Book walk, buy 1,500 with asks 600 @ 100.00, 500 @ 100.05, 1,000 @ 100.10: cost `60,000 + 50,025 + 40,040 = 150,065`, VWAP 100.04333, versus mid 99.975 = 0.06833 = 6.83 bps = 1.37 ticks of 0.05 (the ADR 0038 rule would give 1 tick + 5 % of the minute range, for a 0.40 range 0.07 = 1.4 ticks: the two agree to the tick for this size). Margin table: N = 1 → ×1.0 (0.02 R), N = 4 → ×1.5 (0.03 R), N = 16 → ×2.0 (0.04 R).

## Decisions (researched)
- **D1. Start P13 research only when the evaluable date has arrived; build the readers earlier.** The recorder's clock is the constraint, not the code. P13-01 to P13-04 can be built as soon as 5 sessions are recorded (they need real packets for fixtures), and the verdict slices wait. Rejected: "evaluating" on 1 month. Source: doc 20 §B, P7-00.
- **D2. Time alignment by receipt time with a lag, because the packet has no exchange time stamp.** Source: the packet layout, <https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/full-market-depth/> (2026-10-24). Snapshots are once-a-second and change-only (P7-16), so the book used is the last one received at or before `decision − 2 s`; a quiet book has no new row, which the reader treats as unchanged only up to `extensions.max_stale_s` 120, else `unavailable`; the lag is a setting. Alternative rejected: using the same instant (look-ahead through network latency).
- **D3. The evaluation population is the pinned list, frozen before any result is read, plus the liquid remainder; armed-only names are secondary.** P7-16's set changes daily and its armed part is chosen by the model under test (a selection effect). Recommended owner act: pin the 30 most liquid names by past turnover (`Depth__PinnedSymbols`) before switching on and never change them; a change starts a new epoch and folds never mix epochs. Rejected: pooling armed names into the primary test.
- **D4. 20-level depth, not 200-level.** The 200-level feed is one instrument per connection and at most 5 connections per user (the live feed uses one): at most 4 instruments. Our decisions are at 5-minute bars and up; the literature's signal is at the best levels (order flow imbalance explains most short-horizon price change: Cont, Kukanov, Stoikov, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402>, read 2026-10-24, US data, short horizons).
- **D5. A separate extension side table, not `tf-2`.** `tf-1` rows, training-set ids and the champion's lineage stay valid; P15 uses the same mechanism with its own extension ids. `TrainingDataBuilder` gets an optional extra feature group (additive; the file was read for its inputs only, the exact change is UNVERIFIED until P13-05).
- **D6. Ledger family `ext:<id>`** (one family per extension), because the ledger's family is a strategy id (ADR 0059 D1) and the multiple-testing count must follow the number of looks at *this* extension. Source: `ITestLedger`, `MultipleTesting`.
- **D7. Intraday only.** Depth at a swing decision (a close) says little about a multi-day hold, and swing candidates are too few in 8 months.
- **D8. Margins 0.02 R, 0.005 log loss, 0.002 Brier, 6 folds, 2,000 candidates** are the P5-03/P6-05 numbers or derived from the power table; all printed and tunable.
- **D9. The slippage model changes nothing by default.** For the owner's order size in liquid large caps the order is a small part of the best level, so the walk reduces to about half the spread (worked example: 1.4 ticks against 1.4 ticks). Real fills (P11) decide.
- **D10. Aggressive volume delta needs trade ticks, which P7-16 does not record (its Out list).** Rebuilding trades from once-a-second depth is not reliable.

## Risks and honest limits
1. **No data yet; the evaluable date is 8 months after recording starts** and depends on the owner's answer to the P7-16 question and on the Mac being awake from 09:15 to 15:30 on trading days (coverage rule).
2. **Eight months is one regime sample**; the shadow period and the hold-out are the guard, and the verdict says so.
3. **Home-network receipt jitter** (tens to hundreds of ms) is small against minute-scale features; the lag is a counted trial parameter. The recorded names are liquid: a verdict does not carry to illiquid ones.
5. **Edge size**: published microstructure effects are at very short horizons on US data; at 5-minute bars and up they may be zero. A DROP is a likely outcome; it costs about 23 agent-days and some disk.
6. **Disk**: past 12 months or a pilot ratio under 3:1 needs the external SSD volume (owner act). **Power**: under about 2,000 candidates the answer is NOT_EVALUABLE by rule, not a quiet "no effect".
7. **Survivorship and costs**: members as of today (ISS-22); gross R unless the owner's charge sheet is stored (ISS-25).
