# P12-05 — Kill switch and halts

- **Status:** Draft (written 2026-10-24; decisions below were researched under the owner's delegation; the owner's Approved is still needed before an agent builds it)
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P12 — Execution service, slice P12-05 of [P12-00](P12-00-execution-service-plan.md)
- **Design refs:** docs/design/20-extended-modules.md §A ("Kill switch: ours (stop new, cancel pending, optional exit-all) + broker's where available; triggered by max drawdown, daily loss, feed loss or a button") · 08-exits-and-risk.md §7 (daily loss −2R or −1.5%, max drawdown 8%) · 19 §4 (runaway automated orders) · 04 §4.1 ("enabling execution: only you"), §2 (step-up) · ADR 0091, 0083. **Design change:** doc 20 §A gains the level and trigger tables below. ADR: next free ADR number at build time.
- **Needs:** P12-01, P12-02, P12-04 (flatten walk, alarms). Used by P12-03 (`no_halt`), P12-06 (freeze), P12-07 (kill-switch drill gate), P12-09 (`loss_cap`, `exit_all_on_loss_halt`), P12-10 (screen).
- **Size:** 2 agent-days
- **Safety:** yes. It can cancel working orders and, on the owner's word or a pre-authorised setting, exit every position. Halting is the safe direction, so starting a halt is easy (session and CSRF, no password); releasing one is the risk-raising direction (fresh password, conditions checked). Default: nothing is halted and no trigger is armed until a mode above `assisted` exists.

## Goal
One mechanism that stops new orders within one second on the owner's button or on any of 17 automatic trigger codes, cancels what is pending, optionally exits everything on explicit authority, survives restarts, and is released only by the owner (a few state-of-the-world triggers clear themselves), with a drill that proves it works without placing an order.

## Scope
**In:** Domain `HaltLevel`, `HaltTrigger` table, `HaltPolicy` (effective state, permitted actions, release conditions), `TriggerEvaluator` (pure: daily loss, drawdown, feed loss, runaway, reject burst, crash loop, disaster loss); Application `HaltService` (implements `IHaltRequests` of P12-04 and `IHaltSource` of P12-01), `HaltGate` (in-process, versioned), `HaltEnforcer` (cancels, exit-all through P12-04's flatten), `KillSwitchCoordinator` (Dhan's switch, P&L exit), `DrillService`; tables `halts`, `halt_releases`, `day_marks`, `drills`; commands `halt`, `release`, `drill`; endpoints `GET /api/execution/halts`, `POST /api/execution/halts`, `POST /api/execution/halts/{id}/release`, `POST /api/execution/drills`, `GET /api/execution/drills`; settings.
**Out:** the screen and notifications (P12-10), reconciliation itself (P12-06 requests the freeze), the readiness check that clears token and IP halts (P12-11 supplies the fact), deciding the loss caps of `auto_live` (P12-09).

## Levels and permitted actions
Levels, strongest last in effect on entries: `entries` (no new entry), `cancel` (as entries, and working entry orders are cancelled), `exit_all` (as cancel, and every position is flattened; stops cancelled only once flat), `freeze` (no entry, no tightening, no cancel: our data is untrusted, used by P12-06; the only instructions still allowed are placing or repairing a protective stop and flattening a filled position, each guarded by a fresh broker position read, P12-04 Authority). Effective state of an account = the strongest active halt of the system and the account (freeze > exit_all > cancel > entries).

| Action | none | entries | cancel | exit_all | freeze |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| new entry | yes | no | no | no | no |
| cancel a working entry | yes | yes | yes (done) | yes | no |
| place or repair a protective stop | yes | yes | yes | yes | **yes** (guarded) |
| tighten a stop | yes | yes | yes | no | no |
| square-off, flatten, exit | yes | yes | yes | yes (done) | **yes** (guarded) |

## Triggers (code: when, level, who clears)
`owner_button`: the owner's choice, default `cancel`; owner. `daily_loss`: realised plus unrealised today at or below minus min(`daily_loss_r` x risk budget, `daily_loss_pct` x capital) (the number of P12-03); `entries`; owner. `loss_cap`: P12-09's cap; `entries`, or `exit_all` when `exit_all_on_loss_halt` is ON (P12-09, default OFF); owner. `drawdown`: cumulative result since execution began, peak to now, at least `exec.drawdown_halt_pct` (8.0) of capital; `entries` across sessions; owner with a written review (at least 20 characters). `disaster_loss`: day result at or below minus `exec.disaster_loss_pct` (3.0) of capital; `cancel` plus CRITICAL alarm, or `exit_all` when `exit_all_on_loss_halt` is ON; owner. `feed_loss`: no fresh price for `exec.feed_loss_halt_s` (15) with an open position or working entry during the session; `entries`; auto-clears after 60 s of continuous fresh prices. `reconciliation_break`: P12-06; `freeze`; owner (after the break is resolved). `protection_breach`, `flatten_incomplete`, `oversold`: P12-04; `entries` on the account; owner. `protection_unknown`: P12-04; `entries`; auto-clears when the broker view is fresh again for 60 s. `runaway_rate`: more than `exec.runaway_orders_10s` (6) sends in 10 s, or `exec.reject_burst` (5) broker rejects in 5 minutes, or an attempted send above the limiter; system scope `cancel`; owner. `token_invalid` and `ip_not_allowed`: a DH-901 or an invalid-IP DH-905 (P12-02); `entries` on the account; auto-clears when P12-11 reports ready. `crash_loop`: `exec.crash_loop_starts` (3) starts in 10 minutes; system `cancel` at boot; owner. `clock_skew`: the offset between our clock and the broker's HTTP `Date` header (`skew_ms` of `broker_calls`, P12-02) above `exec.clock_skew_halt_ms` (2000) on 3 consecutive reads; `entries`; auto-clears after 5 consecutive reads inside the limit. `exchange_halt`: the market-wide circuit-breaker or an exchange halt, detected as the Nifty 50 index (from the live feed) at or beyond `exec.index_halt_pct` (9.0%) from the previous close, in either direction (the exchange's first index circuit-breaker level is 10%; secondary sources only, UNVERIFIED), or any exchange-wide halt message the broker surfaces; `entries`; owner. `broker_outage`: broker REST calls failing at the connection level (NotSent or Unknown with no HTTP answer) for `exec.broker_outage_s` (60) with an open position or working order, which is wider than `protection_unknown` (a stale view): it means we cannot send either; CRITICAL alarm, `entries`; auto-clears after 3 successful reads.
`exit_all` is requested only by (1) the owner's explicit choice, (2) P12-04 for one position without a stop (that is a flatten, not a halt level), (3) the pre-authorised setting `exit_all_on_loss_halt` (P12-09, default OFF; turning it on is the owner's act with a password). Nothing else flattens.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Policy table.** `HaltPolicy.Permits(effectiveState, action)` equals the table for all 25 cells; the effective state of any set of halts is the strongest; a halt of scope system covers every account. *`HaltPolicyTests`.*
2. **Latency.** From the insert of a `halt` command to no entry leaving the process: at most 1 s p95 and never after the second gate check of the send path (an entry whose write-ahead row exists but whose send had not started ends `submitting>failed` with reason `halted_before_send` and is not sent); 1,000 seeds of a halt racing a burst of 50 entries on the FakeBroker never show an entry accepted after the halt's commit plus 1 s. *`HaltLatencyTests`, `HaltGateRaceTests`.*
3. **Cancel level.** At `cancel` every working entry leg is cancelled (cancel retried with lookup until the book shows it or three attempts, then alarm), protective legs stay, positions stay protected. *`HaltEnforcerTests`.*
4. **Exit-all.** At `exit_all` each position is flattened with P12-04's walk, stops cancelled only when that position is flat, the halt stays until released; a position without a quantity is skipped; partial failure leaves `flatten_incomplete` and retries. *`ExitAllTests`.*
5. **Triggers.** For each trigger, a hand fixture crosses the threshold and the halt (code, level, scope) appears exactly once (a repeat does not stack); the value just inside does nothing; the auto-clearing ones clear after 60 s of the good state and never otherwise. Daily loss fixture: capital 500,000, risk budget 2,500, limit min(5,000, 7,500) = 5,000; result -4,999 no halt, -5,000 halt. Drawdown: cumulative peak +20,000, now -20,000 gives 40,000 = 8.0%: halt; 39,999 no halt. Runaway: 7 sends in 10 s halt, 6 do not. *`TriggerEvaluatorTests`.*
6. **Persistence.** Halts and releases are insert-only rows; a restart loads active halts (those with neither a release nor an auto-clear) before anything else (the host stays `recovering` until it has); a halt cannot be removed by editing; the same trigger and scope already active returns the existing halt. *`HaltStoreTests`, `RestartKeepsHaltTests`.*
7. **Start is cheap, release is not.** `POST /api/execution/halts` needs a session and CSRF only and is carried out by `xd-exec` through a command; `POST .../release` is handled in `xd-api`: fresh password (the `Elevate: false` pattern of P7-12), actor from the session, a reason, and the release conditions, then **`xd-api` inserts the `halt_releases` row itself as `xd_app`** (an owner-act table, like `mode_trail`; `xd_exec` has no INSERT on it). The database trigger `execution.check_halt_release` enforces what a database can: the halt exists and is not released, the actor starts `owner:`, an `exit_all` halt only when the account's `positions` are all flat, a `freeze` only when every break of the account has a `break_resolutions` row, a drawdown halt only with a 20-character reason. The rest of the conditions (protection halts only with no unprotected position) are checked by the handler from the alarms and the monitor's last verdict; anything refused answers 409 `execution.release_refused` with every reason. The automatic clears are rows in a separate table `halt_auto_clears` written by `xd_exec` (trigger: only for the auto-clearing trigger codes). A halt is active when it has neither. *`HaltEndpointTests`, `ReleaseConditionsTests`, `HaltReleaseTriggerTests`.*
8. **Broker kill switch.** With `exec.broker_kill_switch_on_halt` true (default false) or the owner's per-halt choice, once the account is flat with no pending order the coordinator calls Dhan's switch (`ACTIVATE`) and records the answer; with positions or orders open it does not call, records `broker_kill_switch_deferred` and retries when flat; the P12-03 check reads the status. Never activated by a trigger that auto-clears. *`KillSwitchCoordinatorTests` (FakeBroker precondition: flat, no pending).*
9. **Broker P&L exit.** With `exec.broker_pnl_exit` true (default false) the first thing the day's preflight does after readiness is read `GET /v2/pnlExit`, and only if it is inactive configure `POST /v2/pnlExit` with `lossValue` = capital x `exec.disaster_loss_pct`, product INTRADAY, `enableKillSwitch` false; it is never configured until the owner has recorded the live canary C3 (P12-00) showing the sign convention and the effect of a value beyond the current loss; the sandbox cannot show fills or a P&L (third-party sources), so no result of it is accepted for this; the flag refuses to start when the stored canary record is missing. *`PnlExitConfigTests`.*
10. **Drill.** `POST /api/execution/drills {accountId}` (session, CSRF, password) requires the account to hold no position or working order, raises a `cancel` halt with trigger `drill`, attempts an entry through the real send path built with an **injected refusing transport** (a composition-time object of the drill only; there is no `dry` flag in production configuration and no way to select it from settings or a command) which must be refused by the gate before any transport is called, checks that `no_halt` fails and a stop-repair instruction would still pass, releases with how `auto`, and stores a row (passed, latency, detail). No order is sent in any case. A halt of any other kind active at the time makes the drill refuse (`execution.drill_refused`). *`DrillTests`.*
11. **Day marks.** At the end of each session `day_marks` stores per account the realised result, the marked result, the capital and the peak so far (insert-only), from which drawdown is read; a missing mark is `unavailable` and the drawdown trigger does not fire on it but the check of P12-03 for entries reads `unavailable` too. *`DayMarksTests`.*
12. `scripts/gen-api.sh` run; `scripts/check.sh` green.

## Contract
- **API:** `GET /api/execution/halts?active=` -> `[{id, at, scope, accountId, level, trigger, source, actor, reason, release?:{at, by, how, reason}}]`; `POST /api/execution/halts {scope, accountId?, level: entries|cancel|exit_all, reason, brokerKillSwitch?}` -> the halt (`exit_all` additionally needs the session's step-up window, 403 `auth.step_up_required` otherwise, because it realises losses); `POST /api/execution/halts/{id}/release {reason, password}`; `POST /api/execution/drills {accountId, password}`; `GET /api/execution/drills`. Problem codes `execution.release_refused`, `execution.drill_refused`, `execution.halt_unknown`.
- **Data:** `execution.halts(id bigint identity, at, scope check in ('system','account'), account_id null, level check in ('entries','cancel','exit_all','freeze'), trigger_code, source check in ('owner','system'), actor, reason text up to 300)`; `execution.halt_releases(halt_id pk fk, at, by check like 'owner:%', reason)` (owner acts, written by `xd_app`, trigger `execution.check_halt_release`); `execution.halt_auto_clears(halt_id pk fk, at, trigger_code, detail)` (written by `xd_exec`, only for auto-clearing codes); `execution.day_marks(account_id, session_date, realised numeric(18,2), marked numeric(18,2), capital numeric(18,2), peak numeric(18,2), at, pk(account_id, session_date))`; `execution.drills(id, at, account_id, passed bool, latency_ms int, detail)`. All insert-only; `xd_app` SELECT only except INSERT on `halt_releases`; a halt itself is created by `xd-exec` after a command. Migration `AddExecutionHalts` with `Down`. The two release tables are the only place a halt ends.
- **Settings added:** `exec.drawdown_halt_pct` 8.0 (1..30), `exec.disaster_loss_pct` 3.0 (0.5..10), `exec.feed_loss_halt_s` 15 (5..60), `exec.runaway_orders_10s` 6 (3..20), `exec.reject_burst` 5 (2..20), `exec.crash_loop_starts` 3 (2..10), `exec.clock_skew_halt_ms` 2000 (500..5000), `exec.index_halt_pct` 9.0 (3..20), `exec.broker_outage_s` 60 (20..300), `exec.broker_kill_switch_on_halt` false, `exec.broker_pnl_exit` false. `exit_all_on_loss_halt` belongs to P12-09.
- **Log ids** 6660 to 6674.

## Non-functional
Halt to gate: under 1 s p95 (command poll 500 ms plus `LISTEN`); evaluator tick 1 s; enforcement of `cancel` on 5 working entries under 3 s. Halts are never lost: the host does not leave `recovering` before reading them.

## Test plan
Unit: policy table, evaluator fixtures with boundaries (just inside, at, just beyond), release conditions. Race and property (1,000 to 5,000 seeds): halt versus send; any interleaving ends with no entry sent after the halt's second gate check; negative control: a gate checked only before the write-ahead is caught on at least 10 seeds. Integration: store, restart, endpoints with and without password, drill. FakeBroker: kill switch preconditions, cancel retries, exit-all with partial failure.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **Our halt is the primary control; Dhan's switch is a belt that cannot be the emergency stop.** Dhan's kill switch can be activated only when all positions are closed and no orders are pending, and it disables trading for the day (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/traders-control/, read 2026-10-24); that makes it unusable while a runaway holds positions. Rejected: relying on it. It is opt-in because it also locks the owner's manual trading that day.
2. **Dhan's P&L-based exit as an independent net, default off.** `POST /v2/pnlExit` exits applicable positions when a profit or loss value is breached and resets after the session (same page); the page's own inconsistencies (status values, field names, whether a loss value is signed, immediate trigger when set beyond the current loss) are why it stays off until the sandbox smoke and a one-share canary show the sign convention. It lives at the broker, so it still works if the Mac is down: the only control with that property besides the stops.
3. **Asymmetry: easy to stop, hard to resume.** Same reasoning as P7-12 (a setting that raises risk needs the owner and a fresh password) and doc 16 `step_up_for: execution`. Starting `exit_all` needs the step-up window because it realises losses; starting `entries` or `cancel` never does.
4. **Exit-all only on explicit authority.** Stops already bound each loss; flattening in a panic realises a loss and pays spread. Automatic exit-all exists only as the owner's pre-authorised setting (`exit_all_on_loss_halt`, P12-09), default off. Rejected: automatic exit-all on any loss trigger.
5. **`freeze` as a separate level.** After a reconciliation break our picture is untrusted; tightening or cancelling on it could make things worse, so those stop. Placing or repairing a protective stop and flattening a filled position still run, but only against a fresh broker position read that agrees with our quantity (P12-04): leaving a position naked because of a bookkeeping doubt is the worse error. Rejected: treating a break as an ordinary halt; sending nothing at all.
6. **Thresholds from the design docs.** Daily loss and drawdown are doc 08 §7 (−2R or −1.5%, 8%); the 3% disaster level is twice the daily limit (an assumption); feed loss 15 s and runaway 6 sends in 10 s are assumptions well below the 2 per second cap (2 x 10 = 20 would be the cap's ceiling; 6 shows a fault). The SEBI exchange threshold is 10 orders per second per exchange (secondary sources, section P12-02); we stay at 2.
7. **A drill that sends nothing.** The kill-switch drill gate of P12-07 needs evidence; a drill that placed a test order would itself risk one. The dry send path proves the gate, the latency and the release flow.
8. **Bookkeeping in the database; two halt stores.** Halts, releases and marks are rows, not memory or logs, so a restart, a crash loop or a new container cannot forget a halt. `execution.halts` is the execution truth; the human halts of the P11 stage (`trading.live_halts`, P11-04) are a second, read-only input to `no_halt` (P12-03) and are never written or released from here.

## Risks and honest limits
- Latency is bounded for our process only; an order already accepted by the broker cannot be recalled (only cancelled), and a fill can arrive after the halt.
- If `xd-exec` is down nothing halts; the broker-side stops and the optional Dhan P&L exit are the only controls then.
- The drawdown is on the system's own result curve, which starts when execution starts; it ignores deposits and manual trades.
- Auto-clearing triggers can flap; the 60-second quiet period limits that, not eliminates it.
- The Dhan kill-switch behaviour after `ACTIVATE` (whether `DEACTIVATE` is allowed, whether it resets at midnight) is UNVERIFIED.
