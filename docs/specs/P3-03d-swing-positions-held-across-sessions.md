## Goal
Let a swing strategy that is evaluated inside the session (30m or 1h) be simulated: a position that stays open across sessions with its stop and target still working, an overnight gap that fills at the next open, a longest hold of N sessions (1 to 5, counted with the trading calendar) that leaves at the close of session N, and at most one open position per instrument and side, so that the trades a swing strategy makes can be checked against a chart the same way the intraday ones are. The rule table of ADR 0038 is not rewritten: its stop, target and ambiguity rows already apply to any minute that holds a trade, so a swing walk is the same walk without the square-off and without the end at midnight, with one new exit (`MaxHold`), a new end-of-data meaning, and counters that show how often the overnight gap decided the trade. An intraday trade does not change by one byte (in the schema-2 view of the record; the two appended fields are 1 and 0). What the daily strategy `BASE_BREAKOUT` (P3-06) needs on top (a decision at the close of a session that fills at the next session's open, the 1D evaluation, the level stops) is **P3-03e**; here a decision taken at the close of the session (15:30) is still a counted `NoFillBar`, in a short run and in a long one alike.

- The frame closes a regular 1D bar at the step of the 15:29 minute (`TimeframeBuilder`: the bar that ends the window emits it), and by the first minute of a later date, flagged incomplete, when 15:29 is missing; the scanner's stale rule already drops the second case.

## Definitions (numbers the tests pin)
Prices are rupees; a *minute*, *tick*, *beyond*, *absent* and `HH:MM O/H/L/C` are those of ADR 0038 (a listed minute has Volume 1,000, `(v0)` has Volume 0, a minute not listed repeats the previous close with Volume 1,000). A *day* of a fixture is dense from 09:15 to 15:29 unless stated; **its first minute, when not listed, repeats the previous day's last close**. Dates are in the week of Monday 2026-03-02 (Tue 03-03, Wed 03-04, Thu 03-05, Fri 03-06, Sat 03-07, Mon 03-09). *The default week* is a calendar with no exception: every weekday is a regular session (`new TradingCalendar()`). *Session 1* is the entry session (the India date of the fill minute). The *due session* `T` is session N. *Due minute* of a session is its last minute, the one that starts at `ToMinute − 1` (15:29 for a regular session). Rows continue the numbering of ADR 0038: **X7 to X9** (exit) and **S5** (slippage); the entry rows E8 and E9 are P3-03e's. Hand cases are **M** (trades), **K** (the due session), **G** (the gate), **J** (the window) and **Q** (a flagged boundary).

### D1 — A swing walk is the ADR 0038 walk without the end of the day
A walk is a *swing walk* exactly when `WalkRules.Hold` is set; otherwise it is the walk of ADR 0038, unchanged (D12; its trades only gain the two appended fields, 1 and 0). In a swing walk the rows E0 to E7 (the entry, on the decision's date as before: a decision at or after the close of the session, which only a 30m or 1h bar closing at 15:30 can be, is a `NoFillBar` because the next minute is on another date; the same in every run, so it cannot make a prefix inconsistent), X2 to X5 (stop, target, stop first with the first-print exception, the fill minute of an entry at the limit) and S1 to S4 (slippage, the grid) apply to **every minute that holds a trade (E0) on every date**, in order. A minute with Volume 0 is passed over on every date. The levels `stop` and `target` are fixed at the entry, unrounded and in the prices of the list, and never move.

**X7 — no square-off.** X1 does not exist in a swing walk (there is no `Q`): no `SquareOff` exit, no `AfterSquareOff` no-trade, no `LateSquareOff`. The compile already refuses a `square_off` on a swing strategy and a `max_hold_sessions` on an intraday one.

### D2 — What carries across the session boundary, what does not
| Thing | Across the boundary | Why |
|-------|--------------------|-----|
| `stop`, `target`, `entry`, `risk`, the fill rules, the tick | carried unchanged | one ticket for the life of the trade; the tick is the instrument's today (the `grid_approximate` limit stands) |
| the extremes for MAE and MFE | cumulative over every minute held | R is measured from the exact entry over the whole life |
| the `last` minute that holds a trade (for X9 and X8) | carried | the exit at the last close may be on an earlier date than the one being read |
| `BarsExamined`, `ZeroVolumeMinutes`, `SessionsSeen` | cumulative | counters of the walk |
| "the first minute of the walk" (X5: the fill minute of an entry at the limit checks only the stop) | applies to the fill minute only | unchanged |
| the day's session fields (VWAP, opening range, `today_high`, the 15m bars) | not a concept of the walker | they belong to the scanner's frame, which resets them by ADR 0035; the walker reads minutes and nothing else |
| the entry window, `first_entry` and `last_entry` | decide the entry only | the exit is never windowed |
| the square-off | none (X7) | a swing position has no `square_off` (the compile refuses one) |
| the date check of the intraday loop | removed | the walk goes on to the next date |

### D3 — Counting sessions with the trading calendar
- **With a calendar** (every run through the replay has one: `RunSimulationHandler` reads it once): a *session* is a date for which `calendar.IsTradingDay` is true, special sessions and Saturday sessions included. The due session `T` is reached from the entry date by moving forward **N − 1 trading days** (so N = 1 is the entry session itself, open question 1). `SessionsHeld` is `1 + calendar.TradingDaysBetween(entry date, exit date)`. The window of the due session is `calendar.SessionOf(T)` (a special session has its own `ToMinute`); a bar on a date for which `SessionOf` is null (a closed day) is read with the regular window 09:15 to 15:30.
- **Without a calendar** (`Simulator.Run(..., calendar: null)`, direct callers and tests): the default week, so every weekday is a session, a holiday is a session without bars (it is counted in N and is *unseen*), and the due session can be an empty date; the rule X8 then exits at the last close before it. The result differs from the one with the calendar when a holiday lies in the hold (K3 against K4). A run through the replay always has a calendar, and its key holds the calendar's exceptions, so a different calendar is a different run.
- A search for the next trading day that finds none in 30 calendar days is `simulation.rules_invalid` (a calendar full of holidays must not loop for ever).
- `MaxHoldSessions` outside 1 to 5 is `simulation.rules_invalid` in `SwingHold.Create` as well as in the compile (a second net).
- Special sessions count as sessions: the calendar says they are trading days, and the trade says how many were never seen (D7). Counting only regular sessions would need a second notion of session for no gain.

### D4 — The exit at the close of session N (X8)
**X8 — `MaxHold`.** Visiting minutes in order, for each minute that holds a trade the rows X2 to X5 decide first. A minute on the due session `T` that is its **due minute** (it starts at `ToMinute(T) − 1`) and holds a trade, and in which neither the stop nor the target was reached, exits the position at its **close**, reason `MaxHold`. The minute's whole range is counted in MAE and MFE (the exit price is its close, which lies inside it). The exit instant is the start of that minute, as in every other case. Reasons for the choices:
- **The close of the last 1-minute bar, not a 15:15 open and not a closing auction.** The roadmap row says "the close of session N". The data holds 1-minute bars only; the last of the session, the minute 15:29, closes at 15:30. Taking its close is an idealisation (there is no model of the closing auction, ISS-18 (e)); it is paid for with the base slippage of a market-type fill (S5), so it is not free.
- **S5 — slippage of the new exits.** `MaxHold` and `EndOfData` are market-type fills (S1): they pay `(Ticks × tick) + BarRangeFrac × range` of the exit minute, with no extra ticks (the extra is for stop exits only), rounded `tickDown` for a long (M3: 100.70 → 100.60).
- **The stop and the target are checked in the due minute first** (a stop touched at 15:29 is a `Stop`), because the position is still open during that minute.
- **A due minute that does not hold a trade** (absent, or Volume 0) or **a due session with no minute at all** (a session the replay left out, a missing session, a holiday unknown to the default week): the position leaves **at the close of the last minute that holds a trade on the last date that has one, on or before `T`**, reason `MaxHold`, counted in `MaxHoldAtLastClose`. This is decided the moment the walker reads the first minute of a later date (it reads its start epoch, nothing else); it is never later than `T` and may be earlier. A maximum hold is a maximum: leaving at a known close is the safe reading, where waiting for the next open would hold through a session nobody can see.
- **Short and special sessions.** The due minute of a special session is the one before its `ToMinute` (K6). The replay never feeds a special session or a short one (less than 90 % of the minutes), so for a run through the replay such a `T` has no minute and the previous rule applies; the walker itself, given a list that has the Saturday session of K6, exits at 12:29.
- **Two consequences stated on purpose (review finding 10).** (a) A special session (a Muhurat evening, a Saturday session) is a session of the calendar, so it counts toward N; when it is the due session of a run through the replay it is never fed, and the position leaves at the previous session's close, one session short of N (counted: `MaxHoldAtLastClose`, `SessionsUnseen`). (b) A segment that ends the day before a flagged day, whose last session has no due minute, ends the walk as `EndOfData` and so is excluded across the discontinuity (D13), although a longer list would have made it a `MaxHoldAtLastClose` trade: the exclusion is decided by the shape of the walk at the segment's end, and this is the price of the fail-closed rule. Both are in open question 5.

### D5 — The end of the data (X9) and `OpenAtEnd`
**X9 — `EndOfData`.** In a swing walk the list of bars ends while the position is open and before the due minute: exit at the close of the last minute that holds a trade, reason `EndOfData`, counted, a market-type fill. It is data and not a rule: the replay lists it in `OpenAtEnd` (and, in a segment that a flagged day follows, counts it as `ExcludedAcrossDiscontinuity`, D13), and it is never among the trades that statistics count. The intraday meaning of X6 (no minute at or after the square-off on the date) does not exist in a swing walk, so **for a swing trade `EndOfData` means exactly that the list ended**. A list that ends at the due minute 15:29 with a minute that holds a trade is a `MaxHold`, not an `EndOfData`; a list that ends at 15:29 when that minute is Volume 0 is an `EndOfData` (M7b). An `EndOfData` swing trade blocks every later candidate in the list (D11).

### D6 — The overnight gap, for the stop and for the target
The rows already say it for any minute (X2 and X3); this slice states the consequence for the first minute of a later session:
- **A stop** (BUY) is `low ≤ stop`, exit `min(stop, open)`: a session that opens at or below the stop exits at the open, the whole gap paid (M1: the stop 99.00, the open 98.60, R −1.4 instead of −1.0). The slippage is the stop-exit slippage of the gap minute, whose range is the first minute's (a repaired 09:15 bar has a wider range, ISS-21): bigger, never smaller.
- **A target** exits at the target, never at a better price, also when the session opens beyond it (criterion 2: the numbers of H5 on the second day). It is the rule of ADR 0038 (X3) kept on purpose for three reasons: R of a `Target` exit stays exactly `k`, so the P4 comparisons of `FIXED_RR` values compare what they name; the model is never more generous than the market; and the effect is measured instead of guessed, because every such exit is counted in `OvernightTargetGaps`. A real limit order that is gapped over fills at the open price, which is better, so a swing result is somewhat **low** on gap-up winners (open question 2).
- **Stop and target in one later-session minute** follow X4 (stop first unless the open is already beyond the target).
- **On the grid.** `stop` and `target` are never rounded (T4). The exit price of a gap is the open as stored, which may lie off the grid (CSV prices adjusted at the source, the `grid_approximate` limit); only the modelled fill is moved onto the grid: `tickDown(open − a)` for a long (M1, M1b).
- **Counters.** `StopGapExits` and `TargetGapExits` count the gap exits as before; `OvernightStopGaps` and `OvernightTargetGaps` count those whose exit minute is the **first minute that holds a trade on a date later than the entry date**.

### D7 — Holidays, weekends, halts, missing and excluded sessions
- **A weekend or a holiday of the calendar** is not a session, so it neither counts toward N nor is expected to have bars.
- **A halt** (a stock suspended for part of a session or for days) shows as absent or empty minutes; E0 and X2 to X5 apply; the first minute after it is compared with the stop at its open, so a reopening gap fills at the open. A halt of whole sessions shows as sessions with no minute (next point).
- **An unseen session** is a calendar session between the entry date and the exit date on which the walker read no minute that holds a trade (a session the replay excluded as special, short or missing, or a halt). The stop and the target were not tested in it; the first minute after it is. It is **counted, not excluded**: `SessionsUnseen` on the trade and its sum in the counters (open question 5). `SessionsUnseen = max(0, SessionsHeld − sessions seen)`, where a session is seen when the walker read at least one minute that holds a trade on its date.
- **A corporate action inside a hold.** The replay adjusts the whole fed series to the latest basis (`ReplayBars`), so a split or bonus that is in the applied actions makes the series continuous and the walk sees no jump; R does not change, because stop, target and entry are in the same adjusted prices. One that is not in the table of applied actions leaves a jump: above the threshold (20 %) it is a flagged day (D13); below it, it is a gap like any other and is filled as one (the owner's data has 74 overnight moves above 10 %, ISS-20).
- **Dividends are not adjusted and not received** (doc 05 §4.2): a long held over an ex-date shows the price drop without the dividend. Listed in `FillLimits` (D15).

### D8 — R, MAE, MFE and time in the trade, in a hold
- `risk`, `RExact`, `RFilled` and `SlippageR` are those of ADR 0038 (the unit of every R is the exact ticket). A gap through the stop gives `RExact < −1` (M1: −1.4); `Target` still gives exactly `k`; `MaxHold` and `EndOfData` give any value.
- **MAE and MFE** are cumulative over every minute held, from the exact entry, in units of `risk`, never below 0, with the closing rules of ADR 0038 D5b per exit: after a `Stop` the exit's own distance counts as adverse; after a `Target` MFE is `k`; a minute that opens beyond the level it exits at contributes no range; after `MaxHold` and `EndOfData` the last minute counts in full. `−MAE ≤ RExact ≤ MFE` holds.
- **Time in the trade** is `SessionsHeld` (D3): 1 for an intraday trade and for a swing trade that leaves on its entry date, 2 when it leaves the next session, and so on. It is a count of calendar sessions, not of days or of minutes; the minutes are `BarsExamined` in the counters. The exit date is the date of `ExitEpochSeconds`.

### D9 — `SimTrade` schema 3, `sim-3`, the golden files and compatibility
- `SimTrade.CurrentSchemaVersion` is **3**. ### D11 — The position gate: one open position per instrument and side
- **Where.** In `Simulator.Run`, per side, over the candidates in decision order, after the scanner and between two walks. It is not in the scanner (which sees no position) and not in the walker (which sees one candidate), so the candidates do not depend on any exit and the same candidates can be walked under any exit policy (P4); each policy gets its own gate. The row says "applied after walking": the gate needs the end of the previous accepted position, so the walks are sequential and a blocked candidate is simply not walked; the result is the same as walking all and discarding. The gate is a small internal type, `PositionGate`, so that it can be tested with hand-made candidates.
- **Rule.** Let `E` be the *hold end* of the last *accepted* trade of the side (a trade, `EndOfData` included; a no-trade is not accepted). `E` is `ExitEpochSeconds`, **except** for a `MaxHoldAtLastClose` trade, whose `E` is the **end of its due session** (`ToMinute(T)`, 15:30 for a regular session): the position is treated as open until the session in which it was due ends, even though the last print was earlier. The walker reports it in `WalkOutcome.HoldEndEpochSeconds`. A candidate with decision instant `D` is **blocked** when `D ≤ E`, or when the last accepted trade is `EndOfData`. A blocked candidate becomes a `CandidateOutcome` whose walk is `NoTrade = PositionOpen`; the gate comes before the walk, so a candidate that would have been `LimitNotReached` is `PositionOpen` all the same (no order is placed while a position is open).
- **Why `D ≤ E`.** A decision is taken at the end of a minute; the exit minute starts at `E`. When `D ≤ E` the position had not left at the decision instant; at `D = E` the first minute the new order could fill in is the very minute in which the old position leaves, so the two cannot be told apart and the order is not placed. When `D = E + 60` the position has left by the end of its exit minute. G1 pins both sides.
- **Why `EndOfData` blocks everything, and why `MaxHoldAtLastClose` ends at the session end (review finding M2).** The position was still open when the data ended, so it was open at every later decision of the list. For the gate to be the same in a short run and a longer one (D14 2(d)) the longer run must block everything the shorter one did. Take a position whose due minute is missing and whose last held minute is 15:10 on the last date of a short list: the short run ends it as `EndOfData` and blocks all later candidates, a decision at 15:15 or 15:30 included; the longer run ends it as `MaxHoldAtLastClose`, and with `E = 15:10` a decision at 15:15 or 15:30 would be walked, so a candidate that is `PositionOpen` in one run would be a trade in the other. With `E` = the end of the due session the two agree. The cost is that after such a trade (about 0.5 % of due minutes, UNVERIFIED) the instrument is closed to new orders until the end of the due session. The rule applies **to swing walks only**: an intraday `EndOfData` (the data of a day ended before the square-off) is a flat position on that date, and the next day's candidate is not blocked.
- **Intraday is not gated** (every intraday trade exits on its entry date and the scanner makes one candidate per date), and the test pins that `PositionOpen` is 0 on every intraday run.
- **Sides** are independent; swing is BUY only, so in practice there is one gate per instrument.
- **Segments.** A segment restart (a flagged day) starts a new `Simulator.Run`, so the gate starts empty; the position of the old segment was excluded (D13).
- **Named limit 1 — the gate models the signal's position, not the account's (review finding M4, a decision of the coordinator that the owner may reverse).** P3-03c applies the quantity and cost skips in the replayer after `Simulator.Run`. A swing trade that P3-03c skips (quantity below 1: a share at 3,000 with 10,000 of capital; or `cost_R` above `max_cost_R`) **still counts as the strategy's position for the gate**, and the candidates of the next 1 to 5 sessions are `PositionOpen`: a position never taken is treated as held. Sizing and costs are accounting applied to the trades the signals made; the gate is about the signals. The effect is visible, not hidden: the fates show the skip and the blocked candidates. The test that pins it (row G6) belongs to the slice that merges second, P3-03c, which carries the same wording.
- **Named limit 3 — a candidate suppressed by a flagged day still occupies the gate of its segment (found in the review of the build; the coordinator's decision, the owner may reverse it).** On a flagged day the new segment's scanner makes a candidate, the walk enters it and the gate accepts it; the replay then drops the outcome as `EntriesSuppressed` (D13), but the gate has seen it, so the next N − 1 sessions' candidates are `PositionOpen` though no position was taken. Pinned by a replay test (Monday flat at 100, Tuesday opens at 60 and breaks out at 11:15, Wednesday breaks out at 11:15, N = 3: suppressed 1, position open 1, trades 0, open at the end 0; without Tuesday's breakout: position open 0, open at the end 1). The gate is unchanged.
- **Named limit 2 — a blocked signal spends the day.** The scanner makes at most one candidate per date and side and does not know the gate, so a candidate at 09:45 that the gate blocks (an open position that leaves at 10:00) still spends the date: a valid signal at 11:45 is a `LaterTrigger` of the scanner and is never a candidate. This follows from keeping the scanner exit-independent (the seam) and is accepted; it makes a swing strategy slightly less active than one with an idealised gate.

### D13 — The replay: what a flagged day, a window edge and a position mean together
- **A flagged day inside a hold.** The replay splits the fed list at a flagged day and calls `Simulator.Run` per segment; a position open at the end of a segment is, for the walker, an `EndOfData`. For a swing strategy, **an `EndOfData` trade of any segment that a flagged day follows is not an open-at-end trade: it is excluded and counted as `ExcludedAcrossDiscontinuity`** (the rule that Approved P3-04 hands to this slice, *Out*, and its *Done when*). It appears in neither `Trades` nor `OpenAtEnd`; the count is in `RunFates`, in the totals and, per flagged day, in `DiscontinuityNote.ExcludedPositions`. The `EndOfData` trade of the last segment stays `OpenAtEnd`. The rule is by strategy style (`Simulator.HoldsOvernight`), not by the shape of the trade, so an intraday `EndOfData` is unchanged. The exclusion is fail-closed and it can hide a real crash (SIEMENS 2025-04-07 opens at 0.656 of the previous close and VEDL 2026-04-30 at 0.413, ISS-20; whether each is a missing adjustment or a real gap was not checked), which is why the count and the date are in the record and why it is listed among the decisions (already decided by Approved P3-04).
- **The window.** A swing trade belongs to the window by the date of its entry (P3-03e: of its decision), exactly as an intraday trade: a trade before `From` is `TradesBeforeWindow` whatever its exit; a trade in `[From, To]` may exit after `To` only if the read went past `To`, and it does not. **A trade that is still open when the read ends at `To` is `EndOfData` and goes to `OpenAtEnd`.** The consequence is that the last sessions of a window cannot show trades that need longer than the data: the trades that are dropped from the statistics are the ones that had not hit stop or target by `To`, which is a mild selection (at most N − 1 sessions of entries; open question 3).
- **Fates** (order, extended): dropped by a flagged day (`EntriesSuppressed`); a trade before `From` (`TradesBeforeWindow`); a swing trade excluded across a flagged boundary (`ExcludedAcrossDiscontinuity`); an `EndOfData` trade (`OpenAtEnd`); another trade (`Trades`); a walker no-trade by reason (`NoFillBar`, `AfterSquareOff`, `EntryThroughStop`, `LimitNotReached`, `PositionOpen`). The identity "sum of the fates = the scanner's candidates over both sides and all segments" holds. The replay's `switch` over `NoTradeReason` covers every member (a reflection test enumerates the enum, so a reason added later cannot be silently dropped).
- **Run record schema 3** (`SimulationRun.CurrentSchemaVersion`), run key `xd-run 3`. The key already carries the engine version and the calendar's exceptions, so a run that counted a holiday differently has a different key.

### D14 — The property "window independence", stated for multi-day trades
The window-independence of Approved P3-04 (a trade does not depend on `From`, and `To` only adds or removes the sessions after it) is kept in this form, for any strategy and any two windows over the same stored bars:
1. **`From`.** For `From1 < From2`, the trades, fates and counters of the second are those of the first with the trades whose decision date is before `From2` moved into `TradesBeforeWindow`; every other trade is byte-identical, and so is every `PositionOpen` (the replay starts at the first stored session and the gate state at `From` is the same).
2. **`To`.** For `To1 < To2`: (a) every trade of `Trades` in the first run is in `Trades` of the second, byte-identical; (b) every trade of `OpenAtEnd` in the first run is in the second as a trade, or as `OpenAtEnd`, or as `ExcludedAcrossDiscontinuity` (a flagged day may lie between `To1` and `To2`), with the same entry-side fields (everything up to and including `TargetPrice`, and `EntryFilledPrice`, `EntryFill` and `TickFallback`) and an exit not before the last minute that holds a trade in the first run's data (equal to it when only the missing due minute decided, M7a against M7b); (c) a trade of the second run dated up to `To1` is in the first run as a trade or `OpenAtEnd`; (d) a candidate that is `PositionOpen` in the first run is `PositionOpen` in the second (this is why `EndOfData` blocks all later candidates and why the hold end of a `MaxHoldAtLastClose` trade is the end of its due session, D11). The counters of the second run differ from the first by the sessions after `To1` **and** by the trades of 2(b): the walker counters of a trade that was `EndOfData` in the first run change in the second (its exit counter, `BarsExamined` and the `SessionsHeld` sum), and a test compares counters only through the trades of `Trades`.
This is a statement about prefixes: nothing that happens after `To` decides a trade closed before it, and a trade that needs the future is reported as open, not guessed.

## Acceptance criteria
Numbered, each testable. Rows are those of *Definitions* and *Hand-calculated cases*; "the H rows" are ADR 0038's; "fixture R" is the 1h swing fixture defined before the rows J.

**The swing walk**
1. **Carry and the overnight gap.** Given M1, M1b and M2, then every exit price, reason, R, MAE, MFE, `SessionsHeld`, `BarsExamined` and counter equals the table; the stop is the same level on day 2 and day 3; a low of 99.10 above the stop does not exit; `OvernightStopGaps` is 1 in M1 and M1b and 0 in M2.
2. **A target gives no improvement overnight.** Given Tuesday `09:15 103.20/103.50/103.00/103.40` with setup A and N = 5, then the exit is the target 102.00 (not 103.20), `RExact` +2.0000, `RFilled` +1.9000, MFE 2.0000, MAE 0.2000, `TargetGapExits` 1 and `OvernightTargetGaps` 1 (the numbers of H5 on the second day).
3. **Overnight shift invariance.** Given the shifted H rows, then each equals its original in prices, reason, `RExact`, `RFilled`, `SlippageR`, MAE and MFE, with `SessionsHeld` 2, `SessionsUnseen` 0 and `OvernightStopGaps` and `OvernightTargetGaps` 0. Of the counters of ADR 0038 these are compared as they are: `EntriesAtOpen`, `EntriesAtLimit`, `EntriesAfterMissingMinutes`, `StopGapExits`, `TargetGapExits`, `AmbiguousMinutes`, `LimitNotReached`, `RiskUnderFourTicks`, `ZeroVolumeMinutes` (0 in these rows) and the exit counts. `BarsExamined` cannot be equal (the trade spans two days): the test compares it with the number of minutes that hold a trade from the fill minute to 15:29 on day 1 plus the number from 09:15 to the exit minute on day 2, counted from the bars.
4. **`MaxHold`.** Given M3, M3b, M3c, M4a, M4b, M6, M7a and M7b, then the results equal the table; the exit instant is the start of the due minute; the rows X2 to X5 take precedence in the due minute (M6); `MaxHoldAtLastClose` is 1 in M7a only.
5. **The due session by the calendar.** Given K1 to K7, then the exit date and due minute equal the table, with the real `TradingCalendar` (a holiday, a confirmed special session) and with none (K4 differs from K3).
6. **End of the data.** Given M5 and M7b, then `ExitReason` is `EndOfData`, the trade is in `OpenAtEnd`, and a later candidate in the same list is `PositionOpen`; given a list that ends at 15:29 with a minute that holds a trade (M3 shortened), then the exit is `MaxHold`.
7. **Unseen sessions.** Given M4b, then `SessionsHeld` is 3, `SessionsUnseen` is 1 and the counter `SessionsUnseen` is 1; given a Tuesday with no minutes while the calendar says it is a session (a halt, or a session the replay excluded) in an otherwise M3 trade, then the trade is kept as a trade with `SessionsUnseen` 1.
8. **Hold validation.** `SwingHold.Create` refuses 0 and 6 with `simulation.rules_invalid`; a `Hold` with a square-off other than `NoSquareOff`, and a missing `Hold` with a square-off outside 1 to 1439, are `simulation.rules_invalid`; a calendar in which no trading day is found in 30 days is `simulation.rules_invalid`.
9. **Slippage in a hold.** Given the rows above, `MaxHold` and `EndOfData` exits pay `(Ticks × tick) + BarRangeFrac × range` of the exit minute with no extra (S5), a `Stop` pays the extra ticks, a `Target` pays none; the properties of ADR 0038 criterion 18 (never improves a fill, never changes a decision, monotone in each parameter) hold over multi-day series.

**The gate and the simulator**
10. **Gate.** Given G1 to G6 (G6 by the slice that merges second), then the results equal the table, and the hold end of a `MaxHoldAtLastClose` trade is the end of its due session (G2b); a blocked candidate is not walked (its minutes add nothing to `BarsExamined` or `ZeroVolumeMinutes`); `PositionOpen` is 0 for every intraday run.
11. **Reference gate.** Over 500 seeded multi-day series, the accepted trades and the blocked candidates of `Simulator.Run` equal those of a plain ten-line loop in the test that walks every candidate on its own and applies `D ≤ E` and the `EndOfData` rule.
12. **`Simulator.Create`: what is accepted and what stays refused.** A swing strategy on 30m or 1h (BUY, `at_close`, an `or_opposite` stop read on the evaluation timeframe with `minutes` a whole number of bars, one `FIXED_RR`, `max_hold_sessions` 1 to 5) is accepted. Refused by name with the code of today, each with a located problem and no simulator: swing SELL (the compile's `model.swing_sell_unsupported` reaches the caller), an overlay (`time_stop`, `event_exit`), a filter, a market-context or expiry read, `stop_above_high`, `limit_pullback`, `fixed_pct` and `atr_mult` stops, a buffer, 1W and 1M and 1D and the level stops other than `or_opposite` (until P3-03e). A side that cannot be built always gives a problem: the path of today in which a side with a null square-off yields no simulator and no problem (harmless while the swing style is refused, a defect once it is accepted) is closed. The tests that pin today's refusal of the swing style and of 1D (`SimulatorCreateTests`, `CreateTests`, `StrategyPreparationTests`) are changed on purpose and listed in the review.
13. **End to end, 1h (fixture R).** The `Simulator` over fixture R gives one candidate on Monday at 11:15 (trigger close 101.50, stop 98.00) and its trade equals the hand-made candidate walked by `TradeWalker` (same candidate, same trade, same counters; the trace is in the golden file); with a Tuesday that opens `09:15 97.40/97.60/97.30/97.50` the trade exits `Stop` at the open 97.40: `RExact` −1.1714 (4.10 / 3.50), `RFilled` −1.2429 (exit 97.40 → 97.20, slip `0.15 + 0.015 = 0.165`: 97.235 down; entry 101.50 → 101.55; 4.35 / 3.50), `SlippageR` −0.0714, MAE 1.1714, `StopGapExits` 1, `OvernightStopGaps` 1, held 2.

**The record, counters and compatibility**
14. **Schema 3 and the schema-2 view.** `SimTrade.CurrentSchemaVersion` is 3 and the positional properties are exactly the 30 of schema 2 in order followed by `SessionsHeld` and `SessionsUnseen`; `ExitReason` is `Stop, Target, SquareOff, EndOfData, MaxHold`; `NoTradeReason` is `NoFillBar, AfterSquareOff, EntryThroughStop, LimitNotReached, PositionOpen`; `SimulationEngine.Version` is `sim-3`. The schema-2 view (the formatter of D9, which prints `schema=2` itself) of the sim-3 trades and counters equals the frozen files line by line (or batch by batch): for F1 to F17b and W1 to W14 `Golden/walker.sim-2.frozen.txt`, for H1 to H25 `Golden/hrows.sim-2.frozen.txt`, for the 500 seeded intraday days `Golden/seeded-intraday.sim-2.sha256.txt`; all three were made with the sim-2 code before the first change. The two new fields are 1 and 0 on every one of these trades.
15. **Counters.** The identities of D10 hold on every fixture and every seeded series; the new counters are 0 on every intraday run except `SessionsHeld` (equal to `Trades`).
16. **Golden files.** The five golden files are regenerated with the first line `engine sim-3`; the diff against the sim-2 files is only what D9 step 2 lists (the header line, the schema and engine numbers, the run-key hash, the two fields, the new counters); the `RunAssemblyTests` table of engine versions has the `sim-3` row.
17. **Intraday trades are flat at square-off.** Over 500 seeded intraday days (the three square-off times), every trade's exit date equals its entry date and no trade has `ExitReason.MaxHold`.

**The replay**
18. **Window.** Given J1 to J4, the results equal the table; the properties of D14 hold over 100 seeded multi-day instruments for pairs of windows (1: `From`; 2: `To`, including a flagged day between `To1` and `To2` and a trade that is `OpenAtEnd` in the first run), with two negative controls, each of which must fail the property on at least 10 of the 100 seeds (to be checked when the generator is written, UNVERIFIED): (i) a replay whose scanner starts at `From` (the design P3-04 rejected), run with a history-dependent test strategy, `SWING_1H_EMA_TEST` (1h, trigger `close > ema(10)`, the stop and hold of fixture R), because a strategy that reads only the opening range, like fixture R, gives the same candidates from any start; (ii) a replay whose gate starts empty at `From`, which differs whenever a position from before `From` blocks a candidate after it.
19. **A flagged boundary.** Given Q1, then `ExcludedAcrossDiscontinuity` is 1 (also in the totals and in `ExcludedPositions` of the note), `OpenAtEnd` and `Trades` are 0 for that trade, and the identity of the fates holds; given Q2, then the trade is a trade with the numbers of the table; an intraday `EndOfData` in a segment before a flagged day is still `OpenAtEnd`.
20. **Fates and the record.** Every candidate outcome has exactly one fate (D13); the replay handles every member of `NoTradeReason` (a reflection test enumerates the enum); `SimulationRun.CurrentSchemaVersion` is 3 and the run key starts `xd-run 3`; a run whose calendar has a holiday inside a hold has a different key from the same bars without it.

**Properties** (seeded generator in the test project: see *Test plan*)
21. **A closed trade does not depend on the future, and the controls fail.** (a) For a random cut `k` over 500 seeded multi-day series, every outcome *settled at* an index at most `k` is identical, as rendered by the test formatter, when the minutes after `k` are removed and when they are replaced by extreme values (epochs unchanged); *settled at* is the exit minute, the fill minute of a no-trade, and, for a `MaxHold` exit at the last close (the due minute missing), the first minute of the following date that revealed it. (b) **Negative controls**, test-only wrappers around the real walker: *peek-next-session*, where each session's last minute is given the lower of its own low and the next session's first open (and the higher of the highs), so that a stop could "see" tomorrow's gap, must make the property report a difference on at least 10 seeds while the plain walker reports none on the same inputs; *calendar-from-the-future*, where the due session is computed from the number of dates that occur in the list (so the hold depends on what follows), must fail on at least 10 seeds. (c) The same property through `Simulator.Run` including the gate: the gate decision of a candidate is the same when everything after its decision instant is removed; the generator plants decisions at 15:15 and 15:30 after the last held minute of a due session whose due minute is missing (G2b), so that the guarantee of D11 is exercised and not assumed.
22. **`MaxHold` bound.** Over the same series (calendar given in 70 %, absent in 30 %): `SessionsHeld ≤ N` for every `MaxHold`, `Stop` and `Target` exit; every `MaxHold` exit is at the due session or at the last date with data before it; no trade exits after its due minute; the test finds the due session with its own scan of the calendar.
23. **Gate.** Accepted trades of one side never overlap (the next decision is after the start of the previous exit minute); every blocked candidate has `D ≤ E` (the hold end of D11) or follows an `EndOfData`; and a candidate blocked in a short run is blocked in the longer run of D14 2(d) (the planted cases of 21(c)).
24. **Non-vacuous and deterministic.** Each of `ExitsMaxHold`, `MaxHoldAtLastClose`, `OvernightStopGaps`, `OvernightTargetGaps`, `StopGapExits`, `TargetGapExits`, `ExitsEndOfData`, `PositionOpen`, `AmbiguousMinutes`, `ZeroVolumeMinutes` and the number of trades with `SessionsUnseen` above 0 is at least 10 over the 500 series, all N from 1 to 5 occur, with and without a calendar, and the ticks 0.01, 0.05 and 0.10; a generator that stops producing a case fails. Two walks of the same input, one on a copy, render identical text, also under a culture with comma decimals.

**Structure and documents**
25. **The library stays pure.** The Architecture tests stay green with no relaxed rule: `Walking` depends on Domain (`TradingCalendar` is a Domain type) and the root namespace only, with no I/O, clock, `Random`, task or ambient state; the calendar is passed in, never fetched; `XDrishti.Simulation` keeps its project references; no project, package, migration or endpoint is added; `EntryCandidate` gains no member (a test reads them). The per-minute decision exists once (a test finds no second copy of the X2 to X5 block: the swing loop and the intraday loop call the same routine). A walk allocates the same number of bytes for a hold of 1 session and of 5 (the `AllocationProbe` pattern; `DateOnly` and `TradingSession` are structs).
26. **Documents.** A new ADR (the next free number at merge) that supersedes decision 8 of ADR 0038, extends its decisions 2, 9 and 11, holds the rows X7 to X9 and S5, D1 to D14, the schema, the counters and the gate, and is linked both ways with it; Amendment 4 to ADR 0037 (the replay: R6 completed, R7 and R10 extended, the lifted refusals); doc 08 §5 (the swing column, the counting of sessions, the exit at the close, "overnight gap" pointing to the ADR), doc 07 §1 (swing timeframes and the gate) and doc 19 §1; the roadmap row of P3-03d (Built, with the corrections of *Where the roadmap row and the code disagree*) and its issues log: ISS-18 (a) and (e) reworded and `dividends_not_modelled` added to it, one new ISS entry (the next free number) for the swing limits (a gap through a target not improved, unseen sessions, a gap under the flag threshold, a flagged boundary excludes the position, the strict window edge); `scripts/build-docs.sh` run.

27. **Reflection on the sums.** `WalkCounters.Add` (27 properties) and `RunFates.Add` add every property of the record (a test enumerates them by reflection and gives each a distinct value), so that a field appended later cannot be left out.
28. **Named limits are pinned.** A blocked signal spends the day (a scanner fixture: a candidate at 09:45 blocked by a position that leaves at 10:00, a valid signal at 11:45 is a `LaterTrigger`, not a candidate); the sizing skip still blocks (G6, by the slice that merges second).

## Non-functional
- **Performance:** the swing walk is O(minutes of the position), at most N × 375 minutes (1,875 for N = 5); no allocation per minute or per date. At the 4.6 to 4.9 million minutes per second of the decimal walker (ADR 0038) a 5-session walk is about 0.4 ms (an extrapolation, UNVERIFIED until measured). The gate skips the walks of blocked candidates, which bounds the cost of a swing strategy to one open position per instrument and side. The explicit benchmark prints trades and minutes per second for holds of 1 and 5 sessions; no asserted number. The seeded multi-day properties add to the time of `scripts/check.sh` (not measured): the series count of the slippage ladder is the first thing to cut (*Size*).
- **Determinism:** no clock, `Random` or culture dependence; the calendar is an input; two walks give identical text. The due session is found by the calendar only, never by what the list contains (the control of criterion 21).
- **Security / privacy:** tests use synthetic series only; nothing is read from `data/`; no secret is involved.
- **Observability:** the new counters and flags, `SessionsHeld`, `SessionsUnseen`, `MaxHold`, `ExcludedAcrossDiscontinuity` with the flagged date, `PositionOpen`; no logging in the pure library; the log lines of the handler (P3-04a) are unchanged and carry no prices.

Seed = the series index. 3 to 8 consecutive weekday sessions from a calendar with one planted holiday and, in a third of the series, a planted special session; each session of 375 minutes from a random walk with a per-minute move up to 0.15 %; price log-uniform 20 to 5,000; tick from {0.01, 0.05, 0.10}; overnight gaps on a quarter of the nights, 0.2 % to 8 % up or down (never beyond the flag threshold in the walker series; the replay series add flagged ones); 0 to 15 % of the minutes removed in runs of 1 to 6; 5 % flat minutes; 1 % Volume 0; N from 1 to 5; the calendar given in 70 % of the series and `null` in 30 %; 1 to 3 candidates per series at decision instants between 10:15 and 14:15, a few of them inside the hold of the first, and (planted) decisions at 15:15 and 15:30 on the due session after its last held minute, which `last_entry` normally forbids and which the generator therefore adds by building the candidate by hand (the gate takes candidates, not strategies). **Planted cases** (starting rates, to be tuned until each counter of criterion 24 reaches at least 30 over the 500 series, asserted at 10): 10 % of the series end inside the hold (the list ends); 8 % lose the due minute (absent or Volume 0); 6 % have a whole session removed inside the hold; 12 % open a night 0.5 to 3 stop distances beyond the stop; 8 % open a night beyond the target; 5 % hold a minute that reaches both stop and target on a later day; 8 % of the series have a due minute missing with a later session present, and a third of those a hand-built candidate at 15:15 or 15:30 after the last held minute (G2b). The rates are starting values: that every counter reaches 10 at these rates is UNVERIFIED (ADR 0038's generator was checked with a throwaway replica; this one was not).

**Edge cases to cover:** N = 1 (entry session is the due session) and N = 5; an entry in the last minute of the day (the fill minute is the due minute of N = 1); a Friday entry (a weekend in the hold); a hold over a holiday with and without the calendar; a special session as the due session (present and absent from the list); a due minute at Volume 0, absent, or the last minute of a short session; the list ending at 15:28, at 15:29 and at 15:30 of a bar outside the window; a night with no gap; a night that opens exactly at the stop (equality: an open equal to the stop exits at the open, `StopGapExit` false) and exactly at the target; a stop touched in the first minute of the day and a target touched in the first minute (X4, X5 with the overnight minute); an overnight open beyond the target with the low through the stop (the exception of X4); a candidate decided at the exit minute (`D = E`) and one after it; two candidates one night apart; a swing strategy listing both sides (refused by the compile); a flagged day that is also the first day after a holiday; a position open at the last day of the data (the last segment); zero slippage (`SlippageConfig.None` reproduces the exact layer on every row).

## Open questions
1. **Is the entry session session 1 of the hold?** Recommendation: **yes, session 1 is the entry session, so `max_hold_sessions: 3` leaves at the close of the third session counting the entry day (Monday in, Wednesday's close out), and `max_hold_sessions: 1` leaves at the close of the entry day.** Doc 08 §5 says only "close of session ≤ 5"; both readings are consistent with 1 to 5. With the entry session counted, N is the number of sessions the position can be seen in, a one-session swing is allowed (it is a close-to-close exit and not a 15:15 square-off), and a daily strategy filled at the next open is held at most N sessions from its fill. The alternative (N further sessions after the entry session) adds a night to every value and makes N = 1 the shortest overnight hold; it is a one-line change and a new `Version`.
2. **A gap through a target overnight: fill at the target or at the open?** Recommendation: **keep the rule of ADR 0038 (the target, never a better price).** It keeps `R` of a `Target` exit equal to `k`, the pessimistic side of ISS-18 (f), and it is measured: `OvernightTargetGaps` says how often it happens, so the owner can ask for the open instead after seeing the count on real data. A real limit order that is gapped over fills at the open, so the choice understates gap-up winners (not the losers: the stop already fills at the open). Changing it later is a rule change, a new `Version` and a regenerated golden file.
3. **A position still open at `To`: report it as open (strict), or read past `To` so that it can finish?** Recommendation: **strict in this slice** (`OpenAtEnd`, never in the statistics; the read ends at `To`, the window property of D14 stays a statement about prefixes, and nothing in P3-04a changes). The cost is that trades entered in the last N − 1 sessions of a window and still open at `To` are dropped, which keeps the ones that resolved quickly (mild: about 4 sessions of 500 for N = 5, UNVERIFIED on real data). Reading N − 1 sessions past `To` would need a changed read in P3-04a, a rule for the data after `To` in the bars hash and the key, and a restated window property; do it when the owner asks for the last weeks of the data to count.
4. **Split the daily evaluation (part B of the first draft) into P3-03e, merged after P3-03d and P3-03c? (decided by the coordinator, recommended; the owner confirms.)** Recommendation: **yes.** Only P3-06 needs it, it changes rules (so it needs its own engine version and a third golden regeneration) and it slides with P3-06 if that slice moves to P4. This slice is then 3.5 agent-days (3.2 priced); P3-03e is about 1.5; together 5.0 against the earlier 4 (3 + 1). Where the extra 1.0 comes from: this slice 3.0 to 3.5 (+0.5: the frozen renderings, the test-file changes and the gate instant); P3-03e 1.0 to 1.5 (+0.5: the third golden regeneration and ADR pass 0.3, the pending-order outcome 0.15, tests 0.05).
5. **Sessions the walker never sees, and the choices around them: keep them as drafted?** Recommendation: **yes, as drafted, with the counts in the record:** (a) a trade through a session that was halted or that the replay left out (special, short, missing) is counted (`SessionsUnseen`) and kept, because the stop and target were not tested there but the next minute is compared with the stop at its open, so a missed touch is possible and a gap is not; on the owner's files that is 3 dates (a Saturday session and two Muhurat sessions) and no missing session, a handful of trades (UNVERIFIED); (b) a special or Muhurat session counts as a session of the hold, so a position whose due session is one leaves one session short at the previous close; (c) a due minute that is missing leaves at the last known close (earlier) and not at the next open (holding through a session nobody can see); (d) a position whose segment ends the day before a flagged day without a due minute is excluded although a longer list would have exited it (D4 consequence b). Excluding (a) or counting only regular sessions in (b) are possible and cost a second notion of session or lost trades.

**Decisions taken on research, not asked (the owner may revert any):**
- **A position open across a flagged day is excluded and counted** (`ExcludedAcrossDiscontinuity`, with the date in `DiscontinuityNote.ExcludedPositions`): already decided by Approved P3-04; listed again because the cost is the owner's to know (a real crash beyond 20 % is left out of the results: SIEMENS 2025-04-07, TMPV 2025-10-14, VEDL 2026-04-30 of ISS-20, none checked for a missing adjustment).

- **An `EndOfData` swing trade blocks every later candidate in its list, and a `MaxHoldAtLastClose` trade holds its instrument until the end of its due session** (D11), so that a short run and a longer run agree.
- **The gate models the signals' positions; a sizing or cost skip of P3-03c still blocks** (D11 named limit 1, the coordinator's decision, reversible), and **a blocked signal spends the day** (named limit 2).
- **Dividends are not modelled** (D15, a new limit).

## Risks
- **Overnight gaps dominate the R of a swing strategy.** A 3.4 % stop and a 19.9 % unflagged gap is −5.8 R (Q2), a 1 % stop and a 19 % gap about −19 R, and the threshold of the replay (20 %) leaves them in; the mean R of a swing run can be set by a handful of trades. Mitigation: `OvernightStopGaps`, `StopGapExits` and the gap exits in the record. The advice to read the median and the count of gaps beside the mean is for the owner in the spec and the chat; it stays out of every exported text (P3-04b1 forbids the words `mean` and `average`), like the remark that a gap through a target understates gap-up winners (the roadmap's rule: no number from a real run is quoted outside its run folder). The flag threshold is a `ReplayOptions` value the owner can lower.
- **The 09:15 fills.** Gap exits fill in the minute whose open can be the auction price and whose range the importer may have widened (ISS-18 (a), ISS-21): slippage there is larger, and the first-minute open may differ from the official open (not measured). Mitigation: the corrected limit text and the measurement ISS-18 already asks for.
- **A real crash is excluded, or hidden.** `ExcludedAcrossDiscontinuity` leaves out positions across a day that moved more than 20 % (decisions list); the unseen-session trades are kept (question 5).
- **A position never taken still blocks.** Named limit 1 of D11: after P3-03c a swing trade skipped for quantity or cost blocks the next 1 to 5 sessions of candidates; with a small simulated capital and a high-priced share this can silence a strategy. The fates show it (`SkippedQuantity`, `PositionOpen`); the owner may reverse the decision.
- **Dividends are not modelled.** A long held over an ex-date shows the drop without the dividend; for holds of at most five sessions the share of trades with an ex-date inside is small but not measured (UNVERIFIED), and the sign of the error is against the long.
- **The size.** About 3.5 agent-days for a 2-day row, over the 3-day cap; the test, golden-file and property work is the part most likely to overrun (its 1.0 is light). Cut list in *Size*.
- **Two more slices change `SimTrade` and the record.** P3-03c follows this slice and rebases on schema 3; P3-03e after it; each regenerates the five golden files, so three regenerations in all; P3-04b1, b2 and c need a column and spelling pass and their reflection tests go red on purpose.
- **A count off by one session.** Question 1 changes every hold by one session; the K rows pin the reading, and the property of criterion 22 uses its own scan of the calendar so that a shared mistake is less likely (not impossible: the scan is written from the same sentence).
- **The 15:29 minute.** A missing or empty due minute exits at an earlier close; the rate is not measured. Mitigation: `MaxHoldAtLastClose`.
- **Tests and files that pin today's types change on purpose** (the reviewer pass lists each, so a refusal or a pin is never removed by accident): `SimulatorCreateTests` (the swing style), `CreateTests` and `StrategyPreparationTests`, `SimTradeSchemaTests` (the field list and the enums), `TradeWalkerRuleTests.cs` line 269 (pins `"sim-2"`), `RunAssemblyTests.cs` (the version table), `XDrishti.Domain.Tests`: `RunAssemblerTests` (the `"sim-2"` fixtures), `RunRecordSchemaTests`, `ReplayValuesTests`; `XDrishti.Architecture.Tests`: `DomainResearchSurfaceTests` (the property-name snapshots) and the nine-code `FillLimits` test; `Support/RunText.cs`; and **`XDrishti.Application.Tests/Research/FakeSimulationEngine.cs`, which constructs `SimTrade` with the 30 fields and will not compile until it passes the two new ones**.
- **Sample size.** About 2.1 years of data (ISS-19) make swing strategies produce few trades; hand-checking a trade of each edge category (P3-09) may need the intraday strategies to supply the other categories.
