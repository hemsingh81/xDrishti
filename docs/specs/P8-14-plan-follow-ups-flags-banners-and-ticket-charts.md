## Goal
The Plan review screen stops guessing from sentences and shows what the plan recorded: the plan's smart-rule and data states as stored fields (gate, throttle, correlation, open book, cost basis, risk multiplier) with the full banner text; each account's checklist standing on every ticket; a clear "no plan was published, and why" when the nightly pipeline failed closed; and, per ticket, a chart of the instrument's recent daily bars (with entry, stop and target lines when the ticket carries numbers).

## Scope
**In:**
(a) `trading.plans.basis_flags_json` (migration `AddPlanBasisFlags`), written by `SelectionPlanBuilder` from the `SmartPlan`; `PlanView.flags`; the frontend stops matching strings when the field is present.
(b) Per-account checklist standings on the ticket card (a standing line per account, passed checks listed on request).
(c) The gate / smart-rule banner (full texts) and the "plan not published (reason)" banner from `GET /api/plan-runs`.
(d) `GET /api/plans/{id}/items/{itemId}/chart` and the ticket chart on the card, lazy, on `PriceChart`.
**Out:** producing or storing numeric levels (P7-19) and price-crossing alerts and post-trade prompts (P7-20; its ticket-card badge and Mute are its own; the bell items are P8-15, built, ADR 0101), accept/snooze of suggestions (owner write), notifications (P8-15), changing how tickets are built, sized or approved, live prices on the chart (P12), any control for the autonomy or builder switches (P8-00 Q2), account switcher beyond the existing per-account display.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given the builder publishes a plan, then `basis_flags_json` holds `{schemaVersion:1, gate, gateMissing[], throttle, correlation, correlationUnavailablePairs, openBook, riskMultiplier, ruleRemovedHalf, costBasis, banner[]}` with values from the `SmartPlan` and the `PlanContext` (gate `normal|reduced|no_trade|unavailable|off`; throttle `clear|cut|unavailable|off`; correlation `applied|unavailable|off`; open book `known|assumed_empty|unavailable`; cost basis `gross|costed_unconfirmed|net_confirmed|mixed|unknown`), and `GET /api/plans` and `/api/plans/{id}` return it as `flags`. A plan stored before this change has `flags = null`. *`PlanBasisFlagsTests`, `PlanFlagsApiTests`.*
2. Given each combination in the hand table (gate unavailable and throttle unavailable; gate reduced with risk multiplier 0.75; open book assumed empty; throttle cut; correlation partial with 3 pairs; one rule removed half), then the stored flags equal the table and `banner[]` holds the full text of every flag line (not clamped at 500 characters). *`PlanBasisFlagsTests`.*
3. Given a plan with `flags`, then the chips and the banner are drawn from the fields (text and icon, never colour alone: "Market gate: unavailable (VIX, breadth not known)", "Throttle: cut", "Open book: assumed empty by owner setting", "Risk multiplier 0.75", "Cost basis: costed, sheet not confirmed as yours"); the screen does not call `basisFlags(plan)` for that plan. Given `flags = null`, then the old exact-marker reading is used and the chip row says "read from the basis text; this plan predates structured flags". *`PlanFlags.test.tsx`.*
4. Given a ticket reviewed for three accounts (one `passed` x 12, one with 1 `warned` and 1 `needsconfirmation`, one `refused`), then the card shows one standing line per account ("Main paper: 12 checks, 12 passed" / "Swing paper: 1 warned, 1 needs confirmation" / "Test paper: refused, cannot be overridden") with the account's mode and effective quantity, and "Show all checks" lists every check with its standing as text; given a reserve ticket or a rejected candidate, no standing line and no empty heading. Existing behaviour (confirm-despite codes per account, Approve disabled with the reason) is unchanged. *`TicketChecklist.test.tsx`, existing `PlansDecisions.test.tsx` green.*
5. Given the newest nightly attempt in `GET /api/plan-runs?limit=5` is `failed_closed` with no `planId`, and no later attempt published, then a banner above the plan list says "No plan was published for 28 Oct 2026: the data-quality gate refused (attempt 2 at 18:42): <reason>" with the gate id as text and "manual" when a person started it; given a later published attempt or a newer plan, no banner; given no attempt and no plan, "No plan yet". *`PlanRunBanner.test.tsx`.*
6. Given a plan whose state is `published` or `inreview` and whose flags say gate `no_trade`, then the banner says "Market gate: no-trade day, tickets moved to reserve" as the gate's own text; the countdown and the lapse sentence are unchanged. *`PlanFlags.test.tsx`.*
7. Given a primary or reserve ticket, when "Chart" is expanded, then `GET /api/plans/{id}/items/{itemId}/chart` returns the last 60 daily bars up to the plan's last closed session (adjusted), the instrument resolution and the ticket's rule texts; the chart draws the candles and, when the item's `levels` (P7-19) is non-null, one labelled price line per non-null number: a dashed entry reference "close of {session}", the stop ("Stop level" when `stopAnchor = level`, "Stop, indicative from the close" when `close_of_d`) and the target, with the caption taken from `levels.basis` ("indicative from the close of the session; not tomorrow's price"); a null stop or target draws no line (never a line at 0); when `levels` is null no line is drawn and the three rule texts are printed beside the chart with "levels are rules, not numbers, on this ticket". The chart's lazy chunk loads only on expand. *`TicketChartTests`, `TicketChart.test.tsx`.*
8. Given a symbol that maps to no instrument or to more than one active NSE instrument, then `resolution` is `not_found` or `ambiguous`, no bars are returned and the card says so; given fewer than 60 sessions of bars, the bars that exist are drawn and the count is printed (no padding). *`TicketChartTests`.*
9. Given the chart's table fallback, then the last 10 bars and the lines are listed as text with the same numbers; the chart has the attribution link (P8-11). *`TicketChart.test.tsx`.*
10. Given the plan screen sources, then no new call is a mutation, `approve`/`modify`/`reject` paths are untouched, the allow-list of attention commands is unchanged, and the no-order sentence is still on every action area and dialog. *existing tests + D6 test for the new files.*
11. Given `npm run size`, then the first-load number grows by at most 0.5 KB gzip (the flags type and chip code live in the lazy `plans` chunk); the chart is a separate lazy chunk reusing P8-11's. *size output in the PR.*

## Decisions (researched)
1. **Store the flags, do not derive them again.** The free-text `basis` is clamped at 500 characters (`MaxBasisLength`) and the screen matched two English markers inside it; any rewording or a long banner breaks that silently. A nullable JSON column beside it, written where the `SmartPlan` is in hand, is the smallest change that makes the facts typed. Alternatives rejected: a child table (a join for seven scalars); recomputing flags on read from item codes (the gate mode and the pair count are not on the items); parsing the text on the server (the same fragility moved).
2. **Old plans keep working.** `flags = null` falls back to the existing exact-marker reading, labelled; nothing is back-filled (the table is insert-only and a guess written as a fact would be worse than a label).
3. **Banner text is stored, not rebuilt.** The words the owner saw when the plan was published are the words shown later.
4. **Per-account standings.** P8-00 lists this as not built; reading `TicketChecklist` shows each account's not-passed checks already listed under the account's heading. What is missing is the one-line standing per account, the passed checks, and a clear "refused" for an account that cannot take the ticket; this slice adds those and keeps the confirm-despite logic untouched. (A contradiction with the P8-00 wording is recorded in the index findings.)
5. **Chart lines only from numbers the ticket carries.** The plan stores trigger, stop and target as text ("indicative level 2410.5 as of the close" is inside the sentence). Reading a number out of a sentence would be a regex on prose; Q17 of P8-00 put numeric levels in their own slice, now written as P7-19 (columns on `plan_items`, typed, checked per side, 30 % plausibility cap). So (d) draws the lines from `PlanItemView.levels` and otherwise prints the rule text beside a chart of context bars. The chart's "as of" session is `levels.session` when present (it is the builder's own session D), else the trading-calendar session before `ForDate`. The stop's anchor decides the label so an entry-relative stop is never presented as a market level (P7-19 decision 3). Rejected: parsing the text; deriving the stop from the strategy at read time (a second ticket builder); drawing grade levels (ADR 0090 grades use their own simulated entry and stop, P7-19 decision 9).
6. **Daily bars, last 60 sessions, as of the planning session.** A ticket is a next-session conditional: the picture the planner had is the daily context up to the close of D. Intraday bars of a session that has not happened do not exist; bars after D are never shown on a plan chart (no hindsight on a plan). 60 sessions is about three months, enough for a swing level and light on a phone.
7. **Where the chart's dependency comes from.** P8-11 decided the library (`lightweight-charts@5.2.1`, lazy). This slice adds no dependency.
8. **"Plan not published" is read from `plan_runs`, not inferred.** A failed-closed row names the gate and the reason in words; the absence of any row is shown as "No plan yet", not as a failure (the nightly service may be paused: ADR 0097).
9. Sources: repo only (the files named above).

## Risks and honest limits
- Plans built before this slice have no flags and are read from text; the label says so.
- Plans stored before P7-19 (not back-filled by design) and tickets whose stop input was not ready have `levels = null`: the chart has no price lines: it is context, not an overlay. The screen says "levels are rules, not numbers, on this ticket" instead of drawing nothing silently.
- A ticket's stop and target are conditional on tomorrow's data; an indicative level from the close is not tomorrow's price.
- The instrument match is by symbol; an ambiguous symbol shows no chart rather than a possibly wrong one.
- Layout was never seen in a browser by an agent in P7-17; P8-16 covers the plan screen at four widths.
