# P11-04 — Scale-up rules and the risk ramp: live-ready gate, steps, evidence gates, automatic hold, halts

- **Status:** Draft (written 2026-10-24; decisions below were researched under the owner's delegation; the owner's Approved is still needed before an agent builds it)
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P11 — Small live capital, slice P11-04 of [P11-00](P11-00-small-live-plan.md). Row: "25-50% of intended risk"; exit "Live within expectations => scale gradually".
- **Design refs:** docs/design/08-exits-and-risk.md §6 (sizing), §7 (daily loss -2R or -1.5 %, weekly -5R halve size, max drawdown 8 % halt) · 10 §6, §7 · 16 (`risk_profiles`, `autonomy`) · 19 §4 · 20 §A (modes; kill switch is P12) · 04 §4 (only the owner enables execution) · ADR 0083, 0087, 0089, 0091 (owner switches with a snapshot), 0095, 0096; P7-12 (pattern), P12-07 (paper to `manual_live` "stays the existing step-up setting change"), P12-05 (system halts, later).
- **Needs:** P7-05, P7-07, P7-10, P7-12 (built). **Built in two passes:** **04A** (before P11-01): tables `ramp_events`, `live_settings`, the live-ready gate, the two-pool allocation, the risk scale, `account_live_ready`, live auto-approval guard. **04B** (after P11-02): gates, automatic hold and step-down, halts, notices, the Ramp tab. **Size:** 5 agent-days (04A 2.5, 04B 2.5; **recommend the index lists them as P11-04a and P11-04b**). **Safety:** **yes** — UI writes (the ramp step, clearing a hold, two switches), a gate on the account's mode change, live allocations. Defaults: every switch OFF; no step set means no live account. The Approved status is the owner's act.

## Goal
A `manual_live` account trades only at a fraction of its profile's risk that the owner raised on evidence, that falls back by itself when the evidence turns, and that is halted at the human level (record, notice, refused approvals, invalidated tickets) when the daily or weekly loss limit is reached. The system sizes and warns; the owner places every order.

## Scope
**In (04A):** Domain `Domain/LiveTrading`: `RampStep`, `RampPolicy` (effective scale), `LiveReadiness`, `LiveSettings`; `PerAccountAllocator` gains `PoolMode` (default `Paper`, so P7 output is byte-identical) and the code `account_not_live`; `PlanWiring` calls it twice (live pool, then each paper account replicated) and multiplies a live account's `RiskPerTradePct`, `MaxTotalRiskPct` and `MaxCapitalPerTradePct` by the scale; `AllocationRows.ToNew` writes `manual_live` rows with `risk_scale` and `ramp_event_id`; `ChecklistCodes.AccountLiveReady` (non-confirmable; replaces `account_paper` for a live account); `AutoApprovalPolicy` refuses a ticket with a live allocation unless `live.auto_approve_tickets` is ON; `UpdateAccountTrading` runs `LiveReadiness` on a change to `manual_live`; migration `AddRampAndHalts` part 1 (`ramp_events`, `live_settings`, `plan_item_allocations` columns and check, `approvals` live-settings columns and guard trigger).
**In (04B):** `RampGates` (pure), `BreachRules` (pure), `HaltRules` and `LossMeasure` (pure), `EvaluateRampHandler` (a step of `live-settle`, also after each poll for halts), `SetRampStepHandler`, `ClearHoldHandler`, `ConfirmExitHandler`, `ChangeLiveSettingsHandler`, queries; table `live_halts`; endpoints; derived notices; `LiveTile` on Today; **Ramp** tab of `/live`; `xd live ramp show`.
**Out:** any order or broker write; a system halt or kill switch (P12-05); raising S3 before the owner records `exit_confirmed`; scaling counts (entries per day, open positions stay the profile's); funds or positions reads; changing the risk profile itself.

## Acceptance criteria
1. (04A) Given a `manual_live` account at S1 with profile risk 0.5 %, capital 100,000, entry 200, stop 195, then risk per trade is 0.125 % = 125.00, quantity floor(125/5) = 25, the allocation row has `mode = manual_live`, `risk_scale = 0.2500` and the ramp event id; at S2 (0.25 % = 250.00) quantity 50; the capital cap (25 % x scale) does not bind in either case (31 and 62 shares).
2. (04A) Given a ticket and one live and one paper account, then the live account gets the full pool and the paper account its own full allocation (two pools); a P7 plan with only paper accounts is byte-identical to before.
3. (04A) Given a switch of an account to `manual_live`, then it is refused with 422 `accounts.live_not_ready` and the failing items unless: Trading role complete with a fixed capital, Dhan connection `connected`, a step set (`set_step`), `live-capture` and `live-settle` enabled; soft items (the P10 phase check not MET, the charge sheet not confirmed, order-book capture off) need a typed `liveConfirmReason`, recorded in the audit text; switching back to `paper` is always allowed.
4. (04A) Given a live ticket, then the checklist's first check is `account_live_ready` (not confirmable) and fails when: no step, an open halt, the last session's capture `gap`, or the loss of the day cannot be measured; `account_paper` is unchanged for paper accounts.
5. (04A) Given a ticket with a live allocation, then auto approval is refused with `auto_live_off` unless both `autonomy.auto_approve_tickets` and `live.auto_approve_tickets` are ON in stored snapshots; an auto approval then carries both snapshot ids and hashes; the database refuses an auto approval of such a ticket without the live snapshot.
6. (04A) The database refuses: an allocation `manual_live` without `risk_scale` and `ramp_event_id`, with `risk_scale` above 1 or above the step's factor, a ramp event of kind `set_step`, `clear_hold` or `exit_confirmed` whose actor is not `owner:%`, a `hold`, `step_down` or `auto_step_up` whose actor is not `rule:ramp`, an `auto_step_up` above step 2 or without a settings snapshot, any move to step 3 without an earlier `exit_confirmed` of the account, a step that skips upwards (1 to 3); an owner `set_step` may lower to any lower step.
7. (04B) Given the S1 evidence below, then each gate G1 to G6 has its state, number, threshold and n; `set_step` to S2 is refused with 409 `live.gates_not_met` listing every gate not `pass`; with all `pass` it writes one event carrying the frozen gate snapshot text and its hash; `insufficient`, `unavailable` and `not_evaluable` never pass.
8. (04B) Given an `after_halt` fill, then a `hold` event is written at the next evaluation; a second within 10 sessions also writes a `step_down`; each breach rule B1 to B7 has its hand case; the events are written by `rule:ramp`, never need the owner, and take effect on the next plan (existing positions and tickets are untouched).
9. (04B) Given a hold, then `clear_hold` by the owner is refused with 409 `live.hold_condition_active` while the breach condition still holds (event-type breaches need 5 sessions without a new one), and works with a reason afterwards; a `step_down` is lifted only by a new `set_step` (gates apply).
10. (04B) Given loss today -270.00 (realised -130 and open -140 marked) at 1 R = 125.00, daily limit 2 R (250.00): loss 2.16 R, a daily halt is opened (stop_new); -240.00 (1.92 R) opens none; an open position with no usable price makes the measure `realised only, open unmarked` and is shown so; no realised data (capture gap) makes it `unavailable`, which fails `account_live_ready` and never reads as "no loss".
11. (04B) Given a halt, then a bell item and a Today tile exist, still-Approved or Armed tickets of the account for that session are moved to Invalidated by actor `rule:live-halt` (Triggered ones stay), and a weekly halt applies the profile's `WeeklyLossAction` (`HalveSize` halves the scale of the next ISO week, `StopNewEntries` excludes the account until Monday, `AlertOnly` records and notifies).
12. (04B) Given `live.auto_ramp_up` ON, all gates `pass` and a step below 2, then the evaluator writes `auto_step_up` by `rule:ramp` with the settings snapshot; never above step 2; OFF writes nothing and shows "eligible".
13. Every owner write (ramp step up or down, clear hold, `exit_confirmed`, settings) needs the session, CSRF, the capability gate `Live:Writes:Enabled` (option, default false; 403 `live.capability_off`) and a **fresh password checked on that call** (the `Elevate: false` pattern of `PUT /api/autonomy/settings`; never the 5-minute step-up window), is audited (names and numbers, never the password) and is rate-limited like sign-in; the system's own holds and step-downs are `rule:ramp` and need nothing.
14. `xd_app` can insert and select `ramp_events`, `live_settings`, `live_halts`, not update, delete or truncate them (grants test); no order-named type or route exists.

## Gates (S1 -> S2; S2 -> S3 doubles the volume and needs the P11-06 report MET and `exit_confirmed`)
All count only trades and sessions stamped with the current step's `ramp_event_id`. A gate is `pass`, `fail`, `insufficient`, `unavailable` or `not_evaluable`; the step goes up only when all of G1 to G6 are `pass`.
- **G1 sessions:** >= 20 trading sessions since the step event. **G2 volume:** >= 20 graded round trips (>= 40 for S3); `uncertain` trips <= 10 % of all.
- **G3 slippage:** over entries with one reference kind, n >= 20: mean total slippage R <= 0.05 **and** the one-sided 95 % upper bound <= 0.10; mean > 0.05 is `fail`; mean <= 0.05 but upper > 0.10 is `insufficient` ("not demonstrated"). One-sided t table: df 9 1.833, 14 1.761, 19 1.729, 24 1.711, 29 1.699, 39 1.685, 59 1.671, larger 1.645.
- **G4 discipline:** discretionary round trips / all <= 10 % (needs >= 20 in total); `over_allocation` = 0; `chased_entry` share <= 20 %; protected share of intraday entries >= 90 % (`unavailable` without order evidence; `protection_unknown` CNC entries excluded and counted).
- **G5 containment:** drawdown of closed live trades in R at the step <= 5 R; no `after_halt` fill; at most one daily halt in the step.
- **G6 capture:** `CaptureHealth = complete` on >= 95 % of the step's sessions, no `gap` in the last 5, zero history-versus-trade-book disagreements in the last 10 sessions.
- **G7 cost drag (informational):** median round-trip charges as a share of 1 R, printed with a warning above 0.30 ("R too small for the fixed costs: raise capital or risk, do not read net R"); never blocks.

## Breach rules (automatic, `rule:ramp`)
B1 `after_halt` fill: hold; a second within 10 sessions: step down. B2 two `over_allocation` entries in 10 sessions: hold. B3 two `unprotected` intraday entries in 10 sessions: hold. B4 n >= 15 and the one-sided 95 % **lower** bound of mean total slippage R > 0.10: hold. B5 two of the last 5 sessions `gap` or `not_run`: hold. B6 closed-trade drawdown at the step > the profile's weekly limit in R (5): step down. B7 a second weekly halt within 8 weeks: step down. Hold blocks raising only; step down takes effect on the next plan.

## Non-functional
Evaluation reads at most the step's round trips (hundreds) and the last 30 sessions: p95 < 300 ms. Passwords: never logged, never stored; the audit text carries step numbers and gate states only. Frontend: one lazy chunk; every write goes through the typed client; the Ramp tab is the only place in `features/live` that may call a mutation (a test pins the other tabs read only).

## Decisions (researched)
1. **Q: Steps?** A: 25 / 50 / 100 % (P11-00 D10). Why: the roadmap puts P11 at 25-50 %; doubling keeps each step's worst case one step's loss limit. Rejected: continuous scaling (not auditable).
2. **Q: Gate on profit?** A: No: execution, discipline, containment and capture only. Why: with sd 1 R, 20 trades give a standard error of 0.22 R, wider than the +0.15 R edge the qualification gates ask for; a profit gate rewards luck. Rejected: "net positive after N trades".
3. **Q: Tolerances 0.05 R and 0.10 R?** A: Mean <= 0.05 R and upper bound <= 0.10 R. Why: 0.05 R is a third of the +0.15 R expectancy gate (doc 09 §7); with sd 0.12 and n = 20 the upper bound needs a mean of about 0.05 to stay under 0.10, which is reachable; a bound-only rule at 0.05 would need a mean of -0.02 and almost never pass. Rejected: bound <= 0.05 (unreachable), point estimate only (blind to noise).
4. **Q: Who raises and who lowers?** A: The owner raises (fresh password, reason, frozen evidence); the system lowers by rule. Why: real money rises only by a human act; lowering is the safe direction. An owner's own lowering also asks for the password in this slice (uniform rule for every UI write; P12-07 lets an owner demotion go without one, a difference accepted and stated). Rejected: symmetric approval.
5. **Q: Auto option?** A: Both `live.auto_approve_tickets` and `live.auto_ramp_up` exist, OFF, owner-only, snapshot-stamped, auto step-up capped at S2, `unavailable` never a pass. Why: the standing rule (CLAUDE.md, 2026-10-23) requires an automatic form of every manual approval; an approval is a state, the order stays manual. Rejected: omitting them; an uncapped auto ramp.
6. **Q: What is scaled?** A: Risk per trade, total open risk and capital per trade; not the counts. Why: scaling counts would starve the evidence; scaling only risk leaves the capital cap able to bind. At 1 R = 125.00 the daily limit in R (2 R = 250.00 = 0.25 % of capital) binds long before the percentage limit (1.5 %), which is stated on the screen. Rejected: leaving the capital cap unscaled.
7. **Q: S3 unlock?** A: Only after the owner records `exit_confirmed` while the P11-06 report is MET. Why: it makes "scale gradually" an explicit, recorded owner act and keeps P11 inside 25-50 % by construction (the database refuses the step). Rejected: automatic S3.
8. **Q: What does a halt do?** A: Record, notice, refused approvals, invalidated unfilled tickets, flagged later fills (P11-00 D11). Why: the owner places orders; only visibility and friction are possible. Rejected: silent blocking, realised-only loss.
9. **Q: Where is the mode gate?** A: In the existing `PUT /api/accounts/{id}/trading` handler. Why: P12-07 keeps paper to `manual_live` as the existing step-up change; a gate there adds friction without a new route. Rejected: a separate "go live" endpoint (two ways to the same state).

## Risks and honest limits
A halt is advice: the owner can ignore it, and the only consequence is a breach that holds the ramp. The loss measure marks open positions at the last price the app has (the live feed or the last stored bar); a stale price is shown with its age. The R unit of the halt is the current scaled 1 R. Gates at n = 20 to 40 are weak evidence of anything but gross problems; they are a safety brake, not a proof. A gate that cannot be evaluated blocks the step, which can keep an account at S1 for the whole phase: that is acceptable. The approvals guard trigger is a cross-table rule; the reviewer should confirm trigger versus application-only enforcement.

## Open questions
