## Assumptions
1. **The owner logs in to Dhan every trading morning through the app's existing Connect flow.** The app never generates, renews or stores a token by itself, never holds a Dhan PIN or TOTP secret, and never automates the 2FA (D1).
2. **Dhan facts** (read 2026-10-24 from <https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/authentication/>): an access token is valid 24 hours from generation; the consent flow is `POST auth.dhan.co/app/generate-consent` (25 consent ids a day), the browser login, then `consumeApp-consent`; `GET /v2/RenewToken` extends an **active** web-generated token by 24 hours and "if you try to renew an expired token, it will return an error"; `POST /v2/ip/setIP`, `PUT /v2/ip/modifyIP`, `GET /v2/ip/getIP` manage one primary and one secondary IP (IPv4 or IPv6), "once an IP is setup, you cannot modify the same for the next 7 days"; `getIP` returns `primaryIP`, `secondaryIP`, `modifyDatePrimary`, `modifyDateSecondary` and nothing about the IP the request came from; the static IP is required for order placement, modification and cancellation, not for reads (<https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/orders/>).
3. **SEBI facts, with their confidence.** The retail algo framework of the circular of 4 February 2025 became mandatory for brokers on 1 April 2026 after extensions (SEBI circular of 30 September 2025, `SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/132`, page header read at <https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/sep-2025/extension-of-timeline-for-implementation-of-sebi-circular-dated-february-04-2025-on-safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading-_96979.html>, 2026-10-24; the circular body could not be read). From secondary sources read the same day (broker help pages and blogs, not the circular): orders via API must come from a registered static IP; the exchange threshold is 10 orders per second per exchange, above which an own algo must be registered; every algo order carries an exchange-assigned Algo-ID; a client logs in with 2FA once per trading day and sessions that rely on a continuously refreshed token are not supported (<https://support.fyers.in/portal/en/kb/articles/what-are-the-new-sebi-rules-for-retail-algo-trading-from-april-01-2026>, <https://www.sahi.com/blogs/sebi-algo-trading-rules-2026-what-every-retail-trader-must-know-before-april>, <https://community.upstox.com/t/interpretation-of-sebi-circular-titled-safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading/8020>). Whether an individual's own algo below 10 orders per second gets a generic Algo-ID from Dhan, and how Dhan treats it, is **UNVERIFIED**: the first sandbox and canary responses show whether `algoId` is returned (it is a response field in the order book, <https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/orders/>), and the quarterly re-check (below) is the control. Doc 19 §5's statement (≤ 10 orders/s tagged with the broker's generic algo ID) is the design position until then.

## Goal
Every trading morning the owner can see in one card whether execution can act today and what is missing, the system records at 09:10:30 India time whether it was ready (a fail-closed record nobody can argue with), a token that is absent, short, revoked or on the wrong IP stops new orders at once and explains itself, and the owner has a written runbook for the login, the 09:10 failure, an invalid-IP rejection, an IP change and the quarterly re-check.

## Scope
**In:** Domain `Execution/Readiness` (`ReadinessPolicy.Evaluate`, `TokenCover`, `ReadinessReason`, `DayMark`, `IpFacts`); Application `ReadinessLoop` in `xd-exec` (probe every 300 s, every 30 s from 09:00 to 09:15 while not ready), handlers for the attestation record, queries; ports `IConnectionFacts` (reads `accounts.broker_connections`, no token), `IBrokerFacts` (profile and `getIP` through P12-02); tables `readiness_checks`, `day_states`, `ip_attestations` (the SEBI re-check record is P11-05's `trading.compliance_records`, read through a port, not recreated); migration `AddExecutionReadiness`; endpoints; command `readiness_now`; settings keys; the readiness card and morning checklist (components in `features/execution`, mounted in the Today tab slot P12-10 leaves) and the attestation form (Modes tab); notifications kinds `exec_not_ready`, `exec_login_reminder`, `exec_ip_invalid`, `exec_recheck_due` (derivation in P12-10's file); the runbook (below, copied to `docs/engineering/execution-runbook.md` at build); ADR.
**Out:** procurement of the ISP address and its first registration (P11-05), setting or changing the IP at Dhan (an owner act in Dhan's web app, no write endpoint in `ExecutionEndpoints` for it), any automatic login or renewal, sandbox account creation (P12-02), the halts themselves (P12-05).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given an account in `assisted` or above, when the loop runs, then one `readiness_checks` row is stored when the verdict or any reason changes and at least every 15 minutes from 08:00 to 16:30 IST on trading days (verdict `ready | not_ready | unavailable`, reasons with codes, `token_expires_at`, ages of the broker reads); outside those hours and on closed days nothing is read. *`ReadinessLoopTests` (fake clock, calendar).*
2. Reasons, each an `ExecutionReadiness` check with `pass | fail | unavailable`: `connected` (connection status `connected`); `token_covers_session` (expiry at or after `exec.ready_token_until`, default 16:45 IST of the session date, and at least `exec.token_min_remaining_min` from now: the number P12-03 uses); `token_live` (the broker's profile answered and its token validity agrees, read at most 5 minutes ago); `ip_configured` (`getIP` primary or secondary equals `exec.expected_static_ip`, read at most 15 minutes ago); `ip_attested` (an attestation of this IP with orders allowed, at most `exec.attestation_max_age_days` = 30 days old); `no_blocking_halt` (no active `token_invalid` or `ip_not_allowed` halt that this slice could clear). Any `unavailable` makes the verdict `unavailable`, which blocks exactly like `not_ready` (fail closed). *`ReadinessPolicyTests` (table).*
3. Token cover hand cases (IST): issued 2026-10-26 17:00, expires 2026-10-27 17:00 → on the 27th at 09:10 it passes (cover until 16:45, remaining 7 h 50 min); issued 2026-10-26 15:00, expires 2026-10-27 15:00 → fails `token_short` with "expires at 15:00 today: log in again between 16:45 yesterday and 09:00 today"; expiry exactly 16:45:00 passes, 16:44:59 fails; issued 2026-10-27 08:20 passes on the 27th, and on the 28th at 09:10 (expired 08:20) is `token_expired`; expiry 09:29 at 09:10 → remaining 19 min fails (below 20). *`TokenCoverTests`.*
4. The 09:10 mark: at 09:10:30 IST on each trading day the loop stores one `day_states` row per account (`ready | not_ready | unavailable`, reasons, the newest check id); if `xd-exec` was not running at that moment the first run afterwards stores it with `late = true` and the state is `unavailable` (never reconstructed as ready). A first ready check at 09:10:31 leaves the day state `not_ready`; trading may still start when ready (criterion 6) but the record stays as it was. Insert-only; unique `(account_id, session_date)`. *`DayMarkTests`.*
5. Fail closed on an expired or missing token at 09:10: no intent is created or moved to `checking` for the account; tickets whose trigger fires while not ready are closed `cancelled` with reason `missed_not_ready` (no catch-up after a late login); open positions and their broker-side stops are untouched; the owner gets `exec_not_ready` (error) naming the reason, the position list with the age of the last snapshot and "protection is at the broker; the system cannot see it now". *`NotReadyDayTests` with the intent creator of P12-08 and FakeBroker.*
6. Late login: after a successful consent callback the next probe (at most 30 s later) stores `ready`; entries are allowed again only for triggers that fire after that row and inside the ticket's own entry window; the halts `token_invalid`/`ip_not_allowed` clear automatically when the verdict is `ready` (P12-05 contract); a halt of another kind is untouched. *`LateLoginTests`.*
7. Reminders (in-app, ADR 0027 shape): `exec_login_reminder` (info) at 19:00 IST the evening before a trading day when an account in `assisted` or above has a token that will not cover tomorrow's cut-off, and at 08:15 on the day if not ready; `exec_not_ready` (warning) at 08:45 and (error) at 09:05; each id includes the date so it appears once per day and stops at ready. No message leaves the machine. *`ReadinessNoticeTests`.*
8. DH-905 (invalid IP) or DH-901 (token) in a send or read, as classified by P12-02 (`IpNotAllowed`, `Unauthorized`): the verdict becomes `not_ready` with `ip_rejected`/`token_rejected` at once (not at the next probe), the halt is P12-05's, and the card says what to do (runbook section 4). An `ip_rejected` verdict is cleared only by a fresh attestation (an owner act after he fixed the cause), never by time. *`IpRejectedTests`.*
9. Attestation: `POST /api/execution/ip-attestations` `{accountId, ip, ordersAllowed, note}` (session + CSRF; ip a valid IPv4 or IPv6 literal; note at most 200 characters, plain text) inserts a row stamped with the database time; it never contacts the broker; `ordersAllowed=false` is recorded and keeps `ip_attested` failing. *`AttestationTests`, `ExecutionReadinessApiTests`.*
10. Re-check due: the notice `exec_recheck_due` (info) is derived from P11-05's newest `sebi_recheck` record (`trading.compliance_records`, `GET /api/live-trading/compliance`, `latest.sebi_recheck`): due when there is none or today is on or after its `nextReviewOn`, and an account is in `assisted` or above. This slice stores and posts nothing for it (the record is written on P11-05's Compliance tab, with its step-up). *`RecheckNoticeTests` with a fake `IComplianceRecords`.*
11. `GET /api/execution/readiness?date=` returns per account `{state, checks:[{code, outcome, text, ageSeconds}], tokenExpiresAt, readyAt, ipModifyAfter (from `modifyDatePrimary`/`modifyDateSecondary`), attestation?, lastRecheck?, dayStates[last 20], sendsPerSecondMax (from `broker_calls`, value or empty)}`; never a token, client id, key or secret. *`ExecutionReadinessApiTests`.*
12. `POST` command `readiness_now` (a row in `execution.commands`, P12-01) makes the loop probe within 1 s; the card's "Check now" uses it and shows the command result. *`CommandTests`.*
13. The loop never calls any order endpoint; its only broker calls are `GetProfile` and `GetStaticIp` (a test lists the port members it receives); it reads the token only through P12-02's `ExecTokenSource`. *`ReadinessArchitectureTests`.*
14. Frontend: the readiness card (state word and icon, the checks list, `Check now`, `Connect Dhan` linking to the Accounts connection tab, the time the token ends, "IP can be changed from <date>"), the morning checklist (1 Connect Dhan, 2 Unlock today (P12-08), 3 Plan approved (P7), 4 Feed connected), the attestation form with the five steps of the runbook beside it; all states (loading, error, `unavailable`, never a green dot for anything not `pass`). *`ReadinessCard.test.tsx` (MSW).*
15. Settings keys exist with defaults and risk directions: `exec.ready_token_until` 16:45 (16:45..18:00, so the minimum covers the final reconciliation run at 16:30 of P12-06; earlier is riskier and not allowed), `exec.readiness_interval_s` 300 (60..900), `exec.expected_static_ip` (text, valid literal or empty; empty means `ip_configured` fails), `exec.attestation_max_age_days` 30 (7..90; higher riskier). *`ReadinessSettingsTests`.*
16. Log ids 6740 to 6749 unique; `scripts/gen-api.sh` run; `scripts/check.sh` green; architecture tests unchanged.

## Contract
- **API (`xd-api`):** `GET /api/execution/readiness?date=`; `POST /api/execution/ip-attestations`. Problem codes `execution.values_invalid`, `execution.not_found`. The setting `exec.expected_static_ip` is changed through P12-07's `PUT /api/execution/settings`.
- **Data (schema `execution`, migration `AddExecutionReadiness`):** `readiness_checks(id bigint identity, at, account_id, session_date, verdict check in ('ready','not_ready','unavailable'), reasons jsonb, token_expires_at null, profile_read_at null, ip_read_at null)`; `day_states(id, account_id, session_date, state, reasons jsonb, check_id fk, late bool, at; unique (account_id, session_date))`; `ip_attestations(id, account_id, ip text, orders_allowed bool, note, actor check like 'owner:%', at)`; . All insert-only. Grants (D10 of P12-07): `xd_exec` INSERT on `readiness_checks`, `day_states`; `xd_app` INSERT on `ip_attestations`; SELECT for both.
- **Events/jobs:** `ReadinessLoop` in `xd-exec` (P12-01 host); the 09:10:30 mark is a time-of-day action of the same loop; notices are derived by P12-10.
- **Network:** `xd-exec` reaches `api.dhan.co` for `GET /v2/profile` and `GET /v2/ip/getIP` only (already on P12-02's guard); no third-party host is added.

## Non-functional
At most 2 broker reads per probe, 12 reads an hour at the default cadence (inside the 5 per second read limit); probe latency under 3 s; the 09:10:30 mark written within 5 s of its time. Logs 6740 to 6749: account hash, verdict, reason codes; never a token, IP address or note text. Time through `TimeProvider`; IST day boundaries from the trading calendar.

## Test plan
Unit: policy table (each reason x pass/fail/unavailable), token cover boundaries above, day mark with a fake clock and a late start, reminder schedule (19:00, 08:15, 08:45, 09:05) over a week with a holiday. Integration: stores, insert-only grants, API on empty and on a fixture week, `readiness_now` command. FakeBroker (P12-02) scenarios: expired token (DH-901), wrong IP (DH-905), `getIP` with different primary, profile unreadable. Frontend (MSW, pinned clock): card states, checklist, forms, errors. Edge: a Friday evening reminder for Monday, a closed Monday, daylight irrelevant (IST has none), an account with no connection.

## Decisions (researched)
- **D1. Daily owner login; no automated token, no renewal.** Question: can the day run unattended for several days by renewing or regenerating the token? Answer: no. `RenewToken` works only on an active token, only for web-generated tokens (the doc says so), and is unverified for tokens from the consent flow; a loop that renews forever is a "continuously refreshed" session, which broker guidance for the SEBI framework says is not supported, and the framework asks for 2FA once per trading day. The TOTP route (`POST auth.dhan.co/app/generateAccessToken` with the 6-digit PIN and a TOTP) would need the PIN and the TOTP secret inside the app, automates the second factor the rule exists to keep human, and puts the most valuable secret next to the vault key. Sources: <https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/authentication/> and the Fyers page above (read 2026-10-24); ADR 0009 (consent flow only). Rejected: RenewToken loop; TOTP login; storing the PIN. The login costs the owner about two minutes.
- **D2. The token must outlive the session and the final reconciliation (16:45), not just the open.** Reason: 24-hour validity means a login at 11:00 yesterday dies at 11:00 today, in the middle of a trade where the system could no longer read, modify or cancel; protective stops stay at the broker but monitoring and exits do not. The usable login window is therefore 16:45 the day before to 09:00 the day, which the runbook states.
- **D3. Fail closed at 09:10 and never chase.** Reason: a late login must not turn into a late entry at a worse price; the ticket's own entry window and the trigger decide, and a trigger that fired while not ready is dead. The 09:10:30 record is the evidence the ladder gates (8 of the last 10) and the phase check (idle vs not-ready days) read.
- **D4. The IP is verified by comparison, attestation and the first order, not by the app asking "what is my IP".** `getIP` returns only what is configured at Dhan; it does not report the address a request came from (doc read 2026-10-24), and the "Detected IP / Orders Allowed Status" check exists in Dhan's web sandbox tool (Profile, Get Trading & Data API, switch to Sandbox, IP Setup, Get Static IP; <https://dhan.co/support/platforms/dhanhq-api/i-have-added-my-static-ip-s-on-dhan-web-but-my-orders-are-getting-rejected-stating-dh-905-invalid-ip-while-placing-them-through-the-api-what-should-i-do/>, read 2026-10-24). So readiness compares the owner's declared address with `getIP`, requires a dated attestation from that tool, and any DH-905 voids readiness. **Alignment point:** P12-03's `static_ip_ok` is written as "`getIP` reports orders allowed for the detected IP"; per the doc read here `getIP` has no such field, so P12-03 should read this slice's `ip_configured` and `ip_attested` (UNVERIFIED which is right until the sandbox smoke shows the real answer). Rejected: calling a public "what is my IP" site from the container (a new egress host, a third party learning when the system runs, and it would not show what Dhan sees).
- **D5. An attestation is a claim by the owner, recorded as such.** It is not verified by the app; it is dated, expires in 30 days, and a rejected order voids it. Rejected: skipping the check because the first order will find out (the first order should not be the test).
- **D6. Re-check circulars and broker terms on P11-05's schedule, and say so in the app.** The rules changed twice between February 2025 and April 2026 (extensions and milestones in the SEBI circular above); doc 19 §5 already says to re-check before Phase 12. P11-05 owns the record (`trading.compliance_records`, kind `sebi_recheck`, with `nextReviewOn`); this slice only reads it and raises `exec_recheck_due`. Rejected: a second store of the same fact.
- **D7. Probe in `xd-exec`, notify from `xd-api`.** `xd-exec` is the process that holds the right to read the token (P12-02); the API derives notices from rows. Rejected: the probe in `xd-api` (a second holder of a token-reading path for orders-adjacent calls).
- **D8. Reminders in the evening and the morning, in the app only.** An email or phone message would leave the machine (SAFETY) and P8-21 is not built. If the Mac is asleep at 08:15 nobody is reminded: the 09:10 record says `unavailable` and the day is lost, safely.
- **D9. We stay well under the exchange threshold and say so.** Our default is 2 orders per second (P12-01 registry); 10 per second per exchange is where registration starts in the secondary sources; the card shows today's maximum sends per second from `broker_calls` as a fact.

## Risks and honest limits
- The ISP may change a "static" address, or sit behind carrier-grade NAT (no public address of its own); then every order is rejected with DH-905 and, because Dhan allows an IP change only once in 7 days, trading can stop for a week. Mitigation is procurement of a genuinely fixed public IPv4 (P11-05) and, if the owner has a second fixed line, the secondary slot. This is the largest practical risk of the phase.
- The owner's two minutes each morning are a dependency: a missed login is a lost day by design.
- Everything about the SEBI framework above comes from secondary sources and the header of one circular; the obligations for a personal algo below the threshold (registration, Algo-ID, monitoring) are UNVERIFIED and are re-read from the circular and Dhan's API/algo support at every quarterly re-check; a finding that registration is needed stops P12 until the owner decides.
- A revoked token (the owner generated another, or Dhan invalidated it) is found at the next probe or the next failed call, not before.
- The `day_states` row proves state at 09:10:30, not for the rest of the day; the token cover rule and the continuous probe handle the rest.
- Consent generation is limited to 25 a day per account; repeated failed 2FA could exhaust it (the existing `accounts.consent_limit` message applies).

## Runbook (copy to `docs/engineering/execution-runbook.md` when built)
1. **Every trading morning (target 08:30, window 16:45 yesterday to 09:00).** Open xDrishti, Accounts, Connect (or Reconnect) the trading account, sign in to Dhan with its 2FA, return to the app. Open Execution, Today: the card must say Ready. Unlock today (password) if the account is in `semi_auto` or `auto_live`. Approve the plan before 09:05.
2. **If the card says token short.** The login was made between 09:15 and 16:45 yesterday and ends before today's close. Connect again now; the old token is replaced.
3. **If it is 09:10 and the card says not ready.** Nothing will be sent today for triggers that already fired. Connect now; trading resumes only for triggers that fire after Ready and inside their windows. Open positions keep their stops at the broker; open Dhan to see them; the system cannot see them until Ready.
4. **If an order was rejected with an invalid-IP code (DH-905).** The account is stopped for new entries. Check the router's WAN address and your ISP line; compare with the address saved at Dhan (Profile, Get Trading & Data APIs). If you added or changed the IP after your last login, Connect again once (Dhan: a token made before the IP was added may not reflect it). In Dhan's web app use the sandbox tool (IP Setup, Get Static IP) and confirm Orders Allowed is true; record the attestation in the app. If the address really changed, you cannot change it at Dhan for 7 days after the last change (the card shows the date); use the secondary slot if one exists; otherwise the account stays stopped.
5. **Setting up or changing the IP (planned).** Ask the ISP for a fixed public IPv4 that is not shared (P11-05). In Dhan web add it as primary (and a second fixed address as secondary if you have one); note the 7-day lock. Connect again after adding it. Use the sandbox tool to confirm; save the address in `exec.expected_static_ip` and record the attestation.
6. **Every quarter, or on P11-05's `nextReviewOn`.** Read the latest SEBI/NSE circulars on retail algo trading and Dhan's API terms and release notes; record the re-check on the Compliance tab of `/live` (P11-05); stop and decide before going on if anything says a personal algo needs registration or that the rules for your account changed.
