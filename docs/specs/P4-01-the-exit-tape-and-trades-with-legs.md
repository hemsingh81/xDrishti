## Goal
Give the walker what every later exit policy needs, without adding a policy: (1) a **tape**, the values of the features a trail reads, recorded once per instrument at each closed evaluation bar, and a walker that may read it only as of the closed bar; (2) a **trade with legs**, so that a partial exit, an add or a trail can be several fills of one position with one R. After this slice every existing run gives the same entries, exits and R as before, bit for bit in the fields that exist today; the new machinery is proved by a policy that lives only in the test project.

## Refinement of P4-00 (D1): a tape per instrument, not a series per candidate
P4-00 recommended that the scanner "emits, with each candidate, a per-bar exit series". Reading the scanner shows a better shape: the scanner already steps the feature frame through **all** the minutes of an instrument, one frame per instrument and side, and a candidate is only a mark on that walk. Copying the post-entry values into every candidate would store the same numbers many times (a day with many candidates, a swing position that lives five sessions) and would add allocations to a loop that today allocates nothing per step. So the recommendation here is **one tape per (instrument, side, evaluation timeframe)**: a struct-of-arrays indexed by the closed evaluation bar, holding the bar's close instant and the value of each feature that some candidate exit reads. The walker gets the tape by reference and finds its start by the decision instant. The look-ahead rule and the pure-function property of P4-00 (D1, D9) are unchanged; this is a change of where the numbers live. If the owner prefers the per-candidate series as written in P4-00, question 1 says so and the size goes up (UNVERIFIED by how much).

## Scope
**In:**
1. **The tape** (D1 to D3): a `ExitTape` value built by the scanner at the steps where the evaluation timeframe closes a bar, from the features named by `ExitPlan.Reads` of the candidate exits (the plumbing of P3-02b2 D12). When the strategy has only `FIXED_RR` (every strategy today) the tape is empty and costs nothing.
2. **The walker over a position state** (D4 to D6): `TradeWalker` is generalised from "one stop, one target, one exit" to a `Position` with a stop, an optional target, a quantity share and a list of legs, driven by an `IExitRule` per candidate exit (an internal interface; `FIXED_RR` becomes its first implementation and must reproduce today's rows X1 to X9 byte for byte). `MinuteRule.Decide` stays the one routine for the minute table.
3. **`SimTrade` with legs** (D7 to D9): schema 5. A trade whose position left in one fill has no legs and every field as today; a trade that left in several carries `Legs`, and its top-level fields are the totals and the **last** leg, as set out in D8.
4. **R, costs and the position gate for a trade with legs** (D10 to D12): R in the unit of the initial risk, costs per order, the gate held until the last leg.
5. **The oracle** extended for legs on a test-only rule (D14) and the leak property of P4-00 D9 (D13).
6. **Run record and stores**: engine `sim-6`, run-record schema 6, the export format and the result store (D15), the cards (D16).

## Definitions
"E" is the evaluation timeframe; "bar *k*" is the *k*-th closed E-bar of an instrument's replay; "leg" is one fill of one order of one position. Prices are decimal inside the walker as today.

**D1. The tape.** For one instrument and side, `ExitTape` holds for each closed E-bar *k*: `CloseEpoch[k]` (the instant the bar closed: the end of the minute that closed it, the same instant as `EntryCandidate.DecisionEpochSeconds` for a candidate decided on that bar), `Close[k]`, and `Values[f][k]` for each feature *f* named in `ExitPlan.Reads`. A value is the feature **as of the close of bar *k*** and nothing later. The scanner appends at the steps where E closes a bar, in step order, so `CloseEpoch` is strictly ascending; a step that closes two bars (the case the scanner already counts as a lost decision) appends both, in order, and the earlier bar's value is as of the earlier close, not of the step (UNVERIFIED that the frame can give the earlier value; if it cannot, the tape records the later one for both and marks the earlier bar `Unreliable`, and a rule must treat an unreliable bar as "no new level"; the build decides and the ADR says which). A feature that is not ready (warm-up) is recorded as NaN and a rule must treat NaN as "no new level".

**D2. Reading the tape.** `ExitTape.LastClosedAtOrBefore(instant)` returns the index of the latest bar whose `CloseEpoch` is at or before the instant (O(log n), or O(1) amortised by the walker's cursor, which only moves forward). A rule at minute *m* (start instant `t`) may use bar *k* only if `CloseEpoch[k] <= t`, i.e. from the first minute **at or after** the close; never a bar that closes inside or after the minute. This is the whole look-ahead rule and the only place the tape is read.

**D3. Size and build cost.** The tape of an instrument is a few thousand rows per year per feature on 15m, 30m, 1h or 1D; it is built during the replay that already runs, never by a second pass over the minutes, and not at all when no candidate exit reads a feature. A strategy with `FIXED_RR` only gives `ExitTape.Empty` (a static instance, no allocation). Memory is bounded by `bars of E × (features + 2)` doubles and released with the instrument's replay.

**D4. The position state.** Today's `Position` is a stack value for one stop and one target (`Stop`, `Target`, `Risk`). It becomes: the entry (price, filled price, fill instant), the **initial risk** `R0 = |entry − initial stop|` (fixed at the entry and never changed: it is the unit of R for the whole trade, D10), the open **share** of the position in (0, 1], the current stop, the current target (or none), and the legs so far. The walker's loops (intraday and swing) keep their shape: they read minutes forward, call `MinuteRule.Decide`-based logic per minute, and stop when the share reaches 0 or a forced exit (square-off, `MAX_HOLD`, end of data) closes what is open.

**D5. `IExitRule`.** One implementation per candidate exit, internal to `XDrishti.Simulation`. It receives, at each minute that holds a trade, the position, the minute and the tape cursor, and answers with zero or more **actions** in a fixed order: `MoveStop(level)`, `MoveTarget(level)`, `ExitShare(share, price, reason)`, `AddShare(share)` (the last one only for P4-04). The rows of ADR 0038 decide the fill of an exit action (a stop at its level or at the open when gapped, a target at its level and never better, market-type exits pay slippage, a resting limit does not). A rule may only tighten a stop (D6). `FIXED_RR` returns nothing: its stop and target are the position's, exactly as now.

**D6. Order inside one minute, with legs (recommended).** The minute table of ADR 0038 (X2 to X5) is unchanged for the stop and target **as they stood at the start of the minute**. A level that a rule moves by D2 takes effect from the first minute after the close that produced it, so it is never in force inside the minute that closed its bar (consistent with the look-ahead rule). When one minute touches both the stop and a partial target, the stop is taken first (the same pessimism as X4) unless the open is already beyond the target. A stop that moves is never loosened: BUY, `new stop ≥ old stop`; SELL, `new stop ≤ old stop`; a rule that asks for a looser one is ignored and counted (`StopLoosenIgnored`, a new counter, D15), so a bug cannot widen risk silently.

**D7. Legs.** A leg is `(Epoch, Price, FilledPrice, Share, Kind, Reason)`: `Kind` is `Entry`, `Add` or `Exit`; `Share` is the fraction of the **maximum** quantity of the trade (the entry leg is `1` or `initial_pct` of it once P4-04 exists; the exit legs sum to the entry's share plus the adds). Slippage and the tick grid apply per leg as in ADR 0038. In P4-01 the only trades with legs are those of the test-only rule (D14); the production path produces trades without legs, as now.

**D8. The `SimTrade` of schema 5.** The 33 positional fields of schema 4 stay in their order and meaning for a trade without legs (an old trade, rewritten with `SchemaVersion = 5` and a null `Legs`, equals its schema-4 value in every other field: the byte-identity test of AC 1). `Legs` is added **last** (an optional 34th parameter, like `Costing`), null when the position entered and left in one fill each. For a trade with legs: `EntryEpochSeconds`, `EntryPrice` and `EntryFilledPrice` are those of the first leg; `ExitEpochSeconds`, `ExitPrice`, `ExitFilledPrice` and `ExitReason` are those of the **last** exit leg; `RExact`, `RFilled`, `SlippageR`, `MaeR` and `MfeR` are for the whole trade (D10); `StopPrice` is the **initial** stop and `TargetPrice` the initial target, so a card shows what the plan was. `Key` is unchanged. UNVERIFIED whether `Legs` as a record list breaks an equality assumption in the export or store (the snapshot tests will show it).

**D9. Invariants of a trade with legs.** The shares of the exit legs sum to the shares in (entry plus adds) with no residue; legs are in ascending order of instant (a partial and a final leg may share a minute only as the table of D6 says); no leg is priced outside the range of its minute except a stop or target level as ADR 0038 allows; a trade has at least one entry leg and one exit leg; a `Legs` list of one entry and one exit is never written (that is a trade without legs, written without).

**D10. R of a trade with legs.** The unit is `R0` of the first fill. For a leg *i* with share *s_i* the contribution is `s_i × (exit_i − entry) / R0` for a buy (`entry − exit_i` for a sell), from exact prices for `RExact` and from filled prices for `RFilled`; the total is the sum over exit legs, with an add leg changing the entry basis to the share-weighted average entry of the legs in (P4-04 specifies the add; here the rule is stated for adds as well so that the test-only rule can exercise it). `SlippageR = RFilled − RExact` as today and never positive. MAE and MFE are measured against the **share-weighted average entry** in units of `R0`, over all minutes the position was open (a minute with a partial exit still counts fully until its exit instant). UNVERIFIED that the average-entry reading is what the owner wants for MAE/MFE of a pyramid: question 3.

**D11. Costs per leg (ADR 0042).** Each leg is one order and is charged as one order on the schedule in force on its date; the quantity of a leg is `floor(Quantity × share)` with the remainder added to the **last** exit leg so that quantities sum exactly; a leg whose quantity would be 0 is merged into the next one (and counted: `LegsMerged`). `TradeCosting` grows: `EntryLeg` and `ExitLeg` become the charge of the first entry leg and the sum of the exit legs' charges for a trade without legs *unchanged*; for a trade with legs a list of per-leg `LegCharges` is added at the end (an optional parameter, null for a trade without legs), and `TotalCost` is the sum of all legs. `CostR = TotalCost / RiskTaken` where `RiskTaken = Quantity × R0`. `RNet = RFilled − CostR`. UNVERIFIED: the schedule of ADR 0042 for a sell leg of a delivery trade and for a position that mixes dates is handled by "the schedule in force on the date of that order"; a test pins it.

**D12. The position gate.** A trade with legs holds the instrument until its **last** exit leg, so `HoldEndEpochSeconds` is the instant of the last exit leg or the end of the due session (ADR 0040, D11). Nothing else about the gate changes.

**D13. The leak property (P4-00 D9).** For any seeded series, any rule of the test-only family (D14) and any E-bar *k*, truncating the 1-minute data after the end of bar *k* does not change the position state (stop, target, share, legs) as of the first minute after *k*. The property is a test in `XDrishti.Simulation.Tests` in the style of `p3-08-leak-properties.md`; a violation is class C (a leak) in the oracle log.

**D14. The test-only rule.** To prove the tape and the legs without a policy of doc 08, the test project holds an `IExitRule` called `TestLadderRule`: at each closed E-bar it moves the stop to the bar's low (a buy; high for a sell) when that is tighter (so D2 and D6 are exercised), and it exits a share `s` at the first close beyond `entry + 1·R0` (so D7 to D11 are exercised). It is not reachable from production code, the model, the verbs or any strategy, asserted by an architecture test (the test assembly is the only one that references the type).

**D15. Version bumps and stores.** `SimulationEngine.Version` becomes `sim-6`, `SimTrade.CurrentSchemaVersion` 5, the run-record schema 6, and the export format `xd-sim-export-3` (UNVERIFIED that the export format must change for a nullable trailing field; the build decides and ADR 0048 says it). A run record of `sim-5` is read as before (the old reader stays); a new run never writes `sim-5`. The result store (`research.sim_trades`, ADR 0045) needs a place for the legs: recommended a `legs jsonb null` column added by the migration `AddSimTradeLegs`, insert-only like the rest; if the store slice is cut (see Size) the migration is not added. New counters in the run record: `LegsTotal`, `LegsMerged`, `StopLoosenIgnored`, all zero for every production run in this slice. The verb banner gains no new line.

**D16. Cards.** A trade with legs shows each leg as a mark on the chart and a row in the recompute sheet (price, filled price, share, quantity, the charge of that order); a trade without legs shows exactly what it shows now. The file name of a card and the index still say nothing about how a trade ended (the blindness of P3-09 is kept).

**D17. Q3-MFE (ISS-28).** Read from the [oracle log](../research/p3-08-disagreements.md): in an entry at the limit whose fill minute is also the last minute of the trade (`EndOfData`, or the `MAX_HOLD` due minute), rule R6 says that minute counts in full while X5 says the favourable extreme of an at-limit fill minute is never counted; both the engine and the oracle follow X5, which breaks `-MAE <= RExact <= MFE` (the log's example: MFE 0 with `RExact` +0.0909). No generator reaches it (0 of 7,832 trades). Recommended, as the log proposes: widen the favourable extreme to at least that minute's close, engine `sim-6`, goldens regenerated; this slice is where it is done because the bump is made anyway and a trade with legs measures MFE over a longer path. If you prefer to keep X5 and amend R6, ADR 0048 records that instead.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Byte-identity of old trades.** For the existing golden series (every walker and replay golden of P3, including the swing and overnight-entry ones, 8 oracle seeds' worth of series and the 100 hand rows of P3-08), the schema-5 `SimTrade` produced equals the schema-4 trade in every field except `SchemaVersion` and the (null) `Legs`; every counter equals; the run record differs only in the versions. Q3-MFE (D17) is the one declared exception, listed by the cases it changes.
2. **The tape.** Given a feature frame over a seeded series and `ExitPlan.Reads` naming `atr(14)`, the tape holds one row per closed E-bar with the value as of that close (checked against the frame stepped alone up to that bar); with `FIXED_RR` only it is `ExitTape.Empty` and the scanner allocates nothing more than today (the allocation test of P3-03a still passes, unchanged).
3. **Reading the tape.** `LastClosedAtOrBefore` returns the right index for an instant before the first close, at a close, inside a bar, between two sessions and after the last; a rule at minute `t` never sees a bar with `CloseEpoch > t` (D2), tested exhaustively on a small series.
4. **Look-ahead (D13).** Property over 10,000 seeded series: truncating after bar *k* leaves the position state at and before the first minute after *k* unchanged, for the test-only rule on a buy and a sell, intraday and swing.
5. **Stop never loosens (D6).** A rule that asks for a looser stop is ignored and counted; the stop of the position is never worse than before, property over the same seeds.
6. **Legs invariants (D9).** On the same seeds the shares sum exactly, legs ascend, no leg is outside its minute's range except as ADR 0038 allows, and a trade of one entry and one exit leg carries no `Legs`.
7. **R with legs (D10).** A hand-computed table of at least 12 trades with two and three legs, buys and sells, with and without slippage, gives `RExact`, `RFilled`, `SlippageR`, `MaeR`, `MfeR` to the printed precision; `SlippageR` is never positive.
8. **Costs with legs (D11).** For the same table with a TEST charge sheet, the charges per leg, the quantities (remainder to the last leg), `TotalCost`, `CostR` and `RNet` equal the hand values to the paisa; a leg of quantity 0 is merged and counted; the sum of the quantities equals the trade's quantity.
9. **The gate (D12).** A second candidate on the same instrument whose decision falls between the first leg and the last leg of an open trade is refused as `PositionOpen` today's way; one after the last leg is walked.
10. **The oracle.** The reference implementation of P3-08 is extended, written from the rule tables of D4 to D11 only (not from the engine code), and agrees with the engine on every field of every trade over at least 18,500 seeded series including the test-only rule; the disagreement log gains a section for P4-01 with the dry-read entries.
11. **The stores.** A trade with legs round-trips through the export and, if the column is built, through `research.sim_trades`; a trade without legs round-trips to its schema-4 text and values; the run record of `sim-5` is still read; the migration is idempotent and insert-only.
12. **Cards (D16).** A card of a trade with legs shows the legs and passes the blindness scans of P3-04c and P3-09 (no exit reason in a file name or in the index); a card of a trade without legs is byte-identical to today's.
13. **No production path produces a leg.** An architecture test asserts that `TestLadderRule` is referenced only from test assemblies, and a run of every built-in strategy over a seeded series gives `LegsTotal = 0`.
14. **Performance.** The walker's benchmark (explicit) shows no more than 5 % slower and the same zero extra allocations per minute for the `FIXED_RR` path than `sim-5` on the same series (UNVERIFIED target; if it is missed the cause is reported and the owner decides).
15. `scripts/check.sh` is green and a `reviewer` pass has no blocker or major; `scripts/gen-api.sh` is not needed (no API change); ADR 0048 and the doc updates are done and `scripts/build-docs.sh` was run.

What differs from the text above, and what the criteria rest on.

**Deviations (all in [ADR 0049](../adr/0049-the-exit-tape-trades-with-legs-and-sim-6.md)).**
1. The ADR is 0049, not 0048.
2. `FIXED_RR` is the walk with **no rule**, not a first `IExitRule` (D5): it keeps the sim-5 rows, the allocation test and the benchmark untouched by construction. Measured +3 % (intraday) and +2 % (swing, hold 5) on the explicit benchmarks against the sim-5 sources on one machine (criterion 14: 5 %).
3. **Cuts, in the spec's order:** the leg marks on the chart (D16; a text section only) and the store column (D15: no `AddSimTradeLegs`, `simulate --store` refuses a trade with legs by name, a sim-5 run is read as before). 4. A step that closes two bars gives one row with the later bar's values; the `Unreliable` flag is not needed (D1).
5. `AddShare` is not an action yet and no walker produces an add; the formulas of D10 and D11 are written for adds and the costing is general over entry, add and exit legs, but only exit legs are exercised.
6. For a trade with legs `TradeCosting.EntryLeg` and `ExitLeg` are the sums of the entry-side and the exit-side orders (D11 said "the first entry leg"; they are the same until an add exists, and the sums keep `EntryLeg + ExitLeg = TotalCost`).
7. The cost oracle does not walk legs: criterion 8 rests on the hand table `TradeCostApplierLegsTests` (charges per leg, quantities, the merge, the date of each order).
8. The test-only rule is `TestLadderRule` (as D14) plus a `ScriptedRule` that makes three exit legs; both are test-project types, held out of production by `ExitRuleArchitectureTests`.
