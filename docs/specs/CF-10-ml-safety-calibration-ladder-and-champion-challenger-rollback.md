## Decisions (researched)
**D1. Calibration by sample size.** Calibration rows below 500: **Platt** (sigmoid on the raw score, two parameters, Platt's smoothed targets `y+ = (N+ + 1) / (N+ + 2)`, `y- = 1 / (N- + 2)`), fitted by a deterministic Newton step in Domain (no package); 500 or more: isotonic as today. Cut `ml.calibration.isotonic_min_rows` = 500 (proposal). Report the ECE with a day-cluster bootstrap interval and the log loss minus the base-rate log loss. *Why:* isotonic is a step function that can have up to one step per distinct score, so on 80 rows it has up to 80 steps against Platt's 2 parameters; the small-sample behaviour is standard but not fetched here (Niculescu-Mizil and Caruana 2005, UNVERIFIED). *Rejected:* a 100-row cut (still too few for PAV); always Platt (loses a real non-sigmoid shape once data exist).
**D2. A complexity ladder, with events-per-variable (EPV) on the minority class.** Rungs: (1) base rate, (2) shrunk cell rate, (3) L2 logistic with at most 8 features, (4) gradient-boosted trees with monotone constraints and depth at most 3. A rung may be used only if it beats the rung below on the paired bootstrap of D4 (probability at least 0.95) and the rung-4 needs at least 100 events (proposal). Rung 3 is also capped by `floor(events / EPV)` with `EPV = 15` (proposal; the literature range is 10 to 20, Peduzzi et al. 1996, not fetched), where **events = the smaller class**. *Correction to the research note:* it counted trades (120 / 15 = 8 features); with a 40 % hit rate 120 trades hold 48 wins and 72 losses, so events = 48 and the cap is `floor(48 / 15) = 3` features (`floor(48 / 10) = 4` at EPV 10). *Rejected:* letting the trainer pick its own complexity from the validation score (selection on the score that is then reported).
**D3. Feature stability.** Per fold, rank features by permutation importance (P6-06); drop a feature whose contribution sign flips in more than one third of the folds (6 folds: 3 flips drop it, 2 keep it); compute the population stability index per feature against the training distribution, `PSI = sum((a - e) * ln(a / e))` over equal-mass bins, with 0.1 to 0.25 "watch" and above 0.25 "alarm" (standard cuts). Hand check: `e = (0.5, 0.5)`, `a = (0.7, 0.3)`: `0.2 x ln(1.4) + (-0.2) x ln(0.6) = 0.067294 + 0.102165 = 0.1695`.
**D4. Probability that the model beats the baseline.** Replace "margin" as the only evidence by a paired **day-cluster bootstrap** (resample whole trade days, 2000 draws, seeded) of the per-row log-loss difference (baseline minus model; positive is better). Report `P(model better)` and the 90 % interval; **pass** needs `P >= 0.95` (equivalently the 5th percentile of the gain above 0); `0.90 <= P < 0.95` is "promising", never pass. *Correction:* the research note asked for `P >= 0.90` and a lower interval bound above 0 together; for a 90 % two-sided interval the second means `P >= 0.95`, so only the stricter is kept. A fold sign test is shown beside it: 5 of 6 folds better has one-sided `p = 7 / 64 = 0.109`, 6 of 6 `1 / 64 = 0.0156`. The existing margins stay as a floor.
**D5. Sample uniqueness weights.** Training rows get weights equal to their average uniqueness (Lopez de Prado, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*, ch. 4; book listing only read): for a label spanning bars `t`, `u = mean(1 / c_t)` with `c_t` the count of labels spanning bar `t`; weights are scaled to mean 1; the effective count `sum(u)` is reported next to n. Hand example: three labels A (bars 1-2), B (2-3), C (3-4): `c = (1, 2, 2, 1)`, `u_A = (1 + 0.5) / 2 = 0.75`, `u_B = (0.5 + 0.5) / 2 = 0.5`, `u_C = (0.5 + 1) / 2 = 0.75`, sum `2.0` (not 3); scaled weights `1.125, 0.75, 1.125`. (The research note's "about 1.8" did not define its overlap; this case gives 2.0.) CF-01 owns n_eff for strategies; this slice only uses the same idea for training rows.
**D6. `MlMayInfluence`, one enforced predicate.** `MlMayInfluence(model) = HoldOutBeatsNoMl AND ForwardShadowOk AND NoCalibrationAlarm AND SwitchOn`, with: **HoldOutBeatsNoMl** (the model's rung beats the best no-ML baseline, base rate or cell rate, on the locked hold-out of CF-06, scored **once per model family**, at least 100 rows, `P >= 0.95` by D4, and the effect on realised R of the P6-13 policy is not negative); **ForwardShadowOk** (at least 30 graded shadow candidates (CF-08) scored by this model and at least 4 weeks, top-half minus bottom-half mean R not negative, rolling ECE within 5 pp); **NoCalibrationAlarm** (P6-09 calibration detector not fired); **SwitchOn** (`ml.influence.enabled`, default OFF, owner act with a fresh password; it is the owner's explicit risk decision because an influencing model changes which trades are taken). A second look at a burned hold-out by the same family is a new trial that **cannot** make `HoldOutBeatsNoMl` true (CF-06 D5); the model then needs a fresh hold-out (the owner advances the hold-out start once a new unseen block exists, CF-06 open question) or stays advisory. `unavailable` input = false. The result carries reasons. A single port `IMlInfluenceGate` answers it; an Architecture test forbids `Planning`, `Selection` and `Autonomy` from reading a model score except through it. *Rejected:* enforcing only by documentation; a score threshold on the model alone.
**D7. Champion / challenger.** A challenger is registered in shadow and scored on the nightly shadow set (CF-08). It can be promoted only if: at least 30 graded candidates and at least 4 weeks; the paired bootstrap of CF-12 (challenger against the champion on the same candidates, with the take rule of P6-13) gives `P >= 0.95`; the P6-12 counterfactual for its recipe is not `FrozenBetter`; `ChampionRule` still returns no reasons. Promotion stays an owner act (P6-08 lifecycle, changelog row). Auto option: `ml.auto_promote` default OFF; ON promotes only a challenger whose `MlMayInfluence` terms (except the switch) hold, recorded `auto` with the setting snapshot, never as an owner approval.
**D8. Rollback.** Triggers (either): the P6-09 calibration detector fires (gap above 5 pp **and** above 2.5 standard errors on a window of 100, three checks in a row), or the P6-09 band detector is in alarm (20-trade means below the band for 3 checks) on the cells the model scores. Action: the champion becomes `challenger` (shadow), the previous champion is re-promoted only if its latest evaluation still satisfies D6 without the switch, otherwise there is **no champion** and tickets show the cell rate only. Setting `ml.auto_rollback` default **OFF**: OFF produces a proposal and a bell notice (P8-15) for the owner; ON executes it, written to the changelog as `auto` with the snapshot. A rollback lowers reliance on ML, so no step-up is needed to turn a rollback on; re-promotion is an owner act.
**D9. Regime is a feature or a shrinkage parent, not a hard split, until a regime cell has 30 trades.** Example: 4 regimes x 60 trades is 15 each, below 30, so each shrinks to its parent. P6-02 (regimes) is not in the spec list; whether regimes are built is UNVERIFIED.

## Goal
A model's probability can never quietly start steering decisions: it must be calibrated in a way that suits its data size, be no more complex than the data justify, beat a no-ML baseline on an unseen hold-out with stated odds, hold up forward, and be rolled back when its calibration or its cells' results slip.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Calibration choice.** 499 calibration rows give Platt, 500 give isotonic, 0 give the constant base rate (unit). Platt on a constant score with 3 wins in 8: targets `0.8` (wins) and `1/7 = 0.142857` (losses), fitted value `(3 x 0.8 + 5 x 0.142857) / 8 = 0.389286`, while isotonic gives `0.375`; Platt is non-decreasing, deterministic and uses two parameters (unit, 6 places).
2. **ECE interval.** 100 outcomes with mean predicted 0.55 and observed 0.50 give a gap of 5 pp and a standard error of `sqrt(0.5 x 0.5 / 100) = 5 pp` (1 SE: not a breach by the P6-09 rule); the bootstrap interval is deterministic for a seed (unit).
3. **EPV cap.** 120 trades with 40 % wins: events 48, cap 3 features at EPV 15 and 4 at EPV 10; 200 trades with 50 % wins: events 100, cap 6 at EPV 15; the cap never exceeds 8 (unit).
4. **Ladder.** A rung that does not reach `P >= 0.95` over the rung below is refused with the reason; rung 4 with 99 events is refused; a model on rung 3 with 5 features and 48 events is refused for EPV (unit).
5. **Exact bootstrap oracle.** Four trade days with per-day gains (baseline minus model log loss, in 0.01 units) `+2, +1, -1, +3`: of the `4^4 = 256` equally likely resamples of four days, 19 give a mean at or below 0 (all four `-1`: 1; three `-1` and one other: 12; two `-1` and the pair `(+1, +1)`: 6), so `P(better) = 237 / 256 = 0.9258`: "promising", not a pass (unit by full enumeration; the seeded bootstrap with many draws agrees within 0.01).
6. **Sign test.** 5 of 6 folds better: `p = 7 / 64 = 0.109375`; 6 of 6: `0.015625` (unit).
7. **Uniqueness.** The three-label example gives `u = (0.75, 0.5, 0.75)`, sum 2.0, scaled weights `(1.125, 0.75, 1.125)`; non-overlapping labels give weight 1 each (unit, property).
8. **PSI and stability.** `e = (0.5, 0.5)`, `a = (0.7, 0.3)` gives 0.1695 ("watch"); `a = e` gives 0; 6 folds with 3 sign flips drops the feature, 2 flips keep it (unit).
9. **Predicate truth table.** Each term false in turn gives false with its reason; all true gives true; any `unavailable` term gives false; a model whose hold-out was already used by its family gives `HoldOutBeatsNoMl = false` with reason `holdout_reused` whatever the numbers (unit); the switch OFF (default, no row) gives false (integration).
10. **Enforcement.** Architecture test: no type in `Domain/Selection`, `Domain/Planning`, `Domain/Autonomy` or `Application/Planning` reads a model score or `AdvisoryPWin` for a decision; today `AdvisoryPWin` is display only and a test pins that (architecture).
11. **Promotion and rollback by hand.** A challenger with 29 graded candidates is refused ("need 30"); with 30 candidates and 3 weeks is refused ("need 4 weeks"). Calibration breaches on three consecutive checks (gap 12 pp with `n = 200`, standard error `sqrt(0.46 x 0.54 / 200) = 3.52 pp`, 2.5 SE = 8.8 pp, so gap 12 > both limits) fire the rollback; a gap of 12 pp with `n = 100` (SE 4.98 pp, 2.5 SE = 12.46 pp) does not (unit).
12. **Auto versus manual.** `ml.auto_rollback` OFF: a proposal row and a notice, the registry unchanged; ON: the champion moves to `challenger`, a changelog row says `auto` with the setting hash, never an owner actor; `ml.auto_promote` OFF by default and promotes nothing; turning any of the three switches ON needs the owner session and a fresh password; the setting history is append-only (integration).
13. **No champion fallback.** After a rollback with no eligible previous champion, plan evidence shows the cell rate only and `AdvisoryPWin` is null (integration over the plan builder with a fake gate).
14. **Regime rule.** A regime cell with 29 trades shrinks to its parent, with 30 it stands alone (unit); skipped with a note if regimes are not built.
15. No secrets, rows or model files in logs; Architecture tests green; existing P6 tests unchanged except where the calibrator default changes (documented in the ADR).

## Non-functional
Bootstrap 2000 draws over hundreds of days is milliseconds; training stays as P6-06. Deterministic seeds (sha256 of model id and purpose). Security: owner session, CSRF, fresh password on switches and apply; audit rows.

## Test plan

## Risks
Platt assumes a sigmoid shape; a badly scaled raw score can mis-fit (the reliability slope in CF-12 is the watch). With 3 folds of development data nothing is eligible, as today. A predicate that is almost always false is the intended outcome on short history. A hold-out used once per family can only be refreshed with new data.

## UNVERIFIED
Whether ML.NET already Platt-calibrates the raw score before the isotonic map. Platt versus isotonic small-sample claim and the EPV 10 to 20 rule (cited from memory). Whether the registry lets a retired champion return. Whether regimes (P6-02) exist. The cuts 500, 0.95, 15, 100 rows, 30 candidates, 4 weeks, PSI 0.1 / 0.25 are proposals. The day-cluster bootstrap with block length 1 ignores serial correlation across days (`block_days` setting, default 1).
