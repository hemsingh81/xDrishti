## Goal
One screen, `/reports`, where the owner opens the app, sees the answer to "what works, in which basket, on which timeframe, with which exit" (including "nothing has enough evidence yet") above the fold on a phone, narrows it by basket, and sees the Overview report (KPIs, auto-insights, equity and drawdown, monthly results, strategy by timeframe heat grid, expectancy by basket, R distribution, win rate by weekday) with the data used and the limits beside every number.

## Scope
**In:** the lazy route `/reports`; the catalogue (built reports active, unbuilt ones listed with their slice and reason); the filter bar; the Data used panel and run picker; the Overview report; the What works view; URL state; the no-write test for the folder; fixtures and MSW tests; nav entry.
**Out:** basket comparison, pivot builder (P8-09); the other catalogue reports (P8-10); drill-down and trade replay (P8-11); exports (P8-20); any server change; regime and account filters beyond a disabled, explained control; a composite score (D10).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given the server holds no run (`runs` empty), when `/reports` opens, then the page says "No stored simulation runs yet" with the verb to make one (`xd simulate`, text), every panel shows the `empty` state, and no chart or tile shows a digit or a flat line.
2. Given runs but no cell with enough evidence, when the page opens, then the first card is the What works headline from the server ("Nothing has enough evidence yet" with the counts: cells looked at, largest n, required trades), above the fold at 360 px, and the answer links to the full view. A test pins that the sentence is the server's `headline`, not page prose.
3. Given a ranked answer, when the What works view is opened, then ranked cells show rank, cell (basket, strategy, timeframe, side, exit, style when present), n, expectancy lower bound, shrunk expectancy, win-rate lower bound, each through the kit; the unranked cells follow with their reasons, never hidden; the line "looked at N cells, compared M; the best of N looks good by chance; about X positive bounds are expected by chance" is printed from the server fields (`cellsLookedAt`, `cellsComparable`, `expectedChancePositives`, `selectionNote`); `unavailableDimensions` is printed ("exit is left out: no run recorded it").
4. Given a basket is chosen in the filter bar, when the URL carries `basket=<ids>`, then every request carries it, the view reloads, and the whole path from landing to the filtered answer takes at most **3 interactions** (click Reports in the navigation; open the Baskets control; pick a basket). A test counts interactions with `userEvent` from a cold route and fails above 4 (P8-17 reuses it).
5. Given each of the six states in a response (value, provisional, insufficient, unavailable, not_evaluable, empty), then every tile, table cell, bar row and heat cell renders through the kit with icon + word + reason, no digit for a non-value, and no non-value in the `good` colour; a property test over the state set per panel.
6. Given the Data used panel, then it lists every run behind the numbers (strategy, universe, window, costed or gross, style, exit, label source, provisional flag) and every run left out with its reason; the picker (checkboxes) sets `runs=` in the URL; given a 409 `reports.run_overlap` or `reports.mixed_basis` for the chosen set, the page shows the server's message with a "Use the default run set" action that clears `runs`; given 422 `reports.too_large`, the message and the same action.
7. Given the filter bar, then date presets (1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, All) are measured back from the **end of the data** (the newest `windowEnd` among the runs of `GET /api/reports/runs`), not from today; baskets are a multi-select (names from `GET /api/baskets`, ids sent as `basket`); strategy, style and exit are selects whose values come from `GET /api/reports/runs` (`strategyId`, `style`, `exit`; exits are sent semicolon separated); timeframe and side are fixed lists; symbols are typed (comma separated, matched exactly and case-sensitively by the server, so the field uppercases); regime and account are disabled controls whose reason text is the message of the 422 `reports.dimension_unavailable` that `GET /api/reports/groups?lens=regime` (and `account`) returns, read once and cached (the server refuses it before reading any trade). Below `md` the bar collapses into one "Filters (n)" button with the active filters as chips that stay visible.
8. Given the Overview, then it holds exactly: KPI tiles (trades, win rate, expectancy, total R, profit factor, max drawdown, longest loss streak; each with n and the basis), auto-insights (the eight server sentences in order, a reason state when not evaluable, no sentence rewritten), equity and drawdown (the `TimeSeriesChart`, two series, with its table), monthly total R (bar list), strategy by timeframe expectancy (heat grid from `/pivot`, rows strategy, columns timeframe), expectancy by basket (bar list), R distribution (columns), win rate by weekday (bar list in calendar order). Each panel carries its evidence beside it and a text summary.
9. Given a basket dimension that is non-additive, then the basket panel and any basket-based cell print "A trade in several baskets appears under each; totals are not additive" from the evidence/limits, and no total row is drawn.
10. Given basis `net` is asked (`basis=net` in the URL) and the server answers 422 `reports.net_not_confirmed` or `reports.net_unavailable`, then the page shows that message and stays on gross; the basis in every evidence line reads "Gross R" unless the server says net with its confirmation text.
11. Given server text (headline, reasons, labels, strategy names, run labels, limits), then it is rendered as text; a test feeds `<img src=x onerror=…>` into each free-text field and asserts no element is created.
12. Layout: no horizontal page scroll at 360, 768, 1440, 2560 px (checked by the P8-16 checklist; unit tests assert the structure only: landmarks, headings, tables inside labelled scroll regions); touch targets 44 px below 480 px; the heat grid, bar lists and columns each have the same numbers as text; `prefers-reduced-motion` adds no animation.
13. Bundle: `npm run size` first-load stays within 200 KB gzip (the only eager addition is one route object and the removal of the `phase` tag of the nav entry); the lazy `reports` route chunk size is printed and set as the route budget in the ADR (target ≤ 60 KB gzip, UNVERIFIED, to be measured).
14. Tests never read the real clock (`pinClock`); `npm run test:clock` is run once for the date presets.

## Non-functional
- First useful content (headline card) after the `runs` and `what-works` requests; the other panels load in parallel and each shows its own `LoadingState`/`ErrorState`; a failing panel does not blank the page.
- At most 10 requests on first load, all cached by TanStack Query and by the server's trade cache; `staleTime` 5 min.
- Charts: only the equity chart uses ECharts (the existing lazy `TimeSeriesChart`); at most about 520 points per series, stated under the chart ("one point per exit date"); no other chart library enters any chunk.
- Accessibility: landmarks and headings per panel; `aria-live="polite"` announces "Report updated" once when filters change; focus stays on the control that changed; every chart has its table.
- No secrets, balances or account numbers on the page; logs on the client: none.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **The answer comes first.** Question: where does "what works" sit? Chosen: a headline card at the top of the landing Overview (the server's `headline` and counts) plus a full view; so the exit question is answered with zero extra clicks and a basket filter is two more. Reason: P8-00 criterion 1 (4 interactions, 2 minutes) and "phone first for review-type screens". Rejected: What works as a deep tab (3 to 4 interactions before any answer).
2. **Presets are measured from the end of the data.** The runs end on a fixed day (history to 2026-04-30); "last 1M from today" would be empty. Rejected: today-relative presets.
3. **Bar lists and columns in CSS, not charts.** Signed bars, a 22-bin histogram and 12 months need no chart library; CSS rows with the value as text give states per row, keyboard and screen-reader access and no bundle cost. The existing `CategoryChart` draws only donut and stacked bars of allocations. Rejected: registering ECharts bar types (bundle, and states inside a canvas are hard to make honest).
4. **The strategy-by-timeframe grid uses the server pivot**, so cells below the minimum are masked by the engine, not by the browser (a masked cell is `insufficient` with n). Rejected: client-side grouping.
5. **No "Net ₹" tile, no Sharpe/Sortino.** The engine has no sized trades (P8-05 assumption 12); showing a rupee figure would be invented. The tiles are the R-based KPIs the engine has.
6. **Order with P8-02 / P8-02b.** `useUrlState` is built. `HeatGrid` does not exist in `shared/analytics` (the built Learning screen has its own `Heatmap`/`HeatTile`); the prerequisite slice **P8-02b** extracts it, so this slice does not copy the Learning code. `router.tsx` and `navigation.ts` are edited once each, as separate lines.
7. **Filter values come from `GET /api/reports/runs`** (there is no `facets` field) and `GET /api/baskets`; the timeframe and side lists are fixed enumerations of the engine.
8. **Q10, Q11, Q12, Q13, Q14, Q19, Q20** (P8-00) are taken as recommended: the answer may be "nothing has enough evidence"; the default run set with a visible Data used panel; no composite score; basket membership as of the run, said on screen; the report engine first; gross unless the sheet is confirmed; the phase length is accepted with the cut line.

## Risks and honest limits
- On about 2.1 years of replayed history with no hold-out the likely first view is "nothing has enough evidence yet"; that is a correct display, not a defect.
- Every number is in-sample, gross unless the owner confirmed the charge sheet, on modelled fills; the evidence block says so on screen and nothing here is a forecast.
- Looking at many cells and taking the best is a selection effect; the page prints the count looked at and the chance positives.
- Missing run labels make the exit dimension unavailable for those runs; the screen names them (Data used).
- Layout has not been seen in a real browser by an agent; "responsive" is a claim until the P8-16 checklist is run.

## P8-17f: request fan-out 21 -> 11 (2026-10-26)
- **Measured** (`phase/whatWorksPath.test.tsx`, the owner's walk): 21 requests from the cold start to the picked basket where P8-17 acceptance 2 allows 12. Cold start 12 (runs, summary, what-works, insights, equity, three group lenses, pivot, distribution, and the two dimension reasons); the basket change then refetched the nine panels that depend on it.
- **Cause**: every Overview panel mounted at once, so the nine panels the basket changes were fetched unfiltered and again filtered. Keys were already right (what-works and summary are shared by the card, the tiles and Data used; the dimension reasons do not depend on the basket); nothing was duplicated, the count was the sum of two full waves.
- **Fix**: `DeferUntilNear` mounts the five panels below the first view (Monthly total R, Strategy by timeframe, Expectancy by basket, R distribution, Win rate by weekday) when they come near the viewport (existing `useInView`, 200 px look-ahead, stays mounted; mounts at once without `IntersectionObserver`). What works, Key figures, Auto-insights and Equity stay in the first view. The 5-minute `staleTime`, the keys and the backend are unchanged; no eager bundle growth (the wrapper sits in the lazy chunk).
- **Acceptance**: at most 12 requests on the walk (now 11: 5 first-view + 2 reasons, then 4 refetched); same figures and same states (every existing Reports test passes; those that read the lower panels scroll first); a scrolled-to panel loads once, with the basket; a 13th request fails the budget test; at most 4 interactions still holds.
- **Review follow-up**: the browser runner scrolls the Overview step by step to its end and back (`e2e/scroll.ts`, `scrollToEnd` flag in `e2e/matrix.ts`; C14 readiness is the first view, the shift on scroll is counted), so axe, touch targets and screenshots see the five deferred panels. The placeholder is sized by breakpoint from the measured panels (1260 / 1050 / 760 px), is `aria-busy` and carries a visually hidden "More panels load as you scroll."
