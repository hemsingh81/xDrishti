# P2-13 — Dhan trade-history and ledger sync

- **Status:** Approved (owner, 2026-10-14: "approved") · decision [ADR 0025](../adr/0025-dhan-trade-history-and-ledger-sync.md)
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P2 — "Account sync (holdings, positions, **trade history, ledger**)"; the sync that P2-03 prepared ("a later story reuses these tables with `source = broker`")
- **Design refs:** docs/design/12-portfolio-monitoring.md §1 (data sources), §3 ("Trades and the rebuild of lots"), 05-data-and-brokers.md (`IAccountReader`), 16-configuration.md (`sync.time`), [ADR 0011](../adr/0011-default-portfolio-bucket-per-account.md), [ADR 0012](../adr/0012-holdings-acquisition-dates-and-returns-model.md), [ADR 0023](../adr/0023-trade-import-and-lot-reconstruction.md)

## Decisions taken by the owner (2026-10-14)
1. **Trades and the ledger in one story** (not split).
2. **Lots are rebuilt automatically** after a trade sync, for every holding whose rebuild result is `ok` — no preview step. Safeguards in criteria 13–15, because a rebuild replaces hand-dated lots.
3. **Windows start at 30 days** (the real Dhan limit is unverified); tuned later from what Dhan returns.

## Goal
The owner no longer exports a tradebook or types cash movements: xDrishti reads each Portfolio account's trades and ledger from Dhan (a one-time backfill, then every evening), stores the trades next to the CSV and manual ones and rebuilds lots from the real dates, and shows the account's cash movements (funds in and out, charges and any dividend-class entries; Dhan credits dividends to the bank account, so the owner enters them from P2-14 — amendment g).

## Scope
**In:**
- Read-only Dhan clients: trade history (`GET /v2/trades/{from-date}/{to-date}/{page}`) and ledger (`GET /v2/ledger?from-date=…&to-date=…`), header `access-token`, behind the same endpoint guard, size and time limits as the holdings client.
- `source = broker` transactions in `portfolio.transactions` (same immutability, void and dedupe rules); new `portfolio.ledger_entries` (immutable); per-account watermarks; run rows.
- `SyncTrades` and `SyncLedger` use cases (backfill from the first-import date in 30-day windows, then incremental); runs never overlap with each other or with a holdings sync of the same account.
- **Automatic lot rebuild** after a trade sync for holdings whose result is `ok` (criteria 13–15).
- A service (`Portfolio trades & ledger`, daily at `sync.time` 16:30 IST on trading days, editable like the others) and **Sync now** per account.
- Ledger entries classified as `funds_in`, `funds_out`, `dividend`, `charges`, `other` from Dhan's voucher description and narration; a **Cash & dividends** panel per account (totals by class and period, list of entries). A `dividend`-class entry is rare (Dhan credits dividends to the bank account, not to the trading ledger; the ledger shows one only for rented stocks) — dividends are entered by the owner from P2-14 (amendment g).
- Frontend: sync status lines for trades and ledger, Sync now, broker marks in the holding's Trades section, the Cash & dividends panel.

**Out (not in this slice):**
- **XIRR, returns and the value history stay as they are** — ledger dividends and charges are shown, not added to cash flows (changing the return model is its own decision, follow-up P2-14, [ADR 0026](../adr/0026-dividends-entered-by-the-owner-and-charges-in-net-returns.md)). The UI says "dividends and charges are not in returns yet". The per-trade charges that Dhan's trade rows carry **are stored** on the transaction (amendment g) but are informational here: lots, cost and XIRR do not use them.
- Matching a dividend to a holding (narrations are free text); dividends are account-level here.
- Positions and intraday P&L; charges used in lots, cost or XIRR (stored, not used, until P2-14); entering dividends (P2-14: Dhan credits them to the bank account, they are not in the trading ledger); derivatives, commodity and currency trades and intraday products (skipped with a count, amendment c); closed holdings created from trades; editing a trade or ledger entry; tax reports.

## Acceptance criteria
**Clients (pure parsing + guarded HTTP)**
1. The trade client calls only `GET /v2/trades/{yyyy-MM-dd}/{yyyy-MM-dd}/{page}` (page from 0, no query) and the ledger client only `GET /v2/ledger?from-date=…&to-date=…` (exactly those two query keys, valid dates); host `api.dhan.co`, HTTPS, no redirects, 30 s timeout, answers ≤ 5 MB; the guard accepts exactly those shapes and still refuses every other path and method. The token goes only into the header and is never logged.
2. Trade rows (`exchangeTradeId`, `orderId`, `transactionType`, `exchangeSegment`, `productType`, `tradingSymbol`, `isin`, `securityId`, `tradedQuantity`, `tradedPrice`, the six charge fields, `exchangeTime` else `createTime` else `updateTime`; `"NA"` counts as missing) map to broker trades; a row missing an id, side, positive whole quantity, positive price or readable time is dropped and counted; times are India time. `tradingSymbol` can be `null` (as in Dhan's published sample); `customSymbol` is a company name and is never used as a ticker; a row with none of ticker, ISIN and security id is skipped as `no_symbol` (amendment g). `charges` is the sum of the six charge fields (amendment g). Every `NSE_EQ` row whose product is **not** one of the intraday products is kept (`CNC`, `MTF`, `MARGIN` and a row with no product included); other segments, BSE rows and the intraday products `INTRADAY`, `INTRA`, `MIS`, `CO`, `BO` (counted as `intraday`) are skipped and counted per reason (amended 2026-10-14, c).
3. Ledger rows (`voucherdate`, `voucherdesc`, `narration`, `vouchernumber`, `debit`, `credit`, `runbal`, `exchange`) map to entries with one signed amount (credit − debit); numbers and dates that do not parse drop the row (counted). The answer may be a list, a list under `data`, or — as in Dhan's published sample — **one object** (or one object under `data`), which is read as one row (amendment g).
4. Paging (trades) stops at the first empty page or at 200 pages per window; DH-907 ("incorrect parameters or no data") ends the paging as an empty result **only for page ≥ 1**; on page 0 of a window longer than one day it is *unclear*: the window is halved once and the halves are used, and every half (or one-day window) that answers DH-907 on page 0 is taken as empty and counted (`emptyByNoData`; amended 2026-10-14, b — the first version of this criterion read every DH-907 as empty). **The ledger has no paging** (one call per window) and follows the same rule: a multi-day window answering DH-907 is halved once (`mayProbe`), a half or a one-day window answering it is empty and counted; 401/429/5xx/timeout map to the existing broker failure kinds with the existing back-off and are never turned into "no data".

**Storage**
5. A trade's identity is `t:yyyyMMdd:` + `exchangeTradeId` — the **same** identity the CSV reader gives a tradebook's trade id — so a trade already imported from a Dhan tradebook CSV is a duplicate, not a second trade; reading the same days twice stores nothing new. **Documented limit (amended 2026-10-14, d):** a tradebook CSV row without a trade id has the `h:` hash identity, so the same Dhan trade (`t:`) is then stored twice.
6. A ledger entry's identity is a hash of (account, voucher date, voucher number, description, signed amount, occurrence index within the same date and voucher); reading a window twice stores nothing new.
7. Each window is stored in one unit of work together with its run row and the watermark move; a failure part-way leaves the watermark where it was (the next run re-reads the window and the dedupe makes that safe). **Every exit of a run completes it** — a failure, a crash or a cancellation ends the row as `failed` with a plain, safe error and the counts so far (amended 2026-10-14, d). Run rows, trades and ledger entries are never deleted (no DELETE for the runtime role).
8. A **voided** broker trade is stored again when its day is read again (its identity is free, as for CSV). Stated in the UI help and covered by a test; a "never re-import" mark is a follow-up.
9. Ledger classification (`funds_in`, `funds_out`, `dividend`, `charges`, `other`) is a pure, table-driven function over the voucher description and narration; anything unrecognised is `other` and the entry is kept with its raw text.

**Use cases, schedule and API**
10. `POST /api/accounts/{id}/sync/broker-history` (Portfolio-role account; optional `from`, default the watermark or the first-import date; a `from` later than the day after the older of the two watermarks is `400 portfolio.invalid`, field `from`; when exactly one kind was never read it may not be later than the first-import date; with neither kind read any start is allowed; an earlier one re-reads — amended 2026-10-14, a) starts a trades-then-ledger run and answers with the run; `409 portfolio.sync_running` while any sync of that account runs; `GET /api/accounts/{id}/sync/broker-history/status` returns watermarks, the last runs, `windowsDone / windowsTotal`, the next scheduled run, `emptyByNoData` of the runs, and `holdingsRebuilt`, `holdingsNeedingReview` and `needsReviewSymbols` (at most 50 tickers) taken from the **latest succeeded trades run and no other** — nothing is carried forward, so a quiet night whose rebuild was skipped shows 0 rebuilt, and a skipped, failed or running run neither changes nor erases them (amended 2026-10-14, d). Re-entry rule, audit (`BrokerHistorySynced`, counts only) and problem-detail codes follow the holdings sync.
11. The scheduled service handles every Portfolio account with a connected Dhan token, one after the other, on trading days at the configured IST time, reports `token_expired` for an expired token (the skipped run is recorded and changes none of the status figures of criterion 10; amended 2026-10-14, d), and honours `Portfolio:BrokerHistory:Enabled = false`.
12. The first run reads from the account's first-import date in windows of at most 30 days (a constant, tuned later); a window Dhan rejects as too large (DH-905) or answers DH-907 for on page 0 (criterion 4) is split in half once and the halves are used, and the run says so.
13. **Automatic rebuild, safeguards (1):** after a trade sync each holding is rebuilt only if its dry-run result is `ok`, in the same pure `LotReconstructor` and the same unit-of-work rules as P2-03; `quantity_mismatch`, `ambiguous`, `no_holding` and `unchanged` are left alone and counted in the run (`rebuilt`, `unchanged`, `notRebuilt[{symbol, reason}]`). The rebuild is **skipped when the run stored nothing, the previous finished trades run succeeded with nothing left to retry (nothing other than `no_holding`) and no holding of the account appeared, was reopened or gained a lot after that run ended** (`IHoldingStore.AnyAppearedSinceAsync`); a run after a failed run always rebuilds (amended 2026-10-14, e). The rebuild is also skipped when a window was not read, and — as a defensive guard behind the start check of criterion 10, which already refuses such a start — when the run's start would leave a hole after the older watermark.
14. **Safeguard (2):** a holding whose lots include a **hand-dated** lot (an owner edit) is not rebuilt automatically; the run reports it as `needs_review` and the holding's Trades section offers the existing preview-then-rebuild. So the automatic step never silently overwrites the owner's own dates.
15. **Safeguard (3):** a rebuild is skipped, not applied, when the holding's `xmin` token changed during the run (a sync or quote raced); the next run retries. Every automatic rebuild writes the existing `LotsRebuilt` audit event with `trigger = broker_sync` (counts only), and the figures (history, benchmark, groups, actions caches) are dropped by the same unit of work as for manual rebuilds. **The owner's lot-date edit takes part in this safeguard:** `UpdateLotDate` marks the holding as changed, so an automatic rebuild running at the same moment is skipped as `changed_meanwhile`; the edit itself is repeated once on what is stored, and a second concurrency conflict answers `409 portfolio.conflict` (amended 2026-10-14, d).
16. `GET /api/accounts/{id}/ledger?from&to&class&page` lists entries newest first with the totals by class for the filter; read-only; limits as the other lists.

**Frontend**
17. The account sync line shows trades and ledger status (last read, new, running, error, "n holdings rebuilt, m need review" with the tickers of `needsReviewSymbols`; "Dhan answered "no data" for N date ranges" only when more ranges answered no data than windows were read, i.e. windows were halved and still empty); **Sync now** disables while running; a failed run shows the broker failure in plain words. The holding's Trades section marks broker rows. The **Cash & dividends** panel shows totals by class and the list with a class filter, and states that dividends and charges are not in returns yet. Component tests with MSW cover idle, running, failed, needs-review, empty ledger and filter.

## Contract
- **API:** as in 10 and 16; the OpenAPI contract is regenerated (`scripts/gen-api.sh`).
- **Data:** `portfolio.broker_sync_runs` (id, account_id, trigger `manual|scheduled|first`, kind `trades|ledger`, from_date, to_date, status, counts jsonb, started/finished, started_by), `portfolio.broker_sync_state` (account_id, kind, watermark_date, updated_at), `portfolio.ledger_entries` (id, account_id, entry_date, voucher_no, description, narration, exchange, amount, class, running_balance, external_id unique per account, run_id), `transactions.source` gains `broker` and a nullable `run_id`; migration `AddDhanTradeAndLedgerSync`.
- **UI:** account detail → Portfolio → sync status and Cash & dividends; holding drawer → Trades; Services screen row.
- **Events/jobs:** `PortfolioBrokerHistoryService` in the services framework (ADR 0018); idempotent; no overlap per account.

## Non-functional
Dhan calls are serialised per account and spaced (≥ 250 ms); a 12-month backfill of one normal account finishes in under 3 minutes. No trade or ledger content, token or URL is logged (counts and ids only). Ledger narrations are treated as untrusted text (rendered as text, length-capped at 500 characters).

## Amendments (2026-10-14)
The independent review of the first implementation (a–e) and the mapping against Dhan's published sample (g) led to these decisions, which change the criteria above where marked. Recorded in [ADR 0025](../adr/0025-dhan-trade-history-and-ledger-sync.md) (amendments a–g).
- **a. A `from` that would leave a gap is refused** (criterion 10). A `from` later than the day after the **older** of the two watermarks is `400 portfolio.invalid` (field `from`), because the dates in between would never be read; an earlier `from` re-reads. When exactly one kind was never read, `from` may not be later than the account's first-import date (or the other kind's watermark + 1 if that is earlier), or the chosen date would become where that kind begins. An account where neither kind was read may start at any date. The pipeline keeps a defensive guard behind this check (no rebuild for a run whose start leaves a hole).
- **b. DH-907 is not always "no data"** (criteria 4, 12; narrows the original criterion 4, which called every DH-907 an empty result). It ends paging as empty only for page ≥ 1. On page 0 of a window longer than one day it is unclear (no data, or a bad range): the window is halved once and the halves are used; a half that also answers DH-907 on page 0 is taken as empty and counted (`emptyByNoData` in the run's counts), so a systematic parameter error is visible. On a one-day window it is empty and counted. **The ledger follows the same rule** (it has no paging, one call per window): a multi-day window answering DH-907 is halved once, a half or a one-day window answering it is empty and counted. The status line says "Dhan answered "no data" for N date ranges" only when more ranges answered no data than windows were read (windows halved and still empty).
- **c. Every NSE_EQ row that is not intraday is kept** (criterion 2). `INTRADAY`, `INTRA`, `MIS`, `CO`, `BO` rows are skipped and counted as `intraday`; they net out within the day and never reach the holdings. Everything else on `NSE_EQ` is kept: delivery (`CNC`, `MTF`), `MARGIN` (Dhan's F&O carry-forward product, which on `NSE_EQ` can only be a position carried overnight; skipping it would cause quantity mismatches) and a row with no product at all.
- **d. Run bookkeeping and documented limits** (criteria 5, 7, 11). Every exit of a run completes it (`failed` with a plain error). The status figures `holdingsRebuilt`, `holdingsNeedingReview` and `needsReviewSymbols` (at most 50) come from the latest **succeeded** trades run and no other, nothing carried forward: a quiet night with the rebuild skipped shows 0 rebuilt, and a skipped (expired token), failed or running run neither changes nor erases them. The owner's lot-date edit marks the holding as changed, so an automatic rebuild running at that moment is skipped as `changed_meanwhile`; a second concurrency conflict on the edit answers `409 portfolio.conflict`. A tradebook CSV row without a trade id (`h:` identity) and the same Dhan trade (`t:`) are stored twice — a documented limit, not fixed here.
- **e. The automatic rebuild is skipped** (criterion 13) when the run stored nothing, the previous finished trades run succeeded with nothing left to retry (nothing other than `no_holding`) and no holding of the account appeared, was reopened or gained a lot after that run ended (`AnyAppearedSinceAsync`); a run after a failed run always rebuilds. A quiet evening then changes no lots and writes no `LotsRebuilt` event. The further skip "the start would leave a hole" is only a defensive guard behind the start check of amendment a.
- **g. Mapping against Dhan's published sample (2026-10-14).** Read from Dhan's documentation (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/statements/, 2026-10-14); the fixtures `dhan-published-trade.json` and `dhan-published-ledger.json` in the Infrastructure tests hold the published rows. It changes criteria 2 and 3 and the "Out" list, and corrects the first text, which said Dhan's trade API carries no charges and no ISIN.
  1. *Trade row.* `tradingSymbol` is `null` and `customSymbol` is a company **name** (never used as a ticker); the row has `isin`, `securityId` and six rupee charge fields (`sebiTax`, `stt`, `brokerageCharges`, `serviceTax`, `exchangeTransactionCharges`, `stampDuty`); `createTime` and `updateTime` are `"NA"` (treated as missing; the time is `exchangeTime`, else `createTime`, else `updateTime`).
  2. *Charges and ISIN are stored.* `charges` = the sum of the six fields (rupees, 4 decimals; numbers, strings or `"NA"` tolerated; a missing, negative or unreadable field counts as 0) on the transaction, and the ISIN too. Charges are **informational** in P2-13: lots, cost and XIRR do not use them (P2-14 decides).
  3. *Symbol resolution when `tradingSymbol` is missing:* Dhan's `securityId` through `market.instrument_broker_ids` (`dhan`, `NSE_EQ`), then the ISIN through the instrument master. If unresolved but the ISIN is known, the trade is stored **ISIN-only** (empty ticker), like a tradebook CSV line with an ISIN and no symbol, and matched to a holding by ISIN. With neither a ticker, a resolvable id nor an ISIN the row is **not stored** and counted as skip reason `no_symbol`.
  4. *Ledger.* The published sample is a **single object** with string fields (`voucherdate` `"Jun 22, 2022"`, `voucherdesc` `PAYBNK`, `narration` `FUNDS WITHDRAWAL`, `debit` `"20000.00"`); an answer that is one object (or one under `data`) is read as one row. The classifier knows `PAYBNK` (`funds_out`), `FUNDS ADDED` / `PAYIN` (`funds_in`) and `DPC Interest` (`charges`).
  5. *Limits.* Dhan documents **no maximum span and no history depth** for either endpoint: an earlier `from` is accepted (floor 1990-01-02), and a window Dhan rejects (DH-905) ends the run `failed` with the "history too large" text; reading far back means many 30-day windows.
  6. *Dividends.* Dhan credits dividends to the owner's bank account; they are not in the trading ledger (a `dividend`-class entry is rare — rented stocks). The Cash & dividends panel shows whatever the ledger holds; dividends are entered by the owner from P2-14 ([ADR 0026](../adr/0026-dividends-entered-by-the-owner-and-charges-in-net-returns.md)).

## Follow-ups from the third review (minor, not blocking)
- `PortfolioUnitOfWork.RunAsync` returns `HoldingChanged` on a second concurrency conflict; three callers discard the result (`BrokerHistoryPipeline` row-by-row save and `FinishAsync`, `PriceAlertChecker`). Unreachable today (those units of work never save holdings) but they should check `IsFailure`.
- The `HoldingChanged` message ("The next run tries again.") reaches the owner on manual flows; give those callers a neutral text.
- `FirstSeenAt` is stamped before the Dhan fetch, so the 1-second margin in `AnyAppearedSinceAsync` can hide a holding created within a second of a trades run ending (rare).
- A database test for the `true` branch of `AnyAppearedSinceAsync`; a dedicated message when `from` is refused because the other kind was never read.
