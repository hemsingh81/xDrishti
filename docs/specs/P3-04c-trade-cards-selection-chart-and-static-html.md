## Goal
Next to the files of b2 the verb writes `index.html` and a capped, deterministic sample of self-contained HTML trade cards that the owner opens offline and checks against a chart: the 1-minute candles of the trade day with the entry, stop, target and exit, the timeline, the R arithmetic with every operand printed, the rule, the stored 09:15 bar and the data notes of the instrument, under the same honest banner as the files. A card has no script and no network reference and escapes everything it prints. Two runs write identical bytes. No performance statistic appears in a card or in the index.

## Scope
**In:**
1. **`--cards <0..500>`** on the verb (default 200, the roadmap's cap; 0 writes no cards and no index). b2 rejects the flag as unknown; this slice adds a row to the parser table.
2. **`CardSelection`** (C1), pure, in `XDrishti.SimExport`, with a shared seeded-order primitive `SeededRank` (the sampler of P3-09, strategy × instrument × year, is a second stratified sampler and uses it).
3. **`BuildTradeCardsHandler`** (C2), `XDrishti.Application/Research`, ports only: the second read of the bars of the instruments that have a chosen trade, one at a time, with the guard against a changed database.
4. **The renderer** (C3 to C5) in `XDrishti.SimExport`: `TradeCardHtml`, `RuleView`, `CandleSvg`, `IndexHtml`, `Html`, `CardNames`.
5. **The folder** (C6): `index.html` and `cards/…` as extra entries, extra manifest lines, the format string `xd-sim-export-2`.
6. Tests, the card section of ADR 0041, and the doc edits in *Done when*.

**Out (not in this slice):**
- **The decision trace and the "conditions at the decision bar" section: P3-04d** (`ISimulationEngine.Explain`, `TradeExplanation`, the extraction of the full input reader). A card here prints the rule from the recorded definition and, where the conditions would be, one line saying they are added by P3-04d. The replay keeps no trace today (`SimTrade.EntrySnapshotJson` is always null and `InstrumentReplay` holds only trades), which is why the conditions need a replay and the chart does not.
- A card for an `OpenAtEnd` trade (a position the data cut short is not a rule's result); a screen, an endpoint, a script in a card, an image file; any option to choose cards by symbol, key or seed. **The P3-09 extension point is `BuildTradeCardsQuery(Run, TradeKeys)` plus `TradeCardHtml`, which render any given set of trade keys; the verb has no `--trade-keys` and no `--seed` option in this slice, and the seed of the selection is the run key**: P3-09 adds the way it names its 20 trades.
- Any performance statistic (b1, *Out*).

**Size.** About **2.5 agent-days** (an estimate, not calibrated): selection and `SeededRank` 0.3; the handler, the shared bound helper and the guards 0.5; the renderer (card, rule view, SVG geometry, index, escaping, names) 1.0; the flag, the entries, the extra lines and the format string 0.15; tests (goldens, scans, Testcontainers) 0.45; docs 0.1. If it overruns, cut `index.html` first, then the raw 09:15 bar next to the adjusted one; never the escaping, the stale guard, the labels or the determinism. Sizes of the four slices: b1 2.4, b2 2.0, **c 2.5**, d 1.3.

## Definitions (decisions the tests pin)
All dates are India dates. Decisions taken on the code; the owner may revert any (recommended, the owner confirms at approval).

**C1 — Which trades get a card.** `CardSelection.Choose(SimulationRun run, int cap)` (pure) works on `run.Trades` only (`EndOfData` trades are in `OpenAtEnd` and get none).
- A *stratum* is an exit reason × a side. The strata are **derived from the enums, not listed**: every `ExitReason` except `EndOfData`, in declaration order (today `Stop`, `Target`, `SquareOff`; P3-03d is expected to add `MaxHold`), times `Side` in declaration order (`Buy`, `Sell`). A trade whose exit reason or side is not in that set (a value added after this code was written) goes into one **last stratum** and is never dropped; a test iterates `Enum.GetValues<ExitReason>` and `Enum.GetValues<Side>` and fails if a trade of any value can be left out.
- **Inside a stratum** the trades are ranked by the lower-case hex SHA-256 of `<run key>|<trade key>`, ascending: `SeededRank.Order(seed, keys)` is the one seeded-order primitive (a seeded sample with no random source, independent of the order of the input, the same for the same run, different when the run key changes). The seed is the run key; there is no `--seed`.
- **Round-robin rounds.** If all trades fit the cap, all get a card. Otherwise round *r* (1, 2, …) gives the *r*-th ranked trade of each stratum, in the fixed order of the strata, skipping a stratum that has no *r*-th trade, until the cap is reached (the last round may be cut). So the cap is spread **equally over the strata that exist**: a rare outcome is shown as often as a common one (a proportional step would show mostly the commonest outcome, which an early look at rule intent does not want; it would also add a rule to hand-calculate — rejected, recommended). The result is returned in the run's trade order.
- **Hand-calculated examples** (strata in the order Stop-Buy, Stop-Sell, Target-Buy, Target-Sell, SquareOff-Buy, SquareOff-Sell). (A) sizes 4, 1, 2, 0, 3, 0 and cap 7: round 1 takes Stop-Buy #1, Stop-Sell #1, Target-Buy #1, SquareOff-Buy #1 (4 cards); round 2 takes Stop-Buy #2, Target-Buy #2, SquareOff-Buy #2 (7). (B) sizes 3, 2, 2, 1, 0, 0 and cap 5: round 1 takes four (Stop-Buy #1, Stop-Sell #1, Target-Buy #1, Target-Sell #1); round 2 is **cut in the middle** and takes only Stop-Buy #2 (5), not Stop-Sell #2 or Target-Buy #2. (C) one stratum, Stop-Buy of 10, cap 3: ranks 1, 2 and 3. (D) sizes 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1 and cap 4: Stop-Buy, Stop-Sell, Target-Buy, Target-Sell. `--cards 5` shows the kinds of outcome that exist, not five of the same.
- The cap, the number of trades and the selected trade keys with their stratum are in `manifest.json` (as extra lines, C6). **The sizes of the strata are not printed** in the index or any card (counts per exit reason and side would be a hit rate in words); the manifest holds only the selected keys and their stratum names.

**C2 — The second read, and the guard against a changed database (`BuildTradeCardsHandler`).** `BuildTradeCardsQuery(SimulationRun Run, IReadOnlyList<string> TradeKeys)` gives `Result<IReadOnlyList<TradeCardData>>` (`internal sealed` handler; ports only: `IBarStore`, `IBarQueries`, `IAdjustmentFactors`, a `TimeProvider`, a logger; it names no engine type and needs no engine port; the request is not an input, `Run.To` is in the record). Why a second read: the record keeps no bars, and the bar-stream hash covers the whole stored history, so the guard needs the whole list.
- **Flow.** Validate the keys against `Run.Trades` (an unknown key is `simulation.invalid`). Take the instruments that have a chosen trade **in the run's universe order, from `Run.Universe.Members`** (the basket is never resolved again, so a basket edited between the passes cannot change what is read). For each, **one at a time**: `FindInstrumentAsync` (the tick), `IBarStore.ReadAsync` with the same bounds as the run (2000-01-01 00:00 to `Run.To` 23:59 India time, both with offset 0; the bound code is a small internal helper shared with `RunSimulationHandler`, moved under P3-04a's tests, which stay green), `IAdjustmentFactors.GetAsync`; check the guard; cut the chosen trade days out of the list; release the list; read the next instrument. Reads never overlap, `BeginMergeAsync` is never called, a cancelled token ends the call with `OperationCanceledException`, an empty key list reads nothing. About one read per such instrument (about 150 ms on invented data: seconds for the owner's 5 to 10 instruments).
- **Cost, stated as a decision.** The default `--cards 200` on a 214-instrument run with at least 200 distinct instruments roughly doubles the read time (about 30 s at the rate of ADR 0037 Amendment 3, UNVERIFIED); the roadmap's cap is kept as the default and the owner passes `--cards 5` for the early look.
- **Guard (all must hold, per instrument, else `simulation.cards_stale`, exit 1, nothing exported, the message naming the instrument and the first difference):** the instrument row exists (a member that vanished is stale); `BarStreamHash.Compute(bars)` equals `InstrumentRun.BarsSha256`; the applied actions that change the bars (`ActionsAfter(date of the first bar)`) equal `InstrumentRun.Adjustments` in ex-date, kind, numerator and denominator (the same fields as the `i=` line of the run key); `TickSpec.FromMasterPaise(row.TickSize)` equals `InstrumentRun.TickSize` and `TickFromFallback`. A changed calendar or CSV date cannot change a chart bar and is not checked; the trade itself is not recomputed here (P3-04d recomputes it and adds the equality guard and `simulation.cards_mismatch`).
- **The adjustment of the chart bars** is the replay's rule, re-stated in Application because `ReplayBars` is internal to `XDrishti.Simulation`: when any applied action changes the bars read, each bar's prices go through `AdjustmentFactors.Price(price, factor)` and its volume through `AdjustmentFactors.Volume(volume, factor)` with `factor = AdjustmentFactors.FactorAt(India date of the bar)` (public Domain functions in `Domain.Market.Actions`, the ones `ReplayBars` calls); otherwise the bars are used as stored. The rule is pinned by an equivalence test (AC 3).
- **`TradeCardData(RunTrade Trade, InstrumentRun Instrument, IReadOnlyList<Bar> DayBars, Bar? OpeningBar, Bar? OpeningBarRaw)`** (Domain): `DayBars` are the adjusted bars of the trade date from 09:15 to 15:29 India time (`Bar` is documented raw in the Domain but the replay feeds adjusted values, so the card says which it shows); `OpeningBar` is the adjusted bar **whose minute is 09:15**, and **null** when the day has no such bar (the card then says *no 09:15 bar stored for this day* and prints no other minute under that heading); `OpeningBarRaw` is the stored one, set only when the factor of that day differs from 1. P3-04d adds an optional last member for the explanation.

**C3 — What a card shows.** One HTML file per trade. Top to bottom: the **banner** of b1 (`RunBanner.Visible` always shown, `RunBanner.Limits` in a `<details>`, which needs no script); the title (`NSE:RELIANCE BUY 2025-03-04, target, +2.00 R`); the **chart** (C4); the **timeline** (decision time; the trigger bar with open, high, low, close, volume, minutes and whether it was complete; the fill minute and how the entry limit filled, at the open or at the limit; entry, stop, target, exit and its reason, each with its time; exact and filled prices); the **R arithmetic** (`risk = |entry − stop|`, `R exact = side × (exit − entry) / risk`, `R filled = side × (exit filled − entry filled) / risk`, `slippage R`, MAE and MFE in R and in price, every operand printed so the owner can redo it with a calculator; the printed R equals the record's to the printed precision); the **rule** (below); the line *conditions at the decision bar: not in this export (P3-04d)*; the **stored 09:15 bar** (open, high, low, close, volume as adjusted, the raw stored values beside them when the factor is not 1, and the sentence that the importer widens an inconsistent opening high/low and the stored bar may differ from a vendor chart, ISS-21); the **data notes** of the instrument from its `InstrumentRun` (adjustments applied with ex-date and kind, discontinuities flagged, sessions excluded, the tick and whether it fell back, the first 12 hex of the bar-stream hash); and the run key. Prices are printed with up to 4 decimals (trailing zeros trimmed), R with 3, times in India time, all with the invariant culture.
- **The rule view (`RuleView`).** It reads `StrategyRecord.DefinitionJson` with `JsonDocument` (tolerant: unknown members are ignored, missing ones are skipped) and prints, for the card's side (`buy` or `sell`), these lines in this order, each omitted when its member is absent: `strategy`: id and the effective parameters in sorted order (`or_min = 15, rr = 2`); `setup`, `trigger`, `invalidate`: `<mode> of:` then each item text on its own line; `entry`: the kind; `stop`: the kind followed by each non-null member as `name = value` in the JSON order (a quantity is a number or the name of a parameter); `exits`: one line per exit, `<policy> (name = v1, v2; …)`; `overlays`: the same; `time rules`: `first entry`, `last entry`, `square off`, `max hold sessions` as given; `filters`: one line each. Example for ORB BUY: `strategy: ORB (or_min = 15, rr = 2)`, `trigger: all of:` / `close > or_high(or_min) and close[1] <= or_high(or_min)`, `entry: at_close`, `stop: or_opposite (minutes = or_min)`, `exits: FIXED_RR (rr = 2)`, `time rules: first entry 09:30, last entry 11:30, square off 15:15`. A golden pins the ORB BUY and SELL views. **Fallback:** if the text does not parse or has no block for the side, the card prints the raw definition text in a `<pre>` (escaped) with its SHA-256, and says so. **Coupling, recorded as a consequence in ADR 0041:** `DefinitionJson` is the interim canonical text of P3-04 until P3-02c defines the real one (stored YAML, schema and hash); a change of that text breaks the golden of the rule view on purpose, and the view then follows the new text.

**C4 — The chart.** Inline SVG built by pure code (`CandleSvg`), with a fixed geometry so that the golden is not arbitrary. `viewBox="0 0 1000 440"`, no `xmlns` attribute (inline SVG in HTML5 needs none). Plot area x from 70 to 980 (910 wide), y from 20 to 390 (370 high). **X scale:** a bar at minute offset *m* from 09:15 (0 to 374) is centred at `x = 70 + 910 × (m + 0.5) / 375`; the slot is 910/375 wide, the candle body is 60 % of a slot, the wick 1 unit; the decision mark is at the slot boundary of the end of the trigger minute, the entry and exit marks at the slot centres. **Y scale:** `y = 390 − 370 × (p − lo) / (hi − lo)` where `[lo, hi]` is the range of the day's bar lows and highs **and the entry, stop, target (when there is one) and exit prices** (a stop or target can lie outside the day's range), widened by 3 % of the range on each side; **a flat range** (`hi == lo`) is widened to `price ± max(0.5 % of price, 0.05)` so there is never a division by zero. **Axes:** five price gridlines at equal steps with labels trimmed to at most 4 decimals; time ticks and labels at minute offsets 45, 105, 165, 225, 285 and 345 (10:00 to 15:00, India time). **Candles:** close ≥ open hollow, close < open filled, so colour is not the only cue; a missing minute is a gap, never a zero bar. Entry, stop and target are horizontal lines, the decision, entry and exit minutes vertical marks, each with a text label. `role="img"`, an `aria-label` and a `<title>`. Coordinates are written with the invariant culture, at most 2 decimals, trailing zeros trimmed.

**C5 — Inert, escaped, deterministic.** A card has no `<script>`, no `on…` attribute, no `<link>`, `<img>`, `<iframe>`, `<object>`, `<embed>`; an inline stylesheet (light and dark by `prefers-color-scheme`) with no `@import` and no `url(`; `<meta http-equiv="Content-Security-Policy" content="default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`; language and title set. **The scan is of attribute and style contexts, not of visible text**: the mandatory banner line `data: <n> instruments; …` and any text that happens to contain `http` or `//` are fine as text (they are escaped); what is forbidden is a URL-bearing attribute (`src`, `srcset`, `action`, `formaction`, `poster`, `xlink:href`, and `href` anywhere but the index, where its value must match `^cards/[A-Za-z0-9._&-]+\.html$`), a `style` attribute or `<style>` content with `url(` or `@import`, and a value starting `javascript:` or `data:` in any attribute. Every dynamic string (symbol, rule text, notes) goes through one `Html.Text`: `WebUtility.HtmlEncode` after **removing every character of Unicode category Control or Format** (so U+202E and the other bidi controls, which `char.IsControl` does not catch, cannot reorder text) and unpaired surrogates. **Attributes:** every attribute value goes through `Html.Attr` (the same encoding, quotes included); the dynamic ones are only the `aria-label` and `<title>` text (built from the ticker, which is already restricted to `[A-Za-z0-9._-]` by `CardNames`, enum words and dates) and the index's `href` (from `CardNames`); numbers, dates and enum words come from formatters, never from free text. A card is at most 150 KB. No clock, no machine path, no culture-dependent text.

**C6 — The folder, the entries, the exit codes.**
- **File names:** `cards/<nnnn>-<ticker>-<side>-<date>-<exit>.html`, built by `CardNames`: `nnnn` is the card's place in the run's trade order (1-based) written with **`max(4, digits of the trade count)` digits** (a run of 126,000 to 217,000 trades needs six; b1 E4), `ticker` is the symbol without the exchange prefix with every character outside `[A-Za-z0-9._-]` replaced by `_` and cut to 24, `side` is `buy` or `sell`, `date` is `yyyy-MM-dd`, `exit` the export spelling of the exit reason; the whole name at most 80 characters (a table pins it). `index.html` has the banner, the cap, the number of trades and a table of the cards (number, symbol, side, date, exit reason, R exact) with relative links.
- **Extending b1 without modifying it.** The verb passes b1's writer the five files of b2 plus these entries (`index.html` and one `cards/…` entry per card) and these **extra lines**, in this order: `cards.cap=<cap>`, `cards.trades=<number of trades>`, then `cards.selected=<trade key>|<stratum name>` for each chosen trade in the run's order. With `--cards 0` there are no entries and the extra lines are `cards.cap=0` and `cards.trades=<n>` only. The format string passed to `ExportManifest.Build`, `ExportKey.Of` and `SimExportJson.Write` is `xd-sim-export-2` (b2 passes `-1`). `ExportKey.Of` therefore covers the cap, the chosen keys and the hash of every card and of `index.html` with no change to its code, so `unchanged` also verifies them (a hand-edited card is never `unchanged`); a different `--cards` is a different export: exit 1 unless `--force` (renamed to `<run-id>.previous`, one copy, b1 E7).
- **Pipeline order in the verb** (b2 D5 plus): resolve, tick gate, run, selection, second read (stale: exit 1, nothing written), render, write. `simulation.cards_stale` is a conflict error added to `SimulationErrors`.
- **Logging:** two more `[LoggerMessage]` lines in `SimulateLog`, ids 4410 (cards written: count, bytes) and 4411 (cards stale: instrument symbol, the name of the first difference); never a price, bar or path.

## Acceptance criteria
Numbered, each testable. Fixture series are invented (P3-03's F-days; P3-04a's seeding through the real stores). Tests: `XDrishti.SimExport.Tests` (pure), `XDrishti.Application.Tests` (fake ports: the `FakeBarStore` of P3-04a with its call log), `XDrishti.Domain.Tests`, `XDrishti.Architecture.Tests`, `XDrishti.Api.IntegrationTests` (Testcontainers; the verb is called as in b2). **No new package:** the tests that need to read HTML and CSV use small readers written in the test project (a strict tokenizer for the tags and attributes of our own output and an RFC 4180 reader), not AngleSharp or a CSV library. Golden files follow the convention of b1 (the shared `GoldenFile`).
1. **The flag.** `--cards` 0, 5, 200 and 500 parse; 501, -1, `abc`, `1.5` and a repeat are usage errors; the default is 200; `--cards 0` writes no `cards/` and no `index.html`, and the manifest has the extra lines `cards.cap=0` and `cards.trades=<n>` only.
2. **Selection.** Given runs with 0, 1, 5, 6, 199, 200 and 5,000 trades and caps 0, 3, 5, 6, 7, 200 and 500 then the result has at most the cap, is the whole list when it fits, equals examples A to D of C1 (hand-calculated, including B, which cuts in the middle of a round), puts every non-empty stratum in round 1 when the cap allows, is identical when the input trades are shuffled, identical for equal runs, different (as a set, for a run of at least 50 trades per stratum) when the run key changes, has no repeated key and returns the run's trade order; strata are derived from the enums (a trade with an exit reason or side outside the known set goes to the last stratum and is selected when the cap allows: tested with a hand-made value); `EndOfData` trades are never selected; `SeededRank` has known answers for three keys computed with an independent tool.
3. **The use case.** Given a fake database and a run with chosen trades on three of five instruments then only those three are read, in the run's universe order, never two reads in flight, each list released before the next read (a weak reference is checked), the basket is not resolved again, `BeginMergeAsync` is never called, the bounds of the read are those of the run (offset 0, from `Run.To`), and an empty key list reads nothing; a different bars hash, a different list of adjustments (an added action, a changed ratio), a different tick or a vanished instrument each give `simulation.cards_stale`; an unknown trade key gives `simulation.invalid`; a token cancelled between two instruments ends the call with `OperationCanceledException`; the tests of P3-04a stay green with the shared bound helper. **Equivalence of the adjustment:** on a fixture with an applied action dated on the trade day and another dated before the first stored bar, the chart bars equal the stored bars adjusted by `AdjustmentFactors` exactly as the replay does (an action dated on or before the first bar is not applied; one on the trade day halves the bars of the days before it and leaves the trade day), and, through the real replay in an integration test, the trade's entry, stop and exit prices lie within the high-low of the chart bars at their minutes (the chart and the trade are in the same prices); `OpeningBar` is null on a day with no 09:15 bar, `OpeningBarRaw` is set only on a day whose factor is not 1.
4. **The card.** Given the golden BUY-at-target trade and the golden SELL-at-the-stop trade with their day bars, one on a split instrument and one on a day with a missing minute and a flat range, then the HTML equals a golden file; it holds the banner (visible lines, then the limits in a `<details>`), the title, the timeline, the R arithmetic (the test recomputes `risk`, `R exact`, `R filled` from the printed prices and they equal the trade's to the printed precision), the rule view (golden for the ORB BUY and SELL, and the raw-text fallback for a definition that does not parse), the line about P3-04d, the 09:15 bar with the ISS-21 sentence (and the raw values on the split instrument; *no 09:15 bar stored* when absent), the data notes and the run key; the SVG follows the geometry of C4 (the entry line is at the y of the entry price and the stop line at the y of the stop, parsed from the SVG; a stop below the day's low is inside the plot; a flat range does not divide by zero), up and down candles differ by fill, a missing minute is a gap, and the size is under 150 KB.
5. **Inert and safe.** Given a trade whose symbol, a rule item and a note hold `<script>alert(1)</script>`, `"><img src=x onerror=alert(1)>`, `&amp;`, `' onmouseover='x`, `</svg><script>`, a NUL, U+202E and U+200B when rendered then no card contains an unescaped `<`, `>`, `"` or `'` from data, the Control and Format characters are **absent from the output** (asserted: the output with U+202E equals the output without it), and the scan of C5 (tags, attributes and style contexts of every card and of `index.html` by the test tokenizer) finds only the whitelisted elements and attributes, no URL-bearing attribute, no `javascript:` or `data:` value, no `url(` or `@import`, and every `href` of the index matches the pattern of C5; the Content-Security-Policy meta is present; the banner line `data: …` and the text `http://x` placed in a note are present as escaped text and are not flagged.
6. **No statistics.** The whole-word scan of b2 D6 (the exact token list of b1 AC 6) finds nothing in the text of any card or of `index.html` (banner included); neither prints the size of a stratum, a count of trades by exit reason or side, a percentage, or a sum or mean of R; the index table shows each card's own R only; the manifest lists selected keys and stratum names only. The fixtures use symbols from a fixed list that cannot collide with a token.
7. **The folder.** Given `--cards 5` over the F1/F2/split fixtures then `cards/` holds exactly the chosen cards named by C6 (a table of at least 10 names, including a symbol with `&`, two instruments whose cleaned names collide separated by `nnnn`, and a trade count of 126,000 giving six-digit numbers), `index.html` links to each by a relative link that exists, `manifest.json` lists them with their SHA-256 and carries the extra lines of C6, the format is `xd-sim-export-2` in the manifest, `run.json` and the export key, the export key equals `ExportKey.Of` over the entries and the extra lines, files and folders have the modes of b1, **and the b1 types were extended without a change to any of their code** (the b1 extension test passes unchanged); two runs into two roots are identical byte for byte and a second run in the same root is `unchanged`, verified by hashing every card.
8. **Overwrite and stale.** The same request with another `--cards` is exit 1 and nothing changes; with `--force` the old folder is renamed to `<run-id>.previous`; a hand-edited card is never `unchanged`; a stale second pass exits 1 with nothing written and no staging folder left, tested **at the handler level** (a hook in the fake store merges one bar, adds an action, changes the tick) and at the verb level through `SimulateHost.Create(overrides, configure)` of b2, whose `configure` replaces `IBarStore` with a decorator that changes the data after the first pass; a cancelled run between two cards leaves no `<run-id>` folder.
9. **End to end.** Given the Testcontainers database of b2 (the F1 day, the F2 day, a split instrument, a basket of the three) when the verb runs with `--cards 3` then the three cards carry the right trades (entry, stop, target, exit, R equal to the record's), the chart of the split instrument is halved once, `trades.csv` and `run.json` equal b2's golden files except the format string and the manifest, and no table changed.
10. **Logging and secrets.** The two new lines are emitted with ids 4410 and 4411 and carry no price, bar or path (argument types checked); the secret scan of b2 AC 11 covers the cards and the index; `LogEventIdTests` passes.

## Contract
- **API / Data / UI / Events:** none. No endpoint, no DTO, no table, no migration, no `scripts/gen-api.sh`. The cards are static files in a git-ignored folder, never read back by the system.
- **Command line:** b2's, plus `--cards <0..500>` (default 200).
- **Surface (names are the contract once Approved):** Domain: `TradeCardData`, `SimulationErrors.cards_stale`. Application: `BuildTradeCardsQuery(SimulationRun Run, IReadOnlyList<string> TradeKeys)`, `BuildTradeCardsHandler`, the shared bound helper. `XDrishti.SimExport`: `CardSelection`, `SeededRank`, `TradeCardHtml`, `RuleView`, `CandleSvg`, `IndexHtml`, `Html`, `CardNames`. `SimulateLog` ids 4410, 4411. Nothing of b1 is changed.

## Non-functional
- **Performance:** no budget is asserted; the cost is one read per instrument that has a card (C2). The explicit benchmark of b1 gains the cards pass (20 instruments, `--cards 200`) and prints the bytes of the cards.
- **Memory:** one instrument's bars at a time; each card is rendered into the writer's sink and not kept.
- **Security and privacy:** C5; files `0600` in `0700` folders; nothing leaves the machine.
- **Determinism and fail closed:** equal request and data give identical bytes; a stale read or a failure writes nothing.

## Open questions
1. **Equal-weight round-robin over the strata, or proportional?** Recommendation: round-robin (C1). Consequence: the cap is spread equally over the strata that exist, so a rare stratum is as visible as a common one; the sample does not mirror the mix of outcomes (it is not an estimate of anything, and the sizes of the strata are not shown so that it cannot be read as one).
2. **Cards before the conditions section.** Recommendation: build P3-04c first and P3-04d after; the early look at rule intent then has the rule text and the chart, and the values the conditions read follow 1.3 agent-days later. Consequence: the first cards carry the line saying so.
3. **Default `--cards 200`.** Recommendation: keep the roadmap's cap; the owner passes `--cards 5` for the early look. Consequence: a default run on a wide universe roughly doubles the read time (C2).

## Risks
- **The cards mislead.** A clean chart and a precise R invite more trust than the engine has earned. Mitigation: the banner on every card, `engine: not yet verified`, no aggregate, the ISS-21 sentence beside the 09:15 bar.
- **The second read is stale or slow.** A backfill, an added action or a master refresh between the passes makes the cards refuse (exit 1); 200 cards on 200 instruments read the data twice. Mitigation: the guard; the owner looks at 5 to 10 instruments.
- **The rule view follows an interim text.** P3-02c changes the canonical definition text; the golden of the rule view breaks on purpose (C3).
- **An unknown exit reason added by P3-03d.** Handled by deriving the strata from the enum (C1), tested.
- **HTML injection or reordering through a symbol or a note.** Mitigation: C5 and AC 5 (including the Format category).
- **The re-stated adjustment drifts from `ReplayBars`.** Mitigation: both call the same public Domain functions and the equivalence test of AC 3.
