## Goal
Finish the set of building blocks of doc 11 §2 that the parser (P3-02) needs: features on the **weekly and monthly** timeframes, the two **calendar** features (`day_of_week`, `is_expiry_day`) and the **market context** (index trend, VIX level and percentile, breadth, and the daily **regime** label) — all with the same guarantee as every other feature: a value at a step depends only on what was known at that step, and a test proves it. Still a pure library: no database, API or screens.

## Scope
**In:**
1. **1W and 1M in the engine.**
   - `TimeframeBuilder` accepts `Timeframe.W1` and `Timeframe.Mo1`, built from the same 1-minute stream, with the stored-aggregate rules of doc 05 §4: a week starts at India midnight of its **Monday** (a Saturday special session belongs to the Monday-to-Sunday week it falls in), a month on the 1st; open = first, high = max, low = min, close = last, volume = sum; `Complete` = every session of the period that has bars is complete (the running period is not "incomplete" because it is not over).
   - **When a weekly / monthly bar is closed.** With a `TradingCalendar`: at the step that closes the **last session of the period** (the same minute the 1D lane closes that day), where the last session is the last date of the period for which `IsTradingDay` is true. Without a calendar the builder cannot know that Friday is the last session (a holiday, or a Saturday special session, may change it), so it emits the bar at the **first bar of the next period** — later, never early. A bar of an already emitted period (a special session the calendar did not know) is ignored and counted, as for 1D today (ADR 0031, "where it can differ").
   - `FeatureFrame` lanes for W1 and Mo1: `BarClosed(W1)`, `ClosedBarsAt`, `TryLastClosed`, history (`At`) in weekly / monthly bars, the raw bar features (`tf("1W").close` is last week's close until the week closes).
   - Catalog: `SupportedTimeframes` gains W1 and Mo1 for the continuous indicators (rolling, trend, momentum, volatility, patterns, raw bar); the intraday-only and session-anchored ones (`vwap`, `or_high/low`) and the session-context ones (`prev_day_*`, `today_*`, `gap_pct`) are refused on W1 and Mo1 with a clear error (the DSL reads them from 1D / 1m). Warm-up is still counted in bars of the feature's own timeframe (`ema(10)` on 1W needs 10 weekly bars).
2. **Calendar features** (no data needed beyond the bar's own date): `day_of_week` (1 = Monday … 7 = Sunday, India date of the step) and `is_expiry_day` (1 when the India date is in the set of expiry dates the caller passes in `FrameOptions.ExpiryDays`, else 0; refused at `Create` when the set was not given).
3. **Market context** — the daily series the strategies condition on:
   - `MarketContext`: a pure, shared object built from daily records (`ContextDay`: date, index high / low / close, VIX close if known, breadth if known). It computes its derived values **per day, from that day and earlier days only**, and answers `AsOf(date)` with the values of the **latest day strictly before** `date` (so a frame at 10:30 on a Tuesday sees Monday's close, never Tuesday's).
   - New features that read it (12 names, total 56; each has a lane of its own that refreshes at the first accepted step of each India date): `index_trend` (+1 up, 0 range, −1 down), `index_adx`, `index_vol_pct`, `vix`, `vix_pct`, `vix_chg`, `breadth`, `regime_trend` (+1 / 0 / −1), `regime_vol` (0 low, 1 normal, 2 high), `regime` (1…9) and the two calendar names above.
   - `Regime` names for the parser and the reports: `UP_LOW_VOL` … `DOWN_HIGH_VOL` ↔ code 1…9, both ways.
   - `FrameOptions` gains `MarketContext? Context` and `IReadOnlySet<DateOnly>? ExpiryDays`; a context feature without `Context` is refused at `Create` (error on `options.context`).
4. Tests and documentation as in P3-01 / P3-01b (independent references, hand examples, prefix property, frame semantics, determinism, reset, zero allocation, benchmarks extended, catalog consistency).

**Out (not in this slice):**
- **`sector_rs(n)`** and the **computation of breadth**: both are cross-sectional (they need the daily series of many instruments, or of a sector composite) and are produced by a data job that writes `market.context_daily` (designed in doc 05, not built). This slice only *reads* a ready-made `Breadth` per day. The context data job, the reader that builds the `MarketContext` from the table, and `sector_rs` follow in their own story, **P3-01d** (see open question 4 and ADR 0033 decision 4).
- Intraday index features (index above its VWAP, index move since the open); the context here is daily, as the regime of doc 07 §6 is.
- Reading anything from the database or the feed: the reader that builds the `MarketContext` from `market.context_daily` belongs to **P3-01d** (it owns the table and its reader; the simulator P3-03 only uses it), and a bar-reader adapter belongs to the simulator story P3-03; computing the regime *statistics* (cells, learning: P6); using the regime in the market gate (P7).
- More indices (Bank Nifty trend, sector indices) — one regime index (Nifty 50 by default) and the VIX.

## Definitions (numbers the tests pin)
These are the rules **as built**; the tests pin exactly these. Where a rule differs from the text the owner approved on 2026-10-16 — or the approved text was silent on a behaviour whose risk the owner should see (Amendment 16) — it is marked **⚠** and the approved wording, where there is one, is kept in the Amendments below, which also say what the owner is asked to confirm. The Definitions are the part a parser or simulator author reads; the Amendments are the record of how they changed.
- **Week / month bucket:** as doc 05 §4 ("Bucket rules as built"): `ts` = India midnight of the Monday / the 1st; `bars` = 1-minute bars; a 1W / 1M `ClosedBar` carries the same fields as any other.
- **Last session of a week / month (with a calendar):** the greatest India date `d` in the same week (Monday to Sunday) / month with `IsTradingDay(d)`. The bar closes at the step where the 1D lane closes `d`. A period with no bar is never emitted; a period with bars but no calendar session is emitted late, at the first bar of the next period. A date outside the range of `DateOnly` is in no calendar: a period there is closed by the first bar of a later period, and no builder throws for it (Amendment 14).
- **⚠ A stream that starts mid-week or mid-month** (Amendment 7): the first, partial period is built from the days it has, and `Complete` is "every day that has bars is complete" (doc 05 §4): the first week is complete when all its days are (a start at the open of a Wednesday gives a complete first week) and incomplete only when one of its days is (a start at 11:00 makes that Wednesday partial). *Approved wording (test plan): "the first partial week is emitted incomplete, as 1D today".*
- **`ContextOptions`** (defaults, all configurable; they will be trading configuration in the database later, ADR 0002): `TrendSmaPeriod = 50`, `TrendSlopeDays = 5`, `AdxPeriod = 14`, `AdxMin = 20`, `AtrPeriod = 14`, `PercentileWindow = 252`, **⚠ `LowVolBelow = 100/3`, `HighVolFrom = 200/3`** (Amendment 15: "one third" and "two thirds" of 0 to 100, exactly: the doubles `100.0/3` and `200.0/3`). *Approved wording (open question 3): "LOW below 33.3, HIGH from 66.7".*
- **`index_trend`** on day d: `sma = SMA(index close, 50)`, `slope = sma[d] / sma[d−5] − 1`; **+1** when close > sma, slope > 0 and ADX(14) ≥ 20; **−1** when close < sma, slope < 0 and ADX ≥ 20; else **0** (range). Not ready until the sma, the slope and the ADX exist (day 55 with the defaults).
- **`index_adx`** Wilder ADX(14) of the index's daily high / low / close (same definition as the `adx` feature).
- **Percentile rank** of the newest value among the last `PercentileWindow` values: `100 × (#below + 0.5 × #equal) / window`, where `#equal` counts the newest value itself (mid-rank for ties). Not defined until the window is full. The counts are integers compared exactly.
- **`index_vol_pct`** the percentile rank of `ATR(14) / close × 100` of the index among the last `PercentileWindow` ATR values (current day included). The ATR exists from day 14, so the first full window is day 14 + 252 − 1 = **265**.
- **`vix`** the VIX close of day d. **`vix_chg`** percent change of the VIX close against the VIX close of the previous day *of the context*; not ready when that day has no VIX or d is the first day (a VIX of 0 is refused by validation, so there is no division by zero).
- **⚠ `vix_pct`** (Amendment 1) the percentile rank of the VIX close of day d among the last `PercentileWindow` days **that have a VIX** (a day without a VIX is not in the window and is never filled). A day without a VIX has no `vix_pct` on its own date; the history before a gap is kept, so the next day with a VIX is ready again if `PercentileWindow` such days exist. Not ready until `PercentileWindow` days with a VIX exist (252 with the defaults, counted from the first day that has one). *Approved wording: "over `PercentileWindow` days (not ready until the window is full; a missing day makes that value not ready, it is never filled)".*
- **`breadth`** the value the day record carries (percent, 0..100, "tracked stocks above their 20-day average"); not ready when the record has none.
- **⚠ `regime_vol`** (Amendments 2 and 15) from the volatility score: the mean of `vix_pct` and `index_vol_pct` **when `vix_pct` is ready**, and `index_vol_pct` **alone on any day where `vix_pct` is not ready** — the day has no VIX, or fewer than `PercentileWindow` days with a VIX exist yet. Score below `LowVolBelow` → 0 (LOW), from `HighVolFrom` → 2 (HIGH), else 1 (NORMAL); a score of exactly one third is NORMAL and of exactly two thirds HIGH (the score is computed from the integer ranks with one division, so it is the double nearest to the true fraction and equals `100.0/3` and `200.0/3` when it is a third or two thirds). Not ready exactly while `index_vol_pct` is not (day 265 with the defaults). *Approved wording: "the mean of `vix_pct` and `index_vol_pct` (just `index_vol_pct` when the VIX is missing on that day)"; with it, `regime_vol` would stay not ready on a day that has a VIX but fewer than 252 of them.*
- **`regime_trend`** = `index_trend`. **`regime`** = `(1 − regime_trend) × 3 + regime_vol + 1` (UP_LOW_VOL = 1 … DOWN_HIGH_VOL = 9). Not ready unless both parts are: `max(55, 265)` = 265 days with the defaults (Amendment 4).
- **Warm-up of the date-driven names is counted in days of the context, not in bars** (Amendment 4): 1 (`day_of_week`, `is_expiry_day`, `vix`, `breadth`), 2 (`vix_chg`), 28 (`index_adx`), 55 (`index_trend`, `regime_trend`), 252 (`vix_pct`), 265 (`index_vol_pct`, `regime_vol`, `regime`) with the default options, for a context with no gaps.
- **`day_of_week`** ISO weekday of the India date of the step (Monday 1 … Sunday 7); ready from the first step. **`is_expiry_day`** 1 / 0 as above.
- **Reading the context.** At the first accepted step of an India date `D` the context lane takes the values of the latest context day strictly before `D`; the values stay the same for the whole date. History (`At`) is written once per date, so `At(h, 1)` is the value the previous session of the frame had. **⚠ Past the last day of the context** (the spec was silent; Amendment 16) a date reads the last day however far away it is, and the features stay ready: the frame does not expose the date the values belong to, so the caller must extend the context and check its age (ADR 0033, known risk for P3-01d and P7).
- **⚠ The cursor** (Amendment 5): `TryAsOf(date, out values)` is a binary search (O(log n)) that writes nothing, so one context is shared by any number of frames; each `ContextFeature` calls an internal overload with a position of its own (`ref cursorHint`), which costs O(1) amortised for increasing dates (at most 3 dates looked at per step, tested) and falls back to the search for a jump. *Approved wording (criterion 13): "AsOf is O(1) amortised for increasing dates (a cached cursor) and O(log n) for a jump".*
- **Ordering:** context days must be fed in strictly increasing date order (`Append` returns an error otherwise); dates may have gaps (weekends, holidays), which count as no day, not as a zero. `Append` is single-writer (one thread at a time) and readers may run meanwhile: a day is read only once it is complete.

## Acceptance criteria
**Weekly / monthly timeframes**
1. For seeded multi-week and multi-month 1-minute series (with a holiday, a half-missing day, and a Saturday special session) the `TimeframeBuilder` for W1 and Mo1 produces exactly the buckets of an **independent batch grouping** in the test project (OHLC, volume, `Bars`, `Complete`, bucket `ts`), with a calendar and without; the sum of the minutes of the emitted bars equals the minutes pushed except for the open period.
2. With a calendar the bar is emitted at the step that closes the last session (holiday on Friday: Thursday; month ending on a weekend: the last weekday; Saturday special session: Saturday); without a calendar it is emitted at the first bar of the next period and never earlier — a test pins both and the "ignored, counted" case of a special session the calendar did not know.
3. `FeatureFrame` with features on W1 and Mo1: `BarClosed`, `ClosedBarsAt`, `TryLastClosed`, `At` (history in weekly / monthly bars), the raw features, `Reset`, the ordering guard and the zero-allocation rule hold exactly as for the other timeframes (the P3-01 / P3-01b test families extended to W1 and Mo1, including a frame with 1m, 1h, 1D, 1W and 1M lanes together).
4. Look-ahead: a weekly feature at a step inside week N reads the bars of weeks < N only (prefix property over ≥ 200 seeds, extended to W1 and Mo1); `tf("1W").close` at any minute of week N before its closing step is the close of week N−1 (exception: at the closing step itself — with a calendar, the step that closes the last session of week N — it already shows week N; the look-ahead test checks the steps before it and checks that this step does show the closed week).
5. The catalog accepts W1 / Mo1 for the continuous indicators with the right declared warm-up (the consistency test measures the first-ready bar on W1 and Mo1) and refuses `vwap`, `or_high`, `or_low`, `prev_day_*`, `today_*` and `gap_pct` on them with an error that names the feature and the timeframe.

**Calendar features**
6. `day_of_week` equals the independent weekday computation for every day of 2026–2030 (including the Saturday special session fixture); it changes at the first step of a new India date and not at midnight UTC.
7. `is_expiry_day` is 1 exactly on the dates of the set (tested with a set containing a Tuesday, a Thursday-before-holiday and a date with no bars); `Create` refuses it when `ExpiryDays` is null.

**Market context**
8. Every derived value of `MarketContext` equals an independent batch reference (plain loops in the test project) on ≥ 24 seeded random daily series within 1e-9, with the stated warm-up; hand-calculated examples (≤ 12 days with small periods) pin the trend rule in each direction and the range case, the percentile mid-rank with ties, the volatility terciles, the VIX-missing fallback, and the nine regime codes.
9. **No look-ahead, by construction and by test:** (a) `AsOf(D)` never returns a value computed from day D or later (a day record dated `D` is present in the context and the frame at any minute of `D` still sees `D−1`); (b) the prefix property: values of the first k days are identical when the series is cut after k days (≥ 200 seeds); (c) a frame stepped over a whole day gives the same context features at 09:15 and at 15:29.
10. Frame semantics for the 12 new features: not ready before the context has data for a previous day; `At(h, k)` equals the value k sessions ago; `Reset` clears the lane; determinism; two frames sharing one `MarketContext` give identical values; a missing VIX day makes `vix`, `vix_chg`, `vix_pct` not ready on that date and does not stop `regime` (index-only volatility).
11. `Regime`: `Name(code)` and `Code(name)` are inverses for 1…9; unknown names return an error; the nine labels are the spellings of doc 07 (`UP_LOW_VOL`).
12. `MarketContext.Append` refuses a date that is not after the last one, a high below the low, a close outside high / low, a non-positive index price or VIX, a breadth outside 0..100 — with problem codes, not exceptions.

**Performance and structure**
13. Steady-state `Step` and `Update` allocate 0 bytes with context features and W1 / Mo1 lanes in the frame (extended allocation test, including the date-change step); building a `MarketContext` of 5,000 days takes ≤ 50 ms; `AsOf` is O(1) amortised for increasing dates (a cached cursor) and O(log n) for a jump **[⚠ as built: the cursor is in the reader, see Definitions and Amendment 5]**; benchmarks report the 20-feature frame with 6 context features (target still ≤ 1 µs per 1-minute step).
14. Architecture tests unchanged and green (no I/O, no clocks, no mutable statics, sealed types; `MarketContext` is shared read-only after build except `Append`, documented as single-writer); no new project references; no contract or migration change.
15. Docs: an ADR (next number) for the weekly rules, the context rules and the "strictly before today" reading; doc 11 §2 (56 names; sector_rs and breadth computation named as following), doc 07 §6 (the regime definition as built), doc 05 §4 (the streaming builder now reproduces 1W and 1M), doc 03 §4.1, the roadmap row of P3 (and a new roadmap line for the context data job).

## Contract (public surface, sketch)
```csharp
public sealed record ContextDay(DateOnly Date, double IndexHigh, double IndexLow, double IndexClose, double? VixClose = null, double? Breadth = null);

public sealed record ContextOptions(int TrendSmaPeriod = 50, int TrendSlopeDays = 5, int AdxPeriod = 14, double AdxMin = 20,
                                    int AtrPeriod = 14, int PercentileWindow = 252, double LowVolBelow = 100.0 / 3, double HighVolFrom = 200.0 / 3);

public sealed class MarketContext {
  public static Result<MarketContext> Create(IEnumerable<ContextDay> days, ContextOptions? options = null);
  public Result Append(ContextDay day);                       // strictly increasing dates
  public int Count { get; }
  public bool TryAsOf(DateOnly date, out ContextValues values);   // latest day strictly before `date`
}
public readonly record struct ContextValues(DateOnly Date, double IndexTrend, double IndexAdx, double IndexVolPct,
                                            double Vix, double VixPct, double VixChg, double Breadth, double RegimeTrend, double RegimeVol, double Regime);

public static class Regime { public static string Name(int code); public static Result<int> Code(string name); }

public sealed record FrameOptions(TradingCalendar? Calendar = null, int HistoryDepth = 8,
                                  MarketContext? Context = null, IReadOnlySet<DateOnly>? ExpiryDays = null);
```
**As built:** `MarketContext.TryAsOf(date, out values)` is exactly the sketch (a binary search); the cached cursor of criterion 13 is the internal overload `TryAsOf(date, ref cursorHint, out values)` used by `ContextFeature` (Amendment 5). `MarketContext` also has `Options`, the problem-code constants and (Amendment 9) a few more members; `ContextOptions` has no public `MaxPeriod`.

`TimeframeBuilder` and `FeatureFrame` keep their signatures; `Timeframe.W1` / `Mo1` are new accepted values. The feature specs of the context names take no parameters and a timeframe of `M1` (the default; any other is refused: they change once per date).

## Non-functional
- Performance: see criterion 13; the weekly / monthly builders add no allocation and O(1) work per bar.
- Security / privacy: no real market data in tests (seeded synthetic series only); no personal data; nothing is read from `data/`.
- Observability: none beyond the existing counters (`IgnoredBars`, `IncompleteBarsConsumed`); the late "special session ignored" case is counted by the builder as for 1D.

## Test plan
Unit only (Indicators.Tests): references (weekly / monthly grouping; context values), hand examples, prefix and frame-semantics families extended, catalog consistency on W1 and Mo1, determinism and reset, allocation, benchmarks. Architecture tests. No integration tests, no UI. Edge cases: a week with one session, a holiday-only week (no bar), month boundaries inside a week, year end, a leap-year February, a Saturday special session, a context day missing the VIX, the first 55 days (not ready), a context longer than the frame's range, a frame that starts mid-week (the first partial week is complete when all its days are, and incomplete only when one of its days is; Amendment 7 ⚠).

## Open questions (all answered 2026-10-16: recommendations approved)
1. **Daily context, "as of the previous session".** The regime of doc 07 §6 is a daily label, so every context feature changes once per date and a strategy at 10:30 sees yesterday's close of the index and VIX. Intraday index features (index vs its own VWAP, move since the open) are a possible later addition. **Recommendation:** daily only now. OK?
2. **Trend rule.** `index_trend` = close above / below the 50-day average, the average rising / falling over 5 days, and ADX(14) ≥ 20, else range (parameters in `ContextOptions`, later in the configuration). Doc 07 says only "DMA slope + ADX". **Recommendation:** as written. OK, or other numbers?
3. **Volatility rule.** The mean of the VIX percentile and the index ATR-percent percentile over 252 days, split in terciles (LOW below 33.3, HIGH from 66.7). Doc 07 says "VIX and ATR percentiles" and "LOW / NORMAL / HIGH". **Recommendation:** as written (VIX missing → ATR alone). OK?
4. **`sector_rs(n)` and the computation of breadth** wait for the **context data job** (the writer of `market.context_daily`: index and VIX bars from Dhan, breadth and sector composites from the tracked stocks' daily bars). **Recommendation:** add it to the roadmap as its own story, placed before the simulator needs `by_regime` (P3-03 / P3-04), and keep `breadth` here as a plain field of the day record. OK, or do you want the job in this slice?
5. **Expiry days.** NSE changed its expiry weekdays more than once, so the library does not guess: the caller passes the dates (from the F&O contracts of the instrument master). **Recommendation:** as written; the simulator story supplies them. OK?
6. **Weekly bar without a calendar** is emitted at the first bar of the next week (late, never early); with the calendar it is emitted at the close of the last session of the week. **Recommendation:** as written; the simulator and the live path always pass the calendar. OK?
7. **One regime index and the VIX only** (Nifty 50 by default); Bank Nifty and sector indices are not part of the regime. OK?

## Amendments (2026-10-16, from the build)
Deviations of the build from the text the owner approved; the guarantees of the acceptance criteria hold. Details and reasons are in [ADR 0033](../adr/0033-weekly-monthly-timeframes-calendar-features-and-the-market-context.md) ("Consequences from the build" and "Amendments"). The Definitions above state the rules as built; this section is the record.

**Amendments 1, 2, 5 and 7 change text that the owner approved; Amendment 15 clarifies a number of it and Amendment 16 records a behaviour it left open (all six marked ⚠ *needs the owner's confirmation*).** The owner confirms them or they are reverted; the others add behaviour the spec left open or record how it was built, and change no approved definition. Amendment 3 (`vix_chg`), 4 (`regime` warm-up), 6 and 8 to 11 are clarifications or additions; 12 to 14 are the record of tests, performance and the review round.
1. ⚠ *Needs the owner's confirmation.* **`vix_pct` window.** The window holds the last `PercentileWindow` days *that have a VIX* (the spec: a missing day makes the value not ready and is never filled). A gap day reads not-ready on its own date and the history before it is not erased.
2. ⚠ *Needs the owner's confirmation.* **`regime_vol` fallback.** The index percentile alone is used on **any** day where `vix_pct` is not ready (the spec: when the VIX is missing on that day), also while the VIX history is shorter than the window. `regime_vol` is not ready exactly while `index_vol_pct` is (first ready at day 14 + 252 − 1 = 265).
3. **`vix_chg`** compares with the previous day of the context and is not ready when that day has no VIX; the spec's "or 0" cannot occur because a VIX of 0 is refused by validation.
4. **`regime` warm-up** is `max(trend, volatility)` (265 with the defaults); the date-driven warm-ups are counted in **days of the context**: 1 (`day_of_week`, `is_expiry_day`, `vix`, `breadth`), 2 (`vix_chg`), 28 (`index_adx`), 55 (`index_trend`, `regime_trend`), 252 (`vix_pct`), 265 (`index_vol_pct`, `regime_vol`, `regime`).
5. ⚠ *Needs the owner's confirmation* (criterion 13 and the contract sketch). **The cursor lives in the reader.** The public `TryAsOf(date, out values)` is a binary search that writes nothing (so one context is shared by any number of frames); the "cached cursor" of criterion 13 is an internal overload `TryAsOf(date, ref cursorHint, out values)` that each `ContextFeature` calls with its own position. A test proves the answer from arbitrary hints, and — through an internal overload that also returns how many dates the read looked at — that the walk looks at no more than 3 dates per step for a context of 60 and one of 5,000 days (a search needs 6 and 12 or more), and that a jump forward or back is a search.
6. **`Regime.Name(int)`** throws `ArgumentOutOfRangeException` outside 1 to 9; `Regime.Code(string)` returns `Result<int>` (`indicators.unknown_regime`, exact and case-sensitive).
7. ⚠ *Needs the owner's confirmation* (the spec's test plan contradicted its scope: `Complete` = every day with bars is complete; the build follows the scope). **A stream that starts mid-week** (test plan): the first partial week is complete when all its days are; it is incomplete only when one of its days is (doc 05 §4: `complete` = every day that has bars is complete), not "incomplete as 1D today".
8. **Three cases the spec left open** for the weekly and monthly builders: a last session whose final minute never arrives (the next bar of a later date of the period closes it and is counted in `Ignored` once), a calendar session without bars (the period closes at the first bar of the next period), a calendar that disagrees with the data (Friday a holiday in the calendar but present in the data: the week closes after Thursday and the Friday bars are ignored and counted).
9. **New public API** beyond the contract sketch: `IndicatorInfo.Supports(Timeframe)`, `IndicatorInfo.SupportedTimeframes`, `IndicatorInfo.DateDriven`, `ContextFeature`, `ContextFeatureKind`, `MarketContext.Options`, the eight problem-code constants of `MarketContext` and `Regime.UnknownNameCode`. The options are validated in `Create` (`indicators.context_options_invalid`, periods 1 to 10,000); a null day or sequence is `indicators.context_day_missing`.
10. **Date-driven features** (criterion 10 and the frame): a frame with one always has a 1m lane, so `BarClosed(M1)` works for it; `ExpiryDays` is copied into a `FrozenSet` at `Create`; a date outside the `DateOnly` range makes the context and expiry features not ready.
11. **Catalog.** `IsSupported` accepts W1 and Mo1; `BarsPerSession` is 1 for a calendar timeframe; a name refused on a timeframe gets an error on `specs[i].timeframe` that names the feature and the timeframe (criterion 5), the date-driven ones a message of their own ("changes once per date; it can only be asked for on 1m").
12. **Existing tests changed** (they asserted W1 / Mo1 were refused or looped over W1 on short series): `TimeframeBuilderTests`, `FeatureFrameTests`, `FrameSemanticsTests`, `PrefixPropertyTests`, the history, determinism and ordering-guard tests, `FrameClosedFlagsTests`, `IndicatorCatalogTests`, `AllocationTests`; their checks are the same, the loops cover 1m to 1D and W1 / Mo1 have theories of their own on long series.
13. **Performance (met).** 28 features on 1m / 1h / 1D / 1W / 1M: 0.057–0.061 µs per step; the 20-feature frame plus 6 context features 0.085–0.093 µs (target 1 µs; the figures carry noise: in one run the larger frame measured faster); `MarketContext.Create` of 5,000 days 11.6–11.9 ms (target 50 ms). The 1W and 1M builders run at 31–111 M bars/s on the same binary (two modes, a factor of 3 or more between runs, cause not found: roadmap issues log ISS-11); the benchmark `Timeframe_builders_bars_per_second` only reports (it has no assertion: it is explicit and noisy), and the recorded figures are all above 10 M bars/s. 4,275 tests in `XDrishti.Indicators.Tests` (8 explicit benchmarks), 111 architecture tests.
14. **Review round (2026-10-16, minors M1 to M20).** Changes after the review, none of which changes an approved definition: the weekly and monthly builders (and the day builders with a calendar) never throw for a bar outside the range of `DateOnly` — month and week bounds are integers, a calendar knows no session on such a date and the period waits for a later one (M1, M12); `MarketContext.Append` writes the day before it counts it and grows the storage by copy, so readers may run during the single writer's `Append` (M6); a context that is not extended keeps its last day's values and the frame does not say how old they are — a documented known risk for P3-01d and P7 (M7); the volatility score is computed from integer ranks with one division, so exactly one third is NORMAL and exactly two thirds HIGH, as the definition says (M4); `ContextOptions.MaxPeriod`, a public constant that Amendment 9 did not list, is gone (the limit is internal and shared with the indicator parameters) (M17); `IndicatorInfo.SupportedTimeframes` is built in the constructor (M20); the internal `TryAsOf(date, ref hint, out values, out datesRead)` exists so that the walk can be tested (M8). Tests, documents and the closing of the findings are in ADR 0033, Amendment 12.
15. ⚠ *Needs the owner's confirmation* (a clarification of a number the owner approved; ADR 0033, Amendment 13). **The volatility thresholds are exact thirds.** Open question 3 said "LOW below 33.3, HIGH from 66.7"; the contract sketch approved with it shows `LowVolBelow = 100.0 / 3` and `HighVolFrom = 200.0 / 3`, and the build uses exactly those doubles, not 33.3 and 66.7. The two readings differ only for a score from 33.3 up to, not including, one third (LOW as built, NORMAL under the literal 33.3) and from two thirds up to, not including, 66.7 (HIGH as built, NORMAL under the literal 66.7); **a score of exactly two thirds is HIGH as built and NORMAL under the literal 66.7**; exactly one third is NORMAL under both.
16. ⚠ *Needs the owner's confirmation* (the spec was silent; ADR 0033, Amendment 14 and *Threading and the end of the context*). **Past the last day of the context.** A date after the last context day reads that day, however far away it is, and the context features stay ready; the frame does not pass on the date the values belong to, so a context that is not extended goes stale without a signal unless the caller checks its age. The alternative the owner may prefer, a maximum age in `ContextOptions` after which the context features are not ready, needs a decision on how old is too old and is not built. P3-01d and P7 must make a stale context visible.

## Follow-ups from the review (minors)
Findings of the P3-01c review that were deliberately not fixed in this slice; each is a candidate for the next slice that touches the area (M7 with the context data job P3-01d, M10 with the next change of the period builders, M18 **before** the parser P3-02 depends on the public names, M19 with the next change of the bucket rules). The finding M11 was informational (no action).
- **M7 — surface the age of the last context day (P3-01d, P7).** Past the last day of the context the frame keeps that day's values and stays ready (Amendment 16). The context data job (P3-01d) must show the age of the last day and raise an attention item when it is stale, and the market gate (P7) must read it; or `ContextOptions` gets a maximum age. Until then the caller must extend the context and check its age.
- **M10 — benchmark noise and resolution.** `Timeframe_builders_bars_per_second` has no assertion (it is explicit and only reports; the recorded figures are all above 10 M bars/s), and the harness is bimodal (a fast and a slow mode, 3x apart, on the same binary): a regression of `WeekMonthBuilder.Push` to a third of its fast speed, or far below it, passes unnoticed, and "the date lane adds nothing measurable" is the limit of the harness, not a measurement. Add an assertion with a lower bound near the slow mode, or find the cause of the two modes (tiered JIT or PGO, or the zeroing of the `out ClosedBar` results through three `Push` layers) before quoting figures. Open in the roadmap issues log as ISS-11.
- **M18 — naming of the date-driven family.** `ContextFeature` and `ContextFeatureKind` are public and also hold `Weekday` and `ExpiryDay`, which read no context; the catalog and `IndicatorInfo` call the same family `DateDriven` / `DateFeature`, and `FeatureFrame.Create` decides what each feature needs by switching on the kind (`ContextFeature.ReadsContext`, `kind == ExpiryDay`), with date-lane-only members on `Lane`. A thirteenth date feature (say `days_to_results`, which needs a results calendar) means editing `FeatureFrame.Create`, `Lane` and `ReadsContext`. **Before P3-02 depends on the public names** (the parser would bake `ContextFeature` into its code and tests, which makes a later rename a breaking change): rename `ContextFeature` / `ContextFeatureKind` to `DateFeature` / `DateFeatureKind`, and move the "needs context" / "needs expiry days" flags onto `IndicatorInfo` so that `Create` loops over them.
- **M19 — `TimeframeBuilder` is two classes in one for 1W and 1M.** For W1 and Mo1 it keeps its intraday fields unused and hands the first line of `Push` to a `WeekMonthBuilder`, which owns a `TimeframeBuilder(D1)`; a mode flag (`_periods is not null`) and an `Ignored` that is read from two sources (diffed by hand in `WeekMonthBuilder.Push`) are the price, and a frame with 1D, 1W and 1M lanes runs three day builders. A small internal bucket-builder interface with a factory behind the public `TimeframeBuilder`, or one shared day builder per frame feeding the 1W and 1M lanes, would remove the flag. Not urgent: the tests pin the behaviour and the cost is under 0.1 µs per step.
