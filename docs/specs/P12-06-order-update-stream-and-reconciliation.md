# P12-06 — Order update stream, crash recovery and reconciliation

- **Status:** Approved (2026-10-30, owner's standing run instruction; decisions below were researched under the owner's delegation 2026-10-24; build decisions in "Amendments at build" and [ADR 0162](../adr/0162-reconciliation-only-makes-the-system-stricter-the-poll-is-the-truth-the-stream-is-the-speed-and-nothing-here-can-send-or-open-a-socket.md))
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P12 — Execution service, slice P12-06 of [P12-00](P12-00-execution-service-plan.md); the measure behind the exit criterion "30 semi-auto sessions with zero reconciliation breaks"
- **Design refs:** docs/design/20-extended-modules.md §A ("order-update stream + end-of-day reconciliation; mismatch ⇒ freeze") · 05-data-and-brokers.md §8.3 (trade history and ledger sync, ISS-01) · 19 §4 (runaway orders, "reconciliation freeze"), §2 · 12-portfolio-monitoring.md (auto-journal) · ADR 0025, 0029, 0093 (journal reads stored fills), ADR 0083. **Design change:** doc 20 §A gains the break table below. ADR: next free ADR number at build time.
- **Needs:** P12-01, P12-02 (reads, FakeBroker), P12-04 (alarms, protection reads the same book), P12-05 (`freeze`). Feeds P12-03 (`streams_alive`), P12-07 (demotion), P12-10, P12-12.
- **Size:** 3 agent-days
- **Safety:** yes. It is the layer that decides whether our picture of the money is true, and it freezes an account when it is not. It sends nothing except reads and, in recovery, the lookups of P12-02; it never edits the event log or the projection to make a number agree.
- **Verified in the repo (2026-10-24):** existing trade-history reads are `GET /v2/trades/{from}/{to}/{page}` in Infrastructure (ADR 0025) and ISS-01 in the roadmap says Dhan's history API misses whole days; the order-update socket is not in `BrokerEndpoints`.

## Goal
Know, within seconds, what the broker did with every order, rebuild the truth after any crash before acting, and compare our orders, fills and positions with the broker's continuously and at the end of the day. Any disagreement freezes that account and is shown to the owner; nothing is corrected automatically.

## Scope
**In:** `Dhan/DhanOrderUpdateStream` (WebSocket client, tolerant parser, reconnect), `Application/UpdateApplier`, `PollLoop`, `GapFill`, `RecoveryService`, `Reconciler` (pure `Compare`), `BreakService`; tables `recon_runs`, `recon_breaks`, `break_resolutions`; command `resolve_break`; endpoints `GET /api/execution/reconciliation`, `GET /api/execution/stream`, `POST /api/execution/breaks/{id}/resolve`; FakeBroker stream and drift faults; settings.
**Out:** fixing a break (the owner does it in Dhan or by an explicit resolution), screens (P12-10), the portfolio sync (P2) and journal (P7-14), which keep reading the broker's trades themselves.

## The rules
**Two fill sources.** The stream gives per-order cumulative `TradedQty` and `AvgTradedPrice` quickly; it carries no trade ids. The trade book (`GET /v2/trades`) carries `exchangeTradeId`. So the leg's `filled_qty`/`avg_price` (from the stream, provisional) drives protection and the position at once; the authoritative rows of `execution.fills` come only from the trade book within one poll (5 s), and reconciliation checks that the two agree.
**Poll is the ground truth.** Order book and trade book every `exec.poll_busy_s` (5) while a position or a working order exists, every `exec.poll_idle_s` (30) during the session otherwise, none outside it except the closing runs; positions every 30 s while exposed; one poll after every reconnect and before `streams_alive` is true again (gap fill).
**Stream.** `wss://api-order-update.dhan.co`, login message `{"LoginReq":{"MsgCode":42,"ClientId":"…","Token":"…"},"UserType":"SELF"}`; messages `{"Data":{…},"Type":"order_alert"}`; fields mapped: `OrderNo`/`CorrelationId` (to our leg), `Status` TRANSIT|PENDING|REJECTED|CANCELLED|TRADED|EXPIRED (a part fill is `TradedQty` below `Quantity` with PENDING), `LegNo` 1 entry 2 stop 3 target, `TxnType` B|S, `Product` C|I|M|F|V|B, `OrderType` LMT|MKT|SL|SLM, `Price`, `TriggerPrice`, `AvgTradedPrice`, `ExchOrderNo`, `AlgoId`, `Source` (P = API), `LastUpdatedTime`. No heartbeat is documented: silence is not an error; the poll decides. Reconnect backoff 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 30 s (cap `exec.stream_backoff_max_s`), jitter 20%.
**Dedup and order.** An update is applied once per key (OrderNo, Status, TradedQty, LastUpdatedTime); a lower rank than the leg's is ignored and counted (P12-01 rank rule); an update for an `XD` correlation id with no leg is a break; one with no `XD` prefix is foreign.
**Recovery on start.** The host starts `recovering` and sends nothing. Steps: (1) read active halts; (2) load non-terminal intents and legs; (3) for every leg in `new` or `sent` without an outcome, look it up by correlation id twice, 2 s apart (P12-02) and adopt or fail it; (4) read order book, trade book, positions and funds, apply the difference as events with source `recovery`; (5) run a reconciliation of kind `recovery`; (6) accounts with zero breaks and no `Unknown` leg become ready; accounts with a break are frozen; the host leaves `recovering` when every account is ready or frozen. Protective repair and the flatten of a filled position (P12-04) are allowed after step 4 **on every account, including a frozen one**, guarded by the fresh broker read of step 4 (P12-04 Authority); tightening, cancels and entries are not. If the broker cannot be read within `exec.recovery_max_s` (60) the host stays `recovering` and retries every 10 s; alarm `recovery_slow` after 120 s. The broker-side stops are live meanwhile.
**Reconciliation.** Kinds: `continuous` every `exec.recon_interval_s` (60) while exposed, `eod` at 15:45 (after square-off) and `final` at 16:30, `recovery`. It compares, per account: (a) broker orders against our legs by correlation id: status class and quantities, with `exec.recon_grace_s` (30) for in-flight differences; (b) broker trades against `fills` by `exchangeTradeId`: quantity exact, price equal to 4 decimals; (c) broker positions (`netQty`, `buyQty`, `sellQty`, `productType`) against our position book plus **explained foreign activity** (trades or orders without our prefix are added to the expected position and listed); (d) every broker order with our `XD` prefix has a leg, and every stop or target leg listed under one of our super orders (`legDetails`) is matched by (parent order, `LegNo`, leg order id) before anything is called unknown, so the broker-created legs never produce a false `unknown_xd_order`.
**Breaks.** `position_mismatch` (net differs after explanation), `trade_missing` (broker trade for our order not in fills after two polls), `trade_extra` (fill we hold the broker does not), `order_mismatch` (status or quantity differs beyond the grace), `fill_mismatch` (quantity or price), `unknown_xd_order` (our prefix at the broker with no leg: a lost write-ahead or a second user of the prefix), `foreign_conflict` (a foreign working order or position in an instrument we manage), `unavailable` (the broker could not be read for a run that was due). All but `foreign_conflict` ask for `freeze` (P12-05); `foreign_conflict` asks for `entries` and a CRITICAL alarm. Every break is a row; a run with a break is not clean. **No automatic correction, ever.**
**Resolution.** The owner (fresh password) chooses for a break: `accept_broker` or `dismiss_foreign` (for a foreign item: remember it as explained for the day). `xd-api` verifies the password and inserts the `break_resolutions` row itself as `xd_app` (an owner-act table written like `mode_trail`, with the trigger `execution.check_break_resolution`: the break exists and is unresolved, the actor starts `owner:`, `dismiss_foreign` only for a foreign kind); `xd_exec` has no INSERT on it. For `accept_broker`, `xd-exec` sees the new row (poll or `LISTEN`) and **appends** events with source `owner` that adopt the broker's figure: nothing is rewritten, the break stays in the history. The freeze can be released (P12-05) only when every break of the account has a resolution row.
**Fills reach the journal through the existing path, and a gap is detected.** The execution never writes `portfolio.transactions` or the P11 live tables. The broker's trades of an execution account enter the portfolio through the account sync already built (P2-13, trade history, daily) and, for the live stage, through P11-01's capture of the order and trade book; P7-14 (auto-journal) then links them to approved tickets by account, instrument, side and day (ADR 0093), and P11-02 grades them. After the 16:30 `final` run, a cross-check compares our `fills` of the day with the stored `portfolio.transactions` (and P11-01's `live_fills` where present: UNVERIFIED column names) by account, instrument, side, quantity, price and day; a fill with no stored trade is the alarm `journal_gap` (warn, not a break, not a freeze), because ISS-01 says the history API misses days; the owner can then import the broker's report (P2-17). Fills of `assisted` accounts have no execution fills; their path is P11-01 unchanged.
**EOD.** `closed>reconciled` is written when the `eod` run is clean for that intent (its orders, trades and position agree). The next morning the stored trade history (existing client) is a second witness; since ISS-01 says it misses days, a missing history day is `unavailable` evidence, never a break by itself.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Parser.** The documented sample (duplicate keys of different case, a blank value after a colon, `RefLtp`/`refLtp`) parses to one update; first non-empty value wins; unknown fields ignored; garbage is dropped, counted and triggers a poll; the login message is built exactly as above. *`OrderUpdateParserTests` with the documented sample.*
2. **Mapping.** Each Product, TxnType, OrderType, LegNo and Status code maps to the domain enum; an unknown code is an `Unreadable` update (poll), never a guess. *`UpdateMappingTests`.*
3. **Dedup and order.** Duplicated, delayed and reordered updates over 2,000 seeds give the same final leg state as the in-order stream, and a late lower status is counted and ignored; leg `filled_qty` never decreases. Negative control: an applier without rank is caught on at least 10 seeds. *`UpdateApplierPropertyTests`.*
4. **Stream plus poll.** With the stream dropped for 40 s, the poll alone keeps legs and protection correct; after reconnect one gap-fill poll runs before `streams_alive` is true; with no connection ever, `streams_alive` is true only while the poll is at most 15 s old. *`StreamPollTests` (FakeBroker, fake clock).*
5. **Fills.** A stream fill of 4 raises the leg and the position at once; the `fills` row appears with the trade book's `exchangeTradeId` within one poll; a trade-book fill that disagrees with the leg's cumulative quantity is a `fill_mismatch`. *`TwoSourceFillTests`.*
6. **Recovery.** Kill the host at each of the four points of the send protocol and in states open, filled, protected, closing; on restart (a) no duplicate order exists at the broker, (b) every leg ends adopted or failed, (c) protected positions get no new instruction, (d) the host sends nothing before step 4, (e) an unreadable broker leaves it `recovering`. 500 seeds with random crash points. *`RecoveryTests`.*
7. **Reconciliation hand cases.** For the table of section "Breaks": a clean day has zero breaks; each break kind is produced by exactly one injected drift (position changed at the broker, trade removed, trade added, status flipped past the grace, price off by 0.01, an `XD` order with no leg, a manual working order in a traded symbol); an in-flight difference inside the grace is not a break; a manual trade in an instrument we do not manage is listed as foreign and explains the position. *`ReconcilerTests`.*
8. **Freeze, never fix.** Any break except `foreign_conflict` requests `freeze` on that account only (other accounts continue); after the run, `intents`, `legs`, `fills`, `positions` are byte-identical to before it; a test hashes them. *`NoAutoFixTests`.*
9. **Resolution.** The resolution row is written by `xd_app` (never by `xd_exec`); for `accept_broker` the engine then appends adoption events; the original break stays visible, the projection equals a replay of the log including the adoption; `dismiss_foreign` is allowed only for a foreign item; both need the password and an `owner:` actor; the freeze can then be released (P12-05). *`BreakResolutionTests`.*
10. **EOD and exit measure.** The `eod` and `final` runs set `closed>reconciled`; a session counts as clean for P12-12 only if both ran, were readable, and the session had zero breaks including resolved ones; a run that could not read the broker is `unavailable`, not clean. *`CleanSessionTests`.*
11. **Reads stay inside limits.** At most 3 broker reads per second on average across polls and runs, never starving sends (own limiter, P12-02). *`ReadBudgetTests`.*
11a. **Journal gap.** A fill present in `execution.fills` and absent from the stored portfolio trades after the 16:30 run raises `journal_gap` (warn) once per fill, is not a break and does not freeze; with the trade stored it raises nothing. *`JournalGapTests`.*
11b. **Super-order legs.** The stop and target legs the broker creates under our super order are matched by (parent, `LegNo`, leg order id) and are never reported as `unknown_xd_order`; a stop leg with the right parent key but a different order id than recorded is an `order_mismatch`. *`SuperLegMatchTests`.*
12. **Endpoints.** `GET /api/execution/reconciliation?date=&accountId=` returns runs and breaks (with resolutions); `GET /api/execution/stream` returns `{connected, lastMessageAt, lastPollAt, reconnects24h, dropped, applied, ignored}`; `POST /api/execution/breaks/{id}/resolve {resolution, note, password}` (note at most 300 characters; `xd-api` writes the row itself) answers 401/423 on a bad password, 409 `execution.break_resolved`, 404 `execution.break_unknown`. *integration.*
13. `scripts/gen-api.sh` run; `scripts/check.sh` green.

## Contract
- **Data:** `execution.recon_runs(id bigint identity, at, account_id, kind check in ('continuous','eod','final','recovery'), readable bool, orders int, trades int, positions int, breaks int)`; `execution.recon_breaks(id bigint identity, run_id fk, account_id, kind check (the eight), severity check in ('warn','critical'), symbol null, ours jsonb, theirs jsonb, detail text up to 300, at)`; `execution.break_resolutions(break_id pk fk, at, by check like 'owner:%', resolution check in ('accept_broker','dismiss_foreign'), note)`. Insert-only. `recon_runs` and `recon_breaks` are written by `xd_exec`; `break_resolutions` is the owner-act table written by `xd_app` (trigger `execution.check_break_resolution`), no command is involved. Migration `AddExecutionReconciliation` with `Down`.
- **Settings added:** `exec.poll_busy_s` 5 (2..30), `exec.poll_idle_s` 30 (10..120), `exec.recon_interval_s` 60 (30..300), `exec.recon_grace_s` 30 (10..120), `exec.stream_backoff_max_s` 30 (10..120), `exec.recovery_max_s` 60 (20..300).
- **Network:** socket allowed by the P12-02 guard only for the selected environment host; the token is in the login message, never in the URL or logs.
- **Log ids** 6675 to 6699.

## Non-functional
Update to leg state under 100 ms p95 after receipt; stream-driven protection reaction within 1 s of the update; recovery end to end under 60 s on a healthy broker; a run compares 50 orders in under 50 ms. No update body is stored: only the mapped fields in event payloads (at most 4 KB), never the token, client id or `Remarks`.

## Test plan
Unit: parser, mapping, applier, reconciler tables. Properties: update ordering (2,000 seeds), recovery crash points (500), reconciliation symmetry (swapping our and their view swaps missing and extra); negative controls: an applier without rank, a reconciler that tolerates a quantity difference, a recovery that sends before reading, each caught on at least 10 seeds. Integration with Testcontainers: runs, breaks, resolutions, grants (insert-only). FakeBroker: stream faults (drop, duplicate, reorder, delay, disconnect), drift injection (position, trade, status, manual order), trade-book lag. Explicit sandbox smoke: connect the socket if the sandbox offers it (third-party sources say it does not).

## Decisions (researched)
1. **Poll is the truth, the stream is the speed.** Dhan documents the socket, its login and fields (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/order-update/, read 2026-10-24) but no heartbeat, no reconnect rule and no replay of missed messages, and its sample payload is malformed JSON (blank values, duplicate keys). A system whose protection depended on the socket alone would fail silently; polling bounds the damage to 5 s.
2. **Two fill sources, one authoritative.** The stream has cumulative quantities without trade ids; the trade book has ids (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/orders/, read 2026-10-24). Fast provisional figures for protection, exchange ids for the record and the comparison.
3. **Freeze, do not fix.** The owner's rule and doc 20 ("mismatch ⇒ freeze"). An automatic fix would hide exactly the errors this layer exists to expose. Rejected: self-healing reconciliation.
4. **Foreign activity is explained, not forbidden.** The owner trades by hand in the same Dhan app (`manual_live`, P11); manual trades without our prefix explain position differences, but a foreign working order in a symbol we manage is a conflict. Source: doc 04 §1 (one account holds several roles), P11. A dedicated execution account would remove the ambiguity (Risks).
5. **Position check uses the broker's `netQty` and day quantities.** The positions endpoint returns `netQty`, `buyQty`, `sellQty`, `productType`, `positionType` (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/portfolio/, read 2026-10-24).
6. **Recovery sends nothing until it has read the broker.** Same reason as the write-ahead rule: after a crash we know that we may have sent; we do not know what happened.
7. **Resolution by append.** An adoption event keeps the log truthful about what we believed and what we accepted. Rejected: editing the projection.
8. **A clean session is defined by rows.** P12-12 needs a number that cannot be argued: both runs readable and zero breaks; `unavailable` is not clean (the owner's rule).
9. **Trade-history API is only a second witness.** ISS-01 (roadmap): the history API misses whole days; using it as a primary source would create false breaks.

## Risks and honest limits
- The stream's field semantics (e.g. whether a part fill is PENDING or its own status) are read from one page; the poll corrects any misreading within 5 s, and the first live day is a one-share canary.
- A broker that lags its trade book more than two polls produces `trade_missing` that is not a real fault; the freeze then costs a session. Better a false freeze than a missed one.
- Sharing the account with manual trading makes `position_mismatch` more likely and the explanation rule imperfect; the owner should trade by hand in another account on execution days (recommendation, not enforced).
- Prices are compared to 4 decimals; a broker that reports averages with other rounding would need a tolerance, decided at the first real comparison.
- Reconciliation proves agreement with the broker's data, not with the exchange's; it cannot catch an error both sides share.

## Amendments at build (ADR 0162)

- **Nothing is composed.** The record composition registers `PgReconStore`, `PgReconOurSource` and `PgFillWriter` only. `BreakService`, `BreakAdoption`, `RecoveryService`, `PollService`, `UpdateApplier`, the feed and `PollProtectionSource` are composed by no host (tests pin it); `IProtectionSource`, `IHaltSource` and `IExitSendPolicy` stay the closed defaults; `IBrokerOrders` is registered nowhere; none of the new classes takes a sender, a stop port or `LegSender` (a reflection test pins it). - **Deviations from the contract, each only stricter or smaller:** (1) the resolution column is `resolved_by` (`by` is awkward in SQL), and `recon_runs`/`recon_breaks` carry `session_date`, `intent_id` and `key` (the identity of a break across runs) in addition to the listed columns; (2) no `resolve_break` command: the owner's row is written by `xd-api` and the engine's adoption is `BreakAdoption` reading the rows; (3) a `foreign_conflict` is asked for through a new halt trigger `foreign_conflict` (default level `entries`, owner release) because `reconciliation_break` is a freeze and the halt service raises a request to its trigger's level; the halt trigger count is 20 and three alarm codes are added (`recovery_slow`, `journal_gap`, `foreign_conflict`); (4) `GET /api/execution/stream` is **not built**: the status lives in the memory of `xd-exec`, the heartbeat does not carry it and nothing composes the feed; the view type `StreamStatusView` exists with the specified fields and the endpoint moves to P12-10 with the screen; (5) a break that is already open (same account and key, no resolution) is not inserted again; the run counts it and the halt request is made again, so one break is one row for the owner to resolve; (6) a resolved key is skipped by later runs of the same session day only while the broker's figure is unchanged (the key holds the figure); (7) positions are compared only in instruments we manage (any leg of the latest session); a broker position in an instrument we never touched is outside our book, while a foreign working order in a managed instrument is the conflict; (8) a broker trade of our order without an `exchangeTradeId` makes the run `unavailable`, never agreement; `DhanReads` now reads `exchangeTradeId` (UNVERIFIED name); (9) `trade_missing` needs a second sighting (the previous run's missing ids are carried), which is the spec's "after two polls" as a pure input.
- **Recovery is stricter than the written order.** The lookups of step 3 are made first (they are reads), but what they found is written only after step 4 has read the order book, trade book, positions and funds successfully; an unreadable broker changes no leg and keeps the host `recovering`; `recovery_slow` is raised once recovery has failed for 120 s (or twice `exec.recovery_max_s`, whichever is longer). The protective repair and flatten after step 4 on every account including a frozen one are P12-04's, guarded by `RecoveryReport.BrokerReadAt`; the wiring is the host's, later.
- **Freeze release.** `execution.check_halt_release` is replaced: a freeze is released only if its trigger is `reconciliation_break`, the account (any account for a system freeze) has at least one break on record and every break has a resolution. A freeze of another cause (`oversold`) stays unreleasable here. `HaltReleaseRules` gets the same facts and still answers `freeze_needs_reconciliation`.
- **Resolution.** `accept_broker` appends one `leg_update` event (source owner, the owner's actor, the break id and kind in the payload) to the intent of an intent-linked break and nothing else: legs, fills and positions keep their rows (a break without an intent, such as a position difference, is recorded by the resolution row itself); the break stays in the history; the next runs treat the key as explained for that day. `dismiss_foreign` only for `foreign_conflict` (trigger and rule). The endpoint checks `Live:Writes:Enabled` before the fresh password; the note (at most 300 characters, no secret-like words) stays in the insert-only row; the audit line carries ids and codes (log ids 6675, 6676).
