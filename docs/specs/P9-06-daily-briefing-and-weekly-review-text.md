- **Status:** Draft (written 2026-10-24; decisions below were researched under the owner's delegation; the owner's Approved is still needed before an agent builds it)
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P9 — AI assistant and Hermes PoC (`docs/design/18-roadmap.md`: "briefing"); slice of [P9-00](P9-00-ai-assistant-plan.md) (written in parallel; UNVERIFIED text).
- **Design refs:** docs/design/14-ai-assistant.md §1 (roles), §3 (guardrails), §4 feature 1, §5 (briefing eval row) · docs/design/15-smart-insights-and-automation.md §6 ("numbers always come from the report engine") · docs/design/04 §4.2 (the briefing is part of the proposed plan) · docs/design/09 §10 + P7-15 (the weekly digest) · ADR 0005 (runtime, on demand) · ADR 0094 (digest) · P8-00 D1 and D3 (evidence block, six states).
- **Needs:** P9-01 (`IChatClient`, `ILlmHealth`, `Assistant:Llm:*`), P9-03 (schema `ai`, the number tokenizer, audit of model calls), P7-09/P7-10 (plan, suggestions), P7-15 (digest), P8-06 (`GET /api/reports/insights`; optional, the weekly review states "insights unavailable" without it). Used by: P9-07/P9-08 (the guard), P9-09 (eval set B), P9-10 (Hermes cron summaries are checked by the same guard).
- **Size:** 3 agent-days (estimate: guard + tokenizer 1.0, facts and deterministic texts 0.7, handlers + services + store + migration 0.8, endpoints + card + tests 0.5).
- **Safety:** no. Text only; no write endpoint, no order, no approval. The services start paused and `Assistant:Briefing:UseModel` is false, so the model is not used until the owner turns both on.

## Goal
Every plan date has a briefing of at most 300 words and every ISO week a review text of at most 400 words that the owner can read in the plan review and the Learning digest tab. The text is written by the local model from stored facts only, and a deterministic checker rejects any text containing a number, symbol or claim not in those facts; a rejected or missing model text falls back to a fixed-template text built from the same facts. The nightly plan never waits for it.

## Scope
**In:**
- Domain `XDrishti.Domain.Assistant.Text` (pure): `BriefingFacts`, `FactNumber`, `FactSymbolScope`, `BriefingFactsBuilder` (from already-read views, no I/O), `DeterministicBriefing`, `DeterministicWeeklyReview`, `TextGuard`, `GuardRules`, `GuardFinding`, `GuardResult`, `BriefingWording` (pinned strings). The number tokenizer and normaliser are **P9-03's** (`NumberTokenizer`, `NumberNormaliser` in `Domain/Assistant/Grounding`, per its spec): this slice reuses it and adds, additively, what it may lack (Indian grouping, unicode minus, `R` suffix, `HH:MM`, ISO dates, number words) plus the matching rule with rounding, the scope rule and the symbol rule below.
- Application `XDrishti.Application.Assistant.Briefing`: ports `IBriefingFactSource`, `IBriefingWriter` (adapter over the P9-01 model port), `IBriefingStore`; handlers `BuildBriefingHandler`, `PreviewBriefingHandler`, `GetBriefingHandler`, `ListBriefingsHandler`; services `BriefingService` (id `briefing`) and `WeeklyReviewService` (id `weekly-review`); wording and logs.
- Infrastructure: `ai.briefings`, `ai.briefing_attempts`, store, migration `AddBriefings` (needs the `ai` schema from P9-03).
- Api: `GET /api/assistant/briefings/daily|weekly|preview`, `GET /api/assistant/briefings`; verb `xd assistant briefing [--kind daily|weekly] [--subject] [--model on|off] [--store]`.
- Frontend: `BriefingCard` (lazy, inside `/plans` review and the Learning digest tab; read only).
**Out:** chat answers (P9-03, P9-05); any write endpoint or "regenerate" button (the CLI makes a new version); strategy drafting (P9-07); proposals (P9-08); push or e-mail delivery (no external channel before the owner decides, P8-00 Q6); text in Hindi or any other language; a briefing for a past plan date older than 7 days (dry run only).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given the fixture plan of P7-09 (primary AAA, BBB; reserve CCC, DDD; rejected EEE, FFF) when facts are built then every ticket symbol, side, strategy, stop, target, quantity-per-account, cell trades, `pWin` and `conservativeExpectancyR` appears as a `FactNumber` or fact string, the plan's `Basis` flags are carried as `risks`, and the regime is `unavailable: market context not stored (P3-01d/e)` unless the plan carries it. The same stored inputs give byte-identical facts JSON and `facts_hash`.
2. Given those facts when the deterministic briefing is built then it opens with the plan state and date, lists all primary and reserve tickets in plan order with one line each, then risks, then system changes, ends with the pinned line, is at most 300 words and is identical on repeat. With zero tickets it says so and lists why (the plan's basis flags) instead of inventing content.
3. **Guard G1 numbers.** Every numeric token of the model text (ASCII and Indian grouping `1,23,456.50`, `₹`, `%`, a trailing `R`, signs including the unicode minus, `HH:MM`, ISO dates) must match a `FactNumber` by the rule in Decisions D2, else finding `number_not_in_facts` naming the token and its character offset. A number word (two to twenty, thirty..ninety, hundred, thousand, lakh, crore, half, double, twice) is `number_word` unless the same word is in the facts text.
4. **Guard G2 symbols.** Every token matching `^[A-Z][A-Z0-9&-]{2,}$` that is neither a fact symbol nor in `BriefingWording.AllowedAcronyms` (`NSE IST ATR VIX ORB VWAP EMA RSI SL R`) is `symbol_not_in_facts`; every primary and reserve ticket symbol must occur ("all tickets covered"): `ticket_not_covered`.
5. **Guard G3 scope.** A sentence naming exactly the tickets T1..Tk may only contain numbers from the facts of T1..Tk plus the global facts; `number_wrong_scope` otherwise (catches AAA's stop written against BBB).
6. **Guard G4 form.** At most the word limit (`max_words`), no markdown other than `- ` bullets and blank-line paragraphs, no URL, no code fence, no instruction to the reader beginning with an imperative trade verb (`buy`, `sell`, `short`, `enter`, `exit`, `hold`, `add`, `average`) except inside the quoted ticket fields, no forecast phrase (`will rise|fall|reach`, `expected to`, `likely to`, `guaranteed`, `sure`, `target price`, `should go`): `form_*` findings. These two word lists are heuristics and are published in `GuardRules` with tests; they are not a proof.
7. **Guard G5 honesty.** Every fact marked `unavailable`, `insufficient` or `not_evaluable` in the facts keeps its word when mentioned (the text may not state a number for it: the tokenizer finds none in a sentence naming that fact label); `provisional` plans must contain the word "provisional" or "not paper-proven"; the text must contain no word from `BriefingWording.NeverSay` (`profit guaranteed`, `risk-free`, `certain`).
8. **Verdict and fallback.** Given a model text that passes all guards then the stored text is the model text plus the pinned footer line, `source = model`. Given a failure on attempt 1 then attempt 2 is made with the finding codes and offending tokens appended to the request (never the rejected text re-presented as an instruction); a failure on attempt 2, a model error, a timeout, a model not ready within `ReadyWaitSeconds`, or `UseModel = false` stores the deterministic text with `source = deterministic` and `reason` in `model_off | model_unavailable | guard_rejected | facts_thin`. Both attempts are stored in `ai.briefing_attempts` with their findings.
9. **Plan independence.** Given the model service is down or the host is swapping then `nightly` and `suggestions` runs are unaffected (no shared lock, no await on the model inside them); `briefing` runs After `suggestions` as its own service and a failure is a failed service run only. It does not start a model call while service `nightly` has a running run, and not before `Assistant:Briefing:EarliestIst` (default `20:00`, India time): P9-01 measurement M3 may show that a loaded model slows the API and worker, and then P9-00 D13 keeps model work out of the nightly window (17:30 to 20:00 IST); the setting is lowered only by the owner after M3.
10. **Immutability.** One row per (kind, subject, version); the first stored wins; a repeat build with the same facts hash returns the stored row and writes nothing; `xd assistant briefing --store --new-version` appends version n+1 (owner act, actor `cli:xd`). `xd_app` can insert and select `ai.briefings` and `ai.briefing_attempts`, not update, delete or truncate (grants test).
11. **Weekly review.** Given a stored digest for week W then facts = the digest's sections (items with ids), the week's report insights (each with its `n`; each insight sentence is quoted verbatim in the facts and the model may only rephrase or shorten: every insight's `n` must reappear, G6 `evidence_dropped`), and the plan-outcome line; the text ends with `Nothing was applied: every item above is a proposal; applying any of it is the owner's own act.` added by code, and without a stored digest the review is `not_evaluable: no digest stored for <week>` (no model call).
12. **Read.** `GET /api/assistant/briefings/daily?date=` returns text, `source`, `reason`, `wordCount`, `factsHash`, `modelId`, `createdAt`, `findings` of attempts, and `evidence` (the plan id, digest id, run keys behind the facts); 404 `assistant.briefing_not_found`; `preview` computes the deterministic text for any plan date and writes nothing.
13. **UI.** The card shows the text, the label "Drafted by a local model from stored records; every number was checked against them." or "Fixed-format text; the model was not used (<reason in words>).", the word count, a "Facts used" disclosure listing the ids, and for a rejected model text a "Why the model text was not used" list of finding codes in plain words. States: loading, empty ("No briefing stored for this date"), error; text rendered as text (markup test).
14. Logs carry ids, counts, finding codes and ms (6160 to 6169, the E2 block of P9-00), never the text or a figure; `scripts/gen-api.sh` run; Architecture.Tests green (Domain pure, the model port reached only through `IBriefingWriter`).

## Non-functional
Facts build: a few small reads (plan with items, suggestions, newest digest, insights, changelog since the last briefing). Guard: linear in text length, under 5 ms for 2 KB. A model attempt is bounded by the P9-01 timeout; measured on the benchmark box a 3.8K-token prompt and a 300-word answer took about 20 s in the container (`docs/research/p0-15-llm-benchmark.md`), so two attempts plus a load fit well inside the 5-minute ready budget. Prompt at most 6000 tokens (ADR 0005 budgets 8K). Security: the facts block is data, quoted inside a fenced block the prompt names as data; free text from owners (journal notes, lesson texts) is **not** put in the facts (injection surface, see Decisions D6). No secret, account number or balance in facts (accounts appear as the account's display name only if the plan view already shows it; quantities per account are summed, UNVERIFIED which the plan view allows).

## Test plan
- **Unit (tables):** tokenizer on 40 strings (Indian grouping, `₹`, `%`, `R`, signs, `09:15`, dates, `1,00,000`, `3.50`, ranges `2-3`, `20-day`, ordinals, a number inside a symbol such as `M&M`, `NIFTY50` is one symbol token not a number); the match rule with the worked case in D2; scope rule with AAA/BBB; form lists; fallback decision table (8 rows: each reason). Determinism: same facts, same text and hash. Property: for any facts, `DeterministicBriefing` passes the guard (it is the oracle for the guard's accept side), and replacing any single numeric token by another value not in the facts fails it (1000 generated cases, FsCheck or the repo's property helper).
- **Handlers over fakes:** model good text; model text with an invented number then good on attempt 2; both bad; model throws; not ready; `UseModel` false; repeat build writes nothing; a dry run writes nothing.
- **Integration (Testcontainers):** migration up and down, grants, endpoints, a real build on the P7-09 fixture with a fake writer returning scripted texts, the nightly service unaffected while the writer blocks.
- **Frontend (MSW):** model text, fallback with each reason, empty, error, markup in the text rendered inert, no digit rendered for a fact marked unavailable (the card prints the stored text only).
- **Architecture:** the Domain types hold no I/O; only `IBriefingWriter`'s adapter references the model port; no member of `Assistant.Briefing` references a plan-writing port (no-order rule stays).

## Decisions (researched)
**D1. Who writes what.** *Answer:* facts, the fallback text and the verdict are deterministic code; the model only phrases. *Reason:* doc 14 §1, doc 15 §6 and the owner's rule "numbers only from tools". *Rejected:* letting the model call tools itself for the briefing (non-reproducible input set). *Source:* docs/design/14 §3, 15 §6.
**D2. The number rule.** *Answer:* a token passes when some `FactNumber` has the same sign and either the same normalised decimal as its printed `text`, or its `value` rounded half away from zero to the token's decimals where the token has fewer decimals than the fact's printed text (45.83 allows 45.8 and 46 but not 45.9 or 45.834); no derived numbers (sums, differences, ratios) are allowed, the facts supply every figure the text may use (including `ticket_count`, `primary_count`, totals). *Reason:* rounding is the one transformation a writer needs; allowing arithmetic would let a wrong sum pass; false precision is refused. *Rejected:* a tolerance band (hides errors of the wrong kind); only exact strings (rejects honest rounding and makes the fallback the common case). *Source:* doc 14 §5 ("No numbers absent from input"), P8-00 D3.
**D3. Attempts.** *Answer:* two attempts, the second told the finding codes and offending tokens only. *Reason:* one repair costs about 20 s and recovers the common slip; more attempts only teach the model the checker. *Rejected:* unbounded retry; asking the model to explain itself. *Source:* P0-15 benchmark timings (briefing 19 to 20 s on the container).
**D4. Greedy, non-thinking.** *Answer:* temperature 0, fixed seed, the server's thinking-off mode for `main`, `cache_prompt` off where the client allows. *Reason:* reproducibility for the eval; llama.cpp states cache reuse can change results because logits are not bit-identical across batch sizes (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/master/tools/server/README.md, read 2026-10-24); the benchmark ran thinking off and `cache_prompt` off. The Qwen3 card warns against greedy decoding **in thinking mode** (https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B, read 2026-10-24), so greedy is only used with thinking disabled. gpt-oss-20b is a reasoning model whose reasoning tokens consume `max_tokens` (benchmark footnote): if it becomes the pick the cap rises and the guard reads the final channel only (reasoning is never checked or stored as text). *Rejected:* temperature 0.6 with thinking (not reproducible; the owner reads one sample). *Source:* ADR 0005, p0-15.
**D5. The service and its default.** *Answer:* `UseModel` false and both services paused. *Reason:* ADR 0005 starts `xd-llm` on demand because the host swaps heavily; starting it nightly is the owner's decision; the deterministic text is useful by itself. *Rejected:* model on by default. *Source:* ADR 0005, ADR 0092/0094 (paused-service pattern).
**D6. Inputs that never enter the prompt.** *Answer:* only structured, system-produced strings and numbers (plan fields, reason codes, insight sentences from the report engine, digest item texts); never owner-typed notes, journal text, lesson free text or broker data. *Reason:* the prompt-injection surface of a briefing must be nil; the injection probe belongs to the chat and Hermes (P9-10) where a tool can return such text. *Rejected:* including journal notes "for colour". *Source:* docs/design/14 §6 (injection risk), P7-14.
**D7. Where it lives.** *Answer:* the weekly review is a separate text from the P7-15 digest (which stays the source and is never changed); the briefing is a separate row from the plan (the plan's basis/limits fields are not edited). *Reason:* the plan and digest are insert-only owner-facing records with their own checks. *Rejected:* a column on the plan.
**D8. One definition of a number.** *Answer:* P9-03 owns the tokenizer (built first); `TextGuard` here is the second layer (facts, rounding, scope, symbols) and is what P9-07/P9-08 call for explanations and reasons and P9-09 calls for set B. Chat answers keep P9-03's own check (tool results as facts). *Reason:* one definition of "a number in the output". *Rejected:* a second, drifting tokenizer; the builder of P9-06 reads P9-03's first and extends it additively.
**D9. UI position.** *Answer:* the card sits in `/plans` review and the digest tab, not on Today. *Reason:* the always-fetched Today chunk spends the first-load budget (frontend/CLAUDE.md, ADR 0098). *Source:* P8-00 D7.

## Risks and honest limits
- The guard proves that numbers and symbols come from the facts and that each sentence mixes no ticket's figures. It cannot prove a sentence is *true* or well judged ("AAA looks strong" has no number). Word lists for advice and forecasts are heuristics; a model can phrase a forecast without a listed word. The fixed footer and the label say this.
- False positives are the price of strictness: a model that writes "20-day" or "two tickets" falls back. If the eval (P9-09 set B) shows more than 2 of 10 first attempts rejected, the prompt (not the guard) changes.
- Greedy decoding is not a determinism guarantee on a GPU; the text is stored once, so the owner sees one stable text per date.
- The regime line is `unavailable` until P3-01d/e; the briefing says so rather than guess. Plans are provisional (P7-00 Q7); every briefing says "simulated on plan prices" where a grade figure is quoted.
- Weekly insights need P8-06; without them the review quotes the digest only and says insights are unavailable.
