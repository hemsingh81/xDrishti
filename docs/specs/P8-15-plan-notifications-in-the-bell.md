# P8-15 — Plan notifications in the bell (read only, in-app)

- **Status:** Draft (written 2026-10-24; decisions below were researched under the owner's delegation; the owner's Approved is still needed before an agent builds it)
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P8 — Reports & UI, slice P8-15 of [P8-00](P8-00-reports-and-ui-plan.md) (P7-10 item 14, P7-11 item 14, P7-14 item 10, P7-17 item 13: "a notification before the cutoff", "plan events in the bell")
- **Design refs:** docs/design/13-reports-and-ui.md "Notifications and pop-ups (P2-16)" · docs/design/19-risks-and-compliance.md §4 (plan not reviewed in time: mitigation "phone review via PWA", not available) · docs/design/15 §1 · ADR 0030 (notices are read-only, derived on read; `Resolved`, `Since`, route/command actions), 0027, 0018 §7 (service notices), 0095 (cutoff), 0096 (pre-open), 0086 (plan runs), 0097 · P8-00 D6, Q6
- **Needs:** P7 (built). Absorbs the ticket notices of **P7-20** (`GET /api/plans/ticket-notices`: price-crossing alerts and post-trade prompts; Draft, written in parallel; relied on by its id and the contract it states): P8-15 owns the **one bell source** for the plan module, `GET /api/plans/notices`, and carries P7-20's items in it (Decisions 10). No P8 slice.
- **Size:** 3 agent-days (backend notices 1.3, bell integration 0.7, pop-up and tests 1)
- **Safety:** no. Read-only notices derived on read, in-app only: no push (P8-00 Q6 stays "not in P8"), no external channel, no command button added by this slice (route actions only), nothing cached offline, nothing written by a read. The notices cannot approve, arm, lapse or order anything. (P7-20, whose items are carried here, is itself Safety: yes and default OFF behind `Planning:TicketAlerts:Enabled`; with it OFF its part of this response is empty and nothing of it runs.)

## Goal
The bell tells the owner, without opening the plan screen, that a plan is waiting for review and how long is left, that last night's pipeline failed closed and why, that undecided tickets lapsed, and what pre-open armed or invalidated: each as one item that goes away by itself when its cause is gone and leads to `/plans`. Once P7-20 is built and switched on by the owner, the same bell also carries "stop level crossed" / "target crossed" on armed tickets and the post-trade prompt.

## Scope
**In:** `GET /api/plans/notices` (derived on read from stored plans, tickets, trails and plan runs; `IQueryHandler<GetPlanNoticesQuery, PlanNotices>`); the frontend `planNoticesQuery` in `shared/api`, `derivePlanNotifications`, the merge in `useNotifications`, bell labels and hrefs, one timed pop-up for the final review band; tests.
**Out:** push notifications and any external channel (Q6), a reminder scheduler or a new service (the minutes are computed at read time), command buttons, building the price-crossing alerts, post-trade prompts, mute / snooze / dismiss or their tables (all P7-20; this slice only carries their items in the bell), remembering resolved plan items for 24 h (plan notices are not stored; see Decisions 4), sound, e-mail, anything for live orders (P12).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a plan in state `published` or `inreview` with undecided primary tickets and a review cutoff in the future, when `GET /api/plans/notices` is called, then one item `plan_review_due` with id `plan:review:{planId}:{band}` is returned, where band is `open` (more than 60 minutes left), `soon` (60 to 16) or `final` (15 down to 1); severity `info`, `warning`, `warning`; the text names the date, the undecided and total primary tickets, the minutes left and the cutoff in IST, and says "Undecided tickets lapse at the cutoff."; one `route` action `Open` to `/plans?plan={planId}`. *`PlanNoticesTests` (hand table below).*
2. Given the cutoff has passed, or every primary ticket is decided, or the plan has no primary ticket, then no review item exists (the cause is gone: no resolved copy). *`PlanNoticesTests`.*
3. Given the newest nightly attempt in `trading.plan_runs` is `failed_closed`, has no plan, no later attempt published for that planned date, and its planned date is today or later (India date) or it finished within the last 24 hours, then one item `plan_failed_closed` with id `plan:failed:{forDate}:{gate}`, severity `error`, title "No plan for 28 Oct 2026", the gate id and the reason shortened to 160 characters, "manual" when a person started the attempt, and an `Open` route to `/plans`. A later published attempt removes it. *`PlanNoticesTests`.*
4. Given a plan with tickets in state `lapsed` whose lapse was recorded within 24 hours, then one item `plan_tickets_lapsed` id `plan:lapsed:{planId}`, severity `info`, text "N undecided tickets lapsed at the cutoff for {date}. Nothing was approved, armed or ordered."; after 24 hours it is gone. *`PlanNoticesTests`.*
5. Given a plan whose pre-open check armed or invalidated tickets, then from that moment to the end of the planned session (India date) one item `plan_preopen` id `plan:preopen:{planId}`, severity `info` (all armed) or `warning` (any invalidated), text "At pre-open N tickets were armed and M invalidated: {first reason shortened}. Armed means watched, not ordered. No order exists."; the pinned `PlanWording.Armed` sentence is part of the text. *`PlanNoticesTests`.*
6. Given P7-20 is built and `Planning:TicketAlerts:Enabled` is true, when `GET /api/plans/notices` is called, then its `items` also hold the ticket notices of P7-20 (`ticket_level_crossed`, `post_trade_prompt`) exactly as that slice derives them (ids `crossing:{id}` / the prompt id, severity mapped `warn` to `warning`, `since` and `resolved` kept so a matched prompt shows in the Resolved group for 24 hours, snoozed and dismissed items already filtered by P7-20's handler), sorted with the plan items by severity then time; with the setting OFF, or P7-20 not built, they are absent and nothing of P7-20 is called. The ids of the two sources never collide (plan ids start `plan:`; asserted). *`PlanNoticesTests` (with a fake `ITicketNoticeSource`), `PlanNoticesApiTests`.*
7. Given the frontend, then the bell shows the items with their severity word and icon, the badge counts the unread ones, an item that disappears from the response disappears from the list, a band change (`open` to `soon`) is a new unread item, and Open navigates to the route and closes the drawer. The browser follows only a route that is a path inside the app; no plan item of this slice has a command action, and `attentionCommands.generated.ts` is byte-identical after `npm run gen:api` with P8-15 alone (any command P7-20 itself adds to the server list is that slice's change, and its notice actions are routes per its contract). *`PlanNotices.test.tsx`, `commands.test.ts` (existing).*
8. Given the `final` band appears while the app is open and unread, then one timed pop-up (not persistent) shows once per plan (remembered in `xd.notifications.popped` under the item's key); a reload does not show it again; opening the app shows at most one pop-up (the most urgent, as today). *`AttentionPopups.test.tsx` extended.*
9. Given the plan notices cannot be read (401 or a failure), then the account, data, service and portfolio notices still show (no all-or-nothing). *`NotificationBell.test.tsx`.*
10. Given markup in a reason or a gate text, then it renders as text. *markup test.*
11. Given `npm run size`, the first-load number grows by at most 1.0 KB gzip (the query, the derive function and the kind labels; everything else is in the lazy bell and plans chunks); if the guard's WARNING band (under 3 KB left) is reached first, the entry is split first (frontend/CLAUDE.md rule). *size output in the PR.*

Hand table for criterion 1 (cutoff 14:00 IST, 5 primary tickets, 3 undecided): now 12:50 -> `open`, info, "70 minutes left"; 13:10 -> `soon`, warning, "50 minutes left"; 13:50 -> `final`, warning, "10 minutes left"; 14:00:00 and later -> no item; 4 of 5 decided at 13:10 -> `soon` text "1 of 5 undecided"; all decided -> no item.

## Contract
- **API (GET, session-protected, read only):** `GET /api/plans/notices` (`GetPlanNotices`; the route sits beside `/{id:long}` and does not collide with it, verified in `PlansEndpoints`) → `PlanNotices { items: PlanNotice[] }`, `PlanNotice { id, kind, severity: error|warning|info, title, text, at|null, since|null, planId|null, itemId|null, forDate|null, actions: AttentionAction[] (route, at most two), resolved }`. Kinds of this slice (derived, `since: null`, `resolved: false`): `plan_review_due`, `plan_failed_closed`, `plan_tickets_lapsed`, `plan_preopen`. Kinds carried from P7-20 when it is built and enabled (fields as P7-20 states them, `warn` mapped to `warning`): `ticket_level_crossed`, `post_trade_prompt`. `GET /api/plans/ticket-notices` stays P7-20's own route (its tests and any other client); the bell does **not** read it (Decisions 10). Shape and `AttentionAction` follow `PortfolioNotice`/`AttentionAction` (verified in `Application/Portfolio`); no new problem code. `scripts/gen-api.sh` in the same change.
- **Data:** none. Reads through `IPlanStore` (`ListAsync`, `ItemsAsync`, `HistoryAsync`, `ListRunsAsync`, verified) and, for the carried items, the new read port `ITicketNoticeSource.ListAsync(ct)` (Application/Abstractions/Planning; P7-20's notice handler implements it in-process, a null implementation returns an empty list, registered when the setting is OFF or P7-20 is absent). Time through `TimeProvider`; India date through `IndiaTime`.
- **Code:** `Application/Planning/GetPlanNotices.cs` (handler) and pure `Domain/Planning/PlanNoticeRules` (band from minutes, undecided count, the 24-hour windows, text builders), so every rule is a hand-tested function; log event `plans.notices.read {Items, Ms}` (id from the next free block at build time).
- **UI:** `frontend/src/shared/api/planNotices.ts` (`planNoticesQuery`, key `['plans', 'notices']`, `refetchInterval` 60,000 ms and on window focus, like the portfolio notices), `features/notifications/lib/derivePlanNotifications.ts` (drops an unknown kind, accepts a route only inside the app, at most two actions), `useNotifications` merges it, `NotificationKind` gains the six kinds (four of this slice, two carried from P7-20), labels and the plan hrefs. The bell and drawer are unchanged. Plan screens invalidate `['plans', 'notices']` after a decision so the item updates at once.
- **Events/jobs:** none; no SignalR event (P8-00: none exists for plans; polling is enough for minutes-scale bands).

## Non-functional
One query reads the newest few plans (this and the previous planned session, at most 4 plans), their items and trails, and the newest 5 plan runs: p95 under 100 ms on the seeded fixture; the bell polls once a minute. Read state is per browser (`localStorage`), may be absent (the existing try/catch). Payload under 4 KB. Security: session required; text carries counts, dates, the gate id and a shortened reason, never an account number or balance. Logs: counts and milliseconds only.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **Derived on read, like the data and service notices, not stored.** ADR 0030 made notices read-only and reconciled attention rows by a writer; that machinery exists for conditions that need a remembered `since` and a 24-hour resolved group. Plan facts are already stored (plan, trail, runs) and every condition is a function of them and the clock, so a derived list needs no table, no scheduler and cannot go stale. Rejected: a reminder job (a new service that would need the owner to switch it on; a missed tick means a missed reminder); persisting plan attention rows (a second source of truth).
2. **Minutes are computed at read time.** The bell polls every 60 seconds, so the text is at most a minute old; the band id changes at 60 and 15 minutes so that crossing a band raises a new unread item, which is the "reminder before the cutoff" with no scheduler.
3. **In-app only, and the limit is stated.** A notification that lives in the bell reaches an owner who has the app open; doc 19 §4 names phone review via the PWA as the mitigation for an unreviewed plan, and that path is not available (the stack binds to 127.0.0.1, P8-00 Q5/Q6). So the honest wording is: the bell and one timed pop-up help while the app is open; **they do not wake the owner**. Undecided tickets still lapse safely at the cutoff (the system default), which is why the in-app level is acceptable until the owner decides on Q5/Q6.
4. **No "Resolved" group for plan items.** An item whose cause is gone simply disappears; the drawer's Resolved group is for stored attention rows. A resolved plan item would need a store to remember it, and the plan screen already holds the history (trail, runs).
5. **Route actions only.** The command allow-list is generated from the server (ADR 0030 decision 6) and each command is a write the browser may call from a notice; a plan notice must never be able to approve or run anything. `Open` to `/plans?plan=` (a supported query, P7-17 item 12) is enough.
6. **No separate "fills unmatched" notice.** An earlier draft of this slice had one; P7-20's post-trade prompt does the same job better (it matches by the journal's key, covers typed-in and synced trades as "trade on record", and has a window and a status), so building both would double-ask. Removed; P7-20's prompt is carried instead (Decisions 10).
7. **One pop-up, the `final` band only.** Popping up every band would train dismissal; the last 15 minutes is the moment the lapse becomes real. Timed, not persistent (persistent is for errors that block data). Alternative rejected: a persistent error pop-up for a failed-closed night (it is shown in the bell as an error and on `/plans` as the banner of P8-14; the owner opens the app to act on it anyway).
8. **Severity.** `error` only for a failed-closed night (nothing was published); `warning` for a closing review window and an invalidated ticket; `info` otherwise. Words and icon carry the severity, never colour alone (existing bell rule).
10. **One bell source for the plan module: P8-15 absorbs P7-20's notices.** P7-20 (decision 11) serves its own derived route `GET /api/plans/ticket-notices` so the two slices cannot collide, and says P8-15 merges or absorbs it. Absorbed: the bell polls `GET /api/plans/notices` only, and that handler appends P7-20's items by calling its handler in-process through `ITicketNoticeSource`, so the browser makes one request, the badge counts one list, ids and read state live in one place, and a second poll of 60 s is saved. Rejected: the bell polling two plan routes (two failure modes, double polling); moving P7-20's derivation into P8-15 (it would pull the alert tables, mute/snooze acks and the 24-hour prompt rules into a read-only slice; P7-20 owns them and is Safety: yes). P7-20's HTTP route stays for its own tests and the ticket card; if P7-20 is built first, P8-15 adds the port and the append; if P8-15 is built first, the port's null implementation keeps the response valid. The `resolved` / `since` fields of P7-20 items are kept (a matched prompt shows in the Resolved group for 24 hours, as the portfolio attention items do), while the four plan kinds of this slice have none because their cause is read live. Snooze, dismiss and mute stay P7-20's owner writes on the ticket card; the bell shows only the route `Open`.
11. Sources: repo only (P7-20 spec; ADR 0030, 0018, 0095, 0096; `PortfolioNotice`; `NotificationBell` code), `docs/design/19` §4.

## Risks and honest limits
- Only works while the app is open; an unreviewed plan lapses at the cutoff and no ticket is approved by default (the system's safe default, ADR 0095). The PWA and phone path are P8-21 and need the owner's network decision.
- The pre-open item depends on the pre-open service having run (paused today, ADR 0097); the ticket alerts and prompts depend on P7-20 being built and `Planning:TicketAlerts:Enabled` being turned on by the owner (default OFF). With them off those items are absent, which is not a reassurance that nothing happened.
- A clock far from the server's (this machine's clock is the server's) would shift the bands; the server computes them, not the browser.
- The text is a summary; the plan screen is the record.
