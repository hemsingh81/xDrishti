# P7-20 — Price-crossing alerts on armed tickets and post-trade prompts (alerts only; no order)

- **Status:** Draft (written 2026-10-24; decisions below were researched under the owner's delegation; the owner's Approved is still needed before an agent builds it)
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P7 — follow-ups named in ADR 0096 ("alerts on the trigger/stop level are a follow-up") and P7-14 assumption 10 / P7-17 item 13 ("post-trade prompts"). Not a row of the original P7-00 table.
- **Design refs:** docs/design/15 §4 (price alerts), 04 §4.2 (Armed means watched), 10 §7, 12 §8 (plan link, discretionary), 19 §4-§5 · ADR 0022, 0024, 0093 (journal match key), 0096. New ADR: **next free ADR number at build time**.
- **Needs:** P7-19 (numeric levels), P7-11, P7-14, P2-09, P2-10 (built). P8-15 (built 2026-10-25, ADR 0101: `GET /api/plans/notices`). Consumed by the follow-up slice **P8-15b** (carry P7-20's items in that endpoint) and P8-22 (optional audit source).
- **Size:** 3 agent-days (estimate).
- **Safety:** **yes** — a new check in the live feed's hot path (a failure must never cost a price), three owner writes from the UI (mute, snooze, dismiss: notification preferences only), next to armed tickets. `Planning:TicketAlerts:Enabled` defaults **OFF**; the owner's Approved is the owner's act. No order, no credential, no broker endpoint, no ticket state move.

## Goal
While a ticket is armed (watched), the owner is told once when the live price crosses its indicative stop level or its target level, and after the session is asked once whether the trade was taken; the question closes by itself when the owner's fill (synced or typed in) exists. Everything is an alert or a prompt; "armed means watched" is on every item.

## Verified facts (code opened 2026-10-24)
- Armed tickets reach the feed through `ArmedTicketsProvider` (Application/Live). `LiveFlush` (Application/Portfolio/Live): `Add(LivePrice(InstrumentId, Price, TickAt))`, `FlushAsync` every 2 s; `CheckAlertsAsync` keeps firm ticks (`!LivePricePolicy.IsIndicative`, 09:15 India or later) and calls `PriceAlertChecker.CheckAsync(..., "system:live-feed")` (conditional update once, audit in the same transaction). `PriceAlert.Holds`: equality counts. Plan tickets have no second (5-minute) path.
- `IInstrumentSecurities.FindAsync(symbols)` -> `InstrumentSecurity(Symbol, InstrumentId, SecurityId)`; `IArmedTicketReader.ListAsync(forDate)`; `TicketLifecycle` has `Triggered` but nothing moves a ticket there.
- Bell: **`GET /api/plans/notices` exists (P8-15 built 2026-10-25, ADR 0101, `PlansEndpoints.NoticesAsync`)**: `GetPlanNoticesHandler(IPlanStore, IJournalStore, TimeProvider)` reads the last three sessions' newest plans, the newest run and stored fills and hands them to the pure `PlanNoticeRules.Derive(now, plans, latestRun)`, returning `PlanNotices { Items: PlanNotice(Id, Kind, Severity, Text, Route, PlanId?, At?) }`; kinds `plan_awaiting_review`, `plan_nightly_failed`, `plan_tickets_lapsed`, `plan_tickets_armed`, `plan_tickets_invalidated`, `plan_fills_unmatched`. Items are **route only, no command, counts and no symbol**, derived on read, nothing stored.
- Journal key (ADR 0093): account, normalised symbol (`TradeRules.NormaliseSymbol`), side, day. `JournalStore.FillsAsync` reads `portfolio.transactions` with `source = 'broker'` and `voided_at IS NULL` only: **typed-in (`manual`), file (`csv`) and bill rows are not journaled**.
- Patterns: `MakeAppendOnlyForAppRole(Schemas.Trading, ...)` (AddJournal); owner actor `owner:<email>` from the session, never a body (`AutonomyEndpoints`).

## Scope
**In:** Domain `Planning` pure `TicketCrossing`, `CrossingKinds`, `PromptRules`; port `ITicketAlertStore`; new seam `ILiveTickObserver` (`Application/Abstractions/Live`; `LiveFlush` passes each flush's firm ticks to registered observers) and `TicketAlertObserver` with a watched-ticket cache; service `post-trade-prompts`; the new port `ITicketNoticeSource` (Application/Abstractions/Planning) and its Infrastructure reader; the endpoints below; migration `AddTicketAlerts`; ticket-card alerts, prompts and the Mute/Snooze/Dismiss controls on the plan screen; options; tests; ADR.
**Out:** any order; any ticket state change; alerts on entry or on text triggers; user-defined levels (P2-09 price alerts stay the tool); push, e-mail, sound; unarmed, reserve or rejected tickets; repeated crossings; journaling typed-in trades (finding below); any bell button or command (the bell stays route-only, ADR 0101); the change to `GetPlanNoticesHandler` that carries these items (slice P8-15b, below); a trade-entry form.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given an Armed BUY ticket (entry ref 100.00, stop 95.00 `level`, target 110.00) and firm ticks 99.00, 95.00, 94.50, 110.20, then exactly one `stop_crossed` (price 95.00, equality counts) and one `target_crossed` (110.20) are stored and 94.50 stores nothing; the SELL mirror (200, stop 210, target 180) fires at 210.05 and 179.95; a tick that jumps over a level fires once.
2. Given ticks before 09:15 or at/after 15:30:00 India time, a ticket not Armed (Approved, Expired, Invalidated, Closed), or `levels` null (only a non-null level can fire), then nothing fires.
3. Given stop anchor `close_of_d`, the notice says "indicative stop from the close of {D}"; with `level`, "stop level {x} crossed". Neither says a stop was hit or an order exists; both end with "Alert only: no order is placed and none exists. A stop is only an alert."
4. Given parallel flushes or a restart, a crossing is stored once (unique `(item_id, kind)`, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`) and its notice id is stable (`crossing:{id}`).
5. Given mute on a ticket, no further crossing is stored for it that session (earlier ones stay visible); snooze hides a notice until `until` (15/30/60 min), it reappears once, at most 3 snoozes; dismiss hides it for good. Ack rows are insert-only, the newest decides, the actor is the session's owner e-mail (`owner:` prefix), never a body field.
6. A crossing counts in the plan notices until the end of the next India calendar day, then no longer (derived on read, no job); rows stay.
7. Given service `post-trade-prompts` at 16:45 on session D, one prompt per (ticket that was Armed on D, paper allocation account) is stored once (unique); tickets only Approved or invalidated at pre-open get none; a second run stores nothing; paused stores nothing.
8. Given a prompt, when a non-voided `portfolio.transactions` row exists for the same account, normalised symbol, side and trade date (any source), the status is `matched` with `via = journal` if a `trading.journal_fills` row links it to the ticket's entry, else `via = trade_on_record` (text: the journal grades broker-synced fills only); with none and the window open, `open`; after the window (`Planning:TicketAlerts:PromptTradingDays`, 3 trading days after D) with none, `expired_no_fill` ("no fill found; treated as not taken; nothing is written to the journal"); a match closes the prompt with no click: it no longer counts in the bell and stays there as `resolved` until the end of the day after its session (the port contract of P8-15b; ADR 0115).
9. Given two armed tickets with one account, symbol, side and day that both fit one trade, both prompts read `ambiguous` and name the items; nothing is guessed (ADR 0093). An ambiguous prompt is its own notice (`prompt:{id}:ambiguous`) with its own acts, so snoozing the open question does not hide it.
10. With `Enabled = false` (default) no observer is registered, no watched list is read, no row is written by the feed and the service is paused. With it true and the database failing, the flush still writes prices and logs one line (isolation test: the checker throws, the price batch is unaffected).
11. `xd_app` can SELECT and INSERT but not UPDATE, DELETE or TRUNCATE the three new tables (grants test); the database refuses a third kind, a non-positive level or price, a non-owner ack actor, a snooze without `until`, a mute without an item, a second prompt for one (item, account), a prompt for an allocation that does not exist.
12. Notice reads write nothing (statement-recording test); no type or route is named for an order (tripwire green); no code of this slice writes `plan_events`.
13. Frontend (the plan screen only): the ticket card shows "Alerts: stop crossed at {price} {time}", "target crossed ...", or "none yet", the prompt status, and the owner-act controls Mute, Snooze and Dismiss (owner session); server text is rendered as text. **The bell gets no button**: after P8-15b it shows route-only items (`ticket_level_crossed`, `post_trade_prompt`, severity warning/info) with `route = /plans?plan={planId}`, text with counts and no symbol or price ("1 watched ticket crossed its indicative stop level; open the plan"), and a snoozed or dismissed crossing or prompt is not counted.

## Contract
- **API (session; POST also CSRF; no step-up, nothing moves money or credentials):** `GET /api/plans/{id}/alerts` (crossings, prompts with status and `via`, acks; for the ticket card); `POST /api/plans/items/{itemId}/alerts/mute` (409 `plans.alert_closed` after the session, 404 `plans.not_found`); `POST /api/plans/alerts/{noticeId}/snooze` body `{minutes: 15|30|60}` (`noticeId` = `crossing:{id}` or `prompt:{id}`; 422 `plans.invalid`, 409 `plans.snooze_limit`); `POST /api/plans/alerts/{noticeId}/dismiss`. Called from the plan screen only. **No new notices route:** the existing `GET /api/plans/notices` is extended by **P8-15b** (a small follow-up slice, about 0.5 d): `GetPlanNoticesHandler` also takes `ITicketNoticeSource` (`CountsAsync(sessions, now)` -> per plan: crossings not snoozed or dismissed within their lifetime, prompts open or ambiguous) and `PlanNoticeRules` gains the two kinds; with the setting OFF the source returns nothing and the endpoint answers exactly as today. `ApiJsonContext`; `scripts/gen-api.sh`.
- **Data (migration `AddTicketAlerts`, insert-only, schema not re-granted):** `trading.ticket_alerts(id bigint identity pk, plan_id, item_id, session date, kind varchar(16) in ('stop_crossed','target_crossed'), level numeric(18,4) > 0, stop_anchor varchar(12) null, price numeric(18,4) > 0, tick_at timestamptz, created_at, unique(item_id, kind), fk (item_id, plan_id) to plan_items, index (session))`; `trading.ticket_prompts(id, plan_id, item_id, account_id uuid, session date, created_at, unique(item_id, account_id), composite fk (item_id, account_id) to plan_item_allocations)`; `trading.ticket_notice_acks(id, notice_kind in ('crossing','prompt','ticket'), notice_id null, item_id null, action in ('snooze','dismiss','mute'), until null, actor like 'owner:%', at database-stamped, shape check: snooze needs notice_id and until, dismiss needs notice_id, mute needs item_id and kind 'ticket')`.
- **Settings (`Planning:TicketAlerts:*`, ranges, `ValidateOnStart`, doc 16):** `Enabled` false; `PromptTradingDays` 3 (1-10); `WatchedCacheSeconds` 30 (5-120). Service `post-trade-prompts`: daily 16:45, trading days, advice after `plan-expiry` and the 16:30 portfolio sync, starts paused, catch-up at most 3 trading days.
- **Jobs:** the observer runs inside the existing flush of `xd-feed`; the watched list is cached and invalidated by the existing `NOTIFY active_set_changed`; log ids after the highest in use (4801 on 2026-10-25; `LogEventIdTests` decides at build time).

## Non-functional
The observer adds one cached lookup per flush and one insert per crossing; flush p95 stays within the P2-10 budget (tick to database <= 3 s at 100 instruments) with 50 watched tickets (measured on 1,000 synthetic ticks). It swallows and logs its own failure (narrow `CA1031` pragma as in `ArmedTicketsProvider`). Logs: ids, counts, kind; never price, symbol, e-mail or note. Owner session only; all text server-built; no export.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **Levels of the stored ticket, evaluated in the existing flush; not user price alerts.** P2-09 alerts are user-created, one-shot, capped at 200 and bound to holdings; 50 daily tickets would exhaust the cap with create/cancel churn and mix modules. Rejected: auto-creating `portfolio.price_alerts`. Source: P2-09 criteria 13-15, ADR 0022.
2. **An observer seam** keeps Portfolio/Live ignorant of Planning. Rejected: a direct dependency.
3. **One crossing per (ticket, kind), no repeats.** A wobbling price would flood the bell; the first crossing is the information; stop then target can both fire. Rejected: periodic repeats, hysteresis bands (an invented parameter).
4. **A crossing never changes state.** Armed stays Armed until expiry or invalidation; `Triggered` stays unreachable because the system cannot know the owner entered. Source: ADR 0096, doc 19 §5.
5. **Two kinds only.** The entry reference is yesterday's close and the trigger is text; an "entry reached" alert would be false precision.
6. **Mute/snooze/dismiss are insert-only owner acts** needing a session and CSRF but no password (no money, credential or autonomy). Snooze is capped (60 min, 3 times) so a crossing cannot be hidden into irrelevance. Rejected: updating or deleting rows; quiet-hours setting.
7. **A prompt is a stored row plus a derived status.** The row gives a stable id, time and window; the status is computed from `portfolio.transactions` on read, so the prompt closes the moment the fill exists, with no job and no UPDATE. The owner enters trades where trades are entered today. Rejected: a form that writes a fill (it would invent a trade the broker never reported); a job-updated status column.
8. **Match key = the journal's**, so prompt and ADR 0093 never disagree; `via` shows whether the journal linked it. **Typed-in trades resolve the prompt but are not journaled** (P7-14 reads broker rows only): stated on the prompt, raised as a finding, not changed silently.
9. **Window 3 trading days** (typed or imported fills usually arrive the same evening or next morning; Dhan history syncs daily); calendar-aware; range 1-10.
10. **Prompts only for tickets that were Armed**, one per paper allocation; asking about unarmed tickets is noise.
11. **Bell items ride the existing `GET /api/plans/notices`; controls live on the plan screen.** ADR 0101 fixed that plan notices are derived on read, route only, no command, no symbol. A Snooze/Dismiss/Mute button in the bell would break that allow-list; a second notices route would give two bell sources. So P7-20 supplies counts through `ITicketNoticeSource` and the owner acts on the ticket card. Rejected: bell commands; `GET /api/plans/ticket-notices`.
12. **Default OFF and fail-isolated:** the flush is the one path that writes prices; ON by default with a kill switch rejected.

## Risks and honest limits
- A spike between two flushes is not seen (ticks coalesce to the latest per instrument every 2 s; same limit as P2-09 criterion 15); a "crossed" alert is a last-traded print, not a fill.
- An alert can fire for a ticket the owner did not take; the text says "if you hold this trade". A `close_of_d` stop moves with the real entry; the alert is indicative.
- It is not protection: no order rests at a broker; if `xd-feed`, the Mac or the network is down nothing fires (broker-side stops are P12).
- Levels are indicative from the close of D (P7-19); a prompt can say "no fill found" when the owner traded where the app does not sync or has not entered it yet; typed-in trades are not journaled or graded.
- Not observed on a real morning; verified on fakes only (the P2-10 manual checks apply).
