## Goal
Let a strategy whose signal is known at the close of a bar that ends the session (a 1D bar, or the last 30m or 1h bar) place its order for the next trading session and be filled at that session's open within the entry limit, as doc 07 §2 says of swing tickets, so that `BASE_BREAKOUT` can be simulated. The mechanism is one rule of the entry (an order that rests overnight), one lift of two refusals (the 1D evaluation, the level stops), one new honest outcome (an order that is still pending when the data ends is not a missed fill), and a key that follows the day of the decision.

## Scope
**In:**
1. **The overnight entry order** (E8, E9): a decision at or after the close of its session fills at the first minute of the next trading session that holds a trade, within the limit and within 5 minutes of that session's open; a gap above the limit is a counted miss, a gap through the stop is `EntryThroughStop`.
2. **An order pending at the end of the data** (D3): its own no-trade reason and fate, never `NoFillBar`.
3. **The daily evaluation**: `EntryScanner.Create` and `Simulator.Create` stop refusing 1D (1W and 1M stay refused); a late daily bar stays stale, counted and not evaluated.
4. **The level stops** `swing_low`, `swing_high`, `bar_low`, `bar_high` (zero buffer, evaluation timeframe) in `Simulator.Create` (D5).
5. **`Key` and the window cut by decision date** (D6).
6. **Counters and fates**: `OvernightEntries`, `OrderPendingAtEnd` in `WalkCounters`; `OrderPendingAtEnd` in `RunFates`.
7. **`FillLimits.auction_open`** gains the clause for a daily entry (D7).
8. The hand rows, the properties, the third golden regeneration and the documents.

**Out (not in this slice):**
- **The strategy `BASE_BREAKOUT`** (P3-06): its parameters, conditions, fixtures and reference loop. D4 lists what it gets from here.
- **Tickets that live across bars** (`stop_above_high`, `limit_pullback`, BASE_BREAKOUT variant B) and "signal valid N sessions": still refused or absent.
- **ATR stops, a stop buffer, `fixed_pct`, a stop on another timeframe**: P3-05.
- **A read past `To`**: the read ends at `To` (P3-03d, open question 3).
- **Costs** (P3-03c), **the exporter and the cards** (P3-04b1, b2, c: they need a spelling for the new `NoTradeReason` and a column pass, D8).

**Size.** About **1.5 agent-days** (an estimate, not calibrated): the overnight order with its rows 0.3 and the pending outcome with its fate and the last-day test 0.15; the daily scanner lift and the stale check 0.2; the level stops 0.1; `Key` and the cut 0.1; the third golden regeneration, the frozen views of the P3-03d outputs, the ADR and the docs 0.3; the hand rows, the seeded daily series and the properties 0.35. **What exists (read on 2026-10-18; after P3-03d these are its words).** `EntryScanner.Create` returns `simulation.scanner_timeframe_unsupported` for 1D and `Simulator.Create` repeats it, both with the statement "a bar of a day or longer is always stale at the first minute of the next date". The frame closes a regular 1D bar at the step of the 15:29 minute (`TimeframeBuilder`: the bar that ends the window emits it), and by the first minute of a later date, flagged incomplete, when 15:29 is missing; the scanner's stale rule already drops that case (`StaleBucketSteps`). The scanner supports `swing_low`, `swing_high`, `bar_low`, `bar_high` and `or_opposite` with a zero buffer (P3-03a, F22); `Simulator.Create` accepts only `or_opposite`, which is intraday-only (the catalog marks `or_high` and `or_low` intraday-only: read, the compile error not run), and its message says the other kinds "come with P3-05". The walker's entry (E1) requires the fill minute on the decision's date, so a decision at 15:30 is `NoFillBar`.

## Definitions (numbers the tests pin)
Notation as in P3-03d (`HH:MM O/H/L/C`, Volume 1,000 unless `(v0)`, a minute not listed repeats the previous close, the default week, the week of Monday 2026-03-02). Rows continue ADR 0038 and P3-03d: **E8** and **E9** are entry rows.

### D1 — E8: when an order rests overnight
In a swing walk, a decision whose minute of the day is **at or after the end of its session** (`ToMinute` of `calendar.SessionOf(its date)`, 15:30 without a calendar) places its order for the next session. That is every decision of a 1D evaluation (taken at the step of the 15:29 minute, the decision instant 15:30), and the decision of the last 30m bucket (15:00 to 15:30) and of the last 1h bucket (15:15 to 15:30), which close at 15:30. Under P3-03d such a candidate is a `NoFillBar`; this is the change. An intraday walk never rests overnight. *Decided on research (the owner may revert, open question 2):* a 30m or 1h swing strategy with no `last_entry` before 15:30 would otherwise lose its last bar's signals to `NoFillBar`, one in seven (30m) or one in six (1h) bars of the day.

### D2 — E9: the fill session and the fill minute
The *fill session* is the next trading day after the decision date by the calendar (the next weekday without one: a Friday decision fills on Monday; an order rests over a holiday). The fill minute is the first minute that holds a trade (E0) on the fill date that starts at or after the **open of the fill session** (`FromMinute`, 09:15) and at most `MaxFillDelayMinutes` (5) after that open (09:20 is allowed, 09:21 is not). If a minute of another date follows the candidate's own minute and nothing on the fill date starts in time — the next date in the list is not the fill session (a missing or excluded session), or the fill date's first minute holding a trade is after 09:20 — the candidate is `NoFillBar`, **and a later session is never used**. E2 to E6 are those of ADR 0038 with the trigger close of the trigger bar (the daily close): the limit is the close plus 0.1 % rounded down to the tick; an open at or inside the limit fills at the open (`AtOpen`), a session that opens beyond the limit and trades back to it fills at the limit (`AtLimit`), one that opens beyond it and does not is `LimitNotReached` (a gap above the limit is a counted miss); an open at or through the stop is `EntryThroughStop`. `TradeDate` is the fill date, `DecisionEpochSeconds` stays on the decision date, `EntriesAfterMissingMinutes` counts a fill later than the open, `OvernightEntries` counts every trade filled on a later date than its decision. The 09:15 minute is the one with the auction price and the repaired range (ISS-18 (a), ISS-21): **a daily strategy fills there by design**.
The gate of P3-03d is unchanged: a candidate decided at 15:30 on the day a position leaves at the close (its hold end is the start of the due minute, 15:29) is not blocked, so a position can be followed by a new order the same evening.

### D3 — An order still pending when the data ends (review finding M3)
With a list that ends at the candidate's own minute (no minute follows it) the order cannot be said to have missed anything: a longer list may fill it. Under a plain E9 it would be `NoFillBar` in the short run and a trade dated before `To1` in a longer one, which breaks the prefix property of P3-03d D14 2(c) for the last day of a window. Therefore **a candidate with no minute after its own, whose order rests overnight, is `NoTradeReason.OrderPendingAtEnd`** (appended to the enum; spelled `order_pending_at_end` by the exporters): a no-trade, a fate of its own (`RunFates.OrderPendingAtEnd`), a counter (`WalkCounters.OrderPendingAtEnd`), never `NoFillBar` and never a trade. `NoFillBar` stays for the cases in which a minute of another date is present. P3-03d D14 2(c) becomes: a trade of the second run whose decision date is on or before `To1` is, in the first run, a trade, an `OpenAtEnd` trade or an `OrderPendingAtEnd` candidate. The identity of the fates gains the term. A pending order does not set the gate (it is not a trade). The mirror case under an intraday walk (a decision at the last minute of the list, in session) stays `NoFillBar` as ADR 0038 N3 pins it; it cannot arise at the end of a day's data because the decision of the last bucket is at 15:30, which is the overnight case.

### D4 — The daily evaluation, and what BASE_BREAKOUT (P3-06) gets
`EntryScanner.Create` and `Simulator.Create` no longer refuse 1D; they refuse 1W and 1M as before (`simulation.scanner_timeframe_unsupported`, with a corrected message: the frame emits a 1D bar at the 15:29 step; only a late bar is stale). The decision is taken at the step where the frame closes the 1D bar; a bar closed late by the first minute of the next date (15:29 missing) is *stale*: counted in `StaleBucketSteps`, not evaluated, because no order may be placed on yesterday's bar (the roadmap's "late, flagged and counted"). How often 15:29 is missing is UNVERIFIED (a minute is Volume 0 in 0.4 % of the minutes of the sampled files, ADR 0038 E0; the rate for 15:29 was not measured), and each such day is a lost signal. The P3-06 row: swing, BUY only, evaluated on 1D at the close, filled at the next open within the limit; setup on `tf("1W")` and a 1D trend, a tight base and `rel_volume(20)`; trigger `close >= highest(base_len, source=close)`; stop `lowest(base_len)`; `FIXED_RR` 2.0 plus `MAX_HOLD` 5. From P3-03d and this slice it gets: (1) the swing style, `max_hold_sessions` 5 by the calendar and the exit at the close of session 5; (2) the daily evaluation at the 15:29 step and the stale late bar; (3) the fill at the next session's open within 0.1 %, a gap above the limit a counted miss, a gap below the stop `EntryThroughStop`; (4) a level stop on 1D (D5; the row's `lowest(base_len)` is a `swing_low` stop with `lookback = base_len`, UNVERIFIED until its spec); (5) the gate and the counters, so that the 20-trade check can draw a `MAX_HOLD` exit and a gap exit. The weekly bars of the frame exist (P3-01c) and are not touched. It does **not** get its parameters, conditions, fixtures or reference loop (P3-06's own), a buy-stop ticket, or a signal valid for several sessions. How many trades it makes on about 2.1 years of data (ISS-19; about 500 daily bars and a weekly series of about 100) is UNVERIFIED and may be small.

### D5 — The level stops in `Simulator.Create`
`Simulator.Create` accepts `swing_low`, `swing_high`, `bar_low` and `bar_high`, with `buffer_atr` 0 and the stop read on the evaluation timeframe, in addition to `or_opposite`: the scanner supports them already, and a daily strategy cannot use `or_opposite`. `fixed_pct` and `atr_mult` (no level in the frame), a buffer and a stop on another timeframe stay refused by name (P3-05). The code's message says the other kinds "come with P3-05"; this takes the four level kinds early because P3-06 needs `swing_low` and P3-05 needs `bar_low` (open question 3).

### D6 — `Key` and the cut by decision date
With an overnight fill the trade's date (`TradeDate`, the fill date) is no longer the date on which the scanner made the candidate, and two trades of one instrument and side can have one fill date (a candidate decided at 15:30 on Monday fills on Tuesday at 09:15 and exits at 09:20; another decided on Tuesday at 10:15 also has `TradeDate` Tuesday). `SimTrade.Key` therefore uses `SessionClock.DateOf(DecisionEpochSeconds − 60)` instead of `TradeDate`; for every intraday trade and every P3-03d swing trade the two dates are equal, so their key text is unchanged. The scanner makes one candidate per instrument, side and decision date, so the key stays unique. The replay cuts trades to the window by the same date (`CandidatesInWindow` already counts by decision date, so `TradesInWindow` cannot exceed it at `From`). `Key` is computed and not a positional field, so `SimTrade` gains no field and its schema number does not move (D8).

### D7 — `FillLimits`
`auction_open` gains the daily entry: "The first 1-minute open of the data can be the auction price. A daily strategy enters there, and a position held overnight that gaps through a stop or a target is filled in that minute (a stop at the open, a target at its level); a repaired opening bar has a wider range, so its slippage is larger, never smaller." Only this constant is edited here (P3-03d edited it before, P3-04b1 edits `touch_is_fill`); the text uses none of the words forbidden by P3-04b1 AC 6.

### D8 — Versions, the third regeneration and the neighbours
This slice changes rules, so it needs its own engine version: **expected `sim-5` and run-record schema 5, the next free numbers at merge** after P3-03d (`sim-3`, schema 3) and P3-03c (`sim-4`, schema 4); `SimTrade` gains no field (its schema stays as P3-03c leaves it, 4), `NoTradeReason` and `RunFates` gain `OrderPendingAtEnd` (appended), `WalkCounters` gains `OvernightEntries` and `OrderPendingAtEnd` (appended, default 0). The five golden files are regenerated a third time (P3-03d, P3-03c, this); the diff is read against the list of P3-03d D9 step 2 plus the new counters and the new fate. **Before the first change** the sim-4 renderings of the P3-03d rows (M, G, J, Q) and of the intraday fixtures are frozen exactly as P3-03d freezes sim-2, and a test requires that under the new version every one of them is byte-identical in that view: this slice changes the entry of a candidate decided at 15:30 and nothing else. Neighbours: the exporters (P3-04b1) need `order_pending_at_end` and the `Key` of D6; P3-04c's strata derive from `ExitReason`, which does not change; the P3-04 replay has a case for the new reason (the reflection test of P3-03d criterion 20 enumerates the enum).

### D9 — Counters and fates
`Candidates = Trades + NoFillBar + AfterSquareOff + EntryThroughStop + LimitNotReached + PositionOpen + OrderPendingAtEnd`; `OvernightEntries ≤ Trades`; the fates identity of P3-03d gains `OrderPendingAtEnd`; the reflection test of the two `Add` methods covers the new properties.

## Acceptance criteria
Numbered, each testable.
1. **Overnight entry.** Given O1 to O7 and O5b, then the results equal the table, in both fill modes (`SlippageConfig.None` gives `EntryFilledPrice = EntryPrice`); `OvernightEntries` counts O1, O3 and O5b.
2. **A 30m or 1h decision at 15:30** rests overnight (a candidate from fixture R's family with no `last_entry` before 15:30 fills at the next 09:15) and an intraday walk never does.
3. **Pending at the end.** Given O8, then the outcome is `OrderPendingAtEnd`; the replay for `[Mon, Mon]` has it as a fate and `[Mon, Tue]` has the trade of O1; the prefix property holds for the last day of the window over 100 seeded daily series (a trade of the longer run decided on or before `To1` is a trade, an `OpenAtEnd` or an `OrderPendingAtEnd` in the shorter); a missing fill session with another date present is `NoFillBar` (O5).
4. **Daily scanner.** Given DF1 and DF2, then the candidates, indexes, stop levels and counters equal the table; 1D is accepted and 1W and 1M are refused (`simulation.scanner_timeframe_unsupported`); a late bar is never evaluated.
5. **Level stops.** `Simulator.Create` accepts the four level kinds with a zero buffer on the evaluation timeframe and refuses by name `fixed_pct`, `atr_mult`, a buffer and another stop timeframe, with the codes of today. Everything else refused before stays refused: an overlay, a filter, a context or expiry read, `stop_above_high`, `limit_pullback`, swing SELL.
6. **`Key` and the cut.** The key of every intraday and every P3-03d swing trade is the text it had; for an overnight fill it uses the decision date; the replay cuts a trade by the decision date (a candidate decided on the Friday before `From` that fills on `From` is `TradesBeforeWindow`); `TradesInWindow ≤ CandidatesInWindow` at every window.
7. **`FillLimits`.** The ten codes of P3-03d D15, the `auction_open` text of D7, no word of the forbidden list of P3-04b1 AC 6.
8. **Compatibility and versions.** The frozen sim-4 view of the intraday fixtures and of the P3-03d rows (M, G, J, Q) equals the output under the new version, byte for byte; the engine version and the run-record schema are the next free ones, the five golden files are regenerated and the diff is read; the version table has its row.
9. **Counters and fates.** The identities of D9 hold on every fixture and seeded series; the reflection test of the `Add` methods covers the new properties.
10. **A daily strategy through the replay.** A test-only daily strategy over 12 invented sessions of one instrument, through `InstrumentReplayer`, gives candidates decided at 15:30, entries at the next 09:15, one `MaxHold` exit at session 5, one gap-through-stop exit, one counted miss, one `PositionOpen` and one `OrderPendingAtEnd` (the last session), with the fates adding up to the candidates; this is the fixture that P3-06 extends.
11. **Look-ahead.** Over 500 seeded daily series, an outcome settled at an index at most `k` (the fill minute of an overnight order; for a no-trade the first minute of a later date or the fill minute) is identical when the minutes after `k` are removed and when replaced by extreme values; the control *limit-from-the-fill-session's-high* (a wrapper that lets the limit use the fill session's high) fails on at least 10 seeds while the plain walker reports none.
12. **Structure and documents.** The architecture tests stay green (no new dependency; the calendar is passed in); a new ADR (the next free number at merge) holds E8, E9, D3, D5 and D6 and is linked with P3-03d's; the amendment to ADR 0037 for the replay; doc 07 §2, doc 08 §5, the roadmap and ISS-18 (a); `scripts/build-docs.sh`.

## Where the roadmap row and the code disagree
(a) The row says a 1D signal "is evaluated at the step that closes the daily bar"; the code refuses 1D with the statement that such a bar "is always stale at the first minute of the next date", which is not what the frame does. (b) The only stop `Simulator.Create` accepts is intraday-only, and the message hands the others to P3-05 whose row does not list them. (c) The row's "a missing 15:29 minute makes the daily bar late, flagged and counted" is what the scanner does already (`StaleBucketSteps`); it is only reachable once 1D is allowed. (d) The row does not say what happens to an order whose fill session is not in the data yet; D3 does.

## Open questions
1. **Split this part from P3-03d and merge it last? (decided by the coordinator, recommended; the owner confirms.)** Recommendation: **yes.** Only P3-06 needs it, it needs its own engine version and a third golden regeneration, and it slides with P3-06. Together with P3-03d the pair is 5.0 agent-days against the earlier 3 + 1.
2. **Does the 15:30 decision of a 30m or 1h swing strategy rest overnight (E8)?** Recommendation: **yes**, the same mechanism as the daily bar; otherwise the last bar's signals are all `NoFillBar`. The alternative is to leave them counted as misses and to require `last_entry` before 15:30 in swing tests.
3. **Lift the four level stop kinds here, or leave them to P3-05?** Recommendation: **lift them here** (zero buffer, evaluation timeframe): the scanner supports and tests them, a daily strategy cannot use the one stop the simulator accepts, and P3-05 (`bar_low`) and P3-06 (`swing_low`) both need them; ATR stops and buffers stay for P3-05. If the owner prefers a strict order, P3-06 cannot start until P3-05 merges.
4. **Report an order pending at the end of the data as its own outcome (D3), or as `NoFillBar`?** Recommendation: **its own outcome**: the alternative makes the last day of every window depend on how far the read goes (a trade appears when the window grows), which is what window independence forbids.

## Risks
- **The 09:15 fills.** A daily entry and every overnight gap exit fill in the minute whose open can be the auction price and whose range the importer may have widened (ISS-18 (a), ISS-21): slippage there is larger and the open may differ from the official one (not measured).
- **Gaps above the limit.** A limit of the close plus 0.1 % misses most gap-up opens: the number of `LimitNotReached` may be large; the counter shows it. The 0.1 % is doc 07's, not tuned.
- **Lost signals.** A missing 15:29 minute loses the day's signal (`StaleBucketSteps`, rate not measured).
- **Three golden regenerations and three record schemas** (P3-03d, P3-03c, this): the diff reading is the net; the frozen views prevent drift of the earlier rows.
- **A small sample.** About 2.1 years of data (ISS-19): a daily strategy may make few trades.
- **A draft strategy behind it.** `BASE_BREAKOUT` is the author's proposal; nothing here builds it.
