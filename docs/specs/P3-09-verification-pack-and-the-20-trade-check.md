## Goal
The owner can run one command, give it a seed he wrote down first, and get a folder that holds everything he needs to check the engine by hand against charts: about 20 trades drawn at random, balanced over strategy, instrument and year, plus one trade of each edge category that occurs, each as the inert HTML card of P3-04c, an index, the engine's values in a file of their own, an empty check log to fill in, and a README that states the protocol. A second mode (Part B) reads the filled log, compares what he saw with what the engine did to the tolerances of the protocol, and prints which rows agree, which are unexplained and whether the pack is complete. The owner's signature on every row, with zero open model or implementation defects, is the exit criterion of P3. Nothing here says that a strategy has an edge, and nothing leaves the machine.

## Scope
**In:**
1. **`VerificationSampler`** (V1, pure, `XDrishti.SimExport/Verification`): the seeded random part, balanced over strategy, instrument and year, on `SeededRank` of P3-04c.

## Definitions (decisions the tests pin)
All dates are India dates. "Recommended" means the owner confirms at approval.

**V1 — The seeded stratified sampler (outside the engine).** `VerificationSampler.Draw(IReadOnlyList<SimulationRun> runs, string seed, int count)` is pure and works on `run.Trades` only (the `OpenAtEnd` trades are apart and never drawn). The engine, the run key and the run record are not touched; the sampler reads the record the way the export does.
- **The seed** is a text the owner writes down **before** the first draw, `[A-Za-z0-9._-]{1,40}`, **required, with no default and no clock or machine value** (a default would be a hidden random source; a seed chosen before seeing the trades is the pre-registration the check needs). It is recorded in `pack.json`, the README, the index and the manifest. Suggested names: `p3-check-1`, then `p3-check-2` after the first fix.
- **A cell** is a strategy (`SimTrade.StrategyId`) × an instrument (`SimTrade.Symbol`) × the year of `TradeDate`. *Stratified* means balanced over those three, in this precedence: strategy, then instrument inside it, then year (counted over the strategy, then inside the instrument). With a single strategy the strategy dimension is moot; the roadmap's "balanced over strategies" then means nothing, and the record says so.
- **The draw** takes `count` trades (default 20; 1 to 100) one at a time. For each pick it chooses, among the cells that still hold an unused trade, those whose strategy has the fewest picks so far; among them those whose instrument (within that strategy) has the fewest; among them those whose year, **counted across the whole strategy**, has the fewest picks; among them those whose cell has the fewest; remaining ties are broken by `SeededRank.Of(seed, "cell|" + strategy + "|" + symbol + "|" + year)` ascending. **Why the global year count:** with one strategy and about 214 instruments, the first 20 picks each take a new instrument, and a year balanced only inside an instrument would be whichever cell has the lowest hash (all 20 could fall in 2025 and the partial 2026 never be drawn). With the global year tiebreak, years that have trades for the chosen instrument are spread over the picks (each year's count differs from another's by at most one while the instrument choice allows it). Year is still balanced only **as far as the instrument precedence allows**: an instrument that trades in one year only gives that year; AC 2 pins both facts on a hand-made pool. In the chosen cell the trade is the first unused one of `SeededRank.Order(seed + "|trade", trade keys of the cell)`. The draw stops early when the pool is exhausted. The result is in draw order, and the draw order is not a ranking of anything.
- Why this and not a proportional draw: the check is about mechanics, not an estimate, so every strategy, instrument and year that exists is visited as early as it can be (the argument of P3-04c, question 1); a proportional draw over 126,000 trades would be 20 trades from the commonest instruments. Why `SeededRank` and no random source: the same seed and the same trades give the same 20, in any input order, and a different seed gives a different set (the property P3-04c pinned).
- Only the trade `Key` (`StrategyId|Symbol|Side|DecisionDate`, unique across strategies) and the three cell members are read. The sizes of the cells and strata are never printed (P3-04c C1: a count by group is a hit rate in words).

**V2 — The edge categories (a table in code, each a pure function of the record).** Derived from the exit reasons, the entry rows of ADR 0038 (E2 to E7) and ADR 0043 (E8, E9), the counters and the fates of the record. A category **occurs** when at least one trade of the pooled runs belongs to it. The table, with the roadmap's name where it has one:

| # | Code | A trade belongs to it when | Roadmap row |
|---|------|----------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | `sell` | `Side` is Sell | SELL |
| 2 | `stop_gap` | exit reason Stop and `ExitPrice` strictly beyond `StopPrice` (below for a Buy, above for a Sell): the minute opened beyond the stop and the exit is at its open (`StopGapExits`) | gap exit; **and** hole through a stop (see below) |
| 3 | `stop_in_entry_minute` | exit reason Stop and `ExitEpochSeconds` equals `EntryEpochSeconds` | a stop in the entry minute |
| 4 | `entry_at_limit` | `EntryFill` is AtLimit (the fill minute opened beyond the limit and traded back to it; the price is rounded to the tick) | added: the limit and tick rows of ISS-18 (c), (f) |
| 5 | `entry_after_hole` | `EntryEpochSeconds` is later than `DecisionEpochSeconds` on the decision date (minutes after the decision were missing or empty: `EntriesAfterMissingMinutes`) | added: the hole at the entry |
| 6 | `first_evaluation` | `DecisionEpochSeconds` is the instant of the strategy's **first entry** time of the recorded definition (the earliest minute the rule may fire; 09:30 for ORB with `or_min` 15) | the first evaluation of a session (reading UNVERIFIED, see below) |
| 7 | `overnight_entry` | the fill date is later than the decision date (an order that rested overnight, ADR 0043 E8) | added: swing, only when a swing strategy exists |
| 8 | `exit_target` | exit reason Target | added: each exit reason |
| 9 | `exit_square_off` | exit reason SquareOff | added: each exit reason |
| 10 | `exit_max_hold` | exit reason MaxHold (the case whose due minute is missing, `MaxHoldAtLastClose`, is a trade of it whose exit is not the 15:29 minute) | MAX_HOLD |
| 11 | `incomplete_trigger_bar` | `TriggerBarComplete` is false (a minute of the evaluation bucket was missing) | an incomplete bar |
| 12 | `adjusted_instrument` | the trade's `InstrumentRun.Adjustments` holds an applied action with an ex-date after `TradeDate` (the prices of the day were scaled; an external chart can show the unadjusted or the vendor-adjusted figure) | added: ISS-18 (c), the chart differences of ISS-21 |
| 13 | `tick_fallback` | `TickFallback` is true (the NSE default tick was used, not the master's) | added: the tick-grid row |

- **Roadmap categories that are not trades, or cannot be told apart, on the code read:** *special session*: the replay excludes a special session (`SessionExclusion.SpecialSession`: the Saturday and Muhurat sessions in the files, ISS-20), so no trade is ever dated on one; it becomes a spot check (V3). *end_of_data*: those trades are in `OpenAtEnd` (the day's bars ran out, which is broader than the end of the stored data) and get no card; a spot check (V3). *gap exit* and *hole through a stop* are one test on a `SimTrade` (the exit price beyond the stop); whether the gap was a real jump or a missing minute needs the bars: the card (which has the day's bars) shows the minute before the exit, and the owner tells which it was. *The first evaluation of a session* has no field on a trade; the reading above is the one derivable from the definition (UNVERIFIED that it is what the roadmap meant; for a daily strategy the overnight order, category 7, is the nearest case). Targets and gap fills on a target (`TargetGapExits`), an ambiguous minute (`AmbiguousMinutes`), a late square-off and a zero-volume minute are visible only in the bars or the counters; they are **not** sampled (the card would show them only by chance), and the counters are in `run.json`.
- **A category the pooled runs do not contain is listed in `pack.json` as `occurs: false`** (a statement of presence, never a count), so that an absent category is a visible fact and not a silent gap.

**V3 — The cover rule, and the spot checks.**
- **The cover rule.** After the random part, go through the categories in the order of the table. A category that occurs and is already covered, by a random trade or by an earlier edge pick, needs nothing. Otherwise pick the trade of that category (not already in the pack) with the lowest `SeededRank.Of(seed, "edge|" + code + "|" + trade key)`; the trade also covers every other category it belongs to. So the number of extra trades is at most the number of categories (13 today, so a pack is at most 20 + 13 = 33), usually fewer; the roadmap's "at most 29" is its nine categories and is replaced by this bound (question 2). Recommended: a random trade that already belongs to a category counts as its trade (the owner's time is the constraint; the category is still checked). The alternative, one extra trade per category always, is the roadmap's wording; it costs a few more trades.
- **Spot checks** are lines of the log with no card, read from the run records: one `excluded_session` for each reason that occurs (`special_session`, `short_session`, `missing_session`, from `InstrumentRun.Notes`, which are cut at 100 per instrument; the counts are exact), one `flagged_day` (`InstrumentRun.Discontinuities`: a jump of more than 20 % that gets no entry, ISS-20), and one `open_at_end` trade of `run.OpenAtEnd` (a position whose day's bars ran out: the end of the data or a truncated day). Each is chosen by `SeededRank` over the candidates (instrument, date), and each names the instrument and the date and asks one question: *does an external chart agree that this day was a special session, a gap of this size, or that the bars of this day ended where the data does?* A spot check is optional for P3 and signed or marked skipped with a reason (recommended: do the `flagged_day` and the `excluded_session` lines, they are one chart lookup each).

**V4 — The verb `verify-pack` (and why a verb of its own).** The command line, strict like `simulate` (the same parser rules, read in the invariant culture, a repeat or an unknown flag is a usage error):
`verify-pack --strategies <id[,id…]> (--symbols <a,b,…> | --basket <name|id>) --from <yyyy-MM-dd> --to <yyyy-MM-dd> --seed <text> [--count <1..100>] (--gross | --costs <sheet-id> [--capital …] [--profile …] [--max-cost-r …]) [--acknowledge-csv-adjusted] [--force]`, and
`verify-pack --check <path-to-check-log.csv>` (V8, Part B; alone). A `--fills` option is not offered in Part A (modelled fills only); see question 11 for the `no-slippage` alternative.
- **Why a new verb and not `--trade-keys`/`--seed` on `simulate`** (the extension point P3-04c left): a pack spans several strategies, and `simulate` is one run of one strategy with one run key; a draw across strategies needs the runs side by side and one seed for the pool. `simulate --trade-keys` would render cards of one run and could not express the draw. A `--trade-keys` option is **not added** (nothing needs it: a trade is checked in the pack that drew it, and after a fix the answer is a new seed and a new pack); `BuildTradeCardsQuery(Run, TradeKeys)` is used unchanged, once per run.
- **Pipeline** (the order of `simulate` through `SimulatePipeline`, then the pool): parse, check the configuration, resolve the universe once, the tick gate, then for each strategy in the order given: `RunSimulationQuery` with the registered defaults (`engine.BuiltIns`; **no `--param`**: a pack checks the rule set as shipped, and the parameter values are recorded in `pack.json`), the timeframe of the built-in, the modelled fills (`FillRules.Default`; no `--fills`), the cost choice given. Then the draw (V1), the edge picks (V3), the spot checks, `BuildTradeCardsQuery` per run with its chosen keys (a stale database is `simulation.cards_stale`, exit 1, nothing written), render, write. A duplicate strategy id is a usage error. The same exit codes as `simulate` (0, 1, 2, 3, 130).
- **The runs are the P3-04a pipeline unchanged**, so a pack is only as fast as the runs (the timed run of the 214 instruments, UNVERIFIED, is the owner's open item); each run record stays in memory until its cards are read (the order of magnitude of a record is the 217,000-trade bound of P3-04b1, UNVERIFIED for two or three at once; the explicit benchmark prints it).
- **The real extraction (the coupling the review found).** `SimulateCommand` keeps its pipeline `private`: `Rules`, `Request`, `CardsAsync`, `Files`, `ExtraLines`, `Manifest`, `Usage`, `RefuseAsync`, `Problems`, `TickProblems` and `Catalog`, and `SimulateOptions.ReadSymbols`, `ReadBasket`, `ReadCosts` and the date reader are `private`. Duplicating them would fork the pipeline. This slice **extracts a `SimulatePipeline` (internal, `XDrishti.Api/Simulate`)** that holds what both verbs share — resolve the universe and gate the tick (`UniverseResolver`, `TickUnitCheck`), build the request from a strategy id, options and fill rules (`Request`, `Rules`), run `RunSimulationQuery`, label (`RunLabels`), the second read (`CardsAsync`), and the console helpers (`Usage`, `RefuseAsync`, `Problems`, `TickProblems`) — and `SimulateCommand` calls it with no change of behaviour (the tests of P3-04b2 and P3-04c stay green and are the test of the extraction). `Files`, `ExtraLines` and `Manifest` stay with `SimulateCommand` (they are the run folder's). `SimulateOptions.ReadSymbols`, `ReadBasket`, `ReadCosts` and the date reader become `internal` and `VerifyPackOptions` calls them. `VerbDispatch`/`Verb` gain `VerifyPack` (the first argument, tested like `simulate` and `cost-sheet`, before the older rules) and `Program.cs` gains the branch; `SimulateHost.Create` is reused as is (UNVERIFIED that it needs no registration: the pack uses the services `simulate` uses). `SimulateLog` gains the lines of AC 13. These files are also touched by P3-10 (`--store`) and P3-06 (`ReadTimeframe`): this slice is built last and rebased on main after them.
- **`scripts/verify-pack.sh`** is `scripts/simulate.sh` with the verb name changed (the same Keychain item, the same `Export__Directory`).

**V5 — The pack folder.** `<Export:Directory>/verify/<pack-id>/`, git-ignored (`data/**`), `0700`/`0600`, written atomically by `ExportFolderWriter` with the overwrite policy of P3-04b1 E7 (an identical pack is `unchanged`; anything else needs `--force`, and the old folder becomes `<pack-id>.previous`). **The writer gains one optional parameter, the folder name below the root, default `sim`** (today `ExportPaths.SimFolder`); the existing callers and tests are unchanged. The format string is `xd-verify-pack-1`. The files:
- **`pack.json`** (identity and sources only: nothing that names an outcome of a row): the seed; the sampler version (`verify-sampler-1`); `count`; per strategy its id, its parameters as run, its run key, the engine version, the schema, the data range (first and last bars), the universe label and kind, the cost choice and the labels of that run; per instrument that has a trade in the pack the first 12 hex and the full SHA-256 of its bar stream (`InstrumentRun.BarsSha256`); per pack row its sequence number, symbol, side, date and run key (**no source, cell, category or exit reason per row**); and the category table with `occurs: true|false` (a statement about the pools, not about a row). No count of any group, no sum or mean of R, no percentage. The text passes the whole-word scan of P3-04b1 AC 6.
- **`README.txt`**: the banner of each run (`RunBanner`, so `engine: not yet verified (P3-08)` and `costs: none` are the first lines), what the folder is and what it is not (nothing in it is evidence of an edge), the seed, the protocol steps of V6 in a short form, and the instruction to keep the folder on this machine.
- **`index.html`** and **`cards/<nnn>-<ticker>-<side>-<date>-<exit>.html`**: the cards are those of `TradeCardHtml` unchanged (rendered with the labels of the card's own run), named by `CardNames.Of(seq, packSize, trade)`; **the card file names omit the exit reason** (`cards/<nnn>-<ticker>-<side>-<date>.html`, a pack-only name function beside `CardNames`, which includes it), and the index is the pooled table of **sequence, symbol, side and date only** and a relative link, under the visible banners of every run, inert and escaped by the rules of P3-04c C5, with no statistic.
- **`engine-values.csv`**: for each row the engine's side of the comparison, **including the row's source (`random`/`edge`/`spot`) and its categories** (V7 column map). **It is a separate file on purpose** (V7).
- **`check-log.csv`**: the owner's log (V7), one row per pack row and per spot check, the engine columns absent.
- **`manifest.json`**: `ExportManifest.Build` with a pack key as its `run_key` (the SHA-256 of the seed, the sampler version, the count, the basket id or `none` and the sorted `strategy=run key` lines: a valid 64-character lower-case hex, UNVERIFIED that `RequireRunKey` is the only check on it) and the extra lines `pack.seed=…`, `pack.sampler=…`, `pack.count=…`, one `pack.run=<strategy>|<run key>` per run and one `pack.row=<seq>|<trade key>` per row (no source). The basket id goes in the manifest's `basketId` and in the lines of the pack key, because the run key does not cover it. `ExportKey.Of` covers all of it, so a hand-edited card is never `unchanged`; **the manifest hashes `check-log.csv` as written, and the owner edits it afterwards**, so the manifest check of `--check` and of a second run exempts that one file (V8). **The pack id** is `<seed>-<first 12 hex of the pack key>`.
- Two runs into two roots, with the same seed, strategies, window and data, give identical bytes (the determinism of P3-04c). The check log is written once with its header and identifiers; the owner edits it afterwards, and a second run of the verb never overwrites an edited log silently (the overwrite policy above refuses and names the file). **`--force` after the owner started the log moves his work to `<pack-id>.previous` and writes a blank log**: the verb prints a loud warning first (the log differs from the blank header; the old folder is kept) and **refuses if `<pack-id>.previous` already exists** (never a second overwrite of an earlier copy).

**V6 — The protocol (written into `docs/research/p3-hand-check.md`, and its short form into the README).** What the owner does, and what is fixed in advance so that the check is not a matter of taste. **The protocol is the owner's (Q6); the values below are recommended.**
1. *Before the draw:* write the seed and the window in the record; decide the hold-out (Q7, question 6); do not look at any trade of the window.
2. *Bar convention:* a bar is labelled by its **start**, in India time (the card, the log and the stored data agree on it; an external chart must be set to the same: UNVERIFIED for each vendor, the owner confirms on the first trade).
3. *Comparison view:* the **1-minute chart** of the trade day, resampled by the chart itself to the strategy's timeframe with the first bucket anchored at 09:15 (15m buckets begin 09:15, 09:30, …); not official daily candles and not a chart whose daily bar differs by session definition.
4. *Per trade, in this order:* (a) with the rule text of the card, find the evaluation bar and read the levels from the chart (for ORB: the high and low of the first `or_min` minutes, whether the close crossed, the stop kind); (b) **do not open the cards, the index's links or `engine-values.csv` first** (a card shows the engine's marks and its exit reason; the log and the index are built without them, but the convention is the owner's): write the entry minute and price you expect, the stop, the target and the exit (the instant and the price) into `check-log.csv` **before** opening `engine-values.csv` (the log has no engine columns for this reason); (c) compare; (d) for the rows against an external chart, read the open, high, low and close of the minutes at the entry, the stop, the target and the exit **on the external chart** and write them in the log; (e) sign the row (`agree` or `disagree`).
5. *Tolerances:* a price to **one tick** (the tick printed on the card; a filled price on its tick grid; an exact price compared to the printed decimals), R to **0.01**, a time to the **minute**; a net charge line to the paisa when the pack is costed (V10). The tolerances are the roadmap's.
6. *Which trades go to an external chart:* at least **5**, of which at least one is a `sell`, one is from `adjusted_instrument` if it occurs, and one has the 09:15 bar repaired by the importer where the card says so (ISS-21); the rest are checked against the card's own chart, which is drawn from the stored bars, so those rows check the engine against the data, and the external rows check the data and the conventions as well (question 1).
7. *The defect classes (V9) and the triage order.*
8. *After any model or implementation fix:* the pack is void (the engine version, and so the run key and the pack key, change); a **new seed and a new pack** are drawn and checked in full, edge picks included; the earlier rows stay in the record as history and the earlier seed is never reused.

**V7 — The check log, the engine values, the blindness and the column map.** `check-log.csv` (RFC 4180, UTF-8, `\n`, the escaping of P3-04b1 E3 against spreadsheet formulas) has the columns, in order: `seq`, `trade_key`, `kind` (`trade` or `spot`), then the owner's: `chart` (`card` or `external:<name>`), `seen_entry_time`, `seen_entry_price`, `seen_stop`, `seen_target`, `seen_exit_time`, `seen_exit_price`, `seen_exit_reason` (`stop`, `target`, `square_off`, `max_hold`), `seen_r`, `ext_open`, `ext_high`, `ext_low`, `ext_close` (the external minute at the entry; further minutes in the notes), `verdict` (`agree`, `disagree`, `not_decidable`, `skipped`), `finding`, `notes`, `signed_on`. **It has no `source`, `categories` or engine column** (a category name such as `stop_gap` or `exit_target` names the outcome). `engine-values.csv` has, by `seq` and `trade_key`: `source`, `categories`, and the engine's columns below. Prices are decimals with a dot; times `HH:mm` India time or `yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm`. The blindness is partly a convention (the card, opened, shows the engine's marks): the first five rows, the external ones, are done blind.
**The column map (what is compared with what).** The owner can read **exact levels** off a chart and cannot read filled prices (they depend on the tick rounding and on slippage, `a = (1 tick + 0.05 × the minute's range [+ 2 ticks on a stop exit])`, ADR 0038 decision 4), so there are two checks:
| Owner's column | Engine column (`engine-values.csv`) | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| `seen_entry_price` | `entry_price` (`SimTrade.EntryPrice`, exact) | one tick |
| `seen_stop` | `stop_price` | one tick |
| `seen_target` | `target_price` (empty when none) | one tick |
| `seen_exit_price` | `exit_price` (exact) | one tick |
| `seen_entry_time`, `seen_exit_time` | `entry_ist`, `exit_ist` | the minute, exact |
| `seen_exit_reason` | `exit_reason` | **exact match** |
| `seen_r` (own arithmetic from the exact levels) | `r_exact` | 0.01 |
**The filled-price check is separate and labelled "model-derived, not read off a chart":** `engine-values.csv` also carries `entry_filled_price`, `exit_filled_price`, `entry_fill`, `slippage_r`, `r_filled` (and `r_net` and the charge lines when costed); the owner recomputes them from the card's printed operands with a calculator, in a second sheet section of the protocol, not from a chart; they are compared by the tool only when he writes `calc_entry_filled`, `calc_exit_filled` and `calc_r_filled` in extra columns (optional). `not_decidable` is allowed only where the 1-minute data cannot tell the order inside a minute (ISS-18 d) and needs a note; it counts as explained, not signed.

**V8 — `verify-pack --check` and `CheckLogReader` (Part B).** Reads the filled log and `engine-values.csv` of the same folder, joined by `trade_key`, and prints, **per row and per field** of the map of V7: `ok`, `ok within tolerance`, `beyond tolerance: <field>`, `mismatch: seen_exit_reason`, `missing: <field>`, or `unsigned`; then, never before, the pack verdict lines: rows signed, rows against an external chart, rows that disagree with no finding (**unexplained**), the findings and their class, and `pack: complete` or `pack: incomplete (reasons)`. A row whose `verdict` is `agree` with any field beyond tolerance is `inconsistent` and counts as unexplained until changed or given a finding. It writes nothing and never prints that P3 passes. **Safety:** `--check` takes two paths it derives from the folder; it **refuses a symbolic link** and a path outside the folder; it caps the log at 5 MB and `engine-values.csv` at 20 MB and the cells at 2,000 characters (exit 2 beyond them); **every owner text echoed to the console (notes, a symbol, a finding) is stripped of Control and Format characters** (terminal escape sequences; the CSV defusing of V7 covers only writing); it prints no R value and no statistic. **Which files are verified:** the manifest is verified for every file **except `check-log.csv`** (the one file the owner edits); a card, the index, `pack.json`, `README.txt` or `engine-values.csv` that differs from the manifest makes the check exit 2 with the file named. Exit codes: 0 complete, 1 incomplete, 2 usage, unreadable input, a changed pack file or a refusal above.

**V9 — Disagreements: the classes, the triage and the exit rule.**
- **Triage, cheapest test first.** For a row beyond tolerance or marked `disagree`: (1) **data** — compare the stored bars the card shows with the external chart at the cited minutes: if the stored bar differs beyond the tolerance, it is a data defect, whatever the engine did (the known causes first: ISS-21 the widened 09:15 bar, ISS-20 an unadjusted jump, the adjusted-at-source prices off the tick grid, ISS-18 c); (2) **model** — the stored bars are right and the engine did what its ADR says, but the ADR's convention is not what the owner intends or is ambiguous (the entry limit at the trigger close plus 0.1 %, the minute-path rule, a fill at the open of a gap, the square-off at the open of 15:15): the owner decides the rule, and the fix is an ADR amendment and then code; (3) **implementation** — the engine contradicts its ADR or spec: a bug; the fix starts with a test that fails (the place for it is P3-08's oracle or the walker's tests) and the engine version changes. A finding has an id, a class, the trade key, the evidence (the minute and both values), the decision and the commit or ADR that closed it.
- **The exit rule (recommended; Q6).** The pack passes the owner's check when **all** hold: (a) every row of the pack (the trades and the spot checks the owner did not skip with a reason) is signed `agree`, or `disagree` with a closed or accepted finding; (b) **zero unexplained disagreements** (a row beyond tolerance with no finding, or `inconsistent`); (c) **zero open model or implementation defects** — every one has been fixed and the check redone on a new pack with a new seed that has itself no such finding; (d) a data defect is recorded as an issue of the roadmap and is either a known class that does not change the trade's mechanics (ISS-20, ISS-21) or is accepted in chat by the owner as not affecting the pass; (e) at least 5 rows against an external chart, 20 random trades at least, one trade of each category that occurs; (f) the record of V6 (below) is committed; (g) **the owner says in chat that P3 passes**. The rule is stated to be honest about its power: 20 random trades with no disagreement give, **only informally**, an upper bound of about 14 to 15 % (the rule of three, 3/20; exact 13.9 %) on the share of *independent units* that would fail, and that is **not a bound on the share of trades**: the sample is equal-weight over cells and the trades of one instrument are not independent, and the edge picks are not part of it; read it as "a defect that touches more than about a seventh of the instruments or cells would probably have shown". The edge picks are there for the rare paths; neither says that a strategy has an edge, or that the engine is free of defects.
- **The record** `docs/research/p3-hand-check.md` (committed) holds, per pack: the seed, the pack id, the run keys, the engine version, the data range, the per-instrument bar-stream hashes, the trade keys with source and categories, the findings with class and closing reference, the date and the owner's statement. **It holds no price, no R value and no count of any group** ("no number from a real-data run is quoted outside its run folder", roadmap, *What a P3 number means*); the trade keys name the owner's symbols, so the repository's privacy is the owner's call (UNVERIFIED whether it is private).

**V10 — Gross and net.** The first pack is run `--gross` and every card says `costs: none`: the arithmetic checked by hand is `R exact` and `R filled`. Net R by hand needs the charge sheet (Q3, ISS-25); the bill check of P3-03f (charges within 0.05 rupee per bill, a different tolerance from the paisa of a hand recomputation) is its own exit check. When the sheet is loaded and `checked`, **the same seed** with `--costs <sheet-id>` gives a costed pack (a different run key, so a different pack key; the same draw only while the trades are the same: a cost skip can remove one, in which case the draw differs and the pack says so): the owner recomputes the 16 charge lines and `R net` of three intraday trades from the card by hand against his sheet. Recommended: this is an addendum that does not hold up the exit criterion (question 4).

**V11 — Data privacy.** The pack is files in `data/exports/verify/` (git-ignored, `0700`/`0600`); the verb reads the owner's database and writes only files; no network call, no telemetry; the cards have no script and no network reference; the owner types only a symbol and a date into an external chart in his own browser; the log and the engine values never leave the machine; the committed record has no price or R (V9). No secret is read except the database password of the Keychain item `xdrishti/db/app`, as for `simulate`.

**V12 — Which runs are checked and in what order (recommended; question 5).** **Round A:** ORB v1 on the owner's stored data, modelled fills, gross, with the seed written first. **Round B:** `VWAP_PULLBACK` (P3-05) and `BASE_BREAKOUT` (P3-06), as a second pack with a different seed. **Closing P3 on ORB alone is not what the roadmap says, and this draft does not read it into the wording:** the P3 row lets only `BASE_BREAKOUT` slide to P4, "the exit criterion is then met with the two intraday strategies (the owner's call)". Ending on one strategy is therefore an **explicit decision of the owner to amend the exit criterion** (question 5): if he takes it, a record and an ADR (0046 or an amendment of it) say that P3 passed on ORB only, that "balanced over strategies" is moot with one strategy, that nothing hand-checked `VWAP_PULLBACK` (its VWAP, pullback and relative-volume rules; P3-04d would be needed to read them), and that the swing walk and the overnight order rest on tests and P3-08 until P3-06 runs by hand.

## Acceptance criteria
Numbered, each testable. Fixtures are invented (the F-days of P3-03, P3-04a's seeding through the real stores); nothing is read from `data/`. Pure tests in `XDrishti.SimExport.Tests`, use case and options in `XDrishti.Api.IntegrationTests` (Testcontainers; the verb called as in P3-04b2 through `SimulateHost`), architecture in `XDrishti.Architecture.Tests`. **No new package**: HTML and CSV are read with the small readers P3-04c wrote in the test project.
1. **The seed is required and strict.** `--seed` missing, empty, longer than 40 characters, with a character outside `[A-Za-z0-9._-]` or repeated is a usage error (exit 2); `--count` 0, 101, `abc`, `1.5` is a usage error; `--strategies` with a duplicate id, an unknown id, or an id that differs in case is a usage error naming the known ids; `--param` is an unknown option; `--check` with anything else is a usage error; the cost choice is required as for `simulate`.
2. **The sampler: balance, hand-calculated.** Given hand-made runs (strategies X and Y; X with instruments A and B over years 2024 and 2025, Y with A) and counts 1, 2, 3, 6, 20 and 1,000 then: the result has at most `count` trades, no repeated key, none from `OpenAtEnd`; strategy picks differ by at most one while both have unused trades; inside a strategy, instrument picks differ by at most one while both have unused trades; inside an instrument, year picks likewise; when the pool is smaller than `count` the whole pool is returned and no more; the examples written in the test (cell counts, with the tie order fixed by cell labels whose `SeededRank` was computed with an independent tool) equal the result pick by pick; one strategy only is valid; a cell with one trade is drawn once. **Year spread:** on a hand-made pool of one strategy with at least `count` instruments each trading in 2024, 2025 and 2026 (partial), the years of the `count` picks differ by at most one pick from each other; on a pool whose instruments each trade in one year only, each pick has that instrument's year and the test asserts no more than that (year is balanced only as far as the instrument precedence allows).
3. **The sampler: seed and order.** The same seed gives the identical list; the list does not depend on the order of the trades or of the runs; a different seed gives a different set for a pool of at least 50 trades per cell; the draw reads no clock, `Random` or machine value (a source scan); a seed that differs only in case is a different seed.
4. **The edge table.** Given one hand-made trade per category (13) and a trade in none then each is classified by exactly the categories the table of V2 says (a table-driven test; a trade can belong to several); a stop at exactly the stop price is not `stop_gap`; a Sell with the exit above the stop is; `entry_after_hole` is false for an overnight entry; `first_evaluation` is true at the first-entry time of ORB's recorded definition (09:30 with `or_min` 15, and 09:45 with `or_min` 30) and false one bar later, and false (not an exception) for a definition that does not parse; the table is iterated from the enums of `ExitReason` and `EntryFill` and the test fails if a new value has no category or no recorded reason for having none.
5. **The cover rule.** Given a pool in which categories 1, 2, 3 and 10 occur, the random part already holds a trade of 1, and exactly one trade belongs to both 2 and 3 (no other trade is in 3) then the edge picks are, in the table's order, the lowest-ranked trade of 2 (which, if it is that trade, also covers 3, and otherwise 3 gets its own pick) and one for 10; the test states both outcomes by construction (the shared trade the lowest of 2, and not); the number of picks is at most the number of categories; a category that does not occur is `occurs: false` and adds nothing; no edge pick repeats a random trade; the result is the same for the same seed and different for a different seed (for categories with at least 5 trades).
6. **Spot checks.** Given run records with notes of each exclusion reason, a flagged day and an `OpenAtEnd` trade then one line of each kind that occurs is produced, none for a kind that does not, chosen by `SeededRank`; the notes cut at 100 do not change the choice's determinism; a spot line has no card entry.
7. **The use case and the second read.** Given two runs and a draw across them then `BuildTradeCardsQuery` is called once per run that has a chosen trade, with exactly its keys, never with an `OpenAtEnd` key; a stale database (a changed bar-stream hash, an added action, a changed tick, a vanished instrument) gives `simulation.cards_stale`, exit 1, nothing written and no staging folder left; the cards equal the cards `simulate --cards` renders for the same trades (byte for byte, a golden and a cross-check test).
8. **The pack files.** Given the F1/F2/split fixtures and `--strategies ORB --count 3` then the folder holds exactly the files of V5 with the modes of P3-04b1; `pack.json` parses and holds the seed, the sampler version, the run key, the engine version, the data range, the labels, the bar-stream hash of every instrument that has a card, each row's symbol, side and date and the category table with `occurs`, and **no per-row source, category or exit reason**; the index, the card file names, the manifest extra lines and the log contain none either (asserted by a scan for every category code and exit-reason spelling); `engine-values.csv` has them; it holds **no count of a group, no sum or mean of R and no percentage** (the whole-word scan and the `\d\s?%` scan of P3-04b2 AC 9 find nothing in `pack.json`, `README.txt`, `index.html` or any card); `README.txt` begins with the banner of every run, names the seed and says that nothing in it is evidence of an edge; `engine-values.csv` holds the columns of V5 and equals the record's trades to the printed precision; `check-log.csv` has the header of V7, one row per pack row and per spot check and **none of the engine's values, sources or categories**; the basket id is in the pack key (two baskets with equal members and different ids give different pack ids); the manifest lists every file with its hash and carries the extra lines of V5; the export key equals `ExportKey.Of` over them; every `href` of the index matches the pattern of P3-04c C5 and exists.
9. **Determinism and overwrite.** Two runs into two roots with the same seed give identical bytes for every file; a second run in the same root is `unchanged`; a different seed is a different pack id and folder; a hand-edited log or card is never `unchanged` and the verb refuses without `--force` naming the file; with `--force` over a log that differs from the blank header a loud warning is printed and the old folder is renamed to `<pack-id>.previous`; with `<pack-id>.previous` already present `--force` is refused; the writer's new folder parameter has the default `sim` and the tests of P3-04b1 and P3-04b2 pass unchanged.
10. **The check mode (Part B).** Given a pack and a log in which: every value equals the engine's, one price is one tick off, one is two ticks off, one R is 0.011 off, `seen_exit_reason` differs in one row (a mismatch, with no tolerance), one row is `agree` with a value beyond tolerance, one row is `disagree` with a finding, one has `not_decidable` with a note, one is unsigned, and one external row lacks the external columns then the output has for each the line of V8 (`ok`, `ok` within a tick, `beyond tolerance: …`, `inconsistent`, explained, explained, `unsigned`, `missing: …`), the unexplained count, the external count and `pack: incomplete` with the reasons; with every row signed and explained and 5 external rows it says `pack: complete`; it prints no R value and no performance token; it never prints "P3 passes" (a scan); a log edited so that a `trade_key` is not in the pack, a duplicated row, a file that is not CSV, a symbolic link in place of the log, a log over 5 MB, and a changed card or `engine-values.csv` are exit 2 with a message; a changed `check-log.csv` alone is not a manifest failure; a note holding ESC or U+202E is echoed without them.
11. **Gross and costed.** A pack of a gross run says `costs: none` first; the same seed with `--costs` and the test sheet of P3-03c gives a different pack key, the engine-values file has `r_net` and the charge total, and a trade skipped for cost is not drawn (the draw is over `run.Trades`).
12. **Inert and safe.** The scans of P3-04c AC 5 (tags, attributes, style contexts; a hostile symbol, a Control or Format character) pass for the pooled `index.html`; a symbol with `&` or a comma in `check-log.csv` and `engine-values.csv` is quoted per RFC 4180 and a cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is defused.
13. **Logging and secrets.** New `[LoggerMessage]` lines in `SimulateLog` (ids 4419 to 4422, the central numbering; P3-10 has 4415 to 4418): a pack written (rows, bytes), a pack unchanged, a check read (rows, complete or not); none carries a price, a bar, a path or the seed's draw order; `LogEventIdTests` passes; the secret scan of P3-04b2 covers the pack.
14. **Architecture.** `XDrishti.SimExport` stays pure (no I/O in the sampler, the table, the log reader or the HTML); only `XDrishti.Api` references it; the pooling and the writing are in the verb host, not in a handler; `BuildTradeCardsHandler` is unchanged; the exact-list test of public types gains the new ones; each new rule has a sentinel.
15. **The protocol and the docs.** `docs/research/p3-hand-check.md` exists with V6, V9 and the empty record table; ADR 0046 is written; the roadmap row, doc 03 §4, doc 17, `data/README.md` (`exports/verify/<pack-id>/`) and `scripts/README.md` are updated; `scripts/build-docs.sh` is run.
16. **End to end.** Given the Testcontainers database of P3-04b2 (the F1 day, the F2 day, a split instrument, a basket of the three) when `verify-pack --strategies ORB --basket … --seed t1 --count 3 --gross` runs then the cards carry the right trades (entry, stop, target, exit and R equal to the record's), the chart of the split instrument is halved once, `adjusted_instrument` is in the categories of that trade, no table changed, and `verify-pack --check` (Part B) on the untouched log says `pack: incomplete` with every row `unsigned`.

## Non-functional

The explicit benchmark prints the phases and the bytes of a pack.
- **Memory:** one run record per strategy at a time until its cards are read; one instrument's bars at a time in the second read.
- **Security and privacy:** V11; the escaping and inertness of P3-04c C5; files `0600` in `0700`; the seed and the keys are not secrets but the pack is a private file.

## Open questions
Numbered; each starts with the recommendation. **1 to 5 are Q6** (the verification protocol, the owner's); **6 is Q7** (data). 7 to 11 are decisions the draft took that the owner may want to turn.

1. **Q6 (a) Which external chart, and the depth of 1-minute history.** Recommendation: use the 1-minute chart you already read prices on (TradingView, Kite or Dhan), set to India time, 1-minute, regular session only, prices as the vendor adjusts them; the first trade settles the bar-label convention. Because 1-minute history on a charting site may not reach back to 2024 (UNVERIFIED for each vendor and plan), draw the pack first, then pick the 5 or more external rows from the pack's trades **that the chosen chart can show**, taking the most recent dates first; write down which source and why in the record. Consequence: those 5 are not a random 5 of the 20 (the other 15 are still the random ones against the card's chart, drawn from the stored bars, which checks the engine against the data, not the data against the world); if the chart reaches back only a few weeks, a re-run of the window to its last months for the pack's external rows is the other way (the data ends 2026-04-30, ISS-19). The alternative is the broker's own 1-minute history by API for the same instrument and day (P0-13's allow-list; a data comparison, not a chart; not built here).
2. **Q6 (b) The pack: 20 random plus one per edge category, and the category list.** Recommendation: the draw of V1 and the table of V2 (13 categories: the roadmap's, with *gap exit* and *hole through a stop* merged because the record cannot tell them apart, *special session* and *end_of_data* turned into spot checks because there is no trade or no card, and seven added from the code: `entry_at_limit`, `entry_after_hole`, `overnight_entry`, `exit_target`, `exit_square_off`, `adjusted_instrument`, `tick_fallback`), the cover rule (a category already in the random 20 needs no extra trade), so a pack is 20 plus at most 13. Consequence: more categories than the roadmap's nine means a longer check (up to 33 against 29 trades); the cover rule usually brings it back to about 25 to 28 (UNVERIFIED, depends on the data). The owner can trim the table (each row is one line in code) or choose one extra trade per category always.
3. **Q6 (c) Which instruments or basket, and which window, for the first real pack.** Recommendation: the whole stored set the timed run of P3-04b2 can do (214 instruments, UNVERIFIED that a basket of them exists; otherwise a `--symbols` list), the full stored window 2024-04-01 to 2026-04-30 (ISS-19: about 2.1 years, so three calendar years in the stratification, of which 2024 and 2026 are partial), so that strategy, instrument and year all have something to balance; the trades are then spread over ~20 different instruments, which suits a check of mechanics. Consequence: the second read touches ~20 to 33 instruments (seconds); the instruments with a flagged day (SIEMENS, TMPV, VEDL, ISS-20) are in the pool and are reached by the spot check, not by the random draw.
4. **Q6 (d) The exit rule, and gross against net.** Recommendation: the rule of V9 (every row signed, zero unexplained, zero open model or implementation defects after a new pack with a new seed, at least 5 external, the owner says so in chat), on **gross** figures; the net addendum of V10 after the sheet and the bill check, not before. Consequence: this differs from the roadmap text that "P3-09 waits" for the charge sheet (roadmap, the costs risk): P3 would then pass with `costs: none` on its labels and ISS-25 stays open into P4, where costs are always on (doc 08 §8); the alternative is to hold the exit until Q3 is answered and `cost-sheet check` passes. A data defect (V9 d) does not block when it is a known class, or when you accept it in chat.
5. **Q6 (e) Close on ORB alone, or check P3-05 and P3-06 too; and P3-04d.** Recommendation: by the central order P3-05 and P3-06 are built before this slice, so run **both rounds**: Round A (ORB) and Round B (the other two, as in the roadmap, where only `BASE_BREAKOUT` may slide to P4). Closing P3 on ORB alone is **a change to the exit criterion, not a reading of it**; if you want it (for example because P3-05 or P3-06 slips), say so explicitly and the record and ADR 0046 will state it, with the claim narrowed as V12 says. Build P3-04d (conditions on the cards, 1.3 agent-days, no spec yet) only for Round B, where VWAP and relative volume are not readable off the chart; a swing trade's card does not chart its exit day (it says so), so Round B also needs either a small card extension (an exit-day strip, about 0.4 agent-days, UNVERIFIED, not specified) or the external chart for the exit day. Consequence: Round B is more owner time (a second pack) and may need P3-04d and the exit-day strip (sizes not in the 3.45); closing on ORB alone is sooner with a narrower claim, written in the record.
6. **Q7 (data): the hold-out and the 5-year backfill, as they touch this check.** Recommendation: the check does not wait for Q7. But **decide the hold-out before the draw**: if the last months (say from 2025-11-01 to 2026-04-30) are to be kept as a hold-out that no tuning sees until the P5 verdict, give the verb `--to 2025-10-31`, because looking at 20 to 33 trades of a month is looking at it; if you do not decide, the record says that all the stored months were looked at and there is no hold-out. The daily-history endpoint for index and VIX does not touch P3-09. A longer backfill would give the pack more years to balance and the external chart a longer reach, but the pack is redrawn then, since its bar-stream hashes change.
7. **Verb shape: a verb of its own (`verify-pack`), not `--trade-keys`/`--seed` on `simulate`.** Recommendation: as in V4. Consequence: one more entry in `VerbDispatch`, three helpers of `SimulateOptions` made `internal`, and the writer's folder parameter; the alternative adds two flags to `simulate` but cannot draw across strategies.
8. **Blind log with the engine values apart (no category, source or exit reason in the log, the index, the card names or `pack.json`), and the comparison done by the tool.** Recommendation: as in V7 and V8 (Part B 0.6 agent-days, the first thing cut). Consequence: the check cannot be fooled by reading the engine's number first, only by the convention of not opening the card first; without it the owner compares by eye on one sheet and the exit rule rests on his word.
9. **The seed and the re-draw.** Recommendation: the owner writes `p3-check-1` (or any text) before looking; after any model or implementation fix he uses the next name; the seed text is recorded in the committed record. Consequence: the seed is not secret and is not chosen by the system (no clock); a seed tried repeatedly until the 20 look easy defeats the check, so the record keeps every seed used.

10. **Split into Part A and Part B.** Recommendation: two slices, `P3-09` (the pack builder, 2.85) and `P3-09b` (`--check`, 0.6): the whole is 3.45, over the specs README's 3. Consequence: the owner can start the check with the pack and compare by eye until Part B lands; one spec file may carry both until the owner confirms.
11. **First pack with `--fills no-slippage`?** Recommendation: **no**, keep the modelled fills (the rules the owner will actually trade under), and read the exact levels off the chart as V7 says (the exact entry, stop, target and exit do not depend on slippage; `r_exact` does not either). The alternative, `no-slippage`, makes the filled prices equal the exact ones so the owner can compare more fields by eye, but then the run is not the modelled one (a different run key and label `fills: modelled, no slippage`), and the slippage and tick rows, which are the ones most likely to hide an arithmetic bug, are not exercised by the check; they would be left to the calculator check of V7. Consequence of the recommendation: the filled-price rows are a calculator check, not a chart check.

## Risks
- **The check proves less than it sounds.** 20 clean random trades bound the failure share at about 15 % (95 %, rule of three); the edge picks reach rare paths but a rare bug can still hide. Mitigation: V9 says so, and the pass statement is "the mechanics checked match a chart", never "the engine is correct" or "the strategy works"; P3-08 and the properties carry the rest.
- **Disagreement is blamed on the wrong layer.** A vendor chart differs in bar boundaries, adjustment and the 09:15 print (ISS-18 a, ISS-21), and the stored data is adjusted at the source (ISS-18 c). Mitigation: the triage order data, model, implementation (V9), the bar-label and view conventions fixed in advance (V6), the repaired 09:15 bar on the card.
- **The external chart cannot show the trade date.** History depth for 1-minute data on a charting product is limited (UNVERIFIED). Mitigation: question 1 (recent dates for the external rows, written down), the broker's history as a fallback.
- **Confirmation bias.** The engine's marks are on the card; the owner may see what the card shows. Mitigation: the log has no engine values, the external rows are done blind first, the tool does the comparison, and each external row records the vendor's minute values, not only a verdict.
- **The seed is shopped.** A seed chosen to give an easy sample. Mitigation: required before the draw, recorded, never defaulted, every seed in the record (question 9).
- **The card does not chart a later exit day.** A swing trade (P3-06) exits on a later date than the chart shows (the card says so). Mitigation: Round A needs none; Round B needs a small extension or the external chart (question 5).
- **Edge categories that never occur** (for example `exit_max_hold` with ORB, `overnight_entry`, `incomplete_trigger_bar` if the data has no hole): `occurs: false` is a visible fact; a category that did not occur is a path not checked by hand, and the record says so.
- **Owner time.** 10 to 14 hours for a pack, a similar amount for each re-draw after a fix. Mitigation: the cover rule, the order of the sheet, the timing of the first three rows; the estimate is UNVERIFIED.
- **The pack key and the writer.** The manifest builder is generic over a `run_key` (a 64-character hex) and `ExportFolderWriter` hardcodes the `sim` folder; both are used with a small additive change. UNVERIFIED that no other check on the manifest's run key exists; AC 8 covers it.
- **Coupling to the interim definition text.** The `first_evaluation` category reads the first-entry time from `DefinitionJson` (the interim canonical text of P3-04 until P3-02c); a change of that text changes the category and breaks its test on purpose (the same coupling P3-04c recorded for the rule view).
- **Sibling slices.** P3-08 (the oracle) and P3-10 (the result store) are drafted at the same time; neither is a dependency in the build; the numbers (ADR 0046, Amendment 4, ids 4419 to 4422) are the coordinator's central numbering.

### Disagreements between the roadmap row and the code, found while drafting
1. *Special session* is listed as an edge category, but the replay excludes special sessions (`SessionExclusion.SpecialSession`), so no trade can be dated on one: it is a spot check.
2. *`end_of_data`* is listed, but those trades are in `OpenAtEnd` (the day's bars ran out: broader than the end of the data), get no card (`CardSelection`), and `BuildTradeCardsQuery` refuses their keys (`unknown_trade`); an intraday strategy may have none on the owner's data (UNVERIFIED).
3. *Gap exit* and *a hole through a stop* are one test on a `SimTrade`; only the bars tell them apart. *The first evaluation of a session* has no field on a trade.
4. The row says the cards of P3-04c "render any set of trade keys"; they do, but within one run: a pack across strategies is several runs, hence a verb of its own; and the card charts the entry date only.
5. The row and the roadmap's cost line say P3-09 waits for the charge sheet; this draft recommends a gross check now and a net addendum later (question 4).
6. The row lists P3-05 and P3-06 as needs; the P3 row lets only `BASE_BREAKOUT` slide. Closing on ORB alone would amend the exit criterion; it is the owner's explicit decision (question 5), not a reading of the wording.
7. `ExportFolderWriter` hardcodes the `sim` folder, so the pack needs one additive parameter; P3-04c's note says only that the sampler uses `SeededRank` and the cards use `BuildTradeCardsQuery`.
8. The row says 2 agent-days; this draft counts 3.45 (Part A 2.85, Part B 0.6). The row's "at most 29" becomes "at most 20 plus the number of categories" (33 today).
9. `docs/research/p3-hand-check.md` is named by the row but does not exist (`docs/research/` holds only the P0 notes); it is created by this slice, with the protocol the owner approves.
