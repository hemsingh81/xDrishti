# P10-00 — Paper trading plan: how a paper trade becomes a graded record with no broker order, and what the owner does each week

- **Status:** Draft (written 2026-10-24; decisions below were researched under the owner's delegation; the owner's Approved is still needed before an agent builds it)
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P10 — Paper trading (>= 12 weeks from P7). Exit: >= 60 graded trades; positive expectancy after costs within the backtest band; drawdown within the Monte Carlo 90th percentile. Deliverables: follow plans on paper; weekly review; config changes only via the hypothesis loop.
- **Design refs:** docs/design/18 (P10 row), 04 §4 (human in the loop, ticket states), 09 §5, §9, §12, §13, 10 §6c, §8, 13 §2 (R14, R15, R19, R26, R32), 15 §1, 19 §3 and §6; ADR 0037/0038/0042/0044 (replay, fills, costs), 0083, 0090, 0093, 0094, 0095, 0096, 0097, 0099
- **Needs:** P7 built (plan record, approvals, arming, grading, journal, digest, phase check); P6 (cell snapshots, hypotheses, changelog); P5-05 (Monte Carlo primitives); P8-05 (`Metric`, states). Optional: P8-06 (charge sheet confirmation reader), P7-19/P7-20 (numeric levels, crossing alerts). ADRs: one per slice, next free ADR number at build time.
- **Size:** plan only; slices P10-01 to P10-06 total about 16.5 agent-days, plus 12 weeks of wall-clock time that no agent can shorten.
- **Safety:** no for the plan. Slices P10-01, P10-02 and P10-05 are marked `Safety: yes` (a new automatic writer, a setting that changes what the plan reads, owner-act verbs). **No order is placed anywhere in P10**; real orders start in P12 only behind the per-account mode setting (default OFF).

## Goal
Give the owner twelve weeks of **forward, rules-fixed, human-in-the-loop evidence**: every ticket they approve is followed to a timed, priced, costed paper trade; every week they read one deterministic review; the configuration cannot drift under the count; and at the end a phase check says, with its n and its caveats, whether the simulated paper results agree with the research. It tests the pipeline and the process, not the broker.

## What exists and what does not (verified 2026-10-24 against the code)
- **Exists:** the plan record and trails (`trading.plans`, `plan_items`, `plan_item_allocations` paper-only, `plan_events`, `workflow.approvals`); owner approve/modify (size-down)/reject; arming at 09:10 (`preopen`) and expiry at 15:40 (`plan-expiry`); grading by replaying every ticket through `RunSimulationQuery` (`GradePlansHandler`, `TicketGrader`) into `trading.grades`; the broker-sync journal (`trading.journal_*`); the weekly digest; the P7 phase check; cell snapshots, hypotheses, changelog; `Domain.Reporting.Metric`; the Monte Carlo primitives (`SeededRandom`, `MonteCarlo`).
- **Does not exist:** any live 1-minute bar (the feed keeps only the last price per instrument, ADR 0024; `market.bars_1m_live` of doc 05 is in no migration); anything that moves a ticket to Triggered or Closed (the table allows it; P7-20 says it stays unreachable); a position record for a Trading account (so the open book is `unavailable` and no ticket is primary by default, ADR 0092); a stored owner confirmation of the charge sheet (P8-06 reads it; ISS-25 open); any cell in Paper or Active state.

## Decisions (researched)
**D1. Where paper fills come from: the grading simulator over the official bars, after the close, for armed allocations.** Options: (a) simulate against the live minute feed; (b) the owner enters fills; (c) both. Chosen: **a single path that replays each armed ticket through the existing simulation use case on the stored 1-minute bars of its session** (the `eod` run stores them at 15:50), using the ADR 0038 rules and the stored charge sheet; extracted into one `TicketReplayer` that grading and capture both call. Why: there are no live minute bars to simulate on (ADR 0024; recording them is a new feed writer and storage, a SAFETY slice, for bars worse than the official ones); the backtest used the same bars and rules, so paper and research differ only by the days and the human steps; one code path cannot drift (ADR 0090 rejected a second simulator for the same reason). Rejected: owner-entered fills for the measured set (look-ahead, cherry-picking, 5 to 10 minutes per trade, a typed price is not evidence) and both (the typed fills would contaminate the count). Real trades the owner makes by hand anyway are read by P7-14 as a separate basis and never counted. Source: ADR 0024, 0038, 0090; `GradePlansHandler.cs`.
**D2. What paper adds over P7-13 grades, stated plainly.** The R of an armed ticket is identical to its grade (a test pins it). What is new: (1) only tickets the owner approved and the pre-open check armed (the chain really operated), (2) the owner's size, (3) entry and exit instants and rupees per account, (4) a forward-only record starting when the epoch starts, which nothing can back-fill. It adds **no execution realism**: touch is fill, no queue, no latency, no partial fills (`FillLimits.NotModelled`). That is P11's question. Every response says so.
**D3. Timestamps and prices without look-ahead.** The replay is causal minute by minute (P3-08 leak properties); entry = the fill minute's start and the ADR 0038 rule (open if within the limit, else the limit); exit = the first row of the exit table; filled prices include modelled slippage on the tick grid. The ticket's levels were fixed the evening before and its approval, modification and arming instants come from the real clock (`workflow.approvals.decided_at`, `plan_events.at`). An entry minute before the armed instant is set aside (`entered_before_armed`); the database also refuses it. Capture reads only up to the last closed session.
**D4. The unit of count is the ticket, in R.** One signal is one trade however many paper accounts it was sized for (ADR 0099 count-once); `r_sim` is the replay's R (net of the stored sheet when charged, else gross); rupees are per account at the owner's quantity. Counted = closed, primary group, measured (not straddle), current epoch. Reserve tickets the owner picks are shown, not counted (owner selection would enter the sample). Auto-approved trades (setting default OFF) are counted and labelled; recommended OFF for the measured epoch because the owner's approvals are the process under test.
**D5. Costs.** `Planning:Grading:CostSheetId` and `Capital` are the single source for grading and paper. Charges per leg use `CostModel.Leg` at the account quantity. "After costs" requires the owner's confirmation of the sheet (ISS-25, P8-05 rule); without it the criterion reads `not_evaluable: costs not confirmed`, never a gross pass. The sheet is part of the fingerprint, the confirmation is not (confirming is an owner act on a record, not a change of the system). **Revised 2026-10-25:** the confirmation's only reader is `IChargeSheetConfirmationSource` (ADR 0100), closed by an architecture test to the report loader and runs list, and no spec writes the row; so P10 cannot print net or judge C3/C4 until a separate owner-act slice (P10-07 or P8-06b: write the confirmation with fresh step-up and capability gate, widen the allow-list, supply P10-04's `ICostConfirmationFact`) is built. Until then C3/C4 read `not_evaluable: costs not confirmed`.
**D6. The exit criteria, precisely** (P10-04 and P10-06): *count* 60 counted trades and 84 elapsed days of the current epoch with 80 % session coverage; *expectancy within the band*: paper mean net R > 0 and not below the 5th percentile of the mean of n backtest trades drawn from the same cells in the same proportions (pre-epoch trades only), 10,000 seeded draws, with the paper mean's own 90 % bootstrap interval printed and a MET whose interval includes 0 carrying that in its label; *drawdown*: the paper largest peak-to-trough fall of cumulative R at its own n is no more than the 90th percentile of the same draws taken in sequence. Fewer than 30 trades: `insufficient`; 30 to 59: `provisional`; 60 or more: `value`. Why not the Lab's stored Monte Carlo: it is for the backtest's N and stores the 50th and 95th percentiles; a drawdown grows with the number of trades, so comparing 60 paper trades with a 300-trade drawdown would pass nearly always. Sources: ADR 0062; Wikipedia, Bootstrapping (statistics), read 2026-10-24 (percentile intervals are too narrow below about 50 points); Magdon-Ismail et al. 2004 via a secondary summary (https://www.mathworks.com/help/finance/emaxdrawdown.html, read 2026-10-24; growth rates UNVERIFIED against the paper).
**D7. What 60 trades can show.** With a spread of about 1.5R per trade (a 40 % hit rate at 2R and -1R gives 0.2R and 1.47R), the standard error at n = 60 is about 0.19R and a 90 % interval about +-0.31R. An expectancy of +0.2R (the middle of doc 19 §6's +0.1R to +0.4R) has the interval's lower end above zero only about 27 % of the time. So the exit **can reject** a system clearly worse than research (below about -0.3R from the band) and **cannot prove** a modest edge; a MET is a consistency check, labelled as such. Doc 19 §6 already says losing weeks and multi-week drawdowns are normal; an 80 % win rate or +1R per trade in paper is a warning of a leak, not a success.
**D8. Config governance = detect, class, restart, label (P10-05).** The owner is the only operator; prevention is impossible, consequence is possible. A fingerprint of the measured configuration (eligible cell set with strategy hashes and exits, the cell-statistics snapshot, `Planning:*`, autonomy, paper account settings, smart rules, the champion, the engine/schema/fill-rule/charge-sheet versions) is taken at every capture. A `frozen` difference starts a new epoch and the count restarts at zero; the session captured in that run is set aside (`straddle`); a `risk_down` difference (lower risk, a cell paused, auto-approval off, a protective rule on) does not restart, so safety actions are never discouraged, and is printed as a censoring caveat; `free` settings are not in it. **Resets:** a frozen change; the owner's explicit `xd paper epoch restart --reason`. **Not resets:** approving/rejecting/lapsing tickets, pausing services, deploys that leave the engine and schema versions unchanged, risk-down changes. Earlier epochs are kept and never pooled. Accepted hypotheses wait in a queue; applying one is the owner's act and starts a new epoch. The statistics snapshot is frozen too: the exit is a statement about one fixed system, so paper results do not feed learning during the epoch (offline hypotheses and experiments continue). Source: ADR 0076 (accept applies nothing), doc 19 §3.
**D9. The paper book feeds the plan (P10-02).** Today the open book is unavailable and no ticket is primary; for the plan to produce primary tickets the owner must set `Planning:OpenBook:AssumedEmpty` (ignores open positions) or, better in P10, `Planning:OpenBook:UsePaperLedger` (default OFF, exclusive with the first) so open risk, held symbols, funds, last-session loss and taken trades for the equity throttle come from the paper ledger. The ledger is derived, equity = capital + realised net, funds = equity minus open notional (no leverage), unavailable when capture is behind (fail safe). Recommended: ONE paper account during the measured epoch (R is size-free, a second account adds correlated rows, not evidence).
**D10. "Paper/minimal size" in the roadmap row.** Minimal-size real orders are P11 (manual live) and need the owner's decision (P7 binding: no order, paper only). P10 is paper only.
**D11. Pace is a risk to the count, not the weeks.** About 58 sessions in 12 weeks; with 2 armed primary tickets a session and half triggering, about 58 trades; with 1.5 about 43. The review shows pace and a straight-line ETA once n >= 10. There is no cap: the phase continues until 60 counted; widening the universe or rules is a frozen change (new epoch) and the owner's call.
**D12. UI.** Read-only `/paper` screen, lazy, tabs added by the slices (Ledger 02, Review and Discipline 03, Backtest vs paper 04, Governance 05, Phase check 06). No UI write; owner acts are CLI verbs (`xd paper ...`). Nav entry is the only eager addition (bundle guard, frontend/CLAUDE.md). No prototype screen exists; follow the Plans and Learning look.

## Owner pre-flight (owner acts; each with the default that applies until done)
1. Read `xd plan phase-check` (P7). Not necessarily MET; its caveats are printed on the paper MET.
2. Store the charge sheet and, once the P10-07/P8-06b slice exists, confirm it (ISS-25). Default: unconfirmed, labels as in P10-02, criteria C3/C4 `not_evaluable`.
3. Create one paper account: Trading role, mode paper, Fixed capital, a risk profile.
4. Refresh the cell statistics snapshot once (`xd learn cells`) and decide the cells' states; this is the last refresh of the epoch. **Pause every service that recomputes cell statistics, retrains models or applies learning** (today none runs automatically; `weekly` only writes the digest; any future `cells` or `retrain` service must be paused): each run would store a new snapshot, a `frozen` change that restarts the count. `xd paper config` lists them as blockers (P10-05 criterion 11a).
5. Set `Planning:Builder:UseSelection` true, `Planning:OpenBook:UsePaperLedger` true, `Planning:Grading:CostSheetId`/`Capital`; keep `autonomy.auto_approve_tickets` OFF. Default: all as today (empty skeleton plans).
6. Switch on services: `eod`, `portfolio-broker-history`, `nightly`, `suggestions`, `review-cutoff`, `preopen`, `plan-expiry`, `weekly`, `paper-capture`, `paper-review`. Default: paused.
7. P7-11 precondition: capture one real 09:10 quote answer and record `last_trade_time`, `last_price`, `ohlc.close`. Default: unreadable times invalidate every ticket (safe, useless).
8. `xd paper config` (see the fingerprint and the classes), then `xd paper epoch restart --reason "pre-flight complete"`. The epoch starts the next trading day; from here a frozen change costs the count.

## The owner's weekly routine (about 85 minutes a week; the phase needs 12 of them)
**Every trading day, about 10 minutes.** In the evening after the 18:30 plan (or before 09:05): open the plan review; check the banner (a fail-closed night is a reason, not a plan); for each primary ticket read the checklist and the evidence, then approve, lower the size, or reject; tickets nobody decides lapse at 09:05, which is a decision to skip, so decide on purpose. After the close the bell shows a failed `paper-capture` or `eod` run: fix or run `xd paper capture` (2 minutes). Do not change settings.
**Once a week (Sunday), about 45 minutes.** (1) Paper -> Review (15): where you are (n of 60, week k of 12, pace), pipeline health (plans published, failed-closed, pending captures), the funnel (proposed, approved, armed, entered, closed), the closed trades of the week. (2) Discipline (8): lapsed tickets (unreviewed) and rejected/size-down lines with their R and n; read them as questions, not verdicts (n is small). (3) Backtest vs paper (5): read the position word (`below`, `within`, `above`) and the interval; `earlyWarning` means stop and think. (4) Ledger (4): open positions and open risk. (5) Governance (5): changes detected this week, unexplained ones, the queue of accepted hypotheses. (6) Decide (8): leave the queue alone (default), or choose to apply one now knowing it restarts the count, or restart on purpose with a reason. Nothing is applied by the system.
**Once a month, about 30 minutes.** Dhan token and data-quality status; charge sheet against a bill; restore check of the database backup; read `xd paper phase-check` (week lines, blockers).
**Stop rules (written before the money is real, doc 19 §3).** An early warning; three weeks with coverage under 80 %; two or more unexplained frozen changes in the epoch (you are tweaking): stop adding trades, read the review, decide in writing.

## Acceptance criteria (plan level; each slice carries its own)
1. Given an approved and armed ticket whose session bars are stored, when `paper-capture` runs, then a paper entry (and, when closed, an exit) exists per paper allocation with instants, filled prices, owner quantity, R and rupees, equal in price and R to the ticket's grade, and no order, broker call or ticket state move happened. *P10-01.*
2. Given the plan is built with `UsePaperLedger` on, then the open book, funds, held symbols, loss carried and taken trades come from the ledger, and a stale ledger makes the book unavailable (nothing taken). *P10-02.*
3. Given a week of paper activity, then the review and discipline report reproduce a hand fixture exactly, with n and honest states and no composite score. *P10-03.*
4. Given the counted trades and the pre-epoch reference, then the band, the interval and the drawdown reference reproduce the stated fixtures and the verdict labels never over-claim. *P10-04.*
5. Given any frozen configuration change, then a new epoch starts, the straddle session is set aside and the earlier epoch is never pooled; risk-down changes are noted not reset. *P10-05.*
6. Given the stored records, then the phase check prints MET only for a clean 12-week epoch with 60 counted trades, confirmed costs, a within-band result and no integrity fault, and `NOT EVALUABLE` is a normal answer. *P10-06.*
7. The whole phase places no order and approves nothing on the owner's behalf; the no-order architecture rule, the insert-only grants and the Domain purity rules stay green.

## Risks and honest limits
- **Fills are simulated.** Paper cannot reveal slippage, partial fills, rejections, latency or the owner's behaviour with real money. Agreement of paper and research means the system behaves on new days as it did on old ones; nothing more.
- **History is short:** about 2.1 years, no hold-out, members as of today; the cells were chosen on that history (selection bias makes the band stricter, the safe direction).
- **A weak test by arithmetic** (D7). The label says so.
- **Frozen statistics go stale** over 12 weeks; the price of a clean measurement.
- **The count may bind** (D11) and the weeks may stretch.
- **The human is part of the system:** lapsed or hurried approvals change the sample; they are shown, not corrected.
- **Config read from the `xd-api` process;** a change outside the fingerprint is invisible.
- **Not measured on the owner's data:** no armed ticket exists yet; capture timing, the reference read and review reads are budgets.
- **Net is blocked on a missing slice:** the charge-sheet confirmation writer (P10-07 or P8-06b) is not in this plan's six slices; without it the exit cannot read MET.
- **Cut order if time runs short:** the Review and Governance tabs (keep verbs), the `paper-review` service, the explainer of P10-05, the Ledger chart. Never cut: P10-01, the ledger read for the plan, P10-04's honesty states, the straddle/epoch rule, P10-06.
