# P12-08 — Semi-auto: the one-tap confirm ticket (exact text, time-boxed, idempotent, double-submit safe)

- **Status:** Draft (written 2026-10-24; decisions below were researched under the owner's delegation; the owner's Approved is still needed before an agent builds it)
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P12 — Execution service, slice P12-08 of [P12-00](P12-00-execution-service-plan.md) (id only; written in parallel)
- **Design refs:** docs/design/20-extended-modules.md §A (`semi_auto`: one-tap confirm in the UI; idempotent client order ids) · 04 §4.1 ("semi-auto orders" are Proposed → you confirm) · 19 §4 (always-ask list) · 16 (`step_up_for: execution`) · 13 (UI) · ADR 0095 (decisions through one owner path), 0091
- **Needs (build order of the group: P12-07, P12-11, P12-08, P12-09, P12-10, P12-12):** P12-07 (mode, `ModeRouting`, `TriggerWatcher`, settings catalogue, grants pattern), P12-11 (readiness and `day_states`, which the intent creator and the confirm policy read), P12-01 (intents, state machine `checking>submitting`, `ClientOrderId`, commands), P12-03 (check runs and `check_confirmations`; it leaves "creating intents" and "the owner's confirm gesture" to P12-08/09), P7-19 (numeric entry, trigger, stop and target levels: P12-00 finding 2; without them there is no intent), P12-05 (halts and kill switch state), P12-11 (readiness state). Feeds P12-10 (screens), P12-12 (the exit measure counts confirmed sessions).
- **Size:** 4 agent-days (estimate: 2.5 for the ticket and the tap, 1.5 for the intent creator that P12-03 leaves to this slice and P12-09; if it overruns, split the creator as P12-08a, which P12-09 also needs).
- **Safety:** yes. It is the first slice where a person's tap causes a broker order. Default: no account is in `semi_auto`; no ticket can exist until the owner has promoted an account (P12-07) and unlocked the day; the spec being Draft holds the build.

## Assumptions
1. **The tap is the manual approval; the automatic form is `auto_live`** (P12-09), behind its own switch, OFF by default (CLAUDE.md setting rule). Nothing here weakens the tap: no setting turns a ticket into an automatic send.
2. **Only risk-adding actions need a tap.** Risk-reducing actions (tighten a stop, partial or time exit, square-off, cancel a pending entry) run automatically in `semi_auto` and are notified (P12-10). The kill switch never needs a tap, a password or an unlock (stopping is the safe direction).
3. **The ticket is a record the server writes, not a dialog the browser builds.** The text, the parameters and the hash are fixed when the ticket is issued; the browser can only confirm that exact text or skip it.
4. **Dhan facts** (read 2026-10-24): order placement, modification and cancellation need the whitelisted static IP; `correlationId` is optional, at most 30 characters of letters, digits, space, `_`, `-` (<https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/orders/>); the order-update socket `wss://api-order-update.dhan.co` returns `CorrelationId`, `Status` (`TRANSIT`, `PENDING`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`, `TRADED`, `EXPIRED`) and `AlgoId` (<https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/order-update/>). The ticket id and the client order id are therefore the idempotency anchors on our side and at the broker.

## Goal
In `semi_auto`, when a trigger fires on an Armed, owner-approved ticket and every pre-trade check passes, the owner sees one card with the exact order in words, has 90 seconds to tap once, and the tap sends that order exactly once; anything else (late, changed, moved, halted, locked) sends nothing and says why.

## Scope
**In:** Domain `Execution/Confirm` (`ActionClass` catalogue, `ConfirmText`, `ConfirmPolicy`, `ConfirmTicket`, `TicketOutcome`, `DayUnlock`); Application (issue, confirm, skip, expire handlers, day unlock, queries), ports; Infrastructure stores; migration `AddConfirmTickets`; endpoints; the frontend feature `execution` created with the lazy route `/execution`, its nav entry, `ConfirmTicketCard`, `useConfirmTickets`, `DayUnlockDialog` and the "Confirm" section only; settings keys; tests; ADR.
**Also in (named by P12-03 "creating intents (P12-08/09)" and not owned by P12-01 to P12-06): the intent creator**: `IntentCreator` in `XDrishti.Execution` (it reuses P12-07's `TriggerWatcher` for the crossing test) turns an Approved, then Armed plan item with an allocation for an account in `semi_auto` or `auto_live` into `execution.intents` (state `planned`, then `armed` once the plan item is Armed at 09:10), and moves it to `checking` when the live price reaches the ticket's trigger level inside the entry window while the account is ready (P12-11), not halted and not blocked. It writes no `trading.*` row (P12-00 D11); the plan ticket's `Triggered`/`Closed` states are moved by the separate ticket-state bridge of P12-00 finding 1. One intent per account and plan item (P12-01's unique key); a trigger that fires while not ready, halted or outside the window closes the intent `cancelled` with reason `missed_not_ready`, `halted` or `window` (no catch-up).
**Out:** the orders blotter, positions, mode panel, kill-switch button, notifications (P12-10, which extends the same page); sending to the broker (P12-01/02, they read the outcome); the caps (P12-09); a phone/PWA surface (P8-21 is not built, the access path is LAN/localhost); text or push messages outside the app.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given an intent in state `checking` of a `semi_auto` account whose latest P12-03 run is `cleared`, or `rejected` only by Soft checks (all 25 Hard Pass: the confirmable case), when the intent creator (below) hands it over, then exactly one open ticket is stored with params, text, `text_hash`, `client_order_id` (P12-01's 16-character id for the entry, attempt 1), `ref_price` (the last price at issue), `notional`, `risk_at_stop`, `issued_at`, `expires_at = issued_at + ttl` (default 90 s, 15 to 300); a second hand-over of the same intent returns the same ticket. *`IssueConfirmTicketTests`.*
2. Hand example: BUY 120 TATAPOWER, limit 412.35, stop 405.10, target 425.80, `ref_price` 412.30 → text "BUY 120 TATAPOWER at LIMIT Rs 412.35 (NSE, intraday), valid today. Stop-loss Rs 405.10 and target Rs 425.80 are placed at the broker with the order (one super order). Notional Rs 49,482.00. Worst case at the stop Rs 870.00 (120 x 7.25). Account: Dhan · Main. Plan ticket approved by you on 23 Oct at 19:41. This card expires at 09:47:30." The text is a pure function of the stored params (a property over 2,000 generated tickets: same params, same text and hash; any changed field changes the hash; every param is in the text). *`ConfirmTextTests`.*
3. Given an open ticket, when the owner confirms with the right `textHash` before `expires_at`, with the account still `semi_auto`, the day unlocked, no halt, kill switch or readiness failure, and the price within the drift limit, then one outcome row `confirmed` is inserted and the response is 200 `ConfirmResultView { outcome: confirmed, alreadyDecided: false }`. *`ConfirmPolicyTests`, `ConfirmApiTests`.*
4. Boundaries: confirmed at 09:47:29.9 passes, at 09:47:30.000 answers 409 `execution.ticket_expired` (the database clock decides, not the browser); the countdown shown uses the server's `serverNow` offset, never the browser clock alone. *`ConfirmPolicyTests`, `ExecutionTicketsDatabaseTests`.*
5. Drift: with `ref_price` 412.30 and limit 0.30 %, a last price of 413.53 passes (0.298 %) and 413.54 answers 409 `execution.price_moved` (0.301 %); a fall counts the same as a rise; an unavailable last price refuses (`execution.price_unavailable`). A refused ticket is closed `refused` and, once per intent while the plan item's window is open, a new ticket with new text may be issued (at most 2 per intent). *`ConfirmPolicyTests`.*
6. Idempotency: given 20 parallel confirms of one ticket (and the same with the `Idempotency-Key` header), then exactly one outcome row exists, one answer is `alreadyDecided: false`, nineteen are 200 `alreadyDecided: true` with the same `confirmedAt`; a confirm after a `skipped` or `expired` outcome answers 409 `execution.ticket_closed` with that outcome; a header that differs from the ticket id is 400. The race of confirm against the expiry job leaves one row (the table's unique key on `ticket_id` decides). *`ExecutionTicketsDatabaseTests`.*
7. The browser's text cannot differ: a confirm whose `textHash` is not the stored one answers 409 `execution.text_changed` and nothing is stored. *`ConfirmApiTests`.*
8. Day unlock: without a row in `execution.day_unlocks` for the account and the India session date and before its `expires_at` (15:30 IST), a confirm answers 409 `execution.not_unlocked`; `POST /api/execution/day-unlocks` needs the password (fresh, not the step-up window) and inserts the row; it works only on a trading day and for an account in `semi_auto` or `auto_live`; locking the session or its idle timeout does not remove the unlock but the confirm still needs the live session. *`DayUnlockTests`.*
9. Password with the tap: required when the ticket's notional is at least `exec.confirm_step_up_notional_pct` (default 10 %) of the account's capital (capital Rs 400,000 → threshold Rs 40,000 → this ticket, Rs 49,482, needs the password; with Rs 600,000 it does not), or the checklist carried a confirmed-despite or warned check, or the capital is unavailable; the ticket stores `requires_password` and the reason codes; a missing or wrong password is 400 `execution.password_required` / 401 `auth.invalid_credentials` and counts toward the lockout. *`ConfirmPolicyTests`.*
10. State refusals each have a code and a message that names the cause: mode no longer `semi_auto` (`execution.mode_changed`), day halt or kill switch (`execution.halted`), readiness failed (`execution.not_ready`), `execution_enabled` OFF (`execution.disabled`). Nothing is sent in any of them. *`ConfirmPolicyTests`.*
11. Skip: `POST .../skip` inserts `skipped` (a decision, counted in the discipline reports as "declined", not as an error); an unanswered ticket is closed `expired` by `system:confirm-expiry` within 5 s after `expires_at`. *`ExpireTicketsTests`.*
12. Hand-off safety: the move `checking>submitting` of a `semi_auto` entry is made by `xd-exec` only after `IConfirmGate` (port, closed default, implemented here) reads a `confirmed` outcome at most 30 s old; later the intent is closed `checking>cancelled` with reason `expired_after_confirm`, no order is sent, and the owner is told. A Soft-only `rejected` run is not final while a ticket is open (alignment point for P12-01/P12-03, see Risks). A restart between the tap and the send therefore sends nothing stale. *`ConfirmHandOffTests` (with a fake of P12-01's state machine and `LegSender`).*
13. Routing by class (catalogue in Contract): in `semi_auto` only `Entry` issues a ticket; `StopTighten`, `ExitReduce`, `CancelPending` need none and run automatically; `StopLoosen`, `AddToPosition` and `ReEntry` are `Refused` in every mode until P12-13 specifies them (409 `execution.not_supported`); `OutsidePlan` is refused in every mode (409 `execution.outside_plan`). Entries are BUY only until P12-13. While `exec.canary_pending` is ON (default, P12-00) P12-03 caps the quantity at 1 and the ticket text says "canary: 1 share". At most 3 open tickets per account; a fourth is closed `refused` (`execution.too_many_open`) without an owner prompt. *`ModeRoutingTests`, `IssueConfirmTicketTests`.*
14. Frontend. The card shows the text verbatim (never re-typed), the countdown, the account, the plan link, the worst case; the confirm button is disabled until 800 ms after the card (or its text) first rendered, while a request is pending, after a decision, and when expired; the card never takes focus, never opens as a modal over the page and never reacts to Enter unless the button itself has focus; labels state outcomes ("Place this order at Dhan", "Skip"); states: loading, empty ("No order is waiting"), open, confirming, confirmed (waiting for the broker), refused/expired/skipped with the server's reason, error with retry that cannot double-submit; status never by colour alone. *`ConfirmTicketCard.test.tsx` (MSW).*
15. Bundle: the eager additions (route definition and nav entry) are at most 0.6 KB gzip; the lazy `execution` chunk at most 25 KB gzip; `npm run size` is green and, if headroom is under 3 KB, the slice first frees space as `frontend/CLAUDE.md` demands. No new dependency. *`npm run size`.*
16. Intent creator. Given an Approved plan item with an allocation of 120 for a `semi_auto` account, when the plan item becomes Armed, then exactly one intent exists (`planned` then `armed`, `plan_id`, `plan_item_id`, `approval_id`, `mode_trail_id` filled); a second run changes nothing; an account in `paper`, `manual_live` or `assisted` gets none (`assisted` gets a sheet from P12-03's `advisory` run, P12-07, with no intent); when the live price first reaches the trigger inside 09:20 to 15:00 IST the intent moves to `checking` once, and when it is not ready, halted or the window is closed it is `cancelled` with the reason code; a trigger already passed when the intent is created (price beyond the entry by more than P12-03's collar) is `cancelled` `void`. *`IntentCreatorTests`, `IntentCreatorDatabaseTests`.*
17. Architecture unchanged; log ids 6710 to 6719 unique; `scripts/gen-api.sh` run; `scripts/check.sh` green.

## Contract
- **API:** `GET /api/execution/confirm-tickets?state=open|all&date=` → `[ConfirmTicketView { id, accountId, accountName, planItemId, text, textHash, kind, clientOrderId, refPrice, notional, riskAtStop, issuedAt, expiresAt, serverNow, requiresPassword, passwordReasons[], state: open|confirmed|skipped|expired|refused, outcomeReason?, decidedAt? }]`.
  `POST /api/execution/confirm-tickets/{id}/confirm` body `{ textHash, password? }`, optional header `Idempotency-Key` (= the ticket id) → `ConfirmResultView { ticketId, outcome, alreadyDecided, decidedAt }`; problems `execution.ticket_expired | text_changed | price_moved | price_unavailable | not_unlocked | mode_changed | halted | not_ready | disabled | ticket_closed | too_many_open | outside_plan` (409), `execution.password_required` (400), `auth.invalid_credentials` (401), `auth.locked` (423), `execution.not_found` (404).
  `POST /api/execution/confirm-tickets/{id}/skip` body `{ reason? }` (at most 200 characters, plain text).
  `POST /api/execution/day-unlocks` body `{ accountId, password }`; `GET /api/execution/day-unlocks?date=`.
  No route segment is `orders`, `execute` or `square-off` (tripwire).
- **Action classes** (the enum is introduced by P12-07 criterion 12, which also pins the full routing table; this is what each member means): `Entry` (risk-adding), `StopTighten` (reducing), `StopLoosen` (adding), `ExitReduce` (partial exit, time exit, max hold, square-off by plan: reducing), `CancelPending` (reducing), `AddToPosition` (adding), `ReEntry` (adding: a second entry in an instrument stopped out the same day), `OutsidePlan` (an action with no approved ticket behind it). Routing: `semi_auto`: `Entry` → ticket; `StopTighten`, `ExitReduce`, `CancelPending` → automatic; `StopLoosen`, `AddToPosition`, `ReEntry` → refused until P12-13; `OutsidePlan` → refused. Refusing the three risk-adding classes is how doc 19 §4's always-ask `risk_increase` is held until P12-13 gives them a ticket.
- **Data** (schema `execution`; migration `AddConfirmTickets`): `confirm_tickets` (insert-only): `id uuid pk`, `intent_public_id uuid` (P12-01's `public_id`), `account_id`, `plan_item_id bigint`, `session_date date`, `attempt smallint check in (1,2)`, `kind varchar(16)`, `params jsonb`, `text`, `text_hash char(64)`, `client_order_id varchar(30)`, `ref_price numeric(18,4)`, `notional numeric(18,2)`, `risk_at_stop numeric(18,2)`, `requires_password bool`, `password_reasons jsonb`, `issued_at`, `expires_at`; unique `(intent_public_id, attempt)`. `confirm_tickets` also carries `soft_codes text[]` (Soft checks not passed at issue) and `confirm_reason text null` (the owner's reason when a Soft non-pass is confirmed, 1 to 200 characters); a `confirmed` outcome with `soft_codes` non-empty writes P12-03's `check_confirmations` row through the same transaction (actor `owner:`, the codes, the reason), and a Hard code can never be listed. `ticket_outcomes` (insert-only): `ticket_id uuid pk fk` (the primary key is the one-decision guarantee), `outcome varchar(10) check in ('confirmed','skipped','expired','refused')`, `reason varchar(40)`, `actor text`, `decided_at` (stamped). Trigger `execution.check_ticket_outcome`: `confirmed` only with actor `owner:%`, `decided_at < expires_at`, a `day_unlocks` row for the account and date still valid, the account's effective mode in (`semi_auto`, `auto_live`), `settings.execution_enabled` ON; `expired` only by `system:%` with `decided_at >= expires_at`; `skipped` only by `owner:%`. `day_unlocks` (insert-only): `id`, `account_id`, `session_date`, `unlocked_at` (stamped), `expires_at` (15:30 IST of the date), `actor owner:%`; unique `(account_id, session_date)`. Settings keys (P12-01 registry, `exec.` prefix; risk direction in brackets): `exec.confirm_ttl_s` 90 (15..300) [higher = riskier], `exec.confirm_max_drift_pct` 0.30 (0.05..1.00) [higher], `exec.confirm_step_up_notional_pct` 10 (1..100) [higher]. **Grants** (D10 of P12-07): `xd_app` INSERT on `day_unlocks` and on `ticket_outcomes` (`confirmed`, `skipped`); `xd_exec` INSERT on `confirm_tickets` and on `ticket_outcomes` (`expired`, `refused`); the trigger checks the outcome against the actor kind; SELECT for both; no UPDATE, DELETE or TRUNCATE.
- **UI:** route `/execution` (lazy), nav entry under "Decisions" (icon already in the entry chunk if possible), the "Confirm" section; P12-10 adds the other sections to the same page.
- **Events/jobs:** `NOTIFY execution_ticket` on issue and on outcome (the channel name follows P12-01's `execution_command`) (the UI polls `confirm-tickets` every 2 s while a ticket is open and the tab is visible, otherwise every 30 s); the expiry loop runs in `xd-exec` every 2 s; a poll fallback finds `confirmed` tickets without a send (restart safe).

## Non-functional
Confirm path: one transaction with three small reads; p95 under 100 ms on the local database (the broker call is not in it). Logs 6710 to 6719: ticket and intent as short hashes, account hash, outcome, reason code, milliseconds from issue to decision; never the password, e-mail or text. Security: session + CSRF; the actor is the session's e-mail, never a body field; the sign-in rate limit applies to routes that check a password, and a separate limit (60 per minute) to the others; the ticket text is rendered as text (no HTML), 2,000 characters at most.

Integration (Testcontainers): the trigger refusals (confirm after expiry by clock, without unlock, wrong mode, by a system actor), 20 parallel taps, confirm racing expiry, grants as `xd_app`. Edge: ticket text with an apostrophe or `<`; a ticket for an account the owner then demoted; two tickets for the same instrument (second refused as `ReEntry` path or `AddToPosition`).

## Decisions (researched)
- **D1. One tap, not a password per tap, but a password per day.** Question: step-up for every order? Answer: a fresh password once per account and day (`day_unlocks`), plus a password with the tap for large or irregular tickets (rule above). Reason: a password on every order trains the owner to type it without reading (NN/g: reserve the strongest friction for the rarest, most dangerous actions) while the daily unlock proves presence at the start of the day, matching the daily 2FA that SEBI's framework asks of API users (<https://www.nngroup.com/articles/confirmation-dialog/>, read 2026-10-24; <https://support.fyers.in/portal/en/kb/articles/what-are-the-new-sebi-rules-for-retail-algo-trading-from-april-01-2026>, read 2026-10-24, a broker help page, not the circular). Rejected: the 5-minute step-up window (too short and used by other actions); a password per tap.
- **D2. Exact text, bound by hash.** The confirm must echo the hash of the text the person saw. Reason: "Are you sure?" gives nothing to check; naming the item and the effect makes a mistake visible (NN/g, same page). The server, not the browser, renders the text. Rejected: confirming a ticket id only (a stale tab could confirm changed content).
- **D3. 90 seconds, drift 0.30 %.** An entry is a conditional limit order at the trigger price (+0.1 %, ADR 0038); beyond a minute and a half the trigger context is old, and 0.30 % is three times the ADR 0038 limit allowance so ordinary jitter does not refuse. Both are settings that raise risk when raised (cooling-off, P12-07). Rejected: no expiry (a tap on a stale card); 15 s (the owner cannot read the text).
- **D4. Idempotency by a primary key, as in payment APIs.** The one-decision guarantee is a primary key on `ticket_id`; a retry returns the saved result instead of repeating, as Stripe's idempotency design does (saved result, same key, mismatch is an error; <https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests>, read 2026-10-24). Duplicate suppression at the broker side relies on the client order id (`correlationId`) and P12-06's reconciliation by it. Rejected: client-side debounce alone.
- **D5. Undo is not offered, but nothing is final before the broker accepts.** Reason: NN/g prefers undo to confirmation; an order cannot be undone (it can be cancelled, which is `CancelPending`, automatic and always allowed). So the confirm is the single gate, and the card keeps the cancel visible while the order is pending.
- **D6. Confirmed means sent within 30 s or not at all** (criterion 12). Rejected: sending a confirmed ticket whenever the service comes back.
- **D7. Focus and layout safety.** No focus steal, no modal, an 800 ms lock after any text change: a layout shift or a stray Enter must not confirm an order. This is an owner-safety rule, not decoration.
- **D8. No phone surface now.** The access path is localhost/LAN and the PWA (P8-21) is not built; a push to a phone would leave the machine (a SAFETY item). The card lives in the app only; the owner is at the machine in `semi_auto` by definition.

## Risks and honest limits
- A tap is one human decision under time pressure; the card shows worst-case loss at the stop, not the chance of winning (no number without n; the plan's P(win) appears only as the stored evidence line with its n, as in Plan review).
- The drift guard compares to the last price the feed holds; with a stale feed the guard says `execution.price_unavailable` and refuses. A refusal costs a missed trade, never an unintended one.
- `risk_at_stop` assumes the stop fills at its price; gaps and slippage can lose more (stated on the card).
- The browser countdown can lag; the server decides. The poll interval of 2 s means a ticket can appear up to 2 s late.
- Alignment points: (a) a Soft confirmation is bound to the exact set of Soft codes that were not Pass at issue and is void if the set changes before the send; the post-tap P12-03 run must pass every Hard check (H1's decision of 2026-10-25; this slice checks the set at confirm and answers 409 `execution.soft_set_changed`, closing the ticket `refused` and allowing the one re-issue); (b) P7-19's numeric trigger level (name UNVERIFIED) must reach the intent as `entry_price` and a trigger; (c) `IConfirmGate` is a new port in `XDrishti.Execution`.
- A confirmed order can still be rejected by the broker (price band, margin, DH-905 invalid IP); the card says "sent" only after the order stream shows the broker's `TRANSIT`/`PENDING`, and "rejected" with the reason otherwise (P12-06).
