- **Phase / roadmap row:** P8 — Reports & UI, slice P8-12 of [P8-00](P8-00-reports-and-ui-plan.md)
- **Design refs:** docs/design/09-learning-engine.md §11 (Journey, "the frozen-model line is what makes progress honest"), §10 (lessons), §15 · docs/design/13-reports-and-ui.md §2 R30 · ADR 0077 (counterfactual), 0078 (lessons), 0073 (changelog), 0070 (registry), 0080 (phase check), 0090 (grading, "simulated on plan prices"), 0094 (digest, insert-only pattern) · P8-00 D1, D3, D6
- **Needs:** P8-01, P8-02 (built: Learning frame) and **P8-02b** (extracts `HeatGrid` into `shared/analytics` and the tab registry; the built `LearningPage.tsx` has a hard-coded `tabs` array and no registry), P8-03 (lesson and model data already read by the Learning tabs). Feeds P8-13 (it reuses the table and port created here).
- **Size:** 4 agent-days (migration and store 1, endpoints and verb 1, screen 1.5, tests 0.5)
- **Safety:** no. The screen only reads. The one new write path is a CLI act (`xd learn counterfactual --store`, run by the owner like `xd plan digest --store`) into an insert-only evidence table; it changes no setting, applies no model and starts no job from the UI. Enabling the weekly service stays the owner's act and is not touched.

## Goal
Answer "is the system getting better?" from stored evidence: whether a model that kept learning beat a frozen copy on the same later trades, window by window, with "no measurable difference" and "too little history" as ordinary answers, beside the lessons (problem to fix) and the model versions of the same period, the plan-price grades by week, and a live line that says it is `not_evaluable`.

## Scope
**In:** table `research.learning_reports` (generic, insert-only, kind-keyed; this slice uses kind `counterfactual`; P8-13 adds kinds without a migration); port `ILearningReportStore`; `StoreCounterfactualHandler`; `--store` on the existing verb; read endpoints; `JourneyView` aggregator; the Journey tab; if P8-04 has not already exposed it, `GET /api/learning/phase-check` (the P6 phase check of ADR 0080, computed on read from stored records).
**Out:** computing the counterfactual from the UI or on a GET (it fits a model per window: a job; stays a verb), adding it to the paused `weekly` service (see Decisions 3), a weekly live line (no live trades, P10/P11), regime or market-gate bands, a composite progress score (D10), apply/revert of anything (P8-19).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given the verb runs with `--store`, when the report is complete, then one row is inserted with kind `counterfactual`, the 64-hex `input_hash`, outcome, the printed text (which contains the limits), the report as JSON and the sources; the verb prints `stored as #N`. Given the same inputs again, then no row is added and it prints `already stored as #N (same inputs)`. Given any knob changed (months, trials, hold-out, runs), then a new row is added (every re-run is another trial and the row says so). *`StoreCounterfactualHandlerTests`, `LearningReportStoreTests`.*
2. Given the runtime role `xd_app`, then it can SELECT and INSERT on `research.learning_reports` and cannot UPDATE, DELETE or TRUNCATE it; the database refuses a row whose text lacks "limit:", a bad kind, a bad hash or an empty builder. *`LearningReportsGrantsTests`, `LearningReportsConstraintTests`.*
3. Given a stored report, when `GET /api/learning/counterfactual` is called without `id`, then the newest is returned with outcome, period (cut day, to day, months of stopped learning), knobs, windows (index, dates, rows, days, instruments, train rows each side, `informative`, per-side log loss, Brier, AUC, take-R, kept, skipped mean R, the paired gain and its standard error clustered by day, cumulative take-R uplift), the judged and pooled blocks, reasons, limits and `basis`; with none stored it is 404 `learning.counterfactual_not_found`; an unknown `id` is the same; `limit` outside 1 to 50 on the list is 422 `learning.invalid`. *`CounterfactualApiTests`.*
4. Given windows with take-R frozen 0.10 and 0.12, retrained 0.16 and 0.09 on 40 and 50 rows, then the uplift per window is +2.4 R and -1.5 R and the cumulative uplift is +0.9 R (hand table); a window with `informative = false` is shown "same model on both sides, not judged" and contributes a zero-width bar with that sentence, never a bar of 0. *`JourneyViewBuilderTests`.*
5. Given fewer than 3 judged windows, then the headline says "Inconclusive: N judged windows, 3 needed" and no gain figure is coloured or called an improvement; given outcome `NoDifference`, then the headline reads "No measurable difference on this data" as an ordinary result (neutral icon and word); given `RetrainingHelps` or `FrozenBetter` on fewer than 6 judged windows, then the headline carries "weak (n windows)". *`JourneyTab.test.tsx`.*
6. Given the lessons, changelog and models of P8-03, then a lesson draws as a band from its `createdAt` to its `updatedAt` when closed or dismissed, to today when open or linked (labelled "open"), with its result word (`improved` / `no measurable change`) and a link to the lesson; a model registration or champion designation is a pin at its time with the id and the first 8 hex of its hash; with no lessons or models the band area says "No lessons recorded" / "No model stored". *`JourneyTab.test.tsx`, `JourneyBands.test.ts`.*
7. Given graded plans, when `GET /api/learning/journey` is called, then `planPrice.weeks[]` holds per ISO week the graded ticket count, the mean R and the total R, basis "simulated on plan prices"; a week with fewer than 5 graded tickets is `insufficient` (n shown, no mean); no graded plan gives `empty`. `live` is `{ state: "not_evaluable", reason: "no graded real trade exists (paper fills arrive in P10, live in P11)", n: 0 }` whatever else is stored. *`JourneyViewBuilderTests`, `JourneyApiTests`.*
8. Given the screen, then every number comes from the response (the browser computes no statistic: sums, uplift and means are server fields), every figure is drawn through the analytics kit (`MetricValue`/`DataTable`, so a null is a word), the `EvidenceBlock` shows basis, population (run keys behind the report), range, n (trades scored) and the report's limits as text, and the chart has a table with the same numbers. *`JourneyTab.test.tsx`.*
9. Given no counterfactual stored, then the tab shows `empty` with the command to run (`scripts/xd.sh learn counterfactual --run RUNKEY:STYLE:EXIT … --store`, newest run keys filled from `GET /api/reports/runs` when P8-06 is built) and still draws lessons, pins and plan-price weeks. *`JourneyTab.test.tsx`.*
10. Given the runs of the sources mix costed and gross, then the store is refused with `learning.invalid` ("gross and costed runs are never mixed"); given all costed on a sheet that is not the owner's confirmed one, `basis` is `costed_unconfirmed` and the screen prints "costed on the stored sheet, not confirmed as yours" beside the numbers. *`StoreCounterfactualHandlerTests`.*
11. Given the P6 phase check endpoint is built here, then it returns the same `PhaseCheckView` as the verb (criteria MET / NOT MET / NOT EVALUABLE, limits always), writes nothing, and is skipped entirely if P8-04 already exposes it (check `Api/Endpoints` first). *`PhaseCheckApiTests`.*
12. Given the feature folder, then it imports no mutation and calls only GET; server text renders as text. *D6 test, markup test.*

## Contract
- **Data:** migration `AddLearningReports` (name; timestamp from `dotnet ef`), table `research.learning_reports`:
  `id bigint identity always (pk_learning_reports)`, `kind varchar(40) not null`, `input_hash varchar(64) not null`, `outcome varchar(40) not null`, `as_of date not null` (the last day of the data used), `basis varchar(24) not null`, `sources_json text not null`, `options_json text not null`, `report_json text not null` (the view DTO of this slice, `schemaVersion: 1`, never the domain record, so NaN cannot enter), `text text not null`, `built_by varchar(100) not null`, `created_at timestamptz not null`.
  Constraints: `ck_learning_reports_shape`: `kind ~ '^[a-z][a-z_]{1,38}$' AND input_hash ~ '^[0-9a-f]{64}$' AND length(outcome) > 0 AND basis IN ('gross','costed_unconfirmed','net_confirmed') AND position('limit:' IN text) > 0 AND length(built_by) > 0`. Indexes: `uq_learning_reports_kind_hash (kind, input_hash)` unique; `ix_learning_reports_kind_created (kind, created_at DESC, id DESC)`.
  Grants (pattern of `AddWeeklyDigests`, read and copied 2026-10-24): `migrationBuilder.GrantTableToAppRole(Schemas.Research, "learning_reports"); migrationBuilder.MakeAppendOnlyForAppRole(Schemas.Research, "learning_reports");` — per table, never by re-granting the schema. **Rule for later migrations:** any later `research` migration that calls `GrantSchemaUsageToAppRole` re-grants UPDATE and DELETE on every table, so it must call `MakeAppendOnlyForAppRole` for this table again (as `backend/CLAUDE.md` says for `sim_runs`). `Down` drops the table. Never edit the migration once applied. Config class `LearningReportRecordConfiguration`, record `LearningReportRecord` in `Infrastructure/Learning`.
- **Port (Application/Abstractions/Learning):** `ILearningReportStore { InsertAsync(NewLearningReport, ct) -> (StoredLearningReport, bool Created); FindAsync(long id, ct); NewestAsync(string kind, ct); ListAsync(string kind, int limit, ct) }`, implemented in `Infrastructure/Learning/LearningReportStore` (insert-or-skip on the unique index, as the digest store).
- **Application:** `StoreCounterfactualCommand(EvaluateCounterfactualQuery Query, string Actor)` and handler (calls the existing `EvaluateCounterfactualHandler` through its `IQueryHandler`, builds `CounterfactualReportView` from `CounterfactualReport`, derives `basis` from the run headers' `CostsSheetId`, computes `input_hash`, inserts); queries `GetCounterfactualQuery(long? Id)`, `ListCounterfactualsQuery(int Limit)`, `GetJourneyQuery`. Pure `Domain/Learning/CounterfactualUplift` (cumulative take-R uplift per window, hand-tested) and `JourneyWeeks` (ISO-week grouping of graded items).
- **Input hash:** SHA-256 hex of the invariant-culture text `counterfactual|v1|` + sorted `runKey:style:exit` + all `CounterfactualOptions` fields + `HoldOutMonths` + the training set id + trainer name and parameters + the `ValidationOptions` (min train months, embargo, min train trades).
- **API (read only, GET, session-protected):** `GET /api/learning/counterfactual?id=` (`GetLearningCounterfactual`), `GET /api/learning/counterfactuals?limit=` (default 12, 1 to 50), `GET /api/learning/journey` (`JourneyView { counterfactual: header|null, planPrice { state, weeks[] { isoWeek, graded, meanR|null, totalR, state } , totalGraded, basis }, live { state, reason, n }, evidence }`), optionally `GET /api/learning/phase-check`. Problem codes `learning.counterfactual_not_found` (404), `learning.invalid` (422). Metric fields that can be absent are nullable with a sibling `state` and `reason`; `scripts/gen-api.sh` in the same change.
- **Verb:** `xd learn counterfactual … [--store]`; exit codes as the other `xd` commands; the printed text is unchanged, with one added line.
- **UI:** the **Journey** tab of `/learning?tab=journey` (a tab added through P8-02b's registry; lazy component `features/learning/journey/JourneyTab`), prototype `docs/prototype/assets/app-pages-4.js` Learning "journey" (UNVERIFIED line) minus its demo series and its health score. Sections: headline, period and knobs, windows (grouped bars frozen vs retrained with the gain and its error bar; the bar-with-error chart type registered by P8-10 in the lazy ECharts chunk, else registered here once; a table beside it), cumulative uplift, lesson bands and model pins on one time axis (list fallback), plan-price weeks, the `not_evaluable` live line, evidence block.
- **Events/jobs:** none. Log events (counts and codes only, ids from the next free block at build time): `learning.counterfactual.stored {Outcome, Windows, Created}`.

## Non-functional
Reads: the journey view is four indexed reads (newest counterfactual, lessons and changelog already cheap, graded items up to the store's limit) and aggregates in memory: p95 under 300 ms on the seeded fixture. The stored `report_json` is bounded (windows are at most a handful). The phase check endpoint, if built, is single-flight with a 60 s in-memory result keyed by the newest experiment and model ids (`TimeProvider`), because it re-judges the newest experiment each time; its p95 is measured and written in the ADR. No secret or account number is stored or logged.

- **Frontend (Vitest + MSW, clock pinned):** empty, inconclusive (2 judged windows), no difference, weak help, clear help, frozen better; band and pin placement on a 12-month axis; the non-informative first window; the live line; markup in lesson text; keyboard order; no-write test.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **A generic insert-only `learning_reports` table, not a counterfactual-only table.** P8-13 needs the same shape for entry windows and the meta-labelling policy (heavy fits that must not run on a GET). One table with a `kind` whitelist-by-pattern lets P8-13 add kinds with no migration. Alternative rejected: one table per report (three migrations, three grant tests, no gain). The per-kind view DTOs keep the typed contract.
2. **Store the view DTO, not the domain record.** The domain report holds `double.NaN`-capable metrics and enums by name; JSON cannot carry NaN. A versioned view with nullable numbers is stable across later domain changes (the stored text stays readable) and is what the API returns.
3. **Computed by the verb, not by the weekly service or a GET.** ADR 0094 says the weekly service "does not run cell statistics, drift replay or hypothesis triggers" and that the progress line against the frozen model "is not built"; so P8-00's wording "the verb and the paused weekly service" is corrected here: only the verb writes. A model refit per window is a job; a GET that starts one is the hidden job P8-00 D6 forbids. Wiring the verb into the weekly service is a later owner decision (it makes a service start compute), not part of this slice.
4. **Windows are quarterly, not weekly.** Doc 09 §11 and doc 13 R30 say "weekly expectancy". The evaluator retrains every `RetrainMonths` (1 to 12, default 3) and scores whole windows; the period is at most about a year. The screen shows the windows as stored, one bar pair each, and does not interpolate a weekly line. **Design change: doc 09 §11 and doc 13 R30 wording** ("per retrain window", not weekly); ADR at build (next free ADR number). The plan-price line is weekly because grades are per planned session.
5. **The headline never says "improved" on a thin verdict.** The judgement rules are the evaluator's (3 judged windows minimum, labelled weak under 6, capped by disagreement with the take-R measure); the screen repeats the stored reasons and adds nothing.
6. **Cumulative uplift is take-R, a fixed rule.** `Σ rows × (retrained take-R per candidate − frozen take-R per candidate)` over scored windows; the take rule (score at or above the side's own train base rate) is the evaluator's, not a tuned threshold. Label: "R of taking trades the model scores at or above its base rate; a convention, not a strategy". Rejected: a "live expectancy" line (no live trades) and a log-loss cumulative sum (not in R, so not comparable to anything the owner trades).
7. **Gross/net.** `basis` follows P8-05/P8-06: net only when the charge sheet is the owner's confirmed one (ISS-25); until then `costed_unconfirmed` or `gross`.
8. **Phase-check endpoint scope.** ADR 0080 says "no endpoint"; P8-00 lists the P6 check as needing P8-12. It is a pure read of stored records plus one timed re-judge; exposing it adds no write. The replay seconds the verb accepts are never taken from a request.
9. **Source:** repo only (ADR 0077, 0094, specs P6-12, P7-15); no external facts needed.

## Risks and honest limits
- The period is at most about one year of "stopped learning", less after the 6-month hold-out and the 9 minimum train months; the answer is usually `Inconclusive` on 2.1 years and that is the correct display. "No difference" is not proof of no benefit.
- Net R is on modelled fills; replayed history, data seen before, no hold-out of the whole system (ISS-19). Every re-run with other months is another trial and is stored as another row; the screen lists the number of stored rows for the same runs so a cherry-picked window is visible.
- The model recipe is the running trainer's (`Model recipe` line in the limits); a trainer change changes the hash.
- Lesson bands show when a finding opened and closed, not that the fix caused a change.
- Not seen in a browser until P8-16.
