## Goal
Move the owner's history from about 2.1 years toward the honest maximum: about 5 years of 1-minute bars for instruments Dhan still serves, and 10 or more years of daily bars (with delivery percentage and an as-of-date universe) from NSE's published end-of-day files, with corporate actions applied correctly at every seam and every limit (survivorship, depth per instrument, adjusted or raw) printed on every run record.

## Verified facts (read 2026-10-27)
- **Only one Dhan chart endpoint is allowed:** `POST /v2/charts/intraday` (`BrokerEndpoints.DhanIntradayChart`, `Infrastructure/Brokers/BrokerEndpoints.cs`). `POST /v2/charts/historical` (daily) is **not** in the allow-list, contrary to the research note's reading of the P0-13 text.
- `DhanIntradayChartClient` sends one instrument and one window of **at most 90 days** per request; 401/403/DH-901/DH-902 are `Unauthorized`; 429/5xx/DH-904 are retried once; the throttle is `RequestThrottle` with `Data:Dhan:RequestsPerSecond` in the range **1 to 5** (message in `DataOptions`); its default value was not read (the note says 4: UNVERIFIED).
- `market.bars_1m` has a `source` column (`csv` or `dhan`); `BarStore` lets a Dhan bar **replace** a CSV bar of the same timestamp ("taken"). A backfill that overlaps the CSV range would therefore overwrite the owner's adjusted bars with Dhan's.
- The owner's data: **214** CSV files, 40,946,344 rows, starting 2024-04-01 to 2024-12-18, ending 2026-04-30 15:29 IST (ISS-19). The research note sized 500 instruments; the real universe in files is 214.
- Corporate actions are `split` or `bonus` only; `csvAdjustedThrough` per instrument guards double adjustment; `RunSimulationHandler` skips an instrument as `csv_adjusted_twice` unless acknowledged; the merged series from `ReadAsync` carries **no source per bar** (doc 05 §4.2). The CSVs still hold unadjusted discontinuities (74 overnight opens beyond ±10 %, 3 beyond ±20 %, ISS-20).
- Non-broker NSE hosts are guarded by `MasterDataEndpointGuard` over `MasterDataEndpoints` (`Infrastructure/Market`); its current contents were not read: UNVERIFIED.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **Cheapest honest first: Part A (daily) before Part B (1-minute).** Reason: daily bhavcopy needs no broker call and no Dhan limit, adds 10+ years of regime and 252-day context, and brings the corporate-action file that Part B's adjustment needs. Rejected: Part B first (it adds the evidence the owner wants most, but it depends on correct actions for 2021-2024). Source: research §1.4.
2. **Daily data go to a new table `market.daily_eod`, not into `bars_1m` or its aggregates.** Columns by logical field: instrument (matched by ISIN first, then symbol, as BK-01), `trade_date`, `series`, open, high, low, close, previous close, traded quantity, turnover, number of trades, delivery quantity, delivery percent, `source`, `file_sha256`. Reason: official end-of-day figures are a different thing from the minute aggregate (the official close is not the last minute's close: UNVERIFIED for NSE cash), and delivery is not in minute data. Rejected: synthesising minute bars from daily; overwriting `bars_agg` 1D. Source: repo structure; research §1.2 (2).
3. **No column names are assumed. A probe captures real files first.** NSE changed the report layout (a forum post puts the new "UDiFF" format at 2024-07-06: UNVERIFIED) and the exact names and the start date of the archive were not confirmed (the pages timed out in the research). Step 0 of Part A is an owner-run probe: with `Nse:Enabled` on, fetch three dates (2021-06-01, 2024-07-05, 2024-07-08) once, store the raw files under `data/` (git-ignored), print the header lines. Parsers and fixtures are written from those files; a parser maps header names to logical fields by an explicit table and refuses a file whose header it does not know (fail closed, like BK-01). Rejected: coding against names remembered from forums.
4. **The NSE terms are read by the owner before the first fetch, recorded in an ADR, and the host is added to the allow-list by the owner.** Reason: redistribution and index-derivation are restricted by NSE Data policy; personal internal analysis is the low-risk reading; the bhavcopy-specific terms were not opened (UNVERIFIED). Local copy only, nothing exposed: the Reports export (P8-20) must not include raw third-party bars. Rejected: shipping with the fetch ON. Source: research §1.1, §1.2 (5); BK-01 decision 8 (same stance).
5. **The universe as of a date is derived from "securities that traded on that day"** in `market.daily_eod` (series EQ and the other cash series the owner chooses), kept in a view `market.eod_universe(asOf)`. It lists dead names too (`inCatalog = false`, shown by ISIN and symbol) and never auto-adds them to tracked instruments. Reason: this is the only free survivorship repair, and only for daily data. Rejected: claiming intraday for delisted names (not obtainable) or buying vendor data (not priced here). Source: research §1.2 (3).
6. **Part B writes only before the first CSV bar of each instrument.** Window per instrument = `[max(today - 5 years, first available), firstCsvBar)` (half-open, India dates). A guard in the planner and again in the writer drops any timestamp at or after the first CSV bar and counts it `beyond_seam`. Reason: `BarStore` would otherwise replace the owner's adjusted CSV bars. Rejected: overlapping with the CSV for convenience.
7. **Chunks of at most 90 calendar days, newest first, one ledger row per (instrument, chunk), idempotent.** Table `market.backfill_chunks (instrument_id, from_date, to_date, status planned|done|failed|empty, rows, attempts, error, updated_at)`, unique on `(instrument_id, from_date)`. A re-run skips `done`, retries `failed` up to 3 times, and writes by the existing `source = 'dhan'` upsert (an identical bar is not rewritten). Reason: resumable over a night; the gap backfill (doc 05 §6.1) is the pattern. Rejected: one request per instrument (the 90-day limit forbids) and a oldest-first order (the newest chunks sit next to the CSV and matter most if the run is stopped early).
8. **Source-aware adjustment, not the single-predicate guard.** The rule: a bar with `source = 'csv'` receives the factors of applied actions whose ex-date is **after `csvAdjustedThrough`** only (the file already contains the earlier ones); a bar with `source = 'dhan'` that lies **before the first CSV bar** is raw and receives the factors of **every applied action whose ex-date is after the bar's date** (including those inside the CSV span, because the CSV basis contains them); a Dhan bar after the CSV end (the existing daily gap backfill) follows the standard rule. This needs the read model to carry the source (or the seam date per instrument), UNVERIFIED how `ReadAsync` can expose it. The `csv_adjusted_twice` skip of the simulation run stays for CSV bars and is relaxed for pre-seam Dhan bars only when the overlap probe (decision 9) has passed for that instrument. Rejected: adjusting everything with one predicate (it either double-adjusts CSV bars or leaves Dhan bars raw).
9. **An overlap probe proves the basis before bars are trusted.** For each of up to `Data:History:Probe:Instruments` = 20 instruments (all with an applied action inside the CSV span first), fetch Dhan 1-minute bars for the first `Probe:Sessions` = 10 sessions **inside** the CSV span into memory only (never written), apply decision 8's rule to them as if they were pre-seam, and compare with the CSV bars with the existing `BarComparer` semantics: pass when the median absolute close difference is at most `Probe:MedianBps` = 5 basis points and at least 99 % of bars are within `Probe:MaxBps` = 50. This answers the UNVERIFIED question "are Dhan's candles split-adjusted?" (P0-13 open item) with data: ratio near 1 means Dhan is already adjusted to the CSV basis; ratio near the cumulative factor means raw; anything else is `seam_unresolved`, the instrument is not backfilled, and the owner is told why. Rejected: trusting documentation.
10. **Seam continuity check.** After a chunk set for an instrument completes, the adjusted close of the last pre-seam session is compared with the first CSV open: a move beyond ±20 % (the ISS-20 threshold) with no applied action is `seam_jump`; the instrument's pre-seam bars stay in the table but are excluded from simulation (`seam_unverified`) until the owner confirms or adds the action. Reason: demergers, rights issues and capital reductions are not corporate-action kinds today (split and bonus only), and the CSVs already show such jumps. Rejected: auto-adjusting by the observed jump (it hides real moves; the same rule as P1-10a detection which only suggests).
11. **Calendar before data.** Holidays, Muhurat sessions and special Saturdays for 2021-10 to 2024-03 must be in `market.trading_days` before the first backfill, from the NSE calendar fetch of P1-11a (best effort, UNVERIFIED depth) or by the owner's confirmation of the observed proposals. Zero-bar weekdays that are neither holidays nor specials are quality findings (P1-14a), not errors. Reason: otherwise the quality gate flags legitimate gaps by the hundred. Rejected: suppressing gap findings for the backfilled range.
12. **Disk check.** Before enabling, the job estimates rows and refuses to start when free space is under twice the estimate (`Data:History:Backfill:DiskHeadroomFactor` = 2, `BytesPerRowEstimate` = 60, an upper-bound assumption). Reason: the note's estimate of "+4 to 9 GB for 135 M rows" is itself UNVERIFIED. Rejected: starting blind.
13. **Hold-out is not decided here.** Roadmap Q7 (keep the last months as a hold-out no tuning sees) stays an owner question; this slice only adds depth per instrument to the run record. The hold-out itself is the Lab's ([CF-06](CF-06-preregistration-trial-ledger-and-locked-holdout-in-the-lab.md)). Rejected: deciding it in a data slice.
14. **The daily Dhan endpoint is out.** Index and VIX daily (roadmap Q7 part 1) is a separate owner decision about the allow-list. If the owner wants it, a separate slice adds the entry as the owner's security act. Rejected: adding `/v2/charts/historical` in this slice.

## Acceptance criteria
Numbers recomputed on 2026-10-27.
1. **Zero calls when OFF.** With `Nse:Enabled` OFF (default) or `Backfill:Enabled` OFF (default) the matching job and the API make no outbound request (counting handler = 0) and the run records `skipped: disabled`. *Integration with a counting handler.*
2. **Allow-list is read, never edited.** A request to an NSE host not in `MasterDataEndpoints` is refused before sending (`...NotAllowed`); a request to `/v2/charts/historical` is refused by `BrokerEndpointGuard`; no code path adds an entry. *Guard tests; architecture test that no type writes the allow-list.*
3. **Chunk plan.** Today 2026-10-27, five years back = 2021-10-27. An instrument whose first CSV bar is 2024-04-01: 887 days, **10** chunks, newest `[2024-01-02, 2024-04-01)` (90 days), then 90-day steps back; the oldest chunk is the short one, `[2021-10-27, 2022-01-12)`, **77** days (`887 - 9 × 90`). First CSV bar 2024-12-18: 1,148 days, **13** chunks, oldest 68 days. The two together: 23 requests. A first CSV bar earlier than 2021-10-27 + 1 day gives **zero** chunks. *`BackfillPlannerTests` (half-open windows; the client's own inclusive/exclusive date handling is covered by its tests: UNVERIFIED).*
4. **Sizing.** 214 instruments with a 2.9-year average gap (725 sessions × 375 minutes = 271,875 rows each): **58,181,250** rows (range 50.2 M at 2.5 years to 64.2 M at 3.2 years); requests `214 × 12 = 2,568`, which is 10.7 minutes at 4 requests a second and 42.8 minutes at 1 a second. For the note's 500 instruments: 135.9 M rows and 6,000 requests (25 minutes at 4 per second), not the 7,500 / 31 minutes of the note. *Unit on the estimator.*
5. **Disk guard.** 58,181,250 rows × 60 bytes = 3.49 GB; free space under 6.98 GB refuses to start with `history.disk_low` and the numbers. *Unit.*
6. **Resume and idempotency.** Interrupt after 4 of 10 chunks: the next run starts at chunk 5; a second complete run writes 0 rows and changes 0 ledger rows; a `failed` chunk is tried at most 3 times then stays `failed` with its error and the instrument is reported `partial`. *Runner tests with a fake source.*
7. **Never overwrites the CSV.** A fake Dhan response containing a bar at or after the instrument's first CSV bar: that bar is dropped and counted `beyond_seam`; the stored CSV rows are byte-identical afterwards. *Integration on a real TimescaleDB.*
8. **Adjustment hand case.** Instrument with a split 1:5 (factor 0.2) ex 2023-03-10 and a bonus 1:1 (factor 0.5) ex 2024-08-01, both applied; first CSV bar 2024-04-02; `csvAdjustedThrough` 2026-04-30. A Dhan raw close of **2,500.00** on 2022-12-01 becomes `2,500 × 0.2 × 0.5 = 250.00`. A CSV close on 2024-05-02 receives **no** factor from the bonus (ex-date 2024-08-01 is on or before `csvAdjustedThrough`: already in the file) and stays as stored. A further split 1:2 (factor 0.5) ex 2026-06-15 applies to both: the Dhan bar `250.00 × 0.5 = 125.00`, the CSV bar `× 0.5`. Volume divides by the same factors (Dhan volume ×10 then ×2 = ×20). *`SourceAwareFactorsTests`.*
9. **Overlap probe.** Three bars Dhan-adjusted 100.00, 100.10, 99.95 against CSV 100.00, 100.10, 99.90: differences 0, 0, 0.0500 % (0.05 / 99.90); median 0 ≤ 5 bps, max 5.0 bps ≤ 50 bps: **pass**. A Dhan series at exactly 0.2× the CSV (raw, not adjusted): median 8,000 bps: after applying decision 8's cumulative 0.2 it matches and passes; with no actions applied it fails and the instrument reads `seam_unresolved`. 100 of 10,000 bars off by more than 50 bps = 1 % → exactly at the limit; 101 → fail. *Unit with `BarComparer` semantics.*
10. **Probe is memory only.** After a probe run `market.bars_1m` row counts and hashes are unchanged. *Integration.*
11. **Seam continuity.** Last adjusted pre-seam close 250.00, first CSV open 252.10: +0.84 % passes. Same with first CSV open 330.00: +32 % beyond ±20 % with no applied action is `seam_jump`; the instrument's pre-seam bars are marked `seam_unverified` and a simulation run skips them with that reason, others unaffected. *Unit + run test.*
12. **Run record carries depth.** Every run record lists per instrument `historyFrom` and the count by depth bucket (under 2 years, 2 to 3, 3 to 5), and the text "members as of today; instruments delisted in the window are absent (Dhan serves active instruments only; ISS-22)"; the "about 2.1 years" sentence is replaced by the real range. A run made before the backfill keeps its stored text. *Handler test + golden record test.*
13. **Daily parse (logical fields).** From fixtures captured by the probe, both formats map to the same logical row. Synthetic rows fix the arithmetic: traded quantity 10,000, delivery quantity 6,000 gives delivery **60.00 %** (recomputed within 0.01 pp of the file's own figure when both exist); a row with delivery quantity above traded quantity is rejected. A raw close of **1,000.00** before a split 1:5 reads **200.00** adjusted. An unknown header refuses the whole file (`history.unknown_format`) and nothing is written. *Parser tests on the captured fixtures.*
14. **Universe as of a date.** A security with rows from 2021-11-01 to 2023-01-04 is in `eod_universe(2022-05-10)` and absent from `eod_universe(2023-06-01)`; it is `inCatalog = false` when not in the instrument master and is not added to tracked instruments; `knownSince` is its first row date. *Integration.*
15. **Daily versus minute is a finding, never a gate.** For a session with minute volume sum 9,950,000 and daily traded quantity 10,000,000 the difference is 0.50 %, inside the tolerance (2 %, setting) and silent; 12 % raises `daily_minute_mismatch` in Data quality. The close is not compared for equality (official close vs last minute); high and low are compared as envelope (daily high must not be below the highest minute high by more than one tick). *Quality tests.*
16. **Corporate-action file never auto-applies.** The NSE actions file is parsed into the existing import **preview**; lines that repeat an existing action are skipped; unknown symbols are listed; nothing becomes `applied` without the owner (P1-10a rules, including `data.csv_already_adjusted`). *Integration.*
17. **Calendar first.** With no calendar rows for a requested range, Part B refuses to start (`history.calendar_missing`) and names the range. *Unit.*
18. Migrations (`market.daily_eod`, `market.backfill_chunks`, any seam flag column) have Down; grants as the schema's pattern (`xd_app` DML; no DELETE on `daily_eod`); `scripts/gen-api.sh` clean; architecture tests green; `scripts/check.sh` green; no log line carries a token, a URL with credentials or file contents.

## Honest limits (printed on the History card and every run record)
- **Intraday depth stops at about five years** (Dhan), and only for **instruments active today**. Delisted and merged names are absent from the 1-minute data, so a longer window widens survivorship bias: more years in which losers disappeared. The daily universe can be repaired from bhavcopy; the minute universe cannot.
- **Bhavcopy cannot replay intraday strategies.** It adds regime context (10+ years daily), 252-day signals and delivery percentage, not trades.
- More years are more of the same market; 2021 to 2026 is not every regime.
- The seam rests on corporate actions the system knows: demergers, rights issues and capital reductions are not kinds today (ISS-20 shows the CSVs already contain such jumps); the backfill adds more of them, flagged by `seam_jump` and the ±20 % rule, not repaired.
- Dhan's claims (5 years, 90 days, active only) were read from its docs and an FAQ snippet; they are verified in practice only by the first probe.
- Licence terms for NSE files were not read; the fetch stays OFF until the owner has.

## Risks
1. **Seam error silently corrupts research** (wrong adjustment): mitigated by the overlap probe, the ±20 % check, and `seam_unverified` exclusion; no backfilled bar enters a simulation before its instrument's probe passed.
2. **Dhan changes limits or the archive depth is less than 5 years for 1-minute**: the probe fetches one window at the 5-year boundary; a refusal becomes the per-instrument `historyFrom`.
3. **Run time and rate limit (DH-904)**: resumable chunks, off-hours window, the existing retry-once rule.
4. **NSE blocks non-browser clients or changes formats**: a failed fetch is `failed`, never worked around; unknown header refuses the file.
5. **Disk**: the guard refuses to start; compression ratio is unmeasured.
6. **Reproducibility changes**: a new run on more bars has a different bars hash and a different key; old runs keep theirs. Owners comparing old and new verdicts must expect differences by design.

Status stays Draft; size 9.5 -> **10.5 agent-days** (+1: seam from stored bars, guard change, extra cases). Amends the text above.
1. **Probe rule (Decision 9 vs Risk 1).** An instrument's pre-seam bars enter simulations only after its **own** probe passed. Default: probe every instrument that has an applied action after its first backfilled date, and a sample (`Probe:Instruments`) of the rest; instruments neither probed nor sampled are `seam_unresolved` and excluded until probed.
2. **Seam from stored bars.** The seam date per instrument is `min(ts)` of rows with `source = 'csv'` in `market.bars_1m` (not `csv_files.first_bar`, which an undo via `undone_at` can move). The planner and the writer **re-check the seam on every run**. A CSV import that reaches before the seam is refused or flagged (`BarStore` marks a CSV row `kept` against an existing Dhan row, so it would otherwise leave Dhan bars in the CSV span unnoticed).
3. **Factor by date against the seam.** Adjustment decides by bar date versus the per-instrument seam date (bars before the seam: Dhan rule; at or after: CSV rule). No dependence on `ReadAsync` exposing the source. Added case: a Dhan bar dated **exactly on the ex-date** takes the rule "factor applies to bars strictly before the ex-date", so it gets no factor from that action, and the previous day's bar does (hand case: bonus 1:1 ex 2024-08-01, raw close 2024-07-31 = 400.00 -> 200.00; 2024-08-01 = 205.00 stays).
4. **Calendar (AC 17).** `trading_days` for the backfilled range is derived from Part A's `daily_eod` (a date with any cash-series rows is a trading day). **Part A is a prerequisite of Part B.**
5. **Allow-list guard change.** `MasterDataEndpoints.IsAllowed` matches exact paths, so dated bhavcopy paths need a code change (pattern-aware guard) that lands with an **empty entry list**; the owner then adds hosts and patterns as the security act.
6. **Times are minimums.** The 10.7 and 42.8 minute figures (and 25 minutes for 500 instruments) are "at least": retries, DH-904 back-off and the off-hours window add to them.
