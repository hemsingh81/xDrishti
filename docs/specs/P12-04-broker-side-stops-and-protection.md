# P12-04 — Broker-side stops and the protection invariant

- **Status:** Approved (2026-10-29: the owner's standing run instruction covers the build; amendments recorded below under "Amendments at build", ADR 0160). Built 2026-10-29 in the reduced form described there.
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P12 — Execution service, slice P12-04 of [P12-00](P12-00-execution-service-plan.md)
- **Design refs:** docs/design/20-extended-modules.md §A ("broker-side stop first — protection never depends on the Mac staying up", Protected, Managing) · 08-exits-and-risk.md §1, §2, §5 (FIXED_RR, trails, time rules, gaps) · 19 §4 ("Mac asleep/offline: broker-side stops once execution exists") · 04 §4.1 · ADR 0038 (stop fills at the open when gapped), ADR 0050. **Design change:** doc 08 §2 gains "what the live system supports" (FIXED_RR and a tighten-only stop; the other policies stay simulator-only until a later slice). ADR: next free ADR number at build time.
- **Needs:** P12-01, P12-02 (sender, FakeBroker), P12-03 (the entry gate; exits bypass it). Feeds P12-05 (halt requests), P12-06 (stream facts), P12-07 (demotion on a breach), P12-09/12.
- **Size:** 3 agent-days
- **Safety:** yes. It places and changes protective orders and can send an automatic exit (flatten). It only ever reduces exposure: no instruction of this slice can open or enlarge a position (there is no corrective buy; a short is a fault the owner fixes). Active only for an intent that already passed the entry gate under an open account gate.

## Goal
Every position the system opens has a stop that lives at the broker, on the correct side, for at least the filled quantity, within `exec.protect_deadline_s` (10 s) of the first fill, and stays there if the Mac or `xd-exec` dies. A position found without one is alarmed, repaired once, and flattened if it cannot be repaired. Stops are only ever tightened. Every intraday position is flat before the broker's own square-off.

## Scope
**In:** Domain `ProtectionPlan` (instructions for the structure), `ProtectionMonitor` (pure verdict), `StopRules` (tighten-only, reduce-only), `FlattenWalk`, `SquareOffPlan`; Application `ProtectionWatchdog` (1 s tick), `ProtectionPlacer`, `ManagementService` (tighten, break-even), `SquareOffService`, `IHaltRequests` port (call-out to P12-05); tables `execution.alarms`, `execution.alarm_clears`; `GET /api/execution/alarms`; settings; FakeBroker faults for stops (P12-02 gets them here); sandbox smoke additions.
**Out:** the kill switch and halts (P12-05, this slice only requests), recovery and the stream (P12-06), the confirm gesture (P12-08), partial-exit and pyramid management (`PARTIAL_BE`, `PARTIAL_TRAIL`, `PYRAMID`, `EMA_TRAIL` stay simulator-only: Decision 4), swing positions held overnight (Decision 5), bell and screens (P12-10).

## The rules
**Structure.** Per account setting `structure`: `super` (default) sends one Dhan super order: entry LIMIT with `targetPrice`, `stopLossPrice`, `trailingJump` 0; the broker creates the stop and target legs for the traded quantity (UNVERIFIED mechanism, canary-tested). `plain_stop` sends a plain entry LIMIT and, on the first fill seen (stream or poll), a reduce-only `STOP_LOSS` leg for the filled quantity (trigger = stop, limit = trigger less/plus `exec.stop_limit_buffer_pct`); the target is then an engine rule (alert, then a reduce-only marketable limit, cancelling the stop only after it fills), never a second resting sell (a resting stop and target of the same shares would double-sell).
**I1 Protection.** For each intent in filled, protected, managing or closing with net quantity q > 0 (from our fills), the sum of the remaining quantity of live stop legs at the broker (status PENDING; TRANSIT does not count; the other legs of a super order do not count) is at least q. Judged from the broker's order book as last read (stream or poll), never from what we intended. Stop and target legs of a super order are identified by (parent order, `LegNo`, the leg's broker order id), the key of `legs` in P12-01, and the canary records that key as the broker returns it.
**I2 Deadline.** The position is unprotected from the instant q exceeds protected quantity; if that lasts longer than `exec.protect_deadline_s` it is a breach. A partial fill raises q: the stop must follow it within the same deadline.
**I3 Tighten only.** A long's stop trigger never moves down (entries are BUY only, P12-03); `StopLoosen`, `AddToPosition` and `ReEntry` are `Refused` in every mode (P12-01 `ActionPolicy`, criterion 17), until a future P12-13; it must stay at least 2 ticks on the loss side of LTP; a target never moves away; at most `exec.max_modifications_per_intent` (20) modifications per intent (the broker allows 25). The one exception is I6.
**I4 Reduce only.** Every exit, flatten or square-off instruction has quantity at most q minus the quantity of exits already in flight; none is sent when q = 0.
**I5 Never negative.** q must never go below 0; a fill that does (a double sell, i.e. a short) is `oversold`: alarm CRITICAL `oversold` and a `freeze` halt request on the account (P12-05); **nothing is sent to correct it**. The owner repairs it in Dhan's app and resolves the break (P12-06). This keeps one rule across the phase: no automatic fix, and no instruction of P12 ever increases a holding other than an owner-approved entry.
**I6 Stop triggered, not filled.** If LTP is beyond the stop limit and the stop leg is still open after `exec.stop_unfilled_s` (5 s), the stop leg's limit price is modified through the market (to LTP less/plus `exec.exit_collar_pct`), keeping the trigger: one order, no double sell.
**Breach ladder.** At the deadline: alarm `protection_breach` (CRITICAL); step A, one repair: `plain_stop` stop for the uncovered quantity (new client order id `S2`, lookup-before-resend of P12-02); step B, if the stop is not live `exec.protect_deadline_s` later, or A is rejected, and `exec.flatten_on_unprotected` is true (default): flatten, a reduce-only marketable limit at LTP less/plus `exec.exit_collar_pct`, re-priced by `exec.flatten_step_collar_pct` per step for up to `exec.flatten_walk_steps` (3) steps 3 s apart; the position moves `closing`. If still not flat: CRITICAL `flatten_incomplete`, halt request L1, retry every 5 s up to attempts F1..F3, then the owner. With the setting false, the alarm and the halt request are the whole response. Blind (broker view older than 30 s): `protection_unknown` alarm and halt request L1; no automatic flatten while blind.
**Working entries.** At `exec.entry_window_end` (15:00) every working or part-filled entry is cancelled (a part-filled one ends `open>filled` with the filled quantity protected); at `exec.square_off_time` any entry still working (a DAY order can still fill after the square-off and open a position nobody is watching) is cancelled again and the cancel is verified in the order book before the exit sequence starts; an entry found working after 15:15 is `exit_failed` CRITICAL.
**Square-off.** At `exec.square_off_time` (15:15): cancel the target leg and wait for the ack (3 s), send the reduce-only exit, cancel the stop leg only after the exit fills, verify net 0 by a position read; if the stop fills first, cancel the exit at once. Dhan may square off INTRADAY positions itself around the close (UNVERIFIED): a position closed by a fill that is not ours is recorded `broker_squareoff` and is not a break.
**Authority.** The owner's tap (P12-08) or the auto-live rule (P12-09) covers the intent's whole protective life: stop, target, tighten, flatten, square-off. These actions do not need a new tap. **Placing or repairing a protective stop and flattening a filled position are permitted at every halt level, including `freeze`** (P12-05 table; P12-06 recovery step 4 onward); tightening and cancelling a working entry are not under `freeze`. Under `freeze` our picture is untrusted, so each such instruction additionally requires a fresh broker position read (at most 15 s old) whose `netQty` is at least our q, and its quantity is at most the broker's `netQty`. The actions also continue when the mode is lowered while a position is open; nothing continues without the account having been open when the entry was sent.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Instructions.** For intent 361 (BUY 10 of `1594`, entry 1,500.00, stop 1,485.00, target 1,530.00) `ProtectionPlan` gives the `super` entry of P12-02 criterion 3; for `plain_stop` it gives the entry then, on a fill of 4, a SELL `STOP_LOSS` for 4 with trigger 1,485.00 and limit 1,477.55 (trigger less 0.5% = 1,477.575, rounded down to the 0.05 tick); entries are BUY only, so the stop, target and exit instructions are SELLs and there is no SELL entry. *`ProtectionPlanTests`.*
2. **Monitor table.** `ProtectionMonitor` over 40 hand rows (q, live stop quantities and statuses, ages) returns Protected, Pending(deadline at), Breach(reason) or Unknown; TRANSIT stops and the target leg of a super order do not protect; a stop smaller than q breaches for the difference; a stop looser than the intent's breaches as `stop_mismatch`. *`ProtectionMonitorTests`.*
3. **Timeline.** With a fake clock: a fill at t=0 and a live stop at t=4 gives no alarm; no stop at t=10.5 gives `protection_breach` and step A; the repaired stop at t=13 clears it. A partial fill of 4 then 6 more requires cover for 4 within 10 s of the first and for 10 within 10 s of the second. *`ProtectionWatchdogTests`.*
4. **Ladder on the FakeBroker.** Stop leg silently deleted: breach, step A re-places it, protected. A rejecting broker: A fails, B flattens, the intent goes filled>closing>closed with fills, alarms `protection_breach`, `flatten_started`, no negative position. A broker that accepts but never fills the flatten: three re-prices, `flatten_incomplete`, halt request, retries, owner. With `flatten_on_unprotected` false: alarms and halt request only. *`ProtectionLadderTests`.*
5. **Tighten and reduce properties.** Over 5,000 seeds of random management requests, (a) the stop never loosens, never comes within 2 ticks of LTP, modifications never exceed the cap; (b) the sum of exit quantities in flight never exceeds q and no exit is sent at q = 0; negative controls (a version allowing a loosening, one ignoring in-flight exits) are caught on at least 10 seeds each. *`StopRulesPropertyTests`.*
6. **Break-even move.** With `exec.breakeven_at_r` > 0 (default 0, off) a stop is tightened to entry plus one tick once LTP is at least that many initial risks in favour; never otherwise. *`ManagementServiceTests`.*
7. **Stop triggered, not filled.** LTP through the stop limit for 5 s with the leg open: the leg's limit is modified to the marketable price, the trigger unchanged, one order only. *`StopUnfilledTests`.*
8. **Square-off race.** On the FakeBroker the stop and the exit fill in the same second: the net becomes −q, `oversold` is raised as CRITICAL, a `freeze` halt is requested and **no instruction is sent** to correct it; in 1,000 seeds the net ends at 0, or the CRITICAL alarm and the freeze stand and the intent is not marked reconciled (P12-06 shows the break). *`SquareOffRaceTests`.*
8a. **Late entry fill.** On the FakeBroker a DAY entry is still working at 15:00 and at 15:15 (price path reaches the limit at 15:16): it is cancelled at 15:00; a part-filled one has its remainder cancelled and the filled quantity protected; at 15:15 any working entry is cancelled and verified before the exit sequence; no fill occurs after the square-off in 1,000 seeds. *`LateEntryTests`.*
8b. **Halt levels.** At each of `entries`, `cancel`, `exit_all`, `freeze` a stop repair and a flatten are sent when the broker position read agrees; under `freeze` with a broker `netQty` below our q they are not sent and `protection_unknown` is alarmed. *`ProtectionUnderHaltTests`.*
9. **The Mac dies.** FakeBroker with a live stop and target; the host is stopped for 20 minutes of virtual time; the stop fires at the broker; on restart the fills are read, the intent closes, no instruction is sent for a protected position. *`HostDownTests`.*
10. **Blind.** No stream and no poll for 30 s with an open position: `protection_unknown`, halt request L1, no flatten. *`BlindTests`.*
11. **Alarms.** Each alarm is one insert-only row (code, severity, account, intent, detail, at); a clear is one row (`auto` when the condition ends, `owner` by command); `GET /api/execution/alarms?open=true` lists the open ones, newest first, at most 200. Codes: `protection_pending`, `protection_breach`, `stop_replaced`, `stop_mismatch`, `stop_triggered_unfilled`, `flatten_started`, `flatten_incomplete`, `oversold`, `send_unknown`, `protection_unknown`, `exit_failed`, `broker_squareoff`. *integration.*
12. **Reporting for demotion.** A `protection_breach` writes the fact P12-07 reads ("filled entry without a confirmed broker-side stop at the deadline"): alarm row plus event `alarm` on the intent. *integration.*
13. `scripts/gen-api.sh` run; `scripts/check.sh` green.

## Contract

- **Data:** `execution.alarms(id bigint identity, at, code, severity check, account_id null, intent_id null, detail text up to 300)` and `execution.alarm_clears(alarm_id pk fk, at, by text, how check in ('auto','owner'))`, insert-only, `xd_app` SELECT only; migration `AddExecutionProtection` with `Down`.
- **Settings added:** `exec.stop_limit_buffer_pct` 0.5 (0.1..2), `exec.flatten_step_collar_pct` 0.5 (0.1..2), `exec.flatten_walk_steps` 3 (1..5), `exec.breakeven_at_r` 0 (0..3), `exec.stop_unfilled_s` 5 (2..30), `exec.blind_alarm_s` 30 (10..120), plus `structure` per account in the P12-07 catalogue (default `super`).
- **Port out:** `IHaltRequests.RequestAsync(scope, level, triggerCode, detail)` (P12-05 implements; until then a recording stub).
- **Log ids** 6645 to 6659.

## Non-functional
Watchdog tick 1 s, evaluation under 5 ms for 10 positions; broker order-book poll every 5 s while any position or working entry exists (inside the 5 per second read limit); alarm write under 10 ms; a repair or flatten instruction leaves within 500 ms of the decision.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **Super order as the default structure.** The stop and target legs exist at the broker from the entry, so there is no window in which our process must act, and they survive the Mac. Dhan documents entry, target and stop-loss legs, a trailing jump, modification of legs, cancel of a leg or the whole order, and nesting of legs under the entry in the list (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/super-order/, read 2026-10-24). Rejected as default: plain entry plus stop on the fill event (a gap of seconds that depends on us; it stays as the fallback and as the answer if super orders misbehave in the canary).
2. **The stop is a stop-loss limit with a 0.5% buffer, not stop-loss market.** Reason: other brokers require market protection on market and SL-M orders (https://www.kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/52157, read 2026-10-24) and Dhan's order page shows no such field (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/orders/, read 2026-10-24); a limit with a buffer is a plain accepted order. Its known weakness (a gap through the limit leaves the stop open) is covered by I6 and the 5-second rule. The leg type inside a super order is UNVERIFIED.
3. **Flatten by default when unprotected, and say so.** A position without a stop breaks the one invariant the design promises (doc 20), and the exit only reduces risk. The setting can be turned off by the owner (a risk-raising direction, so password; P12-07). Rejected: alarm only (a human may be away; the alarm cannot protect). The 10-second deadline, one repair and a second 10 seconds are chosen so that a typical stop distance of 0.2% to 1% is rarely crossed meanwhile; they are assumptions to review after P12-12.
4. **First cut supports FIXED_RR plus a tighten-only stop.** Partial exits, pyramids and signal exits need several broker orders sharing shares; a super order has one target and one stop. They remain simulator-only; the doc 08 `managing` state is used for tightened stops. Rejected: emulating partials with extra resting sells (double-sell risk).
5. **Intraday only.** Super-order validity is DAY; carrying a stop overnight needs another product (CNC/MARGIN with re-placed stops, or Dhan forever orders; semantics UNVERIFIED). Swing stays `manual_live` (P11) until a later slice (P12-00 findings).
6. **Reduce-only and no-negative are domain rules, not broker features.** Dhan's order page lists no reduce-only flag. There is no exception: an `oversold` position is a fault that freezes the account and is repaired by the owner, because an automatic buy would be an unapproved entry and an automatic fix (P12-06).
7. **Protection judged from the broker's book.** The order-update stream carries `LegNo` 1 entry, 2 stop, 3 target and status (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/order-update/, read 2026-10-24); the order book and super-order list are the ground truth when the stream is silent.
8. **Square-off by us, before the broker's.** Doc 08 §5 `square_off` 15:15; broker timing unknown (UNVERIFIED), so we never rely on it and never fight it (`broker_squareoff`).

## Risks and honest limits
- Super-order behaviour (leg type, part-filled entry, modify rules, auto square-off) is read from documents, not observed: the first live day per structure is a one-share canary with the owner present, enforced by the Hard check `canary_cap` (P12-03, setting `exec.canary_pending`), not left to habit (P12-00).
- A stop-limit is not a guarantee of exit; a fast gap can leave the position open until I6 acts (5 s), and the loss can exceed 1R.
- Flatten at a marketable limit pays spread and collar; the setting trades a possible small loss for the invariant.
- A broker or network outage longer than the watchdog's reach leaves the broker-side stop as the only protection, by design.
- The deadline and thresholds were not fitted to data.

## Open questions
None. (`flatten_on_unprotected` defaults to true, the safe direction; turning it off is the owner's act.)

- **Nothing is composed.** No host composes the watchdog, the policy, the manager, the square-off service, `IBrokerStops` or the halt stub; `IExitSendPolicy` stays the closed default. They are tested against the FakeBroker only. Composition needs the real snapshot (stream, poll, stored fills: P12-06), the halt store (P12-05) and the mode ladder (P12-07). The conditions for opening the default are therefore not all met.
- **Authority amendment.** Legs that only reduce risk (stop, exit, flatten) are gated by `ProtectionGate` (the global switch, the environment and an answer), not by the mode: an account lowered to paper while a position is open can still protect it. An entry still needs an executing mode. The reduce-only rule decides whether there is anything to protect.
- **Flatten walk.** The sender refuses a price outside `exec.exit_collar_pct` of the reference, so the walk starts at one `exec.flatten_step_collar_pct`, widens by one step per attempt and is capped at the exit collar (with the defaults 0.5%, 1.0%, 1.0%). After the walk steps: `flatten_incomplete`, a halt request, three retries at 5 s (attempts 4 to 6), then the owner. A resting flatten is cancelled and gone from the book before the next is sent, so two flatten sells never rest together.
- **Resend gate.** A stop or flatten leg of ours found in TRANSIT, or a send that came back Unknown, makes the next send of that kind a resend: allowed only under `ResendPolicy`, i.e. never while `exec.lookup_verified` is OFF (the default). The repair is then not sent, `send_unknown` is raised, and the alarm and halt request stand.
- **Wire (all UNVERIFIED, fake handler tests only):** a stop is `STOP_LOSS` with `triggerPrice` on `POST /v2/orders` (sell only, limit at or below the trigger, tick); a stop change is `PUT /v2/orders/{id}` with `STOP_LOSS`, trigger and limit; a super entry is `POST /v2/super/orders` with `targetPrice`, `stopLossPrice`, `trailingJump` 0. This is the one new row of the guard table. A resting target leg is still refused (the target is an engine rule). Super-order changes and leg cancels are not built, so a super target leg cannot be cancelled at the square-off: the exit is not sent and `exit_failed` is raised rather than risk a double sell. The order book read now carries `triggerPrice` and `legName` when present.
- **Not built, moved:** the per-account `structure` setting (P12-07; the intent's structure is used), `broker_squareoff` recording (needs the fills of P12-06), a fix for `stop_mismatch` (alarm and halt request only; no automatic change), super-leg identification by (parent, `LegNo`, leg id) beyond the leg name (canary C1), the sandbox smoke additions, `HostDown` beyond a restarted watchdog on the FakeBroker, the Design doc 08 text beyond the pointer added, a screen (P12-10), the owner clear command (the table accepts `owner`; no command writes it yet), criterion 12's read by P12-07 (the facts exist: alarm row and event).
- **Column name:** `alarm_clears.cleared_by` (not `by`); the API field is `clearedBy`.
- **Criteria met in tests:** 1 `ProtectionRulesTests`; 2 the monitor table (25 rows plus mismatch, blind and boundary rows); 3, 4, 8b, 9, 10, 12 `ProtectionWatchdogTests`; 5 `StopManagementTests` (5,000 seeds, four negative controls caught on at least 10 seeds each); 6 and 7 `StopManagementTests`; 8 and 8a `SquareOffTests` (1,000 seeds each); 11 `ExecAlarmsTests` and `ExecutionAlarmsApiTests`; 13 `gen-api` run.

### Review fixes at build (adversarial review, 2026-10-29)
- **Shortfall owed, stated plainly:** criterion 2 asks for 40 hand rows. The table in `ProtectionRulesTests` has **25** rows, plus 4 rows of the stop-mismatch test and 4 of the blind-view test (33 monitor cases in all). **7 rows are owed** (to be added in the slice that composes the watchdog).
- **Unknown exit.** The square-off exit is never sent again after an Unknown ack, or while the book read predates the send, or within 3 s of it, unless `ResendPolicy` allows it (`exec.lookup_verified`, default OFF); `send_unknown` is raised. Tests include a negative control.
- **Stop plus flatten.** Before a flatten, our resting stop legs are cancelled and must be gone from the book; after three unconfirmed cancels, or for a stop with no client id of ours, `exit_failed` is raised and nothing is flattened. Once flat, any stop of ours still resting is cancelled (a sell stop on a flat intraday position could open a short).
- **Repair with an Unknown outcome** is pending lookup, not failed: no flatten starts and nothing is sent again while unresolved.
- **Halt request** is made before any event append, an event store that throws cannot prevent it, and it is made again while the alarm stands and no (or a milder) halt is in force.
- **Wire:** an exit or flatten with a BUY side is refused in `DhanWire.Validate`, as well as by the policy.
