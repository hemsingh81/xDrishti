## Goal
In Learning the owner reads where the edge goes between the plan and the fills (only the steps that have data, the rest honestly not evaluable), whether their decisions and the ranking added value, what the autonomy switches are and how they changed, and the P6 and P7 phase checks as MET / NOT MET / NOT EVALUABLE lists with the limits always printed. Today (no fills, no model) most of it is correctly empty.

## Scope
**In:** four tabs through the P8-02b registry (`plan`, `decisions`, `autonomy`, `checks`); a pure `Domain/Reporting/PlanVsActual`; the endpoint `GET /api/reports/plan-vs-actual`; the thin endpoint `GET /api/learning/phase-check`; the "Execution gap" tile; an additive `basisText` on the kit's `EvidenceBlock`; one link from the `/plans` grade panel; fixtures and tests; an ADR.
**Out:** any autonomy form or switch (P8-19); monthly execution-quality charts, slippage by liquidity bucket, "execution alerts", the capacity score (no data source); recomputation of grades; the fill-grade pipeline (P7-14); a structured fold table; a stored P6 check (the check is computed on read); exports.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given the registry of P8-02b, then four tabs are added by four files and registry lines; the one other built file touched is `OverviewTab.tsx` (the built overview has its tiles inline; the "Execution gap" tile is added there, as a single new tile, unless P8-02b already turned the tiles into a list, in which case one list entry).
2. **Plan vs actual, no data.** Given plans exist but no journal entry has a fill grade, then the chain is `empty` ("No ticket has been graded on fills yet; fills arrive with paper and live trading, P10/P11"), the counts are printed (approved ticket lines, without fills), the waterfall draws no bar, and the "Execution gap" tile is `not_evaluable`.
3. **Plan vs actual, hand-worked** (`MinTickets` 2). Given four tickets graded on fills (plan R, entry slippage R, charges rupees with quantity and planned entry/stop, fill R after charges): T1 2.0, 0.10, 40, 100, 500/490, 1.80; T2 -1.0, 0.05, 30, 200, 250/245, -1.12; T3 1.5, 0.20, 20, 50, 1000/980, 1.20; T4 0.5, 0.00, 50, 100, 400/395, 0.40, then the chain (means over those four) is plan prices +0.75 R, entry slippage -0.0875 R, charges -0.0475 R (charges R = charges ÷ (quantity × |entry - stop|): 0.04, 0.03, 0.02, 0.10), not attributed -0.045 R (the remainder: exit slippage and anything else, 0.06, 0.04, 0.08, 0.00), actual +0.57 R, and 0.75 - 0.0875 - 0.0475 - 0.045 = 0.57; each step has n 4.
4. Given tickets whose fill grade is `uncertain` or `no_plan_levels`, or whose planned risk per share is zero, then they are left out of the chain and counted with their reason; given an approved ticket line with no matched fill, it is counted as "without fills", and when its plan-price grade triggered, its plan R is summed as "missed triggers" (a count and a total plan R with n, outside the chain).
5. Given fewer graded tickets than `MinTickets` (default 15), then every chain step and every by-strategy figure is `insufficient` ("n 4 of at least 15") with no number. By strategy (`MinTickets` 2, the tickets above with T1, T2 = ORB and T3, T4 = VWAP): ORB plan +0.50, fill +0.34, entry slippage 0.075; VWAP plan +1.00, fill +0.80, entry slippage 0.10; at `MinTickets` 3 both rows are `insufficient`.
6. Given exit slippage and the effect of the owner's overrides, then they are listed as "not decomposed" with their reason (the plan grade stores no simulated exit price; overrides are tickets, see Decisions) and are never given a number in the chain.
7. Given approvals in the range, then `approvals` counts the approve decisions by kind (`owner`, `auto`) from the stored approval rows, and a plan decided by the auto rule is counted under `auto` (never under the owner); zero plans show `empty`.
8. **Decisions.** Given `GET /api/reports/decisions?from&to`, then the tab shows the top-1/3/5/all cumulative R curves (the existing `TimeSeriesChart`, series by role and dash, with its table), the groups table (primary, reserve, rejected), the decisions table (approved, modified, rejected, lapsed, undecided), the four comparisons with their verdict words (first better, second better, equal, not enough data) and the server notes verbatim (including `TRUNCATED`); the basis "simulated on plan prices" is printed. A stats row with zero triggered tickets is `empty`; a row whose `note` is present ("indicative only: 3 triggered ticket(s), fewer than 30") is `provisional` with that note as the reason; a comparison with null `meanDifference` is `not_evaluable`. The page never says "significant".
9. Given the date range selector (`from`, `to` in the URL, default the plans' range), then a range with `from` after `to` shows the server's 422 message.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **A server read, not browser arithmetic.** Question: the waterfall needs means over many tickets and charges ÷ risk; where? Chosen: a pure `PlanVsActual` core behind `GET /api/reports/plan-vs-actual` over existing stores. Reason: "the browser computes no statistic" (P8-00) and the fill grade, plan grade and approvals are in separate stores. Rejected: N+1 browser calls (`/plans`, `/journal` per plan) and a client mean.
2. **Draw only the steps the stored data support.** The fill grade stores entry slippage (R), recorded charges (rupees), the plan entry/stop and the fill R after charges, but no simulated exit price. So the chain is plan prices, entry slippage, charges, a remainder labelled "not attributed" (exit slippage and anything else) and the actual result, over the same tickets so the arithmetic closes. Exit slippage and overrides are "not decomposed": a ticket the owner rejected or let lapse has no fill, so its effect is read in Decisions, not in a per-fill chain. Rejected: the prototype's invented step sizes; allocating the remainder to exit slippage (unproven).
3. **Missed triggers outside the chain.** They are tickets, not per-trade deltas: a count and total plan R with n. Rejected: spreading them over traded tickets.
4. **Below 30 triggered tickets the Decisions numbers are `provisional`, not hidden.** The server (ADR 0090) already prints them with the note "indicative only", and the Plans screen shows them; the note is the server's, so no threshold is duplicated in the browser. Chain figures use the stricter rule (minimum 15, `insufficient`) because they are new and have no built precedent. Inconsistent on purpose, stated on screen; Rejected: hiding the Decisions figures (changes a built contract) or reusing 15 there.
5. **The P6 check gets a read endpoint.** ADR 0080 chose "no endpoint" so P6 would not grow an API; P8 is the screen phase and the check only reads stored records and re-judges two runs, writing nothing. Chosen: a thin GET, no client-supplied replay time (a client could otherwise print an unmeasured "replayed in 4 s"). Rejected: leaving it a verb (the owner then never sees it in the app); storing the result (a write path and a stale answer).
6. **Autonomy is read only.** Q2 and Q8 are taken as recommended; the PUT exists and demands a fresh password on every change; this tab names where, shows the history and the auto/owner split, and adds nothing that could change a switch.
7. **`basisText` on the kit.** The kit offers gross/net/unknown; fills are neither the stored charge sheet nor unknown. An additive optional text keeps P8-01's contract and tests and avoids a wrong "Net R (after the stored charge sheet)" label.
8. **No monthly execution charts or capacity score.** The prototype's monthly slippage and missed-trigger charts and fill-rate table need fills over months and a capacity score that is not stored; shown as a single line "not built: needs fills".
9. **Q1, Q2, Q8, Q10, Q12, Q14** (P8-00) as recommended: no writes from the UI, no composite, the answer may be "not evaluable".

## Risks and honest limits
- No graded real trade exists (P10/P11): plan vs actual, execution gap and the live line are `not_evaluable` or empty today; the plan-price grades are simulations on plan prices, not fills, and provisional plans used cells that passed gates but are not paper-proven.
- The "not attributed" step can hide more than exit slippage (partial exits, timing); it is named a remainder for that reason.
- Charges in R use the planned risk per share, so a modified size or a moved stop changes the meaning; modified tickets are graded at the original plan prices (ADR 0090).
- Fewer than 15 graded tickets will be the norm for months; the screen then shows counts only.
- The P6 check may take seconds on real data; it is measured, and it is a read, not a replay by the owner.
- Auto-approval counts rely on approval rows only; a plan approved before P7-12 has none.
