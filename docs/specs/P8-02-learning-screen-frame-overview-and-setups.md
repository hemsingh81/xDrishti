## Goal
`/learning` exists and is honest: a frame with tabs in the URL, an Overview that says what the learning engine holds (counts and the newest stored verdicts, or that it holds nothing yet), and a Setups tab that shows every stored cell by strategy, side, style, timeframe and exit with its sample size, expectancy and credible bound, where it stands in its lifecycle, and what changed, with a thin cell never reading as a winner.

## Scope
**In:** the lazy route and nav entry; the tab frame and a tab registry that later slices extend; the Overview (tiles, record sections, limits); Setups (heat grid, window/exit/style selectors, lifecycle funnel, cell drawer, changelog timeline); `HeatGrid` and `useUrlState` in `shared/analytics`; the read-only folder test; fixtures and MSW tests.
**Out:** Lessons, Experiments, Models, Digest (P8-03); Plan vs actual, Decisions, Autonomy, phase checks (P8-04); Journey (P8-12); Smart rules (P8-13); any composite "Learning health" score (D10, Q12); a cell move, revert or designation control (P8-19, owner-gated); a server change.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given `/learning`, then a lazy route renders the page header, the tile row and a tab list (WAI-ARIA tabs via `ButtonTabs`) whose selected tab is the `?tab=` URL parameter (`overview` default, `setups`); an unknown `tab` falls back to Overview; Back works; the nav entry "Learning" no longer carries a phase tag and the route no longer renders the "coming in phase" page.
2. Given the tab registry (`tabs.ts`: `{ id, label, load: () => import(...), slice }` entries), then adding a tab is one new file and one registry line (P8-03, P8-04, P8-12, P8-13 do exactly that); tabs not built yet are not rendered as tabs; the Overview prints one line "Not built yet: Journey (P8-12), Smart rules (P8-13)" from the registry's planned entries.
3. Given an empty learning store (`cellRows` 0, every section `counted` 0), then the Overview shows `empty` for every section with the verb that fills it as text (`xd learn cells`, `xd learn model`, `xd learn experiment`, `xd learn hypothesis`, `xd learn lesson`, `xd learn lifecycle`), no tile shows a digit other than 0 counts written as words ("No records yet"), and the limits from the response are printed in an `EvidenceBlock`.
4. Given records, then each section shows its counted number, the split by role/outcome/state (names as the server sends them), the newest record (`label`, stored `verdict`, `detail`, time in India time), and, when `truncated` is true, "at least 200 (the newest 200 are counted)"; the champion of Models is shown when present, otherwise "No champion model" as `not_evaluable`.
5. Given the tiles, then they are: Setups (rows in the newest snapshot and its as-of date), Models, Experiments, Hypotheses, Lessons, Changelog (counts above), Calibration error (the champion's `pooledEcePp` from `GET /api/models?role=champion&limit=1`, else `not_evaluable` "no champion model"), Live expectancy (`not_evaluable` "no live trades exist until paper and live trading, P10/P11"), Learning uplift vs the frozen model (`not_evaluable` "the frozen-model comparison is not stored yet, P8-12"). No tile combines parts into a score.
6. Given Setups with a snapshot, then a heat grid has one row per (strategy, side, style) and one column per timeframe for the chosen exit (`exit=`, default the exit label with the most cells, ties alphabetical) and window (`window=all|250d|60d`, default `all`), with a metric choice (`metric=expectancy` default | `winlow`: win rate, lower credible bound); each cell is a button named "strategy, side, style, timeframe, exit, window: <figure or state>, n <n>"; cells that do not exist in the snapshot are blank cells labelled "No such cell" (not a zero).
7. Given a cell whose `enough` is false, then it is `insufficient` ("n 12 of at least 30" from `n` and `minTrades`), hatched, and its expectancy, win rate and bounds are not drawn anywhere on the screen, including the drawer (they remain in the API); given `enough` true, the figure is drawn with its sign as text; the colour scale uses only enough cells, is symmetric around 0 for expectancy and around 0.5 for the win-rate bound, and a legend states "hatched = insufficient data". A property test: no insufficient cell renders a figure digit other than its n and the minimum.
8. Given a cell is activated (click or Enter), then a drawer opens (`cell=<key>` in the URL, key URL-encoded because it contains `|`) showing the three windows side by side from `GET /api/cells/{key}`: n, wins, raw and shrunk win rate with credible lower and upper bound, expectancy, profit factor, max drawdown (R), MAE and MFE p50/p90, basis ("gross R (before charges)" or "net R"), prior strength and half-life, `asOf`; a missing value is `unavailable` (expectancy, MAE/MFE) or `not_evaluable` ("no losing trade", profit factor and average loss; "no winning trade", average win); the server's `limits` text in an `EvidenceBlock`; 404 `learning.cell_not_found` shows "No stored cell with this key".
9. Given the drawer, then it also shows where the cell is (`GET /api/changelog/cell-state?cell=`: `state`, `legalNext`, `paperDaysBeforeActive`, `problems`, and the server's note "A record only: nothing acts on a cell's state") with the legal next states as plain text (no buttons), and that cell's changelog rows (`GET /api/changelog?subjectKind=cell&subject=<key>`).
10. Given the lifecycle funnel, then it counts cells by state (Research, Candidate, Paper, Active, Paused, Retired, in the order of doc 09 §7): cells with changelog rows are resolved with `cell-state` (the server replays the rows; reverts are handled there, not guessed), for at most 25 distinct cells taken from the newest changelog cell rows; every other cell of the snapshot is Research by definition; when more than 25 cells have rows the funnel prints "state resolved for the 25 newest of N cells with moves" and marks the counts `provisional` with that reason; each bar carries its count as text and a text list is the table fallback.
11. Given the changelog timeline, then the newest 50 rows (or fewer) are listed: kind (transition, designation, revert), subject (cell key or `model:ID`, linking to the cell drawer for a cell), from and to state, actor, reason, evidence ids, time; a revert reads "reverts #id"; an empty log says "No changes recorded yet".
12. Given no cell snapshot, then Setups shows `empty` "No cell statistics yet; run `xd learn cells`" and requests no cell detail; given `GET /api/cells` fails, `ErrorState` for Setups only; Overview still works.
13. Given server text (reasons, limits, actors, labels, strategy names, evidence ids), then it is rendered as text; a test feeds markup into each field and asserts no element is created.
14. Given `src/test/readOnlyFeatures.test.ts` (created here, a list of feature folders), then `features/learning` contains no `useMutation`, no `api.POST|PUT|DELETE|PATCH` and no `method: 'POST'`; later slices add their folder to the list.
15. Bundle and clock: first-load JavaScript within 200 KB gzip (the only eager changes are one route object and removing the `phase` tag); the Learning route chunk size is printed by `npm run size` and recorded; all times use `pinClock`.

## Non-functional
- First paint of the Overview needs 2 requests; Setups needs 2 plus at most 25 `cell-state` calls (parallel, only for cells with rows, normally zero) plus one detail call when a cell is opened.
- Heat grid up to 40 rows by 8 columns renders without virtualisation; above 400 cells the row labels stay fixed and the grid scrolls inside its card.
- Accessibility: tab panel `aria-labelledby`, polite live region "Setups updated" when selectors change, visible focus ring, hatched cells also carry the word, `prefers-reduced-motion` adds no animation, contrast tests extended for the hatch and the funnel bars in both modes.
- No secrets; actors may contain the owner's address (it is the owner's own screen).

## Decisions (researched)
1. **The heat grid is HTML, not an ECharts heatmap.** Question: how are setups drawn? Chosen: a CSS/HTML `role="grid"` of buttons, because each cell needs an accessible name, a hatched insufficient state with its word and a keyboard path; the APG grid pattern prescribes exactly these roles and keys ([W3C APG grid pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/grid/), read 2026-10-24); and it adds no chart code to any chunk. Rejected: the prototype's ECharts heatmap with a `visualMap` (a canvas cannot carry per-cell states; it would add the heatmap and visual-map components to the lazy chunk).
2. **A cell below the minimum is not drawn as a figure at all,** although `GET /api/cells` still returns numbers for it. Reason: D3 and the P6 rule `enough` (ADR 0064): a shrunk estimate from 12 trades invites a read. Rejected: drawing it faded (a number is still read).
3. **The funnel counts what the server replays.** There is no endpoint that counts cells by state; replaying in the browser would guess at reverts. Chosen: `cell-state` per cell that has rows, capped at 25, with a provisional note beyond. Rejected: taking the newest row's `toState` (wrong after a revert); a new count endpoint now (backend work for a store that is normally empty; written under Findings if cell moves become common).
4. **A tab registry, not a switch.** Four later slices add tabs; extending by adding a file satisfies the open-closed rule and avoids four writers on one file (parallel worktrees).
5. **Tiles show what exists; the rest are honest `not_evaluable`.** The prototype's tiles (live expectancy, uplift, calibration, execution gap, lessons, auto changes) are kept where a source exists and shown as `not_evaluable` with the reason where it does not; the "Learning health" ring and its five sub-scores are dropped (no definition exists in code, D10, Q12). The execution-gap tile arrives with P8-04.
6. **`useUrlState` lives in `shared/analytics`,** not `shared/lib`, because `frontend/CLAUDE.md` counts eager `shared/lib` additions against the 200 KB budget and only lazy screens use it.
7. **No action buttons.** "Run weekly retrain now", "Weekly digest" and "New experiment" start jobs or open writes; Q3 and Q9 are taken as recommended (none in P8 until the owner answers); the empty states name the verbs instead.
8. **One window and one exit at a time in the grid.** Cells of different exits for the same strategy/timeframe are different cells and cannot be merged without a statistic; the selector keeps the grid honest. Source: `CellId` (key includes exit and style).
9. **Staleness 60 s.** Cell snapshots, models and the changelog are written by verbs and the weekly service (paused); polling would only cost.
10. **Q10 to Q14 and Q19** (P8-00) taken as recommended; this slice touches Q12 (no composite) and Q8/Q9 (read only).

## Risks and honest limits
- On the owner's machine the first view is mostly empty (no model, no experiment, as last read in ADR 0080); the fixtures, not the owner's data, prove the layouts.
- A cell snapshot is on replayed history with modelled fills, in-sample, with a prior whose parent includes the cell's own trades and an interval that treats trades as independent (the server's `limits`, printed).
- The funnel's states are records; nothing acts on them before paper and live data exist (P10 and later); the screen says so.
- Layout is not seen in a real browser until the P8-16 checklist; jsdom cannot assert 360 px or 4K.
- The grid shows one exit at a time; a strategy tested with several exits needs several looks, and the screen does not rank across them (that is the Reports What works view).
