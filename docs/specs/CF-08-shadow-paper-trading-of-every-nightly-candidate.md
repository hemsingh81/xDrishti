- **Needs:** P7 nightly plan, plan record and `TicketReplayer` (built), P10-01/04/05 (built), CF-00. Feeds CF-09, CF-10, CF-11, CF-12. CF-01 supplies the effective-n estimator (this slice stores what it needs and uses a placeholder rho of 0.3 until then).
- **Size:** about 6 agent-days (migration and writer 2, grader reuse 1, hash chain and DB guards 1, edge count and API 1, features and score persistence 1). The edge-count tab on `/paper` is a follow-up, not in this number.
- **Safety:** yes (light). A new automatic writer service. It places no order, calls no broker, moves no ticket, changes no setting and changes **nothing the plan reads**. It starts **paused**; switching it on is the owner's act (setting default OFF).

## Assumptions (taken as recommended)
1. **What is stored today (read).** The nightly plan builder (`Application/Planning/SelectionPlanBuilder.cs`) writes plan items in groups `Primary`, `Reserve` and `Rejected` (candidates dropped by the portfolio fill, with reason codes) and handles `selection.NotEligible`; so more than the primary list is in the plan record already. The research note's UNVERIFIED ("is every candidate stored?") is therefore partly answered: the **ranked, reserve and fill-rejected** candidates are plan items. Candidates merged away as duplicates (`CandidateMerger`, reason `merged_duplicate`) and candidates failing eligibility filters are pre-plan and whether they are persisted is UNVERIFIED; they are **not** edge evidence anyway (below).
2. **P10 counts only armed tickets** (P10-01 AC2) and, of those, the primary group, measured epoch, once per ticket (`CountedSet.Select`); reserve tickets are "shown, not counted". About 43 to 58 trades in 12 weeks (P10-00 D11). This slice does not change any of it.
3. **One replay path.** `TicketReplayer` is the single place that walks a ticket through the stored 1-minute bars with the ADR 0038 rules and the stored sheet; grading and paper capture both call it. Shadow grading calls it too and nothing else.
4. **Honest scope.** A shadow trade is a **modelled** fill (touch is fill, no queue, no latency: `FillLimits.NotModelled`). It adds evidence about the **rules' edge**, not about execution (that is P11). Every response says so.

## Decisions (researched)
**D1. Shadow every candidate of every nightly plan, graded after the close by the same simulator.** A candidate = a plan item of any group (Primary, Reserve, Rejected-by-fill). Set aside as `counts_for_edge = false` but still recorded: `merged_duplicate` (a duplicate of a kept signal is not an independent observation) and `held`/not-eligible items. *Why:* paper only sees owner-approved and armed tickets, so 60 trades take about 8 weeks at 1.5 armed tickets a session while 6 candidates a session give 240 trades in 40 sessions; correlation lowers the real gain (D5). *Rejected:* back-filling shadow trades from history (that is the backtest); merging shadow trades into the P10 count (owner selection would enter the sample and the exit criterion D6 would change silently); recording live minute bars to simulate on (a new feed writer for worse bars than the official ones, P10-00 D1). *Source:* confidence-learning-and-validation-speed.md §2.1.
**D2. Forward-only, timestamped by the database, written before the open.** A row is inserted by the writer from the **stored** plan after it is complete and before the session it is for. The database clock stamps `written_at` (trigger, as `workflow.approvals`); a trigger **refuses** an insert whose `session_date` is today or earlier in India and whose `written_at` is at or after 09:00 IST of `session_date` (03:30 UTC). Insert-only for the runtime role (as `lab.test_ledger`); no update, no delete. A night the writer did not run is a `gap` row in `paper.shadow_sessions`; **never** back-filled. *Why:* a candidate written after the open could have seen the outcome. *Rejected:* application-clock checks only (a bug or a clock step could back-date).
**D3. Tamper evidence by chain.** Each entry has `row_hash` = sha256 of its canonical JSON; each session has `digest` = sha256 of (previous digest + the sorted row hashes of the session). A deleted or inserted row after the fact changes every later digest. The weekly report prints the last digest; a verb `xd paper shadow verify` recomputes. *Why:* "no hindsight" must be checkable by the owner, not promised.
**D4. What each row records (for later learning curves and the loop test).** Plan and item ids, trade key (the same key P10 uses, so the two can be joined), group, cell key, strategy version hash, symbol, side, the numeric levels (entry limit, stop, target, time rule), the frozen config fingerprint hash (`ConfigFingerprint`, ADR 0119), the champion model id and its P(win) (`AdvisoryPWin`), the feature vector with the feature version (computed point-in-time by the P6-04 source, UNVERIFIED that the plan builder already holds it), and a flag `counts_for_edge`. A grade is a separate insert-only row (`paper.shadow_grades`: status, r_sim, r basis net or gross, entry and exit instants, exit reason, engine version, sheet id) written by a second service after the session's bars are stored; a pending grade is retried, a graded row is never rewritten.
**D5. A separate "edge" count, with effective n.** `EdgeCount` is its own pure Domain type; the owner-approved, armed, primary, measured trades stay the "behavioural" count of P10 and **the P10 exit criterion D6 is untouched** (a golden test pins P10-06's output). Raw edge n counts graded rows with `counts_for_edge`. Effective n per day uses the design effect `1 + (m - 1) * rho` with `m` the day's candidates and `rho` the average same-day correlation (CF-01 estimates it; placeholder 0.3). Research hand numbers: `m = 5`, `rho = 0.3` gives `1 + 4 x 0.3 = 2.2`; 6 candidates a session gives `1 + 5 x 0.3 = 2.5`, so 240 trades in 40 sessions are `240 / 2.5 = 96` effective, 2.4 effective per session, and 60 effective are reached in `60 / 2.4 = 25` sessions (5 weeks) against 40 sessions (8 weeks) for 60 armed trades at 1.5 a session. The gain is about 1.6 times, not 4 times. Any pass/fail use of the edge count is CF-11's, not this slice's.
**D6. No selection on shadow results of the same epoch.** The rule that chooses what to approve (the owner, the P7-12 auto rule, reserve promotion) must not read shadow outcomes of the current epoch. Enforced by structure: a read port `IShadowResultReader` exists only in the Learning/Reports/Api assemblies' use cases; an Architecture test forbids `Planning`, `Selection` and `Autonomy` namespaces from referencing it, and cell statistics (P6-01) do not ingest shadow grades while an epoch is measured (P10-00 pre-flight keeps cell snapshots frozen in an epoch).
**D7. Switch.** Service `shadow-write` and `shadow-grade` start **paused**; setting `paper.shadow.enabled` default OFF (no row = OFF); turning it ON is an owner act with a fresh password (the P7-12 pattern); no approval is involved so no auto setting is needed beyond this. Epoch restarts (P10-05) start a new `edge` epoch id on the rows; shadow rows are never deleted.

## Goal
Every candidate the nightly plan generates is recorded before the session opens, graded after the close by the same simulator and sheet as paper, and counted separately as edge evidence, so the owner gets roughly 1.6 times faster effective evidence without touching the behavioural count or letting hindsight in.

## Scope
**In:** migration `AddPaperShadow` (`paper.shadow_entries`, `paper.shadow_sessions`, `paper.shadow_grades`, the guard triggers, grants insert-only); Domain `ShadowEntry`, `ShadowDigest`, `EdgeCount`, `ShadowWording`; Application `WriteShadowHandler`, `ShadowWriteService` (id `shadow-write`), `GradeShadowHandler`, `ShadowGradeService` (id `shadow-grade`), `GetShadowSummaryHandler`, ports (`IShadowStore`, `IShadowResultReader`), the verb `xd paper shadow verify|summary`; `GET /api/paper/shadow/summary`; settings; ADR; docs; `gen-api`.
**Out:** the Shadow tab on `/paper` (a small follow-up, lazy tab like P10-02..06); any pass/fail use (CF-09, CF-11); changing P10's criterion or count; recording live bars; features beyond the existing catalogue; real orders (never).

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Every candidate, once.** Given a stored plan with 2 primary, 3 reserve and 1 fill-rejected item and one `merged_duplicate`, when the writer runs before the open, then 6 rows have `counts_for_edge = true`, the duplicate (if the pre-plan record exposes it, UNVERIFIED) is a row with `counts_for_edge = false`, and a second run inserts nothing (unique on session and trade key) (integration).
2. **Before the open.** Session 2026-11-03 (Tuesday), plan stored 2026-11-02 21:10 IST: accepted. An insert stamped 2026-11-03 09:01 IST (03:31 UTC): refused by the database even by a direct insert; at 08:59 IST: accepted; for session 2026-11-02 stamped 2026-11-03: refused (trigger test with a controllable clock).
3. **Forward-only.** The writer given a past session date writes nothing and records a `gap` row; there is no code path that inserts a row for a session already opened (architecture test over the store's public surface).
4. **Insert-only and chained.** The runtime role cannot update or delete the three tables (grants test). Recomputing digests over a stored week matches the stored ones; flipping one byte of one stored row (done as the owner role in the test) makes `verify` fail from that session onward.
5. **Same replay path.** A shadow grade of an armed primary ticket equals the P7-13 grade and the P10 paper R for the same ticket to the last digit (test pins it); no other code builds a `RunSimulationQuery` for a ticket (existing test extended).
6. **P10 untouched.** For a fixed fixture, `PaperPhaseCheck`, `CountedSet.Select` and the P10-04 band produce byte-identical output with and without shadow rows (golden). `Domain/Paper` counting types do not reference `Shadow*` types (architecture test).
7. **No selection on shadow.** Architecture test: no type in `Domain/Selection`, `Domain/Planning`, `Domain/Autonomy` or `Application/Planning` (other than the writer's own read of the plan) references `IShadowResultReader` or any shadow grade type.
8. **Effective n by hand.** Raw 240, 6 candidates a session, rho 0.3: `n_eff = 96.0`; raw 100 with `m = 5`: `n_eff = 100 / 2.2 = 45.45`; `m = 1`: `n_eff = n`; a session with `m = 0` contributes nothing (unit). The summary prints raw n, n_eff, rho used and whether rho is the placeholder.
9. **Pace by hand.** 1.5 armed trades a session gives 60 trades in 40 sessions; 6 shadow candidates a session give 25 sessions to 60 effective trades; the summary computes an ETA from the observed rate once n is at least 10, the way the P10 review does (unit).
10. **Summary honesty.** `GET /api/paper/shadow/summary` returns raw n, n_eff, mean net R with a 90 % interval, hit rate, by group (primary / reserve / rejected), the two counts side by side and the line "modelled fills; no queue, no latency"; with the sheet unconfirmed the figure is gross and labelled (P10-00 D5); with no rows it says so.
11. **Switch.** With `paper.shadow.enabled` OFF (default, no row) the services idle and write nothing; ON needs the owner session, CSRF and a fresh password; the change is an append-only audited row. No log line carries levels, features or a symbol list; Architecture tests green.

## Contract
- **API:** `GET /api/paper/shadow/summary` (owner session) -> `ShadowSummaryView`; `PUT /api/paper/shadow/settings` (owner act, fresh password) for the switch. Problems: 401, 403 step-up, 409 `paper.shadow_gap`. Types in `ApiJsonContext`; `gen-api`.
- **Data (migration `AddPaperShadow`, schema `paper`):** `shadow_entries(id, session_date, plan_id, plan_item_id, trade_key, ticket_group, cell_key, strategy_hash, symbol, side, entry_limit, stop, target, time_rule, config_hash, model_id, p_win, feature_version, features jsonb, counts_for_edge, edge_epoch, written_at, row_hash)` unique `(session_date, trade_key)`; `shadow_sessions(session_date, n_rows, status 'ok'|'gap', digest, prev_digest, written_at)`; `shadow_grades(entry_id unique, status, r_sim, r_basis, entry_at, exit_at, exit_reason, engine_version, sheet_id, graded_at)`. Triggers: DB-clock `written_at`, before-the-open guard, no update/delete. Retention: kept (small rows). Down drops the tables.
- **UI:** none in this slice (summary through the API and the verb); follow-up tab Shadow on `/paper`, lazy, read-only.
- **Jobs:** `shadow-write` after the nightly plan and before the review cutoff (the evening); `shadow-grade` after the `eod` run stores the session's bars (like paper capture). Idempotent by `(session_date, trade_key)`; a failure leaves the plan and P10 untouched and logs one event.

## Non-functional
A plan has tens of items; rows are about a kilobyte; the grade pass replays each ticket once (as paper capture does, about a plan's tickets per night). Writer p95 under 2 s; grade pass bounded by the replayer. Security: no secrets, no strategy text in logs; features are numbers. Observability: one log event per run with counts only; the Services screen shows the two services (P1-13).

## Test plan
Unit (Domain): `EdgeCount`, digest chain, wording. Unit (Application): writer with fake plan and store (groups, duplicates, gaps, idempotence), grader reuse. Integration (Postgres): the three guard triggers, grants, uniqueness, chain verify, the golden P10 output, the summary endpoint, the switch. Architecture: the two reference rules above. Frontend: none in this slice.

## Risks
Same-day candidates are correlated, so n_eff is about 40 % of n at rho 0.3; the speed-up is about 1.6 times and the 12-week calendar for the behavioural count still applies. A pre-plan candidate list that is not persisted limits what can be recorded. A wrong feature version stored would corrupt later learning curves: the version is part of the row.

## UNVERIFIED
Whether duplicates and not-eligible candidates are persisted before the plan record. Whether the plan builder holds the feature vector at plan time. The exact group names and reason codes of `Rejected` items beyond the lines read. That `TicketReplayer` is reachable from a new service without widening its `internal` surface (assumed through the Application assembly). rho 0.3 is a placeholder until CF-01.
