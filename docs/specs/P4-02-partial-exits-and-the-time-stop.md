## Goal
The simulator runs a strategy whose exit is `PARTIAL_BE` (book part at T1, stop to break-even, the rest at T2) or `PARTIAL_TRAIL` (book part at T1, trail the rest by the ATR), with or without the `time_stop` overlay, and gives trades with legs whose R, costs, MAE and MFE follow P4-01. This is the first slice in which a real policy uses the legs and, with `PARTIAL_TRAIL`, the first that reads the tape. It runs **one** exit set at a time; comparing candidates is P4-05.

## Scope
**In:**
1. **`PARTIAL_BE`** (D1 to D3), **`PARTIAL_TRAIL`** (D4 to D6) and **`time_stop`** (D7 to D9) as `IExitRule`s of the P4-01 seam, for intraday and swing walks, buy and sell.
2. **What the simulator accepts** (D10): `Simulator.Create` runs a side whose `exits` is **exactly one candidate** of `FIXED_RR`, `PARTIAL_BE` or `PARTIAL_TRAIL` with **one value in every list**, and at most the `time_stop` overlay; `ExitTables.RunBySimulator` and the warning `exit_not_evaluated` follow.
3. **One new exit reason**, `TimeStop` (D8), with its counter, card text, export and store value.
4. **The oracle** extended for the three rules, written from the rule tables here and in ADR 0049 only (D12), and the leak property for each.
5. **Tests, ADR 0049, the docs** (Done when).

**Out (not in this slice):** `ATR_TRAIL`, `STRUCTURE_TRAIL`, `EMA_TRAIL` (P4-03), `PYRAMID` (P4-04), `EVENT_EXIT` (P4-07); more than one candidate or a list with more than one value in a run (P4-05); a change of the tables of P3-02b2 other than the confirmations in D13; the stop kinds, the entry rules, the fill rules or the charge sheet; per-trade tuning (P7).

**Size.** About **2 agent-days** (P4-00 estimate; not calibrated). Itemised, UNVERIFIED: `PARTIAL_BE` 0.5, `PARTIAL_TRAIL` with the tape read 0.5, `time_stop` for both styles 0.4, `Simulator.Create`, the reason, the counters, the export, the store and the cards 0.2, oracle and properties 0.3, ADR and docs 0.1. If it overruns, cut in this order: the swing form of `time_stop` (D9; the intraday form stays), the cards' extra text, never the oracle or the leak property.

## Definitions
Notation: `R0 = |entry − initial stop|` of the exact entry (P4-01 D4); "buy" is written, a sell mirrors every comparison and every sign. `book` is `book_pct / 100`. A **level** is an exact decimal as in ADR 0038; the tick grid applies to a filled price only. "Minute" is a 1-minute bar that holds a trade (volume above 0); "bar *k*" and the tape are P4-01's.

**D1. `PARTIAL_BE` (doc 08: "Book x% at T1, stop to breakeven, rest at T2").** Parameters as P3-02b2 D3: `book_pct` in (0, 100), `t1_rr` in (0, 10], `t2_rr` above `t1_rr` (one value in this slice). Three orders:
- **T1:** a resting limit to sell `book` of the position at `entry + t1_rr × R0`, live from the fill minute (the entry-at-limit fill minute follows X5: only the stop is checked in it, as for the target of `FIXED_RR`).
- **Stop:** the initial stop for the whole position until T1 fills.
- **T2:** a resting limit for the rest at `entry + t2_rr × R0`, **placed in the first minute after the minute in which T1 filled**, together with the break-even stop (D2).
T1 and T2 fill like a target in ADR 0038 (X3: at the level and never at a better price, even when the minute opens beyond it); a resting limit does not slip.

**D2. Break-even and the order inside the T1 minute (recommended).** The break-even stop is the **exact entry price** (no cost offset, no tick offset: a stop at the entry exits at `RExact = 0`, `RFilled` slightly negative by slippage and costs; the walker's rule, not a parameter, as P3-02b2 D3 said). It and T2 take effect from the first minute **after** the minute that filled T1, because inside that minute the order of the high and the low is unknown (ADR 0038, minute path): the minute that fills T1 is therefore never also a break-even stop or a T2 fill. A minute that touches the initial stop and T1 follows the existing table: the stop first, for the whole position, unless the open is already beyond T1. After T1 the position is `1 − book` of the original; a stop exit at break-even is a gap-aware stop (filled at the level or the open if the minute gapped through it; market-type, pays slippage); T2 is a resting limit (no slip).

**D3. Where `PARTIAL_BE` can end.** At T2; at break-even; at the square-off or `MAX_HOLD` as today (on whatever share is open); by the `time_stop` overlay (D7); with the initial stop before T1; at the end of the data. Worked example (hand row 1, buy, exact fills, no slippage): entry 100.00, stop 99.00 (`R0 = 1.00`), `book_pct` 50, `t1_rr` 1.0, `t2_rr` 2.0. T1 fills at 101.00 (+0.5R on half), then the stop is 100.00. (a) T2 reached at 102.00: +0.5 + 0.5 × 2.0 = **+1.5R**. (b) price returns to 100.00: +0.5 + 0 = **+0.5R**. (c) the stop 99.00 before T1: **−1.0R**. (d) square-off at 100.60 after T1: +0.5 + 0.5 × 0.6 = **+0.8R**.

**D4. `PARTIAL_TRAIL` (doc 08: "Book x% at T1, trail remainder").** Parameters as P3-02b2 D10: `book_pct` in (0, 100), `t1_rr` in (0, 10], `trail_atr_mult` in (0, 20], optional whole `atr_period` (14). T1 is as D1. The remainder has the initial stop until the first trail level (there is **no** break-even move; recommended, because doc 08 says "trail remainder" and break-even is the other policy's name; question 2).

**D5. The trail (recommended; reads the tape).** After T1 has filled, at every closed E-bar *k* whose close is **after** the T1 fill minute, with `ATR_k` the tape value of `atr(atr_period)` as of the close of bar *k*:
`level_k = H_k − trail_atr_mult × ATR_k`, where `H_k` is the highest high of the minutes from the T1 fill minute through the end of bar *k* (a sell: the lowest low, plus). If `ATR_k` is not a number the bar gives no level (P4-01 D1). The level takes effect from the first minute after the close of bar *k* (P4-01 D2), only if it is **tighter** than the stop in force (P4-01 D6: a looser level is ignored and counted), and the stop is then a gap-aware stop as in D2. This is the chandelier of doc 08 with the anchor "since T1" and the activation "T1 filled"; P4-03 defines `ATR_TRAIL` with its own activation and anchor and the two do not share code beyond the minute table (UNVERIFIED: whether they should; see question 3).

**D6. Where `PARTIAL_TRAIL` can end.** At the trailed stop; at the square-off or `MAX_HOLD` as today; by the overlay; with the initial stop before T1 (a minute that touches the stop and T1: the stop, for the whole). There is no T2. Worked example (hand row 2, buy, exact fills, `ATR` 0.50 at every bar, `trail_atr_mult` 2.0): entry 100.00, stop 99.00, `book_pct` 50, `t1_rr` 1.0. T1 fills at 101.00 in the 10:20 bar; at the close of the next bar the highest high since T1 is 102.40, so `level = 102.40 − 2.0 × 0.50 = 101.40`, tighter than 99.00, effective from the next minute. The price falls to 101.40: remainder exits there, **+0.5 + 0.5 × 1.4 = +1.2R**.

**D7. `time_stop` overlay (doc 08 §5; P3-02b2 D8).** Parameters: `min_progress_rr` in [0, 10]; `intraday_bars` (intraday strategies only) or `swing_sessions` (swing only, below `max_hold_sessions`). It applies to whatever share is open and to every candidate. **Progress** at a check is `(close − entry) / R0` for a buy, with `close` the close of the E-bar (intraday: the tape's `Close`) or of the last minute of the session (swing); a check that finds progress strictly below `min_progress_rr` exits the open share (D8); at or above, the overlay does nothing for the rest of the trade.

**D8. The intraday check and the exit.** The check is at the close of the **N-th E-bar that closes at or after the fill minute**, N = `intraday_bars`, counted in the entry's session only (N = 1 is the bar in which the fill happened if it closes at or after the fill minute, which every bar containing the fill minute does; hence N = 1 checks at the end of the fill bar: question 4). The open share leaves at the **open of the first minute after the close of that bar** that holds a trade, as a market-type exit (pays slippage), reason `TimeStop`. A check bar that closes at or after the square-off minute makes no check (the square-off exits first). The stop and target in force stay live until that minute. `ExitReason.TimeStop` is a new member (stored by name; additive, no schema change: question 5); it counts in the run record as `ExitsTimeStop`.

**D9. The swing check.** Counted in sessions of the trading calendar from the fill session as session 1 (the count of `MAX_HOLD`, ADR 0040 D1; a special session counts, a holiday does not). At the close of the last minute that holds a trade of session `swing_sessions`, if progress (against the close of that minute) is strictly below `min_progress_rr`, the open share leaves **at that close**, market-type with slippage, reason `TimeStop`; otherwise the overlay is spent. Because `swing_sessions < max_hold_sessions` (P3-02b2 D8), this never collides with `MAX_HOLD`. If that session held no minute with a trade, the check is made at the last close of the last earlier session that held one, counted like `MaxHoldAtLastClose` (`TimeStopAtLastClose`, a counter; UNVERIFIED that ADR 0040 X8's wording carries over as written).

**D10. What `Simulator.Create` accepts.** A side whose `exits` has exactly one entry, `FIXED_RR`, `PARTIAL_BE` or `PARTIAL_TRAIL`, with one value in each list parameter, and whose `overlays` has at most `time_stop`. Anything else (two candidates, a list of two values, `event_exit`, the policies of P4-03 and P4-04) remains `simulation.unsupported_exit`, one problem at `buy.exits` / `buy.overlays[i]`, now naming the policy and, for a list, the parameter. `ExitTables.RunBySimulator` becomes `{FIXED_RR, PARTIAL_BE, PARTIAL_TRAIL}` and a new `OverlaysRunBySimulator` is `{time_stop}`; the model-time warning `exit_not_evaluated` is no longer given for them; a test asserts that the model's and the simulator's lists agree (the test of P3-02b2 criterion 13, updated).

**D11. Legs, R and costs.** All of P4-01 D7 to D12: T1 is an exit leg of share `book`, the rest one or more later legs; `R` in units of `R0`; MAE and MFE against the entry; charges per leg, the quantity of the T1 leg `floor(Quantity × book)` and the remainder to the last leg; a T1 leg whose quantity would be 0 is merged and counted (`LegsMerged`), so a tiny position is a trade without a partial; the position gate holds to the last leg. `StopPrice` and `TargetPrice` of the `SimTrade` are the initial stop and **T1** for the partial policies (the plan the card shows; T2 and the trail are in the legs and the rule text).

**D12. The oracle.** The reference implementation of P3-08 gets the three rules written from D1 to D9 and ADR 0049 only, not from the engine; the agreement is on every field of every trade, over at least 18,500 seeded series per rule family that include the cases: T1 and the initial stop in one minute; T1 filled and break-even in adjacent minutes; a gap through the break-even stop; a trail level that is looser (ignored); a NaN ATR; an overlay check on the square-off bar; a swing time stop on a session without a trade; a sell; a position of one share (merge of the T1 leg). The disagreement log gets a section for P4-02. **The leak property (P4-00 D9)** is run for each rule: truncating the data after bar *k* changes nothing at or before the first minute after *k*; a negative control that uses the value of bar *k* inside bar *k* must fail it.

**D13. Confirmations of the provisional tables.** The names `book_pct`, `t1_rr`, `t2_rr`, `trail_atr_mult`, `atr_period`, `min_progress_rr`, `intraday_bars`, `swing_sessions` stand as P3-02b2 wrote them (no rename; question 6). The accepted-but-provisional status of those three tables ends with this slice (ADR 0036 Amendment 2 says so); the other four stay provisional until P4-03/P4-04.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Old runs unchanged.** Every `FIXED_RR` run, golden and oracle case of P4-01 is unchanged byte for byte; adding the three rules changes nothing for a strategy that does not use them.
2. **`PARTIAL_BE` hand rows.** A table of at least 20 hand-computed rows (buy and sell; intraday and swing; T2 reached, break-even, initial stop, square-off after T1, `MAX_HOLD` after T1, end of data, gap through the break-even stop, T1 and the initial stop in one minute, T1 at the open of a gap, a T1 leg merged for a one-share position) gives the legs, `RExact`, `RFilled`, `SlippageR`, `MaeR` and `MfeR` to the printed precision; the worked rows (a) to (d) of D3 are in it.
3. **The break-even order (D2).** In a seeded series, the minute that fills T1 never also fills T2 or the break-even stop, and T2 and the break-even stop are first live in the next minute; a property over the oracle seeds.
4. **`PARTIAL_TRAIL` hand rows.** At least 20 hand rows with a tape given by hand (a NaN ATR, a looser level, an ATR that grows, a bar that closes on the first minute after T1, a trail across a session break in a swing walk, a sell); the worked row of D6 is in it. The trail never loosens (counter `StopLoosenIgnored` counts the ignored levels).
5. **`time_stop` hand rows.** At least 16 rows (intraday N = 1, N in the middle, N past the square-off; progress below, equal to and above the minimum; swing with and without a minute on the session; with and without a partial before it; a sell).
6. **Look-ahead.** The leak property of D12 holds for each rule over 10,000 seeds, and the negative control fails.
7. **Refusals (D10).** Two candidates, a list of two values, `event_exit`, `ATR_TRAIL`, `STRUCTURE_TRAIL`, `EMA_TRAIL` and `PYRAMID` are refused by name at the right path; `FIXED_RR`, `PARTIAL_BE`, `PARTIAL_TRAIL` and `time_stop` are not; the lists of the model and the simulator agree.
8. **The new reason.** `TimeStop` appears in the run record's counters, in the export (CSV and JSON), in the store round trip, on the cards (reason text and the leg marks of P4-01 D16) and nowhere in a file name or the index (the blindness scans of P3-04c and P3-09 pass).
9. **Costs.** For the hand rows of criteria 2 and 4 with a TEST charge sheet, the charges per leg, `TotalCost`, `CostR` and `RNet` equal the hand values to the paisa (P4-01 AC 8 with real legs).
10. **The oracle** agrees on every field of every trade over the seeds of D12; its log has the P4-02 section with the dry-read entries.
11. **The verbs.** `simulate --strategy` and `verify-pack` run a strategy with each policy over a seeded fixture end to end (Testcontainers as in P3-04b2 and P3-09); the banner is unchanged; a run of a built-in strategy with an exit changed to `PARTIAL_BE` says in its record which policy ran (the definition's text in the record, as today).
12. **Performance.** The `FIXED_RR` path stays within the 5 % of P4-01 AC 14; the partial rules allocate nothing per minute beyond the legs of a trade (explicit benchmark).
13. `scripts/check.sh` green; a `reviewer` pass with no blocker or major; ADR 0049 and the doc updates (08 §2 as built, §5; 16 `exits`; 18-roadmap; the oracle log) done and `scripts/build-docs.sh` run.

## Contract
- **API:** none.
- **Data:** `ExitReason.TimeStop` (new member, stored by name); counters `ExitsTimeStop`, `TimeStopAtLastClose`, reused `LegsTotal`, `LegsMerged`, `StopLoosenIgnored`; no migration if the store keeps the reason as text (UNVERIFIED: if `research.sim_trades.exit_reason` is a constrained text or an enum, the migration `AddTimeStopExitReason` is added; the build checks).
- **UI:** none; cards as in P4-01 D16.
- **Events/jobs:** none.

## Non-functional
As P4-01 (allocation-free `FIXED_RR`, deterministic bytes). The tape is built only when a strategy has `PARTIAL_TRAIL` (it names `atr`); `PARTIAL_BE` and `time_stop` read no feature except the E-bar `Close` for the intraday overlay (so an overlay on a `PARTIAL_BE` strategy makes the tape hold `Close` only). No secret, no network, no new log event id.

## Test plan
Unit: each rule's rows as hand tables, the order inside the T1 minute, the trail's anchor and tightening, the overlay's counting of bars and sessions, refusals. Property: leak, break-even order, trail never loosens, legs invariants (P4-01 AC 6) on the new rules. Oracle: D12. Integration: verbs, export, store, cards. Edge cases listed in D12 and criteria 2, 4 and 5; additionally a T1 with `book_pct` rounding a leg to 0 shares; a strategy whose `atr_period` differs from another strategy's (separate tapes); a swing position with a partial leg overnight; a session with a special close.

## Open questions
1. **One candidate at a time (D10).** Accept that this slice runs exactly one exit set and refuses lists of more than one value until P4-05? Recommended: yes.
2. **`PARTIAL_TRAIL` has no break-even move (D4).** After T1 the remainder keeps the initial stop until the trail is tighter. Recommended: yes (doc 08 says "trail remainder"); the alternative is a break-even stop at T1 as in `PARTIAL_BE`.
3. **The trail's anchor and activation (D5):** highest high since the T1 fill, activation by T1. Recommended: yes, and let P4-03 decide separately for `ATR_TRAIL` (it has its own `activate_at_rr`); the two may end up sharing a helper but not a rule.
4. **Counting bars of the time stop (D8):** the N-th bar closing at or after the fill minute, so N = 1 checks at the end of the fill bar. Recommended: yes; the alternative, counting from the first bar that begins after the fill, is one bar later for every N.
5. **A new `ExitReason.TimeStop` without a schema bump (D8).** Recommended: yes (additive member, stored by name).
6. **Names (D13).** Keep the P3-02b2 names for the three tables and end their provisional status. Recommended: yes.
7. **Engine version.** The existing requests give unchanged results, so keep `sim-6` and record the new capability in ADR 0049, rather than `sim-7`. Recommended: keep `sim-6` (UNVERIFIED that the version policy of ADR 0037 agrees; the build checks it).
8. **Hand check.** The partial and overlay rules are checked by hand like P3's, but later, once, for P4's policies together (P4-00 question 4), not per slice. Recommended: yes.
