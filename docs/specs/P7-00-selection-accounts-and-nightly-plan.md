## Goal
Every trading evening the system turns the owner's baskets, the stored strategies and what P6 learned into a **proposed plan** for the next session (primary tickets, a reserve list, the rejected candidates with reasons), sized per Trading-role account, with a pre-trade checklist on every ticket. The owner reviews it before the review cutoff; what is unreviewed lapses; what is approved is armed at the pre-open check and joins the live active set (prices and alerts only). Everything is graded afterwards. Like P3 to P6, P7 first proves the machinery and its honesty rules; what the plan can *conclude* is bounded by about 2.1 years of data, replayed history, modelled fills and advisory models. **P7 places no order of any kind** (section "What P7 may and may not do").

## What P7 may and may not do (doc 19, exactly)
- Doc 19 §5: "v1 places no orders; Phase 12 caps at 2 orders/s." Doc 04 §1: the Trading role has "auto-journal of fills; **later** order execution". Doc 16: `execution: { enabled: false, ... }` ("disabled until their phase", P12). Doc 01: "Execution: v1 manual (system auto-journals your fills); assisted, semi-auto, auto in a later phase." Static IP is "not needed until Phase 12" (Q6). Doc 04 §4.1 lists "enabling execution" under "Only you".
- **P7 may:** compute and store a plan; show it; record the owner's decisions; **arm** a ticket, which means a state change in our own database plus adding the instrument to the live **active set** (quotes, trigger/stop/target alerts, a notification); read the owner's Trading accounts' funds, positions and trade history through the **existing read-only** connector (account sync, P2); journal what the broker reports; grade the plan on stored bars; start recording market depth (if the owner allows the endpoint, Q9).
- **P7 may not:** place, modify or cancel an order at any broker; add an order endpoint to the Dhan allow-list; hold an order-capable credential or scope; present "armed" as "an order is resting at the broker"; add a one-click "send to broker". Armed means "watched"; the owner enters any order by hand in Dhan's own app (mode `manual_live`) or nothing is entered (mode `paper`). A **new architecture test** (P7-03) fails the build if any port, handler or type in Application, Domain or Infrastructure declares a member whose name denotes placing, modifying or cancelling an order (`PlaceOrder`, `SubmitOrder`, `ModifyOrder`, `CancelOrder`, in any casing); verified 2026-10-23 that no such name exists in `backend/src` today.
- Dhan broker-side stops "once execution exists" (doc 19 §4) are P12; until then a stop is a level on the ticket and an alert, not a protection. The plan says so on every ticket.

## The seam (the decisions the slices share)
**Fact.** P7 sits on three stored things: the **cells** (what worked, P6), the **bars of the last close** (EOD, P1/P2) and the **owner's accounts and baskets**. P3 to P6 were all replays over history; P7 is the first time the system speaks about tomorrow, so look-ahead is the new hazard: a ticket for day D+1 may use only data up to the close of D.

**D1. Planning is a pure core behind ports, in the shape P6 used.** Pure pieces in `Domain/Planning` (filters, merge, rank, portfolio fill, allocation, sizing, checklist, smart rules, the ticket and plan state machines, grading); orchestration in `Application/Planning` (one internal sealed handler per use case, explicit registration); I/O behind ports in `Application/Abstractions/Planning` (`IPlanStore`, `ICandidateSource`, `IApprovalLog`, `IJournalMatcher`, `IPlanClock` is not needed: `TimeProvider`); persistence in `Infrastructure`. Doc 03 names a separate `XDrishti.Planning` project; P6 shows the folder-per-area pattern passes `Architecture.Tests` without it. Recommended: follow P6, record it in the first P7 ADR (0082, the next free number: the ADR index ends at 0081).

**D2. A candidate is a ticket-to-be built from the last close, not a replay trade.** The nightly stage "scan" takes, per instrument of the `scan_scope` baskets and per **stored strategy version whose cell is eligible**, the point-in-time frame **as of the last closed session**, evaluates the strategy's `setup` with Kleene logic, and classifies each part: known at the close (true / false) or knowable only intraday (unknown). `setup` true or unknown with nothing false yields a **conditional ticket**: instrument, side, strategy version (hash), the remaining intraday conditions as text for the live check, validity window (the learned entry window if one is accepted, else `time_rules`), the stop rule and level as of the close (levels that depend on the entry price are stated as rules with the formula), the target by the cell's chosen exit, risk-based size per account, P(win) and conservative expectancy from the cell statistics. This reuses the compiler, the frame and the stored strategies; it adds the "tomorrow" evaluation and a ticket record. It is the largest unknown of the phase (P7-04). It never re-runs a replay to produce a number: every number on a ticket is read from stored cell statistics and labelled with its data range.

**D3. The number on a ticket comes from cell statistics; models and the meta policy are advisory.** P6 ends with no stored champion on the owner's data and ADR 0075 "no sizing multiplier is fitted". So P7 ranks and filters on the **credible lower bound of cell expectancy** (doc 10 §2 "conservative"), and shows the champion's calibrated `p_win`, its top contributors and the meta-policy verdict **beside** it, labelled advisory, when a champion exists. The meta size multiplier 0.5 to 1.5 and the "score below `min_take` goes to reserve" rule are **not applied** in P7 (open question 8). This keeps P7 from acting on a number the evidence does not yet support.

**D4. Nothing is dropped silently.** Every filter, rule and limit that removes or demotes a candidate writes a reason code on it; rejected and reserve candidates stay in the plan as rows and are graded (doc 10 §8). A filter whose data is missing (events, F&O ban, surveillance, breadth, context) records `unavailable`, shows it on the ticket and in the checklist, and never counts as a pass.

**D5. A plan is a record with an append-only decision trail.** `trading.plans` and `plan_items` hold what the pipeline proposed (immutable once published; a re-run for the same date and the same inputs is idempotent by a plan key, a different input is a new version, never an edit); `workflow.approvals` holds each decision as a new row (actor, reason, modifications, cutoff, auto-approved flag). The ticket state at a moment is the fold of the trail (the same idea as ADR 0073). `xd_app` has insert-only on the record tables (grants test). Modifications are re-validated against the limits before they are accepted.

**D6. Plan prices are modelled; grading is on stored bars until fills exist.** Until the owner's real fills exist (paper P10, live P11) a ticket is graded by walking it through the next sessions' stored 1-minute bars with the same fill rules as the simulator (ADR 0038) and the stored charge sheet, so the grade is "what the plan would have returned under the P3 rules", not what happened to any real order. Every grade says `basis: simulated on plan prices`. When the auto-journal links a real fill (P7-14), the real fill is stored beside it and `basis: fills` is used only for those trades (doc 09 §9); `keep_plan_r` keeps both.

**D7. Fail closed.** The pipeline publishes a plan only if every gate passes: the trading calendar says D is a session and D+1 is the next one; the EOD run for D finished and the data-quality gate passed for the instruments scanned (an instrument that fails is skipped and flagged, per doc 15 §5; **more than a stated share failing, or the index context older than a stated age, aborts the plan**); the account sync ran; the cell statistics snapshot is current (`as_of` is D); the strategy versions used are the ones stored. A failed gate writes a `plan run` row with the reason, raises a notification, publishes **no** plan (never a partial or stale one) and the previous day's plan is not extended. Doc 19 §4: "Bad data → wrong plan: quality gates; fail closed".

**D8. The nightly run is a registered service, not Hangfire.** Docs 03/04/16 say "Hangfire recurring job"; roadmap and doc 03 §3 already record that Hangfire was planned and is not used, and the framework in `xd-api` (`ServiceScheduler`) runs `eod` today. The `nightly`, `preopen`, `suggestions` and weekly services register as `ServiceDefinition`s with the run-after rules of doc 04 §3.1 (nightly after `eod` and the portfolio sync; pre-open after the cutoff and before 09:15, warning before about 09:08). Long work uses the table-as-queue runner pattern of the Lab (ADR 0058). Document the departure from "Hangfire" in the first ADR.

**D9. Arming is a database state and a feed subscription, nothing else.** `Armed` adds the instrument to the active set through a new `IActiveSetProvider` (reason `ArmedTicket`, mode quote, ranked by ticket order when the cap cuts) and raises `NOTIFY active_set_changed`; trigger/stop/target alerts are evaluated by the existing live flush (ADR 0022/0024 pattern), and a notification says "triggered; place it yourself in Dhan or ignore". Nothing in the system can turn a trigger into an order.

## Acceptance criteria (of the phase; each slice carries its own list)
1. **Plans published 10 consecutive sessions unattended (roadmap exit).** For 10 consecutive NSE trading sessions, per the trading calendar, the `nightly` service publishes a proposed plan (or the pipeline fails closed with a visible reason on a bad-data day, which the phase check reports separately and which **breaks the run of consecutive sessions**, Q10) with no manual run, no code change and no owner action on the pipeline. Evidence: `xd plan phase-check` reading `plan_runs`, with the ten dates, the scheduled and finish times, the gate results and the count of manual runs (must be 0).
2. **Fail closed.** Every gate of D7 has a test showing: gate fails, no plan row is written, one run row carries the reason, one notification is raised, yesterday's plan is not extended. A stale cell snapshot, an EOD run that finished partially, a quality-gate failure above the share, and a context older than its age each abort.
3. **No look-ahead.** A truncation property: the tickets built for D+1 are identical when all data after the close of D is removed; a ticket's evidence (cell stats) has `as_of` not after D; grading of D+1 uses only sessions after D.
4. **No order, ever.** The new architecture rule passes and was seen to fail on a planted member named `PlaceOrder`; the Dhan allow-list is unchanged except by an owner-approved item (Q9); no handler calls a broker write endpoint.
5. **Selection is explainable and deterministic.** The same inputs give the same plan (same ordering, same reasons, same plan key); every candidate that is not primary has a reason code; hand tables exist for each filter, the merge, the rank and each portfolio constraint.
6. **Honest evidence on the plan.** Each ticket carries, as data, the cell, the number of trades, the data range ("about 2.1 years, replayed history, modelled fills, no hold-out"), the P(win) basis (cell statistics lower bound; the champion's value beside it when one exists, labelled advisory) and any `unavailable` input. A plan built while the champion is absent says "cell statistics only".
7. **Human in the loop.** Unreviewed tickets lapse at the cutoff; only an Approved ticket is armed; a failed check needs an explicit confirmation reason recorded; every decision is an insert-only row; a modification that breaks a limit is refused. Auto-approval does not exist until the owner decides (Q3, Q4).
8. **Accounts.** Allocation goes only to accounts with the Trading role; a Data-only or Portfolio-only account never receives a ticket; per-account risk profile, overrides and capital are applied; the sum of risk across accounts is within `max_total_risk_pct_all_accounts`; a `manual_live` account receives nothing until the owner allows it (Q2).
9. **Grading.** Primary, reserve and rejected tickets of a day are graded once, insert-only, labelled `simulated on plan prices`; the top-1/3/5/all curves and the decision groups are reproduced by hand on a fixture.
10. **The active set.** An armed ticket's instrument is in the feed's set within the feed's recompute interval (the existing path, `NOTIFY` plus the one-minute safety net), within the cap, and leaves it when the ticket expires, is invalidated or closes.
11. **Auto-journal.** A fixture day with fills matches tickets by account, instrument, side and day, tags unmatched trades discretionary, never double-counts a fill, and the grade on fills is stored beside the plan grade.
12. Every slice ends with `scripts/check.sh` green, `scripts/gen-api.sh` for API changes, a `reviewer` pass, an ADR where it decides, and `scripts/build-docs.sh`.

## Honest limits (read this before any P7 number)
- **No live-feed history.** The system keeps only EOD data and the live quotes of the active set; there is no recorded intraday tick or depth history (the depth recorder starts in P7 and by design needs six months before any feature use, P13). Anything the plan says about opening gaps, pre-open prices or intraday behaviour is from the 1-minute bars of the owner's CSVs and the Dhan EOD fetch, with fills **modelled** (ISS-18).
- **About 2.1 years of data, no hold-out** (ISS-19): files from 2024-04-01 to 2024-12-18 to 2026-04-30. Cell statistics and the rules that filter the plan were all shaped on the same data; with quarterly folds there are about 5 or 6 out-of-sample windows (P6-00). A ticket's P(win) is a shrunk replayed-history estimate, not a forecast.
- **The models are advisory and may not exist.** On the owner's data no champion is stored (ADR 0080); the meta policy "does not help" is an ordinary outcome (ADR 0075). P7 does not size or rank on a model.
- **No cell can be Active before P10.** Lifecycle Paper/Active are inert until paper days exist (ADR 0073). A P7 plan therefore ranges over cells that passed the gates (Candidate) and is **provisional**; this is stated on the plan (Q7).
- **Plan prices are not fill prices.** The grade is on simulated fills, so the first year of "graded trades" are re-simulations, not execution. Real slippage, partial fills and missed triggers appear only with fills (P10/P11).
- **Missing inputs.** No breadth or VIX context until P3-01d/e; no events, results, F&O ban or surveillance data; survivorship as of today (ISS-22); costs net only if the stored charge sheet is the owner's (ISS-25, UNVERIFIED). Each is `unavailable` on the ticket, never a pass.
- **"Unattended" depends on Dhan's login.** The Data account's token is created by an interactive login with 2FA and lasts about a day; if it lapsed, the EOD fetch fails, the pipeline fails closed and that day does not count. Whether 10 sessions in a row is reachable without the owner reconnecting is not known today.
- **Ten sessions is a small sample of the pipeline's health**, and says nothing about whether the plans make money; that is P10's exit (60 graded trades, positive expectancy after costs).

## Risks
1. **The empty plan.** With no Active cell (limits above) and conservative filters, most days may yield no ticket, and the 10-session exit would be met by empty plans. Mitigation: Q7 sets the minimum cell state; the phase check reports plans with zero primary tickets separately; the owner decides whether "published" requires at least one ticket (Q10).
2. **The ticket builder (P7-04) is the largest unknown.** Evaluating a setup "as of the last close" with Kleene logic, and translating a strategy's stop/target rules into levels for tomorrow, may need engine changes (a frame API for the day boundary, a stop level not yet known until entry). Mitigation: the slice has a spike-first step (0.5 d) that reports what the compiler and frame already give; strategies that cannot be classified are `unavailable` and excluded, and the plan lists them.
3. **Look-ahead in a new place.** The first "tomorrow" code. Mitigation: criterion 3, a truncation property in the first slice that reads bars, and the cell snapshots' `as_of < date` rule from P6.
4. **Auto-approval and autonomy over-reach** (doc 19 §4). The design default (Supervised) auto-approves at the cutoff. Mitigation: SAFETY stop at P7-12, stored default Advisory, password to raise, auto-approved tickets marked, no order exists to be approved into.
5. **"Armed" read as "protected".** A stop is only an alert before P12; the owner could believe a resting order exists. Mitigation: wording on ticket, notification and screen (a test pins the string), and the architecture rule.
6. **Reserve promotion arms an unreviewed ticket.** Mitigation: Q5, off until decided.
7. **Wiring a real account.** A `manual_live` Trading account getting tickets is real money once the owner trades them. Mitigation: Q2; the allocator takes the account set as an input; paper accounts first.
8. **Scale of the phase.** About 58 agent-days against 3 weeks; the cut line protects the exit.
9. **Calendar and clock edge cases:** half-day or special sessions, a holiday between D and D+1, catch-up after the machine slept through 18:30 or 09:10, DST-free but date-boundary bugs between UTC and IST. Mitigation: `TimeProvider` and the trading calendar in every gate; tests for a Monday plan (Friday close), a holiday gap and a catch-up after the pre-open time.
10. **Data account token lapse breaks the run of 10** (limits above); the exit may need more than 10 calendar sessions to reach 10 consecutive ones.
11. **Config home missing** (no `config_versions`): P7 would invent a second settings store. Mitigation: Q14, and reuse `platform.app_settings` (JSON, fixed ranges, audited; used by the Action center in P2-09) unless the owner prefers the versioned design of ADR 0002.
12. **Over-claiming later.** P8 and P9 consume P7 outputs; the honest-limits strings are a field of the data, not page prose (as P6 risk 7).
