## Goal
The Lab verdict and report show, beside today's multiple-testing factor and Monte Carlo interval: (a) the probability that the best variant's Sharpe ratio beats what luck alone produces among the variants tried (deflated Sharpe), (b) an interval for mean R that respects same-day and serial clustering (stationary block bootstrap), and (c) for sweeps, the probability that the in-sample winner is below the median out of sample (PBO).

## Decisions (researched)
1. **Deflated Sharpe next to the heuristic, never replacing it until validated.** `DSR = Phi( (SR - SR0) sqrt(T - 1) / sqrt(1 - skew SR + (kurt - 1)/4 SR²) )` with `SR0 = sigma_SR [ (1 - g) Phi^-1(1 - 1/N) + g Phi^-1(1 - 1/(N e)) ]`, `g = 0.5772` (Euler-Mascheroni), N the trial count, `sigma_SR` the standard deviation of the trial Sharpe ratios, all in **per-trade units**, `T` = **effective** trades (CF-01). Reason: the heuristic `1 + 0.25 log2(N)` has no calibration; DSR is the published deflation. Rejected: switching the gates to DSR directly; annualised units (a per-trade SR has no period ambiguity; the research note's worked example is annual and is reproduced below for continuity). Source: Bailey and López de Prado, "The Deflated Sharpe Ratio", Journal of Portfolio Management 2014 (https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551; formula constants hand-checked here, paper abstract only: UNVERIFIED against the full text).
2. **N is the ledger count of the family; `sigma_SR` comes from stored per-variant statistics.** `lab.test_ledger` holds run identity but no results (columns read from the P5-03 contract: run id, run key, strategy hash, parent hash, version, universe, window, method, created at). So a new insert-only table `lab.variant_stats` stores, for every run that ends in a result, `(run_id, strategy_hash, n, mean_r, sd_r, skew, kurt, sr_per_trade, method)`. For families whose earlier variants have no stored stats, `sigma_SR` is `unavailable` and DSR reads `unavailable: trial Sharpe ratios not stored` unless the owner sets `Confidence:Deflation:AssumedTrialSrSd` (default unset). Rejected: guessing sigma_SR; recomputing old variants (their trades are in `research.sim_runs` only for the full and quick runs, sweep points are not stored there: P5-05 assumption 4). Source: repo specs P5-03, P5-05.
3. **N counts correlated variants, so DSR is conservative.** The ledger counts every sweep point and exit candidate; neighbours of one strategy are highly correlated, so the effective number of independent trials is smaller. The paper clusters trials; v1 does not (the report says "N counts neighbouring variants as independent: the bar is higher than strictly needed"). Rejected: clustering trials in v1 (an algorithm of its own). Source: Bailey and López de Prado 2014 section on the effective number of trials (not opened: UNVERIFIED).
4. **DSR enters the verdict as a 7th evidence line, informational in `ReportOnly`.** In `Gate` it becomes a 7th gate: DSR >= `Confidence:Deflation:MinProbability` (default 0.95). The six existing gates and the factor stay; both are shown. Before the owner may set `Gate`, a **known-null validation** must be recorded in the ADR: seeded families of N = 16 pure-noise variants (iid R, mean 0) with T = 100 trades pass the DSR gate no more often than 10 % of the time (AC 6). If that test fails, `Gate` is not offered. Reason: the research note requires validation on known-null data first. Rejected: shipping a gate that was never checked for its false-pass rate.
5. **Block bootstrap resamples sessions, not trades.** The unit is the session (all trades with the same India entry date, kept together in their order); blocks are drawn by the stationary scheme of Politis and Romano: a block continues to the next session with probability `1 - 1/L` and restarts at a random session with probability `1/L`, wrapping around. `L = round(k^(1/3))` with `k` the number of sessions that have trades, minimum 2 (setting `Confidence:Bootstrap:BlockLength`, auto when 0). Reason: the documented weakness of `MonteCarlo` is clustering; resampling whole sessions keeps same-day dependence, blocks keep serial dependence. The research note proposes `n^(1/3)` in trades; using sessions is the more direct fix for the dominant dependence and is the decision here. Rejected: the circular-block bootstrap with fixed length (edge effects, fixed L); resampling trades in blocks (breaks same-day groups). Source: Politis and Romano 1994 (https://gnosis.library.ucy.ac.cy/handle/7/57533); arch docs (https://arch.readthedocs.io/en/latest/bootstrap/generated/arch.bootstrap.StationaryBootstrap.html).
6. **The old Monte Carlo stays.** `MonteCarlo.Run` (iid) is unchanged; the block result is a new member `monteCarloBlock` beside `monteCarlo` with both widths and their ratio. Reason: show beside. The seed rule is the existing one (first 8 bytes of the sha256 of the run key, ADR 0062) with a salt so the two streams differ.
7. **PBO by CSCV on per-variant daily series of a `robust` run.** Variants are the base plus the sweep points (up to 17). Each stores the daily sum of net R by exit date over the whole window, aligned on the union of session dates (zero on a day with no exit). `S` = `Confidence:Pbo:Blocks` (default 8, even, 4..16) contiguous blocks; all `C(S, S/2)` splits; in each, the in-sample best variant by the mean of daily R (setting-selectable to Sharpe), its relative out-of-sample rank `omega = rank / (N + 1)`, logit `lambda = ln(omega / (1 - omega))`, PBO = share of splits with `lambda <= 0`. At least `Confidence:Pbo:MinVariants` = 10 variants, otherwise `unavailable` (the research note: "needs enough variants (>= ~10) to mean anything"). Reason: the sweep is the only place the Lab has many aligned variants. Rejected: PBO on walk-forward winners (not aligned variants); storing every trade (a series is enough). Source: Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado and Zhu, "The probability of backtest overfitting" (https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253; abstract only: UNVERIFIED in detail).
8. **PBO cannot change the outcome unless `Confidence:Pbo:Mode = Gate`** (owner act); then PASS is refused (capped at MARGINAL with the reason) when PBO > `Confidence:Pbo:MaxPbo` (default 0.5). Default mode `ReportOnly`.
9. **Sweep neighbours limit what PBO can say.** One-parameter-at-a-time neighbours are not independent strategies; PBO measures whether the best of *these* generalises, not whether the strategy family has an edge. The report prints that.

## Acceptance criteria
Recomputed on 2026-10-27 (python and by hand).
1. **Luck bar (the research example, annual units).** N = 16, `sigma_SR` = 0.5: `Phi^-1(1 - 1/16) = Phi^-1(0.9375) = 1.5341`; `Phi^-1(1 - 1/(16 e)) = Phi^-1(0.97701) = 1.9955`; `0.4228 × 1.5341 + 0.5772 × 1.9955 = 0.6486 + 1.1518 = 1.8004`; `SR0 = 0.5 × 1.8004 = 0.9002`, **0.90**. A candidate at SR 0.85 is below the bar (DSR below 0.5); at SR 0.90 exactly DSR = 0.5. *`DeflatedSharpeTests.ExpectedMax`.*
2. **Per-trade worked case.** N = 16, trial `sigma_SR` = 0.05 per trade, so `SR0 = 0.05 × 1.8004 = 0.0900`. Candidate per-trade SR 0.15, T = 100, skew 0, kurt 3: `z = (0.15 - 0.09) × √99 / √(1 + 0.5 × 0.0225) = 0.06 × 9.950 / 1.0056 = 0.5937`, **DSR = 0.7236** (below 0.95: not significant). SR 0.25: `z = 0.16 × 9.950 / √(1 + 0.5 × 0.0625) = 1.5677`, **DSR = 0.9415** (still below 0.95). Skew -1 at SR 0.15: denominator `√(1 + 0.15 + 0.01125) = 1.0777`, `z = 0.5540`, DSR 0.7102 (negative skew lowers it). T = 30 at SR 0.15: z 0.3213, DSR 0.626. *Unit.*
3. **N = 1 degenerates correctly.** With a single trial `SR0 = 0` and DSR is the probabilistic Sharpe ratio against 0. *Unit.*
4. **Unavailable inputs.** `sigma_SR` not stored or fewer than 2 stored trials, T < 20, SR NaN, or `1 - skew SR + (kurt-1)/4 SR²` not positive: `unavailable: <reason>`, never a number, never a pass. *Unit.*
5. **Beside, not instead.** In `ReportOnly` the outcome, six gates and reasons of any stored verdict equal today's byte for byte (regression over the P5-03 and P5-04 fixtures); the DSR line and the old factor both appear. *`VerdictEvaluatorTests`.*
6. **Known-null validation (required before `Gate` is offered).** 400 seeded families, each of N = 16 variants of T = 100 iid trades with mean 0 and sd 1 R; `sigma_SR` taken from the 16 sample Sharpe ratios; the family's best variant passes `DSR >= 0.95` in at most 10 % of families (expected near 5 %, and the same fixture is run against the heuristic gates at their default and reported for comparison). If it exceeds 10 % the slice records it and `Gate` stays unavailable. *`DeflatedSharpeNullSimulationTests` (fixed seed, runs in under 2 s).*
7. **Stationary bootstrap indices, hand case.** k = 5 sessions, L = 2 (restart probability 0.5), uniforms 0.7, 0.7, 0.6, 0.2, 0.45, 0.9 (the rule: first index `floor(u × k)`; later, if `u < p` take a fresh `floor(u' × k)` from the next uniform, else the previous index + 1 mod k): indices **3, 4, 0, 2, 3** (0.7→3; 0.7 ≥ 0.5 → 4; 0.6 ≥ 0.5 → 0 by wrap; 0.2 < 0.5 restart with 0.45 → 2; 0.9 ≥ 0.5 → 3). *Unit with a scripted uniform stream.*
8. **Block length.** k = 64 sessions gives `L = round(64^(1/3)) = 4`; the note's 100 trades would give 4.64 → 5; k = 1 or 2 gives 2 (minimum) and a note. *Unit.*
9. **L = 1 equals a session-level iid bootstrap**, and a block length at least k reproduces circular resampling of the whole series; both by fixed uniforms. *Unit.*
10. **Width follows the clustering (statistical).** 20 sessions × 5 trades, within-session correlation 0.5 (generated with a seeded normal), sd 1.2 R: expected design effect `1 + 4 × 0.5 = 3`, so the block-bootstrap 95 % interval for mean R is wider than the iid interval by about `√3 = 1.73` (iid half-width about `1.96 × 1.2 / √100 = 0.235`, block about 0.41). Test: 2000 draws, ratio between 1.4 and 2.1. The research note's illustrative ±0.12 → ±0.18 is not reproduced (its inputs were not stated). *Statistical unit test, fixed seed.*
11. **Same seed, same numbers; different salt, different numbers**; the old `monteCarlo` member of any stored result is unchanged. *Unit.*
12. **CSCV combinations.** S = 8 gives `C(8, 4) = 70` splits; S odd or outside 4..16 is refused. If 28 of 70 splits have `lambda <= 0`, PBO = **0.40**. *Unit.*
13. **CSCV hand case.** Three variants, four blocks (one value per block), A = 2, 1, -1, 0; B = 1, 0.5, 1, 1.5; C = -2, 0, 3, 0.25; `C(4, 2) = 6` splits, in-sample mean decides the winner, `omega = rank / 4`: split {1,2}: A wins, out-of-sample rank 1, `lambda = ln(0.25/0.75) = -1.0986`; {1,3}: B wins, rank 3, +1.0986; {1,4}: B wins, rank 2, 0; {2,3}: C wins, rank 1, -1.0986; {2,4}: B wins, rank 3, +1.0986; {3,4}: C wins, rank 1, -1.0986. Splits with `lambda <= 0`: 4 of 6, **PBO = 0.667**. *Unit.* (The number is also the proof that PBO > 0.5 gets flagged.)
14. **Too few variants.** 9 variants: `unavailable: needs at least 10 variants`; a `robust` run with fewer sweep points reports it and the outcome is unchanged. *Unit + runner test.*
15. **Series are stored once, aligned, finite.** Each variant's series covers the same dates; a NaN day is refused (`unavailable`); the document size for 17 variants × 520 sessions stays under 100 KB. *Runner test.*
16. **Gate modes.** `Off`: no figure; `ReportOnly`: figures, no outcome change; `Gate` (PBO): a would-be PASS with PBO 0.667 becomes MARGINAL with the reason; a FAIL stays FAIL. Changing a mode is an owner act with a recorded snapshot. *Handler tests.*
17. **Old documents.** A stored result without the new members reads "not computed". No migration touches existing rows. Contract regenerated; architecture tests and `scripts/check.sh` green.

## Non-functional
Deterministic. DSR is O(1). The block bootstrap costs `draws × sessions` (1000 × 500 = 0.5 M ops). CSCV costs `C(S, S/2) × N × S` (70 × 17 × 8 for the default; S = 16 gives 12,870 splits, still milliseconds). No network. Logs carry counts only.

## Test plan
Unit (Domain): AC 1 to 4, 7 to 9, 12, 13 hand tables; property tests (DSR monotone in SR and in T, decreasing in N and in `sigma_SR`; PBO in [0, 1]; PBO of N identical variants is undefined and refused; bootstrap indices always in range). Statistical tests with fixed seeds: AC 6 and AC 10. Application: variant stats written once per run (idempotent retry), `LabRobustness` stores series and the block result, cancel stores nothing. Integration: the API shows the blocks; old documents show none. Frontend (MSW): rows render and degrade to "unavailable".

## Honest limits (printed with the numbers)
- DSR rests on normal-theory approximations and on a trial count that is not the number of truly independent ideas; it can be too harsh (correlated neighbours) or too lenient (unrecorded hand edits and strategies never logged).
- The block bootstrap needs enough sessions (about 30 or more) to be meaningful; below that its interval is not trustworthy and it says so.
- PBO on sweep points answers a narrow question (does the best neighbour hold?); it is not the probability that the strategy is overfit as an idea.
- About 2.1 years gives few sessions per CSCV block (about 65 at S = 8); a shorter window makes PBO noisy.

## Risks
1. A sweep with a single parameter has too few variants for PBO (below 10): the common case reads `unavailable`. Accepted; PBO becomes useful with CF-06's grids.
2. `Gate` for DSR depends on the null validation passing; if it does not, the slice ships display only.
3. A new write per run (`variant_stats`) must not break retries: unique key plus `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`.
