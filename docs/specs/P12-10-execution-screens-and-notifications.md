## Assumptions
1. **One page, five tabs.** Route `/execution` (made by P12-08), tab in the URL (`?tab=today|orders|positions|activity|modes`, default `today`; `useUrlState` if P8-02 built it, else `useSearchParams`) so a link reproduces a view and Back works.
2. **Server-side numbers, honest states.** Every figure is computed by the API from insert-only rows and arrives as `{state: value|insufficient|unavailable|not_evaluable|empty, value?, reason?, n?}` (P8-00 D3). The UI never draws a digit for a non-value (kit: `@/shared/analytics`, P8-01, `MetricValue`/`NonValue`, imported by path inside the lazy chunk). No composite score (P8-00 D10).
3. **In-app notifications only.** The bell and attention popups exist (ADR 0027). Nothing is pushed to a phone, mail or chat: it would send trading data off the machine (SAFETY) and P8-21 is not built.
4. **The stop button is P12-05's.** Levels and rules are P12-05's: `entries` and `cancel` need the session and CSRF only; `exit_all` needs the step-up window (403 `auth.step_up_required`); release needs a fresh password and reason (P12-05 criterion 7, read 2026-10-24).

## Goal
At any minute of a trading day the owner can see, on one page and in the bell, what the system is allowed to do, what it did, what is waiting for him, what could hurt, and can stop it with one deliberate action; and on the Modes tab can move an account up or down the ladder and edit its limits, each with the evidence beside it.

## Scope
**In:** read models in `xd-api` (GET only): `GET /api/execution/overview`, `/blotter`, `/positions`, `/activity`, `/notices`, and two extra fields on P12-01's `/status`; frontend tabs Today (mode, caps meters, readiness slot, open tickets, last actions, alarms and halts), Orders (blotter), Positions, Activity, Modes (ladder and gates, promotion request/confirm, demotion, switches, limits form, sheets of `assisted`); the stop chip in the app bar and its dialog; `deriveExecutionNotifications`; fixtures and MSW handlers; ADR note.
**Out:** any new write endpoint, any chart library, the phase-check page content (P12-12 provides `GET`, this page links to it), slippage and capacity reports (P11-02/03, P10), the readiness card body (P12-11), sound or desktop notifications, a phone surface.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given no `execution` data (fresh database, `Execution:Environment` none), when the page opens, then Today says "Execution is off" with the reasons from `/status` (service state, environment, per-account gate reason) and nothing else is drawn as healthy; the other tabs show `empty` states; no request fails. *`ExecutionPage.test.tsx`.*
2. Today shows per account: effective mode (`paper` … `auto_live`) with its `effectiveFrom`, the caps meters (orders used of the cap, notional used of the cap, worst-case loss against `max_loss_per_day`, open positions of the cap), and the open halts; a meter whose input is unavailable shows the `unavailable` word and reason, never `0`; the cap values are the sheet in force, not the pending one (the pending row is shown beside with its `effectiveFrom`). Hand fixture: used 3 of 6 orders, Rs 148,446.00 of Rs 150,000.00 notional, worst case Rs 1,470.00 of Rs 1,500.00. *`TodayTab.test.tsx`.*
3. Blotter rows: time, account, symbol, side, quantity, type and price, the state in P12-01's vocabulary (planned, armed, checking, rejected, submitting, open, filled, protected, managing, closing, closed, reconciled, cancelled, failed) with an icon and a word, who caused it (`tap`, `auto`, `system`, `owner typed` for `assisted`), short client order id, broker order id when known, fill price and slippage against the ticket price in ticks and percent (`value` per fill; `empty` when unfilled), the rejection codes (from P12-03 check results) or broker code, and `algoId` when the broker returned one (shown as received, never invented). An unknown state shows "Unknown state: X" in the error style. Filters: date, account, state group. *`BlotterTab.test.tsx`.*
4. Positions rows: symbol, net quantity, average, last price, unrealised result (`unavailable` when the price is older than `exec.feed_max_age_s`), risk to stop, **protection** `confirmed | missing | unknown` (never green unless confirmed from the broker's order book; `unknown` has the word and a warning icon), source (`ours` or `foreign`), reconciliation `matched | break | not_run | unavailable`. A position with `missing` protection also appears in an error banner at the top of every tab. *`PositionsTab.test.tsx`.*
5. Activity lists `execution.action_log` plus alarms, halts, drills, mode and settings changes newest first, 50 per page, filters kind/by/date; every automatic send reads as plain language ("Placed BUY 120 TATAPOWER ... because ..."). It is read only and says the log is insert-only. *`ActivityTab.test.tsx`.*
6. Modes tab, per account: the ladder with the next gate's checks (`pass | fail | unavailable`, text, n and needed), disabled "Request promotion" unless the gate is `eligible`; after a request, the cooling-off countdown from the server's `eligibleFrom`, "Confirm promotion" enabled only after it and before `expiresAt`; both ask the password in a dialog with the request's reason field; "Move down one step" needs a confirm dialog and no password; the page always states "Moving up needs your password twice, at least two sessions apart; moving down is instant." *`ModesTab.test.tsx`.*
7. Switches and limits: `execution_enabled`, `auto_live_enabled` and the other risk keys appear with their risk direction ("raising risk waits one closed session and needs your password; lowering is immediate"); the limits form (P12-09) shows the sheet in force beside the form, highlights each field that would raise risk, says whether the change needs the password, shows `effectiveFrom` of the answer; an empty sheet reads "Empty: nothing may be sent automatically". The form never offers a value above a ceiling and shows the server's field errors. *`LimitsForm.test.tsx`.*
8. Stop chip: visible in the app bar on every page when `status.anyAutomated` or any halt is active; opens a dialog with three outcome-labelled actions ("Stop new orders", "Stop new orders and cancel pending ones", "Stop new orders, cancel pending ones and exit all positions with marketable limit orders"), each line stating what happens in numbers from `overview` (working orders N, open positions M, stops stay at the broker); the first two send `POST /api/execution/halts` with level `entries`/`cancel` at once on one click (the request becomes a row in `execution.commands` and the halt row is written by `xd-exec`'s command path, since `xd_app` cannot write halts: the chip shows "Stop requested" until the halt row appears, then "Trading stopped", and never "stopped" on the request alone); `exit_all` first shows the exact list of positions, says each is exited with a marketable limit order (the system never sends MARKET orders, P12-02) so a fill is not guaranteed, and needs the step-up (the app's step-up dialog) and a second click; the dialog never defaults focus to the destructive action; the applied halt (halt id, level, time) replaces the chip with "Trading stopped" and a link to release. Release is a separate dialog with password and reason and shows the server's refusal reasons verbatim. *`StopChip.test.tsx`.*
9. Kill-switch drill: a "Run drill" button on Modes (password) calls `POST /api/execution/drills`; the last result and its age are shown; a drill older than 30 days is flagged because the ladder gate needs it (P12-07). *`ModesTab.test.tsx`.*
10. Notifications. `GET /api/execution/notices` returns items in the shape of the portfolio notices (`id, kind, severity, text, title, at, actions (at most 2: route or command), since, resolved`); the bell merges them (`deriveExecutionNotifications`); kinds and severities: `exec_confirm_waiting` (warning, text names the order and the expiry, action "Open"), `exec_auto_action` (info, one per automatic action, born resolved so it is listed but not counted), `exec_order_rejected` (error), `exec_protection_missing` (error), `exec_halted` (error), `exec_kill_switch` (error), `exec_demoted` (error), `exec_recon_break` (error), `exec_not_ready` (warning, error from 09:05 IST), `exec_ip_invalid` (error), `exec_cap_near` (warning at 80 % of orders, notional or worst-case loss), `exec_attended_pause` (warning), `exec_day_summary` (info at 15:45 IST). ## Non-functional
Read models are straight projections over small day-sized tables (a day has at most 20 orders, 200 activity rows); no unbounded query (every list is paged, at most 200). Security: session required; the notices and blotter never include client ids, tokens or account numbers (a scan of the response types); the stop chip's requests carry the CSRF header; the sign-in rate limit applies to password-bearing routes of other slices. Observability: log ids 6730 to 6739 (counts and codes only).

## Test plan
Frontend (MSW, pinned clock): every state of every tab; the stop dialog paths (entries, cancel, exit_all with step-up, release refused with reasons); promotion request then confirm across a pinned cooling-off; limits raise vs lower messaging; notices derivation table (each kind, resolved, popup once); poll interval switching; the write allow-list; 360 px and 1440 px layouts by class names, not pixels. Backend (Testcontainers): each read model on an empty database, on a fixture day (3 entries, one rejected, one protection breach, one halt), paging, filters, the no-secret scan. Edge: an account archived after trading (still listed with its history), a day with only `paper` accounts, the market closed.

## Decisions (researched)
- **D1. One page with tabs, not five routes.** Reason: the owner on a trading day moves between "what is waiting" and "what is open" constantly; one lazy chunk, one polling set, one URL state. Rejected: separate routes (five chunks, five polls, nav clutter).
- **D2. The stop control lives in the app bar, not only on the page.** The emergency does not happen when the owner is on the Execution page. Cost: small eager code (criterion 16). Source: doc 20 §A (kill switch "a button"), P12-05.
- **D3. Outcome-labelled buttons, no default on the destructive one, the strongest friction only for exit-all.** Reason: NN/g advises naming the specific action and its effect, avoiding generic yes/no, and reserving the strongest measures for the rarest, most dangerous actions so they do not become automatic (<https://www.nngroup.com/articles/confirmation-dialog/>, read 2026-10-24). Stopping new orders is the safe direction and takes one click; exit-all realises losses and needs the step-up P12-05 already requires. Rejected: a password for every stop (slows the emergency).
- **D4. Poll, do not push.** Reason: the app's notices already poll, SignalR carries only live prices and Lab progress, and a 5 s poll bounds latency; a push channel is a follow-up. Rejected: Server-Sent Events/SignalR for execution now (a new surface to secure and test).
- **D5. Notices derived from insert-only rows, not stored.** The portfolio notices remember what they derive (ADR 0027); here every cause already has a row with an id, so a derivation is deterministic and needs no second place that can disagree. Resolved state is "the cause row has a successor" (an outcome, a release, a clear).
- **D6. In-app only, and the page says so.** A running `auto_live` with nobody looking is why P12-09 requires attendance; this slice shows "attended / not attended" on Today so the owner sees what the guard sees.
- **D7. Per-fill slippage only.** Aggregates need n and a model (P11-03); here each fill shows its own difference to the ticket price, `value` or `empty`, never a rolled-up "slippage score".
- **D8. Foreign orders are shown, labelled, and not hidden.** The owner may still trade by hand in Dhan; those orders appear as `foreign` in Positions and Activity so a break explanation is visible (P12-06 decides what is a break).

## Risks and honest limits
- A screen cannot prove protection exists: `confirmed` means the broker's order book (read at a stated time) showed the stop; `unknown` is shown whenever that read is older than its limit.
- Polling means the view can be up to 5 s behind (2 s for a ticket); the stale banner makes that visible, it does not remove it.
- The stop chip depends on `xd-exec` consuming the command (P12-05); with the service down the command waits in `execution.commands`, the chip says "Stop requested, xd-exec is not running" and nothing is described as stopped until the halt row exists (the broker-side stops and, if configured, P12-05's broker controls are the only protection meanwhile).
- Marks are at the last price from the app's feed; day P&L excludes charges until the owner's charge sheet is in (ISS-25).
- Notifications are in the app: if the browser is closed nothing reaches the owner (stated on Today).
