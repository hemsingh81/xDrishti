## Goal
The rest of the report catalogue that the stored data can honestly serve is on the Reports screen: strategies, side analysis, calendar and time of day, MAE/MFE, R distribution, instrument scorecard, symbol and sector, exit-policy comparison on identical entries, and Monte Carlo risk from the Lab; and every report the data cannot serve yet is listed with the reason it is missing instead of an empty chart.

## Scope
**In:** additive Domain/Reporting extensions (new dimensions `Sector` and `Hour`, extra trade fields, MAE/MFE grid); two new read endpoints (`mae-mfe`, `comparisons`); the catalogue entries and panels; pointer entries with reasons; tests; ADR.
**Out:** a regime report (R7), calibration (R12), selector effectiveness (R13) and decisions (R26) as new computations (R13/R26 link to Learning, Decisions of P8-04; R7 and R12 are pointer entries); backtest vs paper vs live (R14), discipline (R19), execution gap (R32) (need P10/P11 data; pointer entries); account comparison (R17: a simulated trade has no account); a scatter chart; the stored per-cell exit choice (`research.exit_selections`), a follow-up (Findings); exports; drill-down (P8-11).

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Engine, additive.** Given the P8-05 population, when `ReportTrade` gets the optional trailing fields `MaeR`, `MfeR`, `EntryMinuteIst`, `ExitReason`, `Sector` (all with null defaults, so P8-05 tests compile unchanged), and `ReportDimension` gets `Sector` and `Hour`, then P8-05 tests stay green unchanged, a group by `Hour` uses the India-time hour of the entry (09 to 15) for intraday trades only (swing trades are `not recorded` for it, not hour 0), a group by `Sector` uses the sector at read time (null sector = not recorded, shown "Unclassified"), and both are valid in the `lens` of the built endpoints (`ReportNames` lists the dimensions by enumerating `ReportDimension`, so the whitelist grows with the enum). The built `IReportTradeSource.StreamTradesAsync` returns `ReportTrade` without MAE, MFE, exit reason or the entry minute: this slice extends the Infrastructure reader's projection (`mae_r`, `mfe_r`, `exit_reason`, `entry_epoch_seconds`) and the sector lookup; it does not rely on columns P8-06 did not read.
2. **Strategies (R4).** Given the report, then a strategy by timeframe heat grid with a side selector (both, BUY, SELL) and an equity-by-strategy chart (at most 12 series); all from existing endpoints (`lens=strategy,timeframe` pivot with `side=` filter, `equity?lens=strategy`; the server caps equity at 40 series, the screen shows at most 12).
3. **Side analysis (R6).** Given the report, then long against short KPIs side by side and cumulative R per side (`equity?lens=side`, `groups?lens=side`), each figure through the kit.
4. **Calendar and time of day (R10).** Given the report, then bar lists of expectancy by weekday, by entry hour (intraday only, header says so) and total R by month; hours with fewer than the minimum show their state; there is no calendar heatmap in this slice.
5. **MAE / MFE (R8).** Given `GET /api/reports/mae-mfe`, then the response has per group: a grid of MAE bins (0.25 R wide to 2 R, then "2 R or more") by result R bins (0.5 R wide, -5 R to +5 R with the two tails), counts only; an MFE histogram (0.5 R wide to 5 R then "5 R or more"); `reachedMfe` at 1 R, 2 R and 3 R as `{atR, count, share}` with n; and `winnersMaeP90` (nearest-rank 90th percentile of MAE over winning trades) as a `MetricView` (insufficient below the minimum number of winners). Server side, deterministic, no points are sent. The screen draws the MAE grid with `HeatGrid` (counts, a shade by count, the count as text) and the MFE histogram with `ColumnBars`, with target markers as labelled columns, and prints the sentences from the fields ("8 of 10 trades reached +1 R at some point").
6. **R distribution (R9).** Given `distribution?lens=…` (built: bins of half an R from -4 R to 6 R with an open bin at each end, a thin group has no bins), then one histogram per group with the tails; counts and n; no smoothing or fitted curve.
7. **Instrument scorecard (R21).** Given the report, then a table from `what-works?lens=instrument` (ranked by credible lower bound; instruments below the minimum listed after with their n, never hidden), with strategy best-by-bound taken from `what-works?lens=instrument,strategy`; no sparkline equity per instrument (stated).
8. **Symbol and sector (R11).** Given the report, then ranked bars by sector (expectancy lower bound where available; others show their state) and by symbol for the top and bottom ten by total R among instruments that reach the minimum; no treemap.
9. **Exit-policy comparison (R5).** Given `GET /api/reports/comparisons`, then the stored comparisons are listed (key, strategy, entries key, unit, common entries, members with candidate ids, created); choosing one sets `runs=<its member run keys>` for the report (the built query has no `comparison` parameter; the keys come from the comparisons list), whose groups are `lens=exit` (the engine requires the exit split when the same signals appear under several exits, so a grouping without it is refused `reports.double_count` and the screen adds it); the table shows per exit: n, expectancy, total R, profit factor, max drawdown, longest loss streak, expectancy lower bound, on identical entries; the screen says "same entries, different exits: n is the same for every row".
10. **Monte Carlo risk (R15).** Given Lab runs that stored a robustness block, then the screen lists them (`GET /api/lab/runs`, then `GET /api/lab/runs/{id}/report`) and shows `MonteCarloView` as a table: trades, draws, seed (text), observed mean R and max drawdown, bootstrap mean R p05/p50/p95, drawdown p50/p95, share not positive, each with its n = `trades` and the Lab's limits; no fan chart (the stored result has percentiles, not paths); a run without a robustness block or without a report (409 `lab.no_report`) says so.
11. **Pointer entries.** Given the catalogue, then Regime (R7), Calibration (R12), Selector effectiveness (R13), Decisions (R26), Backtest vs paper vs live (R14), Discipline (R19), Execution gap (R32), Account comparison (R17) each appear as unavailable or linked entries with the missing input stated from the server where it has one (`facets.dimensions` for regime/account) and otherwise a fixed reason (no champion model stored; no fills; no paper trades), plus the slice or phase that supplies it; R13 and R26 link to Learning › Decisions. A test pins that a pointer entry renders no chart and no digit.
12. **Honest states everywhere:** every panel passes the P8-08 property (no digit for a non-value, no non-value in the `good` colour), has its evidence, and shows the multiple-looks sentence where it ranks.
13. Contract regenerated; Architecture.Tests unchanged; the new endpoints carry the `evidence` block and the `reports.*` problem codes of P8-06; determinism (same runs, same bytes).

## Contract
- **API (new, read only):** `GET /api/reports/mae-mfe` (name `GetReportMaeMfe`; the built lens query shape of `ReportLensParams`: `runs`, `basis`, `lens`, filters; same caps and errors, evidence is `ReportEvidenceView`), response `ReportMaeMfeView { Dimensions, Groups: [{ Key, Label, N, Grid: { MaeEdges, ResultEdges, Counts[][], BelowCount, AboveCount }, Mfe: { Edges, Counts[], AboveCount }, ReachedMfe: [{ AtR, Count, Share }], WinnersMaeP90: MetricView }], Evidence }`. `GET /api/reports/comparisons` (name `ListReportComparisons`), response `ReportComparisonsView { Comparisons: [{ ComparisonKey, EntriesKey, StrategyId, Unit, CommonEntries, Members: [{ RunKey, CandidateId }], CreatedAt }], Evidence? }` from a new method on `IReportTradeSource` (`ListComparisonsAsync`, reads `research.sim_comparisons` header columns only, not the CSV text); the built `ReportRunInfo` already carries each run's exit label from the same table. Both endpoints are added to `ReportsEndpoints` with their params records, and the architecture tests that cover it (GET only, no confirmation) apply unchanged.
- **Domain (pure, additive):** `ReportTrade` trailing optional fields (above), `ReportDimension.Sector`, `ReportDimension.Hour`, `ReportPopulation.MaeMfe(dimensions)` → `ReportMaeMfe`. Existing members, ordering of the enum values already used and all P8-05 tests unchanged; the new enum members are appended.
- **Infrastructure:** the sector is read at load time from the instrument master for the instruments of the cached trades (`IInstrumentQueries`; `Sector`, `SectorSource` of `InstrumentDetail`, UNVERIFIED which port returns it in bulk: first step), attached once per run and cached with the trades; `Hour` is derived in the mapper from `entry_epoch_seconds` in India time, intraday runs only (style label `intraday`; swing and unlabelled runs give null).
- **UI:** catalogue ids `strategies`, `side`, `calendar`, `maemfe`, `distribution`, `instruments`, `sectors`, `exits`, `montecarlo` plus pointer entries; components (one per file, ≤ 250 lines) `StrategiesReport`, `SideReport`, `CalendarReport`, `MaeMfeReport`, `DistributionReport`, `InstrumentScorecard`, `SectorReport`, `ExitComparison`, `ComparisonPicker`, `MonteCarloReport`, `PointerEntry`. Shared: none new (reuses `HeatGrid`, `BarList`, `ColumnBars`, `DataTable`, `TimeSeriesChart`).
- **Settings:** none new. **Data / migration:** none.

## Non-functional
- Each new endpoint p95 under 1 s warm on the fixture; the MAE/MFE grid is one pass over the cached trades. Responses are counts and series, never per-trade points (at most 12 groups, about 25 by 22 cells each).
- Queries and logs as P8-06 (`report.read` with kind); no new secrets; read only.
- The Lab report call is one request per chosen run, on demand (not on page load).

## Test plan
- **Domain unit tests, hand-worked:** R distribution of R = -1.0, -0.7, -0.2, 0.0, 0.3, 0.5, 1.9, 2.0, 6.0, -7.0 gives bins [-1.0,-0.5) 2, [-0.5,0) 1, [0,0.5) 2, [0.5,1.0) 1, [1.5,2.0) 1, [2.0,2.5) 1, above 1, below 1. MFE values 0.5, 1.0, 1.2, 1.9, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.1, 4.0, 0.0 give reached 1 R = 8 (share 0.8), 2 R = 5 (0.5), 3 R = 3 (0.3). Winners' MAE (nearest rank, p90) of ten winners with MAE 0.1 … 1.0 in steps of 0.1 is the 9th value, 0.9. Hour of an entry at 09:20 IST is 9; a swing trade has no hour. Sector null is "not recorded". Properties: grid counts sum to n; adding a trade to one group changes no other group; permuting input changes nothing; P8-05 tests untouched.
- **Application / integration (Testcontainers, the P8-06 fixture extended):** comparison listing from a stored comparison with two members and candidate ids `FIXED_RR{rr=2}` and `FIXED_RR{rr=3}`; the report over `runs=<the two member keys>` with `lens=exit` has the same n in both rows; without the exit split the call is 422 `reports.double_count`; mae-mfe endpoint equals the in-memory engine; 401 without a session; contract snapshot.
- **Screen tests (MSW):** each report for empty, thin (all groups below the minimum), full; the pointer entries; Monte Carlo with a run that has no robustness block; markup in strategy names; keyboard through the MAE grid.
- **Read-only folder test, contrast of the grid shades, bundle (route chunk size printed), browser checklist** as P8-08.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **No scatter: bin on the server.** Question: R8 asks for a MAE/MFE scatter. Chosen: counts in bins (a 2-D grid and a histogram). Reason: a run can hold 100,000+ trades; a scatter needs a capped sample (a silent downsample) or a big payload, a new chart type in the lazy chunk and gives no accessible name per mark; bins are exact, tiny, deterministic and have a table. Rejected: a sampled scatter (P8-00's "downsample rule stated" becomes unnecessary because nothing is sampled).
2. **Exit comparison runs through the engine, not the CSV.** The stored comparison's member runs are runs of the same entries under different exits; the P8-05 population rule already allows that case and requires the exit split. Rejected: parsing the stored comparison CSV text (a second source of the same numbers that could disagree).
3. **Exit-selection (walk-forward) results are not in this slice.** `research.exit_selections` and its folds exist, but they answer "which exit per cell, out of sample", a separate read model; named under Findings so the owner can order it. Until then the exit comparison is in-sample on identical entries and says so.
4. **Hour only for intraday.** A swing trade's entry hour is an artefact of the fill minute; mixing it would bias the hour bars. Rejected: all trades by hour.
5. **Sector as of today, labelled.** The master carries one current sector (and its source); history of sector is not stored; the limits line says "sector as of today". Unclassified shares stay "not recorded", not a zero.
6. **Monte Carlo from the Lab's stored robustness,** not recomputed by reports: one source of the bootstrap (P5-05, ADR 0062, seeded) and the Lab's wording; percentiles only.
7. **Regime, calibration, selector, decisions, execution gap, discipline are pointers.** Showing an empty regime heatmap would read as "flat". Regime needs P3-01d/e; calibration needs a champion model (none stored, ADR 0080); decisions exist in P8-04; the others need fills (P10/P11). Source: P8-00 "What exists", ADR 0080, 0090.
8. **No account comparison:** a simulated trade has no account (P8-00 D4); account results exist only on plan allocations and journal fills (P8-04).
9. **Q10, Q12, Q13, Q19** as recommended in P8-00.

## Risks and honest limits
- Strategy, side, hour and sector tables are many cells over thin samples; they describe the past and invite a selection effect. Ranked views print the multiple-looks sentence; descriptive views say "descriptive, not a ranking".
- Hour and weekday bars of intraday strategies depend on each strategy's entry rules (ORB enters early by construction); the page says the hour reflects the rules.
- The exit comparison is in-sample, on the same entries, with modelled fills; the "best" exit is not validated out of sample until the exit-selection read exists.
- Monte Carlo is a bootstrap of the out-of-sample trades the Lab used (independence assumed); percentiles of 3 to 6 years are not forecasts.
- Sector may be missing for many shares; the table prints how many are unclassified.
