# P12-03 — Pre-trade checks (34 checks, `unavailable` is a reject)

- **Status:** Draft (written 2026-10-24; decisions below were researched under the owner's delegation; the owner's Approved is still needed before an agent builds it)
- **Phase / roadmap row:** P12 — Execution service, slice P12-03 of [P12-00](P12-00-execution-service-plan.md)
- **Design refs:** docs/design/20-extended-modules.md §A ("same limits as planning + price band vs LTP, freeze quantity, margin") · 10-trade-selection.md §5, §6b · 08-exits-and-risk.md §6, §7 · 04 §4.1 · 16-configuration.md (`risk_profiles`, `execution.price_band_pct`) · 19 §4 · ADR 0087 (pre-trade checklist: unavailable never passes), ADR 0091 (auto means every check Pass), ADR 0085, ADR 0084. **Design change:** none to docs 08/10; doc 20 §A gains the check list as a table. ADR: next free ADR number at build time.
- **Needs:** P12-01, P12-02 (reads: quote, funds, margin, kill switch, profile, static IP, positions). Soft inputs wait for P7-06 (correlation) and P15 (results, ban); P12-05 and P12-06 supply `no_halt` and `streams_alive` (until built they read `unavailable`, which rejects: correct).
- **Size:** 2.5 agent-days
- **Safety:** yes. It decides whether an order may be sent. It only ever makes the system more restrictive than the plan; nothing here can send anything. No setting turns a check off; settings only move a limit inside a compiled range.

## Goal
Before any instruction reaches the broker, one pure function judges the intent against 34 checks with exact limits, writes every result as a row, and answers `Cleared` or `Rejected` with the codes. Every mandatory input that is missing, old, not finite or unreadable makes its check `unavailable`, and `unavailable` never passes. The same function is re-run for the time-critical checks at the moment of the send.

## Scope
**In:** Domain `ExecChecklist` (`Evaluate(IntentFacts, AccountFacts, MarketFacts, SystemFacts, ExecLimits) -> CheckRun`), `CheckClass` (Hard, Soft), `CheckOutcome` (Pass, Fail, Unavailable), the 34 codes, `ExecLimits` (from the settings registry and the account's risk profile), the fact builders (`FactsLoader` over ports: quotes, funds, margin, positions, kill switch, profile, static IP, calendar, plan, master), the `CheckGate` step that `LegSender` (P12-02) calls (full run at `checking`, fast re-run before every entry send), tables `execution.check_runs`, `execution.check_results`, `execution.check_confirmations`; `GET /api/execution/intents/{id}/checks`; settings keys; parity tests against `PreTradeChecklist`.
**Run kinds.** `full` (at `checking`, authorises a send), `fast` (the re-run before an entry send, section criterion 8) and `advisory` (every check except `gate_open`, evaluated for an `assisted` account so that P12-07's order sheet can show what would pass; it is stored with kind `advisory`, belongs to a **sheet** and not to an intent (`intent_id` null, `sheet_id` set, the sheet has an intent-less id), and its verdict can never authorise a send: `LegSender` accepts only a `full` run of its own intent that is still valid).
**Out:** the owner's confirm gesture and its screen (P12-08/10), halts (P12-05), the recovery that provides stream facts (P12-06), creating intents (P12-08/09). Exit orders and protective stops are **not** checked by this list (they only reduce risk and must not be blocked); they have the reduce-only rule of P12-04.

## The checks (code: class: rule, with the numbers)
**Gates.** `gate_open` H: `AccountGate` open (P12-01): `execution_enabled` ON, effective mode `semi_auto` or `auto_live`, process environment `sandbox` or `live`; the switch OFF is a closed gate. `no_halt` H: no active halt for the account or the system in `execution.halts` (P12-05, the execution truth) **and** no active human halt of the P11 stage (`trading.live_halts`, P11-04) for the account; either store unreadable is `unavailable`. `broker_kill_switch_off` H: `GET /v2/killswitch` is `DEACTIVATE`, read at most 60 s ago. `token_valid` H: `tokenValidity` from the profile minus now at least `exec.token_min_remaining_min` (20) minutes, profile read at most 5 min ago. `static_ip_ok` H: `getIP` reports orders allowed for the detected IP, read at most 15 min ago and again after any `IpNotAllowed`. `session_window` H: a trading day of the calendar, now within `exec.entry_window_start`..`exec.entry_window_end` (09:20..15:00 IST), no after-market. `feed_fresh` H: a live quote for the instrument no older than `exec.feed_max_age_s` (5). `streams_alive` H: the order-update stream connected, or the order-book poll no older than 15 s.
**Ticket and shape.** `ticket_valid` H: the plan ticket is Armed (or Approved before arming for a same-day owner tap), not Invalidated/Expired/Lapsed, the approval row exists (kind `owner`, or `auto` with its settings snapshot), the allocation exists for this account and the quantity equals it. `levels_valid` H: entry, stop, target are numbers, positive, on the instrument's tick; stop on the loss side, target on the profit side; stop distance at least 2 ticks and 0.2% of entry; (target-entry)/(entry-stop) at least 1.5 (doc 10 §2). `quantity_valid` H: whole, at least 1, at most `exec.max_order_qty` (2,000) and notional at most `exec.max_order_value_inr` (100,000). `product_segment` H: `NSE_EQ`, `INTRADAY`, BUY or SELL; **entries are BUY only** until the future P12-13; a SELL instruction is accepted only as an exit that closes an existing long (it is not checked by this list, see criterion 10) and a SELL entry (a short) fails with `short_not_supported`. `instrument_tradable` H: the security id maps to the symbol in the master with the same ISIN, trading allowed (`BUY_SELL_INDICATOR` A), not suspended.
**Prices.** `ltp_collar` H: with LTP the live price, |entry-LTP|/LTP at most `exec.price_band_pct` (2.0%) and, for a BUY, LTP above stop + 2 ticks and below target : a trade whose levels the price has already passed is void. `circuit_band` H: from the quote's `upper_circuit_limit`/`lower_circuit_limit`: entry at least lower + 2 ticks and at most upper - 2 ticks, the stop on the right side of the lower (BUY) or upper (SELL) limit, the target inside the band. `spread_depth` H: best ask - best bid at most max(`exec.max_spread_ticks` (3) ticks, `exec.max_spread_pct` (0.25)% of LTP) and the opposing side of the five visible levels holds at least `exec.min_depth_multiple` (2) times the quantity. `freeze_quantity` H: quantity at most min(own cap, the exchange freeze quantity when a source exists). **No source exists for cash equity** (UNVERIFIED, section Decisions 6), so the check passes by the own caps of `quantity_valid` and says so in `detail` ("own cap applies"); a known freeze below the cap fails it.
**Money and risk.** `margin_funds` H: `POST /v2/margincalculator` for the exact instruction: `insufficientBalance` false and `totalMargin` at most funds (`availabelBalance`, read at most 60 s ago) minus the margin already reserved by non-terminal intents; the system never assumes leverage; and the notional is at most the account's capital source. `risk_per_trade` H: risk = |entry-stop| x quantity at most min(capital x profile `risk_per_trade_pct`, `exec.max_loss_per_trade_inr` (1,000)). `exposure_headroom` H: open risk (non-terminal intents at their stops plus broker positions at theirs, positions read at most 60 s ago) plus this at most capital x `max_total_risk_pct`. `open_positions` H: filled or working entries below the profile's `max_open_positions`. `entries_today` H: entries sent today below `max_new_entries_per_day`. `daily_loss` H: today's realised plus unrealised loss (fills and LTP) strictly below min(`daily_loss_r` x the per-trade risk budget, `daily_loss_pct` x capital); reaching it fails the check (P12-05 also halts). `one_per_symbol` H: no non-terminal intent, no broker position and no working order, ours or foreign, in the instrument on the account (broker data at most 60 s old). `order_budget` H: orders today (from `broker_calls`) below `exec.max_orders_per_day` (200) and the minute and second limiters have room. `canary_cap` H: while the setting `exec.canary_pending` is ON (default, P12-01) the quantity must be at most 1; the check is `Fail` for 2 or more and `Unavailable` if the setting cannot be read; only the owner turns the setting off, with a password, after recording canaries C1 and C2 (P12-00). P12-08 and P12-09 reference it.
**Soft (the owner may confirm a non-pass in `semi_auto`; `auto_live` needs every one to Pass).** `sector_exposure` (sector count + 1 at most `max_per_sector`), `holdings_conflict` (a SELL of an instrument held long in any account, or an open trade in it), `adv_share` (quantity at most 1% of the average daily volume, the P7-05 number), `results_within_days` (more than 3 days), `fno_ban`, `surveillance` (the master's ASM/GSM flag, once ingested), `correlation` (at most 0.70 with the book, P7-06), `data_quality` (latest validation of the instrument clean). Today `results_within_days`, `fno_ban`, `correlation` and `surveillance` have no source: they read `unavailable`, so **`auto_live` cannot clear anything until P7-06 and P15 provide them**, and `semi_auto` shows them as "confirmed despite" with the owner's name and reason.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **List.** `ExecChecklist.Evaluate` returns the 34 codes in the fixed order above, 26 Hard and 8 Soft; two runs on the same inputs give identical results (deterministic); every result carries code, class, outcome, value, limit, detail, `input_age_ms` and `source`. *`ExecChecklistTests`.*
2. **Unavailable rejects.** For each mandatory input, a null, NaN, infinite, negative-where-impossible or stale value (older than its maximum age) gives `Unavailable` for exactly the checks that read it, the verdict is `Rejected`, and no Hard check can be overridden by anyone. A table of 80 rows (input x defect) is the test. *`UnavailableTableTests`.*
3. **Boundaries.** At the limit passes where the P7 rule says "at most" (risk, exposure, entries, depth, spread, collar) and fails where it says "below" (daily loss, open positions): hand fixtures in the section Test plan; the floating-point tolerance of P7-05 (1e-9) is reused. *`LimitBoundaryTests`.*
4. **Verdict.** `Cleared` only when all 26 Hard pass and every Soft passes or is confirmed; `Cleared` by confirmation records the confirmation (actor starting `owner:`, reason, the codes) in `check_confirmations`; a confirmation is bound to the exact set of Soft codes that were non-pass when it was given and is **void** if a later run's set of Soft non-passes differs in any way (a new Soft fail, a Soft that changed outcome); a confirmation naming a Hard code, an `auto` actor, an `unavailable` Hard input or a mode other than `semi_auto` is refused (`execution.confirm_refused`). `auto_live` with one Soft non-pass is `Rejected`. *`VerdictTests`.*
5. **Parity with planning.** For the checks that have a twin in `PreTradeChecklist` (risk_per_trade, the capital part of margin_funds, exposure_headroom, daily_loss, data_quality, holdings_conflict, sector_exposure, adv_share, correlation, results_within_days, fno_ban, surveillance, and circuit_band against `price_band`) the Pass/Fail/Unavailable result equals `PreTradeChecklist.Evaluate`'s on 60 generated rows, except where this spec is stricter (the per-trade INR cap, depth); the P7-05 file is not changed. *`ChecklistParityTests`.*
6. **No short-circuit.** All 34 are evaluated and stored even when the first fails, so the owner sees everything. *same.*
7. **Persisted.** One `check_runs` row (verdict, settings hash, inputs hash) and 34 `check_results` rows per run, insert-only; the intent event `check_run` points to the run id; `checking>rejected` carries the codes in its payload. *integration.*
8. **Fresh at send.** A run is valid for `exec.check_valid_for_s` (10) seconds. After a tap (P12-08) the run that precedes the send must itself pass all its Hard checks (a Soft confirmation covers only Soft codes); `LegSender` re-runs `gate_open`, `no_halt`, `broker_kill_switch_off`, `feed_fresh`, `ltp_collar`, `circuit_band`, `streams_alive` as a `fast` run before the write-ahead of an entry; any non-pass there aborts with `checking>rejected`, a run older than its validity forces a full run. A non-pass found **after** the write-ahead (the second halt check of P12-02) ends `submitting>failed` (P12-02 criterion 6), never `checking>rejected`. *`FreshAtSendTests` (fake clock).*
9. **Foreign activity.** A working order or a position in the instrument that is not ours (no `XD` client order id, or source not `P`) fails `one_per_symbol` and is named in `detail`. *`OneSymbolTests`.*
10. **Exits are not blocked.** A protective stop, a target and a reduce-only exit never pass through this list (they are checked by the reduce-only rule); a test shows a halted, over-limit, out-of-window account can still send an exit, and the protective stop placement and the flatten of a filled position are permitted at **every** halt level including `freeze` (the P12-05 table), proved for each level. *`ExitsNotBlockedTests`.*
11. **Read model.** `GET /api/execution/intents/{id}/checks` returns the latest run with its 34 results and any confirmation; 404 `execution.intent_not_found`; the answer never includes a token, client id or secret. *integration.*
12. `scripts/gen-api.sh` run; `scripts/check.sh` green.

## Contract
- **API:** `GET /api/execution/intents/{id}/checks` -> `{runId, at, verdict: cleared|cleared_by_confirmation|rejected, settingsHash, results:[{code, class, outcome, value, limit, detail, inputAgeMs, source}], confirmation?:{actor, reason, codes, at}}`.
- **Data:** `execution.check_runs(id bigint identity, kind check in ('full','fast','advisory'), intent_id null fk, sheet_id uuid null (exactly one of the two is set; an advisory run has only the sheet), at, verdict, settings_hash, inputs_hash, valid_until)`; `execution.check_results(run_id fk, ord smallint, code, class, outcome, value double precision null, limit_value double precision null, detail text, input_age_ms int null, source text, pk(run_id, ord))`; `execution.check_confirmations(run_id pk fk, actor check like 'owner:%', reason, codes text[], at)`. All insert-only; `xd_app` SELECT only. Migration `AddExecutionChecks`.
- **Settings added to the registry:** `exec.max_loss_per_trade_inr` 1000 (100..50000), `exec.max_spread_ticks` 3 (1..20), `exec.max_spread_pct` 0.25 (0.05..1), `exec.min_depth_multiple` 2 (1..10), `exec.check_valid_for_s` 10 (2..60).
- **Fixed input ages (compiled, not settings):** quote 5 s (the setting `exec.feed_max_age_s`), funds, margin, positions, order book 60 s, kill switch 60 s, profile 5 min, static IP 15 min, stream/poll 15 s.
- **Log ids** 6635 to 6644 (counts and codes, never prices of a named order).

## Non-functional
A full run under 200 ms p95 excluding the broker reads; the broker reads of one run are issued in parallel and cached within their ages, at most 4 broker GETs per run (budgeted against the 5 per second read limit); the pure function under 1 ms. Observability: one log line per run (verdict, count of fails and unavailables, run id).

## Test plan
Unit: every check with pass, fail, boundary and each defect of each input. Properties (5,000 seeds): a doctored input set that makes any one Hard input unavailable never yields `Cleared`; adding a failing Hard check never turns `Rejected` into `Cleared`; the order of the 34 is fixed; negative control: a version that treats `Unavailable` as pass is caught on at least 10 seeds. Parity table with `PreTradeChecklist`. Integration: persistence, the read endpoint, the fresh-at-send race against a halt (the send is aborted). Hand fixtures (capital 500,000; profile risk 0.5%, daily loss 1.5% / 2 R, max total risk 3%): `risk_per_trade`: entry 1,500, stop 1,485, quantity 66 risks 990 and passes, 67 risks 1,005 and fails the INR cap of 1,000 although the profile allows 2,500; `daily_loss`: limit = min(2 x 2,500 = 5,000, 1.5% x 500,000 = 7,500) = 5,000: loss 4,999 passes, 5,000 fails; `ltp_collar`: entry 1,500, LTP 1,498 passes (0.13%), LTP 1,540 fails (2.6%); for a BUY with stop 1,485 and tick 0.05 the floor is 1,485.10: LTP 1,486 passes, LTP 1,485.05 fails; `circuit_band`: lower 1,350, upper 1,650, tick 0.05: entry 1,649.90 (upper - 2 ticks) passes, 1,649.95 fails; `spread_depth`: bid 1,499.90, ask 1,500.10, spread 0.20; the allowed spread is the larger of 3 ticks (0.15) and 0.25% of 1,500 (3.75), so 0.20 passes and 4.00 fails.

## Decisions (researched)
1. **A separate checklist in the Execution domain, not an extension of P7-05's.** `PreTradeChecklist` refuses every non-paper account with its first check and its private functions cannot be reused. Changing it would change an Approved, built slice and the paper path. Parity tests keep the two honest. Rejected: add a live mode to P7-05 (one function with two meanings). Source: `Domain/Checklist/PreTradeChecklist.cs` (opened 2026-10-24).
2. **Hard versus Soft.** Hard = anything about the broker, the money, the price and the system state: no override by anyone, because a human cannot judge them better in one tap. Soft = judgements about the instrument (sector, ban, results) where P7-05 already lets the owner approve despite. Reason: the owner's rule "unavailable never counts as a pass" (ADR 0091) and "human in the loop". `auto_live` has no human, so it takes every check as Hard.
3. **Exits and stops are not gated by entry checks.** A halt, a closed window or a loss limit must never prevent a risk-reducing order. Rejected: gating everything (a halted account that cannot exit is the worst case).
4. **Margin from the broker, no leverage assumed.** Dhan has `POST /v2/margincalculator` (fields `totalMargin`, `availableBalance`, `insufficientBalance`, `leverage`) and `GET /v2/fundlimit` (`availabelBalance`) (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/funds/, read 2026-10-24); the multi-order variant has inconsistent field names, so only the single-order call is used. The notional cap on the capital source stays so that a generous intraday leverage never sizes a position beyond the P7 plan.
5. **Circuit limits from the quote.** `/v2/marketfeed/quote` returns `upper_circuit_limit` and `lower_circuit_limit` and five depth levels (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/market-quote/, read 2026-10-24); the instrument master has no price-band column (https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/instruments/, read 2026-10-24). The collar `price_band_pct` 2.0 is doc 16's `execution.price_band_pct`.
6. **Freeze quantity.** NSE publishes freeze quantities for derivatives (secondary sources, e.g. https://www.5paisa.com/blog/nse-quantity-freeze-limits-index-derivatives, read 2026-10-24); a cash-equity per-order freeze exists in brokers' risk systems ("RMS:Rule: Check freeze quantity for NSE CASH", https://tradingqna.com/t/what-does-the-order-rejection-error-rms-rule-check-freeze-quantity-for-nse-cash-mean/24478, read 2026-10-24) but no primary source with numbers was found (UNVERIFIED). The own cap (2,000 shares, 100,000 rupees) is chosen for risk, not for the freeze; an exchange rejection of a smaller order is harmless. The unsliced rule (no slicing endpoint) means an order over a freeze is never split.
7. **Spread and depth thresholds are assumptions.** 3 ticks or 0.25% of LTP, and 2 times the quantity in five levels, are stated, not derived from the owner's data; they only make the system more restrictive and are the first numbers to review after the first 30 sessions (P12-12).
8. **BUY-only entries, one-share canary.** A short needs margin, borrow and unlimited-loss handling the stop logic does not model (and `oversold` is a fault, P12-01); entries are BUY only until P12-13. Until the owner records the canaries, `canary_cap` limits every entry to one share (a Hard check, not a rollout habit). Rejected: SELL entries allowed by the master's flag.

## Risks and honest limits
- Four Soft inputs have no source today; the practical effect is that only `semi_auto` with explicit owner confirmation can clear a ticket until P7-06 and P15 exist. That is intended.
- A check is only as good as its input: the quote may be a snapshot (Dhan quote limit is 1 request per second); a spike between the check and the broker's acceptance is possible (10-second validity, 2% collar, 1-second re-check).
- The limits protect one account of small size; they were not fitted to data.
- Daily loss uses LTP for unrealised results; a stale LTP is `unavailable` and rejects entries rather than hiding a loss.
- Margin figures are the broker's at one moment; a margin call later is not predicted.

## Amendments required by later specs (added in the 2026-10-24 consistency review)
- **Soft-only rejection is not final while a confirm ticket is open.** [P12-08](P12-08-semi-auto-one-tap-confirm.md) criterion 12 needs an intent whose latest run is `rejected` only by Soft checks to stay in `checking` while its ticket is open. The rule `checking>rejected` above therefore applies at once only when at least one Hard check fails or the account is not `semi_auto`; for a Soft-only failure in `semi_auto` the move waits for the ticket outcome (`skipped`/`expired` then rejects; `confirmed` writes the `check_confirmations` row and continues). Change criterion 7 and the state-machine test of P12-01 accordingly.
- **`static_ip_ok`:** Dhan's documentation lists configured IPs and modify dates, not whether orders are currently allowed. Until a probe proves otherwise, the check reads the owner's attestation recorded by [P12-11](P12-11-daily-login-token-and-static-ip-operations.md) D4.
- **Soft inputs:** the Soft checks that need smart-rule or event data have no source until P7-06 and P15; until then they are `unavailable`, which for `auto_live` is never a pass ([P12-09](P12-09-auto-live-within-hard-limits.md)).
