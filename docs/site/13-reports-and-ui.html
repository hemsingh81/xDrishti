1b. Clickable prototype#

A working front-end prototype of these screens lives in prototype/index.html (open it directly in a browser — offline, demo data, no backend; services and the live feed are simulated in the browser). It demonstrates the login (demo credentials prefilled); Accounts (list/detail tabs, role matrix, risk profiles, wizard, data failover); plan review with approve / modify / reject, the pre-trade checklist and auto-approval at the cutoff; suggestions inbox; portfolio with per-account tiles, Action center, price alerts, XIRR and animated grouping; instruments (one scrollable grid with sticky header and symbol column) & baskets; CSV import with aggregation; the Data quality window; the Learning screen (journey, lessons with loss tags, plan vs actual with fills ledger, setups, smart rules, tweak & re-run, autonomy, weekly digest); live tickets filled with simulated slippage and graded on fills; Reports (overview with auto-insights, basket comparison, pivot and 10 more reports); Strategy Lab; Services with schedule configuration, dependency checks, timeline and catch-up; settings with password re-entry; the audit log — responsive from phones to 4K monitors, light and dark.

Visual language (carried into the React app): Inter font and a standard, calm palette (Tailwind slate/gray neutrals with one indigo accent; green/red/amber only for meaning), a light sidebar with a soft colour-tinted icon per screen, tiles with tinted icons and count-up numbers, collapsible cards, animated dialogs/drawers/toasts (respecting reduce motion), light and dark themes, and scrollable data grids with sticky headers instead of pagination. In MUI this is one theme object (palette, shape, typography) shared by every screen.

Navigation for speed: a command palette (⌘K / Ctrl K or /) searches pages, Learning tabs, actions (run nightly, digest, lock, theme), baskets and every instrument (opens its chart drawer); g + letter jumps to a page (g t Today, g r Plan review, g p Portfolio, g l Learning …); ? lists all shortcuts; the browser tab title shows the current screen.

1c. Reports experience#

  • Landing = Overview: KPI tiles (trades, win rate, expectancy, total R & ₹, profit factor, max drawdown), auto-insights in plain sentences (doc 15 §6), equity & drawdown, monthly results, strategy × timeframe heatmap, expectancy by basket, R distribution, win rate by weekday.

  • Left-hand catalogue: Overview · Basket comparison · Strategies · Exit policies · Side & regime · Calendar & time · MAE/MFE · Calibration · Instruments · Accounts · Decisions · Pivot builder · Trade list.

  • Global filters: date range with presets, multi-select baskets, side, style, account.

  • Basket comparison: choose 2+ baskets → side-by-side KPIs (trades, win rate, expectancy, total R, profit factor, max drawdown, net ₹, best strategy, buy-and-hold return), overlaid cumulative-R curves, expectancy by strategy per basket, basket × side and basket × style heatmaps.

  • Pivot builder: any two dimensions (strategy, timeframe, basket, side, style, exit, regime, account, instrument, sector, weekday, month, decision) × metric (expectancy, total R, win rate, trades, profit factor).

  • Honest states (P8-01, ADR 0098): every figure on a report or Learning screen is drawn through the shared analytics kit (frontend/src/shared/analytics): a value, a provisional value (number plus the word and the reason), or a non-value shown as an icon and a word — Insufficient data (with n and the minimum), Not available, Not evaluable, No records yet, Loading, Could not load — never as a digit, a zero, a flat chart or the good colour. Beside a number sits the evidence (n, data range, gross or net basis, provisional, data used, limits).

  • Learning screen (P8-02, spec): read only, a lazy route with the tab, window and open cell in the address. Setups is a table of strategy rows by timeframe columns with one button per stored cell (never an average of cells): Enough cells print the expectancy with its sign, Not enough cells are hatched, labelled and print no figure, so a cell below the minimum trades never reads as a winner. The credible bound shown is the win-rate bound the cell stores; no interval on expectancy exists yet.

Prototype parity (P2-12)#

The prototype's spacing, type and colour were audited against the app (spec P2-12, Audit result) and brought into the theme (tokens.ts, componentsControls.ts, componentsSurfaces.ts, globalStyles.ts) and the shell (AppShell, TopBar, Sidebar, NavRow, PageHeader, StatTile), never per screen. The two large causes of the heavier look were the 16 px root (the scale is written for 14) and MUI 9 ignoring color="text.secondary" on Typography (now mapped in the theme). Values: page padding 14 / 24 / 28·36 / 32·48 by width; top bar 60 px; controls 36 px (small 30, large 44) with radius 10 (8); icon buttons 36 square; cards 12 px radius with the soft shadow, body 14 / 16, header 13 / 16; tonal pill chips (22 px); banners tinted with a 22–25 % hairline; segmented control tray; dialog bands on the surface with hairlines. Deliberately not copied: the prototype's 1560 px page cap (screens are full width), the 861–1200 px icon rail, hover lifts and native checkbox / switch sizes. The sign-in page is the prototype's two-panel layout without the status line.

Layout and shared list controls (P1-03a)#

Portfolio screen (P2-01 / P2-05)#

Route /portfolio (lazy chunk; nothing of it is in the initial bundle). Same viewport-height layout as Instruments from md up (header, filters, tiles and sync bar stay; the holdings list scrolls inside its card, loads further pages of 100 as it is scrolled and keeps the "Showing N of total" footer fixed; the page scrolls normally below md). All view state is in the URL: account, bucket, status (open default, closed, all), q, sort and holding (the open drawer); the summary tiles follow the account and bucket only, so loading more rows never changes them.

  • Scope: Account type-ahead (All accounts, or an account with the Portfolio role), Bucket type-ahead, a debounced search (symbol, name, ISIN) and an Open / Closed / All toggle. With "All accounts" and more than one account the list gets an Account column. Import, Sync now and Auto refresh act on one account: the chosen one, or the only one there is; with several accounts and "All accounts" they ask to pick one first. An account that is not connected shows "Connect the account first" with a link to its Connection tab, and its Import and Sync buttons are off (what is already stored stays visible).

  • Summary tiles (totals of the chosen account and bucket, from GET /api/holdings/summary): Invested · Current value (as of the valuation date) · Unrealised P&L ₹ with % · XIRR · Realised (est.) · Holdings (open, closed, not in master list). Gains and losses always carry a sign and an arrow (▲ +₹18,400, ▼ −₹12,000), never colour alone. An XIRR that cannot be shown is "—" with its reason (insufficient: needs 30+ days and money in and out; n/m: beyond ±1,000 %; no data: no price) as the tile hint and a tooltip. An unpriced holding (no usable price) shows a "No price yet" chip and dashes for value, P&L, % and XIRR (never −100 %), and the totals leave it out with the note "{n} holdings without a price are left out of value, P&L and XIRR."; an XIRR beyond ±1,000 % shows "n/m"; every reason is reachable by keyboard. Under the tiles: a chip "12 lots use the default date" (it lists the oldest purchase dates first, where the default dates are) and a footnote that these gross figures are price returns (while no dividend or charge is recorded) or that the net figures count dividends and costs only from the dates on their tiles (once some are); corporate actions are not included.

  • Income tiles (P2-14, built): a second row under the gross tiles from income of the summary: Dividends (the sum entered, "from " = the first dividend entered, "none entered yet" otherwise, and the unallocated part when there is one), Costs (trade charges plus the ledger's account-level costs, "from " = chargesFrom, "none read yet" otherwise), Net total return (with the return before costs and the date the net figures are complete from, coverageFrom = the later start of the sources that exist) and Net XIRR (with the reason when it cannot be shown, as for the gross XIRR). The gross tiles do not change; alpha, the benchmark and the value history say "price return, before dividends and charges". While nothing is recorded (income null) the row is replaced by an invitation — Dividends and costs are not in the figures yet, with Add a dividend (opens the Cash & dividends panel on the Add dividend form) and Read the ledger (takes the owner to the Sync trades & ledger control); zeros would claim there were none. With a bucket chosen the tiles count only the bucket's holdings' dividends and trade charges.

  • Sync bar: the last run in words ("Synced 5 min ago — 1 new, 1 closed"; a skipped run shows the reason the API recorded, e.g. "The account is not connected to Dhan."), the auto-refresh setting and next run ("Auto refresh: every 15 min in market hours (Mon–Fri 09:15–15:30 IST) · next run 09:30, 12 Oct IST"), Sync now (progress "Syncing…", "already running" and "connect the account first" shown in words, and the safeguard: when Dhan's answer would close all or most holdings the API answers 409 portfolio.confirmation_required and a dialog says "Dhan returned no holdings — 5 of 5 would be closed. Confirm only if that is right."; Confirm and sync sends the request again with confirm: true and the numbers it showed (expectedOpenHoldings, expectedWouldClose); if Dhan's answer changed meanwhile (409 portfolio.confirmation_stale) the dialog reopens with the fresh numbers and "Dhan’s answer changed since you looked — review again", never retrying by itself; the wording uses what Dhan listed: "Dhan listed 0 holdings while 5 are open" / "Dhan listed 2 holdings but 4 of your 6 open holdings are missing") and Auto refresh (dialog: Off · Every 5/15/30/60 minutes during market hours · Daily at HH:MM IST). The setting is one shared control, AutoRefreshSettings, also used in the account's Portfolio tab and the add-account wizard (default Off); it is saved with PUT /api/accounts/{id}/portfolio, sending only the refresh fields (the API keeps every field that is null or left out; the account tab sends "" for the account's own bucket). A refusal for a field the form has no place for is shown, never swallowed. A run that finished elsewhere (an automatic refresh) reloads the figures by itself (status polled every 60 s when auto refresh is on, every 3 s while a run is in progress).

  • Trades and ledger from Dhan (P2-13, built): under the sync bar of the chosen account (one line each, BrokerKindLine): "Trades read up to 11 Oct 2026 — 3 new" (with " · Dhan answered "no data" for N date ranges" only when more ranges answered no data than windows were read, i.e. windows were halved and still empty), "Ledger read up to … — nothing new", "Reading trades… 3 of 12 windows done" (a backfill takes minutes), "Trades read failed" with the reason in plain words and "Read up to …", "Trades read skipped" (an expired token on the scheduled read); not read yet: "Trades not read yet". Then "2 holdings rebuilt, 1 needs review (SYMBOL) — open the holding and use Rebuild lots" when the latest succeeded trades run rebuilt or left holdings (the figures and the tickers, needsReviewSymbols of the status, at most 50, come from that run and no other: nothing is carried forward, so a quiet night with the rebuild skipped shows none rebuilt, while a skipped, failed or running read neither changes nor erases them), and "Next scheduled read 16:30, 15 Oct IST" (or "No scheduled read is planned." when the service is paused). Next to it Read from an earlier date (P2-14, built) opens a dialog with one date field (between 2 Jan 1990 and today): the trades and the ledger are read from that date instead of from where the last read ended, so older costs and trades are stored too; days read before are read again and nothing is stored twice (already stored broker trades are also completed with an ISIN, ticker or charges they lacked, doc 12 §3 "Dhan sync"). Dhan documents no history depth: if it refuses the range or has nothing that old, the read says so in plain words in the trades and ledger lines, and a start the API refuses (one that would leave a gap) is shown at the date field in the API's words. The button Sync trades & ledger ("Reading…" while it runs) starts POST …/sync/broker-history, is off while the run or a holdings sync of the account runs or the account is not connected, and the status is polled while a run goes on, and a run that ends (also the evening read, or one started in another tab) makes the holdings, the trades, the ledger and (the Cash panel open) the dividend suggestions read again (P2-18: a new ledger entry can be one). A failed start or status shows the API's problem in words.

  • Sync health lines (P2-16, built): while the app deals with a failure by itself the sync lines say so in plain words and nothing is raised: "Holdings sync: retrying in 5 minutes (attempt 2 of 3)" under the sync bar and "· retrying in 5 minutes (attempt 2 of 3)" after the trades and ledger lines (a time that has come is "retrying now"; the numbers come from retryAt and attempts of the status). When no retry remains and the sync has failed for a day or more the line gives how long and why — "failed for 2 days: Dhan could not be reached" (reasons: could not be reached, asked for fewer requests, the run was interrupted, rejected the request, the history was too large to read at once, the Dhan login has expired, Dhan refused the login, the account is not connected, something unexpected went wrong; a code this version does not know is "an unknown problem") — and the same failure is an item in the bell once it has lasted two days (or at once when only the owner can fix it). The trades line ends with how far the nightly deepening reached ("History read back to 3 Mar 2023", "History read back to 3 Mar 2023 — no earlier history found" or "No earlier history found"); nothing is shown before it begins.   • Dropped rows on the sync line (ISS-03, doc 12 §3 "Dropped rows are explained"): when the latest run of the trades or the ledger dropped rows, its line says so with the reasons in plain words, for example "15 rows could not be read: 9 quantity missing or not a whole positive number, 4 no trade id, 2 time unreadable" (one phrase per reason code, the biggest first, at most three named and then "+n more"; a code this version does not know reads as "other"). When the only reason is a zero amount (no money moved, for example an opening balance) the line says only that such lines were ignored, quietly, and offers no Details. A Details disclosure under the line lists the first five rows by place - the dates of the request, the page and the row number - so the owner can find them in Dhan's own statement; it never shows a name, amount, id or narration. Nothing is shown when no row was dropped. The data is droppedByReason and droppedRows of the kind's status.   • Import files (P2-17, built; ADR 0029, doc 12 §3 "Dhan report imports (P2-17)"): a dialog over the Portfolio screen (?import=open, so Back and Close agree like the other panels) opened by Import files in the sync bar and in the Cash & dividends panel, and by the button a wrongly dropped report offers. Two tabs: Import files and History (the batches of trades, dividends and costs with Undo). The account is the page's (the owner picks one when the page shows all accounts). The flow is the one of the trade import, for several files at once:

    • Drop. One or several files at once (CSV, XLS or XLSX; at most 20, 10 MB each, 40 MB together) by dropping or choosing; the account is chosen above. The browser does not refuse a file by its extension or MIME type (only by the counts and sizes above): the server detects the kind from the content, and a file of no known kind is that card's error. Preview reads and stores nothing.
    • One card per file, with the detected kind as a chip (global transaction report, DP charges, demat holding, profit and loss, tradebook, dividend list …), summary tiles (rows, valid, to add, already imported, skipped, bad), the warnings in words and the block of the kind: for a transaction report the trades to add, the lots that would be rebuilt (hand-dated lots that would be replaced are said first), the rows skipped per reason (futures and options, commodities, currencies, BSE, covered by Dhan's own trades), the charges check against the report's total and the names — each scrip name with what it was taken to mean and the rule that decided; for DP charges the lines by status (new, ₹0, buy side, already imported, already in the ledger), the rupees that would be added to the costs and the names it teaches; for demat holding the securities that agree, the quantity differences, the securities held at Dhan but not synced and the synced holdings missing from the statement, marked informational — nothing is changed; a profit-and-loss report says it is a statement of results and is not imported; a dividend list says to use Import dividends and offers the button; a file that cannot be read shows its reason on its card, and the other cards are unaffected. States of a card: detected, needs a match, warnings, nothing new, imported, error.
    • Choices. A name that matched nothing or more than one security has a picker (the account's holdings, the instrument list, or an ISIN typed in) and Remember this match (ticked); the choices apply to every file of the batch and are read again with Preview again. A name left alone is imported without an ISIN and does not join a holding.
    • Another client code. A report whose client code differs from the one remembered for the account is flagged and imported only when its box is ticked.
    • Import and Undo. One Import for all files (each file all or nothing); a file whose preview went stale gets its fresh preview in place and is confirmed again, the others are done; every stored batch has Undo (asks first, says how many it voided), and importing the same file again stores nothing and says so.
    • Wrong place. The Trades and Cash & dividends imports send a Dhan report to Import files with a button, and a dividend list found among the files to Import dividends.

  • Cash & dividends (P2-13, built): a header button next to Trades opens a right-hand drawer (?cash=open; 920 px wide from md up, full width below) for one account (a select when several have the Portfolio role; the page's account when one is chosen). It opens with an info note: Dhan credits dividends to the bank account, so they rarely appear in the ledger and the owner enters them (or imports a dividend payout file, below); the dividends entered count in the net return and net XIRR, and so do the ledger's account-level costs only (DP charges, annual maintenance, delayed-payment interest, platform fees and the like); the other entries (brokerage, STT, GST, stamp duty, settlements) are shown only, because the trade charges are already counted from the trades (P2-18, R1: the note at the top of the panel says exactly this). Per-share amounts show their stored decimals - up to 4, never fewer than 2 (0.0125 reads "₹0.0125", 31.25 "₹31.25") - in the list of dividends entered and in the dividend import preview (formatInrPerShare; P2-18, R3). Dividends (P2-14, built) come first, for the chosen account: the suggested dividends (ledger entries of class dividend, usually none; Accept asks for the holding or none, Dismiss hides it; both final) and Dividends you entered (every holding's and the unallocated ones, newest first, voided ones flagged and kept in the list) with Add dividend — a dialog with account, holding (or none: unallocated), pay date (not in the future), amount, per share and note, checked here and by the API at their fields; the same account, holding, date and amount as an existing dividend asks "add it again?" first (409 portfolio.possible_duplicate, repeated with confirm); Void asks first and the dividend stops counting but stays listed. Then the ledger: a line saying from which day it is stored ("Ledger read from .   • Import dividends (P2-15, built; ADR 0028, doc 12 §3 "Dividend import (P2-15)"): the Dividends part of the Cash & dividends panel has an Import dividends button and a drop zone for a CSV file; the dividends go to the panel's chosen account (one file per account). The flow is the one of the trade import — choose the file, preview, confirm, result with Undo — in a dialog that shows nothing stored until the owner confirms:

    • Preview. The detected format (a dividend payout list), summary tiles (rows read, valid rows, rows to add with their rupees, already imported, possible duplicates, names needing a match, bad rows), the rupees to add by year, and notices in words: payments listed twice in the file that are counted once (with the lines, and the hint to add the second by hand if it is real); rows whose per-share × quantity differs from the amount paid (the paid amount is used); and dividends dated before the account's first lot, with a button to the Read from an earlier date dialog, because the net XIRR is only as good as the history behind it. Tables, each with its count: To add (date, name as the file wrote it, the holding or instrument it was matched to with the rule that matched it as a word-chip — remembered, ISIN, symbol, same name, part of the name, instrument list — a mark asking to check matches made by part of the name, amount, warnings), Already imported, Possible duplicates (equal to a dividend typed in; unticked, tick to import anyway), Needs a match (names that matched nothing or fit more than one holding) and Bad rows (line and reason, the first 200 and how many in all). Long tables are paged, 50 rows at a time (a page control under each table): a file can have 20,000 rows. Names are shown as text, never markup.
    • Choices. Every name that matched nothing or more than one holding has a holding picker (type-ahead over the account's holdings and the instrument list, and "keep unallocated"); Remember this match is ticked by default and saves the choice for the account, so the next file with that name needs no question (nothing already imported changes). A name left alone is imported unallocated with the file's name: it counts in the account and total figures, not in a holding, bucket or sector.
    • Confirm and result. The confirm step says how many dividends and how many rupees will be stored and how many names will be remembered; the file is sent again with the preview's digest. If holdings, remembered names or dividends changed meanwhile the dialog shows the fresh preview with a notice that things changed since the owner looked, and asks again — nothing is stored. The result shows what was stored (allocated to a holding, to an instrument only, unallocated), what was skipped (already imported, possible duplicates) and the names remembered, with Undo (it voids the batch's dividends, asks first, and says how many); importing the same file again stores nothing and says so.
    • History. The imports of the account, newest first: file name, who and when, counts, rupees, status (Undone flagged); Undo on each one still in force. The Dividends you entered list marks a dividend read from a file with a CSV chip and shows its quantity and, for an unallocated one, the name the file used; voiding one by hand works as for any other.
    • Wrong kind of file. A dividend list dropped on the Trades import says it is a dividend file and shows a button that opens Import dividends for the same account; a tradebook with no buy/sell column (a sideless Dhan tradebook export) shows the explanation — it cannot be read because purchases cannot be told from sales — with the ways forward (export one that has a side, or Sync trades & ledger); a tradebook dropped on Import dividends points back to the Trades import; since P2-17 a Dhan report (transaction report, DP charges, demat holding) dropped on either import shows a button to Import files, and a profit-and-loss statement is said to be not imported. Any other unreadable file shows the API's message, which names the missing columns and the accepted header names.

The hub contract (methods, events, payloads, limits) is in doc 03 §6 and generated into frontend/src/shared/realtime/hubContract.generated.ts.

User button Avatar (28 px, accentSoft background, accent 13 px bold initials), name (from lg), chevron; menu with name, email, Sign out. Real
Market open + NIFTY pill, services-health pill Shown in the prototype. Later: need market data (P1-11 / P1-12 / P2-07) and the service framework (P1-13); not faked.

Notifications and pop-ups (P2-16, ADR 0027)#

What the app can mend by itself it mends quietly (doc 12 §3 "Self-healing and attention items (P2-16)"); what only the owner can do arrives once, as an item in the bell and as a pop-up, each with a button that goes straight to the fix, and goes away by itself when the cause is gone.

  • The bell and its drawer. The bell reads GET /api/portfolio/notices every 60 seconds beside the data, service and account notices and shows an unread badge (max "9+") that does not count resolved items. The right-hand drawer (400 px, full width on phones) has two groups: Unresolved — the open items, most urgent first (error, warning, information), newest first within a level — and a collapsed Resolved (n) group of what was resolved in the last 24 hours (greyed, labelled "Resolved", with no buttons; the group header shows only when there is something resolved). Each item has a severity chip (icon and a hidden word, never colour alone), a bold title, the text, the relative time and at most two buttons (the first filled, the second outlined; "Retry now", "Reconnect Dhan", "Review", "Open trades", "Open"); a notice without buttons keeps its Open link. "Mark all read" and the empty state "You're all caught up" are as before. A button marks the item read first; a route button navigates (and closes the drawer), a command button calls the existing endpoint it names (POST /api/accounts/{id}/sync/broker-history …) with the session and CSRF token, keeps the drawer open and waits on that button, then reads the notices and the screens again and says the result in a toast ("Retry now: started. This notice goes away by itself once it works." or the API's own words when it refused, e.g. a run is already going). The browser follows only a route that is a path inside the app (one slash, no host) and only a command that is a POST to one of the whole-path patterns of the server's one list of commands - /api/accounts/{guid}/sync/broker-history, /api/accounts/{guid}/holdings/sync and /api/services/{id}/run - anything else is dropped, never followed. The list is not copied by hand: shared/api/commands.ts builds its allow-list from shared/api/attentionCommands.generated.ts, which npm run gen:api writes from the x-xdrishti-attention-commands extension of the API contract (doc 12 §3 "Self-healing and attention items"; P2-18, ADR 0030). A holdings Retry now cannot confirm a sync that would close all or most holdings: the refusal is shown with a pointer to Sync now on the account's Portfolio tab. Read state stays per browser (localStorage); it is keyed on the id plus since for the seven server attention kinds only (reconnect_dhan, sync_failing, holdings_need_review, trades_unexplained, dividends_pending, securities_unmatched, and since P2-18 rows_dropped), so a condition that clears and returns is unread again; every other notice (price alerts, 'holdings need a decision', the API-key expiry) is keyed on its plain id, which already changes with the situation or the day. The server sends Since = null for the 'holdings need a decision' notice (its At still moves on each poll: cosmetic). Items of the account notices that the browser still derives itself are the API-key expiry (key_expiring, key_expired); the Dhan login (missing, expired, about to expire) is the server's reconnect_dhan.
  • Where the buttons lead (deep links). /accounts?account={id}&tab=connection (Reconnect Dhan, Connect to Dhan) and …&tab=portfolio (a failing sync's Open), /services (a failing price poll's Open), /portfolio?holding={id}&section=trades (a holding open on its Trades section) with &rebuild=preview (the rebuild preview open too; both parameters are taken out of the URL once the section has scrolled into view, so the link can be used again), /portfolio?cash=suggestions&account={id} (the Cash & dividends panel with the keyboard on the suggested dividends), /portfolio?focus=sync (P2-18, a rows_dropped item's Open: the keyboard goes to Sync trades & ledger, or - when that button is off because a read runs or the account is not connected - to the sync bar itself, or - with several accounts and "All accounts", where a read belongs to one account - to the Account picker; the parameter is taken out of the URL once it has been handled, the others stay), /portfolio?unmatched=1&account={id} (the holdings with no match in the instrument master, with a banner and Show all holdings), and the older /portfolio?alerts=triggered, /portfolio?focus=actions, ?trades=import|history, ?cash=open|add|import and ?import=open (Import files).
  • One toast provider. app/layout/AppShell owns the single ToastProvider of the app, so a toast raised anywhere — a page, a dialog, the bell, a background check — shows in one stack (a test covers /system, /settings and /services); the attention pop-ups are mounted beside it. useToast().notify(message, severity, actionOrOptions) keeps its form and gains the options title, actions (up to two buttons), persistent, id (notifying again with the same id replaces the toast in place and restarts its time) and durationMs; dismiss(id) closes one.
  • The stacking toast. Up to three show at once, newest at the bottom: in a column at the bottom right from md (400 px wide) and the full width above the bottom edge and the safe area on phones; a fourth makes the oldest leave — a timed one before a persistent one. Look as in the visual system above (accent bar, tint, icon chip, title, message, buttons in the severity colour, a close button) plus a thin countdown bar for a timed toast; under prefers-reduced-motion nothing slides and there is no bar, the toast simply stays its full time. Timed toasts stay 5 s (10 s with a button); a persistent one — the pop-up of an error item — stays until it is closed, one of its buttons is pressed or its item is resolved. Hovering (a mouse, not a touch) or focusing a toast pauses every countdown, Escape closes the newest, and a toast is announced once, as alert (error, warning) or status (success, information). The keyboard rules of the undo toasts are kept: an action started with the keyboard moves focus to its button and gives it back when the toast closes (the place to give it back to is remembered only while an open toast holds focus - when none does, for example a toast timed out with focus elsewhere, it is forgotten, so the next toast never sends focus to a stale place; P2-18, R5), and Ctrl/Cmd+Z runs the newest undo while the toast is open (not while typing in a field). The stack sits in its own portal and stays announced above an open dialog.
  • Attention pop-ups. AttentionPopups (mounted once in the shell, below the provider and the router) pops up an item the first time it appears while the app is open and it is unread — title, text and the item's buttons; an error one is persistent. A button marks the item read, then navigates or runs the command; a toast closes by itself when its item is resolved, gone or read in the bell. What popped up is remembered in this browser apart from "read" (xd.notifications.popped), so a reload does not show it again; when the app opens only the most urgent unread item pops up — the rest are remembered as seen and wait in the bell. The kinds that pop up are the seven server items and the API-key expiry (attentionKinds); price-alert and decision notices wait in the bell. A rows_dropped item is a warning (or information when only zero amounts were dropped), so its pop-up is a timed one, not persistent; its text is the server's (counts and the plain words of the reasons, doc 12 §3), and its single button Open goes to /portfolio?focus=sync.

3. Exports & delivery#

Plan: page + PDF/HTML. Portfolio: CSV/Excel of positions, transactions and realised P&L. Any report: CSV/Excel. Weekly review pack (PDF): R3, R4, R5, R12, R13, R16 + assistant summary. Notifications appear in-app (SignalR); optional external channel only if you enable one.

4. Implementation notes#

  • Report queries live in the .NET Reporting module; heavy aggregates via SQL materialised views refreshed nightly; responses are chart-ready series.
  • Lightweight Charts for anything price-based; ECharts for analytics.
  • The SPA is a PWA so it can be "installed" on your phone (LAN access).
