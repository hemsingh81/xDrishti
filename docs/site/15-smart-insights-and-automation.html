15 — Smart Insights & Automation

How xDrishti turns data into decisions you can act on, for both trading and portfolio management — without taking decisions away from you. Everything here is deterministic rules and statistics evaluated by the services; the LLM only explains (doc 14).

3. Portfolio Action center#

Built in P2-09 (ADR 0022): evaluated on read, not by a service. GET /api/portfolio/actions computes the items from the current holdings, lots, prices, sectors, baskets and benchmark with pure functions (one rule = one function over a read model), exactly like the summary and the groups, so the card is always consistent with the numbers beside it and there is no job to run, lag or fail. Only the owner's decisions are stored (portfolio.action_states). Thresholds are configurable (Settings → Action center thresholds; stored as JSON in platform.app_settings, fixed ranges, Restore defaults, audited). Seven rules are built; Max holding exceeded (needs trading buckets and plans, P7) and Results due (needs events data, P15) are not, and the per-account tiles of doc 12 §10 are not part of this slice (Group by Account covers the figures).

Rule Default trigger Suggested action
Below cost Unrealised ≤ −10% (high if ≤ −20%) Hold with a stop alert, average only if the thesis holds, or exit
Large gain Unrealised ≥ +25% Book part of the gain or set a trailing-stop alert
Overweight Weight > max(15%, 1.5 × equal-weight share) of the account (≥ 3 positions) Trim or rebalance
Sector concentration One sector > 35% of the account Diversify
Max holding exceeded (not built yet) Trading-bucket position older than the 5-session limit Exit per plan rules
Results due (not built yet) Results within 7 days Decide before the event
Long-term soon Profitable holding becomes > 12 months within 30 days Check tax impact before selling early (informational only)
Lagging Nifty Held > 180 days and XIRR more than 10 pp below the same cash flows in Nifty 50 Review the thesis / replace
Not in a basket Holding not assigned to any basket Assign for basket analytics

Each item offers Open, Set alert, Add to basket, Snooze 7 days or Done; snooze, done and reopen are audited. Per-account tiles on the Portfolio page (value, P&L, XIRR, day change, top holding, number of open action items) come later; for now the Action center takes the Portfolio screen's account and bucket scope.

How the built rules behave (P2-09). Severity, text and numbers are recomputed on every read; an item has a stable, path-safe id ({rule}:{holdingId}, or sector_concentration:{accountId}:{12 hex of SHA-1 of the lower-cased sector}). Holdings without a usable price never fire a price-based rule (they are counted as "no price"). Overweight counts only priced positions and needs at least min_positions (3) of them in the account; Sector concentration is one item per account and sector, "Unclassified" never fires, and the item lists the share and the largest holding; Long-term soon uses calendar dates (India): a lot is 12 months old on the same date a year later (29 February → 28 February) and the text only asks to check the tax impact; Lagging Nifty compares the holding's XIRR with the XIRR of the same cash flows put into the benchmark index, and a holding whose benchmark has no bars from its first flow (or whose XIRR is not meaningful) is not evaluated and counted as unavailable, never as fine; Not in a basket ignores holdings that are not in the instrument master.

Order. Items are sorted by severity, then by the rule (below cost, lagging Nifty, overweight, sector concentration, large gain, long-term soon, not in a basket), then by the size of the trigger within the rule — the sizes of different rules are in different units (percent, rupees, days) and are never compared. Long-term soon lists a lot that cost nothing (bonus or transferred-in shares) as profitable, with no gain percentage.

Decisions. Snooze hides an item until the India date 7 days later; Done hides it for as long as the condition holds and is then set to cleared (an UPDATE — the runtime role has no DELETE) so a later recurrence fires again. The reconciliation runs after every quotes tick and every holdings sync, after every change of the thresholds, and opportunistically inside the Action-center read (the one write a read makes — idempotent, and a failure there never fails the read); edits of baskets and lots are reconciled at the next tick, sync or read. The tick, the sync and the threshold write look at every active Portfolio account, so a done decision about a holding that is no longer open (sold, or its account left the Portfolio) is cleared too — the same holding row is reopened by a later sync and its item must start open. The read of one scope cannot tell a closed holding from one outside its scope and leaves such decisions alone. A decision about something the evaluation could not judge (no benchmark bars, no price) is left alone. Two identical decisions sent at once are both successful (the second finds its decision stored).

4. Price alerts#

  • Conditions: price at or below / at or above a level (quick presets ±5/10/20%), optional note.
  • Evaluated by services: alert symbols join the polled set (or the live set if already streamed); triggered alerts create a notification and audit entry. Alerts survive logout and browser close.
  • Later: indicator alerts (e.g. close below 50 DMA), trailing alerts, alert on basket-level drawdown.

Built in P2-09 (ADR 0022). An alert is a master-list NSE share or ETF (not retired, quotable by Dhan; indices and futures are out of scope), a direction, a level (above 0, at most 10,000,000, 4 decimals), an optional note (200 characters of plain text) and a status active → triggered | cancelled; never deleted, at most 200 active. It is one-shot. The holdings-quotes service quotes the union of the open holdings and the active alerts' instruments in the same request (still at most 1,000 per call) and checks every active alert against the last price of that tick: at_or_below ⇒ last ≤ level, at_or_above ⇒ last ≥ level. A spike between two ticks is not seen, so the latency is up to one tick (5 minutes, market hours) — doc 15's "every minute" returns with the live feed (xd-feed). An alert that is hit becomes triggered with the trigger price and time in the same transaction as its audit event, by a conditional update on status = 'active' (so it triggers once even with overlapping runs); an instrument with no quote in the answer stays active; a failed run triggers nothing; the run message adds "N alerts triggered". Creation refuses a condition that already holds at the last known price (the holding's last price, else the last stored daily close if it is at most 5 trading days old; unknown: allowed) with the price in the message. The bell shows price_alert_triggered (warning, for 7 days after triggering) and actions_need_decision (information, at most once a day) from GET /api/portfolio/notices; the bell polls it every minute, so the count of open items is kept for at most 60 seconds in the portfolio cache (dropped at once by the writes that drop the cached histories, and by decisions and threshold changes), and a failure to count never hides the alert notices. Since P2-10 (ADR 0024) the alerts are also checked on every live flush (every 2 s while xd-feed is connected, with the live price and its time; pre-open ticks trigger nothing), so the latency above applies only when the feed is off; the conditional update keeps a trigger single when both checks run at once, and the audit actor is system:live-feed for the feed. Since P2-16 (ADR 0027) the same endpoint also carries the attention items (reconnect Dhan, a sync that keeps failing, holdings to review, trades that do not explain a holding, possible ledger dividends, unmatched securities and, since P2-18, rows a Dhan read dropped), each with a title, at most two actions and a resolved flag, and the alert and decision notices get a title and an Open action too (doc 12 §3 "Self-healing and attention items (P2-16)").

5. Data quality gate#

After every end-of-day fetch the Data validation service re-checks the last N sessions of every tracked instrument (doc 05 §10b). Results drive:

  • a data quality score (pass = 1, warning = ½) shown on the Data quality page and Today,
  • per-instrument fail-closed: failing instruments are skipped by the nightly scan and flagged on any ticket,
  • one-click repairs (re-fetch the day, refresh aggregates) followed by automatic re-validation.

6. Report auto-insights#

Computed from graded trades for the current filters (minimum 15 trades per group): best and weakest strategy (pause candidate), best vs worst basket gap, long vs short bias, best exit policy, most/least favourable regime, best/worst weekday, calibration quality (predicted vs actual win rate). The local LLM can turn these into the weekly review text; numbers always come from the report engine.

7. Schedule intelligence#

  • Rules checked on every schedule change: pre-open check after the review cutoff and before 09:15 (and ideally after NSE pre-open price discovery ~09:08); EOD fetch after 15:35; nightly pipeline after EOD fetch and portfolio sync; live window covering the session; no backups in market hours; no circular "run after" chains. Errors block saving; warnings are shown.
  • Run-after chains (e.g. data validation after EOD fetch; AI suggestions after the nightly pipeline).
  • Catch-up of missed runs after the machine wakes; automatic data failover to the standby Data account.
