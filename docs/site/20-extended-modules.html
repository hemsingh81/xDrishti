A. Automated order placement#

Modes per account: paper → manual_live → assisted (system prepares exact order / super-order parameters) → semi_auto (one-tap confirm in UI) → auto_live (within hard limits, every action notified). As built (P12-07, ADR 0163): only paper, manual_live and assisted exist; the mode is an insert-only trail, up one step at a time by the owner (live-writes gate, then a fresh password, a cooling-off of two to five sessions, the evidence gate evaluated again at the confirm) and down at any time (the owner without a password, the system when the Trading role is off or the account archived). The gate to assisted needs a sandbox suite result and a static-IP attestation that no module stores yet, so it is closed and nothing is promotable. The assisted sheet, semi_auto and auto_live are later slices; nothing here can send. As built (P12-08a, ADR 0165): the routing table (ModeRouting) is total over every mode and action class: semi_auto waits for the tap on a BUY entry and acts on its own for the reducing classes, auto_live acts on its own, StopLoosen, AddToPosition, ReEntry and OutsidePlan are refused in every mode. The intent creator turns an owner-approved ticket with an allocation for an account that may act into one intent (planned, armed from 09:10, checking once on the first crossing inside 09:20 to 15:00 while ready and not halted; otherwise cancelled with the reason, no catch-up; void if the price already ran past the collar; one share while the canary is pending). semi_auto is still not storable and the creator is composed by no host, so no intent exists today.

As built (P12-08b, ADR 0166): the confirm ticket is a server-written record (execution.confirm_tickets): exact text and its SHA-256, the entry's client order id, reference price, notional, worst case at the stop, a 90-second life (exec.confirm_ttl_s) and the password rule; one decision per ticket by the primary key of execution.ticket_outcomes, whose trigger admits confirmed only from the owner through the API, before the expiry by the database clock, with the switch ON, semi_auto, no halt and the day unlocked (execution.day_unlocks, fresh password, until 15:30 India time). A price beyond 0.30 % or a changed Soft set closes the ticket refused (one re-issue); the expiry loop in xd-exec closes unanswered tickets. IConfirmGate hands a confirmed entry to submitting only within 30 seconds, else the intent is cancelled expired_after_confirm. Routes: GET /api/execution/confirm-tickets, POST .../{id}/confirm, POST .../{id}/skip, GET|POST /api/execution/day-unlocks. The issuer, the stored gate and the hand-off are composed by no host and no account can be semi_auto, so no ticket exists today; the frontend card is a separate pass.

As built (P12-09, ADR 0167): auto_live sends only inside a per-account limits sheet (execution.limits, insert-only snapshots; an unset cap, empty allow-list or no window allows nothing; ceilings 20 orders a day, 10 open positions, 50 symbols, 09:16 to 15:00, EQ_MIS only). A raise needs the password and takes effect at 09:00 India time after one full closed session; a lowering is in force at once. Every system-sent entry (in semi_auto too) is one execution.ledger_entries row whose trigger proves orders a day, notional per order and per day, window, allow-list and product under an advisory lock per account, and requires its execution.action_log row in the same transaction; every automatic action and refusal is a row with its codes and plain text. The guard (AutoLiveGuard) runs only with auto_live_enabled and execution_enabled ON (else mode.not_auto, nothing written) and refuses on the worst-case loss cap, a non-owner approval, a provisional plan, a quantity above the plan, a duplicate exposure, a strategy's first live use (routed to a ticket), failed pre-trade checks, no day unlock, an unattended owner, readiness, a stale feed (15 s), a reconciliation break or a halt. The loss-cap halt asks P12-05 for loss_cap. Routes: GET /api/execution/limits, PUT /api/execution/limits/{accountId}. Composed nowhere and no account can be auto_live, so nothing new can send.

  • As built (P12-01 and P12-01b, ADR 0155, ADR 0156): the machine has 14 states (submitting before the call and closing for our own exit in flight are added to the diagram) and exactly 23 legal moves; the same 23 pairs are a function and a check constraint in schema execution, so the database refuses what the Domain refuses. Events are insert-only with a chain (seq, prev_event_id); intent_state is a projection moved by trigger in the same transaction and equals the fold of the events. xd-api instructs the service only by inserting rows into execution.commands; the service answers each once in command_results. No broker call exists yet (P12-02).
  • Broker-side stop first — protection never depends on the Mac staying up. As built (P12-04, ADR 0160): the invariant is judged from the broker's order book (only a PENDING stop leg counts; unavailable never counts as protected); an unprotected position past exec.protect_deadline_s is a CRITICAL protection_breach that halts new entries, is repaired once and, by default, flattened with reduce-only marketable limits; a stop only tightens; the target is an engine rule, never a second resting sell; a negative net is oversold (freeze, nothing sent). Insert-only alarms; GET /api/execution/alarms. Built and tested on the FakeBroker; no host composes it yet; the Dhan wire for stop and super orders is UNVERIFIED.
  • Pre-trade checks: same limits as planning + price band vs LTP, freeze quantity, margin. As built (P12-03, ADR 0159): 34 checks in a fixed order, 26 Hard (no override by anyone) and 8 Soft (the owner may confirm in semi_auto only, bound to the exact Soft set). Gates: gate_open, no_halt, broker_kill_switch_off, token_valid, static_ip_ok, session_window, feed_fresh, streams_alive. Ticket and shape: ticket_valid, levels_valid, quantity_valid, product_segment (entries BUY only), instrument_tradable. Prices: ltp_collar, circuit_band, spread_depth, freeze_quantity. Money and risk: margin_funds, risk_per_trade, exposure_headroom, open_positions, entries_today, daily_loss, one_per_symbol, order_budget, canary_cap. Soft: sector_exposure, holdings_conflict, adv_share, results_within_days, fno_ban, surveillance, correlation, data_quality. A missing, impossible or stale input is unavailable, which never passes. Exits and protective stops are never judged by this list.
  • Idempotent client order ids; order-update stream + end-of-day reconciliation; mismatch ⇒ freeze. As built (P12-06, ADR 0162): the poll (order book, trade book, positions) is the truth and the order-update stream only the speed; streams_alive is true only within 15 s of a good poll with no gap fill owed. Reconciliation compares broker and record per account and returns one of eight breaks, each a row: position_mismatch (net differs after explained foreign trades), trade_missing (a broker trade of our order not in fills at its second sighting), trade_extra (a fill the broker does not hold), order_mismatch (status class or quantity differs beyond the grace), fill_mismatch (quantity, or price at four decimals), unknown_xd_order (our prefix at the broker with no leg), foreign_conflict (a foreign working order in an instrument we manage), unavailable (something could not be read). All but the foreign conflict ask for a freeze of that account; the foreign conflict asks for entries and a critical alarm. Nothing is ever corrected automatically; the owner resolves a break (accept_broker or dismiss_foreign) with the live-writes gate, a fresh password and an owner actor, and a freeze from a break can be released only when every break of the account has a resolution. Recovery after a crash looks unknown legs up by client id, reads the broker, and only then writes; it sends nothing. Built and tested on the FakeBroker and an in-process fake transport; no socket client exists and nothing is composed by a host.
  • Kill switch: ours (stop new, cancel pending, optional exit-all) + broker's where available; triggered by max drawdown, daily loss, feed loss or a button. As built (P12-05, ADR 0161): halts are an insert-only record with four levels (entries, cancel, exit_all, freeze) and a 25-cell permission table; a halt only removes permissions and the entry gate reads the record on every check (an unreadable record is halted). Starting a halt needs a session and no password (exit_all needs the step-up window); releasing one is the owner's act with the live-writes gate, a fresh password, a reason and the release rules, and a few state-of-the-world triggers (feed loss, clock skew, broker outage, protection unknown) clear themselves after a quiet period. Cancel and exit-all reuse the protection logic; the broker's own switch is a port that nothing implements yet; the drill sends nothing. Built and tested on the FakeBroker; the entry gate's source stays closed and no host composes the enforcer.
  • Prerequisites: ISP static IP whitelisted at Dhan; daily login/2FA; ≤ 2 orders/s; Dhan sandbox testing first.

B. Tick & order-book data#

  • Dhan live feed + 20-level depth (up to 50 instruments/connection) and 200-level depth (NSE equity & derivatives) over WebSocket (binary). No historical depth from brokers ⇒ record our own. Built in P7-16 (ADR 0135): the 20-level book of up to 50 shares, about once a second, in market.depth_snapshots; off by default behind three gates; nothing reads it until P13. Built in P7-16b (ADR 0173): the last-trade updates (receive time, raw exchange time, price in paise, last quantity, cumulative volume) of the same set in market.trade_ticks, a row only when the cumulative volume rises, from a second Quote-mode socket to the allowed live-feed host; the source of P13's aggressive volume delta (classified against the recorded book, not stored); its own 12 GB cap, the shared 20 GB free floor; off by default behind three gates; nothing reads it until P13.
  • Extends the v1 xd-feed (which already streams the active set): adds 20-level depth subscriptions for a configured recording list; writes to Timescale hypertables (compressed); ~50–100 GB/year.
  • Start recording in P7 so ≥ 6 months exist by P13.
  • Uses: order-book imbalance, depth pressure near trigger, aggressive volume delta, spread → meta-model features and realistic slippage. Kept only if OOS improves.
  • One comparison core (P13-05, ADR 0183): the depth and tick features (md-1) are judged by the same code as the filings features of §D — model A (tf-1) against model B (tf-1 plus the group) on identical rows, monthly folds here, a paired block bootstrap, margins widened for the ledger count.
  • HFT/latency arbitrage not pursued: home latency vs co-located firms, order-rate limits and costs leave no retail edge.

C. Options & statistical arbitrage#

  • Data: Dhan option chain API (OI, Greeks, IV, bid/ask; ~1 request / 3 s per underlying-expiry) and expired options data API for backtests; own chain snapshots (5-min for indices, EOD for stocks).

  • Chain recorder (built, P14-02, ADR 0176): options.chain_snapshots (strike by strike with bid, ask, last, OI, volume, IV and Greeks as Dhan sends them, prices in paise, a missing quote null with a flag), chain_requests (every planned request and how it ended) and chain_sessions; the index chains every 5 minutes of the session (09:20-15:30) and the stock chains once a day (14:30-15:25); two new allow-listed endpoints, one request per 3 seconds at most; off by default behind three gates, its own 4 GB cap and the shared 20 GB free floor; compressed, insert-only, nothing reads it yet.

  • Expired-options backfill (built, P14-03, ADR 0177): options.rolling_bars (five years of 5-minute NIFTY and BANKNIFTY and 15-minute share option bars at ATM offsets, rolling contracts, prices in paise, a missing value null) and options.fetch_log (every attempt of every key: the resume point and the honest coverage); one new allow-listed endpoint, one request a second at most, only between 16:30 and 08:30; off by default behind the deployment flag, the owner's switch, the owner's acceptance of an owner-run probe and a paused service, its own 3 GB cap and the shared 20 GB free floor; compressed, insert-only, nothing reads it yet.

  • Archive quality (built, P14-04, ADR 0178): options.quality_runs and options.quality_results (insert-only) hold one verdict per underlying, session and check - coverage, put-call parity, IV and Greek sanity, volume and open-interest jumps, rolling-series stitching - as pass, warn, fail or unavailable with a reason (unavailable never counts as pass); the service options-quality runs daily at 16:15 and is bounded per run; GET /api/options/coverage gives the statuses, the usable flags and the cumulative usable chain sessions (the number the spread model of P14-07 compares with 60). The first reader of the archive; aggregates only; nothing trades on it.

  • Reference data (built, P14-01, ADR 0175): options.contracts (the contracts of the listed underlyings from the daily master refresh, never deleted), options.lot_sizes (dated lot history) and options.market_epochs (dated rule changes); every seed is unconfirmed until the owner checks it against the circular; the list is the app setting options.underlyings (doc 06 §2.1).

  • Option pricing core (built, P14-05, ADR 0191): pure Black-76 on a forward (Black-Scholes as its spot form), an implied-volatility solver, Greeks, the parity forward and a closed list of unavailable reasons; no reader yet.

    Quantity Unit
    Prices rupees (double); paise only at the caller's edge, rounded half away from zero
    sigma fraction per year
    T (expiry date 15:30 India time minus now) / (365 x 86,400)
    Delta, gamma per rupee of spot (F = S e^((r-q)T))
    Vega per 1 volatility point (sigma + 0.01)
    Theta per calendar day (annual / 365)
    Vendor IV read as percent / 100, label percent_assumed (UNVERIFIED until the P14-02 pilot)
    Model price labelled bs_fallback by its caller

  • Option cost and liquidity model (built, P14-07, ADR 0194): optional option and futures lines on the owner's effective-dated charge sheet (absent: the leg is gross, labelled costs: none (no option lines), never zero), a half-spread measured from the usable chain sessions once there are 60 (research.option_spread_models and research.option_spread_buckets, insert-only, content-addressed; xd options spread-model build), else the owner's labelled assumption, else spread_unavailable; rupee liquidity filters (a null open interest or volume is liquidity_unknown) and the lot-feasibility count (not_sizable, lot_unknown) kept apart from the research unit. No decision, live or execution code reads any of it yet.

  • Pairs core (built, P14-12, ADR 0192): pure Domain/Pairs - log-price spread, OLS hedge ratio, the Engle-Granger residual test with one pre-registered lag (default 1), MacKinnon (2010) finite-sample critical values and the MacKinnon (1994) asymptotic p-value (labelled asymptotic), half-life, return correlation, z-score, Benjamini-Hochberg (an unavailable test counts in m with p = 1) and the entry/exit/stop state machine (an unavailable z holds); closed reasons, no reader yet (P14-13 is the first).

  • Pairs study (built, P14-13, ADR 0196): same-sector F&O cash pairs (today's sector label; ASM/GSM-flagged shares excluded; ban_list: unavailable) are formed each calendar quarter on the 252 daily closes before it - candidates at return correlation >= 0.8 (at most Pairs:MaxCandidates, 2,000), every candidate appended to the test ledger before any test, Engle-Granger and Benjamini-Hochberg over all candidates, half-life 1 to 20 sessions - and traded intraday by the separate walker pair-1 on 5-minute spreads (z against the previous 5 or 10 sessions, entry at 1.5, 2.0 or 2.5 and below the 3.5 stop, exit at 0.5, no entry after 14:45, flat at 15:15, both legs filled at the next minute by the simulator's fill and slippage rules, sim-9 untouched). Four fills of intraday cash are charged by the owner's sheet (Pairs:CostSheetId), else the trade is gross and labelled. R is the net rupees over Y notional x (3.5 - |z|) x sd(W). xd pairs run --from --to [--variant e,L] and xd pairs eval --family pairs:intraday-1 [--holdout] store research.pair_formations, research.pair_trades and protocol S verdicts in research.extension_evaluations (fold unit quarter, GET /api/research/extensions?family=pairs:intraday-1): NOT_EVALUABLE below 6 evaluable quarters or 300 pooled trades; KEEP needs the day- and 5-session-block lower bounds of mean R at 0.05 R x Factor(N) (N counts every candidate and variant), two thirds of the quarters positive, the Lab gates, no pair at 25 % of the net profit and net costs; a gross DROP is valid, a gross KEEP is impossible. With today's 2.1 years of bars the answer is NOT_EVALUABLE until CF-04 Part B. Nothing selects, plans or orders a pair.

  • Option expression simulator (built, P14-08, ADR 0197): xd options remark --run <run-key> --profile <hash> re-marks every trade of a stored cash run as one long option under a registered option_buy profile (strike atm or itm1, expiry nearest_safe, optional premium_stop_pct, max_hold_sessions): a BUY is a call, an intraday SELL a put; the strike is read once from the entry bar and then held by following strike_px across the rolling offsets (outside the window the bar is unavailable, never a zero or the last price); entry at the open of the first option bar at or after the cash minute, exits mapped from the cash exits, the lower premium when a premium stop and the cash exit share a bar, a D6 exit at the close of the last session before the expiry; a missing bar is priced by Black-76 on an observed IV no older than Options:Remark:MaxIvAgeMinutes (60) and labelled bs_fallback, else the trade is strike_left_window. Size is the cash trade's rupee risk over the option's delta (fractional units, R_equiv = rupees over that risk, both sides gross or both net, else basis_mismatch); lot feasibility is a column. Expiry dates come from options.contracts or a derived weekday rule (expiry: derived, secondary-source data, UNVERIFIED). Every cash trade gets one row in research.expression_trades (insert-only, with research.expression_runs holding the counts per unpaired reason: no_series, no_series_for_expiry, expiry_unknown, strike_left_window, archive_unusable, no_iv, illiquid, liquidity_unknown, basis_mismatch, open_at_end, spread_unavailable, risk_invalid); the run id hashes every input so a second run writes nothing. Works on synthetic data; with no recorded archive every trade is unpaired. The first user of the pricing core and the cost model; the simulator sim-9 is unchanged and no decision, live or execution code reads any of it.

  • Expression evaluation (built, P14-09, ADR 0198): xd options expression-eval --family expr:option_buy --run <run-key> [--profile <hash>] [--holdout] gives the option family a stored protocol O verdict (KEEP, DROP, INCONCLUSIVE or NOT_EVALUABLE with each missing quantity named) in research.extension_evaluations (fold unit quarter, GET /api/research/extensions?family=expr:option_buy; the P15 phase check does not list it). The population is the out-of-sample test windows (the Lab's walk-forward geometry; the winner of each fold is not re-derived) of a run stored by a full Lab test and re-marked by P14-08; the unit is the paired d = R_equiv(option) - R_equiv(cash) on the same rupee risk. The profile is appended to the test ledger and read back before any date or figure of the run is read; the hold-out (last six months) is cut by date and filtered out of every query before any number is built, and is read once, after a development KEEP, with --holdout. NOT_EVALUABLE below 6 evaluable quarters (20 pairs each) or 400 pooled pairs, with no paired trade (no_paired_trades, spread_unavailable) or a bound unavailable; KEEP needs both block lower bounds (days and 5 sessions) at 0.05 R x Factor(N), two thirds of the quarters positive, the latest rule epoch (from 2025-09-01, 30 pairs or more) positive, the option's own evidence through the Lab's gates, net charges and a Black-76 fallback share of 5 % or less; every gross result is INCONCLUSIVE; DROP needs net and an upper bound under 0.05 R. A KEEP is a record: expression stays cash on every decision path. Expected first answer: NOT_EVALUABLE.

  • IV and event features (built, P14-11, ADR 0199): the extension iv-1 (ATM IV, IV rank and percentile over 252 sessions from the previous session, term slope, IV minus 20-day realised volatility, the NIFTY IV rank for every trade, put-call open interest ratio, results before the nearest expiry) read from options.rolling_bars at the decision instant through one bounded extractor (only bars that had ended, only the same day, only usable key-sessions, NaN with a closed reason for every unknown); xd learn features --extension iv-1 and xd learn extension-eval --family ext:iv-1 (protocol X, quarterly folds); GET /api/research/extensions?family=ext:iv-1. NOT_EVALUABLE until five years of cash trades exist (CF-04 Part B); a KEEP is a record, no scoring switch.

  • Defined-risk structures (built, P14-10, ADR 0200): each structure is its own family with its own ledger count and verdict. xd options remark --run <run-key> --profile <hash> also takes debit_spread and credit_spread profiles (strike, width 2 or 4 strike steps, structure_stop_pct, max_hold_sessions): a BUY is a bull call spread (credit: bull put), an intraday SELL a bear put spread (credit: bear call), every leg read from vendor bars on the P14-08 held strike, the exit mapped from the cash exit and the D6 exit before the expiry, a missing leg bar priced by the counted fallback or the structure is strike_left_window. The maximum loss is known at entry and stored (a debit: the debit; a credit: width less credit); a structure value outside [0, width] at the exit or stop bar is the counted fate structure_inconsistent, never a price, so no bar loses more; units = risk / max loss, so the worst case equals the cash trade's risk; a stop inside a bar takes the short leg's high and the long leg's low; one half-spread per fill (4 per spread, 8 per condor). No naked short leg, no margin, no order path. A stock spread of width 4 is refused (width_outside_window); a credit spread is for an index. The iron condor (xd options condors --profile <hash> --from --to, k 6 m 1 wings 2 steps, counted variants N = 4) is an index range family with no cash twin, judged by protocol R (6 quarters, 200 condors, the Lab's gates, the block lower bounds at 0.05 R x Factor(N), net, hold-out once). Credit spreads and condors are gated before a trial or a bar: no stored regime (P6-02, P3-01d not built) is NOT_EVALUABLE regime_unavailable, and no measured half-spread of 60 usable sessions (an owner assumption is not enough) is spread_not_measured; only debit spreads can be evaluated today. Tables: research.expression_legs (insert-only; migration AddExpressionLegs, which also widens four checks of expression_trades). GET /api/research/extensions?family=expr:debit_spread|expr:credit_spread|expr:iron_condor. A KEEP is a record: nothing selects, plans or orders a structure.

  • Families: directional expression (ITM/ATM options for our signals), defined-risk debit spreads (swing), index credit spreads / iron condors in range regimes, event/IV-aware positioning.

  • New learning dimension instrument expression (cash / future / option_buy / debit_spread …).

  • Simulation from historical option prices; Black-Scholes with recorded IV only as fallback; premium and underlying-based exits; exit before expiry by default; liquidity filters (index options first).

  • "Slow" statistical arbitrage: PAIRS (cointegration mean reversion) and BASIS (cash-futures) on 5m+ bars — two-leg, so practical with module A.

D. Fundamentals & events#

  • Data: exchange filings (results XBRL/PDF), shareholding (promoter holding, pledges), announcements, results calendar — stored point-in-time by filing timestamp. Source (P15-01, ADR 0179): the owner files downloaded files into data/filings/inbox/ with a sidecar carrying the exchange's broadcast time (with an offset; never guessed); a read-only folder-drop adapter lists them. No automatic fetch is built — it needs the owner's reading of the exchange terms first. Store (built, P15-02, ADR 0180): schema filings with five insert-only tables (filings envelopes with a nullable broadcast time, then result_facts, shareholding, announcements, calendar, empty until P15-03/04); IPointInTimeFilings.AsOf returns what the market knew at an instant: a filing is visible iff broadcast_ts + Filings:LagMinutes <= t (5 minutes, a deployment setting), the latest visible revision of a period is current, a filing without a time is stored, counted and never used; the paused service filings-ingest (17:05 on trading days) copies each file once into a 0600 store folder; GET /api/filings/coverage, GET /api/filings, GET /api/filings/{id} return counts and metadata only. Nothing trades on it yet. Calendar and shareholding (built, P15-04, ADR 0181): the owner drops a board-meeting csv (symbol,period_end,meeting_date,purpose,status,intimation_ts) and a shareholding csv (symbol,period_end,category,pct,pledged_pct,broadcast_ts); each row carries the time the exchange published it (an offset is required; blank or invalid is unavailable, never guessed). Rows load inside the filings-ingest run. days_to_results for an instrument and an instant counts trading sessions from the India date of the instant to the event session (the first session on or after the meeting date) of the earliest visible, not cancelled results meeting, and a meeting intimated after the instant, or a reschedule before its own time, never counts; no entry is unavailable (no_entry, only_cancelled, no_upcoming_entry), never zero and never an estimated date. GET /api/calendar/results lists the visible upcoming meetings and the instruments without an entry. Nothing trades on it yet (P15-06 wires the event filter). Results XBRL parser (built, P15-03, ADR 0184): every stored results .xml/.xbrl file is parsed by one hardened reader (no DTD, no resolver, at most 20 MB, 64 levels, 200,000 elements; each refusal a closed reason on a rejected parse row) into filings.result_facts (revenue, total_income, pbt, pat, eps_basic, eps_diluted, exceptional_items, audited) per span (q, ytd, fy from the context period) and basis (consolidated, standalone), in rupees as filed, with unavailable and a closed reason (not_in_file, conflicting_facts, unit_unknown, scale_unknown, bad_value) for anything the file does not carry plainly; a file with no readable basis, a bank file and a PDF store no fact (basis_unknown, format_not_supported, not_xbrl). The step runs at the end of the filings-ingest run and parses each file once per parser version. IResultFactsAsOf answers per period, span and basis from the newest visible envelope that carries that span and basis, so one basis never hides the other. The parser version is xbrl-1-synthetic until the owner's real filings pass; GET /api/filings/{id}/facts shows the outcome. Nothing trades on it yet. Announcements and the event-flags eval set (built, P15-07, ADR 0188, amends ADR 0179): the owner drops an announcements csv (symbol,broadcast_ts,category,subject,text, sidecar kind announcement, symbol *); filings-ingest stores each row as its own envelope (no period, never grouped) with its text in the files store; an announcement as a text or pdf file stays an ordinary envelope and is never sampled. xd eval announcements sample|import|show draws the stratified 240 for labelling (seeded, order-independent), imports the owner's 200 labels and prints the state; the exchange category baseline is committed before the first sample. No model, no network.
  • Uses: swing universe quality filter; features (growth, margin trend, surprise vs last 4 quarters, valuation percentile); PEAD (post-earnings drift) swing family; event-risk filter.
  • Assistant extracts structured fields from PDFs/announcements; outputs are features measured like any other, never trade decisions. Announcement flags (built, P15-08, ADR 0189): one schema-checked answer per announcement from the local model with no tool, text fenced as data, an order value kept only if it is in the text (the plan's XBRL cross-check has no field to run on: order value, guidance and rating action are not in results XBRL, and results numbers are never taken from text), stored insert-only with the model and prompt hashes in filings.extractions; graded by set D (precision of material at least 0.80 with at least 30 predicted, better than the category baseline by the sign test) before protocol L lets llm-1 be tested; PDFs are not read.
  • Measured by the shared comparison core (P13-05, ADR 0183): ev-1 and fu-1 rows go to research.trade_features_ext and are judged on calendar-quarter folds with season blocks, the same verdict table as §B. The features and their extraction are built in P15-05 (ADR 0185): days_to_results and days_since_results are also frame features a strategy rule may name (engine sim-8).
  • PEAD (built, P15-09, ADR 0187): an event side may hold up to 20 sessions, the event_exit overlay never fires on unavailable, the surprise quintiles use the previous complete season, and PEAD is a long-only built-in with engine sim-9; it is judged by protocol P.
  • Phase check (built, P15-12, ADR 0190): xd events phase-check [--cut ...] [--json] reads the stored verdicts of the five families for the current versions only and says MET, NOT MET or NOT EVALUABLE; read only, exit 0.
  • Verdicts (P15-10, ADR 0186): protocols F (event-risk filter), X (ev-1, fu-1), P (PEAD) and L (the flag) store one insert-only verdict per family in research.extension_evaluations (GET /api/research/extensions, /{id}). With today's data every family is NOT_EVALUABLE with the missing quantity named, except possibly F, whose plausible first verdict is DROP: the calendar cannot say between a meeting and the next intimation, the fail-closed filter then removes more than half of the trades, and the report prints the share lost to no_upcoming_entry. A KEEP is a record; no slice turns it into scoring. The event filter's auto option (default OFF, owner-armed) turns the filter ON only for a KEEP that the hold-out confirmed, for the switch's N.
