09 Trading engine · Markdown source

09 — Learning Engine

"Self-learning" = continuously re-measuring which setups, timeframes, sides and exits work in which market conditions, and shifting recommendations accordingly — with guards against fooling itself.

1. Four layers#

Layer 1 — Cell statistics. For each cell (strategy, tf, side, style, exit_policy, regime) and windows (all / 250 days / 60 days): n, win rate, avg win/loss R, expectancy, profit factor, max drawdown, MAE/MFE quantiles. Win rate uses a Beta prior shrunk toward the parent group when n is small; confidence = lower bound of the credible interval; exponential recency weighting.

Built (P6-01, ADR 0064): the pure calculator reads only trades that exited on or before the as-of date; the prior is the strategy's rate shrunk toward the whole system's (strength 20), the cell's toward the strategy's; the confidence is the 5 % quantile of the posterior (90 % interval); recency weight 0.5^(days/180). Snapshots are insert-only in research.cell_stats; xd learn cells --as-of D --run KEY:STYLE:EXIT computes, GET /api/cells reads. Regime is * until P6-02; rows below min_trades read "not enough evidence".

Built (CF-01, ADR 0203): every cell row also carries how many independent trades it rests on (IndependentN, shown as "n=34, effective 11"): the smaller of a cluster estimate (Kish design effect, one-way ANOVA intra-cluster correlation, cluster = the India entry session) and a serial estimate (lag-1 autocorrelation of the daily mean R), never above n and never below 1; with it the trades needed to see the target edge (default 0.15 R, sigma floored at 0.5 R), the smallest edge visible now, and the minimum track record in iid trades. They are unweighted, shown beside the old figures and decide nothing in the default ReportOnly mode; in Gate (the owner's switch, Confidence:EffectiveN:Mode) the Enough flag compares effective n with learning.min_trades. Below 5 trades, or without entry dates, the figures read unavailable: <reason>. Three different numbers are never mixed: EffectiveN is the recency-weight sum, IndependentN the independence count (shrinkage.nUsed, CF-02, is the smaller of IndependentN and the Kish weight n (sum w)^2 / sum w^2).

Built (CF-02, ADR 0204): every cell row also carries a shrunk expectancy beside the raw one (shrinkage): the cell's mean R pulled toward its parent group, parent + B (m - parent) with B = tau^2 / (tau^2 + sigma^2/n), where n is the smaller of effective n (CF-01) and the Kish weight n, tau^2 is the DerSimonian-Laird between-cell variance across the siblings (floored at 0.01 R^2, the floor says so), and the parent is the leave-one-out mean of the other cells (a regime cell: its * cell without it, then its strategy, then the system; the first group with at least 30 trades). With it a lower bound (the 5 % quantile of the posterior; it ignores the uncertainty in the pooling parameters, so it is optimistic) and a win rate shrunk with an estimated Beta strength (the constant 20 stays on the old column and is the fallback). Shown beside, decides nothing: Enough, the evidence label and every stored field are unchanged; Confidence:Shrinkage:Mode is ReportOnly by default and Gate is not offered until the lower bound's coverage has been validated. No parent with 30 trades reads unavailable: no parent with 30 trades and the raw figures stand alone.

Built (P6-02, ADR 0146, spec P6-02): the regime of a day is one of the nine of doc 07 §6, read from the stored context as of the day before and only where it is ready; otherwise the label is unavailable with its reason (no_context, before_history, stale, context_invalid, regime_not_ready), never a default. A trade's regime is the label of its entry day. xd learn cells --by-regime also writes, for each cell, one cell per regime part and window with its own n, posterior and "not enough evidence" flag, shrunk toward the strategy's rate like the * cells; the * rows are the same with and without it, the regime key part is UP_LOW_VOL or, when the label was built from the index alone (the VIX percentile not ready), UP_LOW_VOL~index_only, so a cell never mixes the two bases, and trades with no label are counted by reason and sit in no regime cell. The readers of the newest snapshot (selection, plan, digest, lifecycle, GET /api/cells) see only the * cells; GET /api/cells/regimes shows the regime cells and GET /api/data/regimes the label of each session and the counts per regime and basis (a regime with no day shows 0). The Lab by_regime block is lit (text plus buckets). Not yet: the Reports regime dimension, the regime training feature and the against_regime loss tag (P6-02b).

Layer 2 — Exit selection. See doc 08 §3. Its input, the replay of the same entries under every candidate with the MAE/MFE tables, is built (P4-05, ADR 0053); the choice by out-of-sample expectancy per cell, with its folds, minimum sample and stability report, is built (P4-06, ADR 0054); it applies nothing until a stored strategy exists to apply it to.

Layer 3 — Meta-model (meta-labeling).

  • Labels: triple barrier — target, stop, time limit (session end / max hold). Built (P6-03, ADR 0065): label 1 only when the target ended the trade, 0 for stop, session end, max hold and exit-rule exits; end-of-data trades are left out; R at resolution is kept as a second target.

Built (P6-04, ADR 0066): the decision-time snapshot is a side table research.trade_features (the trade record is unchanged), catalog tf-1 of 41 features expressed as ratios; the extractor steps the feature frame and reads it before the first bar at or after the decision instant, and a property test proves that cutting or replacing later bars changes nothing. The cell statistics in a row are those of the day before the decision. Context and regime features are unknown until the daily context is loaded (P3-01d). The training set joins labels to features, drops and counts rows with a required feature unknown, and its record is kept in research.training_sets (insert-only); xd learn features and xd learn training-set run it.

  • Model: gradient-boosted trees (ML.NET LightGBM, FastTree fallback) per style × side (strategy as feature), isotonic (PAV) calibration, per-prediction feature contributions + permutation importance; trained and scored in-process in .NET.
  • Must beat baselines (logistic regression; cell stats only) out-of-sample, else not used.
  • Top contributing features stored with each recommendation.

Layer 4 — Monitoring & adaptation.

  • Regime-conditioned statistics.
  • Drift: rolling expectancy below the backtest band for K checks ⇒ auto-pause; paused cells keep being shadow-graded and re-qualify automatically.
  • Champion/challenger for models and parameters.
  • Learning changelog for every automatic change, with evidence.

2. Validation: purged walk-forward#

|---- train (3y) ----|-gap-|-- test (3m) --|
       |---- train -------------|-gap-|-- test --|

Only test windows count. Gap ≥ max holding (5 sessions). Tuning only inside train windows.

Built (P6-05, ADR 0067): quarterly folds (first test 9 months in) with train rows purged by label horizon, scored against base-rate, cell-rate and logistic baselines; a model is eligible only if it beats both of the last two by a margin in 2/3 of the folds and pooled, with calibration error within 5 pp; fewer than 6 folds is "inconclusive"; the last 6 months are a hold-out used once.

Built (P6-06, ADR 0068): the model is a candidate behind the same port as the baselines; trees are fitted on the early 75 % of a fold's train rows, the PAV calibration map on the latest 25 % (never on test rows), LightGBM falls back to FastTree when its native library cannot load (and the model says so), every score can carry its top feature contributions, and the model bytes have a SHA-256. It can lose: on pure noise it is CannotBeatBaselines. Built (P6-13, ADR 0075): the meta-labelling policy (rule 6 of section 14) is evidence only: a take/skip threshold on the champion's calibrated probability, chosen in each fold only from the out-of-fold scores of earlier folds (net R per candidate, at least half of the candidates kept; optional split by style or side), judged on skill (lift beyond a random skip of the same share, kept beat skipped, a standard-error bound, margins widened for the trials) against taking everything on the purged folds with the skipped trades' realised R beside the kept ones, and the section 14 flags (more than half removed, skipped not worse over the last 100). A champion is required, else "no policy"; "does not help" is an ordinary outcome; at most inconclusive below 6 folds. Nothing is wired (P7) and no sizing multiplier is fitted; verb xd learn policy.

Built (CF-10 part 10a, ADR 0214): the calibration of a model is chosen by the number of calibration rows: fewer than Confidence:Calibration:IsotonicMinRows (500) get Platt scaling (a sigmoid of the raw margin, two fitted parameters, Platt's smoothed targets, a deterministic Newton fit, slope never negative), 500 or more the isotonic map of P6-06, none the constant base rate. ML.NET's own Platt probability is not used: the map receives the raw Score margin. The kind and parameters are in the artifact header (an older artifact loads as isotonic). The pooled expected calibration error of each candidate in the validation report carries a day-cluster bootstrap interval (1000 draws, 90 %, seeded) with the gap and its standard error and the log loss minus the base-rate log loss; it reports only and no verdict reads it. Not built yet: the paired bootstrap and the MlMayInfluence predicate (10c), champion / challenger and rollback (10d).

Built (CF-10 part 10b, ADR 0215): the validation report of a model carries a complexity block, evidence only (no verdict, eligibility or champion rule and not the trainer reads it): the events (the smaller class of the largest training set) and the events-per-variable cap on a logistic model (min(floor(events / 15), 8): 120 trades at 40 % wins are 48 events and a cap of 3), the ladder (base rate, cell rate, logistic within the cap, trees with at least 100 events; a rung above the base rate needs a probability of at least 0.95 of beating the rung below, which part 10c-1 computes (below), and reads "not available", the rung "not earned", where the paired bootstrap is off or has too few days; the candidate also reads "not earned" for a structural reason, the trainer having 8 leaves and no monotone constraints where the spec's trees rung has monotone constraints and depth at most 3), the population stability index of each feature (each fold's test rows against its training rows, upper-inclusive training-quantile bins, fewer on small samples, one bin per value for a flag whichever value is the minority; 0.10 watch, 0.25 alarm; a feature that never changes in training or a side with too few rows is "not computed", said and never 0) and the direction check (the sign of the correlation of a feature's value and its contribution per fold; flipping in more than one third of the folds means it would be dropped). Settings Confidence:Ladder (Mode Off or ReportOnly, Gate refused). Not built yet: the MlMayInfluence predicate (10c-2), champion / challenger and rollback (10d).

Built (CF-10 part 10c-1, ADR 0216): the ladder's probability comes from a paired day-cluster bootstrap (PairedDayBootstrap, pure, reused by CF-12): the gain of a scorer over the rung below on each of the same out-of-sample rows (baseline log loss minus the scorer's) is averaged over whole trade days drawn with replacement (2000 draws, seeded, 90 % interval); the probability is the share of draws with a mean above zero (a tie counts against; fewer than 10 days is "not available"). 0.95 and above is a pass, 0.90 to 0.95 "promising" and never a pass (four days of +2, +1, -1, +3 give 237 / 256 = 0.9258); the fold sign test is shown beside it (5 of 6 folds better is p = 7 / 64 = 0.109); the effective count of the training rows (the sum of the average uniqueness of overlapping labels; bars 1-2, 2-3, 3-4 are worth 2.0 of 3) is shown next to their number. The trees rung still carries its structural reason, so it cannot read earned until the trainer meets the spec's rung. Settings Confidence:PairedBootstrap (Mode Off or ReportOnly, Gate refused); 2000 draws, 90 %, 10 days, 0.90 and the 0.95 bar are proposals, open owner questions in the ADR. Evidence only: no verdict, eligibility or champion rule and not the trainer reads it. The predicate and the gate follow (10c-2, below).

Built (CF-10 part 10c-2, ADR 0217): a model may influence a decision only through one gate. The predicate MlMayInfluence = HoldOutBeatsNoMl AND ForwardShadowOk AND NoCalibrationAlarm AND SwitchOn is pure (Domain), has four terms that read holds, fails or unavailable, and lists a reason for every term that does not hold; an unavailable figure is never a pass, and a model whose hold-out its family already used is refused as holdout_reused whatever the numbers. IMlInfluenceGate answers it (and also asks that the model is the current champion) and is the only way Planning, Selection and Autonomy may read a model's score; an architecture test forbids them the scorers, the registry, the score query and the predicate itself, and pins that AdvisoryPWin is read only to be shown. The hold-out is looked at once per model family and block (the family is the feature version): the look is a kind = model row of the CF-06 unlock table keyed by family and block start, so a new, non-overlapping block that the owner advances gives a fresh look (the advance itself is 10d and must enforce the non-overlap); a later model of the family, a block that was already seen, or a model whose kept hold-out starts after the block reads as reused. Each changed answer of the gate is an insert-only row of research.ml_influence_checks (migration AddMlSafety), and GET /api/learning/models/{id}/influence shows the terms and reasons. Today the gate refuses everything: the owner's switch has one implementation, always off (its store and owner act are 10d), and the hold-out figures, the forward shadow figures and the calibration detector have no source yet (the evidence port answers none) (no step scores the hold-out, a shadow candidate does not record its model, the detector is not stored per model). Thresholds are Confidence:MlInfluence (100 rows, 0.95, 30 candidates, 4 weeks, 5 pp). Not built yet: champion / challenger, rollback, the switch, the verbs and the Models-screen additions (10d).

Built (CF-12 part 12a, ADR 0218): read-only evidence on a live-forward edge and on the learning loop; it proposes, stores, pauses and changes nothing and places no order. DecayMonitor reads a strategy's edge series (graded shadow trades) and the P10 behavioural series in non-overlapping windows of 60 trades and alerts when a window's mean R is below mu_ref - 1.5 sigma sqrt(DEFF / 60 + 1 / N_ref) (-0.0910 with DEFF 1, -0.1975 with DEFF 2.5), twice in a row being two consecutive completed windows below; the P6-09 CUSUM runs on the same series (the existing detector) and a by-quarter table is added. KillRules evaluates, with their numbers, cost stress on the last 100 trades (net(1.5) = net(1) - 0.5 c strictly below zero, 20 new trades before it fires again), drawdown against 2 times the 95th percentile at the series' own n, decay twice in a row, and the CF-09 failure boundary as a proposal-only input; a missing input is "not evaluated", never a pass. ThreeArmLoopTest compares the frozen champion, the champion now and no model on the same candidates with the paired day-cluster bootstrap of CF-10 (retrained against frozen, and retrained against no model, "helps" at 0.95, "hurts" at a fixed P(worse) of 0.95, fewer than 20 days inconclusive, "no measurable difference" an answer); LearningCurve reads the slope of the last 3 to 5 gains (rising, flat, unclear; projection); ReliabilitySlope fits the logistic slope of the outcome on logit(p) over the trailing 200 outcomes (alarm below 0.8 or above 1.2 and 2 standard errors from 1, never below 200 outcomes); DecisionLift compares the top and bottom half of a night by the stored score and the owner's approvals with 50 % by chance. Today the loop report is unavailable in every section (no per-candidate score, frozen artifact or refit loop is stored) and drawdown is not evaluated on the edge series (a shadow row has no cell); GET /api/confidence/decay?strategy= and GET /api/confidence/loop. Nothing that chooses reads these types (Planning, Selection and Autonomy are pinned); the verbs and the screens are part 12c.

Built (CF-12 part 12b, ADR 0219): kill proposals, the pause and the owner's re-enable; no order is placed. The service decay-monitor (daily 18:00 after shadow-grade, paused at start) evaluates each strategy with shadow edge candidates once a day; a kill rule that fires (never one that is not evaluated, held or holding) writes a proposal in confidence.kill_proposals (rule, numbers, the Paper or Active cells it would pause, the settings hash; one per strategy, rule and day, none while one is open) and an open proposal is a bell notice. With confidence.kill.auto OFF (the default; no row means OFF) nothing else changes; the owner applies a proposal (POST /api/confidence/kill-proposals/{id}/apply, session and CSRF): each cell moves to Paused through the lifecycle, demote first and then one compare-and-set of the proposal, so a repeat or a race pauses nothing twice. With the setting ON, which only the owner switches (PUT /api/confidence/kill-settings, a fresh password on every change, a missing one 403, stored like the shadow switch with its id in commit order), the same run applies the open proposals as actor auto with the switch row and the settings hash, never as an owner; the CF-09 failure boundary is a proposal that auto never executes (the database also refuses to record it as decided by auto; that guard is on the proposal record only). The lifecycle gains the move Paper to Paused (the diagram above) and the changelog actor auto. Re-enabling (POST /api/learning/cells/{key}/re-enable) is only ever the owner's act with a fresh password, Paused to Paper and never to Active; when a kill proposal paused the cell every rule that fired must hold now and none may fire (409 confidence.rule_not_cleared; a rule that cannot be evaluated is not a pass), and the answer always warns that the CF-11 readiness gate R judges the re-enable too: with the enforce switch ON a gate that does not pass refuses it (409 confidence.gate_failed), with it OFF the answer's first warning states the gate's own decision. No path re-enables automatically (an IL closure over the automatic path, with sentinels), neither a revert nor xd learn lifecycle --to paper can lift a kill pause (the recorder refuses it unless the command comes from the re-enable handler), an automatic pause stays on the bell for three days as a resolved notice, the automatic run applies only proposals made today or whose rule still fires today, and the cost multiplier (at least 1.5) and drawdown factor (at most 2) of Confidence:Kill can only be tightened.

Built (CF-10 part 10d-1, ADR 0220): champion / challenger promotion, rollback and the ML switches; ML stays advisory and no order is placed. Promotion (MlPromotion): a challenger is promoted only with at least 30 graded candidates over at least 4 weeks, the paired day-cluster bootstrap against the champion at P >= 0.95, a P6-12 counterfactual that is not FrozenBetter (an inconclusive one is not a pass) and nothing against it from ChampionRule; an unavailable figure is never a pass. POST /api/learning/models/{id}/promote is the owner's act (session, CSRF, a fresh password) over the P6-07 designation and the P6-08 changelog row; a retired previous champion is re-promoted only by the owner and only if its latest evaluation satisfies the influence predicate without the switch. The paired comparison, the candidates' scores and the counterfactual link have no source yet (a shadow candidate does not record which model scored it), so every promotion is refused with the reasons today. Rollback (RollbackPolicy): the triggers are the P6-09 calibration detector (gap above 5 pp and 2.5 standard errors on a window of 100, three checks in a row) and the P6-09 band detector (20-trade means below the band for three checks) on the cells the model scores, read as "in alarm now" with no number of their own; a firing trigger writes a proposal in research.rollback_proposals and a bell notice (the previous champion and whether it still qualifies are shown, never acted on). The owner applies it (POST /api/learning/rollback/{id}/apply, session and CSRF): the champion becomes a challenger (shadow) through the registry's designation and its changelog row, and there is no champion until the owner names one. The daily service ml-monitor (18:15, after decay-monitor, paused at start) evaluates each champion once a day. The switches ml.influence.enabled, ml.auto_promote and ml.auto_rollback are stored state (research.ml_switch_settings, append-only, no row means OFF) changed by PUT /api/learning/ml-settings: turning influence or auto-promotion ON needs a fresh password, ml.auto_rollback and any OFF need the session only. With ml.auto_rollback ON the daily run applies the open proposals as actor auto with the switch row and the settings hash, never as an owner (the database also refuses an auto decision unless that switch is ON and recorded, and any auto dismissal); with ml.auto_promote ON it promotes only a challenger that meets the promotion rule and the predicate without the switch and has no rollback proposal. The influence switch is now the stored one, but the gate still refuses every model (the hold-out figures have no source). A regime cell shrinks to its parent below 30 trades and stands alone at 30 (RegimeCellRule, the bar RegimeBreakdown already used). Planning, Selection and Autonomy name none of it; the verbs and the Models-screen additions are part 10d-2.

Built (CF-10 part 10d-2a, addendum to ADR 0220): the verbs xd learn influence MODEL-ID (the predicate term by term with its reasons, the hold-out state and figures, the champion flag and the three ML switches with what each means) and xd learn rollback (the rollback proposals with trigger, numbers, state, actor and time, the ml.auto_rollback setting the champion now and the proposals that leave no qualifying previous champion), read only over the same handlers as GET /api/learning/models/{id}/influence and GET /api/learning/rollback/proposals. Applying a rollback stays the owner's act in the app. Anything unavailable, refused or not evaluated reads "not a pass" with its reason (today the gate refuses every model and says why); a failed read reads unavailable, never "none". The Models-screen additions and calibration_kind are part 10d-2b.

Built (CF-12 part 12c-1, addendum to ADR 0218): the verbs xd learn decay --strategy ID, xd learn loop and xd learn curve, read only over the same handlers as GET /api/confidence/decay and /loop (decay also lists the strategy's open kill proposals and shows the automatic setting). Plain wrapped text; unavailable with its reason wherever the report says so (today every section of the loop report, and the learning curve until the refit loop exists), kill-rule states fires, holds, held and not_evaluated (the last two labelled "not a pass"). The Learning-screen tabs are part 12c-2.

Built (CF-11 part 1, ADR 0221) Readiness scorecard (derived on every read from stored results; read only; raises no size and places no order): per strategy version, the 15-row edge checklist (pass, fail or unavailable per row, each with its value, threshold and producer: registration, independent trades, net expectancy and t-statistic against the multiple-testing factor, cost stress, fill one minute later, random-entry null, placebo and mirror, plateau, stability, drawdown, hold-out, benchmark, capacity and tier, paper confirm) and the readiness score = the minimum of six sub-scores (evidence, effect, power at 0.3 R, backtest agreement, calibration when a model takes part, stability), piecewise linear between the spec's anchors, with hard fails that turn it into 0 and are named, four labels (Not ready below 40, Building, Usable with advice from 70, Ready for the next phase from 85), the weakest link and what would move it computed from the anchors; for the system, min(integrity, weighted mean of the strategy scores) (equal weights, labelled, until the plan exposes a risk share). unavailable is a fail everywhere: the inputs that have no producer yet (benchmark, capacity and tier, the 20-trade check, the by-quarter and per-instrument shares, the Monte Carlo 90th percentile, the integrity checks, the tier-C evidence, the risk share) sit behind one port whose only implementation says unavailable with the reason, so a strategy reads Not ready with a "waiting for" list until they exist. GET /api/confidence/scorecards, GET /api/confidence/scorecards/{strategyId}/versions/{version}, GET and PUT /api/confidence/readiness-settings (a session, the CSRF token and a fresh password, ON and OFF alike: OFF removes a refusal); the service readiness-snapshot (daily 18:30 after decay-monitor, paused at start) stores an insert-only snapshot per strategy and for the system with the settings hash (readiness.scorecard_snapshots), and a gate use stores its own. Built (CF-11 part 2a, addendum to ADR 0221): the read-only verbs xd readiness [system] (the system card, one line per Lab strategy weakest first, the enforce setting) and xd readiness STRATEGY [--version N] (the card of one version, the latest by default: the score as the minimum of the sub-scores with each sub-score and what would move it, the label, the hard fails, the 15 rows with each input, threshold and producer, gates R and S with the rows that stop them, what it is waiting for, the newest daily snapshot id and the settings hash, and whether enforce is ON or OFF with its meaning), over the same use cases as GET /api/confidence/scorecards; an unavailable input prints unavailable: <reason>. Unavailable is not a finding and not a pass. and counts as a fail; exit 0 done, 1 refused, 2 usage, 3 failed, 130 cancelled; no verb stores, switches or moves anything (an IL scan with sentinels, as for xd learn decay|loop|influence|rollback). The screen /readiness is part 2b.

Built (P6-12, ADR 0077): the frozen-model counterfactual answers "does continued learning help?" as evidence only: a model fitted once before a cut date (never refitted) against the same recipe refitted every 3 months, both scoring exactly the same later trades out of sample (embargo and purge as the folds, hold-out never read), with the P6-05 metrics and the realised R of taking what each side scores at or above its own train base rate. The period is what the data allow, at most a year (under a year after the hold-out today) and is printed; judged on the windows where the retrained model saw new labels (at least 3, else inconclusive), margins widened for the trials; "no difference" is an ordinary answer. No plan-price comparison and no screen (P7, P8); verb xd learn counterfactual.

Built (P6-14, ADR 0069): learned entry windows per cell are a proposal only: the best run of 30-minute entry buckets (win rate shrunk toward the cell, at least 30 trades, a lift floor, no weak bucket inside), re-learnt on each purged fold's train rows and judged on its test rows; "no stable window" is an ordinary outcome, fewer than 6 folds is at best "inconclusive", and nothing is applied to the scanner or a strategy. Built (P6-07, ADR 0070): trained models are registered with their file (data/models/<sha256>, never loaded without its hash), the training-set id and the validation verdict; champion / challenger / retired is an append-only record, a model that cannot beat the baselines can be kept as a challenger but only an Eligible one can be named champion, and the champion is advisory until P7 (GET /api/models, xd learn model, xd learn designate).

Built (P6-15, ADR 0071): automatic loss tags are pure descriptions of a closed losing trade (gap through stop, gave back profit, high slippage, late entry, quick stop, wide stop, normal variance), each with the numbers behind it; a loss that fits none is untagged. Tags are never features. Against regime, news/results, execution gap and decision drag are not built (they need P6-02, P15, real fills); a growing tag is reported as a candidate lesson and none is opened until P6-16.

Built (P6-11, ADR 0072): an experiment judges two stored runs made on the same entries with one knob changed (the candidate may skip entries, never add one; cost assumptions must match), by the fold windows of the exit choice scaled to the data: better needs 2/3 of the used folds, at least 6 used folds (fewer is inconclusive), a gain above 0.02 + 0.006 sqrt(trials) R per trade, total R within 10 % after an assumed live gap, and a cheaper slippage cannot win. Every experiment is recorded in the insert-only research.experiments, whose 30-day count is the trials (xd learn experiment, GET /api/experiments). The replay time is not measured; the 10-minute target is not claimed.

3. Daily feedback loop#

  1. Grade yesterday's primary, reserve and rejected items against actual data.
  2. Attach real fills (auto-journal) → measure live vs paper slippage; grade on executed prices (§9).
  3. Append outcomes with decision-time feature snapshots.
  4. Update cell stats; run drift and lesson monitors (§10).
  5. Weekly: retrain, recalibrate, challenger test.

Feature extensions (P13-05, ADR 0183). A new feature group (md-1, ev-1, fu-1, llm-1) never changes tf-1. It is stored in research.trade_features_ext and judged by one pure comparison: model A (tf-1) and model B (tf-1 plus the group) are the same recipe fitted on identical rows and calendar folds; the gain is net R per candidate under the take policy, with a paired block bootstrap and margins widened for the number of looks recorded in the ledger (the ledger row comes first). The answer is KEEP, DROP, INCONCLUSIVE or NOT_EVALUABLE with the missing quantities named, stored in research.extension_evaluations; the hold-out is read once. Nothing in a decision path reads either table.

ev-1 and fu-1 (P15-05, ADR 0185). xd learn features --extension ev-1|fu-1 --run <key> extracts, per trade and only from what was public at its decision instant, the event features (days_to_results, days_since_results, results_car3) and the fundamental features (sue_yoy, sue_ttm, rev_growth_yoy, margin_chg_yoy, promoter_pledge_pct, promoter_chg_qoq, pe_pctile_5y) into research.trade_features_ext, and one record per run in research.extension_extractions (lag, parser version, calendar hash, newest broadcast time read, NaN counts per feature and closed reason). Every unknown is NaN with a reason, never imputed; values built on the synthetic parser are labelled and counted.

iv-1 (P14-11, ADR 0199). xd learn features --extension iv-1 --run <key> extracts, per trade and only from what had ended at its decision instant on its own day, eight option-implied features into research.trade_features_ext: iv_atm, iv_rank_252, iv_pct_252 (ranks over the 252 sessions before the trade date, from the previous session's value), iv_term_slope, iv_minus_rv20, nifty_iv_rank_252 (for every trade, so instruments without options still get one value), pcr_oi and results_before_expiry; the extraction record adds the archive watermark, the quality run ids and the IV unit (UNVERIFIED, percent_assumed). Every unknown is NaN with a closed reason (no_options, no_eligible_series, no_bar_before_t, iv_missing, archive_unusable, short_history, flat_history, no_term_series, oi_missing, zero_call_oi, event_unavailable), never zero. xd learn extension-eval --family ext:iv-1 is protocol X on the options preset (quarterly folds, 12-month first train window, 5-session embargo, day blocks with the 5-session sensitivity, 2,000 candidates, 300 flips); the family is not one of the P15 families and the P15 phase check never lists it. A KEEP is a record: no scoring switch exists and no decision path reads the features.

Verdicts for the P15 families (P15-10, ADR 0186). xd learn extension-eval --family filter:results|ext:ev-1|ext:fu-1|ext:llm-1|pead [--holdout] judges each family by its pre-registered protocol: F (the event-risk filter: trades of unfiltered runs classified skipped, taken or unavailable by reason; unavailable counts as removed because the filter fails closed), X (the shared comparison with quarterly folds and season blocks, gated first by the extraction record: an unverified parser is parser_unverified, stale rows features_stale), P (PEAD: four variants, the choice frozen on the first eight seasons, 20 seasons needed) and L (the flag, read from the eval run, never recomputed). Each trial is in the ledger before its numbers; the verdict is stored insert-only with the missing quantity named; the last four seasons are read once by --holdout. A KEEP is a record. The only reader is the event-filter verdict port, for the switch's N, behind the owner's default-OFF auto option.

Instrument expression in the cell key (P14-06, ADR 0193). A cell key is strategy|tf|side|style|exit|regime, then an optional seventh part universe (reserved for BK-11; only * is allowed now) and an optional eighth part expression (cash, future, option_buy, debit_spread, credit_spread, iron_condor, pair, basis). The key is written with the fewest trailing parts: universe * and cash is the six-part text every stored key already has, byte for byte; any other expression is written with eight parts, the universe explicit. There is exactly one text per cell, so ...|*|cash and ...|* are refused. How a cash signal becomes an expression is an expression profile (kind plus parameters, hashed from a canonical text, stored insert-only in lab.expression_profiles), never a strategy-file field. Selection, planning, paper, live and execution read cash cells only; a cell of another expression is left out and counted as non_cash_cell_skipped (logged), never an error and never a candidate.

5. Hypothesis loop (one variable at a time)#

As built (P9-08, ADR 0142): the local model may propose a hypothesis (stored source model) into the Suggestions inbox; the engine re-reads the evidence and checks the bounds; only the owner's accept writes it, still proposed.

Hypotheses can come from the LLM, a parameter grid, or you. Live strategies are never edited directly by an LLM. Record example:

{"id":"H-2026-10-18-01","cell":"ORB|15m|BUY|INTRADAY|*","variable":"exits.FIXED_RR.rr","from":2.0,"to":2.5,
 "rationale":"Median MFE 2.7R over 120 OOS trades","status":"shadow",
 "backtest":{"oos_trades":212,"exp_R_champion":0.21,"exp_R_challenger":0.26}}

Built (P6-10, ADR 0076): a hypothesis is one knob from the owner or a deterministic grid (no LLM until P9), kept in the insert-only research.hypotheses with the states proposed, approved, running, judged, accepted, rejected, expired (at most 3 open; only the owner approves unless the autonomy level is autonomous, which nothing passes yet). "One variable" is checked on the two stored runs (parameters, exits, fill rules), the test is the P6-11 experiment and counts as a trial, and accepting records the verdict only: applying a change is the owner's own act (a changelog row). Triggers (30 new trades in a cell, a drift alarm) are computed on replayed data and returned as proposals. The record example above is the intended shape; the stored one carries a stored baseline run instead of the backtest summary. Shadow and paper stages wait for P10 and the weekly trigger for P7. Built (P10-05, ADR 0119): during the paper phase a measurement epoch freezes the measured configuration; a configuration fingerprint is taken at every capture, a change that adds risk starts a new epoch (the count restarts, earlier epochs are kept and never pooled), a change that only lowers risk is noted, and an accepted hypothesis waits in a visible queue until the owner applies it, which then starts a new epoch. The system detects and labels; it applies and blocks nothing.

6. Overfitting guards#

  1. Out-of-sample only for decisions.
  2. Minimum 30 OOS trades per cell.
  3. ≤ 4 parameters per strategy; coarse grids; plateaus.
  4. Multiple-testing adjustment (deflated Sharpe, stricter thresholds as tests grow).
  5. Costs always on.
  6. Monte Carlo trade reshuffling for drawdown expectations.
  7. Paper stage before live.
  8. Random-entry baseline with identical exits.
  9. Built (CF-06, ADR 0207): the ML validation hold-out (HoldOut.Reserve) takes the start of the Lab's locked hold-out block when one is fixed, so a model and a strategy keep the same untouched sessions; with no block fixed the rule is unchanged.
  10. Built (CF-08 parts A and B, ADR 0211): the shadow record, more evidence than the armed tickets give, without hindsight and without touching the P10 count. Every candidate of a nightly plan (primary, reserve and rejected by the fill) is written, from the stored plan record only, to paper.shadow_entries before 09:00 India time of its session, stamped by the database clock, once per (session, trade key), with a flag counts_for_edge (false for a candidate that never reached the fill: held, not eligible, merged duplicate or conflict drop). Features, the model id and the feature version are null until the plan record holds them. The runtime role can only insert; each session's digest is the SHA-256 of the previous digest, the session date, its status and the sorted row hashes, so a changed, added or removed row after the fact is found by recomputing; a night the writer did not run is a gap row and is never back-filled. The switch paper.shadow.enabled is the owner's and OFF by default, and the service shadow-write starts paused. The edge count is its own pure type (EdgeCount): raw n and an effective n that divides each day's graded trades by the design effect 1 + (m - 1) * rho (rho 0.3 is a placeholder until CF-01 and says so); it is not the paper count and the P10 exit criterion is untouched. No part of Planning, Selection or Autonomy can read shadow outcomes (architecture tests). The fills are modelled. Part B (ADR 0212): the grader shadow-grade (daily 17:30 after the eod run, paused, idle while the switch is OFF) replays each candidate that counts for edge through the one replayer that grading and paper capture use, and inserts one grade (graded with R, entry, exit and engine; not_triggered; not_gradable); a pending replay is retried and a stored grade is never rewritten, so the shadow R equals the P7-13 grade and the P10 paper R of the same ticket. GET /api/paper/shadow/summary and xd paper shadow summary give raw n, effective n with the rho used and its placeholder flag, the mean R with a 90 % interval and the hit rate by group, the P10 count beside the edge count, the pace once 10 trades are graded and the chain head; the figures are the gross R (stored beside the net one as r_gross) unless every charge sheet is confirmed as the owner's, and then the net R, with label, flag and numbers in agreement; every answer says "modelled fills; no queue, no latency". PUT /api/paper/shadow/settings is the owner's switch (session, CSRF, a fresh password on every change, an audited insert-only row whose id follows commit order); xd paper shadow verify recomputes the digest chain, whose format now binds the date and the status. The weekly export of the last digest to the Reports export and to a file outside the database is an open follow-up. Leftovers (ADR 0232): a weekly service shadow-digest (paused at start) appends the verified chain head to a hash-chained shadow-digest.log in Shadow:DigestFolder outside the database, and every export and xd paper shadow verify check each logged digest against the chain (paper.shadow_digest_diverged; tail truncation needs an offsite copy to be seen); turning the record ON over missed sessions is refused with 409 paper.shadow_gap (and with 409 paper.shadow_digest_unwritable when the digest folder cannot be written) unless acknowledgeGap is sent (Shadow:MaxResumeGapSessions, default 0); a graded row must carry r_gross; the shadow summary carries the CF-09 sequential line.
  11. Built (CF-09, ADR 0213): the sequential failure test of the paper edge series, beside the fixed P10 gate and never a pass. A Gaussian-mean SPRT (H0 mean net R 0 against mu1 +0.2 R, sigma 1.5 R, the log likelihood ratio 0.088889 x (x - 0.1) per trade) over the graded shadow trades that count for edge in the current paper epoch, in session then trade-key order, each trade divided by the design effect of its day (rho 0.3, a placeholder until CF-01 is wired in). The running sum is reflected at the on-track level 2.7726 and flags caution at -1.5581 (evidence ratio 4.75) and failure at -2.9444 (ratio 19); failure is latched for the epoch and read from the replay of the series, so nothing is stored, and a P10-05 epoch restart starts an empty series. The sigma is never below the configured one and is raised to the sample sd of the earlier trades from 20 trades (which only delays a flag). The indifference point is mu1 / 2 = +0.1 R: the words say the evidence favours a mean near 0 over +0.2 R at the stated odds, never "no edge", and the on-track label claims nothing more. A wrong flag of a true +0.2 R strategy is at most 5.3 %, then about 0.33 % per later return to the cap (Gaussian, stated in every answer). The P10 behavioural series is run through the same test and shown beside it, never merged; the R is the gross R unless the charge sheet is confirmed (as the shadow summary), and the fills are modelled. GET /api/paper/sequential, xd paper sequential and one extra line after xd paper phase-check (the phase check itself is untouched); settings Confidence:Sequential, no mode and no switch because it only flags. The action taken on a flag is CF-12's (behind the auto/manual setting) and a failure is a hard-fail input of CF-11; neither is built.

7. Cell lifecycle#

Default gates: OOS trades ≥ 30 · expectancy ≥ +0.15R · profit factor ≥ 1.2 · positive in ≥ 60% of folds · max drawdown ≤ 10R · calibration error ≤ 5 pp.

Built (CF-11 part 1, ADR 0221): the lifecycle recorder asks the readiness gates for two moves, Candidate to Paper (gate R: rows 1 to 12 and 14 of the 15-row edge checklist), the owner's re-enable Paused to Paper (gate R too) and Paper to Active (gate S: gate R, rows 13 and 15, and the label "Ready for the next phase"), and for no other (a gated move carries at most 18 evidence ids of its own, else 422). With the owner's switch confidence.readiness.enforce OFF (the default; no row means OFF) a failed gate only warns: the move goes on and the changelog row's evidence cites the stored gate snapshot and the failing rows; with it ON the move is refused (confidence.gate_failed, 409). See "Readiness scorecard" in the Built paragraphs of section 2 above.

8. Compute (M4 Pro, 48 GB)#

Full walk-forward (~20 strategies × 6 TFs × 2 sides × ~8 exits × 200 symbols × 5 years): a few hours in parallel .NET workers, monthly/weekends. Nightly incremental: minutes. Model training: minutes.

9. Execution feedback: learning from what actually happened#

The system learns from your real fills, not only from chart prices. Every graded trade carries both the plan prices and the executed prices, and the difference is decomposed every week:

Feedback What the system changes automatically
Labels use real fills Outcomes, R-multiples and MAE/MFE are computed from executed prices — the model learns what is achievable
Slippage per liquidity bucket Re-estimated weekly from your fills; used in every backtest, experiment and E[R]
Missed triggers (touched, not filled) Trigger buffer and order type (market vs limit-with-buffer) per instrument
Partial fills / broker rejections Lower the cell's capacity score; selection prefers tickets that can actually be filled
Your overrides Graded against the system's proposal (Reports → Decisions); feeds the autonomy recommendation (§13)

A setup that is profitable on plan prices but not after execution is treated as not profitable.

Built (P11-02, ADR 0150, spec P11-02): for a manual-live account each filled entry of an approved ticket is measured against the ticket's numeric level or, failing that, the plan grade's simulated entry (two reference kinds, never pooled), split into the delay before the order and the execution itself (positive is worse for the owner, delay + execution = total exactly), in ticks, basis points and R, and placed in a liquidity bucket by the 20-session average turnover (edges 10, 50, 150, 500 crore, an unknown bucket for no turnover). A weekly, paused service stores per bucket, side and style the n, the mean with a Student-t interval and the median with an order-statistic interval; below 10 observations the row is insufficient and has no mean; a proposal sentence naming slippage.ticks is stored at n >= 20 with a positive lower bound and read by nothing. The capacity score is the share of entry orders filled in full (full, partial, rejected and queue-miss orders counted; a limit the market never went through is a price miss, not counted), a Beta posterior with a credible interval, shrunk by bucket toward the account's rate. A closed round trip has an exact waterfall (plan, entry slippage, exit difference, actual gross, charges, net). Display only: no estimate reaches the simulator, selection or a cell, and the grade on executed prices (journal of live fills) waits for P11-01b.

10. Lessons: problem → diagnosis → fix → result#

When a monitor fires, the system opens a lesson — a record of what went wrong and what was done about it. Monitors: drawdown beyond the Monte Carlo band, drift (CUSUM on expectancy), calibration error, execution gap, decision drag, tail losses, data-quality failures.

Regime Setup fails in one volatility/trend regime Regime gate for that cell
Exits MFE after stop is high (stops inside noise); targets rarely hit Stop/target multiple (one variable)
Execution Fills worse than plan on illiquid names or at the open Liquidity filter, order type, entry window
Calibration Predicted win rate above actual Recalibration (PAV), stronger shrinkage prior
Decisions Your rejections or modifications cost R Auto-approval rule (autonomy)
Drift Edge decays over months Pause / retire; promote a shadow challenger
Events Gaps through stops around results Event filter, gap-aware sizing
Data Bad bars caused false signals Repair, fail-closed gate

Built (P6-16, ADR 0078): a lesson is a monitor finding from replayed history (growing loss tag, rolling expectancy below the band, CUSUM, calibration), opened once per finding (monitor, subject, evidence key; a growing tag by monitor, subject and tag kind while its lesson is open or linked), optionally linked to a hypothesis or experiment, and closed with the before and after numbers read from that fix's stored experiment (improved only when it was Better, otherwise no measurable change, which is a legitimate result). Not built: execution_gap and decision_drag (need real fills and decisions, P10/P11), data_quality, drawdown beyond the Monte Carlo band, tail losses, generated diagnoses (P9), the Learning screen chart (P8). The JSON example above is the intended shape; the stored lesson carries the finding text, the fix link and the experiment numbers instead of diagnosis and fix.how.

As built (P8-04, ADR 0103). The weekly decomposition is shown on the Learning screen as Plan vs actual: plan prices, entry slippage, charges, a remainder "not attributed" (exit slippage and anything else; the plan grade stores no simulated exit price) and the actual R after recorded charges, as means over the same tickets, insufficient below 15 graded tickets. Missed triggers are a count outside the chain. Exit slippage and the effect of overrides are listed as not decomposed. Nothing is graded on fills before paper and live trading, so today the chain is empty.

As built (P8-19, ADR 0130). A lesson can be linked to a hypothesis or an experiment, closed and dismissed, and a hypothesis approved, rejected, expired or accepted, from /owner-acts/lesson/:id and /owner-acts/hypothesis/:id once the owner switches learning_moves_ui on (off by default). Each move is the existing handler behind the same state table, with a reason and a fresh password, and writes one new row; closing a lesson takes no number (the result is read from the fix's stored experiment); accepting records a verdict and applies nothing. Proposing, starting and judging a hypothesis and opening lessons stay command-line verbs.

12. Tweak & re-run (experiments)#

You can change any learning knob and replay the last 12 months walk-forward before anything goes live.

Knob Current (demo) Effect
History window 18 months How much history each cell learns from
Recency half-life 120 days Older trades count less; shorter adapts faster but is noisier
Min trades to activate a cell 30 Evidence needed before a setup can trade
Shrinkage prior 35 trades Pulls small samples toward the strategy average
Breakout VIX gate 18 (or off) Regime gate for breakout cells
Intraday stop 1.2 × ATR Initial stop for 5m/15m setups
Selection threshold P(win) ≥ 52% Minimum calibrated probability to enter the plan
Slippage model 8 bps Cost assumed in backtests

Rules that keep experiments honest:

  1. Same trades, same days (common random numbers): candidate and current model differ only by your change.
  2. 12 out-of-sample folds; the verdict needs ≥ 8 folds better.
  3. Judged after the expected live gap: a cheaper slippage model cannot "win".
  4. Total R matters: higher expectancy on far fewer trades (total R/year down > 10%) is not "better".
  5. Multiple-testing guard: the required improvement rises with the number of experiments in 30 days (0.02R + 0.006R × √trials).
  6. Next step after "better" is shadow (paper alongside live for 30 trades); Apply needs your password, is audited, and is monitored for 60 trades with automatic rollback if live results fall below the old version.

As built (P8-18). Only two of the knobs above are replays of the simulator: an exit parameter of a stored run and a slippage assumption (dearer only). They can be started from the Learning area, one job at a time and at most three a day, after the owner has switched the capability experiments_ui on (off by default) and typed the password again for that start. The job rebuilds the stored run's request with exactly one field changed, replays it by the existing simulation, stores the candidate run (experiment:<job>) and judges it with the rules above; the replay time is measured and printed with the instrument count. The result is a proposal: nothing is applied to a strategy, a plan, a cell, a model or a setting, and no screen has an apply control. Learning-side knobs stay out (they are not replays). On today's data (about 3 folds) every result is at best Inconclusive, and the screen says so before the start. ADR 0129.

14. Smart rules: from learned signals to a safer plan#

Five learned rules sit between the scanner's candidates and tomorrow's plan, plus two feedback features. All run in the nightly pipeline without you; their state is shown on Plan review and Learning → Smart rules, and switching one off needs your password.

1 Grade on fills (§9) Executed prices from the broker (auto-journal); simulated fills in paper mode Every R, label and statistic; "R plan" kept alongside for the execution gap

3 Lessons + automatic rollback (§10, §12) Monitors; 60-trade monitoring after every change A change that does worse live is rolled back without you (example L9)

Honesty rules for these features: each rule's evidence (trade counts, R by bucket) is shown next to it; a rule that would remove half or more of the candidates it saw in one plan (as built in P7-06, per plan; the same test over the last 100 trades needs graded reserve outcomes, P7-13), or whose "skipped" trades are not worse than the "taken" ones over the last 100 trades, is flagged for review instead of being trusted blindly.

Cash cells only (P14-06, ADR 0193). None of the smart rules, the selection or the plan reads a cell whose expression is not cash; a stored option or pair cell changes no plan, ticket or order.
