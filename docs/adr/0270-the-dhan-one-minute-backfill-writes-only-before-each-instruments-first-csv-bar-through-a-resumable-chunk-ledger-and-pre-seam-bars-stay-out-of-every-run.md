# 0270 — The Dhan 1-minute backfill writes only before each instrument's first CSV bar, through a resumable chunk ledger, and pre-seam bars stay out of every run

- **Status:** Proposed (CF-04 Part B). Approved by the coordinator's recommendation under the owner's instruction of 2026-11-11 ("continue with your recommendation only"); **the owner confirmed Part B as built on 2026-11-11, in chat ("Yes, merge it"), with the fetch held OFF until CF-04d.** **Safety slice: YES** (network fetches of an already allow-listed Dhan endpoint). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it and done the owner acts below.
- **Date:** 2026-11-11.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0195](0195-the-nse-daily-history-is-an-off-by-default-fetch-behind-an-empty-owner-edited-allow-list-pattern-with-fail-closed-header-maps-and-a-daily-table-that-nothing-reads-yet.md) (Part A: the daily files whose ledger is the calendar here; the pattern of an OFF-by-default fetch), 0016 (the Dhan pipeline and the bar precedence: a Dhan bar replaces a CSV bar of the same minute), 0017 §5 (the factor rule), 0240 (recovery classes), 0243/0248 (the AU-12 entry gate), 0207 (the locked hold-out is not moved by older bars).

## Context
The owner's CSV history is about 2.1 years. Dhan serves 1-minute bars about five years back for active instruments, 90 days per request. The bar store lets a Dhan bar replace a stored CSV bar of the same minute, and the CSV bars are adjusted at source (to `csvAdjustedThrough`), so a backfill that overlapped the CSV span would overwrite the owner's adjusted bars with Dhan's raw ones. The research bound to depth (survivorship, adjustment, seam) is in the CF-04 spec; this ADR records what was built and where it differs.

## Decision
1. **Off by default, zero HTTP.** `Data:History:Backfill:Enabled` defaults to false. With it off the runner, the probe and the service return `history.disabled` (or end as skipped) before reading targets or sending a request (handler tests with a counting source). The service `history-backfill` starts paused.
2. **Writes only before the first CSV bar.** The seam of an instrument is the start of its oldest stored bar of source `csv`, **read again from the stored bars on every run** (never from the CSV file log, which an undo can move). The planner (`BackfillPlanner`) tiles `[today - Years, seam)` in India dates, half-open, in chunks of at most `ChunkDays` (default 90), newest first; the writer guard (`SeamGuard`) drops any bar at or after the seam and counts it `beyond_seam`. A first CSV bar earlier than the start gives no chunk. A seam that moved **earlier** than the recorded one (a CSV import reached before it) makes the instrument `seam_unresolved` until it is probed again.
3. **Never replaces the owner's CSV bars.** A Dhan bar goes in through the existing `source = 'dhan'` merge only for minutes before the seam, so no CSV minute is touched; an integration test on a real TimescaleDB compares a hash of the stored CSV rows before and after, with the fake Dhan answering with bars at and after the seam.
4. **Resumable chunk ledger.** `market.backfill_chunks (instrument_id, from_date, to_date, status planned|done|failed|empty, row_count, attempts, error, updated_at)`, key `(instrument_id, from_date)`. A run plans every pending chunk up front (so unfinished work is visible), then goes instrument by instrument, newest chunk first; `done` and `empty` chunks are never asked for again, a `failed` one at most `MaxRetries` (default 3) times in all, then it stays `failed` with its error and the instrument reads `partial`. One chunk failing never fails the run; a refused token stops it (`dhan_not_connected`) and leaves the chunk planned. A chunk whose window changed (the seam moved) is reset to planned; a planned chunk the plan no longer has is removed (the runtime role may delete in this table: it is a work ledger).
5. **Calendar before data.** The run refuses (`history.calendar_missing`, naming the range) unless every weekday of the planned range has an entry in Part A's daily-file ledger (`market.eod_files`: loaded, or recorded absent as a holiday). Part A is therefore a prerequisite of Part B (spec review fix 4). Zero-bar weekdays that are neither holidays nor specials remain Data quality findings.
6. **Disk and size.** Before it starts the run estimates rows (250 sessions a year, 375 minutes each) and requests, and refuses with `history.disk_low` and the numbers when free space is under `DiskHeadroomFactor` (2) times `BytesPerRowEstimate` (60) times the rows, or cannot be read. `xd data backfill --dry-run` prints the estimate and the refusal, if any, and requests and writes nothing. Times are minimums (retries, back-off and the run window add to them).
7. **Source-aware factor.** `SourceAwareFactors` decides by the bar's date against the seam (review fix 3): a bar before the seam is Dhan's and raw and takes every applied action whose ex-date is after it (including those inside the CSV span, because the CSV basis contains them); a bar at or after the seam takes only the actions after both the bar and `csvAdjustedThrough`. A bar on an ex-date takes no factor from it. It is pure, in `Domain/Market/History`, and used by the probe and the seam check.
8. **Overlap probe.** `xd data probe-seam [--instrument ID]` fetches Dhan's bars for the first `Probe:Sessions` (10) sessions **inside** the CSV span into memory (never written; the only row stored is the instrument's seam row), adjusts Dhan's bars by the pre-seam rule and the CSV bars by the CSV rule and compares the closes: pass when the median absolute difference is at most `MedianBps` (5) and at least `MinWithinMaxPct` (99 %) of the minutes are within `MaxBps` (50). A fail makes the instrument `seam_unresolved`: it is not backfilled. No overlapping minute proves nothing and changes nothing. Every instrument with an applied action is probed first, then others up to `Probe:Instruments` (20); instruments not probed are still backfilled, and are held out of every run (point 10).
9. **Seam continuity.** When every chunk of an instrument is finished, the adjusted close of the last pre-seam session is compared with the adjusted first CSV open; a move beyond ±20 % (ISS-20) is a `seam_jump` (stored, `xd data history`). Applied actions are already in the adjustment, so a jump that is left is one no applied action explains (a demerger, a rights issue): it is flagged, never repaired by the observed jump.
10. **Pre-seam bars stay out of every run (fail closed).** One shared read, `SeamAwareBars` (`Application/Market/History`), drops the bars before an instrument's **high-water** seam, and every simulation, learning, planning and report read of `IBarStore` uses it: the run and trade-card reader (`InstrumentInputReader`), the AU-13 training and feature extraction, the pairs run, the option-expression re-mark and condor handlers, the trade replay, the planning ticket source and the assisted-check days. An architecture test (IL scan) lists the only raw callers of `IBarStore.ReadAsync` (the Dhan pipeline, the repair, the quality checks and the backfill itself), so a new `IBarStore` path cannot read raw bars unnoticed. The raw-SQL readers are covered differently: they apply the SQL function `market.backfill_cut(instrument)` where listed here, and a source-scan architecture test fails any Infrastructure file that reads `market.bars_1m` or `market.bars_agg` without it unless the file is on the allow-list below. Readers with the cut: `DailySeries`, `BreadthSource`, `UniverseSeriesSource`, `DerivedUniverseReader`, `ContextBarSource` (regime index and VIX), `OpeningBarsQueries` (stored 09:15 bars), `BarQueries` (daily window and minute closes by instant) and `OptionArchiveReader` (underlying minutes). The cut is the **high-water mark**: `market.backfill_seams.high_water_at` is the latest seam ever recorded and is never lowered, so a CSV import that moves the seam earlier does not admit raw Dhan bars. The replay adjusts by one predicate, so `SourceAwareFactors` cannot be applied inside it without changing the replay and the bars hash; until CF-04d every pre-seam bar is excluded for every instrument. Not covered, and why: the pipeline that writes, aggregates, compares and counts bars (`BarStore`, `BarAggregates`, `BarComparer`, `BarDayCounts`, `BarCoverageSql`), quality (`QualityData`) and the observed calendar (`ObservedDays`) need every bar; the daily-versus-minute finding (`EodStore`) compares raw with raw; the liquidity universes (`DepthUniverse`, `OptionUnderlyingSetting`), the paper-ledger marks, portfolio prices and live execution context read recent windows only, never before a seam; `BarQueries` bar lists for display and the charts show stored bars as they are; the evaluation fixture seeder writes. A run made after the backfill has the same bars, hash and key as before it.
11. **Convergent service, visible to the AU-12 gate.** `history-backfill` (daily 22:00, off-hours window 20:00 to 06:00 India time, paused by default) declares `RecoveryClass.Convergent` (ADR 0240) and is pinned in `RecoveryClassTableTests`. The AU-12 entry gate (`SimAllBackfillGate`) reads `market.backfill_chunks`: while any instrument has a `planned` or `failed` chunk the backfill is incomplete (fail closed: the owner's recommendation to the review's question 10); instruments that are `seam_unresolved` or `seam_jump` count as complete without pre-seam bars.
12. **No broker, order or account endpoint** is touched; the Dhan source is the existing `IIntradayBarSource` over `/v2/charts/intraday` (already allow-listed); the throttle is the existing `Data:Dhan:RequestsPerSecond`. No new allow-list entry. Tokens never enter a log or a ledger row (the stored error is the client's short message, cut to 200 characters).
13. **Verbs, no API.** `xd data backfill [--instrument ID] [--dry-run]`, `xd data probe-seam [--instrument ID]`, `xd data history`. The `GET /api/data/history`, `POST .../backfill` and `.../probe` routes and the Data/Services "History" card of the spec are **not built** in this slice (the verbs and the Services screen's `history-backfill` entry are the surface); no `gen-api` change.

## Owner acts
**Running `xd data probe-seam` alone, before any fetch, writes the instrument's seam row and so starts the cut for that instrument** (its bars before the seam, none yet, are excluded from every run from then on); it fetches only in memory and stores no bar.

**Do not run the fetch until CF-04d (the source-aware replay) is built**: the owner confirmed Part B as built on 2026-11-11 and decided the fetch stays OFF. `xd data backfill --dry-run` and `xd data history` are safe. When CF-04d exists:
1. Run Part A's `xd data eod-load` far enough back that every weekday of the planned range has a ledger entry (the calendar guard), after Part A's own acts (ADR 0195).
2. Set `Data:History:Backfill:Enabled=true`; run `xd data backfill --dry-run`, then `xd data probe-seam`, then `xd data backfill` (or enable the service `history-backfill`). In the replay slice only instruments whose probe is `passed` (and that are not `seam_jump`) count as verified.
3. Decide, per `seam_jump` instrument, whether an unknown demerger or rights issue is to be added as a corporate action; nothing is repaired automatically.

## Consequences
- A wrong adjustment cannot reach a simulation, training or report: pre-seam bars are excluded for every instrument; the probe and the continuity check are evidence for the later wiring slice.
- A stopped night resumes where it stopped; a failed chunk is visible and blocks AU-12 until the owner resolves it (more retries after raising `MaxRetries`, or the instrument's probe).
- The seam read is one ordered primary-key scan per instrument per run (`ORDER BY ts LIMIT 1` with `source = 'csv'`). No extra index: the primary key is the only index of `bars_1m` by design (a second one slows every load; `DataTableGrantsTests` pins it). After a backfill the scan walks past that instrument's Dhan bars (at most about 270,000) before the first CSV bar; measure it before the backfill is enabled (CF-04d) and add a partial index then if it is slow.

## What is NOT yet met
The five-year 1-minute goal, the spec's "closes the data half of ISS-19" and the combinatorial purged cross-validation dependency are **not met**: no run sees a pre-seam bar until roadmap row **CF-04d** (source-aware replay, about 1.5 to 2 d) is built. The History API routes and card are row **CF-04e**.

## Known limits (and deviations from the spec text)
- Dhan's depth (5 years, 90 days a request, active instruments only) and whether its candles are split-adjusted are verified in practice only by the first probe.
- The 90-day window's end handling in the Dhan client is UNVERIFIED; the writer guard makes it harmless.
- The calendar is Part A's ledger, not `market.trading_days` rows derived from `daily_eod` (the weekday test is a stricter, simpler reading of review fix 4).
- Not built: the three API routes and the History card; Part C's run-record depth; a simulation that uses pre-seam bars with the source-aware factors (decision 10).
