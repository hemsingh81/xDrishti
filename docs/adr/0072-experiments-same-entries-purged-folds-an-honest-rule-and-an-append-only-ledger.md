## Context
Doc 09 §12 lets the owner change a learning knob and replay before anything goes live, and fixes five rules for honesty: same trades and days, enough out-of-sample folds, judged after the expected live gap, total R must not fall, and a bar that rises with the number of experiments. P6-00 scales the fold rule to the data (about 2.1 years). Nothing yet judges a replay or counts the looks.

## Decision
1. **An experiment judges two stored runs.** The baseline and the candidate are runs of the existing simulator on the same data; the candidate was made with one knob changed. The replay is deterministic, so "common random numbers" is implemented as **the same entries**: pairing by trade key, the candidate may skip entries and may not add one (`experiments.entries_added`). Cost assumptions must match except the knob: the cost sheet always, the fill basis unless the knob is the slippage (`experiments.assumptions_differ`).
2. **Judge (`ExperimentJudge`, Domain, pure).** Fold windows of the exit choice (first test 9 months in, 3-month tests). A fold is used with at least 10 trades per side. Per used fold, better means candidate mean R minus baseline mean R above `0.02 + 0.006 sqrt(trials)`. **Better** needs `ceil(2/3 x used)` folds better, the pooled mean-R gain above the same bar, total R after the live gap not more than 10 % below the baseline's, and **at least 6 used folds** (the floor cannot be lowered by the command line; below it the best outcome is `Inconclusive`). Other outcomes: `NotBetter` (with the reasons), `Inconclusive` (also with no used fold), `CheaperAssumption`.
3. **A cheaper slippage cannot win.** The direction is read from the two stored runs' canonical fills JSON (`slippage.ticks`, `bar_range_frac`, `stop_exit_extra_ticks`), never from the typed values (labels only): any component lower, or components that cannot be ordered, is `CheaperAssumption` regardless of the numbers; only the `slippage.*` fields may differ. The runs must also share engine, schema versions, window, universe, CSV acknowledgement and strategy id (and definition hash unless the knob is a parameter or exit knob). Together with decision 1 this closes the two ways to win by assuming less cost: changing the slippage down, or changing a cost assumption unseen.
4. **Expected live gap** is 0.02 R per trade, an assumption (P10 will measure live fills), taken off every trade of both sides in the totals. With the same entries it cancels in a per-trade difference and matters when the candidate trades less; it is printed and stored.
5. **The ledger is `research.experiments`**, insert-only for `xd_app` (`MakeAppendOnlyForAppRole`, grants test extended, `Down` drops it), with foreign keys to `sim_runs (run_key)`; a check makes `Better` impossible below 6 used folds. A sibling of `lab.test_ledger` rather than an extension: that ledger counts strategy variants of a Lab family by strategy hash, an experiment is a knob change between two runs of any strategy; the formulas differ (`1 + 0.25 log2 N` against `0.02 + 0.006 sqrt N`). **Trials** = experiments recorded in the last 30 days other than this one, plus one; `--trials` can only raise it. The append takes one global advisory lock, recounts inside it and, if the count moved, the judgement is redone with the ledger's number, so trial numbers are never shared; a concurrent identical request answers with the stored row. Every judged experiment is recorded whatever the outcome, because every look is a trial. The experiment key (hash of the run keys, knob and rules) makes the same experiment idempotent: it is returned as recorded and not counted twice.
6. **Hold-out** as P6-05: the last 6 months by default (0 keeps none; the report says so and the outcome can then be at most Inconclusive), cut with the purge of trades whose exit (the later of the two runs') reaches into it.
7. **Timing.** The record stores the milliseconds spent reading both runs and judging them (`compare_ms`, measured with `TimeProvider`). The replay of the candidate is a separate run and is **not measured**; the report states that and does not claim the 10-minute target.
8. **Surface.** `xd learn experiment` (writes through the handler), `GET /api/experiments` and `/{id}` (read only, no write endpoint, no screen). Log ids 4540 to 4543 (ledger ids, counts, codes, milliseconds).

## Consequences
- On today's data (about 3 development folds after the hold-out) no experiment can read `Better`; the report says so in its limits.
- The knob was chosen by looking at the same history, so the folds show spread over time and not out-of-sample proof for the choice (ISS-19).
- Refused or failed judgements are not recorded and are not trials. The fold minimum (10 trades a side) and the live gap (0.02 R) are floors a command line cannot lower.
- The trial count sees only experiments recorded here; tweaks tried by hand elsewhere are invisible to it.
- Knob changes that alter which entries exist are not comparable; they need a learning-side experiment (P6-10 and later).
- The 30-day window is read at the time of the judgement; an old experiment drops out of the count, the record keeps the trials it was judged with.
- Not verified here: the replay and comparison time on the owner's data.
