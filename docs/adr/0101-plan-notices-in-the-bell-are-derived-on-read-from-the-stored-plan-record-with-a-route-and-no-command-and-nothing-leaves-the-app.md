# 0101 — Plan notices in the bell are derived on read from the stored plan record, with a route and no command, and nothing leaves the app

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [P8-15](../specs/P8-15-plan-notifications.md), approved with the P8-00 questions answered as recommended; built 2026-10-24). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-10-24.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0030](0030-read-only-notices-retention-and-structured-failures.md) (notices are read only; one source of truth for the commands a notice may carry), [0018](0018-services-framework-and-editable-schedules.md) §7 (service notices derived on read), [0083](0083-the-plan-record-insert-only-state-trails-owner-only-approvals-lapse-by-default-and-no-order-by-name.md) and [0095](0095-the-owners-review-approves-from-the-stored-checklist-confirm-despite-names-the-codes-modify-only-lowers-and-the-cutoff-job-lapses.md) (plans, review, cutoff, lapse), [0086](0086-the-fail-closed-nightly-pipeline-gates-one-row-per-attempt-a-null-builder-and-a-paused-service.md) (plan runs), [0096](0096-the-pre-open-check-arms-only-approved-tickets-as-a-service-move-a-missing-input-invalidates-and-armed-tickets-join-the-feed-set-last.md) (armed, invalidated), [0093](0093-auto-journal-reads-the-stored-fills-matches-them-to-approved-tickets-by-account-instrument-side-and-day-and-stores-a-fill-grade-beside-the-plan-grade.md) (journal).

## Context
The bell was fed by accounts, data, services and the Portfolio. Nothing told the owner that a plan waits for review before its cutoff, that the nightly run failed closed, or that tickets lapsed or were armed. Reminders could be built with a scheduler and a stored notice table (the attention items of ADR 0027), or derived when the bell asks. P8-00 question 6 (SAFETY) asked about push: recommended and taken, not in P8.

## Decision
1. **Derived on read, nothing stored.** `GET /api/plans/notices` computes its items from the stored plan record, the newest plan run and the stored fills each time. There is no scheduler, no notice table, no reconcile, no migration. An item exists while its cause stands and is absent once the stored state changes; "resolves by itself" is the absence of the item, so two open tabs cannot disagree and the read cannot write (ADR 0030 decision 1). The minutes to the cutoff are counted at read time from the stored cutoff (rounded up), not from a timer.
2. **Six kinds.** `plan_awaiting_review` (plan published or in review, an undecided ticket, now before the cutoff; a warning from 30 minutes, information before), `plan_nightly_failed` (the newest attempt of the newest planned session failed closed: gate and reason, one line, 160 characters; an error), `plan_tickets_lapsed` (warning), `plan_tickets_armed` (information: "watched, no order is placed"), `plan_tickets_invalidated` (warning), `plan_fills_unmatched` (information: an approved ticket has a stored fill on the session day for the same account, symbol and side and no journal entry yet). Armed, invalidated and lapsed items are shown only while the session is today or later and the plan is not expired; every item is limited to the last three India days and three sessions (the newest version of each).
3. **A route, never a command.** Each item carries a route (`/plans?plan=<id>`; `/plans` for a run that failed closed, which has no plan). The item has no actions member at all, so the allow-list of bell commands (ADR 0030 decision 6, generated for the client) is untouched and no notice can start a job, approve, retry or journal anything. The client keeps only a route that is an in-app path and drops a kind it does not know.
4. **Counts and words, no plan content.** The text names counts, dates, the gate and the stored reason of a failed run; never a symbol, a size, a price or a strategy. Nothing is logged by the read.
5. **In-app only.** No push, no e-mail, no webhook, no call that leaves the app (P8-00 Q6). If push is ever wanted it is its own slice and ADR with no plan content in the payload. The bell polls once a minute and on window focus like the other sources; a decision on the Plans screen invalidates the key so the item leaves at once.
6. **Read only on the plan record.** The handler uses read members of `IPlanStore` (`ListAsync`, `ItemsAsync`, `ListRunsAsync`) and `IJournalStore` (`FillsAsync`, `EntriesAsync`), proved by a test that lists every member called. The no-order tripwire architecture tests scan the new code and stay as they were.

## Consequences
- A change in the stored state shows at the next poll (up to a minute) or at once after a decision made on the Plans screen.
- An item is not remembered: a lapsed or failed item disappears when the session is over or a later run publishes, with no "resolved" entry in the bell. The bell's own badge counts only unread, unresolved items; plan items are never marked resolved because they are removed.
- The fills item is a prompt, not a form: it points to the plan, and the journal (`xd plan journal`) still does the matching. It matches by account, normalised symbol, side and day only; where two tickets fit one fill the journal itself refuses to guess, and this item may still count both as unmatched.
- Cost: per read one plan list, one run list, the items of at most three plans, and (only for approved tickets of a session that has begun) one fill query and one entry read per such plan.
- The first-load guarded JavaScript is unchanged at 195.2 KB of 200 KB: the mapping lives in the lazy bell chunk and the query definition is a few lines in the shared API module.

## Not verified
Only the tests and the derivation were run; the wording was not read by the owner on a real plan day, and no browser was opened. The `plan_fills_unmatched` rule has not seen real broker fills.
