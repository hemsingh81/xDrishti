# 0178 — The option-archive quality checks read the recorded chains and rolling bars in bounded ranges and store insert-only verdicts, with an honest `unavailable` and no trading use

- **Status:** Proposed (built in P14-04 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-01, with the recommended answers: the thresholds below and the parity rate of 6.5 % as drafted; Services-page switches and the Data-quality card are a later frontend slice). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it and the first real recorded session has been checked (see "Pilot"). Not a safety decision: no broker call, no setting that sends, aggregates only; it is the first reader of the option archive and so changes the "no reader" rule of 0176 and 0177 in exactly one place.
- **Date:** 2026-11-01.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** reads what [0176](0176-the-option-chain-recorder-stores-five-minute-index-chains-and-a-daily-stock-pass-from-two-new-allow-listed-data-api-endpoints-behind-three-gates-its-own-cap-and-no-reader.md) (chain recorder) and [0177](0177-the-expired-options-backfill-fetches-rolling-atm-offset-bars-from-one-new-allow-listed-data-api-endpoint-at-night-behind-four-gates-after-an-owner-run-probe-its-own-cap-and-no-reader.md) (backfill) store and the reference data of [0175](0175-option-contracts-of-a-configured-underlying-list-are-kept-from-the-master-refresh-in-their-own-schema-with-a-dated-lot-history-and-dated-rule-epochs-never-in-the-master-list.md); copies the style of the bar quality checks of [0017](0017-trading-calendar-eod-schedule-quality-adjustments-and-csv-undo.md) (own result tables, one verdict per check) and the no-reader tests of [0135](0135-the-depth-recorder-stores-the-20-level-book-of-up-to-50-shares-about-once-a-second-behind-three-gates-a-disk-guard-and-no-reader.md) and 0173; no allow-list change (ADR 0133 and the pinned list are untouched).

## Context
P14-07 (the spread model) needs to count *usable* chain sessions (at least 60) instead of trusting raw rows, P14-08 needs the rolling keys that stitch, and the owner needs to see where the archive has holes. Nothing yet reads the archive, and the market quality tables (`market.quality_*`) are keyed per instrument with a foreign key, so they do not fit a verdict per underlying, session and series.

## Decision
1. **Pure checks in `Domain/Options/Quality`, one class per check, no I/O.** `ChainCoverageCheck`, `RollingCoverageCheck`, `ParityCheck`, `GreeksCheck`, `OiVolumeCheck`, `StitchingCheck`, the roll-up `QualityStatuses.Worst` and `SessionUsability`, `OutOfEra`. Every result is `pass`, `warn`, `fail` or `unavailable` with a reason from a closed list (`not_recorded`, `gap`, `no_two_sided_quote`, `no_underlying_bar`, `too_few_points`, `out_of_era`, plus `no_strike_step`, see the deviations). **`unavailable` never counts as `pass`**: the roll-up ranks fail above unavailable above warn above pass, nothing to roll up is unavailable, and an empty archive writes no row at all (nothing is invented).
2. **Thresholds (the drafted set, shares of 1).**

   | Check | Pass | Warn | Fail |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Chain coverage: ok requests / planned requests of the underlying on the India day | >= 95 % | >= 80 % | below |
   | Rolling coverage: stored bars / bars of a session (75 at 5 min, 25 at 15 min; `ceil(375 / interval)`) | >= 95 % | >= 80 % | below |
   | Parity: share of tested strikes with `res <= tol` | >= 90 % | >= 70 % | below |
   | IV and Greeks: violations / points | <= 2 % | > 2 % | > 10 % |
   | OI and volume: any volume fall | | OI jumps > 1 % of pairs | volume fall (an OI jump alone never fails) |
   | Stitching: strike jumps (> 2 strike steps between bars) / bars; spot mismatch (> 0.5 %) / compared bars | none | any jump, or mismatch > 1 % | jumps > 1 % of bars |

   Parity: forward from the strike nearest the money, `F = K0 + (Cmid - Pmid) / DF`, `DF = exp(-r T)`, `r = Options:Quality:Rate` = **0.065 (UNVERIFIED)**, `T` = calendar days to expiry / 365; `res = |(Cmid - Pmid) - DF (F - K)|`, `tol = (Cask - Cbid + Pask - Pbid) / 2 + 1 tick` (tick 5 paise, setting). NSE options are treated as European (UNVERIFIED for stocks), so there is no early-exercise term. IV and Greeks: `0 < iv < 300`; call delta in [0, 1], put delta in [-1, 0]; delta non-increasing in strike (tolerance 0.02) for both sides; gamma and vega >= 0; the at-the-money total variance `iv^2 T` across the expiries of one snapshot not falling by more than 5 %; a row flagged `bad_greeks` is one point and one violation; fewer than 5 points is `unavailable/too_few_points`.
3. **Deliberate readings of the spec (each the safer of the two).**
   - The spec's hand fixture says `tol = 0.2` for spreads of 0.1 each plus a tick of 0.05; the formula gives `(0.1 + 0.1) / 2 + 0.05 = 0.15`. **The formula is implemented**; the example's conclusion (a put mid of 6.0 gives `res = 0.9`) holds either way.
   - K0 itself is not tested (its residual is zero by construction and would inflate the share). The session's parity value is the share pooled over all snapshots and expiries of the day; a snapshot with fewer than 5 two-sided strikes is skipped and counted in the detail.
   - A crossed book (bid above ask, or flag `crossed`) and a zero or missing bid or ask are not a two-sided quote: they are left out and the crossed ones counted in the detail.
   - An OI change from a prior open interest of zero has no percent change and is not a jump (that would count every new strike).
   - **Usable is stricter than "not a fail"**: a chain session is usable when coverage passes **and parity passes or warns**; a rolling key-session when coverage passes and stitching passes or warns. An `unavailable` parity or stitching (too few quotes, unknown strike step) makes the session **unusable**, so the count P14-07 compares with 60 never rests on an unchecked session.
   - **Stitching on a 5-minute session fails on one strike jump** (1 of 75 bars is 1.33 %, above the 1 % line); only a 1-minute series (375 bars) can warn on a single jump. That is the literal rule of the spec, kept.
   - Results are stored **per session and underlying** (the `expiry` column stays null for chain results); the per-expiry numbers are in the pooled value. The seven reasons: `no_strike_step` is the one word added to the spec's closed list, because a share (or any underlying before its first stored contract) has no strike step and calling that `too_few_points` or `not_recorded` would mislead. The step is read only from a contract whose expiry is at most 100 days after the session date (today's contracts say nothing about 2021: strike steps are revised); otherwise NIFTY and BANKNIFTY fall back to the figures the backfill labels its bars by (50 and 100 rupees), and every other underlying is `unavailable/no_strike_step`.
4. **When it runs (bounded cost).** The service `options-quality` (scheduler of `xd-api`, **on by default** because it only reads the archive and writes its own result rows), daily at 16:15 India time on trading days, retries 1, catch-up of the same day, timeout 60 minutes. "After each backfill run" is covered by the next daily run: the run picks up every finished fetch window that has no result yet, so no code couples to the backfill (an architecture test forbids it). One run takes at most `MaxChainSessionsPerRun` (20) recorded sessions with no result and at most `MaxRollingWindowsPerRun` (5,000) finished fetch windows with no result (oldest first, fetched from the pending list in batches of 200 until the limit), and stops between two units when `MaxRunMinutes` (30) have passed; **the deadline is the real bound**, the count is a ceiling. A unit that throws is logged (`options.quality.failed`, event 6974, the exception type only), counted in the run note, skipped for the rest of the run and left pending; ten failed units end the run; a failure to write the closing run row is logged and never hides the exception that ended the run. A day counts as recorded when at least one `options.chain_requests` row exists for the India day (not by `options.chain_sessions`: a crashed day has an end row of zero requests, or none, and is still checked). Trading days with no request within `LookbackDays` (10) of today, after the first recorded day, are reported once per underlying as `unavailable/not_recorded`. A rolling day is a session when the calendar says so and, if any series of the underlying has a stored bar in the window, when one has a bar that day: a past holiday the calendar does not know gets no verdict, and a window with no bar at all is judged by the calendar. "Run now" starts a `manual` run through the ordinary path.
5. **Reads are bounded ranges.** The reader never touches a hypertable without a time range and an underlying: one India day for chain snapshots (`underlying_id` and `ts` range, about 23 k rows for NIFTY), one 30-day window of one series for rolling bars, the underlying's 1-minute closes of that window read once and kept for the neighbouring keys (windows are taken in the order window, underlying), every other read by the day or a limit. One NIFTY-sized session of 23,136 rows is read and checked in well under the 2 s budget on the test container (the whole test, seeding included, 0.8 s; UNVERIFIED on the production disk).
6. **Schema (migration `AddOptionArchiveQuality`, raw SQL, `Down` drops both).** `options.quality_runs(id, trigger in (daily, manual), started_at, ended_at, sessions, results, note)` and `options.quality_results(run_id, session_date, underlying_id, source in (chain, rolling), expiry, fetch_key, check_code, status, reason, value, threshold, n, detail jsonb)` with checks on every coded column (an `unavailable` without a reason, a rolling result without a key and a chain result with one are refused by the database), a unique key on `(run_id, underlying_id, session_date, source, check_code, fetch_key)` and the indexes `(underlying_id, session_date DESC)`, `(source, session_date)` and `(fetch_key, session_date)`. `fetch_key` is `underlying id:expiry flag + code:offset:side:interval`. **Both tables are insert-only for `xd_app`** (granted table by table, then `MakeAppendOnlyForAppRole`): the run id is taken from the identity sequence at the start of a run (`USAGE` on `options.quality_runs_id_seq` is the one extra right), the results are copied in per session, and the run row is inserted when the run ends, so a crashed run leaves valid results and no run row. A re-run writes new rows; readers take the newest run per session and check. `xd_exec`, `xd_mcp_read` and `xd_mcp_sql` are granted nothing. `xd_app` already reads the archive tables (their grants of 0176 and 0177 include SELECT); no grant on them changes. About 1 M rows and under 0.3 GB a year (UNVERIFIED), so no cap; the free floor is untouched.
7. **One route, aggregates only.** `GET /api/options/coverage?underlying&from&to` returns per session `{date, source, fetchKey?, checks: [{code, status, value?, threshold?, reason?}], usable}`, `usableChainSessions` (cumulative up to `to`, counted over every stored chain session of the underlying, not only the range) and `truncated` (more than 5,000 rolling sessions exist than are listed; the chain list is never cut). It returns **no price, IV or Greek** (a test pins the field names), answers 400 `options.invalid_query` for a missing or unknown underlying (a bare ticker means `NSE:`), an inverted range or more than 400 days, and an empty list when nothing is recorded. The route needs a session like every route.
8. **The "no reader" rule changes in exactly one place, and stays strict.** The quality code is the only reader of `options.chain_snapshots`, `chain_requests`, `chain_sessions`, `rolling_bars` and `fetch_log` besides the recorders' own resume reads, and the only reader of `options.contracts`, `lot_sizes` and `market_epochs`. The older tests were changed deliberately and minimally to name exactly the new file, and the new test now pins the readers **per table** (chain_snapshots and chain_requests: the chain store and the quality reader; chain_sessions: the chain store only; rolling_bars: the bar store and the quality reader; fetch_log: the fetch log, the bar store and the quality reader). The three older tests: `OptionArchiveReader.cs` (in the chain and backfill table tests, which now also catch a table name inside a multi-line SQL string) and `OptionReference.cs` (in the reference-table test); a third file fails them. The new `OptionQualityArchitectureTests` pins: no decision, live, recorder, simulation, strategy, indicator or execution code (and no assembly outside the DI roots, the JSON context and the one route) references a quality type; the quality code references no network, order, plan, live-session, broker, depth, tick or recorder type; the reader and the reference issue only SELECTs; the store only inserts; only the reader names the archive tables and only the reader and the store name the result tables; the ports have exactly the listed methods; the API names no archive or result table; the log events (6971 to 6973: `options.quality.run|session|done`) carry counts only.
9. **Settings `Options:Quality:*`** (bound on `xd-api`, bounds checked at start-up): `Rate` 0.065 (0 to 0.2), `MaxIv` 300, `OiJumpPct` 50, `CoveragePass` 0.95, `CoverageWarn` 0.80, `ParityPass` 0.90, `ParityWarn` 0.70, `GreeksWarn` 0.02, `GreeksFail` 0.10, `OiWarnShare` 0.01, `OiJumpMinLots` 10, `TickPaise` 5, `LookbackDays` 10, `MaxChainSessionsPerRun` 20, `MaxRollingWindowsPerRun` 200, `MaxRunMinutes` 30.

## Runbook (owner)
1. Nothing to switch on: the service `options-quality` is on, 16:15 India time on trading days. Its runs are on the Services screen.
2. **Check:** `GET /api/options/coverage?underlying=NSE:NIFTY&from=2026-11-02&to=2026-11-30`; Seq events `options.quality.*` (6971-6973); `SELECT trigger, started_at, sessions, results, note FROM options.quality_runs ORDER BY id DESC LIMIT 5`.
3. **A session shows `unavailable`:** `not_recorded` - the recorder was off or the day has no session row; `no_two_sided_quote` / `too_few_points` - the chain had too few bid-and-ask strikes; `no_strike_step` - no stored contract gives the underlying's strike step for the date; `out_of_era` - the series did not exist (the Bank Nifty weekly after 2024-11-20, from the unconfirmed epoch).
4. **Run again by hand:** "Run now" on the Services screen (trigger `manual`). It checks only what has no result yet; to see the effect of a changed setting, results already written stay (insert-only) and nothing is re-checked.
5. A run that stops at its bounds says so in its note; the next run goes on.

## Consequences
- P14-07 counts `usableChainSessions` from this route's store; P14-08 counts usable rolling key-sessions; P14-16 reports coverage from the same rows.
- Until the pilot, the rate `r`, the European-style assumption for stocks, the bar timestamp meaning (the end minute is `ts + interval - 1` minute, valid only if `ts` is the bar start; UNVERIFIED until the backfill probe) and the epoch dates (unconfirmed) are assumptions that can bias a verdict slightly; every verdict is a report, never a repair, and nothing is deleted or re-fetched.
- Vendor Greeks may come from another model, so a delta-order violation can be the vendor's, not ours.
- **Catching up after the full backfill** (31,444 windows): at the ceiling of 5,000 windows a run needs at least 7 daily runs (31,444 / 5,000 = 6.3), and only if a window costs under 0.36 s (30 minutes / 5,000); at 1 s per window a run does 1,800 and it takes 18 runs. The cost per window is UNVERIFIED until measured on the real disk; read `sessions` and `note` of `options.quality_runs` to see which bound is binding, and raise `MaxRunMinutes` (up to 120) to go faster. The daily run takes the oldest first, so nothing is skipped, only late.
- "The quality code cannot write the archive" rests on the code and the architecture tests, not on the database: `xd_app` keeps INSERT on the recorders' tables (the recorders run as `xd_app`), so the database cannot refuse an insert from the quality code.
