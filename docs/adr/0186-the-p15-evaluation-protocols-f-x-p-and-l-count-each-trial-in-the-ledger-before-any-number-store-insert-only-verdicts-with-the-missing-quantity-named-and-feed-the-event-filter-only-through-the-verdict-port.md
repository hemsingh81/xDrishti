# 0186 — The P15 evaluation protocols F, X, P and L count each trial in the ledger before any number, store insert-only verdicts with the missing quantity named, and feed the event filter only through the verdict port

- **Status:** Proposed (built in P15-10 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-02). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no** for the evaluation (read-only over stored runs, writes only its own records and ledger rows). The one path with an effect is the event-filter auto option of P15-06, which this slice only feeds through `IEventFilterVerdict`; that path stays default OFF and owner-armed.
- **Date:** 2026-11-02.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0059](0059-the-test-ledger-and-the-multiple-testing-adjustment.md) (ledger), [0069](0069-learned-entry-windows-are-proposals-chosen-on-purged-folds.md) (entry-window pattern), [0080](0080-phase-verdict-words.md) (KEEP, DROP, INCONCLUSIVE, NOT_EVALUABLE), [0182](0182-the-event-risk-filter-reads-the-results-calendar-in-the-simulator-and-behind-an-owner-switch-in-the-plan-and-the-results-loss-tag-describes-the-past-and-unavailable-is-never-a-pass.md) (fail closed; the share lost to `no_upcoming_entry` must be reported), [0183](0183-the-feature-set-comparison-fits-one-recipe-twice-on-identical-rows-widens-its-margins-for-the-ledger-count-and-reads-the-hold-out-once.md) (the comparison core and its table), [0185](0185-the-event-and-fundamental-feature-sets-read-only-what-was-public-at-the-decision-instant-store-nan-with-a-closed-reason-for-every-unknown-and-the-frame-gets-two-results-day-features-with-engine-sim-8.md) (the extraction record this slice gates on).

## Context
P15 builds a results calendar, a parser, two feature sets, an LLM flag and a PEAD strategy. Each must be judged by a rule fixed before the numbers are read, counted among the looks, and answered honestly when the history is too thin. Today the history is thin: the parser is synthetic, there are no PEAD events, no eval run grades the flag, and about eight seasons of bars exist.

## Decision
1. **One verb, four pure protocols.** `xd learn extension-eval --family filter:results|ext:ev-1|ext:fu-1|ext:llm-1|pead [--holdout]` calls one handler that routes to the domain functions `ProtocolF`, `ProtocolX` (an adapter over the P13-05 core with quarterly folds and season resampling), `ProtocolP` and `ProtocolL`. Every verdict is KEEP, DROP, INCONCLUSIVE or NOT_EVALUABLE, with the missing quantity named (`skipped trades 56 of 100`, `seasons 19 of 20`, `labels 199 of 200`). A quantity that is not a value is the text `unavailable` in the stored JSON, never a number.
2. **Ledger first.** Each counted trial is appended to `lab.test_ledger` (families `filter:results`, `ext:<version>`, `pead`) before its metric is computed; N is the family's distinct trial hashes; every margin is multiplied by `Factor(N)`. A trial hash identifies the variant and its options, not the data, so N does not grow when the same variant is run again; the ledger run id includes the data window, so a re-run on the same data appends nothing and a record id (SHA-256 of the inputs and the result) is the same. A thin evaluation that stops before any metric (no extraction record, no events) counts no trial. A ledger that cannot be written stops the evaluation before anything is stored.
3. **F classifies, it does not compare two runs.** The population is the closed trades of the stored runs whose record does not name `not_event_day`, one per trade key, gross R from the filled prices. Each trade is skipped (the next results event within N sessions), taken (outside), or unavailable by reason (`no_entry`, `only_cancelled`, `no_upcoming_entry`), with the one window rule of the filter (`ResultsWindow`). The "removes at most half" rule counts skipped plus all unavailable, because the filter fails closed (ADR 0182); the report prints every share and the share lost to `no_upcoming_entry`. KEEP needs at least 100 skipped trades, at least 4 development seasons that have both sets (otherwise NOT_EVALUABLE `judged seasons n of 4`), the mean gap (day-block, one-sided 90 % lower bound, at least 0.03 R times the factor) or the tail test (share with R at most -1.5: the lower Wilson bound of the skipped share at least twice the upper Wilson bound of the taken share; this test is not widened by the factor, a limit below), the removal rule, and the same direction in two thirds of the seasons that have both sets. A verdict is stored per N in {1, 2, 3}.
4. **X is gated by the extraction record before any number.** For `fu-1`, a record whose parser version is missing, empty or ends in `-synthetic`, or any feature with `parser_unverified` above zero, or counts that cannot be read, is NOT_EVALUABLE `parser_unverified`; a record that does not match the run (rows, version) or the current lag or parser is NOT_EVALUABLE `features_stale`; no record is `no_extraction`. A gated evaluation reads no row and counts no trial. `llm-1` is evaluated only after protocol L has passed; until P15-08 builds its rows the X record names that.
5. **P and L.** P chooses the variant of four on the first eight seasons and freezes it in the record, judges the next seasons (at least 8, 250 matched top-bucket events, season-cluster lower bound of at least 0.05 R times the factor, two thirds of seasons positive) and needs 20 seasons; it reads its events through a port that the PEAD strategy will implement (today: none, NOT_EVALUABLE `seasons 0 of 20`). L reads the newest accepted eval run's set D grade through a port that P15-08 will implement (today: none, NOT_EVALUABLE) and never recomputes it: KEEP iff it passed with at least 200 labels, DROP iff a gating run with at least 200 labels failed.
6. **The last four seasons are reserved.** Development uses everything before the fourth-last season with data (rows are also purged: resolved plus one bar before the first hold-out decision). `--holdout` reads the reserved seasons once, only after a development KEEP, and writes a second record that says so and names the development record it confirms (`development_record` in its sources). For F the one reading is **per family**, not per window: all windows judge the same reserved seasons, so any window's reading spends it; for X and P it is per family and version key. A second `--holdout` is refused `extensions.holdout_used` **before** any record is written or the auto rule run (the table's partial unique index per family and version key is the second guard). The F hold-out confirms with its own interval: the day-block lower bound of the mean gap above zero, or the Wilson interval of the skipped tail share clear of the taken one, with at least 30 skipped and 30 taken trades. With four seasons or fewer nothing is left for development and the answer is NOT_EVALUABLE.
7. **The verdict port, and the auto turn-ON needs a confirmed KEEP (owner decision, amends spec criterion 6).** `EventFilterVerdictReader` replaces the unavailable default. It returns the newest stored protocol-F verdict for the rule version and **the N of the effective switch**, with the record id as the reference; a verdict for another N or rule version, or none, is null (unavailable), and `NOT_EVALUABLE` is a verdict that is never read as KEEP. The verdict carries `HoldOutConfirmed`: true for a hold-out record that is KEEP, and for a development record only when a stored hold-out KEEP names that very development record, so a failed hold-out followed by new data and a new development KEEP is not confirmed. `EventFilterAutoDecision` turns the filter ON only for a KEEP with `HoldOutConfirmed` (a development KEEP alone is `keep_not_confirmed`), and OFF on any explicit non-KEEP as before; a migration makes the trigger require `verdict.state = KEEP` and `verdict.hold_out_confirmed = true` in the row's snapshot for an automatic turn-on, so the database agrees with the pure rule (cross-check test with mutants kept). The verb runs the existing auto rule **once, after all the new records of a run** (development and hold-out), never between them, so there is no ON-then-OFF flapping; the answer is printed. It acts only with `approval = auto` (set by the owner with the fresh password), records its row as `auto`, never changes N or the approval. `GET /api/selection/event-filter` shows `KEEP_NOT_CONFIRMED` for a development KEEP. No setting allows an order.
8. **No decision use beyond the port.** The protocol code and records are named by the four evaluations, the verb, the two read endpoints (`GET /api/research/extensions`, `/{id}`) and the reader; selection, planning, the scorer and Execution name none of them (an architecture test pins the sets). A KEEP for `ev-1`, `fu-1`, `llm-1` or `pead` is a record: no slice turns it into a scoring switch (owner decision 3 of the spec).
9. **One migration, no table.** `RequireConfirmedKeepForAutoOn` replaces the trigger function of `filings.event_filter_decisions` (one more check, no grant change, nothing for `xd_exec`); the table `research.extension_evaluations` (insert-only for `xd_app`, nothing for `xd_exec` or the read roles) is P13-05's.
10. **Counts per style.** The X record's estimates carry `style_counts`: rows and paired candidates of the intraday and swing trades (counts only; the comparison is pooled and makes no per-style verdict).
11. **The population of F** is read from the run record's structured `events=` line (only a run that names `not_event_day` has one), a duplicate trade key takes the newest engine, and the limits text states the selection bias and the correlation of strategies on the same instrument and day.

## Consequences
- With today's data every family answers NOT_EVALUABLE with the named reason, F possibly excepted if the owner's calendar covers enough stored runs; it may well be a DROP, because a calendar that cannot say between a meeting and the next intimation makes the fail-closed filter remove more than half of the trades.
- The hold-out of P is a simpler check than the development rule (the sign of the gain), with a floor of 30 matched events that this ADR sets; the F hold-out has its own interval and floors of 30 skipped and 30 taken trades. The owner may change the floors before a hold-out is ever read.
- The style of a run for the cell features of the comparison is derived (intraday if no trade is held past its first session) and the exit label is fixed, because the cells are not what the comparison judges; the intraday and swing split is reported as counts, not as separate verdicts.

## Known limits
- Gross R unless the owner's checked charge sheet exists (ISS-25); survivorship (ISS-22) carries into every protocol.
- The `not_event_day` test of a run reads its stored record text for the filter name.
- The calendar hash in the X record comes from the extraction records, not from a fresh read; stale calendar content with the same versions is not detected.
- Seasons, not trades, are the independent units; intervals are wide.
- **The auto OFF is a one-way latch once the hold-out is spent.** The reserved seasons are read once. After that, any newer explicit non-KEEP verdict (a transient NOT_EVALUABLE from thin new data included) turns an automatically enabled filter OFF, and it cannot be turned ON again automatically until a new hold-out exists; the owner re-enables it by hand.
- The tail test of F compares Wilson intervals and is not widened by `Factor(N)`; widening it by the factor would need far more trades than a small book has.
