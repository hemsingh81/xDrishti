# 0096 — The pre-open check arms only approved tickets as a service move; a missing input invalidates; armed tickets join the feed's set last and leave at expiry

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [P7-11](../specs/P7-11-pre-open-check-arming-and-plan-tickets-in-the-active-set.md), approved under the owner's standing run instruction and the owner's binding decisions of 2026-10-23; built 2026-10-24). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-10-24.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0083](0083-the-plan-record-insert-only-state-trails-owner-only-approvals-lapse-by-default-and-no-order-by-name.md) (the table and triggers that allow Approved to Armed, Invalidated and Expired are used unchanged), [0091](0091-auto-approval-is-an-option-behind-an-owner-setting-default-off-a-new-approval-kind-the-database-checks.md) (the autonomy setting is not read here), [0095](0095-the-owners-review-approves-from-the-stored-checklist-confirm-despite-names-the-codes-modify-only-lowers-and-the-cutoff-job-lapses.md) (the cutoff job runs before this), [0024](0024-live-feed-xd-feed-and-live-prices.md) (the active set).

## Context
P7-10 left the plan with Approved tickets after the review cutoff. Doc 04 section 4.2 says only Approved to Armed tickets enter the live active set and alerts; doc 10 section 7 has an optional pre-open check at 09:10 that invalidates on a gap (and, in the design, promotes from the reserve). The owner's binding decisions: P7 places no order; paper accounts only; no reserve promotion at all; approvals are manual or by the P7-12 setting (default OFF).

## Decision
1. **Arming is a service move on the existing table.** `RunPreOpenCheckHandler` moves each ticket that is `Approved` to `Armed` or `Invalidated` through `MoveTicketHandler` as the service actor `system:preopen` (the table lets only services make those moves; the database holds the legal pairs, the chain and the plan triggers). It then moves the plan to Armed (a ticket is armed) or Expired (none left). **No migration**: a test shows the database refusing an armed row after proposed, modified, rejected and lapsed.
2. **It approves nothing and promotes nothing.** It looks only at Approved tickets; it does not read the autonomy settings; the reserve is not a source. An architecture test fails if the pre-open types depend on the autonomy settings or the selection builder, or have a member, field or parameter named for promotion or the reserve.
3. **A pure rule.** `PreOpenRules.Decide`: `|last - previous close| > 0.5 x ATR` invalidates (either direction); a missing or unusable price, previous close or range invalidates with `unavailable_*`. Never armed on a missing input. The numbers are in the state row's reason. The ban list, surveillance and price band have no source and are not re-checked (the approval carried them as unavailable).
4. **Existing read-only data only.** `PreOpenSource` joins `IQuoteSource` (the market quote with the primary Data account, as the holdings poll) and the stored day bars before today. No new allow-list entry, endpoint or credential; a failed request is a snapshot with a safe sentence, and everything is then invalidated.
5. **The feed.** `ArmedTicketsProvider` is one more scoped `IActiveSetProvider` (reason `ArmedTicket`, quote mode): the Armed tickets of the plan dated today in India. The builder ranks a set that has only this reason **after** alerts, indices and holdings, so when `Live:MaxInstruments` cuts, the armed ticket is the one cut (existing warning) and no other member is displaced; the provider orders armed tickets by rank with a value of at most 1 (so merging with a held share cannot lift a holding). A read failure proposes nothing. The set is unchanged with nothing armed.
6. **Expiry.** `plan-expiry` (15:40, paused) moves Armed and never-armed Approved tickets and the plan to Expired as `system:expiry` at or after the close, and closes earlier open plans. The provider reads state, so the instrument leaves the set at the next recompute (a `NOTIFY` follows arming and expiry).
7. **Cap.** At most 50 armed per session, the lowest rank refused with `armed_cap_exceeded`.
8. **Owner told by an inbox row** (`ticket_invalidated_preopen`, insert-only, one per ticket, text carries "No order was placed and none exists"). The services are `preopen` (09:10, paused, no catch-up, refuses at or after 09:15) and `plan-expiry`. Log ids 4780 to 4787.
9. **Wording.** Every arming row's reason begins with the pinned sentence "Armed: watched with alerts. No order is placed and none exists. A stop is only an alert." (a test pins it).

10. **Safety-review changes.** (a) The check refuses before 09:08 India time (`plans.preopen_early`). (b) The same quote answer is read for `last_trade_time` and `volume` (no new endpoint, field request or credential): a price whose trade time is missing, unreadable, not today or outside 09:00 to 09:15 is `unavailable_quote`. (c) Batch guard: from 3 readings, at least 80 % with last exactly equal to the previous close marks the batch stale and invalidates those tickets (`stale_quote`). (d) Every arming reason says the last price may be the previous close before the open, that ban list, surveillance and price band were not re-checked, and that price alerts on trigger and stop are not built yet. (e) Expiry never moves a plan whose ticket move failed; (f) the NOTIFY and the inbox write are non-fatal; (g) the ATR needs the previous trading session as the newest bar.
11. **Precondition (not done).** Before the owner switches `preopen` on, a real quote answer at about 09:10 must be captured and recorded (format of `last_trade_time`, meaning of `last_price` before the open). That is an owner/operator task and a follow-up; nothing here claims it was done.

## Consequences
- With both services paused (the default) nothing changes anywhere: Approved tickets stay Approved and the feed is as before.
- **Price-crossing alerts are not built**: the trigger and stop are text on the ticket, and no notification port exists. Follow-up: numeric levels on the ticket and a notification port.
- The gap is measured against the previous close, not against the trigger. If Dhan's last price before 09:15 is the previous close the gap reads 0 and the rule passes; this was not verified on a live morning.
- `gap_invalidate_atr` (0.5) and the cap (50) are constants until a settings home exists.
- The feed host and the API both register the new ports (`IArmedTicketReader`, `IInstrumentSecurities`); the plan record is read by the feed process.
- **Shortcut:** the ATR is a plain mean of 14 unadjusted daily true ranges; upgrade when an adjusted series is wired in.
- Not verified: behaviour on the owner's machine; no plan has been armed by a real morning run.
