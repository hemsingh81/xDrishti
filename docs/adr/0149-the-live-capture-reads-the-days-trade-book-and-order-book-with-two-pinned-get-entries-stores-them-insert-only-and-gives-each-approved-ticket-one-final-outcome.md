# 0149 - The live capture reads the day's trade book and order book with two pinned GET entries, stores them insert-only and gives each approved ticket one final outcome

- **Status:** Accepted (decided in P11-01 under the owner's instruction of 2026-10-28 to build the slice; spec [P11-01](../specs/P11-01-manual-live-fill-capture.md), with its "Amendments at build").
- **Date:** 2026-10-28.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0025](0025-dhan-trade-history-and-ledger-sync.md) (intraday trades stay out of `portfolio.transactions`), [0093](0093-auto-journal-reads-the-stored-fills-matches-them-to-approved-tickets-by-account-instrument-side-and-day-and-stores-a-fill-grade-beside-the-plan-grade.md) (the matcher is reused unchanged), [0097](0097-the-p7-phase-check-ten-consecutive-scheduled-sessions-from-stored-plan-runs-and-never-met-on-fewer.md) and [0133](0133-new-broker-endpoints-enter-the-allow-list-in-the-commit-of-the-slice-that-uses-them-index-and-vix-history-needs-none.md) (the pin and its change in the same commit), [0147](0147-the-compliance-record-is-one-insert-only-owner-written-store-for-the-static-ip-sebi-and-dhan-read-policy-enquiry.md), [0148](0148-the-ramp-and-the-live-switches-are-insert-only-owner-acts-live-accounts-are-sized-in-their-own-pool-at-the-step-scale-and-the-database-guards-the-live-allocation-and-the-live-auto-approval.md) (the live-ready gate checks the two services this slice registers).

## Context
A manual-live account trades by hand in Dhan's own app. To judge the live phase, the system must know what the owner actually did that day, matched to the tickets they approved, without ever placing, changing or cancelling an order.

## Decision
1. **Two read-only entries join the pinned allow-list**, in this commit: `GET api.dhan.co/v2/trades` (the day's trade book) and `GET api.dhan.co/v2/orders` (the day's order book). Each is an exact path with no id and no query (`/v2/orders/1`, `/v2/orders?x=1`, `/v2/trades?x=1`, a trailing slash, another host or port, HTTP stay refused). Every order write (POST, PUT, DELETE), the `super` and `forever` order paths, positions, funds, the kill switch and the other market-feed paths stay refused. `PlanPhaseCheck.PinnedAllowList` gains the two lines; `RefusedProbes` loses `GET /v2/orders` and gains `GET /v2/orders/1`, `GET /v2/orders?x=1` and `POST /v2/trades`; the tests that pinned the old list (`BrokerAllowListTests`, `BrokerEndpointsTests`, `PlanPhaseCheckTests`, `DhanTradesClientTests`) change with it. One dot-segment case changed meaning: `/v2/trades/../orders` resolves to the now-allowed order book and is no longer a refused example; `/v2/trades/../positions` replaces it. The no-order tripwire is unchanged and green: no type, member or route is named for an order action ("book" is not an order verb).
2. **The order book has its own switch** (`Live:Capture:OrderBook:Enabled`, OFF). Without it, or when it fails, a poll is `partial` and every order-dependent field is `unavailable`: a ticket whose replay traded but has no fill is `missed_unknown`, never `missed_no_order`; protection is `unavailable`. Order evidence for a day means at least one poll that read both books (`ok`).
3. **Five insert-only tables** (`trading.live_capture_runs`, `live_fills`, `live_fill_charges`, `live_orders`, `live_ticket_outcomes`; `GrantTableToAppRole` + `MakeAppendOnlyForAppRole`). Same trade seen ten times is one row (`uq_live_fills_account_trade`); an order is one row per (status, filled quantity) revision; one outcome per (ticket, account). The tables refuse a fill without a side, a status outside the seven, a missed R on an outcome that is not a missed one, and `missed_no_order` without order evidence.
4. **Matching reuses `JournalMatcher` unchanged** (`LiveFillMatcher` wraps it): same account, symbol, side and planned day, approved before the fill; a fill that fits two tickets stays unmatched with the reason. The tolerances (`Live:Match`: chased entry 0.25 R or 1.0 %, size below 90 %) only flag. The stop window (`Live:Protect:WithinSeconds`, 300) decides `protected` / `unprotected` (intraday products) / `protection_unknown` (delivery: forever orders are not read).
5. **One outcome per ticket, final, nine words** (filled, partial, over_allocation, not_triggered, missed_no_order, missed_unfilled, rejected, missed_unknown, unknown). A ticket without a fill whose plan grade is not stored writes nothing and is tried again; after five trading sessions it is `unknown`. Only the two missed outcomes carry the plan grade's R.
6. **Two services, both registered paused**: `live-capture` (interval every `PollSeconds` rounded up to whole minutes, 09:10 to 15:40, trading days; the handler also refuses outside the window unless the owner polls by hand) and `live-settle` (after `portfolio-broker-history`; the last seven days are looked at). `Live:Capture:Enabled` (OFF) only seeds them at the first start. A rate limit is a quiet retry recorded as `rate_limited`; an expired token is `token_expired` and fails the run; neither is ever an empty day. The live-ready gate of ADR 0148 now finds both services (paused), so a switch to `manual_live` still needs the owner to enable them.
7. **Health of a day** is `healthy`, `gap` (no successful poll for more than 30 minutes of 09:10-15:40, or the latest settle failed) or `not_run` (no run at all).
8. **API and CLI are read only**: `GET /api/live-trading/accounts/{accountId}/capture` and `GET /api/live-trading/outcomes`; `xd live capture` (one poll and one settle, GET only) and `xd live outcomes`. There is no owner write in this slice, so `Live:Writes:Enabled` and the fresh password are not involved; no setting allows an order.

## Amendments at build (what the spec left open or got wrong)
- **`journal_fills` is not changed** and the live fills are not journaled in this slice. The spec's `live_fill_id` / `transaction_id` either-or change touches the journal's grader, its reader and the double-journal rule at once; the matching and the outcomes do not need it. The journaling of live fills (and with it the fill grade, slippage and capacity of P11-02) is **P11-01b**. `JournalPlansHandler` is untouched: a CNC fill of a manual-live account still reaches the journal through `portfolio.transactions` as before, so no fill is journaled twice.
- **The history read at settle keeps the history mapper** (delivery trades only), so the charges of intraday fills stay `unavailable` (no charges row). The agreement of the history with the trade book is reported in the settle's summary and the log, not stored (the runs table is insert-only and has no column for it); whether Dhan's history returns today's trades is still unknown, and a miss is worded as a miss.
- **`IHaltSource` is not built** (it returned "none known" until P11-04b); P11-04b adds it.
- **The settle does not grade today's session** (`GradePlansCommand` refuses today); it grades a finished session and reads the stored grade otherwise. Nothing schedules grading except the owner's `xd plan grade` today, so a ticket without a stored grade waits (and is `unknown` after five sessions).
- **Added columns**: `live_ticket_outcomes.protection` (protected / unprotected / protection_unknown / not_applicable / unavailable) and `reject_text` (the broker's own, capped at 200, rendered as text), so a rejected ticket and a stop are stated without a second read.
- **Poll outcome words**: `ok` = both books read, `partial` = the trade book only. Order evidence needs an `ok` poll.
- **Outcome rules decided where the spec was open**: `partial` = fills below the allocation and an entry order that never filled in full; `size_below_allocation` only when no order explains the shortfall; an `over_allocation` carries its flag; `rejected` only when every entry order was rejected; the plan entry and stop are the ticket's own levels, else the stored grade's.
- **Symbols** are found through the same resolution as the history (ticker, else ISIN, else Dhan security id); a row whose share cannot be told is left out.
- **The mappers are tested against made-up samples** shaped like Dhan's documentation (`Brokers/Samples/dhan-trade-book-day.json`, `dhan-order-book-day.json`), because the owner's real, scrubbed answer does not exist yet. The field names are unverified; replace the samples when the owner supplies a real answer.

## Consequences
- The pinned allow-list has 11 lines instead of 9; the P7 phase check's criterion 4 compares to the new pin.
- `/live` gains a lazy Capture tab (read only; the first-load bundle is unchanged).
- Limits: a price modification of a pending order is not a new revision (stated on screen); forever, super and GTT orders are not read; a fill that arrives after the settle does not change a written outcome; a shared Dhan account used by someone else cannot be told from the owner.
- Rejected: lifting ADR 0025's intraday skip (changes P2 lots and returns); polling every 10 seconds (no gain); updating outcome rows (insert-only discipline); a new drop reason for an unknown order status (the existing `other` is used, so the attention texts of the history stay as they are).
