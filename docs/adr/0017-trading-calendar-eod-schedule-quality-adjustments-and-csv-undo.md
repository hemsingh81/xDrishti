# 0017 — Trading calendar, scheduled end-of-day run with gap backfill, data quality, price adjustments and CSV undo

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-12: "scheduled end-of-day fetches, gap backfill, quality checks, holiday-aware 'behind' detection, price adjustments, and CSV preview or undo") — builds on [ADR 0016](0016-market-bars-csv-load-dhan-fetch-and-app-managed-aggregates.md); defaults are mine, object to any
- **Date:** 2026-10-12

## Decisions
1. **Trading calendar (`market.trading_days`)** — one row per date that is not an ordinary weekday session: kind `holiday` (weekday with no session) or `special` (a weekend/holiday date with a session, e.g. Muhurat, with its session window). Ordinary weekdays are trading days unless listed. Sources, in order of trust: `manual` (owner) > `observed` (derived from stored bars: a weekday on which fewer than 5 % of the instruments that have bars on neighbouring days have any bar is a suspected holiday and is *proposed*, a weekend or holiday date with bars is a *special* session) > `nse` (best-effort fetch of NSE's public trading-holiday list, tolerant of failure like the sector list of ADR 0010). Observed proposals never become holidays without the owner's confirmation; manual add/remove is always available. "Behind" = the last bar's date is before the **last trading day whose session has closed** (15:30 IST, or the special session's end); days behind count trading days.
2. **Scheduled end-of-day run:** a hosted service in `xd-api` (same shape as the instrument refresh's daily service, ADR 0010) starts the job `dhan → aggregate → quality` at `Data:Eod:TimeIst` (default 15:50 IST) on trading days only; `Data:Eod:Enabled` switches it off; catch-up at start-up when the day's run is missing; recorded with trigger `daily`. Its result is shown on the Data page (next run, last automatic run) and a failed run or one that leaves gaps raises a notification. A proper schedule editor belongs to the service framework (P1-13).
3. **Gap backfill:** the Dhan phase also plans, per tracked instrument, the sessions of the last `Data:Gaps:LookbackSessions` (default 30) trading days after the instrument's first bar whose bar count is below the session's expected minutes (375, or the special session's length) and refetches those days (consecutive days merged into ≤ 90-day windows). `market.bar_gaps` records instrument, date, expected, found, attempts, last attempt and status `open | filled | unfillable`; a refetch that returns no more bars than stored marks the day `unfillable` (halts, circuit-locked days) and it is not retried by scheduled runs; a manual "Re-fetch" retries it.
4. **Data quality (`market.quality_runs`, `quality_results`):** the checks of design doc 05 §10b over the last N sessions (default 5) of every tracked instrument — completeness (against the calendar and the gap table), bar sanity, spikes, **aggregates vs 1-minute recomputed by an independent implementation**, freshness; "derived vs official daily" is reported as *not available* until official daily candles are fetched (a later story). Result per instrument: pass / warning / fail, score, per-check detail. Fail-closed gate endpoint for the nightly plan (no consumer yet). Screen: Data quality.
5. **Price adjustments:** `market.corporate_actions` (instrument, ex-date, kind `split | bonus`, ratio, factor, status `suggested | applied | ignored`, source `manual | detected | csv`, note). Bars stay raw; adjustment is applied **on read** (`adjusted=true` multiplies OHLC by the cumulative backward factor and divides volume), research reads adjusted, plans raw. Suspected actions are **detected** from the stored daily series (a close-to-previous-close ratio near a common split/bonus factor with a volume jump) and only *suggested*; the owner confirms, edits or ignores. Dividends are not adjusted (config `adjust.dividends: false`). An unreviewed suggestion shows as an alert on the Data and Data quality screens.
6. **CSV preview and undo:** *Preview* is a dry run of the real reader over a file (rows, range, repaired/rejected counts with examples, alignment verdict, symbol mapping, first rows) and a **review step** before loading chosen files. *Undo* of a loaded file removes the bars that file's source wrote — the instrument's `csv`-source bars inside the file's time range, never Dhan bars — through one narrow `SECURITY DEFINER` function owned by `xd_owner` (`market.undo_csv_bars`), because the app role has no DELETE on bars (ADR 0016); it marks the range dirty (aggregates rebuild), un-records the file, and is audited. Values a later CSV load had overwritten are not restored (no history is kept): the UI says so.

## Consequences
- The calendar makes "behind", gaps, the schedule and quality agree on what a session is; a missing calendar entry only costs a false "behind" until confirmed.
- One function can delete market data on the app's behalf; it is scoped to csv-source rows of one instrument and range, executable only by `xd_app`, and tested for exactly that.
- Adjusted prices are computed at read time, so confirming or editing an action takes effect at once without rewriting bars.
- **Independent review fixes (2026-10-12), amending decisions 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6:**
  - *Calendar (1):* a session seen on a **confirmed** NSE holiday no longer replaces the holiday with a proposal (which switched the date to an ordinary weekday and produced false gaps and quality results); the observation is stored **next to** the confirmed row (`proposal_status`, `proposed_*` on `trading_days`) and listed under `proposals` with `replaces`; confirming it turns the day into the special session, dismissing it keeps the holiday and remembers the dismissal. Any weekday that is a confirmed holiday and has bars from at least 5 % of the instruments is proposed as a `special` session (moved holiday: regular window; Muhurat: evening window). `trading_days` has an `xmin` concurrency token: withdrawing a stale proposal never deletes or overwrites a row the owner confirmed meanwhile.
  - *Scheduled run (2):* the start-up catch-up waits for the recovery of jobs a previous process left `running` (`DataJobRecovery`), a start that fails re-reads the day's automatic jobs before it records a skipped run, and the wait slice is clamped at zero.
  - *Gaps (3):* the overview, the list, the `hasGaps` filter and the bell notice count only gaps of tracked, not retired instruments; the records stay and come back when the instrument is tracked again.
  - *Quality gate (4):* it judges the latest **finished** full run (a running run neither hides a failed one nor clears the gate; none finished = blocked) and blocks a finished run older than 3 trading days (stale).
  - *Adjustments (5):* a 1W/1M bar whose bucket contains an ex-date is rebuilt from the adjusted day bars (the first-day factor was wrong for the days from the ex-date on). The owner's CSV files are adjusted at source, so an action with an ex-date on or before `csvAdjustedThrough` (the last bar of the instrument's loaded files) is refused with `409 data.csv_already_adjusted` unless `acknowledgeCsvAdjusted=true`; the import preview reports the lines (`adjustsCsvTwice`, `warnings`). `detected_ex_date` stops the detection from re-suggesting a date the owner moved an action away from. A split and a bonus on one ex-date do not both apply (`409 data.corporate_action_conflicts_same_day`; a pending detected suggestion of the other kind is declined by itself).
  - *CSV undo (6):* the removal is by instrument and range, so it also removes what an overlapping loaded file wrote there; `overlapsWith` on the file list and a warning on the undo job say so. A preview request is bounded to 2 × the per-file limits and 60 seconds. **Residual risk:** `xd_app` has `UPDATE` on `bars_1m` including `source`, so it could relabel Dhan bars as `csv` and have them removed through `undo_csv_bars`; the privilege is needed by the merge, so the risk is accepted (design doc 05 §9). The function does not reject an infinite range end (it would remove all CSV bars of that one instrument only); documented, not changed.
