# 0118 — The paper ledger is derived from entries and exits, and the plan may read it behind an off-by-default setting

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [P10-02](../specs/P10-02-paper-ledger-and-open-positions.md), built 2026-10-26; the spec is still Draft). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-10-26.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0116](0116-paper-fills-are-the-grading-replay-captured-per-armed-allocation.md) (the paper tables), [0092](0092-the-wired-plan-selection-smart-rules-fill-allocation-and-checklist-in-order-unavailable-never-passes-and-an-append-only-suggestion-inbox.md) (the open book is unavailable by default), [0089](0089-per-account-allocation-a-pure-split-of-the-ticket-by-each-paper-accounts-own-limits-and-unavailable-allocating-nothing.md) (allocation), [0073](0073-the-plan-record-is-the-log-and-the-state-is-derived.md)-style derived state, [0100](0100-the-charge-sheet-confirmation-is-read-through-one-port-and-the-sheet-label-is-what-the-owner-confirmed.md) (confirmation reader allow-list), [0042](0042-costs.md) (costs).

## Decision
1. **Derived, never stored.** The paper ledger of an account is computed on read from `paper.entries`, `paper.exits` and the account's capital (`Domain/Paper/PaperLedger`, pure, order-independent). No table, no migration, no snapshot writer. Closed trades are ordered by exit instant, count-once key, account and entry id, so the same rows in any insert order give the same figures.
2. **Equity = capital + realised result; funds = max(0, equity - open notional); no leverage.** Open gains are shown beside equity, never in the sizing base (a marked base would size up after an open gain). Open risk is `qty x |entry - initial stop|` as a percent of equity. Closed-trade drawdown is the largest fall of the running realised result from its peak (the start counts as a peak), as a percent of capital plus that peak.
3. **Capital is the account's own fixed capital** (the amount the plan sizes on), not `epoch_accounts.capital` (which holds the grading notional `Planning:Grading:Capital`). A BrokerFunds account has no capital: its ledger result is shown, equity and every percentage are `unavailable` with the reason, and the plan treats its limits as unknown.
4. **Marks are honest.** A position is marked at the close of the last closed session only when the newest stored complete daily bar is exactly that session; otherwise the mark is `unavailable` with the reason, the position's unrealised figure is null (never 0) and the account's unrealised total is `unavailable`, not the sum of the others.
5. **Gross/after-sheet wording, never net.** With no stored sheet charge the result is labelled `gross, no charges`; with one it is labelled `after the stored sheet; the sheet is not confirmed as the owner's in this view (ISS-25)`; a mix says so. No `Application/Paper` type reads or sets the charge-sheet confirmation (ADR 0100 closes `IChargeSheetConfirmationSource` to the report loader and the runs list; an architecture test pins the paper code to have no such reference). The JSON field `realised.net` is the contract's name for "result after the stored charges"; the screen does not print the word net. Net as a result of record needs the unwritten confirmation-writer slice.
6. **The plan reads the book only when told to.** `Planning:OpenBook:UsePaperLedger` (exact text `true`, default off) is a second property of `IOpenBookSettings`. Off: `StoredPlanContextSource` does not call the paper reader and the context is byte-identical to P7-09. On together with `AssumedEmpty`: the host stops at start (`OpenBookCheck`, `ValidateOnStart`). On: `PlanContext.Book` lists every open paper position of the accounts that have a capital figure (side, style, `RiskPct` of that account's equity, sector, baskets), each paper `ContextAccount` takes the ledger's `Equity`, `OpenRiskPct`, `FundsAvailable`, `HeldSymbols` and `DailyLossPct` (the last closed session's realised loss as a positive percent of the equity at that session's start, 0 when not a loss: a proxy, printed in the note), and `TakenTrades` has one entry per counted ticket (primary group, closed, current epoch, `TradeId = trade_key`, `ClosedOn` = India date of the exit, `R = r_sim`), counted once however many accounts were sized for it. `PlanContext.BookFromLedger` keeps an empty paper book from being mistaken for the owner's "assumed empty" in the plan's flag and item note.
7. **A stale book fails safe.** The book is available only if every trading session from the epoch's first to the last closed one has a `paper.capture_runs` row with `outcome = 'done'` and `pending = 0` (an empty session has one). Otherwise `Book` is null, `TakenTrades` null, the note reads `paper book unavailable: capture is behind (sessions without a finished capture: <dates>)` (first five dates) and the plan takes nothing (the `book:unavailable` rule of ADR 0092). No epoch yet is also unavailable.
8. **Read only.** Ports `IPaperLedgerData` (raw selects) and `IPaperBookReader` (plan side) have no write member; endpoints `GET /api/paper/accounts`, `/accounts/{id}/positions`, `/equity`, `/reconcile` answer 404 `paper.not_found` (unknown epoch, or an account that is not a Trading-role paper account now) and 422 `paper.invalid`. `reconcile` compares the derived ledger with sums read straight from the stored rows. Log ids 5210 to 5213.
9. **Frontend.** Lazy `/paper` frame with a tab registry and the Ledger tab; no write control; the only eager additions are one nav entry and one lazy route.

## Consequences
- Switching the setting on is an owner act and a `frozen` configuration change for P10-05; the default keeps today's behaviour.
- The loss proxy ties tomorrow's plan to today's result; marks are daily closes; the realised drawdown ignores open positions' unrealised loss; risk is to the initial stop (a trailing stop is not modelled); positions held in the owner's real broker app are not in the book.
- Percentages use the account's capital as it is now; a capital change is a frozen change (P10-05).
- A raw read per call: one query of the epoch's trades, one close per open symbol; no cache.
