# 0223 — The Lab verdict counts each trade's liquidity tier and capacity breach from day bars, report-only, and readiness row 14 reads them, with `unavailable` as a fail

- **Status:** Proposed (built as scorecard-source slice S2, the CF-05 follow-up "tier wiring, turnover and capacity", 2026-11-07). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no.** Report-only research numbers; nothing places an order, sizes a trade, changes a verdict or moves a cell. The scorecard row that reads them can only block an owner act that the owner's enforce switch (OFF by default, ADR 0221) already governs.
- **Date:** 2026-11-07.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** completes the follow-up listed in [0206](0206-the-lab-verdict-shows-a-cost-stress-at-1x-1-5x-and-2x-and-a-fill-one-minute-later-test-beside-the-outcome-in-report-only-mode-and-enforce-is-the-owners-switch.md) (CF-05); gives row 14 of [0221](0221-the-readiness-scorecard-is-derived-and-read-only-unavailable-is-a-fail-and-the-owners-enforce-switch-off-by-default-makes-the-two-lifecycle-gates-refuse-instead-of-warn.md) (CF-11) its data and narrows its port `IReadinessGaps`.

## Context
The pure rules `LiquidityTiers` (tier from the 20-day median traded value) and `Capacity` (an order is at most 1 % of the fill minute's traded value and at most 0.5 % of the 20-day median) were built in ADR 0206 and called by nothing. Row 14 of the scorecard ("all trades tier A or B, order at most 1 % of the minute value") therefore read `unavailable` from the port of inputs without a producer. The Lab verdict had no per-trade tier and no order size against traded value.

## Decision
1. **A tier and a capacity reading for each out-of-sample trade at Lab run time.** `LabTiers` (Application, internal) takes the simulated trades behind the out-of-sample trades, reads the stored day bars once per instrument (`IBarQueries.ReadDailyBarsAsync`, bounded: 60 calendar days before the first entry up to the last entry day) and asks the pure `LiquidityTiers.Assess` for each. Only the Lab runner calls it, after the verdict is judged, in the robust method only (where the cost stress block exists).
2. **The result is an optional `Tiers` block on `CostStressView`** (jsonb, last position, no migration): trades, counts of tier A, B, C and unavailable, capacity breaches, capacity readings that could not be made, the text and hash of the tier and capacity settings, and the basis text. `Tiers` is null in a verdict produced before this change; row 14 then reads `unavailable: the verdict was produced before the tier and capacity check; re-run the robust test`. Nothing is re-run automatically. The verdict evaluator, the gates and the outcome never read the block (golden: the serialised verdict with `Tiers` removed is byte for byte the verdict without the stage; architecture test: the block is read only by the scorecard's Lab reader and the report builder).
3. **Row 14 is produced in `LabEvidenceReader`, not through the port.** `IReadinessGaps.CapacityOkAsync` is removed (the port narrows; `UnavailableReadinessGaps` stays the only implementation and says unavailable for every input that remains). Row 14 passes only when the block exists, has at least one trade, no trade is tier C, no order is over a cap and no tier or capacity reading is unavailable. Any tier C or any measured breach is a fail (the definite failure wins over unavailable readings); a reading that could not be made (fewer than 20 usable sessions of bars, no order value, the fill minute not read, a failed bar read) is unavailable, which counts as a fail wherever the gate reads it. **Because the fill-minute cap is unavailable (see the first interpretation), no block written by a run today can make row 14 pass: it reads `unavailable` unless tier C or a breach of the 0.5 % median cap makes it a fail.** The pass branch exists for the follow-up slice S2b that reads the fill minute.
4. **Settings (`Confidence:CostStress`)**: `TierACutCrore` 100, `TierBCutCrore` 20, `CapacityFillShare` 0.01, `CapacityDailyShare` 0.005 (the spec's figures). **They are tighten-only, which is true in both directions here**: a higher cut puts names in lower tiers and a lower share fails more orders, so either move can only fail more of row 14, and start-up refuses a value on the loose side: an A cut below 100 or above 100000 crore, a B cut below 20 or not below the A cut, a fill-minute share or a median share at or below 0 or above its default. Plain configuration cannot loosen the gate; loosening needs a change of the defaults in code and an ADR (the spec's own open owner question 2 on the cuts would then be answered by an ADR, not by a setting). The hash of the values is stored with every block. The tier settings have their own text and hash (`TierKeyText`, `TierSnapshotHash`) so the stress hash stored with every earlier verdict does not change.

## Interpretations and deviations (proposals, for the owner)
- **The fill-minute cap is unavailable until a fill-minute read exists: follow-up slice S2b (fail closed).** The spec's capacity rule holds the order against 1 % of the fill minute's traded value. Reading the minute bar of every out-of-sample trade is a heavy extra read of the largest table (cost not measured, UNVERIFIED), so this slice reads day bars only. A day bar cannot stand in for the minute: the entry day's traded value is roughly 375 times the minute's, so 1 % of it would be a nearly dead check and a thin fill minute could count toward a pass (a looser proxy standing in for an unavailable figure). The minute cap therefore reads `unavailable: the fill minute's traded value is not read`, the 0.5 % cap on the 20-day median is measured as specified, and a breach of it is still a breach (and the definite fail wins). The count of orders over 1 % of the entry day is kept as **information only** (`OrdersOverEntryDayShare`); it cannot make a pass. The result: from day bars alone, row 14 is `unavailable` (a fail at the gate) or a fail, never a pass, until S2b. The `Tiers.Basis` text, the row's threshold text and note, and the capacity reasons say exactly what is measured and what is not read.
- **A failed bar read does not fail the run.** The block is report-only, so a read error for one instrument is caught (cancellation is not), logged as `lab.tiers.bars_unread` (instrument id and trade count only), and that instrument's trades count as tier-unavailable and capacity-unavailable.
- **The median is of the 20 sessions strictly before the entry date**, not including it (the spec says "on the entry date"): the entry day's volume was not known at the entry, and including it would let a busy entry day lift the tier. The 20 are the last 20 stored day bars, so a halted day is not a gap; fewer than 20 bars in the 60-day read is `unavailable`.
- **The order value is the costed entry turnover** (quantity x filled entry price) of the trade; a trade with no costing has an unavailable capacity reading.
- **Traded value is close x volume of a raw (unadjusted) day bar**; a bar with a non-finite or negative close or a negative volume makes the median unavailable; a zero volume is a traded value of zero (tier C), not a missing one. The `complete` flag of the aggregate is not read.
- **The hard fail "evidence only from tier C" stays `unavailable`.** The spec's wording ("evidence that comes only from tier C is a hard fail", CF-05 review fix 1 and CF-11 D3) does not say whether the evidence is the Lab trades or the forward (paper) trades. The block now allows the Lab reading (`TierC == Trades`), but implementing one reading would be a guess, and a hard fail sets every score to 0. **Owner question: is the hard fail about the Lab out-of-sample trades, the forward trades, or both?** Until answered, row 14 already fails on any tier C trade in the Lab, which covers the Lab reading's case.
- **The slippage tier bps (3 and 8) and the tiered slip option are not wired by this change** (ADR 0206 follow-up, `slippage: conservative` request field); only the counts of tiers and capacity are.
- **Old verdicts are not re-run.** A robust Lab run made before this change has no `Tiers` and row 14 says re-run; the owner re-runs it.
- **Cost of the read:** one indexed read of the day aggregate per instrument of the trades, once per robust run, after the walks; no minute bar.

## Consequences
Row 14 cannot pass from this slice alone: it reads `unavailable` (or `fail` on tier C or a measured breach) until slice S2b reads the fill minute. That is deliberate (fail closed), and it means Gate R is still blocked by row 14 as well as by rows 10 and 11 and the other inputs that have no producer. Architecture tests pin: the tier computation has one caller (the Lab runner), the stored block is read by the scorecard's Lab reader and shown by the report builder (never by the evaluator, the gates or the stress calculator), `IReadinessGaps` has eight methods and one implementation that says unavailable, and the tier and capacity rules stay free of I/O and clock. Tests: hand tables of the CF-05 spec with the exact equalities at the cuts and the caps (one rupee over), unavailable volume, an old JSON without the block, row 14 pass, fail and unavailable at the scorecard, and the refusal table of the settings.
