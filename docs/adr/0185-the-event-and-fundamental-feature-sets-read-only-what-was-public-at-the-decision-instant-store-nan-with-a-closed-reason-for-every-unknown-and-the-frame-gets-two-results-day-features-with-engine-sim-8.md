# 0185 — The event and fundamental feature sets read only what was public at the decision instant, store NaN with a closed reason for every unknown, and the frame gets two results-day features with engine `sim-8`

- **Status:** Proposed (built in P15-05 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-02). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no** — research features on stored runs and two frame features a strategy may name; no plan, selection, check or order path reads them; no setting.
- **Date:** 2026-11-02.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0033](0033-weekly-monthly-timeframes-calendar-features-and-the-market-context.md) (date features come from the caller), [0037](0037-the-simulation-engine-version-the-run-key-and-the-golden-files.md) (engine version, run key; Amendment 11 records `sim-8`), [0066](0066-decision-time-features-a-side-table-and-the-training-set-record.md) (side tables, no imputation), [0180](0180-the-point-in-time-filing-store-keeps-immutable-envelopes-and-answers-what-was-known-at-an-instant-from-the-broadcast-time-plus-a-lag-and-nothing-else.md) (store the lag), [0181](0181-the-results-calendar-keeps-each-meeting-row-with-its-own-intimation-time-counts-days-in-trading-sessions-to-an-event-session-and-answers-unavailable-for-an-instrument-with-no-entry.md) (`days_to_results`), [0182](0182-the-event-risk-filter-reads-the-results-calendar-in-the-simulator-and-behind-an-owner-switch-in-the-plan-and-the-results-loss-tag-describes-the-past-and-unavailable-is-never-a-pass.md) (the snapshot), [0183](0183-the-feature-set-comparison-fits-one-recipe-twice-on-identical-rows-widens-its-margins-for-the-ledger-count-and-reads-the-hold-out-once.md) (the table and the store this slice writes through), [0184](0184-the-results-xbrl-parser-reads-untrusted-files-through-one-hardened-reader-stores-facts-per-span-and-basis-with-unavailable-for-every-field-the-file-does-not-carry-and-is-labelled-synthetic-until-real-filings-pass.md) (the facts, labelled synthetic).

## Context
Protocol X (P15-10) compares `tf-1` against `tf-1` plus an event set and a fundamental set, and a strategy may want to say "not within two sessions of results". Both need numbers that were public at the decision instant and that say plainly when they are not known. The facts come from a parser that is still labelled `xbrl-1-synthetic`.

## Decision
1. **Pure features in the domain.** `EventFeatures` (`ev-1`: `days_to_results`, `days_since_results`, `results_car3`) and `FundamentalFeatures` (`fu-1`: `sue_yoy`, `sue_ttm`, `rev_growth_yoy`, `margin_chg_yoy`, `promoter_pledge_pct`, `promoter_chg_qoq`, `pe_pctile_5y`) take the answers visible at the instant and compute; they read no clock and no store. `ExtensionFeatureExtractor` (application) reads through the ports as of the instant (`IResultFactsAsOf`, `IShareholdingAsOf`), the stored filing times, the calendar snapshot, bars and applied actions.
2. **Reaction session `R`.** The first session whose own open is at or after `broadcast + lag`. `days_since_results` counts sessions from `R` of the newest visible results filing, `results_car3` is the stock minus NIFTY 50 over the three sessions ending at the close of `R`, and only once that close has passed. A split inside the window is taken out of the ratio with the factors of actions in `(R-3, R]`, never an action after `R`.
3. **NaN with a closed reason, never a number we do not have.** The reasons are `no_filing`, `parser_unverified` (reserved), `basis_mixed`, `short_history`, `no_calendar`, `no_context`, `no_bars` and, added here, `degenerate` (the inputs are there but the quantity is undefined: a deviation of 0, a base of 0 or less, a TTM EPS of 0 or less). Counted per feature and reason in the extraction record.
4. **One basis per feature, EPS on the decision date's share basis.** Consolidated when every quarter the feature needs has it, else standalone when all have it, else `basis_mixed` (together they would cover) or `short_history`. Every quarter's EPS is multiplied by the factors of applied splits and bonuses with ex-date after the day the quarter was **published** and at or before the decision date. This deliberately deviates from the spec's "after its period end": under Ind AS 33 a quarter published after an action already reports EPS on the new share count, and adjusting it again would count the action twice. The deviation of `sue_*` is the sample one (divisor n-1) of the previous eight differences; one at or below 1e-9 of the largest of them is rounding noise and the surprise is `degenerate`. `pe_pctile_5y` ranks the previous close (on the decision date's share basis) over the TTM EPS visible at the decision against the P/E of each of the previous 1,250 sessions computed from the quarters public at that session's close (needs 1,000 values); the rank is the share below plus half the ties.
5. **Facts of a synthetic parser are read, labelled and counted, and P15-10 must gate on the label.** The extraction record stores the parser version and counts, per feature, the known values built on a parser that is not verified (`parser_unverified`; a missing or empty version is unverified too). Nothing in this slice makes such a value a pass: P15-10 must read `parser_version` and `nan_counts` from the record and return NOT_EVALUABLE for `fu-1` when the parser is unverified (a P15-10 acceptance criterion).
6. **Storage.** Rows go through `IExtensionFeatureStore` into P13-05's `research.trade_features_ext` (extension `ev` / `fu`), together with the extraction record in one transaction (`AppendWithRecordAsync`), so the record always describes the stored rows. Daily closes are read from the 1D aggregate (`IBarQueries.ReadDailyBarsAsync`, complete days only), never the minute bars. New table `research.extension_extractions(run_key, extension, version, lag_minutes, parser_version, calendar_sha256, filings_watermark, rows, nan_counts, extracted_at)`: insert-only for `xd_app`, granted table by table, nothing for `xd_exec` or the read roles. One record per run, extension and version; a second call stores nothing and says so. The verb is `xd learn features --extension ev-1|fu-1 --run <key>`; without the flag it is the `tf-1` extraction unchanged.
7. **Frame features.** `days_to_results` and `days_since_results` are date-driven 1m features read from `FrameOptions.ResultsDays`, a dictionary of neutral numbers per India date (Indicators knows no filing type). `ResultsDaySeries` (domain) builds it per instrument as known at the **start** of each date (rows and filings with `time + lag <=` 00:00 India time), so it only sees less than the instant rule of `not_event_day`. A date it does not hold, or holds as NaN, is unknown to every rule (the kernel is three-valued), so no rule passes on a missing calendar. A frame asked for either without the option is refused at `Create` (`options.resultsDays`).
8. **Simulator and engine.** A strategy that names either feature gets the series built from the run's snapshot and filing times by one read before the walk; a window with neither a calendar row nor a filing is refused `simulation.filter_not_available` ("no results calendar or filings for the window (P15)") before any bar is read. The run key gains `rdays=<snapshot sha256>|<filings sha256>|<lag>|rds-1` for such a run only. Engine `sim-8`, run-record schema 8; goldens regenerated on purpose and a differential test against the frozen `sim-7` files shows the diff is the label.
9. **No decision use.** The extension catalogs, `ExtensionFeatureExtractor` and the new port are named only by the verb's handler and tests (an architecture test pins the exact set); Execution, Selection, Planning, the checklists and the scorer read neither them nor the tables. The no-order tripwire is unchanged.

10. **The BUY to SELL mirror treats both counts as side-independent numbers** (`Plain` rows, exact), and the value-range table of the lint gets `0 .. +inf` for both. Rows for names that did not exist cannot change any mirrored definition stored before, so `MirrorTable.Version` stays `mirror-1` and only its pin changes; the owner may prefer a bump (it would change the definition hash of every mirrored strategy).

## Consequences
- P15-10 reads `research.trade_features_ext` and the extraction record; it can tell from `filings_watermark` and the calendar hash when the inputs changed.
- `pe_pctile_5y` is NaN (`short_history`) until a quarter-by-quarter history of about four years exists; `sue_yoy` needs 13 quarters and `sue_ttm` 16.

## Known limits
- Market adjustment uses NIFTY 50, not a sector index; beta is not removed. Survivorship (ISS-22) and gross R (ISS-25) carry into every comparison.
- The earlier P/E values use the facts visible at the decision, so a restatement visible then replaces the old figure in the history (nothing later than the decision is read).
- A restatement envelope counts as the newest filing for `days_since_results`.
- The daily close is the last minute bar of the India date; a session with a truncated tape gives a truncated close.
