# 0217 — A model may influence a decision only through one gate, whose predicate needs an unspent hold-out, forward evidence, no calibration alarm and the owner's switch

- **Status:** Proposed (built in CF-10 part 10c-2 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: yes** (it is the predicate and the single door that any later influence of a model on a decision must pass). Default OFF and fail closed: in this version the gate refuses every model, nothing can turn influence on, no consumer asks the gate yet and no order is placed. The champion / challenger rules, the rollback writer, the owner switch and the `auto` marker are 10d and are not built.
- **Date:** 2026-11-06.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** completes the safety half of CF-10 after [0214](0214-a-models-calibration-is-chosen-by-sample-size-platt-below-500-calibration-rows-isotonic-from-500-and-the-error-is-reported-with-a-day-cluster-bootstrap-interval.md) (calibration), [0215](0215-a-models-complexity-is-shown-against-a-ladder-with-an-events-per-variable-cap-and-its-features-against-a-stability-index-and-a-direction-check-as-evidence-only.md) (ladder) and [0216](0216-the-probability-that-a-model-beats-the-rung-below-comes-from-a-paired-day-cluster-bootstrap-with-a-fold-sign-test-and-sample-uniqueness-as-evidence-only.md) (paired bootstrap); reuses the shared locked hold-out of CF-06 (ADR 0207, `kind = 'model'` was reserved there) and keeps P7-00 Q8: ML is advisory, `AdvisoryPWin` is shown beside the cell figure and used for nothing else.

## Context
Spec CF-10 D6 says a model's probability must never quietly start steering decisions. It names one predicate, `MlMayInfluence = HoldOutBeatsNoMl AND ForwardShadowOk AND NoCalibrationAlarm AND SwitchOn`, one port that answers it (`IMlInfluenceGate`) and an architecture test that forbids `Planning`, `Selection` and `Autonomy` from reading a model score any other way (AC9, AC10). This part builds those, the one-use mechanics of the hold-out per model family, the table that records how the predicate stood, and the read endpoint.

## Decision
1. **The predicate (`MlInfluence.Evaluate`, pure Domain).** Four terms, each `Holds`, `Fails` or `Unavailable`; only `Holds` counts, so an unavailable figure (null, missing or not finite) is never a pass. `MlInfluenceResult.MayInfluence` is derived from the terms and from nothing else (a result without all four is false). Every term that does not hold lists its reasons, each a stable code and a sentence. `HoldOutBeatsNoMl`: a spent hold-out gives `holdout_reused` **whatever the numbers**; otherwise a kept hold-out (`holdout_not_kept`), at least 100 rows, the probability of beating the best no-ML baseline at least 0.95 (D4), and a take-rule effect on realised R not below zero. `ForwardShadowOk`: at least 30 graded candidates, at least 4 weeks, top-half minus bottom-half mean R not below zero, rolling ECE at most 5 pp. `NoCalibrationAlarm`: the detector has been read and has not fired. `SwitchOn`: the owner's switch. An unset state is `Unavailable` (the enum's zero value), so it fails closed, and the result is true only when its terms are exactly the four in order. The bars are inclusive and are `Confidence:MlInfluence` settings (proposals of the spec; bound and validated at start, see below).
2. **The gate (`IMlInfluenceGate`, Application port; `MlInfluenceGate`).** `AnswerAsync(modelId)` and `ScoreAsync(modelId, features)`. Beyond the four terms the gate adds `IsChampion`: only the current champion's score is ever handed out (`not_champion`). The composed answer (the five terms, the allow and the reasons) is built once, in `MlInfluenceEvaluation`, and the gate, the stored check and the view all read it, so they cannot differ. A score is read through the existing score query only after an allow; a score that is not a probability, or a file that cannot be read, gives no score (`score_unavailable`). An unknown model is refused (`model_not_found`). Each answer is recorded as a check (item 5) when it differs from the model's last recorded one; the read endpoint records nothing. `ScoreAsync` makes a full answer per vector (marked `// shortcut:`; an "answer once, score many" call is for the first consumer that scores in bulk).
3. **Enforcement (AC10), `MlInfluenceArchitectureTests`.** No type of `Domain/Selection`, `Domain/Planning`, `Domain/Autonomy`, `Application/Planning`, `Application/Selection` or `Application/Autonomy` may depend on a score source: every implementation of `IRowScorer`, `IScorerFactory` or `IExplainableScorer` and every calibrator, **found by reflection over every project under `backend/src`** (a test pins that the list of projects is the directory listing), and the scorer loader, the registry, the model files, the trainer, the score query and its view, the predicate, the evidence and switch ports, the evaluator, the hold-out looks, the checks port or the gate's implementation. The model read queries (`GetModelQuery`, `ListModelsQuery`) are not sources: they return the registry's record and take no features. The model's figure is pinned by the IL: `AdvisoryPWin` (the getter and the shadow fact `ShadowPlanFacts.AdvisoryPWin`, which is also on the forbidden list) is read in those namespaces only by `PlanCandidate`, `RankedCandidate` and `SelectionPlanBuilder`; the advisory note it is carried in (`AdvisoryNote`) only by the two plan views; `TryReadAdvisoryNote` (which parses the figure back out of the note) in the whole Application only by `ShadowPlanFacts`; and nobody deconstructs a `PlanCandidate`, a `PlanEvidence` or a `PlanEvidenceView` (which would hand out the figure or the note without naming it). A new reader fails the test. Each scan has a sentinel that the same code must catch (a type reading the scorer port, a type reading `AdvisoryPWin` and its note, a second switch, a reader of the switch, a second evidence source, the shadow plan fact, the note parser and a deconstruction), and the gate and its evaluator, which do read scores, are seen by the type rule. **No consumer asks the gate yet; that is pinned empty**, so the first consumer (a later slice behind the switch) must be added to the test in review and may use the gate only. The predicate types are pure (no clock, no I/O, no system random); the influence code names no broker or execution type.
4. **The hold-out look per model family (`ModelHoldOutLooks`, `holdout_reused`).** A look is a row of `lab.holdout_unlocks` of `kind = 'model'` whose id is the family **and the block** (`model-family:{feature version}@{block start}`) and whose version is 1, so the existing unique index lets a family have exactly one look **per block**, and the existing partial unique index lets a block have one fresh look. A new, non-overlapping block (one that starts after the old one ends) that the owner advances therefore gives the family a fresh look. **Freshness assumes blocks do not overlap and nothing here enforces it** (`// shortcut:` in the code): `HoldOutStore.CurrentAsync` returns the first block ever stored and has no advance operation, so whoever builds the advance (10d, the owner act) must enforce the non-overlap. The family is the **feature version** (the unit the registry already counts trials in). The look is read for the current block. The row's run id is a stable id of the model's file hash, so the row says which model owns the family's look. A model that kept no hold-out reads as `not_kept` (`holdout_not_kept`) whatever the look. A model reads as `used` only when it owns the look, the look was fresh and the hold-out the model kept excludes the locked block (its kept start is not after the block's start; a model that kept a later start trained on days of the block, so its look reads as reused and `ClaimAsync` refuses it with `learning.holdout_unavailable`, nothing written); a later model of the family, or any look at a block somebody had already looked at (`reused_window`, CF-06 D5), reads as `reused`, and `HoldOutBeatsNoMl` is `Fails` with `holdout_reused`. `ClaimAsync` takes the look before anything is scored (insert-only: an abandoned look stays burned), validates the mode (`owner` or `auto`), the actor and the setting hash as a `Result` before the database could throw, retries a lost race for the block's fresh place as a reused window, and refuses a second look at the same block with `learning.holdout_reused` (409). It reads no session of the hold-out.
5. **`research.ml_influence_checks` (migration `AddMlSafety`).** One row per changed answer of the gate: model id (key to `model_versions`), time, answer, the terms with their reason codes as jsonb, the hash of the settings and of the switch, and a hash of the whole evaluation (the dedupe key). Insert-only for the runtime role (`MakeAppendOnlyForAppRole`; `ResearchGrantsTests` and a migration test cover privileges, checks, key, snapshot agreement and Down).
6. **The read endpoint `GET /api/learning/models/{id}/influence`** returns the terms, the reasons, the hold-out state (`not_kept`, `kept`, `used`, `reused`), the switch, the settings hash and notes. 404 `models.not_found`. It is a pure read.
7. **The switch is always off in this version, and the evidence is none.** `IMlInfluenceSwitch` has one implementation, `MlInfluenceSwitchOff`, and `IMlInfluenceEvidence` (hold-out figures, forward figures, calibration alarm) one, `NoMlInfluenceEvidence`, which returns that nothing is available. Architecture tests pin, over every project, that each is the only implementation and that only the evaluator reads them; the hold-out figures count only for the model that owns a fresh look. The stored switch (append-only history), the owner act with a fresh password, `PUT /api/learning/ml-settings` and the `auto` recording are 10d (spec "left for 10d"), so there is nothing to turn on.
8. **Settings `Confidence:MlInfluence`** (`HoldOutMinRows` 100, `HoldOutMinProbability` 0.95, `ForwardMinCandidates` 30, `ForwardMinWeeks` 4, `ForwardMaxEcePp` 5): bound, range-validated at start **Configuration can only tighten them**: each proposal is the floor (the ceiling for the calibration error), so 99 rows, a probability under 0.95 or at 1, 29 candidates, 3 weeks and more than 5 pp are refused at start, and so is a `HoldOutMinProbability` below `Confidence:Ladder:MinProbability`. This holds until the owner-act settings exist (10d). There is no mode and no switch in the section, and no value can make an unavailable figure a pass.

## What is available today, and so what the gate says
The predicate is complete and tested over every combination, but three of its inputs have no source yet, and the evidence port answers not available for them (marked `// shortcut:` in `NoMlInfluenceEvidence`): the **hold-out figures** (rows, probability against the no-ML baseline, effect of the take rule) because no step scores a model on the hold-out rows (this part claims and reads the look, it does not score); the **forward shadow figures**, because a shadow candidate does not record which model scored it (the plan has no model id, CF-08); and the **calibration detector**, which is replayed on demand over named runs and not stored per model (the daily `ml-monitor` job is 10d). With the switch also off, the gate refuses every model with its reasons. That is the intended outcome on a short history ("a predicate that is almost always false", spec Risks) and is the safe default; each source is a later step and none changes the predicate.

## Deviations and interpretations (proposals, for the owner)
- **Table schema.** The spec names `learning.ml_influence_checks`; there is no `learning` schema (models, designations and the changelog live in `research`), so the table is `research.ml_influence_checks`.
- **Route.** The spec's `/api/learning/models/{id}/influence` is kept; the older registry reads stay at `/api/models`.
- **The family is the feature version, one look per family per block.** The spec says "model family" without defining it. Alternative: feature version plus trainer. Keying the look by block is this part's reading of "a fresh hold-out once the owner advances the start" (spec D6).
- **Not terms of the predicate:** the ladder rung, PSI and the direction-flip check. The spec lists them as D2 and D3 evidence (shown in the validation report), not in D6; the hold-out term already asks the model to beat the best no-ML baseline. If the owner wants "ladder earned" as a further term it is one more `Check` in the Domain predicate.
- **`calibration_kind` column not added.** The predicate does not need it (the kind is in the artifact header since 10a, ADR 0214); it is a display column for the Models screen and stays with 10d. `rollback_proposals` and the `auto` marker on changelog rows are 10d.
- **The interval** that the spec lists in `MlInfluenceView` is not in the view: no step produces the hold-out probability yet, so there is no interval to show.
- **`HoldOutMinProbability` is a second copy of the 0.95 bar** (`Confidence:Ladder:MinProbability`); they are separate settings, and the host refuses a hold-out bar below the ladder's.
- **A model whose kept hold-out starts after the locked block does not count** (it trained on days of the block). A model trained before CF-06 kept its own cut and so reads as reused until retrained.
- **Four shortcuts, marked in the code:** the dedupe of the checks is not race-safe under READ COMMITTED (a duplicate row is harmless in an append-only record); `ScoreAsync` makes a full answer per vector; the stored check and its inputs hash carry reason codes and term states, not the figures (put them in when the first real evidence source lands); and the non-overlap of advanced blocks is assumed, not enforced.
- **The gate records, the endpoint does not.** The table has a writer (the gate) although no consumer calls the gate yet; the endpoint stays a pure read.

## Open owner questions
1. Is the **feature version** the right unit of a model family for the one look? 2. Should the ladder rung, PSI or direction flips be terms of the predicate (not in D6)? 3. Which slice builds the **hold-out scoring pass** (and the plan record carrying the model id, for the forward evidence)? Until one exists no model can pass. 4. Keep the older `/api/models` registry reads as they are, beside the spec's `/api/learning/models/...` route?

## Consequences
Model influence has one door, written down, tested and pinned, and it is shut: nothing can open it in this version, and when the sources of its inputs and the owner's switch exist the predicate and the architecture rule are already in place. No decision, plan, size, ranking, approval or order changes; the registry, the champion rule and the validation report are unchanged.
