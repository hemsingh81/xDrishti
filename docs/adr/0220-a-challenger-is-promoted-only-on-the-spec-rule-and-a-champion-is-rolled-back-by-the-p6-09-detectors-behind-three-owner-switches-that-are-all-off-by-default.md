# 0220 — A challenger is promoted only on the spec's rule and a champion is rolled back by the P6-09 detectors, behind three owner switches that are all OFF by default

- **Status:** Proposed (built in CF-10 part 10d-1 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: yes** (it decides how far an ML model may matter: who may name a champion, an automatic demotion behind a setting, and the stored switch that is the last term of the influence predicate). Everything is default OFF / manual; ML stays advisory; no order is placed or touched anywhere in this part. The verbs and the Models-screen additions are part 10d-2.
- **Date:** 2026-11-06.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** completes the safety half of CF-10 after [0217](0217-a-model-may-influence-a-decision-only-through-one-gate-whose-predicate-needs-an-unspent-hold-out-forward-evidence-no-calibration-alarm-and-the-owners-switch.md) (the predicate and the gate, whose always-off switch is replaced here by the stored one); follows the pattern of [0219](0219-a-firing-kill-rule-writes-a-proposal-and-a-notice-the-owner-or-an-automatic-setting-that-is-off-by-default-pauses-the-cells-and-only-the-owner-with-a-fresh-password-ever-re-enables.md) (a proposal and a notice, the owner or an automatic setting that is OFF by default, a stored switch with its id in commit order, the database guard on the proposal record, the IL closure of the automatic path with sentinels); reuses the P6-07 designation and the P6-08 changelog row (ADR [0070](0070-model-registry-content-addressed-files-and-an-append-only-champion-record.md), [0073](0073-the-learning-changelog-and-the-cell-lifecycle-the-log-is-the-state.md)), the P6-09 detectors (ADR 0074, unchanged) and the settings of 0217.

## Context
Spec CF-10 D6 to D9 and AC11 to AC14 ask that a challenger be promoted only on stated evidence, that a champion whose calibration or cells slip be moved back to shadow (by a proposal, or automatically if the owner chooses), that three switches (`ml.influence.enabled`, `ml.auto_promote`, `ml.auto_rollback`) exist and default OFF, and that a regime cell stand alone only from 30 trades. The approvals rule of the project adds that anything with manual approval must also run automatically behind a setting that is OFF by default, that an automatic decision is recorded as automatic with the setting snapshot (never as an owner approval), and that an `unavailable` input never counts as a pass.

## Decision
1. **Promotion (`MlPromotion`, pure Domain; `PromoteChallengerHandler`).** A challenger may be promoted only when five terms hold: at least 30 graded candidates, at least 4 weeks, the paired day-cluster bootstrap against the champion on the same candidates gives `P >= 0.95`, the P6-12 counterfactual for its recipe is not `FrozenBetter`, and `ChampionRule` has no reason. The bars are those of `Confidence:MlInfluence` (30 candidates, 4 weeks, the 0.95 bar), so they can only be tightened. A missing or non-finite figure is unavailable and never a pass; and an `Inconclusive` counterfactual (too little history) is read as unavailable, not as "not FrozenBetter" (stricter than the spec's letter). The figures come through `IChallengerEvidence`, **whose only implementation says nothing is available** (see below), so every promotion is refused with the reasons today. The route is `POST /api/learning/models/{id}/promote` (the spec said to reuse the designate route; **there is none**: naming a champion was only the CLI verb `xd learn designate`). It needs the owner's session, the CSRF token and a fresh password for that act; the actor comes from the session. The designation itself is the P6-07 path (`DesignateModelCommand`, now carrying optional evidence ids for the changelog row), so the registry and the changelog row are written in one transaction as before. A **retired** model (the previous champion after a rollback) is **re-promoted** only by the owner, and only if its latest evaluation satisfies the influence predicate without the switch (`MlInfluenceResult.HoldsWithoutSwitch`). Promotion never reads or writes `ml.influence.enabled` (a test and an architecture rule pin that the handler holds no switch store).
2. **Automatic promotion (`ml.auto_promote`, default OFF).** The daily run offers each challenger to the same handler as the actor `auto`. The handler then needs the switch ON, a model that is a challenger (a retired model is the owner's), that has no rollback proposal of its own (a model that was proposed for rollback comes back only by the owner), the promotion rule **and** the predicate without the switch (stricter than the spec: "whose MlMayInfluence terms hold" is added to D7, not substituted for it). It is recorded as actor `auto`; the changelog row carries the evidence ids `ml-switch:{row}` and `settings:{hash}` and the reason names the switch row, never an owner. Because the evidence source does not exist, this can never fire today; it is correct and tested with fakes.
3. **Rollback (`RollbackPolicy`, pure Domain; `ProposeRollbackHandler`, `ApplyRollbackHandler`, `DemoteModelHandler`).** The two triggers **are** the P6-09 detectors, not a copy of their numbers: `CalibrationDriftDetector` (gap above 5 pp and above 2.5 standard errors of the window, window 100, three checks in a row) and `RollingBandDetector` (20-trade means below the frozen band at three checks) on the cells the model scores, each read as "in alarm now" (the newest event is a fire that has not cleared). The policy source holds no threshold or arithmetic of its own (an architecture test reads it). AC11's numbers are run through the real detectors: a 12 pp gap fires on a window of 200 at three consecutive checks and never on a window of 100 (2.5 standard errors of 100 outcomes are 12.3 pp). Inputs come through `IRollbackEvidence`, **whose only implementation says nothing is available**: an unavailable series is neither a trigger nor a pass. A firing trigger writes a proposal in `research.rollback_proposals` (trigger, numbers, cells, the previous champion found in the changelog and whether its latest evaluation satisfies the predicate without the switch, the settings hash; one per model, trigger and day, none while one is open) and a derived bell notice; the daily evaluation is recorded in `research.ml_monitor_checks` (one per model and day, insert-only, after the proposals so that a failure leaves nothing recorded).
4. **Applying a rollback.** The champion becomes `challenger` (shadow) through `DemoteModelCommand`, whose handler builds the designation with `ModelRole.Challenger` and no other role (it is the only builder of the designation command in the closure of the automatic rollback; a source pin and an IL pin). Demote first, then one compare-and-set of the proposal (`proposed` to `applied`); a model that is no longer champion is skipped, so a repeat or a race demotes nothing twice. The owner applies with the session and CSRF only (`POST /api/learning/rollback/{id}/apply`: it lowers reliance on ML). `ml.auto_rollback` ON lets the same daily run apply the open proposals made today or whose trigger still fires, as `auto`: the changelog row carries `auto`, the evidence ids `rollback-proposal:{id}`, `ml-switch:{row}` and `settings:{hash}`, never an owner; the database refuses an `auto` decision unless the newest `ml.auto_rollback` row is ON and is the one recorded, and refuses an `auto` dismissal. **Applying never re-promotes the previous champion**, even when it qualifies: the proposal and the answer say whether it qualifies, there is then **no champion** until the owner names one (the promote route above checks the same condition). A test shows the gate hands out no score for any model afterwards and the real plan builder shows the cell rate only with no model note (AC13).
5. **The service `ml-monitor`** (`MlMonitorService`, daily 18:15, must follow `decay-monitor`, starts paused, registered with the other scheduled services and run by the API host). `EvaluateMlModelsHandler` evaluates every champion (`ProposeRollbackCommand`, idempotent per model and day) and, only when `ml.auto_promote` is ON, offers every challenger to the promotion handler; one model that fails or throws is counted and does not stop the others and records nothing for itself.
6. **The switches (`research.ml_switch_settings`, `MlSwitchSettings`).** Stored state, append-only, one row per change per name, the id drawn after an advisory lock so that the highest id of a name is the last to commit (the pattern of `confidence.kill_settings`); no row means OFF. `PUT /api/learning/ml-settings` takes any of the three values and a reason; a value equal to the one in force writes nothing; the actor is the session's owner. **A fresh password is needed to turn `ml.influence.enabled` or `ml.auto_promote` ON** (a missing one is 403 `auth.step_up_required`, a wrong one the sign-in 401 or 423); `ml.auto_rollback` and turning any switch OFF need the session and the CSRF token only (review fix 2 of the spec, which lowers reliance on ML; the spec's AC12 sentence "any of the three needs a fresh password" is read in that light). `MlInfluenceSwitchOff` is replaced by the stored switch, still OFF with no row; ON alone opens nothing: the other terms of the predicate must hold, and **the gate still refuses every model** because the hold-out scoring evidence has no source.
7. **The regime rule (`RegimeCellRule`, AC14).** A regime cell shrinks to its parent below 30 trades and stands alone at 30. Regimes (P6-02) **are** built (`RegimeCellBuilder`, `RegimeBreakdown`), and no decision consumes a regime cell, so the rule is the same 30 that `RegimeBreakdown.MinTrades` already used for "enough evidence" (it now reads the rule's constant); there is nothing further to integrate with.
8. **Storage (`AddMlRollback`).** Schema `research` (the choice of `AddMlSafety`: models, designations and the changelog live there, and the spec's `learning` schema does not exist). Raw SQL, no EF entity, so the model snapshot does not change; Down drops the three tables and two functions only. The runtime role has insert-only access to the switch rows and the evaluations, and to a proposal INSERT on its own columns only and UPDATE on four decision columns, with a transition trigger; no DELETE or TRUNCATE anywhere. **The changelog needs no new column**: its free-text actor says `auto` and its evidence ids carry the proposal, the switch row and the settings hash (as 12b needed none either).
9. **Enforcement (`MlRollbackArchitectureTests`, every scan with a sentinel it must catch).** (a) The closure of the automatic rollback (propose and apply) reaches the registry only through `DemoteModelHandler`, the only builder of the designation command in it, with no promotion, no re-promotion, no switch writer, no CLI designation, no revert and no cell re-enable; (b) the switch has one writer (the owner's handler), the proposals two, each contract one implementation, in Infrastructure; (c) the promotion command is built only by the daily run (as `auto`, challengers only) and by the owner's route; (d) Planning, Selection and Autonomy name none of it, and they reach a model score only through `IMlInfluenceGate` (the 10c-2 rule, unchanged, with its pin of the switch updated to the stored one and equally strong); (e) none of it names the execution plane, a broker or an order; (f) the policy is the detectors; (g) the routes are the pinned few, the two acts that may raise reliance on ML use the fresh-password step. **No bypass flag exists**, so there is no init-only flag to pin.
10. **Settings.** No new configurable number: the detectors use the P6-09 defaults (`DriftOptions`, not a configuration section today) and the promotion bars are the tighten-only ones of `Confidence:MlInfluence`. The three switches are stored state and are in no configuration section (a test reads the settings file).

11. **Review fixes (Opus review of 10d-1).** (a) **OWNER DECISION NEEDED: the command-line designation bypasses D7 and D8 until the evidence sources exist.** `xd learn designate ... champion` names a champion on `ChampionRule` alone. It is kept on purpose: the owner's open to-do from 10a is to retrain and re-designate the champion by that verb, the first champion has no incumbent to be compared with, the D7 sources do not exist, and the gate refuses every model so no influence can result; refusing it would leave the system unable to name any champion. It is now the use case `OwnerDesignateModelHandler`, which refuses a model that has a rollback proposal in any state (it comes back only through the promote route, with a fresh password and the predicate without the switch), and an architecture **tripwire** fails the day a production implementation of `IChallengerEvidence` or `IMlInfluenceEvidence` other than the "No..." stubs exists while that verb does not go through `PromoteChallengerHandler` (message: the designate path has to go through `PromoteChallengerHandler` first; with a sentinel control). (b) `IMlInfluenceSwitch.IsOnAsync` catches a database failure, logs it and returns false, as its port promises (cancellation still cancels; the read of all three switches lets a failure through so an automatic act does nothing). (c) An unknown counterfactual conclusion is unavailable, never a pass; only `NoDifference` and `RetrainingHelps` hold. (d) An automatic promotion is shown on the bell for three days as a resolved notice (`PromotionNotices`, from the changelog rows of designations to champion by `auto`). (e) The demotion carries an expected current role (`DesignateModelCommand.ExpectedCurrentRole`, the champion role), checked by the registry under the same advisory lock as the write: a model retired in between stays retired and the answer says nothing changed, so a retired model can never re-enter the challenger pool. (f) The builder scans of the architecture tests also see a `with` expression and the init setters of the two commands that name a champion (`<Clone>$`, `set_Role`, `set_Actor`...), with sentinels.

## What is available today, and so what happens
Three sources do not exist, and the code says so (`// shortcut:` in `NoRollbackEvidence` and `NoChallengerEvidence`): (1) **stored challenger scores per shadow candidate**, because a shadow candidate does not record which model scored it (CF-08), so the paired comparison with the champion and the 30 graded candidates over 4 weeks cannot be had; (2) a **link from a registered model to the P6-12 counterfactual of its recipe** (the stored report names the recipe in free text that does not match the registry's parameters line); (3) the **champion's scored outcomes and the cells it scores** for the P6-09 detectors. With the daily run on, the rollback finds no trigger (the readings are stored as `Unavailable`), promotion is refused with the reasons, and the hold-out figures of the predicate stay unavailable (0217), so the gate refuses every model, with the switches ON or OFF. The code, the database guards and the detectors are complete and tested with fakes; each source is a later step behind its port and changes no rule.

## Deviations and interpretations (proposals, for the owner)
- **Table schema** `research`, not `learning` (see 8).
- **A promotion route was added** because the designate route the spec points at does not exist; `xd learn designate` (the owner's local CLI) **still names a champion on the champion rule alone, i.e. it bypasses D7 and D8 until the evidence sources exist. OWNER DECISION NEEDED** (see item 11a): kept on purpose, with a refusal for a model that has a rollback proposal and an architecture tripwire that fails when a real evidence source appears.
- **An inconclusive counterfactual is not a pass**; **an automatic promotion needs the predicate without the switch as well as the promotion rule**, and never touches a model that has a rollback proposal.
- **Apply does not re-promote** the previous champion even when it qualifies (the spec both says it is re-promoted "only if" it qualifies and that re-promotion is always the owner's act; the owner's act wins).
- **The 12 pp hand case** is run with the window as a parameter (`CalibrationWindow` 200 and 100, one check each), exactly as the 12a test of the same numbers does; on the defaults (window 100, every 50) a gap needs to be larger to fire.
- **The plan builder asks no gate** (the consumers are pinned empty and nothing attaches a model's figure to a candidate), so AC13's "plan builder with a fake gate" is evidenced as the real gate's answer after the real rollback beside the real builder's output for the same state, not as a gate wired into the builder.
- **The regime integration** is the shared constant of 30; there is no decision consumer to integrate with.
- **`calibration_kind` is still not added** (a display column of the Models screen, part 10d-2).
- **The interval and the rest of the `MlInfluenceView`** are unchanged (no source for the hold-out probability).
- **One `// shortcut:`:** if an automatic and a manual apply race, the changelog row records who demoted the model and the proposal records who won the final update, so the two can differ (each is true).

## Open owner questions

Is it right that a model that was ever proposed for rollback is never promoted automatically again?

## Consequences
A model can be named champion only on the spec's evidence (which no source supplies yet, so only the owner's local CLI can name one today), can be taken back to shadow by a proposal or, if the owner chooses, by the daily run, and can come back only by the owner's act with a fresh password after its evaluation still satisfies the predicate. The three switches exist, are OFF, and are the owner's. No ranking, size, ticket, plan, approval or order changes; the gate still refuses every model.

## Addendum: part 10d-2a, the verbs (2026-11-06)
`xd learn influence MODEL-ID` and `xd learn rollback` are read only and call the two query handlers behind `GET /api/learning/models/{id}/influence` and `GET /api/learning/rollback/proposals` (the influence verb takes the three switches from the second, with the model id as filter). No new decision, setting, route or table; behaviour is unchanged. The rules of the output: an unavailable, refused or not evaluated term reads "not a pass" with its reason; the hold-out probability ("odds"), the take-rule effect and the interval read `unavailable` with the reason while no source exists (the view carries no interval, as in ADR 0217); a failed switch read reads `unavailable`, never OFF, and a failed proposal read exits 1 as `unavailable`, never "none"; the "no champion fallback" list is worded "proposals that leave (applied) or would leave (open) no qualifying previous champion, as of when they were proposed" (the facts are frozen when a proposal is written), lists each model once by its newest proposal with open and applied apart, and is printed beside "champion now", read through the existing `ListModelsQuery` (no new port or handler; a failed read reads unavailable). Applying a rollback stays the owner's act in the app. An IL rule (the CF-12 12c-1 scan, extended to the two new verb types) pins that the verbs name no command handler, clock, infrastructure type, switch or registry port, nor the lifecycle and designate verbs. The Models-screen additions and `calibration_kind` remain part 10d-2b.
