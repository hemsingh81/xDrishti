# 0231 — A robust Lab post may leave out the chance and plateau block or ask for its own number of random-entry seeds; both are what-ifs, and the cap on draws is a setting

- **Status:** Proposed (built as the CF-07 remainder, 2026-11-09). Decided under the owner's delegation (research, configurable, best default, log in the ADR); approved-by-delegation, becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no.** Research side only; no order is placed.
- **Date:** 2026-11-09.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** completes the `nulls`/`draws` item of the "still open" line of CF-07 after [0208](0208-the-lab-verdict-shows-a-random-entry-null-beside-the-sign-flip-in-report-only-mode-and-the-null-is-one-look-in-the-ledger-with-enforce-the-owners-switch.md), [0209](0209-the-lab-verdict-shows-a-placebo-set-and-a-mirror-gate-beside-the-random-entry-null-in-report-only-mode-and-the-placebo-set-is-a-second-null-look-in-the-ledger.md) and [0210](0210-the-lab-verdict-shows-a-plateau-gate-on-the-swept-parameters-read-from-the-stored-in-sample-sweep-in-report-only-mode-with-its-own-enforce-switch-and-no-new-look.md). Follows the pattern of [0229](0229-conservative-slippage-prices-each-fill-by-the-liquidity-tier-on-the-entry-date-tier-c-and-an-unavailable-tier-are-excluded-and-counted-and-the-stress-request-field-defaults-on-for-robust-only.md) (`stress`, `slippage`).

## Decision
1. **`nulls`** on `POST /api/lab/runs` (boolean). `true` or absent is the default (the block is on for `robust` when `Confidence:Nulls:Mode` is ReportOnly). `false` leaves the whole "chance and plateau" block out of that run: random-entry null, placebo set, mirror and plateau (the plateau gate lives in the same block, ADR 0210), and no `null` ledger rows are written. Refused (`invalid_input`) on `quick` and `full`, and on `robust` while `Confidence:Nulls:Enforce` or `Confidence:Plateau:Enforce` is on (the block, and the plateau gate inside it, would skip the cap; the post is refused and a queued run posted earlier fails at validate with `lab.nulls_off_enforced` or `lab.plateau_off_enforced`), as `stress: false` is under 0229.
2. **`draws`** (integer): the number of random-entry seeds for that run. Bounds 200 to `Confidence:Nulls:MaxDraws`. Refused with `nulls: false`, on non-robust methods, and while the nulls are Off. A `draws` equal to the configured `Confidence:Nulls:Draws` is the default and changes nothing.
3. **Defaults leave existing runs untouched.** Absent or spelled-default values store nothing (`lab_runs.run_options` stays null), add nothing to the key and no label: key, results and stored text are byte-identical. A non-default value adds `nulls=off` and/or `draws=N` to the stored options text and to the key, so it is another run.
4. **What-if, labelled.** A run with either option is non-default (`LabRunOptionsView.isDefault` false), carries the label `nulls: off (...)` or `draws: N random-entry seeds (not the configured number)`, can never be the scorecard's evidence basis and `HoldOutUnlocker` refuses it (no hold-out, manual or auto), by the same rule as conservative slippage (0229 items 8 and 10). `LabRunView.options` gains `nulls` (effective, true unless left out) and `draws` (null when the configured number).
5. **The cap is a setting.** `Confidence:Nulls:MaxDraws`, default **5000**, range 1000 to 20000; the configured `Draws` may not exceed it. Researched basis: CF-07 D1 gives 200 to 5000; the p-value is `(count_ge + 1) / (draws + 1)`, so 200 seeds resolve 1/201 = 0.005, finer than the 0.05 level, and 5000 resolve 0.0002; at 320 trades 5000 seeds are about 1.6 million single-trade walks, a few minutes on the owner's data (not timed, UNVERIFIED). Above 5000 the extra precision does not change a pass or fail at 0.05 except at the edge, so the spec's top stays the default cap; the owner may raise it. `MaxDraws` changes no result, so it is not part of the stored settings text or hash. The runner re-checks the stored number against the cap and falls back to the configured number if the cap was lowered after the post.
6. **Settings snapshot.** A run with its own draws shows `draws=N` in the block's `settings` text and its own `settingsHash`, so a figure says which number produced it.
7. **Served for the "Chance and plateau" block.** Nothing new is needed: `GET /api/lab/runs/{id}` and `.../report` already serve `verdict.nulls` (`mode`, `enforce`, `settings`, `basis`, `notes`, `randomEntry`, `placebos`, `mirror`, `plateau`, `capped`); the run view's `options` now says whether the block was left out or its seeds changed.

## Other open items of CF-07, decided
- **Swing strategies and non-fixed-target exits: not built; stay "left out and counted".** The walker restates intraday trades with a fixed reward-to-risk exit placed from a random minute in the entry window. A swing trade has no entry window and a trailing, time or signal exit has no target multiple to hold equal, so any "random entry with the same exit" would invent a rule. Research view: a null that compares different exit logic is not a null of timing. Behaviour kept: such trades are listed in the notes, and below 80 % coverage the null is `unavailable`, never a pass (ADR 0208). No setting added; the safest default is the existing one. Reopen with a spec for a swing entry-day null (random entry session, same hold rule).
- **Instrument-roll basket: kept as in ADR 0209** (rolls over the instruments that have an out-of-sample trade). Rolling over the whole basket reads every instrument's bars (a larger read, and adds no signal-timing information beyond the existing 15 shapes) and was not built; the safest default (no new read, fewer comparable placebos when the run has under six instruments, never a pass) stays. A setting is not added until the owner wants the larger read.

## Consequences
- The Lab form offers "skip chance block" and "seeds" as what-if inputs; any such run is clearly marked and never evidence.
- A cap lowered later does not break stored runs (fallback to the configured number, said in the block's notes); `draws` on a run whose nulls went Off after the post adds the warning `lab.draws_not_run`.
- Open: timing of 5000 seeds on real data.
