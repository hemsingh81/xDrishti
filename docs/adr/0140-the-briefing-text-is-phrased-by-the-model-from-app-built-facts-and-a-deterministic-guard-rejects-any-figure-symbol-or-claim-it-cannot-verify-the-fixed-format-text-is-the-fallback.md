# 0140 — The briefing and weekly review text is phrased by the model from facts the app builds; a deterministic guard rejects any figure, symbol or claim it cannot verify, and the fixed-format text is the fallback

- **Status:** Proposed (written in P9-06 under the owner's delegation of 2026-10-26; the spec was treated as Approved). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **SAFETY decision: it decides what a model may say to the owner about the plan and which text is stored.**
- **Date:** 2026-10-27.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0005](0005-local-llm-runtime-and-models.md) (the model runs on demand; the host swaps), [0136](0136-the-local-model-server-runs-in-a-container-on-demand-the-api-reaches-it-through-an-optional-chat-client-and-nothing-starts-or-waits-for-it.md) (the model client), [0138](0138-the-assistant-endpoint-runs-the-tool-loop-in-the-application-streams-events-marks-figures-not-found-in-the-tool-results-and-stores-every-turn-insert-only.md) (the tokenizer, the audit tables, the rule to ground only against results and the owner's question), [0094](0094-weekly-learning-digest.md) (the digest stays the source).

## Context
The owner wants a short morning briefing of the plan and a weekly review, readable without opening six screens. A model is good at phrasing and bad at keeping figures: a briefing that says a stop is 98.60 when the plan says 98.50 is worse than no briefing. The model must therefore never be the source of a figure, a symbol or a claim; it may only rearrange what the app hands it, and code decides whether the result is shown.

## Decision
1. **Who writes what.** The app builds the facts (`BriefingFactsBuilder`, from the stored plan and tickets, the digest and the report insights); the fixed-format text (`DeterministicBriefing`, `DeterministicWeeklyReview`) and the verdict (`TextGuard`) are code. The model only phrases. The model does not call tools for a briefing (rejected: a non-reproducible input set).
2. **The guard grounds only against the facts.** Never against the model's text or arguments (the rule of 0138, 0139). Every figure of a model text must be a figure of the facts (same sign, same percent-ness) either as printed or as the fact's value rounded half away from zero to the token's own, shorter precision (45.83 allows 45.8 and 46, not 45.9 and not 45.834). No arithmetic: a sum or a ratio is not in the facts. Tokens are read by P9-03's `NumberTokenizer` (so a year, a timeframe such as `15m` and an identifier such as `ORB15` are not figures, on both sides); dates, clock times and written dates must occur verbatim in the facts; a number word (two to twenty, tens, hundred, thousand, lakh, crore, half, double, twice) needs the same word in the facts.
3. **Scope.** A sentence that names tickets T1..Tk may only carry figures of T1..Tk and of global facts (`number_wrong_scope`); a sentence naming no ticket may carry any figure of the facts (a looser rule than "global only", chosen so that "the gate cut the risk to 0.50" is not refused; it still cannot carry a figure that is in no fact).
4. **Symbols and coverage.** A token matching `[A-Z][A-Z0-9&-]{2,}` must be a ticket symbol, an allowed acronym, or a token that occurs in the facts; every primary and reserve ticket must be named.
5. **Form and honesty.** At most 300 (daily) or 400 (weekly) words; no markup but `- ` bullets, no link, no code; no sentence that starts with a trade verb (`buy sell short enter exit hold add average`; heuristic: the quoted fields of a ticket start with the symbol, so the rule is simply "no sentence starts with the verb"); no forecast phrase; a fact marked unavailable, insufficient or not evaluable keeps its word (no figure in a sentence that names it); a provisional plan must be called provisional; `profit guaranteed`, `risk-free` and `certain` never. The lists are published in `GuardRules` and `BriefingWording` and pinned by a test. They are heuristics, not a proof.
6. **Weekly review.** Each insight's sample size (`over N trades`) must reappear (`evidence_dropped`); the pinned last line ("Nothing was applied: every item above is a proposal; applying any of it is the owner's own act.") is added by code, and a week without a digest is `not_evaluable` with no model call.
7. **Attempts and fallback.** Two attempts at temperature 0 and a fixed seed, the second told only the finding codes and the offending tokens (never the rejected text). A text cut by the token limit is a rejection. A failed guard, an unready or failing model, the switch off, or a plan with no ticket stores the fixed-format text with `reason` `guard_rejected`, `model_unavailable`, `model_off` or `facts_thin`. Both attempts are stored with their findings.
8. **The switches.** No model call unless `ai.settings.enabled` AND `Assistant:Briefing:UseModel`; both default off; the two services start paused. The verb's `--model off` forces the fixed-format text; there is no `--model on` that overrides a switch.
9. **Plan independence.** `briefing` (after `suggestions`) and `weekly-review` (after `weekly`) are services of their own: no lock, no await on the model inside `nightly` or `suggestions`, a failure is a failed service run. A model text is not started while `nightly` has a running run, while a chat turn holds the turn slot, or, for a daily briefing, between 17:30 IST and `EarliestIst` (default 20:00; P9-00 D13 keeps model work out of the nightly window). The run waits up to `GateWaitMinutes` (default 180) for these gates and then **stores nothing** (`assistant.briefing_deferred`), because the first stored text of a subject wins and a fallback stored for a transient reason would block the model text. (Deviation from the spec text "not before `EarliestIst`": the window is 17:30 to `EarliestIst`, so a manual run in the morning is not blocked.)
10. **Storage.** `ai.briefings` and `ai.briefing_attempts`, insert-only for `xd_app` (UPDATE and DELETE revoked; a grants test), one row per (kind, subject, version), the first stored wins, a repeat build returns the stored row and writes nothing, `xd assistant briefing --store --new-version` appends version n+1 (actor `cli:xd`). The facts block is stored with the text (with its SHA-256) so the text can be checked later. Only a plan date within 7 days, the current week or the last completed week can be stored; any other is a preview.
11. **What enters the facts.** Only structured, system-produced strings and numbers; each string is cleaned as untrusted text (`UntrustedText`) and cut; instruction-like patterns are counted in a log line. The digest's lessons section contributes its two count lines only (lesson titles are owner-typed). No account name, account number or balance; a total quantity per ticket only when `ai.settings.portfolio_context` is on.
12. **Reads.** `GET /api/assistant/briefings`, `/daily`, `/weekly`, `/preview`: read only, no write endpoint, no regenerate button; the card sits in the plan review and the digest tab (lazy chunks).
13. **Log events** 6160 to 6166: ids, counts, finding codes, milliseconds; never the text or a figure.

## Consequences
- Added: Domain `Assistant/Text` (guard, facts, fixed texts), Application `Ai/Briefing` (facts builder and source, handler, two services, options), ports in `Abstractions/Ai`, infrastructure store and writer adapter, migration `AddBriefings` (with `Down`), four read endpoints, the verb `xd assistant briefing`, the `BriefingCard`.
- The architecture tests got one allowance: the verb (`XDrishti.Api.Cli.AssistantBriefing*`) may reference the assistant namespaces, like the assistant endpoints.
- The model call goes through the existing chat model adapter (`IChatModel`), not a second client; `model_call_id` of an attempt is left null (the audit port does not return an id), the attempt row and the `ai.model_calls` row share time and purpose.

- Review fixes: digits glued to letters (`pnl1250`) and year-like integers must occur in the facts; a scoped figure needs its ticket named in the sentence; the text of a rejected attempt in `ai.briefing_attempts` is unverified model output by design (`passed` says so). `MaxPromptTokens` only bounds the weekly facts size; `model_call_id` stays null.

## Not verified in this build
- No real model was run. Whether a 30B model with thinking off keeps to the guard (the share of first attempts accepted) is for P9-09 set B to measure; if more than 2 of 10 are refused the prompt, not the guard, changes.
- `cache_prompt` is not controlled by the client (the chat client takes no such field); `params_json` says so. Greedy decoding is not a determinism guarantee on a GPU.
- The guard proves where a figure comes from; it cannot prove a sentence is true or well judged ("AAA looks strong" has no figure).

## Deviations from the spec text
- The facts builder lives in Application (it takes the plan and digest views), not in Domain. The insight's sample size is read from the insight sentence (`over N trades`), because the report view has no separate `n`. "System changes" (the changelog) are not in the facts: the risks carry the plan's basis flags and the notes carry the regime. The fixed-format text drops risk lines from the end, and then the ticket detail lines, when it would pass the word limit.
- The reads' DTO adds `repeated` and, per finding, `words`; the preview's `id` is null. `model_call_id` is null (see above). `--model on` is not offered as an override.
