# 0122 — Backtest versus paper is a composition-matched bootstrap of the pre-epoch backtest trades, computed on read

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [P10-04](../specs/P10-04-backtest-vs-paper-report.md), built 2026-10-26; the spec is still Draft). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-10-26.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0062](0062-the-lab-monte-carlo-is-a-seeded-bootstrap.md)-style Monte Carlo primitives (`MonteCarlo.Percentile`, `SeededRandom`), [0064](0064-cell-statistics-are-kept-as-insert-only-snapshots.md) (snapshots), [0099](0099-metric-states.md) and [0100](0100-charge-sheet-confirmation-is-read-only-by-the-report-loader.md) (states; the confirmation source is closed to paper code), [0116](0116-paper-fills-are-the-grading-replay-captured-per-armed-allocation.md) and [0119](0119-a-measurement-epoch-freezes-the-measured-configuration-changes-are-detected-classed-and-labelled-and-nothing-is-applied-or-blocked.md) (epochs, `measured`/`straddle`).

## Decision
1. **The paper mean is tested against what the same cells in the same proportions give by chance.** For each draw the i-th paper slot takes one R with replacement from the backtest trades of the cell of the i-th paper trade. The band is the 5th to 95th percentile (nearest rank, as `MonteCarlo`) of the draw means; the position is `below`, `within` or `above` (never green for within), with the mid-rank percentile of the paper mean among the draws. Picks are made in cell order so a permutation of the paper trades leaves the band unchanged.
2. **The drawdown reference is at the paper's n.** The same draws in slot order give the largest peak-to-trough fall of cumulative R; the reference is their 90th percentile (also 50th and 95th); `within` means paper <= P90. The Lab's stored Monte Carlo (the backtest's own count) is not used.
3. **Counted set.** Closed, primary, `measured`, one per ticket (`trade_key`, lowest entry id), `r_sim` in exit order (ties by entry instant, ticket id, entry id) of the epoch; reserve, straddle, open and other-epoch entries are counted apart.
4. **Point-in-time reference.** The newest snapshot with an as-of date before the epoch start (`ListLatestBeforeAsync`), its source runs through `CellSourceReader`, trades with an exit date before the start, unweighted. A run window that reaches into the paper period is cut at the start.
5. **Honest states.** Fewer than 30 counted trades: every figure `insufficient` with the count, nothing is read or drawn. 30 to 59: `provisional` (interim). 60 or more: `value`. A paper cell with no reference or under 30 reference trades makes the band and the drawdown `insufficient` and is listed; a basis (net/gross) or charge sheet differing from the cell's costing is `not_evaluable: basis differs`; no snapshot or unreadable sources is `unavailable`.
6. **Seeds** are SHA-256 of `epoch id | snapshot key | n` (the paper mean's own 90% interval uses the same text plus `|interval`) through `SeededRandom`; draws default 10,000 (1,000 to 100,000). The fan holds every trade up to 120 points, else every k-th and the last.
7. **Exit statistics.** E3 (expectancy) and E4 (drawdown) need 60 counted trades, an R after costs, a fact that the owner confirmed the sheet, and a band that could be drawn; otherwise `not_evaluable` with every reason. `ICostConfirmationFact` (`Application/Abstractions/Paper`) is the only way: its sole implementation here is `NotConfirmed`, because paper code may not read `IChargeSheetConfirmationSource` (ADR 0100); until the slice that writes the confirmation exists both statistics read `not_evaluable: costs not confirmed`. E3 `met` carries the interval caveat when the interval includes 0 and a luck-or-leak caveat when above the band. The early warning (30 or more trades and the paper interval's upper end under the band's lower edge) is a sentence, not a verdict.
8. **Sensitivity beside.** The plan-price grades of all primary tickets of the epoch (approved or not) give a second mean R, labelled `simulated on plan prices, all proposals`, never used by E3/E4. It is read through a new read-only `IGradeReader` (the store's existing read member, now its own interface that `IGradeStore` extends).
9. **Read only, nothing stored, no migration.** `GET /api/paper/backtest-vs-paper?epoch=&draws=`; verb `xd paper compare [--epoch ID] [--draws N]` (exit 0 whatever the verdicts); frontend tab **Backtest vs paper** (lazy; SVG fan with a table of the same numbers). Log ids 5246 to 5254.

## Consequences
- A met statistic is weak evidence at 60 trades (the 90% interval is about plus or minus 0.3R at a spread near 1.5R); the label says so. The reference is simulated and in-sample (cells were chosen on it, so the band is a stricter test), draws are independent although trades of one session are correlated, and the percentile bootstrap is approximate below about 50 trades.
- Resolved ambiguities: an unknown epoch id is 404 `paper.not_found` (as the sibling paper reads) and a non-positive id or a draw count out of range is 422 `paper.invalid`; with no epoch yet the view is empty (no number); the percentile rank counts ties as half; the drawdown position words are `within` and `above`; a cell's own paper and reference means need 30 trades; the reference charge sheet is compared only where the run header names one; a basis mismatch outranks a thin reference; n above 120 thins the fan.
- Not verified: the run time on the owner's data (reading the reference streams the source runs once; budget 3 s); the end-to-end run on real paper trades (none exist yet).
