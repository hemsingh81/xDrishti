# 0116 — Paper fills are the grading replay, captured per armed allocation

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [P10-01](../specs/P10-01-paper-fill-capture.md), built 2026-10-26; the spec is still Draft). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-10-26.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0090](0090-grading-replays-each-ticket-through-the-simulation-use-case.md) (grading, pending rules), [0037](0037-fill-rules.md)/[0038](0038-fill-rules-and-limits.md) (fill rules), [0042](0042-costs.md) (charge function), [0083](0083-the-plan-record.md) (no order-named member, insert-only), [0096](0096-pre-open-arming-and-expiry.md) (arming, expiry), [0024](0024-live-feed.md) (the feed keeps only the last price).

## Decision
1. **A paper trade is the grading replay of an armed allocation, taken after the close.** There are no live minute bars (ADR 0024), so each ticket whose trail has an `armed` row is replayed on the stored official 1-minute bars with the same simulator and rules as grading, once the `eod` run has stored them. One entry per ticket and paper account (unique `(plan_item_id, account_id)`), at the owner's quantity (the allocation less any stored size-down); the exit follows once the replay closes the trade. An unreadable stored modification skips the pair (`modification_unreadable`); the larger size is never used.
2. **One code path.** The per-ticket replay moved out of `GradePlansHandler` into `TicketReplayer`; grading and capture both call it, and an architecture test fails if either builds a simulation request itself. `TicketGrader` also hands back the trade it graded (or the open one), so a paper entry and its grade cannot differ.
3. **Nothing moves a ticket.** `plan-expiry` (15:40) ends Armed tickets before the bars exist and Expired is final, so the paper trade is its own record in schema `paper` (`epochs`, `epoch_accounts`, `entries`, `exits`, `skips`, `capture_runs`), all insert-only for `xd_app`. No `plan_events` row is written, no order, no broker call, no approval.
4. **Final facts are skips, pending is not stored.** `not_triggered`, `not_gradable`, `dropped_by_sizing`, `legs_not_supported`, `entered_before_armed` (the database also refuses an entry before arming), `modification_unreadable` and, added here, `approval_missing` (an armed ticket with no stored approval, a data fault never guessed). A refused or unfinished replay is counted in the session's `capture_runs.pending` (outcome `partial`) and retried by the next run. A later replay that disagrees with a stored entry overwrites nothing and is counted as `integrity`.
5. **Costs from the one setting.** `Planning:Grading:CostSheetId` and `Capital` are shared with grading (`ReplayCosts`). Charges are the sum of `CostModel.Leg` for the entry and exit orders at the account quantity on the dates of the fills; a configured sheet that is not stored fails the run `paper.sheet_missing` and writes nothing; without a sheet charges, net rupees and net R are null and the basis says `gross, no charges`. `r_sim` is the replay's R (the grade's), whatever the number of accounts.
6. **Coverage.** Every trading session from the epoch's start to the last closed one gets a `capture_runs` row, empty sessions included, so "captured through" is readable from that table alone. A session is looked at again when its newest row is not `done` or an entry of it has no exit. At most 30 sessions with armed tickets are replayed per run.
7. **Epochs.** `IEpochGate` decides the epoch and whether a session is `measured` or `straddle`. The default `SingleEpochGate` has one epoch of kind `first`, started on the India date of the first capture (forward only, nothing back-filled), every session measured; P10-05 replaces it. `epoch_accounts` rows (capital from `Planning:Grading:Capital`) are written for the accounts of written entries.
8. **Off by default.** The service `paper-capture` starts paused (daily 17:00, after `eod`, not before 16:30, same-day catch-up); switching it on is the owner's act. `xd paper capture [--date]` is the manual run, `xd paper epoch show` reads the epoch (it never starts one). The API is read only: `GET /api/paper/entries`, `GET /api/paper/capture-runs`, each carrying the label and the limits.

## Consequences
- Paper adds the operating chain (approved and armed), the owner's size and a forward-only record; it adds no execution realism (touch is fill, no queue, no latency). Every response says so.
- A strategy that fires twice in a session is captured once, as graded. Trades with legs are skipped.
- The epoch starts when the first capture runs, not before; the owner's pre-flight (P10-00) restarts it later through P10-05.
- Log ids 5200 to 5205 are used; P10 keeps 5200 to 5254. Logs and the stored run reason carry ids, counts and fixed reason codes only: never a symbol, a price, simulator text or an exception message.
- ADR 0115 is reserved for P7-20 (price alerts), which is why this decision is numbered 0116.
- The paper `exit_price` (and `entry_price`) is the **filled** price (modelled slippage and the tick grid); the grade stores the exact simulated `ExitPrice`. The two may differ (equal on a target, different on a stop); R is identical (`r_sim` = the grade's R). A test pins the relation.
- A session that stays pending for good is replayed on every run (no give-up age); the cap of 30 sessions with armed tickets per run bounds the cost.
- The entry/skip pair of a ticket and account can never coexist: each insert is guarded by `WHERE NOT EXISTS` on the other table, and the first epoch is unique (`uq_epochs_first`) so racing first captures store one.
