# 0093 — Auto-journal reads the stored fills, matches them to approved tickets by account, instrument, side and day, and stores a fill grade beside the plan grade

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [P7-14](../specs/P7-14-auto-journal.md), approved under the owner's standing run instruction of 2026-10-22; built 2026-10-24). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-10-24.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0025](0025-dhan-trade-history-and-ledger-sync.md) (the read-only trade-history sync that stores the fills), [0083](0083-the-plan-record-insert-only-state-trails-owner-only-approvals-lapse-by-default-and-no-order-by-name.md) (plan record, allocations, no order), [0090](0090-grading-replays-each-ticket-through-the-simulator-on-its-own-session-and-stores-one-insert-only-grade.md) (the plan-price grade this sits beside). The owner's answers of 2026-10-23 hold: no orders in P7, paper only, no auto-approval, no automatic learning change.

## Context
Doc 12 section 8 wants positions linked to the plan ticket they came from and the owner's other trades tagged discretionary; doc 09 section 9 wants a grade on real fills, kept beside the simulated one. The portfolio sync already stores the broker's fills in `portfolio.transactions`. That table has no `plan_item_id` (verified 2026-10-24), and it is the portfolio's, not the plan's.

## Decision
1. **The journal only reads.** It selects broker-source, non-voided transactions of Trading-enabled or allocated accounts and the plan record. No broker endpoint, credential or order is involved, and no portfolio row is written or changed. A fill from a live account is journaled like any other and is never turned into a ticket.
2. **The link is the journal's own insert-only tables, not a column on the portfolio.** `trading.journal_entries` (one per ticket and account, a composite foreign key to the ticket's own plan and to the real allocation), `trading.journal_fills` (one row per trade, unique, foreign key to the transaction; role entry, exit or discretionary; a check that exactly the discretionary rows have no entry) and `trading.fill_grades` (one per entry, label check; status graded, no_plan_levels or uncertain). A trigger refuses a fill linked to an entry of another account (the portfolio table is not ours to add a key to). `xd_app` has SELECT and INSERT only, granted per table; the migration `AddJournal` has a `Down`.
3. **Pure matcher.** A fill is an entry of a ticket when account, normalised symbol, side and day agree, the ticket is approved (approved, armed, triggered or closed) and the fill is not before the approval. A fill matches at most one ticket; a ticket may have several fills. **A fill that fits two or more tickets is not guessed**: it is left unmatched with the reason, stored as nothing and listed by the verb. Entries are matched across all sessions before any exit, so a later session's entry is never an earlier session's exit. Exits are the other side's later fills, first-in first-out up to the entered quantity, never an entry; **a fill goes whole to the first ticket that takes it** (quantity capped) so a trade has one link and the next ticket stays open; everything else on the day is discretionary. Deterministic: ordered by date, time and id; the same input in any order gives the same links.
4. **Fill grade, labelled `realised fills, not simulated`.** Only a finished round trip, and only when the plan-price grade of the ticket is stored. The plan ticket keeps its levels as text, so the plan's numeric levels are the plan grade's simulated entry and initial stop (a nullable `grades.stop_price`, set from now on; older grades have none). 1R is their distance; slippage is the side-signed difference between the fill's volume-weighted entry and the plan entry (positive is worse), in price and in R; R is gross profit minus the charges the sync recorded, over 1R times the quantity. **An uncertain round trip gets no R:** when the account's earlier trades leave a net position in the instrument before the entry, or an exit fill is larger than the entered quantity, the grade is `uncertain` (rupee result only; flag and reason on the journal entry and in the API). Without usable plan levels the round trip is stored as `no_plan_levels` with the rupee result and no R.
5. **No look-ahead.** The window of fills is the session to the earlier of today and 30 days after it; a session after today is refused; an exit must be strictly after the entry (same day only when both have a time); a grade uses only the stored links and the stored plan grade.
6. **Idempotent; first write wins.** Unique indexes and `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`; sessions are processed oldest first. A late fill that would have changed a stored decision does not change it.
7. **Surface.** `xd plan journal [--date]` and `GET /api/plans/{id}/journal`. Log ids 4750 to 4752.

## Consequences
- The first fill grades exist only when the owner trades an approved paper ticket and the sync stores it; until then the journal reports discretionary fills and approved tickets without fills.
- Fill grades do not include a plan grade that arrives later: they wait (pending) until it exists, then are written once.
- **Shortcuts, stated:** the pre-existing position is read from the app's own trade history (shares bought before it started are invisible; upgrade when per-lot FIFO is available); a same-day fill with no time is matched as an entry even if the approval came later; a trade voided after journaling keeps its insert-only row but is left out of every read; open entries are graded newest first, 500 a run; the symbol is compared after normalisation, not by instrument id; the exit window is 30 calendar days; a swing position held longer stays open; charges are the sync's recorded totals prorated by quantity.
- **Not built:** the nightly service entry, post-trade prompts, grading of discretionary trades, a screen.

## Alternatives considered
- **Add `plan_item_id` to `portfolio.transactions`** (doc 12) — rejected: the table is updatable by the runtime role and belongs to the portfolio; a link column would make a read-only slice write to it and make the link editable.
- **Guess the best ticket when two fit** — rejected: a wrong link would put a wrong R into the learning evidence; unmatched is visible and costs nothing.
- **Compute R from the text levels of the ticket** — rejected: the text is not a number.
