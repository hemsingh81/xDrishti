## Context
Costs are gross forever (ADR 0292), so no confirmed charge sheet can exist. Five gates read only net figures and so could never pass: the forward edge (readiness Effect, Agreement, Stability, row 15), the Lab cost-sheet row, the decay cost-stress kill, the Auto-on `charge-sheet` precondition and the live-readiness soft item `charge_sheet_confirmed`. Gross figures flatter a strategy; a stated cost per trade takes that back.

## Decision
1. **One setting, `Confidence:Costs:BufferR`**: default **0.10 R per round trip**, range 0.05 to 0.50, tier Advanced, declared safer **higher** (tighten-only): raising it needs no password, lowering it is an owner act with the fresh password (the AU-10 rule `LearnedRules.Loosens`); a value out of range stops the host. Not on the learned allow-list. Every figure built with it carries the label `gross_less_buffer(<v>R)` and the setting snapshot `Confidence:Costs:BufferR=<v>`; nothing built this way is labelled net. *Why 0.10 R:* an NSE intraday equity round trip at Dhan is about 0.04 to 0.06 % of turnover (brokerage capped at ₹20 an order, STT 0.025 % on the sell, exchange about 0.003 %, SEBI fee, stamp duty 0.003 % on the buy, GST on brokerage and exchange fees) plus slippage; with stops at about 0.5 to 1 % of price that is 0.05 to 0.12 R, and 0.10 R sits at the top. Swing trades (delivery STT 0.1 % each side) cost more per round trip but have wider stops; the same buffer is used, and a swing strategy whose stops are tight should raise it.
2. **Forward edge.** The one edge series (`EdgeSeries.Figures(selection, buffer)`) is each graded trade's gross R (`ShadowCostBasis.GrossOf`) less the buffer, for the scorecard (`ForwardEdgeReader`), the sequential failure test and the decay report alike, so they still read one series. Mean, SD and the positive-month share use the existing anchors unchanged; fewer than 2 trades stays unavailable. The decay reference of a gross backtest is read on the same basis (each trade less the buffer); the scorecard compares the band only when the reference's basis label equals the series' (same buffer), and names the refusal `reference_basis_mismatch` otherwise. A gross P10 behavioural series is read the same way. The sequential test reading gross less the buffer is a tightening (it can only cross earlier).
3. **Lab cost-basis row.** A gross Lab run's CF-05 cost stress is computed on gross less the buffer: each trade's cost is the buffer, so its R at multiplier m is gross − m × buffer, judged against the existing floors (the buffer is the stated cost, so no bill check is asked); the block carries `CostBasis = gross_less_buffer(<v>R)`. An unusable buffer leaves the rows unavailable. The readiness hard-fail term is renamed **"cost basis not stated or cost stress not run"**: Clear when the verdict's cost stress ran on that basis with a figure on every row, Unavailable otherwise (no block, a verdict stored before GROSS-01, a sheet-costed run), and Unavailable is a hard fail. On a buffered run the 1x row is judged on its own figure (gross − 1 × buffer) against the expectancy gate's adjusted threshold, not on the gross gate's verdict; every row's reason, the report's warnings and its formula name the basis label, never net. The delay test is unchanged (it still needs a checked sheet, so it reads unavailable on a gross run; with `CostStress:Enforce` on that still caps a PASS at MARGINAL). **Deliberate loosening under `CostStress:Enforce`:** before GROSS-01 every gross run was capped at MARGINAL by stress rows that could only be unavailable, because the data they waited for (a checked sheet) can never exist; the new bar is gross − m × buffer against the existing floors. The readiness checklist's rows 3 and 6 (and the backtest side of row 15) read the verdict's out-of-sample expectancy less the buffer with the same label; the spread of row 6 is taken from the gross figures (a constant shift does not change it). The fold counts of the stability sub-score stay the verdict's stored gross counts: the per-fold expectancies are not stored, so they cannot be taken less the buffer without re-running the Lab.
4. **Decay cost-stress kill.** net = gross R − buffer, cost = buffer, then `KillRules.CostStress` with `Confidence:Kill:StressMultiplier` over the trailing 100 trades: it fires when the trailing gross mean − multiplier × buffer is below zero. The kill text names the basis and the setting.
5. **Auto-on.** The precondition `charge-sheet` is replaced by `cost-basis`: Pass when the buffer setting is valid (its label and snapshot are the detail), Fail named otherwise; the ON snapshot records `costs.basis` and `Confidence:Costs:BufferR`.
6. **Live readiness.** The soft item `charge_sheet_confirmed` is replaced by `cost_basis_stated`: it passes when the buffer setting is valid; otherwise it needs the owner's typed reason. Every hard item and the owner's act are still needed; no setting places an order.

## Unreachable branches
The buffer is a singleton validated at start (the host refuses an out-of-range value), so "unreadable or invalid" cannot occur in a running host. The Auto-on Fail arm, the live-readiness false/null arm and the Lab's unusable-buffer arm are kept fail closed (marked `shortcut:` in the code) for a host that skips the validation.

## Left in place (a later cleanup slice)
`CostChoice` and the now-dead net code: `ShadowCostBasis.ReadAsync`/`ShadowCostView` and `EdgeSeries.Figures(selection, ShadowCostView)` (still used by the shadow summary, which shows gross R labelled gross), `ICostConfirmationFact`, `IChargeSheetConfirmationSource` and `NoChargeSheetConfirmation`, the net branch of `LabCostStress` (`RExact` and a checked sheet), `CostStressView.CostsChecked`/`SheetId`, the net reference basis of the decay report, `KillRules.CostStress`'s default "net R" wording, the paper phase check's `ChargeSheetConfirmed` (P10, not one of the five gates; it still reads "costs not confirmed"), net reports (`reports.net_not_confirmed`), replay, options and pairs costing.

## Consequences
- Readiness, kills, Auto and live readiness can pass without any owner input; each judges gross R less a buffer at the top of the researched range, so the gates stay on the safe side of gross.
- Stored verdicts from before GROSS-01 have no `CostBasis` and read Unavailable until the Lab full test is re-run.
- API: `CostStressView.CostBasis` (optional) is added; the contract is regenerated.
