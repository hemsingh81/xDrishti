# 0049 — The exit tape, trades with legs, and engine `sim-6`

- **Status:** Accepted (spec [P4-01](../specs/P4-01-the-exit-tape-and-trades-with-legs.md), which the owner approved on 2026-10-19 with "yes to all recommendations", questions 1 to 6; built on 2026-10-21 ahead of the P3 exit check because the owner pulled P4 forward). The text of this ADR has not been read by the owner.
- **Date:** 2026-10-21.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** ADR 0037 (the two-stage seam, whose "the walker knows no frame" this keeps), ADR 0038 (the rule table of the minute, whose X2 to X5 are reused unchanged; the reading of X5 against R6 is decided here), ADR 0040 (swing positions and the gate, which hold a trade with legs until its last leg), ADR 0042 (costs and sizing, extended to one order per leg), ADR 0043 (run-record schema 5, now 6), ADR 0045 (the result store, which does not get a column in this slice), ADR 0047 (the oracle and the rule tables, whose R6 is decided for the corner of ISS-28). The plan is [P4-00](../specs/P4-00-exits-and-exit-learning-plan.md). The number is 0049 and not the 0048 the spec names: 0048 was taken by the automated P3 gate on the same day.

## Context
Every later exit of doc 08 (a partial exit, a trail, an add) needs two things the P3 walker does not have: **values computed at the close of each evaluation bar after the entry** (an ATR, an N-bar extreme, an EMA) that a stop may follow, and a **trade that is several fills of one position** with one R. P4-01 builds both, adds no exit policy, and proves them with a rule that lives only in the test project. Every existing run must give the same entries, exits and R as before. The slice also had to take the open oracle entry Q3-MFE (ISS-28) in the same version change.

## Decision
1. **A tape per instrument and side, not a series per candidate** (the refinement of P4-00 D1 that question 1 of the spec approved). `ExitTape` (public, `XDrishti.Simulation`) holds, for each step at which the evaluation timeframe closed a bar, the close instant (the end of the minute that closed it), the bar's close and the value of each feature the candidate exits read (`ExitPlan.Reads`), **as of that close**. `EntryScanner.Tape` builds it during the replay that already runs, at no extra pass; when no exit reads a feature (every strategy today) it is the shared `ExitTape.Empty`, never written to, and the scanner allocates nothing more than before. A feature that is not ready is NaN, which a rule reads as "no new level". **A step that closes two bars gives one row, with the later bar's values:** the frame cannot give the earlier bar's value, so the earlier bar has no row and a rule sees no level from it (the spec's `Unreliable` flag is not needed: a missing row says the same).
2. **The one look-ahead function is `ExitTape.LastClosedAtOrBefore(instant)`** (binary search): a rule at the minute that starts at *t* may read bar *k* only if `CloseEpoch[k] <= t`, which is the first minute at or after the close; a bar that closes at the fill instant is the decision bar and is never read.
3. **`IExitRule` is internal to `XDrishti.Simulation`** (one implementation per candidate exit; the test project is a friend assembly). The walker calls it once for each bar it sees for the first time, in order, with a view of the position (side, entry, the initial risk `R0`, stop, target, open share, exit legs so far) and applies the actions it appends: `MoveStop(level)`, `MoveTarget(level)`, `ExitShare(share, code)`. An add of shares is P4-04's and has no action yet. **`FIXED_RR` is the absence of a rule** (`TradeWalker.Walk(candidate, bars, rules)` is `Walk(candidate, bars, rules, null)`), not a first implementation of `IExitRule`: that keeps the sim-5 rows, the allocation test and the benchmark untouched by construction, which the spec's "byte for byte" asked for. `TradeWalker.Walk(…, ExitContext)` is internal; a production caller cannot reach it.
4. **The order inside one minute, with legs** (spec D6, as built). The levels a rule moved by the bars closed at or before the minute's start are in force **from that minute on and never inside the minute that closed their bar** (a test puts a minute inside the bar that touches the new level). A stop is only tightened: a request for a looser (or, for a sell, higher) level is ignored and counted (`StopLoosenIgnored`). The shares a rule asked to leave go at the **open** of the minute that sees the request, as a market fill with the slippage of a square-off (S1 to S3, no extra stop ticks); **if that open is already through the stop, the request is dropped and the stop row takes everything** (the same pessimism as X4), and a partial exit and the stop of the same minute are two legs of one minute. The rows X2 to X5 are applied to the stop and the target as they stood at the minute's start, by the one routine `MinuteRule.Decide`; the intraday loop, the swing loop and `MinuteRule.AdvanceLegs` are the only users of `MinuteRule` (the architecture rule is unchanged).
5. **`SimTrade` schema 5.** The 33 positional fields of schema 4 keep their order and meaning; `Legs` (`ValueList<Leg>?`, a list with value equality) is added last and is null for a trade that entered and left in one fill each, which is every trade of a production run; a trade of one entry and one exit is never written with legs. A `Leg` is `(EpochSeconds, Price, FilledPrice, Share, Kind, Reason)`; `Kind` is `Entry`, `Add` or `Exit`; `Share` (decimal) is the fraction of the maximum quantity; `Reason` is the `ExitReason` name for the last leg and the rule's code for a partial one. For a trade with legs the entry fields are those of the first leg, the exit fields those of the last exit leg, `StopPrice` and `TargetPrice` are the initial ones, and the shares out equal the shares in. The key is unchanged.
6. **R of a trade with legs** (D10). The unit is `R0` of the first fill, never changed. `RExact` is the sum over the exit legs of `share × side × (exit − entry) / R0` (with the share-weighted average entry of the entry and add legs once adds exist; no walker produces an add yet), `RFilled` the same from the filled prices, `SlippageR = RFilled − RExact` (never positive). MAE and MFE are measured against the entry in units of `R0` over all minutes the position was open; a minute with a partial exit counts in full until the exit. The decimal arithmetic is one expression, `side × (final × (exit − entry) + Σ share × (part − entry)) / R0`, in the walker and in the oracle.
7. **Costs per leg** (D11, ADR 0042). Each leg with shares is one order on the schedule in force on **its own date**; the quantity of an entry or add leg is `floor(Q × share)` and of an exit leg the same, the **last exit leg taking what is left** so that the quantities sum exactly; a leg that would have no shares is merged into the next and not charged (`LegsMerged`). `TradeCosting.LegCharges` (new, last, null for a trade without legs) lists the charge of every charged leg; `EntryLeg` and `ExitLeg` stay as before for a trade without legs and are the sums of the entry-side and the exit-side orders for a trade with legs; `TotalCost` is the sum of all orders and `CostR = TotalCost / (Q × R0)`. A charge above `max_cost_R` skips the whole trade, never a leg.
8. **The position gate** holds the instrument until the **last** exit leg (the walker ends at it, so `HoldEndEpochSeconds` is that leg's instant, or the end of the due session as before).
9. **Versions and stores.** `SimulationEngine.Version` `sim-6`; `SimTrade.CurrentSchemaVersion` 5; `SimulationRun.CurrentSchemaVersion` 6; the export format `xd-sim-export-3` (the CSV gains a `legs` column and `run.json` the `legs` and `leg_charges` members, null for a trade without: the format is raised because the file shape changed, not because of a trailing nullable field in general). New walker counters `LegsTotal`, `LegsMerged` and `StopLoosenIgnored` (zero for every production run; they are in `WalkCounters` and not in the run key). **The result store gets no column in this slice** (the spec's cut order): `StoreRunValidation` refuses a trade with legs by name (`run.trades.legs`), and `research.sim_trades` is unchanged, so there is no migration; a run stored under `sim-5` (trade schema 4, no legs) is still read, as the same trade with `SchemaVersion` 5. A migration `AddSimTradeLegs` (`legs jsonb`, `cost_leg_charges jsonb`) is for the slice that first produces a trade with legs (P4-02) or for P4-05, whichever comes first.
10. **Cards.** The card of a trade with legs gets a `legs` section (time, kind and reason, price, filled price, share, quantity and charge of its order when costed) after the costs; the figures above it are the entry and the last exit leg, and the section says that R is for the whole trade. The leg marks on the chart are not built (the spec's first cut); a card of a trade without legs is byte-identical.
11. **Q3-MFE (ISS-28) is decided: the engine changes, not the rule.** The favourable extreme is widened to **at least the close of the exit minute** on the three paths that leave at a close (the intraday end of the data, the swing end of the data, the swing `MaxHold`), which is what R6 says ("the last minute counts in full") and X5 forbids only inside a fill minute at the limit that is not the last. The effect is confined to an entry at the limit whose fill minute is also its exit minute; the oracle does the same (`WalkRun.BuildTrade`). No generator reaches the corner (the regenerated golden files differ from the sim-5 ones in the engine and schema lines and the run key only), so no earlier number moves except in that corner. The exclusion predicate of `ExcursionInvariantTests` is deleted and two planted series check the corner.
12. **The oracle** (`tests/XDrishti.SimOracle`) is extended for the ladder rule (`OracleLadder`, `OracleLeg`, `OracleTrade.Legs`, three counters) from the rules above, and `LadderComparisonTests` compares it with the engine on 19,000 series in both fill modes; the leak property, the stop that never loosens and the invariants of the legs hold over 11,100 series; thirteen trades with legs are worked by hand. See *Tested by*.

## Consequences
- Every `FIXED_RR` run is unchanged except the engine label, the schema numbers, the run key (it holds both) and the corner of decision 11. Measured on the explicit walker benchmarks (three runs each, medians, Release, the `sim-5` sources and these side by side on one machine): the intraday walk **+3 %** (25.9 ms to 26.9 ms), the swing walk with a hold of five sessions **+2 %**; the allocation test of the scanner and the walker passes unchanged. The target was 5 %.
- Nothing in a production path makes a leg: `Simulator.Create` still refuses every exit set but one `FIXED_RR`, the only `IExitRule` implementations are in the test project, and an architecture test holds that. P4-02 is where the first policy produces legs, and the first slice that needs the store column.
- The tape is built only when asked for and is bounded by `bars of E × (features + 2)` doubles per instrument and side; it is not released earlier than the scanner.
- A trade with legs is not in a stored run until the column exists; the export files hold it.
- `docs/design` 08 (§2, §5, §8), 11 and 18 and the oracle log say what is built; the dry-read table of the oracle has rows for the four new fields.

## Honest limits
- The rule that proves the seam (`TestLadderRule`: a stop at the bar's low, one partial exit at +1R) is not a policy of doc 08; the legs, the tape and the order inside a minute are tested with it and with a scripted rule that makes three exits, and the rules of the real policies (break-even, a chandelier, an EMA exit) are for the slices that build them.
- The oracle, the engine and the test rule are written by the same model family from the same spec: the comparison finds differences between two readings, not a misreading both share. One was found on the first run (the oracle reported the moved stop as `StopPrice`; D8 says the initial one), and it was the oracle's.
- MAE and MFE of a pyramid against the average entry are specified (D10) but nothing exercises an add; question 3 of the spec stands as an assumption until P4-04.
- The 3 % cost on the walker is one run on one machine, three samples each; the margin to the 5 % target is within what a second run can move.

## Alternatives considered
- **A per-candidate exit series** (P4-00 D1 as first written). Rejected on the reading of the scanner: it steps one frame per instrument and side and a candidate is only a mark on that walk; copying the post-entry values into every candidate would store the same numbers many times and allocate in a loop that allocates nothing.
- **`FIXED_RR` as the first `IExitRule`.** Rejected here for the reason in decision 3; P4-02 may express it as a rule when there is a second policy to share the machinery.
- **Amend R6 to say what the code did.** Rejected: it would leave `MfeR` below `RExact` on a trade whose whole life is one minute, and the rule text already said the minute counts in full.
- **Store the legs now.** Deferred (decision 9): no production trade has a leg, and a column that nothing writes is a migration without a test of real data.
- **A tolerance for the oracle's legs.** Rejected: legs are compared exactly (prices, shares, kinds, reasons); only the five ratio fields keep the 1e-12 of ADR 0047, and in this run they were bit-equal.
