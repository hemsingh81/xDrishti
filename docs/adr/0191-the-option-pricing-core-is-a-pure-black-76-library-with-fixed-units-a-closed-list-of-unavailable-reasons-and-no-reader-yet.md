# 0191 — The option pricing core is a pure Black-76 library with fixed units, a closed list of unavailable reasons and no reader yet

- **Status:** Proposed (built in P14-05 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Amended by [0192](0192-the-pairs-core-is-a-pure-engle-granger-library-with-pinned-mackinnon-tables-a-benjamini-hochberg-rule-that-counts-unavailable-tests-and-a-signal-state-machine-that-never-trades-on-an-unavailable-z-and-no-reader-yet.md): `NormalDist` moved to `Domain/Common`.** **Safety slice: no** (pure functions: no I/O, no setting, no table, no route, no order).
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0178](0178-the-option-archive-quality-checks-read-the-recorded-chains-and-rolling-bars-in-bounded-ranges-and-store-insert-only-verdicts-with-an-honest-unavailable-and-no-trading-use.md) (parity forward, `r` = `Options:Quality:Rate`, `T` = calendar days / 365, European style).

## Decision
1. **`Domain/Options/Pricing`** holds the pure library: `NormalDist`, `Black76` (price and Greeks on a forward), `BlackScholes` (the spot form, `F = S e^((r-q)T)`, `q` default 0), `ImpliedVol` (safeguarded Newton), `ParityForward` (returns a `Forward` value with its `ForwardSource` label), `ExpiryTime`, `IvUnits`, `PricedValue<T>` and the closed `UnavailableReason`. It depends on nothing but `System` and `Domain/Common`; an architecture test pins that, pins the absence of a clock and a random source, and pins that no other Domain area, no Application or Infrastructure type and no host or library references it yet (P14-07 and later loosen this with an exact allow-list).
2. **Units (printed in doc 20 section C).** Prices in rupees as `double`; sigma a fraction per year; `T` = (expiry date 15:30 at +05:30 minus `at`) / (365 x 86,400); delta and gamma per rupee of spot; **vega per 1 volatility point**; **theta per calendar day** (annual theta / 365). Vendor IV is read as percent / 100 and carries the label `percent_assumed` (UNVERIFIED until the P14-02 pilot).
3. **Honest states.** Ten reasons (`bad_input`, `bad_vol`, `expired`, `bad_rate`, `bad_price`, `below_intrinsic`, `above_upper_bound`, `no_convergence`, `vol_not_identified`, `no_two_sided_quote`). A result holds a value or a reason, never both; a `default` result is unavailable. Nothing returns 0, NaN or infinity for an unavailable value and nothing throws on a number (only a null argument throws; reading `.Value` of an unavailable result is a caller bug and throws).
4. **IV solver.** Bracket [1e-4, 5.0], Newton with bisection fallback, at most 100 iterations, tolerance `1e-10 max(1, price)`; up to three guarded polishing Newton steps after the price test passes (while the step exceeds 1e-13 and the residual does not grow), because the price test alone leaves an error in sigma of price-tolerance / vega for in-the-money prices (three seeded inputs missed 1e-8 with a single step and are fixed regression rows; the round trip runs over five seeds). `vol_not_identified` uses the raw vega (derivative in sigma, not per point) against `1e-8 F`.
5. **`NormalDist.Cdf`** is G. Marsaglia's series (|x| < 3) and the Laplace Mills-ratio continued fraction (|x| >= 3), absolute error below 1e-14 against `erfc`; exactly 0 or 1 beyond +-38. The spec named Cody's rational approximations as an example; the series and fraction need no tabled coefficients to mistype and meet the stated error (tested against tabled values).
6. **Plan errors recorded, not bent.** (a) The hand-table IV cases name a recovery within 1e-8 from prices printed with 6 decimals; the rounding (up to 5e-7) over a raw vega of 28 gives 2e-8. The exact-price recovery is tested at 1e-8 and the printed-price recovery at 1e-6 / raw vega. (b) Finite-difference theta is a central difference in `T` (step 1e-4 relative), not "one calendar day back" (a one-day difference differs from the derivative by far more than 1e-5). (c) The finite-difference test skips cases with sigma sqrt(T) below 0.05: the step is 1e-4 of spot and the price varies on the scale of `S sigma sqrt(T)`, so the difference is not a derivative there. (d) The spec's `vol_not_identified` example is built as F 100, K 150, T 0.25, DF 1, call price 1e-9 (root near sigma 0.115, raw vega about 1e-7).

## Consequences
- P14-07, 08, 10 and 11 can price and mark with one tested core; each must label a model price `bs_fallback` and count it.
- The 10,000-case property tests (parity, bounds, monotonicity, finite differences, round trip, determinism across a thread pool) run in under a second.

## Known limits
- European exercise only (decision D6: positions exit before expiry); stock options UNVERIFIED (ADR 0178).
- Vendor IV and Greeks come from an unknown model and rate; this core only lets the P14-02 pilot compare.
- The speed targets (1 us price and Greeks, 20 us solve) are UNVERIFIED and ungated; no benchmark test was added in this slice.
