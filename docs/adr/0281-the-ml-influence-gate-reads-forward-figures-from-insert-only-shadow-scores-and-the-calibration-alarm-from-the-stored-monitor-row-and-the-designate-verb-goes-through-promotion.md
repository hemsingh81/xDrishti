# 0281 — The ML influence gate reads forward figures from insert-only shadow scores and the calibration alarm from the stored monitor row, and the designate verb goes through promotion

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [CF-10e](../specs/CF-10e-ml-influence-evidence-sources.md), parts 10e-1 and 10e-2, approved 2026-11-12 as the coordinator's recommendation under the owner's standing instruction). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-11-12.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** amends [ADR 0217](0217-a-model-may-influence-a-decision-only-through-one-gate-whose-predicate-needs-an-unspent-hold-out-forward-evidence-no-calibration-alarm-and-the-owners-switch.md) (the sources of its inputs; the truth table, the bars and the switch are unchanged) and [ADR 0220](0220-a-challenger-is-promoted-only-on-the-spec-rule-and-a-champion-is-rolled-back-by-the-p6-09-detectors-behind-three-owner-switches-that-are-all-off-by-default.md) (the designate tripwire is satisfied by D6); builds on [ADR 0211](0211-the-shadow-record-writes-every-nightly-candidate-before-the-open-forward-only-and-insert-only-chained-by-digest-as-a-count-separate-from-the-p10-paper-count-and-the-switch-is-the-owners-and-off-by-default.md) (the shadow entries and their cut-off).

## Decision
1. **Scores go in their own insert-only table.** `paper.shadow_scores(entry_id, model_id, model_sha256, feature_version, p_win, features_hash, scored_at)`, unique per (entry, model), written in the same transaction as the session by the shadow writer. The database stamps `scored_at` and refuses a score at or after 09:00 India time of the entry's session. The hashed model columns of `shadow_entries` are not touched.
2. **Every registered champion and challenger scores every candidate**, up to five models (the champion first, then the newest challengers; the rest are skipped and logged). Only models of catalog `tf-1` and width 41 are scored. Scoring never stops the session: a failure, or a start later than `Shadow:ScoringDeadlineMinutesBeforeCutoff` (default 30) before the cut-off, writes the session without scores, and the score inserts sit behind a savepoint so that a database refusal of a score (the 09:00 guard, a check) rolls back to it and commits the session without scores.
3. **Features are those of the decision at the close of the last session before the candidate's session.** The extractor is given only bars that start before that close; the cell features are the newest stored cell statistics whose as-of date is before the session. A candidate whose features cannot be built, or whose required features are unknown, gets no score row; nothing is imputed.
4. **Forward figures** (pure, `ShadowForwardFigures`): n, weeks, the top half minus the bottom half of mean R by probability (ties by trade key, ordinal; the middle candidate of an odd n is left out) and the existing ECE of the newest 100 graded rows. Scoring is cut off at the deadline itself (a linked cancellation, not only checked at its start); a real shutdown still propagates. The calibration trigger needs max(window, check interval) outcomes for its first check. A win is an R above zero. No row: unavailable.
5. **Below the 100-outcome window the calibration trigger is Unavailable, never Quiet** (`RollbackPolicy`: the detector makes no check there, and "no event" would read as a pass). **The calibration alarm is the calibration verdict of the model's newest `ml_monitor_checks` row**, which is fed by the champion's graded scores (`ShadowRollbackEvidence`): Fires is true, Quiet is false; no row, a row older than the last closed session, an unreadable row or an Unavailable reading (fewer than 100 observations) is null, which is a refusal. The band trigger stays unavailable.
6. **`ShadowMlInfluenceEvidence` is the one `IMlInfluenceEvidence`.** The hold-out figures stay unavailable until part 10e-3, which waits for HO-01. So the gate still refuses every model.
7. **The designate verb goes through promotion.** `xd learn designate ... champion`, once any model has ever been champion (a current one, or one retired or demoted since; one existence query over the designations), asks `PromoteChallengerCommand` (the command line's actor is passed on as `owner:` plus the actor), so the promotion rule decides; only the first champion ever is named on the champion rule alone. This satisfies the tripwire of ADR 0220 without relaxing it.
8. **Nothing else changes.** The truth table, the thresholds, `IsChampion`, the switch (OFF), the empty consumer list and the advisory-only rule are as they were; nothing here reads a score into a plan, a size or an order.

## Consequences
- Migration `AddShadowScores` (raw SQL, no EF mapping; the model snapshot does not change). `Down` drops the trigger, the function and the table. Grants: select and insert only.
- Scoring needs the shadow switch ON (OFF by default) and a registered model of the current catalog; until then no score exists and every forward figure is unavailable.
- `shortcut:` the cap of five models is a constant, not the `Confidence:MlInfluence:MaxScoredModels` option of the spec. Bars are read 730 days back from the decision (not from the start of history, as the training extraction does): enough for every indicator up to the weekly timeframe, while a monthly cell has too few bars and gets no score; an instrument whose backfill seam lies inside the window gets no score either (its candidates are counted as missing), so serving never differs from training; the read is the plain high-water-cut read with the plain factors (only the run reader may admit fresh pre-seam bars, ADR 0273 decision 6), which matches training for every instrument whose run admitted none. The nightly time is logged in `shadow.score`. A grade's win is R above zero, not the triple-barrier label. The known-at instant of a calibration observation is the close of the candidate's session.
- The first challenger cannot be promoted by the verb while `NoChallengerEvidence` stands; the refusal carries the promotion reasons.
