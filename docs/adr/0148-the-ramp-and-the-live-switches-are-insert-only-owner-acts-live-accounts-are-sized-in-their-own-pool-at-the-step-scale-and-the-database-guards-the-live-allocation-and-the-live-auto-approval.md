# 0148 - The ramp and the live switches are insert-only owner acts; live accounts are sized in their own pool at the step scale; the database guards the live allocation and the live automatic approval

- **Status:** Accepted (decided in P11-04a under the owner's delegation of 2026-10-28; spec [P11-04](../specs/P11-04-scale-up-rules-and-risk-ramp.md), first half).
- **Date:** 2026-10-28.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0089](0089-per-account-allocation-a-pure-split-of-the-ticket-by-each-paper-accounts-own-limits-and-unavailable-allocating-nothing.md) (the allocation was paper-only; this relaxes it for `manual_live` with a scale), [0087](0087-pretrade-checklist-pure-unavailable-never-passes-and-an-owner-only-confirm-despite.md) (checklist), [0091](0091-auto-approval-is-an-option-behind-an-owner-setting-default-off-a-new-approval-kind-the-database-checks.md) (auto approval, snapshots), [0130](0130-the-owner-act-forms-are-endpoints-behind-a-capability-a-fresh-password-and-an-audit-row-over-the-existing-handlers-and-state-machines-and-apply-nothing.md) and [0147](0147-the-compliance-record-is-one-insert-only-owner-written-store-for-the-static-ip-sebi-and-dhan-read-policy-enquiry.md) (owner act, gate `Live:Writes:Enabled`).

## Context
A `manual_live` account may receive tickets only at a fraction of its profile's risk that the owner sets and that later rules can only lower. This slice (04A) builds what must exist before any live capture: the step, the scale, the live pool, the live-ready checklist gate, the account-mode gate and the live automatic-approval guard. The evidence gates, holds, halts and the evaluator are 04B.

## Decision
1. **`trading.ramp_events` and `trading.live_settings` are insert-only** (`MakeAppendOnlyForAppRole`). The state of an account is its newest ramp event; no event means no step and no live allocation. A guard trigger checks who writes which kind (owner: `set_step`, `clear_hold`, `exit_confirmed`; `rule:ramp`: `hold`, `step_down`, `auto_step_up`), that the first event is the owner setting S1, the from-step equals the current step, no step is skipped upwards, S3 needs an earlier `exit_confirmed`, a hold blocks raising, and an automatic step-up stays at step 2 with a settings snapshot. The live switches are stamped by a trigger (id under the exclusive lock, time, SHA-256 of the canonical text) like `workflow.autonomy_settings`.
2. **Owner writes** (`POST /api/live-trading/ramp/steps`, `PUT /api/live-trading/settings`) use the gate `Live:Writes:Enabled` first, then a fresh password (`Elevate: false`), the actor from the session, the sign-in rate limit, and one audit event (`RampStepSet`, `LiveSettingsChanged`) with numbers, never the reason. A raise is refused with 409 `live.gates_not_met` in this slice (the gates do not exist, and an input that cannot be read is never a pass); S1 first and a lower step are allowed.
3. **Two pools.** `PerAccountAllocator` takes `PoolMode` (default `Paper`, byte-identical for P7). `PlanWiring` allocates the paper accounts as before and the manual-live accounts in a second call with `PoolMode.Live`, after multiplying their risk per trade, total open risk and capital per trade by the ramp scale; the checklist limits of a live account are scaled the same way. A live account with no step, or no usable capital, gets nothing. The rows carry `risk_scale` and `ramp_event_id`.
4. **The table repeats the rules.** `ck_plan_item_allocations_shape` becomes `quantity > 0 AND (paper OR (manual_live AND risk_scale in (0,1] AND ramp_event_id IS NOT NULL))`; a trigger checks that the event belongs to the same account and that the scale is not above the ceiling of the event's step (0.25, 0.5, 1.0); a paper row has neither. `Live:Ramp:StepFactors` (own sub-section class, validated at start) may be lower than the ceilings, never higher. **P12-07 must keep both requirements and the ceiling for every live mode.**
5. **Checklist.** `account_live_ready` replaces `account_paper` as the first check of a live account and is never confirmable. It fails for no step, an open halt, a capture gap or a loss that cannot be measured; a fact no source reports is `unavailable`, which is not a pass. In 04A only the step has a source, so the gate is `unavailable` and no live ticket can be approved until P11-01 and 04B supply the rest: the safe direction. `StoredChecklist` rebuilds the live list from the plan's standings; the auto-approval rule compares each account to the list for its mode.
6. **Live automatic approval.** A ticket with a live allocation is auto-approved only when `autonomy.auto_approve_tickets` and `live.auto_approve_tickets` are ON in stored rows, both snapshots are carried (`workflow.approvals.live_settings_id/hash`, composite foreign key), and a trigger `approvals_live_auto_guard` refuses the insert unless the row is the newest and ON. Reserve promotion passes no live settings, so a live ticket is never promoted. The rule's policy version is `auto-approval/2`.
7. **Account mode gate.** Switching an account from paper to `manual_live` in `PUT /api/accounts/{id}/trading` runs `LiveReadiness`: hard items (Trading role complete, fixed capital, Dhan connection `connected`, a step, `live-capture` and `live-settle` registered and enabled) refuse with 422 `accounts.live_not_ready` listing every failing item; soft items (paper phase check not MET, charge sheet not confirmed, order book not captured) need a typed `liveConfirmReason` (1 to 300 characters), recorded in the audit text. Switching back to paper is never judged. Until P11-01 registers the two services the gate cannot pass.
8. **Paper readers ignore live rows**: paper fill capture and the weekly paper review read only `mode = 'paper'` allocations.

## Consequences
- The Ramp tab of `/live` (lazy) shows the step, the live risk, the history and the two switches (both OFF), and opens two lazy password dialogs; no setting allows an order.
- A live account in the book makes its standings (`checklist:account_live_ready:...`) part of every ticket's unavailable inputs, so automatic approval declines the plan until the live gate can pass, as the old `account_not_paper` standing did.
- The P7 phase check counts a live allocation as not paper; that is honest once live rows exist.
- Limits: creating an account directly as `manual_live` is not judged (it has no step, so it gets no allocation); the Accounts form does not yet send `liveConfirmReason` (the hard items cannot pass anyway before P11-01); `live.auto_ramp_up` is stored and shown but acts only with 04B.
- Rejected: a separate "go live" endpoint (two ways to one state); raising by the owner with the gates unread (an unreadable gate is never a pass); a raise to S3 without `exit_confirmed` (the table refuses it).
