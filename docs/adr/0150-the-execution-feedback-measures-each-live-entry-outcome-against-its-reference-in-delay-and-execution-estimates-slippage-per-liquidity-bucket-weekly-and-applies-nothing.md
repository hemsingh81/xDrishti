# 0150 - The execution feedback measures each live entry outcome against its reference in delay and execution, estimates slippage per liquidity bucket weekly, scores capacity as the share of orders filled in full, and applies nothing

- **Status:** Accepted (decided in P11-02 under the owner's instruction of 2026-10-28 to build the slice; spec [P11-02](../specs/P11-02-execution-feedback-grade-on-fills-slippage-and-capacity.md), with its "Amendments at build").
- **Date:** 2026-10-28.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0149](0149-the-live-capture-reads-the-days-trade-book-and-order-book-with-two-pinned-get-entries-stores-them-insert-only-and-gives-each-approved-ticket-one-final-outcome.md) (the captured fills, orders and outcomes this slice reads; live fills are not journaled there), [0038](0038-fill-realism-the-entry-limit-gaps-slippage-ticks-and-the-order-inside-a-minute.md) (the modelled slippage the plan-grade reference carries), [0064](0064-cell-statistics-bayesian-shrinkage-recency-weights-and-point-in-time-snapshots.md) (the Beta shrinkage reused for the capacity score), [0090](0090-grading-replays-each-ticket-through-the-simulator-on-its-own-session-and-stores-one-insert-only-grade.md) (the plan grade), [0099](0099-the-report-engine-is-a-pure-core-with-one-population-rule-six-honest-states-and-a-ranking-by-the-credible-lower-bound.md) (the six states and the evidence block), [0148](0148-the-ramp-and-the-live-switches-are-insert-only-owner-acts-live-accounts-are-sized-in-their-own-pool-at-the-step-scale-and-the-database-guards-the-live-allocation-and-the-live-auto-approval.md) (the gates of P11-04b will read these figures).

## Context
After a manual-live session the owner needs to know how far the fills landed from the plan, how much of that is the time before the order and how much the execution itself, how it depends on the instrument's liquidity, and how often an order filled in full. The numbers must be honest at the sizes of a small live account (most buckets will rest on a handful of trades) and must never move a trading behaviour by themselves.

## Decision
1. **The observation unit is an entry outcome of P11-01, not a journal entry.** P11-01 did not journal live fills (that is P11-01b), so `trading.live_executions` is keyed by `outcome_id` (FK `live_ticket_outcomes`, unique): one row per outcome that is `filled`, `partial` or `over_allocation`, built from the matched entry fills (`LiveFillMatcher`, unchanged). The journal-based fill grade (`FillGrader` on live fills) waits for P11-01b; the waterfall below needs only the stored plan grade and the matched fills.
2. **Reference and split.** The reference is the ticket's numeric entry level (`ticket_level`), else the plan grade's simulated entry (`plan_grade`), else none (every slippage figure `unavailable`, never zero). The stop of the ticket level falls back to the plan grade's stop for the R figures. total = d x (VWAP - reference), delay = d x (arrival - reference), execution = d x (VWAP - arrival), in decimal arithmetic so delay + execution equals the total exactly (the table also checks it). Arrival is the quantity-weighted open of the 1-minute bar each filled entry order was created in; if any order has no bar, or there is no order evidence, arrival, delay and execution are null and the total stands. `entry_delay_seconds` is the wait from the first entry order's creation to the first entry fill (spec left the meaning open; the trigger instant is not stored).
3. **Buckets by turnover alone.** The 20-session mean of close x volume of the daily bars before the day, in crore (the plan items do not store it); edges `Live:Buckets:EdgesCr` (default 10, 50, 150, 500, 3 to 6 ascending values, so up to L7), unknown turnover is its own bucket `unknown`. Participation is stored as a covariate, not used.
4. **Statistics.** Per (bucket, side, style, reference kind): n, mean with the 95 % Student-t interval, median with the order-statistic interval (ranks from the binomial tail through the regularised incomplete beta, brute-force checked in the tests). The t table has the spec's rows plus df 5 to 8 (the smallest `Live:Estimates:MinN` is 6). Below `MinN` (10) the row is `insufficient`: n and the words, no mean (the table refuses a mean on an insufficient row). The two reference kinds are separate rows; an entry with no reference is left out of the estimates. A proposal sentence (numbers and the knob `slippage.ticks`) is stored beside a row only at n >= `ProposalMinN` (20) with a positive lower bound of the mean; nothing reads it.
5. **Capacity.** One class per entry order of an approved live ticket (full, partial, rejected, queue_miss, price_miss, unclassified), stored in a fourth insert-only table `live_order_classes` (one row per account and broker order, written by the same step) so the weekly job needs no bars. A limit that was traded through (the lows of its life below a buy limit, the highs above a sell limit) and did not fill is a `queue_miss`; one the market never went through is a `price_miss` (not counted); a non-limit or an order without price or bars is `unclassified` (not counted). The score is the share filled in full as a Beta posterior mean with a 95 % credible interval: the account uses the uniform prior Beta(1 + k, 1 + n - k); a bucket is shrunk toward the account's rate with strength `Live:Capacity:PriorStrength` (10) through the cells' `BetaShrinkage`; fewer than `Live:Capacity:MinN` (10) counted orders is `insufficient` and no score is given. The cell scope of the spec is not built (the live rows carry no cell key). Display only: selection does not read it.
6. **The waterfall** (plan result - entry slippage - exit difference = actual gross, then the charges line to net) is computed from columns stored on the execution (`plan_entry`, `plan_exit`, `plan_stop`, `plan_charges_r`, `exit_vwap`, `exit_qty`, `live_charges`) by a pure function whose rows add up exactly (decimal arithmetic, identity checked by property tests with a doctored-price negative control). It exists only for a graded plan whose position was closed in the session by exit fills of the same quantity; otherwise it is `unavailable` with the reason. Net exists only when every fill of the trip has a recorded charge (intraday fills have none today, so those trips are gross).
7. **Schedule and switches.** The measuring step runs inside the `live-settle` service (after the settle) and in `xd live capture`; the weekly `live-estimates` service (Saturday 10:00, catch-up the same day) is registered **paused**, `Live:Estimates:Enabled` (OFF) only seeds it at the first start. Estimates pool all live accounts and are keyed by the Monday of the week, over every execution up to the end of that week; they are insert-only with one snapshot per week and key (a second run writes nothing).
8. **Nothing is applied and nothing is owner-written.** No estimate reaches the simulator, selection or a cell (D13 of P11-00). There is no owner write, so `Live:Writes:Enabled` and the fresh password are not involved and no auto-approval is needed. The slice reads stored rows and stored bars only; it never calls Dhan, and nothing places, changes or cancels an order.
9. **API and CLI are read only.** `GET /api/live-trading/executions`, `/slippage`, `/capacity` (`weekStart` a Monday; no `accountId`, because the estimates pool the accounts), `/waterfall?entryId=`; `xd live estimates [--week]`, `xd live waterfall --entry`. Every figure is a state and a number only for a value; every response carries the evidence (basis, n, session range, reference kinds, bucket edges, limits). Log ids 5110 to 5112.

## Amendments at build (what the spec left open or got wrong)
- `journal_entry_id` became `outcome_id` (decision 1); `grades.entry_at` is **not** added (the entry time comes from the fills and orders, and no grade logic changes).
- Columns added to `live_executions`: the plan and exit columns of decision 6. A fourth table `live_order_classes` (decision 5).
- `accountId` is dropped from the slippage and capacity endpoints (decision 9); the cell scope of capacity is not built.
- `Live:Estimates:Enabled`, `Live:Buckets:EdgesCr`, `Live:Estimates:MinN` / `ProposalMinN`, `Live:Capacity:PriorStrength` / `MinN`; the edges are optional (unset = default) so the array default is not appended to.

## Consequences
- `/live` gains a lazy Execution tab (read only); the first-load bundle is unchanged.
- Limits: arrival is a bar open, not a quote; for a resting limit the execution part includes the wait for the market; the plan-grade reference carries the modelled slippage, so a positive figure means worse than the model; a partial exit, an addition to a position, or an exit on a later session is not decomposed; charges are recorded only for delivery fills, so intraday round trips are gross; the unit tests and the SQL are checked against hand-worked data and a scratch database, not against real Dhan answers; most buckets stay `insufficient` for weeks; a snapshot is frozen by the first run of its week (run the estimates after the week's settle), so a manual run mid-week omits later executions of that week; two approved tickets with the same symbol and side on one day share their orders (first outcome wins).
- Rejected: updating rows (insert-only discipline); pooling the two reference kinds; a bootstrap interval (too few observations); applying the estimate to the simulator (moves the goalposts of the experiment rule 3); an impact model (unverified at this size).
