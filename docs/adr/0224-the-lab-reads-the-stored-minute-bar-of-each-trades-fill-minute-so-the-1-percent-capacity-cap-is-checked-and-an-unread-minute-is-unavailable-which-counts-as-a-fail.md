# 0224 — The Lab reads the stored minute bar of each trade's fill minute so the 1 % capacity cap is checked, and an unread minute is `unavailable`, which counts as a fail

- **Status:** Proposed (built as scorecard-source slice S2b, 2026-11-08). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no.** Report-only research numbers; nothing places an order, sizes a trade, changes a verdict or moves a cell. Row 14 can only block an owner act that the owner's enforce switch (OFF by default, ADR 0221) already governs.
- **Date:** 2026-11-08.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** completes the follow-up S2b of [0223](0223-the-lab-verdict-counts-each-trades-liquidity-tier-and-capacity-breach-from-day-bars-report-only-and-readiness-row-14-reads-them-with-unavailable-as-a-fail.md); amends its decision 3 (row 14 could not pass) and its interpretation of the fill-minute cap.

## Context
ADR 0223 left the 1 % fill-minute cap unavailable because the minute bar of every out-of-sample trade was not read and its cost was unmeasured. The cost is now measured on the local 60-million-row `market.bars_1m`: a join of the wanted instants to the primary key (instrument, bar start) costs about 0.03 to 0.25 ms per instant; `ANY(array)` was 10 times slower.

## Decision
1. **One bounded minute read.** `IBarQueries.ReadMinuteCloseVolumesAsync(instrumentId, starts)` returns close and volume of the stored 1-minute bars that start at exactly the given instants (up to 1000 per call, no scan; a missing bar is absent, not an error). Its only caller is `LabTiers`.
2. **Fill minute = the minute bar that starts at the entry instant.** Traded value is close x volume (the day bar's definition, `LiquidityTiers.MinuteTradedValue`); a non-finite or non-positive close, or a volume of zero or less, is unavailable (never zero-and-pass). `LiquidityTiers.Assess` already holds the order against `CapacityFillShare` of it.
3. **Bounded cost, fail closed.** New settings `Confidence:CostStress:FillMinuteMaxTrades` (default 5000, 0..20000) and `FillMinuteTimeoutSeconds` (default 60, 1..600). Trades beyond the bound (taken in a fixed order: instrument, then entry time), a failed read (logged, instrument id and count only), a timeout, a missing bar or no volume leave that trade's capacity reading unavailable, never a pass. These two are cost bounds, not gate thresholds: lowering them or raising them cannot make a pass the stored figures do not support, so they are range-validated at start-up rather than tighten-only. A breach of either cap is still a breach and the definite fail wins over unavailable.
4. **The block records the read.** `CostStressView.Tiers` gains `MinuteReads`, `MinuteUnavailable` and `MinuteReadLimit` (optional, last position, no migration). A block written before this slice has them null: row 14 reads it as `unavailable: ... re-run the robust test` (it cannot pass: its 1 % cap was never judged). Row 14 now passes when the block has trades, no tier C, no breach, no unavailable tier or capacity reading and every trade's fill minute was read.
5. The tier settings text and hash are unchanged (the two new settings are not part of them), so earlier stored hashes stay valid.

## Consequences
Row 14 can pass for a freshly run robust verdict whose every trade is tier A or B, within both caps, and whose fill minutes are all stored. With more than `FillMinuteMaxTrades` out-of-sample trades, or any missing minute bar, it stays `unavailable` (a fail). Old verdicts are not re-run. Tests: the pure minute traded value table, the batched read, bound, timeout and failure paths of `LabTiers`, the reader's pass, fail, unavailable and old-JSON cases, the options validation table, and the architecture pin that `ReadMinuteCloseVolumesAsync` has one caller.
