# 0272 — The system replay is research only, fenced from evidence, hindsight-labelled, and runs the live pieces over as-of facts in memory

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [SR-01](../specs/SR-01-whole-system-replay-from-a-chosen-date.md) Part A, approved by the owner 2026-11-11 with every open question as recommended). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-11-11.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** builds on [ADR 0086](0086-the-fail-closed-nightly-pipeline-gates-one-row-per-attempt-a-null-builder-and-a-paused-service.md) (the replay does not call the nightly), [ADR 0116](0116-paper-fills-are-the-grading-replay-captured-per-armed-allocation.md) (one replay path for a ticket), [ADR 0118](0118-the-paper-ledger-is-derived-from-entries-and-exits-and-the-plan-may-read-it-behind-an-off-by-default-setting.md) (the ledger is the only equity), [ADR 0207](0207-a-strategy-version-is-pre-registered-the-ledger-counts-every-extra-look-and-a-locked-hold-out-is-fixed-at-the-first-post-and-read-once-by-an-owner-unlock-with-enforcement-and-the-honest-count-as-the-owners-switches.md) (the hold-out), [ADR 0217](0217-a-model-may-influence-a-decision-only-through-one-gate-whose-predicate-needs-an-unspent-hold-out-forward-evidence-no-calibration-alarm-and-the-owners-switch.md) (no score source in a decision namespace) and [ADR 0271](0271-the-run-record-states-depth-per-instrument-and-the-survivorship-limit-from-its-own-stored-bars-and-only-for-a-run-that-rests-on-backfilled-history.md) (honest survivorship wording).

## Decision
1. **Research only.** A replay places no order, calls no broker, names no `XDrishti.Execution` type and writes to no planning, paper, shadow, learning, model or settings table. Part A writes nothing at all (the book is in memory); part B adds its own `research` tables and nothing else: insert-only, except five columns of a run (state, cursor, result hash, end time, failure reason). Architecture tests pin the three (`ReplayArchitectureTests`).
2. **It is the live plan builder over as-of facts, not a second engine.** One session S is replayed by handing the live `SelectionPlanBuilder` (selection, smart rules, cluster check, portfolio fill, size scaling, wiring) ports that return the facts as of the close of S and a synthetic replay account sized from the replay equity. The nightly handler, its gates and `IPlanStore` are never called. A taken item is filled on the next session through the one `TicketReplayer`; equity, open risk, drawdown and the realised series come only from `PaperLedger`. A trade whose exit is after S is open at S: the book at S shows an exit only on or after its own session. Re-using the builder rather than copying its pieces makes AC 5 (same pieces as live) true by construction; the golden test checks the glue.
3. **Facts are read through `IReplayFacts`, bars only through a clamped reader.** Every read takes the close of S. Bars go through `ClampedBars`: a session after the hold-out start less one day less the embargo (`HoldOut:TrainingEmbargoDays`) throws `ReplayHoldOutException`; with no hold-out block fixed the replay **fails closed**. The driver refuses an end date past the clamp with `replay.holdout` and the last allowed date.
4. **Hindsight is labelled.** `versions_start`, `settings_now`, `survivorship`, `data_seen_before`, `before_costs` (and `lifecycle_as_recorded` in learning mode) are on every run and every session record.
5. **Block reasons are a closed list of eight** (`gate`, `smart_rule`, `max_positions`, `total_risk`, `cluster_cap`, `capital`, `data_missing`, `no_version`). The mapping from the live builder's codes keeps the engine code beside the reason. Choices made: the market gate and the selection eligibility filters are `gate`; the smart-rule codes, the merge conflicts and the other diversification checks (sector, basket, correlation, one per symbol, held long-term, book unknown) are `smart_rule`; open positions, entries per day, plan items and the full reserve are `max_positions`; per-trade, total and net risk are `total_risk`; a basket budget that cuts a ticket out, or a taken ticket with no quantity, is `capital`; an unknown code of a rejected item is `gate` (never a pass).
6. **The eight owner decisions (spec questions), as taken:** (1) `versions=start` by default, `asOf` kept; (2) one settings snapshot, labelled; (3) learning recomputes on the weekly cadence; (4) the lifecycle as recorded in the changelog as of S; (5) sizing from the replay equity (compounding); (6) each replay counts as a CF-06 look of kind `system_replay` (part B); (7) net figures only once the charge sheet is filled in, until then gross and labelled (ISS-25); (8) the scope defaults to the auto scope as of D, through `AutoScopeResolver.ResolveAsync(asOf, ...)`, whose `null` is the live read byte for byte.

## Things found at build, which settle the two UNVERIFIED items of the spec
- **Cadence (question 3).** No scheduled cell-statistics job exists in the code: cell statistics are computed only by the `xd learn cells` verb. The design (docs 04 and 16) schedules a weekly learning service (Saturday 10:00), and ADR 0094 says the `weekly` service retrains nothing. The replay therefore uses **weekly**: the first week of the run uses the frozen rows; each later Monday the P6-01 calculator is run **in memory** over the closed trades of the source runs of the frozen snapshot (`CellSource.TryParseList` of its sources text, read through `IRunStore`), as of the Sunday before, with the options of the frozen snapshot; nothing is stored. The P6-01 calculator is pure and reads only trades that exited on or before its as-of date, so it runs without its store.
- **Lifecycle (question 4).** `CellLifecycle` is a table of eight legal moves, each needing facts the system cannot produce by itself (out-of-sample gates passed, the owner's approval of a candidate, paper days). It is not a self-running rule, so it is not run in memory. Both modes read the changelog: frozen as of the start, learning as recorded through the close of S, labelled `lifecycle_as_recorded`. Before October 2026 the changelog is empty, so every cell is in its initial state there.

## Decisions taken where the spec left room (the stricter choice each time)
- **`CostChoice` is not in the Domain** (it lives in Application), so `ReplayParams` holds `CostSheetId` (null is gross) and the Application maps it to `CostChoice.Sheet(id, capital)` or `Gross`.
- **The freeze date is the plan date of the first session**, not D itself, so that `ListLatestBeforeAsync` and the cell state are what the live plan for that session reads (AC 5 holds exactly). Nothing after the close of D is known.
- **Strategy versions under `asOf`** are derived from `ListVersionsAsync` (`CreatedAt` before the end of S); no version is `no_version`, never a guess.
- **The auto scope's stored definition is the one in force** (the store has no history reader); only its basket members are read as of the date. This is hindsight and is part of `settings_now`.
- **Sector, basket names and the instrument master are today's**, and the replay account holds nothing long-term (`HeldLong` false). Part of `settings_now`.
- **The session after the end of the range is never read**: the last replayed session records its plan and the note `end_of_range`, and books no fill. The range may not pass the clamp.
- **With the default selection rules every candidate with no event, F&O ban or surveillance data is held back** (live behaviour, unchanged); the snapshot takes the rules the live builder has, so a replay of the default settings takes few or no trades. Loosening is the owner's, in part C.

## Review changes (Opus review of part A)
- **Fills know their instrument.** `TicketReplayer.ReplayAsync` has an overload taking the candidate's instrument id, so a past member that has left every basket is still filled and holds its slot (the live callers pass nothing and are unchanged).
- **The clamp holds on the fill and exit paths.** The simulator reads bars outside `ClampedBars`, so the session runner checks the clamp (`LastReadableSessionAsync`) before the plan's session, every fill and every exit check, whatever the run context says. A missing hold-out block is a `replay.holdout` result from the driver.
- **Learning reads the sources of the newest snapshot**, not those of the frozen one: a run made after the start holds the trades that the replay "learns" from, and the P6-01 calculator's rule (only trades that exited on or before the as-of date) makes the cut. The strategies were designed on this history: the `data_seen_before` label covers it.
- **One classification helper** (`TicketClassification`) turns a cell and a version into a compiled strategy and an instrument into tickets for both the live ticket source and the replay's candidate source.
- **Two per-run switches, both off by default (the live behaviour):** `FiltersWithoutHistory` makes the event, F&O-ban and surveillance filters optional (label `filters_without_history`); `IncludeResearchCells` lets a cell in the research state, or with no recorded state, be a candidate (label `research_cells`). Both are in the parameters that are stored with the run.
- **No frozen cell statistics as of the start** is refused with `replay.invalid`, so a run never draws a flat curve.
- **Accepted as hindsight under `settings_now`:** the latest auto-scope definition (part B stores its hash). A basket archived on or before the cut contributes no member. The rolling-window bar load is part B (a `shortcut:` comment marks it).

## Part B decisions (persistence and the job)
- **Tables** (migration `AddSystemReplay`, schema `research`, raw SQL, no EF entity): `replay_runs` (id, pair id, range, mode, params and settings snapshot as jsonb, **input hash**, **scope hash**, result hash, state, cursor, sessions total, created, finished, failure reason), `replay_sessions` (run, session, record jsonb, equity, drawdown, the record's SHA-256) and `replay_trades` (a trade is stored when it closes, or when the run ends with it open). The runtime role reads and inserts, and updates only the five run columns; a trigger keeps a finished run final and every run as it was started. `ReplayStore` is the only code that names them; the architecture tests list the three tables as the whole of what a replay may write (the trial-ledger row goes through the ledger port, which is the ledger's own writer).
- **One running replay, a pair as one request.** The insert takes an advisory lock and refuses (`replay.busy`) while any run is queued, running or cancelling. A frozen and a learning run of one request are stored together under one pair id and run one after the other, oldest first. A claim takes a queued run only when none runs.
- **The cursor and the session are one transaction.** A session, its closed trades and the move of the cursor are stored together, idempotent by (run, session): a kill leaves all of it or none of it.
- **Resume replays the stored sessions again, in memory, without writing them.** The book (taken items, open swing trades) is not stored in a form the replay can resume from, so a run that restarts runs sessions D to the cursor again from the stored settings snapshot (never today's) and compares the hash of each record with the stored one; a difference fails the run with `replay.diverged` instead of mixing two histories. Then it goes on at the next session. The cost is one extra pass over the done sessions per restart; a yield does not pay it, because the run in hand stays in memory across ticks.
- **Cancel** (`cancelling` between the request and the end of the session in flight): a queued run becomes `cancelled` at once; the job looks at the state before each new session, stores the open trades, and ends the run `cancelled` with its finished sessions kept. A run is never `complete` unless every session was replayed.
- **Failure never skips.** A failed session (a `Result` failure, a hold-out read, or any exception) ends the run `failed` with `session <date>: <code>: <message>`; the failed session is not stored and no later one runs. A host stop is not a failure: the run stays `running` and the next start takes it back.
- **Yield.** The rules of `SimAllRunner.YieldAsync` (low disk, market hours with a 30-minute margin, a bar-history job or a recovery pass, an incomplete history backfill) and a running simulate-everything chunk. They are looked at before each new session; a session in flight is not cut short (it takes seconds, not the half hour of a chunk). The settings are in the `Replay` section (Internal descriptors: incomplete-data share 0.2, disk floor, market hours).
- **Recovery class `Convergent`** (`ReplayRunner.Recovery`, pinned in `RecoveryClassTableTests`). The job is a hosted loop like the simulate-everything job, not a scheduled service, so it has no `ServiceDefinition`; running it again closes whatever a stop left open.
- **The input hash** is the SHA-256 of the canonical JSON (keys in ordinal order) of the parameters, the settings snapshot, the scope hash, the strategy version hashes by strategy (those in force for the cells at the start) and the **bar hash** (the day bars, with their count of 1-minute bars and completeness, of the instruments of the universe at the start and at the end, from the lookback before D to E, read through the clamp). The **scope hash** covers the chosen baskets and instruments and the member ids at D and E (this settles the open item of part A: the latest auto-scope definition is hashed as the scope it resolved to). The **result hash** is the SHA-256 of the session record hashes in session order. Two runs with the same input hash give the same result hash because every input is as of a date and the step is deterministic; a resumed run gives the same records and so the same hash.
- **The trial ledger.** A start writes one `lab.test_ledger` row of kind `system_replay` per strategy version of the run, with the run's input hash as the run key and no extra trials (the kind is added to the table's check constraint in the same migration). It is not a training sweep, so evidence readers count it: its hash is a variant of the family (stricter, never looser). A run whose rows cannot be written is cancelled and the failure goes on, so no run starts unrecorded. The number of replays over the same range (this one included) is a column of the run read (`TrialNumber`).
- **Rolling-window bars.** `ClampedBars.PlanRange(E)` (called by the driver through `IReplayFacts.BeginRun`) makes the reader load the bars of an instrument once for the run, from the lookback of the first cut to the close of E (never past the clamp), through `SeamAwareBars`; each cut is a slice that still passes the clamp check and ends at the close of the cut. The window drops what is older than the lookback of the cut. A test pins that every slice equals the read of its own window. **Measured** (explicit `ReplayBenchmarks`, Release, Apple Silicon, in-memory stores, the real facts, ticket builder and session step, 100 instruments, 259 sessions of 2026): 283.6 s in all, **1.09 s per session** (slowest 1.69 s); no candidate was found by the invented walk, so the selection and fill work of a busy session is not in the figure. The database read of the bars (once per instrument per run) and the simulation of a held swing trade are not in it either; a held trade is re-simulated from its fill on every session, at most as many trades as the open-position limit allows, so that cost does not grow with the length of the run and was left as it is. At about 1 s per session the engine takes minutes for a year, far inside the 8-hour target; the owner's database read is the figure still to measure. Memory is about 48 bytes per bar: 100 instruments over a year and the 400-day lookback is about 0.8 GB, which is a limit of the one-year, 100-stock target, not of the design.
- **Read side.** `IReplayReads`: a run with completeness (state, sessions done of total, the as-of session, `Complete`, trial number), the list, a pair, sessions by range, one session, the trades and the summary figures from `PaperLedger` over the stored trades. Reads only; the endpoints are part C.

## Consequences
Parts C to E add the API, the comparison and the screen on this core. The measured time per session of one year over 100 instruments is in the spec's evidence notes.
