# 0027 — Syncs heal themselves; what only the owner can do arrives as one actionable notification

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-15: "Approved previous request" on [P2-16](../specs/P2-16-self-healing-sync-and-actionable-notifications.md)) — builds on [ADR 0018](0018-services-framework-and-editable-schedules.md) (retries, catch-up), [ADR 0022](0022-action-center-and-price-alerts.md) (notices) and [ADR 0025](0025-dhan-trade-history-and-ledger-sync.md)
- **Date:** 2026-10-15

## Context
The Dhan syncs (holdings, quotes, trades, ledger) now run by themselves, but a failure only shows up if the owner opens the right screen, and the notification bell only knows accounts, data and services. The owner asked that most things heal on their own and that anything needing their action arrives as a notification with a way straight to the fix, with better-looking pop-ups. The owner's trade file (`data/dhan/tradebook/ANALYSIS.md`) also showed that the app's history starts at the first-import date while the owner's real history reaches back further.

## Decisions
1. **Transient failures retry quietly** (1, 5, 15 minutes; at most 3 per occurrence, inside the day); permanent ones do not. Nothing is shown while retries remain.
2. **A failure becomes the owner's business at 2 consecutive failed days**, or at once when only the owner can fix it (expired/missing Dhan login, expired API key, role switched off).
3. **Catch-up after reconnect:** a renewed token triggers the missed holdings, trades and ledger syncs by themselves.
4. **History deepens by itself** when trades do not explain a holding in a way earlier history could fix: one earlier year per night, stopping at Dhan's first refused/empty year or 8 years back.
5. **Items are server-derived and self-resolving.** Notices carry id (includes the situation), severity, title, text, at most two actions (route or existing command) and `resolved`; they disappear when the condition clears. Read state stays per browser.
6. **Pop-ups:** a queue of up to three severity-coloured cards with title, message, actions, close and a countdown (not under reduced motion); errors needing action stay until acted on or resolved; a new item pops up once, and after sign-in only the most severe unread one does.

## Consequences
- Nothing new needs a button to stay current; the owner hears only about what they must do, once, with the button.
- Retries and deepening add Dhan calls at night (bounded by the existing spacing and caps).
- Dhan's history depth is undocumented; the deepening stops where Dhan stops and says how far it reached.

## Consequences from the build (2026-10-15)
- **Healing is the holdings refresh runner's second job.** The runner that starts the due holdings syncs once a minute then makes one look with the healer; it starts only the ordinary use cases (the same locks, audit and rebuild as "Sync now"), so no new service exists. **With `Portfolio:AutoRefresh:Enabled = false` the runner never loops: no quiet retries and no catch-up** (the 16:30 service, "Sync now" and "Retry now" still work). The three settings are `Portfolio:Heal:RetryBackoffMinutes`, `PersistDays` and `DeepenMaxYears` ([doc 16](../design/16-configuration.md)).
- **The health of a sync is kept next to its watermark.** `portfolio.broker_sync_state` now has a row per account and kind (`holdings | trades | ledger | quotes`), with the failure counters, the deepening state and the mapper version; `portfolio.broker_sync_windows` notes the windows that dropped rows and `portfolio.attention_items` remembers the items. The write rules are one SQL statement shared by every place that fails a run, so a crashed or interrupted run counts like any other failure.
- **A rate limit is recognised by its status.** A 429 is read as DH-904 even with an empty body, so it is retried and never mistaken for a refusal.
- **A mapper change needs a version bump.** `IBrokerTrades.MapperVersion` and `IBrokerLedger.MapperVersion` are the contract: whoever changes a mapper so that rows an older version had to drop could now be read raises its `Version`, and the next run reads the windows that dropped rows again before its own (identity dedupe makes it safe). An account never stamped before also gets the dates of its earlier runs that dropped rows.
- **Deepening is bounded and honest.** At most one year per India day, only after a scheduled trades run whose rebuild left a holding the trades explain too much of (`negative_opening`) or sold more than was open (`sell_exceeds_open`); it stops at an empty year, a year Dhan refuses for good and `DeepenMaxYears`, and records how far it reached. In the reconstruction model the opening quantity is what remains after the trades, so an earlier year enters the same net: it can supply the buys a sale needs but **rarely changes a verdict**; the item says how far the history was read.
- **`GET /api/portfolio/notices` writes.** Every read does one reconciliation of the items (an upsert of those that hold, one update that resolves the rest, one read) and the bell reads it every minute; the write is idempotent and a failure to derive the items is logged while the alert notices are still returned. Resolved items are kept for 24 hours and never deleted.
- **One toast provider for the app.** `AppShell` owns the single `ToastProvider`; a toast raised from any page, dialog or the bell shows in the same stack, and the attention pop-ups are mounted beside it.
- **Items name no secret and no amount.** Account names, at most three tickers, counts and dates only; the actions are routes inside the app and existing endpoints, called with the session and CSRF token.

## Amendments (2026-10-15)
Made while building [P2-16](../specs/P2-16-self-healing-sync-and-actionable-notifications.md); the spec marks them too.
- **a. Health kinds are added to `broker_sync_state`** (the spec named three new columns on the existing trades and ledger rows): the table now holds `holdings`, `trades`, `ledger` and `quotes` rows, `watermark_date` is nullable (only trades and ledger have one) and the kind column is wider; `Down` deletes the rows that have no place in the old shape.
- **b. The price poll retries by being a poll.** Quotes get no healer retry: the 5-minute poll of `holdings-quotes` is the retry, a transient failure is recorded as a *skipped* run and counted against the primary Data account, and the run **fails quietly until `PersistDays` days** (then the failed-service notice and the item appear); a refused token fails it at once.
- **c. The frontend-derived reconnect notices are replaced by the server's `reconnect_dhan`** (missing, expired, expiring within 2 hours), for every active account whatever its roles. **`key_expiring` and `key_expired` stay in the frontend** (the API-key expiry is derived from the account list in the browser) and still pop up.
- **d. `GET /notices` writes** (the reconciliation above); the spec only said "derived on read".
- **e. `role_off` and the API-key expiry are not recorded server-side.** The reason code `role_off` exists and counts as owner-only, but the healer and the items look only at accounts that have the role on, so in practice nothing raises it; criterion 2's "API key expired" and "role switched off" at once are therefore met only for the Dhan login (`token_expired`, `token_refused`, `not_connected`), with the key expiry in the browser.
- **f. Deepening can rarely change a verdict** (see Consequences): the rule is kept as decided, but the expectation that it resolves mismatches is lower than the spec's text suggests. A mismatch it cannot mend (or that is not deepenable) becomes the item "Trades do not explain a holding" at once, and a deepenable one only after the deepening is over.
- **g. Healing needs the refresh runner enabled** (`Portfolio:AutoRefresh:Enabled`); the spec said "no new service", and indeed none was added, but the switch of the runner is also the switch of the healing.
- **h. The unmatched-securities item counts the trades read from Dhan of the last 90 days** (distinct ISIN or ticker with no instrument-master match) next to the open holdings with none; its link lists holdings only.
- **i. A retry is never scheduled across midnight.** A transient failure is retried at most three times inside its India day; the next scheduled run is the retry after that.
- **j. A recurring item is a new item for the browser: its key is `id` plus `since`.** The server re-raises a resolved condition under the same id with a new `since`; the browser's read set and pop-up memory therefore key an item on `id|since` (`stateKey` in `features/notifications/lib/readState`) **for the six server attention kinds only**; every other notice (price alerts, 'holdings need a decision', the API-key expiry) is keyed on the plain id, because the server may stamp `since` anew on each poll (it sends `Since = null` for 'holdings need a decision', whose `At` still moves on each poll: cosmetic). So a condition that clears and returns is unread again and pops up again, while an unchanged item stays read. State stored for an id is dropped once the item has arrived resolved.
- **k. Follow-ups from the review are listed, not built** (spec P2-16, "Follow-ups from the review"): `GET /notices` still writes (amendment d) and `attention_items` has no retention (the runtime role has no DELETE, so one needs a documented exception or a job); a transient failure is told from a permanent one by the error text; a second tab can make an item flicker between resolved and raised. They are minor and do not block.

## Amendment (2026-10-15, ISS-03)
The run counts that the health of a sync is read from now explain dropped rows ([ADR 0025](0025-dhan-trade-history-and-ledger-sync.md), amendment of the same date).

- **Drop reasons.** A run's counts gain `droppedByReason` (rows per reason code, e.g. `missing_id`, `bad_quantity`, `zero_amount`, `failed_checks`, `store_refused`) and `droppedRows` (the place of the first five: request dates, page, row index, reason; never row content). `BrokerKindStatus` of the history status carries the latest run's two fields. A dropped row is still not a failure: the run succeeds and no attention item is raised for it (a bell item is the open entry ISS-09).
- **Wording change.** The audit text and the log line of a finished run list the reasons - "N dropped (reason×count, ...)", the biggest first - instead of "N unusable".
- **The mapper-version re-read is unchanged.** A window that dropped rows is still noted as `needs_reread` without the reason; carrying the dominant reason there (so only windows a new mapper could mend are read again) needs a migration and is ISS-10.

## Amendment (2026-10-15, P2-18)
The follow-ups left open above (amendments d and k, the ISS-03 amendment) were closed by [P2-18](../specs/P2-18-sync-and-import-hardening.md) and decided in [ADR 0030](0030-read-only-notices-retention-and-structured-failures.md):
- **`GET /api/portfolio/notices` no longer writes.** `AttentionReconciler` reconciles the items at the end of the healer tick, of every holdings, trades or ledger attempt and after a Dhan sign-in; the query only reads (amendment d no longer holds). Resolved items are deleted after `Portfolio:Heal:AttentionRetentionDays` (default 30), the one DELETE grant of the runtime role in the portfolio schema.
- **Failures carry a structured `FailureKind`**; the transient or permanent decision never reads a message text (a rate limit is a 429, or a 4xx with DH-904).
- **A seventh attention kind, `rows_dropped`** (ISS-09), is raised from the latest succeeded trades or ledger run when it dropped rows (information for zero amounts only, a warning otherwise); the ISS-03 amendment's "no attention item is raised" is superseded. Recurring items are keyed on id plus `since` for the seven server kinds.
- **The command allow-list is generated** from the server's one list (`AttentionCommands`), and `SyncGuard` is kind-aware. A run that fails late keeps the progress of its re-read windows (in the run's counts).
