# 0090 — Grading replays each ticket through the simulator on its own session and stores one insert-only grade

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [P7-13](../specs/P7-13-grading.md), approved under the owner's standing run instruction of 2026-10-22; built 2026-10-23). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-10-23.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0037](0037-simulation-conventions-and-the-two-stage-seam.md)/0038/0042 (replay, fills, costs), [0045](0045-the-result-store-sim-runs-and-sim-trades.md) (insert-only), [0083](0083-the-plan-record-insert-only-state-trails-owner-only-approvals-lapse-by-default-and-no-order-by-name.md) (plan record, no order), [0088](0088-ticket-builder-kleene-classification-from-the-close-bars-after-the-close-dropped-selection-builder-off-by-default.md) (tickets). The owner's answers of 2026-10-23 hold: no orders in P7, paper only, no auto-approval, no automatic learning change.

## Context
Doc 10 section 8 wants the selector proved: rejected candidates graded, primary against reserve against rejected, top-1/3/5/all curves, and the owner's decisions compared with what the system proposed. A plan ticket carries levels as text, so grading cannot parse the text and walk the bars by hand without inventing a second simulator.

## Decision
1. **A ticket is replayed by the existing simulation use case, not re-derived from its text.** The stored strategy version (hash), the cell's timeframe, side and exit, and the instrument go through `RunSimulationQuery`. F is the planned session, D the last trading session before F (the session the ticket was built on). A ticket on a daily-or-longer timeframe is read from D, an intraday-timeframe ticket from F; to F (intraday style) or the last closed session (swing style). The grade says `simulated on plan prices`: what the P3 rules would have returned, not an execution.
2. **Which decision is the ticket's, and no look-ahead (corrected twice after review).** The engine cuts trades by the day of the DECISION (ADR 0043 D6), so a window of F alone dropped a daily signal decided at the close of D and filled at the open of F. The window therefore starts on D for a 1D-or-longer ticket, and the pure `TicketGrader` keeps ONLY the decision the ticket could know: at the close of D for a daily ticket (ADR 0088 section 2), on F for an intraday ticket. A daily decision on F is made at the close of F and belongs to the ticket for the next session: grading it for F would count one signal twice. An intraday decision on D used information the ticket did not have. The first qualifying trade by entry instant is the outcome. **Fates are scoped the same way:** the fates of a run cover every day in its window, so a second run from the day after the decision day, to the same end, is subtracted (`RunFates.Minus`; fates count candidates by decision day and do not depend on the window); a dropped trade, flagged day, pending order or open position on another day never makes this ticket final or pending. History before the window is indicator warm-up only; bars after the last closed session are never read. Two replays per daily or swing ticket is the price. Tested end to end with the real simulator on fabricated bars.
3. **Cost follows a stored sheet if configured** (`Planning:Grading:CostSheetId`), else gross, named on every grade. R is `RNet` when charged, otherwise `RFilled` (the convention of the cells).
4. **Not gradable (final) is a stored result with a reason, never a guess:** an exit label that is not exactly one candidate spelling (`FIXED_RR{rr=2}`; a bare policy, an overlay or a spelling that does not read back is refused), a strategy version not stored, a cell key or side that cannot be read, a trade dropped by the sizing or cost rule.
5. **Pending is not written; the next run tries again.** Bars that do not reach F, a position still open or an order still pending at the end of the read, a replay that failed (a charge sheet not in force yet, a missing instrument row, a strategy that does not compile now), a skipped instrument, a flagged day, or a symbol in no basket now. A transient condition must never become a permanent wrong grade.
6. **One insert-only table `trading.grades`**, unique on the ticket, composite foreign key to the ticket's own plan, a check that the basis is the label and that R and the exit exist exactly when graded. `xd_app` has SELECT and INSERT only (granted on the table, never by re-granting the schema). Writing twice is `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`: the first grade stays. Migration `AddGrades` has a `Down`.
7. **Pure report** (`GradeReport`): top-K per plan takes the K best-ranked primary and reserve tickets (the selector ranks only those); non-triggered and not-gradable tickets add 0 R and are counted apart. Groups: primary, reserve, rejected. Decision groups from the newest ticket state: approved, modified, rejected, lapsed, undecided (a modified-then-approved ticket counts as approved). There is **no auto-approved group**. Comparisons give the difference in mean R and a verdict word, with a note when a set has fewer than 30 triggered tickets; never a significance claim. The notes state the not-gradable share and its top reasons, that the replay uses the default fill rules and replay options (not those of the run a cell's statistics came from), and that levels and the owner's modifications are not replayed. The decisions report reads one query (range pushed down, newest plan version per date, at most 5000 tickets, a `TRUNCATED` note when cut).
8. **Surface.** `xd plan grade [--date]`, `GET /api/plans/{id}/grade`, `GET /api/reports/decisions?from=&to=`. Log ids 4720 to 4722. A configured `Planning:Grading:CostSheetId` that is not stored fails the run (`plans.grading_sheet_missing`, logged); unset means gross, named on every grade. Nothing changes a ticket state; no order, account, credential or approval is touched.
9. **A wrong grade has no remedy in the table, on purpose.** There is no `superseded_by` or update path: a grade that must be corrected needs a new migration and an ADR (a superseding row), and was judged not worth building now because the cases that could make a first grade wrong for a transient reason are pending, not final. A correction today is an owner-run SQL repair outside the app role.

## Consequences
- The grade rests on the stored bars and the modelled fills: the first year of graded tickets are re-simulations, not execution (P7-00 honest limits).
- One replay per ticket reads that instrument's history; fine for tens of tickets a night, not measured on the owner's data.
- A pending ticket can wait for ever if its bars never arrive; it shows as pending in the plan's grade.
- **Not built here:** the nightly service entry (the verb runs it), grades on fills (P7-14), partial fills, a screen.
- The grade of the ticket is for the first qualifying trade on F; a strategy that fires twice on F is graded once.
- **Shortcuts:** symbol to instrument through the non-archived baskets, first match wins (an instrument removed from every basket is pending); ticket levels and owner modifications are ignored (a modified-then-approved ticket is graded at the original plan prices); one position at a time inside the replay, so an earlier open position can block an entry on F (reported as such); the cell's source-run options are not reused.

## Alternatives considered
- **Parse the ticket text and walk the bars** — rejected: a second simulator that could drift from the first.
- **Update a grade when more bars arrive** — rejected: grades are final once the trade has left; insert-only.
