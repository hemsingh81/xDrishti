# 0160 - Protection is judged from the broker's book by a pure monitor and enforced by a watchdog whose every action only reduces risk, and which no host composes yet

- **Status:** Accepted (decided in P12-04 under the owner's standing run instruction; spec [P12-04](../specs/P12-04-broker-side-stops-and-protection.md)).
- **Date:** 2026-10-29.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** builds on ADR 0155 to 0159 (`IExitSendPolicy` was the closed default; `ResendPolicy`, `ActionPolicy` and the account gate are used as they were); follows ADR 0038 (a stop fills at the open when gapped). The xd-api allow-list and `Infrastructure/Brokers/BrokerEndpoints*` are not touched; no other project references Execution; `deploy/` is untouched.

## Context
Every position the system opens must have a stop that lives at the broker. The owner's limits stand: no call to Dhan, nothing sends by default, live refused, `exec.lookup_verified` OFF. Dhan's stop-loss and super-order wire is known only from documents.

## Decision
1. **A pure verdict from the broker's book.** `ProtectionMonitor.Judge` returns Flat, Protected, Pending, Breach or Unknown from the net quantity (from our fills), the stop legs in the order book as last read and the time the position became uncovered. Only a PENDING stop leg counts (TRANSIT does not; a target or entry leg of a super order never); a stop smaller than the position breaches for the difference; a stop looser than the intent's is `stop_mismatch`; a missing or older-than-30-s book is Unknown, never Protected; a negative net is `oversold`.
2. **A watchdog, one snapshot per tick.** Pending inside the deadline; at the deadline a CRITICAL `protection_breach`, a halt request for entries (an unprotected position halts new entries), one repair (a stop for the uncovered quantity, attempt 2), then, if it fails or does not appear within another deadline and `exec.flatten_on_unprotected` is on (default), a flatten walk. Blind or unreadable is `protection_unknown` with a halt request and never a flatten. `oversold` requests a freeze and sends nothing. A source that cannot answer is unknown, and settings that cannot be vouched for are unknown (no default is substituted).
3. **Every protective send can only reduce risk.** `ProtectionExitPolicy` (the reduce-only `IExitSendPolicy`) allows only a SELL stop, exit or flatten for a position that exists with a positive net: a stop covers only what no live stop covers, is not below the intent's stop, and has its limit at or below its trigger; an exit or flatten sells at most the net less the exits already resting; a target is never a resting leg; under a freeze halt a broker position read at most 15 s old whose net is at least ours is required and bounds the quantity. `StopManager` only tightens (`ActionPolicy.StopLoosen` stays refused, and the rule is checked again: no loosening, two ticks from the last price, at most 20 modifications) and changes only a stop of ours; the stop-triggered-not-filled rule changes only the limit, keeps the trigger, once per leg.
4. **Authority by switch, not by mode.** `ProtectionGate` lets a leg that only reduces risk pass when the global switch is on, the environment is sandbox (live is refused) and an answer exists, whatever the mode, so a demoted account can still protect an open position; an entry still needs an executing mode.
5. **Resend gate unchanged.** A stop or flatten found in TRANSIT, or a send that returned Unknown, makes the next one a resend; `ResendPolicy` allows it only with `exec.lookup_verified`, which stays OFF by default. Without it the watchdog raises `send_unknown` and sends nothing.
6. **Square-off.** Entries are cancelled at the entry window end and again at the square-off time and an entry found working after the square-off is CRITICAL `exit_failed` (no exit on top of it); then the target, then the exit (re-priced while resting), the stop only after the position is flat, and an exit larger than what the stop left is cancelled at once. A target leg that cannot be cancelled here (a super-order leg; its path is not built) stops the exit with `exit_failed`. If the stop and the exit both fill the net is negative: `oversold`, a freeze request, nothing sent, the intent never marked reconciled.
7. **Record.** Migration `AddExecutionProtection`: `execution.alarms` and `execution.alarm_clears`, insert-only for every role including the owner (trigger), the twelve codes and three severities as constraints, `xd_exec` INSERT and SELECT, `xd_app` SELECT only. An alarm is open while no clear row exists; raising an open alarm again adds nothing. `GET /api/execution/alarms?open=&accountId=&take=` (read only, newest first, at most 200) and `gen-api`. A new alarm also appends an `alarm` event to the intent (the fact P12-07 reads).
8. **Wire, UNVERIFIED.** The wire is added behind the existing guard table with fake-handler tests only: a stop is `STOP_LOSS` with `triggerPrice` on the order path (sell only), a stop change is a PUT with the new trigger and limit (a separate port `IBrokerStops`, so `IBrokerOrders` is unchanged), a super entry is `POST /v2/super/orders` (the one new row of the table). Nothing was verified against real Dhan.
9. **Nothing is composed.** No host composes the watchdog, the policy, the manager, the square-off service, `IBrokerStops` or the halt stub. `IExitSendPolicy` stays the closed default; the conditions for opening it (a real snapshot, the halt store, the mode ladder) are not met. The only registration added is the insert-only alarm store in the record composition (it is a database writer, not a sender).
10. **Settings.** Six registry settings with compiled ranges (`exec.stop_limit_buffer_pct` 0.5, `exec.flatten_step_collar_pct` 0.5, `exec.flatten_walk_steps` 3, `exec.breakeven_at_r` 0, `exec.stop_unfilled_s` 5, `exec.blind_alarm_s` 30). The flatten walk is bounded by the exit collar because the sender refuses a price outside it.
11. **Needs real Dhan data.** The stop-loss order type and trigger field name; whether a stop-loss limit order accepts a modify of trigger and limit together; the super-order body, the leg names and ids in the order book, a part-filled entry, auto square-off time and the cancel path of a super leg; the order-book `triggerPrice` and `legName`; TRANSIT duration of a stop; what a stop does on a gap (stays open?). These are settled by canaries C1 and C2 (P12-00), not by this ADR.
12. **Review fixes.** (a) A stop and a flatten must never rest together: before a flatten our resting stops are cancelled and confirmed gone from the book (else `exit_failed`, no flatten), and a stop left after the position is flat is cancelled. (b) The square-off exit is not sent again after an Unknown ack, with a book read older than the send, or within 3 s, unless `ResendPolicy` allows (default OFF); `send_unknown` is raised. (c) A repair stop with an Unknown outcome is pending lookup: no flatten, no resend. (d) The halt request is made before the event append, survives a failing event store, and is repeated while the alarm stands and no halt is in force. (e) Exit and flatten are sell-only in the wire. Shortcuts, each with its upgrade: the de-dup of alarms is check-then-insert (one watchdog assumed; a partial unique index when a second writer exists); "in transit" is read from the book only (LegLookup once the lookup path is verified). Known shortfall: 33 monitor cases against the 40 the spec asks for.

## Consequences
The protection invariant is a tested function and a tested loop with negative controls for the loosening and the double-sell cases, and the only actions it can take reduce exposure. Because nothing composes it and the exit policy default stays closed, nothing new can send; wiring the snapshot, the halts and the modes (P12-05, P12-06, P12-07) is what turns it on, slice by slice.
