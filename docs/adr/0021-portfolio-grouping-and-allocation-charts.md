# 0021 — Portfolio grouping by account, bucket, sector or basket, computed on the server, with allocation charts

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-13: "approved as written", after the review below) — builds on [ADR 0012](0012-holdings-acquisition-dates-and-returns-model.md) (returns, XIRR), [ADR 0014](0014-portfolio-buckets-appear-as-system-baskets.md) (buckets), [ADR 0019](0019-holdings-quotes-day-change-and-the-first-interval-service.md) (day change) and [ADR 0020](0020-portfolio-value-history-benchmark-and-charts.md) (ECharts wrapper)
- **Date:** 2026-10-13

## Context
Design doc 12 §10 gives the Portfolio screen a **Group by** control (bucket · basket · account · strategy · sector · none) with group rows that expand to their holdings, and charts for the allocation and the sector exposure; doc 12 §5 says buckets are additive (a holding is in exactly one) while baskets are a lens (an instrument can be in many). Today the screen is a flat holdings grid with an account/bucket filter. The group figures of §3 — invested, value, P&L, XIRR from the **union of member cash flows**, day change, weight — cannot be assembled from the rows on the client: XIRR of a group needs the lots, and a page of holdings is not the whole group.

## Decisions
1. **Groupings in this slice:** `account`, `bucket`, `sector`, `basket`, and `none` (today's flat grid, still the default). `strategy` is not offered until plans and positions exist (P7); the control lists only what exists, nothing disabled and unexplained. The sector is the instrument master's sector (ADR 0010) — a holding without one, or not mapped to the master, is in **"Unclassified"**.
2. **Groups are computed on the server** by one read model that takes the same scope filters as the holdings list (`accountId`, `bucketId`, `status`, `q`) plus `by`, partitions the holdings, and reuses the existing pure calculators per partition: `ReturnsCalculator` (invested, value, P&L, group XIRR from the union of the members' flows), `DayChangeCalculator`, and the weight = group value ÷ the scope's total value. Unpriced holdings are left out of value/P&L/XIRR and counted, as in the summary; the sum of the **additive** groups' invested/value/P&L equals the summary's. Groups sort by value (default), invested, P&L, P&L %, XIRR, day change or name; `GET /api/holdings/groups` returns at most 200 groups.
3. **Baskets are a lens, not a partition.** A holding appears in **every** basket that has its instrument as a member today (current members, ADR 0014's portfolio system baskets excluded because they duplicate the buckets) and in **"No basket"** when none; the response says `additive: false` and the screen shows "Baskets overlap — totals do not add up to the portfolio" on that view, and the weights are shares of the scope total (they can add up to more than 100 %). Accounts, buckets and sectors are `additive: true`.
4. **Expanding a group** shows its holdings with the existing columns: the holdings list takes the group as a filter (`groupBy` + `groupKey`, the key being the account id, bucket id, sector name or basket id, or `~none` for the unclassified/no-basket group) so paging, sorting and the drawer work unchanged; the screen fetches the children when the group opens and keeps them in the query cache. Lots stay in the holding drawer.
5. **Charts** use the ADR 0020 wrapper pattern with a **second lazy chunk** for categorical charts (pie/donut and bar, not part of the time-series chunk, so the History chart does not grow): an **allocation donut** of the current grouping (top 8 groups + "Other", % of value) and, for the sector/account views, a **sector exposure stacked bar by account** (value by sector, stacked by account). A table alternative and an accessible summary accompany each chart as for the history chart. Budget for the added chunk ≤ 60 KB gzip beyond the shared ECharts core (core shared with the time-series chunk by the bundler); the initial bundle stays ≤ 200 KB gzip. A treemap is not built (doc 13 mentions it for later).
6. **State in the URL:** `groupBy` joins the existing filters (`account`, `bucket`, `status`, `q`, `sort`); the expanded group keys are local UI state.

## Review before approval (2026-10-13)
Gaps found in the first draft and settled here (the decisions above stand):
- **A second dimension for the stacked bar.** The groups read model also takes `stackBy` (`account` when grouping by sector, `sector` when grouping by account) and returns, per group, `parts[{key, name, value}]`; no other combination is offered. The sector exposure chart is shown for the Sector and Account groupings only.
- **Reserved key.** The group key of "Unclassified" (sector) and "No basket" is `~none`, which cannot clash with an id; a master sector spelled `~none` (any case) is deliberately treated as Unclassified, so no real sector name can use it (sector names are the master's, up to 100 characters, URL-encoded in `groupKey`).
- **Baskets.** Only live rows of `market.baskets` count (archived ones are left out). The portfolio system baskets of ADR 0014 are not stored there (a view of the buckets), so they are excluded by construction. Membership is "today's members".
- **Expanded groups do not scroll inside the page.** A group's holdings load 50 at a time with a *Show N more* row (the project's infinite-scroll rule applies to the main grid; nested scrollers are avoided).
- **Weights and filters.** The search (`q`) and `status` filters apply before grouping, so groups and weights describe the filtered holdings; weight = group value ÷ the scope's total value of priced holdings.
- **Chunks.** A second chart chunk makes the bundler move the shared ECharts core into its own shared chunk; the time-series chunk therefore shrinks, and the single-importer rule (ESLint + boundary test) is extended to the new canvas file.
- **Palette.** A categorical palette (8 + "Other") joins the theme tokens and the existing contrast test covers it in light and dark.

## Consequences
- The same pure calculators serve the summary, the history and the groups, so the numbers agree; the group endpoint is the single place that knows how to partition.
- Basket grouping is honest about overlap instead of faking additive totals.
- Group XIRR reads the lots of every group once per request; for a few hundred holdings this is trivial, and the 200-group cap bounds the response.
- Strategy grouping, the sector concentration alerts and the Action center (P2-09) build on the same partitions later.
