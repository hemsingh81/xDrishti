# 0046 — The verification pack and the owner's 20-trade check

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-19: the agent work of spec [P3-09](../specs/P3-09-verification-pack-and-the-20-trade-check.md), the pack builder and the check mode in two parts, with the recommended answers). **The protocol values of the section *The protocol* (Q6) and the hold-out (Q7) are recommendations until the owner confirms them in chat before the first draw;** the first pack is not drawn without the owner's seed. Extends [ADR 0041](0041-the-simulate-verb-the-export-library-and-the-pipeline-in-the-host.md) (Amendment 4: the shared pipeline and a further verb); supersedes no sentence of an earlier ADR.
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** the cards are Amendment 1 of 0041 and `SeededRank` its primitive; the rule tables the owner checks by hand are ADR 0038, 0040 and 0043; the cost lines are ADR 0042 and 0044; the oracle (P3-08) is [ADR 0047](0047-the-oracle-and-the-rule-table-gaps.md) and the result store (P3-10) [ADR 0045](0045-the-result-store-sim-runs-and-sim-trades.md), which this decision does not use.

## Context
The exit criterion of P3 is a human one: the owner takes about 20 trades drawn at random, balanced over strategy, instrument and year, plus one trade of each edge category that occurs, and checks each against a chart by hand. The oracle (0047) shows that two implementations of the written rules agree; only a chart shows that the rules describe the market. For the check to mean something three things must be fixed before it starts and not by taste: the draw (a seed written down first, no random source in the system), the sheet (what the owner sees and in what order, so that the engine's number is not read first) and the comparison (tolerances and what counts as a disagreement). This ADR records those decisions and the shape of the code that serves them.

## Decision

### The draw (V1)
1. **The seed is the owner's.** `--seed` is required, has no default, and is 1 to 40 characters of `A-Za-z0-9._-`. A default would be a hidden random source; a seed chosen after seeing the trades is not a pre-registration. The seed is in `pack.json`, the README, the index and the manifest. The record of the check keeps every seed ever used; after any fix the next seed is a new one (`p3-check-2`).
2. **The sampler is `VerificationSampler.Draw`** (pure, `XDrishti.SimExport.Verification`; version string `verify-sampler-1`, changed whenever a change alters a draw). A cell is strategy × instrument × year of the trade date, over `run.Trades` only. Each pick takes, among the cells that still hold an unused trade: those whose strategy has the fewest picks, then those whose instrument inside the strategy has the fewest, then those whose year, **counted over the whole strategy**, has the fewest, then those whose cell has the fewest; ties go to the lowest `SeededRank.Of(seed, "cell|strategy|symbol|year")`; the trade of the cell is the next of `SeededRank.Order(seed + "|trade", keys)`. The year is counted over the strategy and not inside the instrument because with one strategy and about 214 instruments the first 20 picks each take a new instrument, and a year balanced only inside an instrument would be whichever cell hashes lowest. The year is therefore balanced only as far as the instrument precedence allows; the hand-calculated pick-by-pick example and the year-spread test pin both facts.
3. **No count of a group is printed, ever** (the argument of P3-04c): the cell and stratum sizes are not in any file.

### The edge categories and the cover rule (V2, V3)
4. **Thirteen categories**, a table in `EdgeCategories`, each a pure function of a trade, its instrument's record and the recorded definition: `sell`, `stop_gap`, `stop_in_entry_minute`, `entry_at_limit`, `entry_after_hole`, `first_evaluation`, `overnight_entry`, `exit_target`, `exit_square_off`, `exit_max_hold`, `incomplete_trigger_bar`, `adjusted_instrument`, `tick_fallback`. The roadmap's nine categories are covered: *gap exit* and *hole through a stop* are one test on a record (the exit price beyond the stop), told apart only by the bars; *special session* and *end_of_data* are not trades or have no card and are spot checks; seven are added from the code. The table is iterated from the enums of `ExitReason` and `EntryFill`: a new value fails a test until it has a category or a recorded reason for having none. `first_evaluation` reads `time_rules.first_entry` of the interim definition text (the same coupling as the rule view of the cards); a text that does not parse gives false, not an exception.
5. **The cover rule.** After the random part, the categories are visited in table order; a category that occurs and is not covered gets the trade of it with the lowest `SeededRank.Of(seed, "edge|code|key")`, which also covers every other category it belongs to. A pack is at most 20 + 13 trades and usually fewer; the roadmap's 29 was its nine categories. A category that does not occur is `occurs: false` in `pack.json`, a statement of presence and never a count.
6. **The order of the sheet** is the order of `SeededRank.Of(seed, "order|key")`, so that the place of a trade does not say which was drawn at random and which was picked for its category.
7. **Spot checks** are log lines with no card: one `excluded_session` per reason that occurs in the session notes (the notes are cut at 100 per instrument), one `flagged_day`, one `open_at_end` trade, each chosen by `SeededRank`. Each asks one question of an external chart and says nothing about a trade's outcome.

### The pack (V5, V7)
8. **The folder** is `<Export:Directory>/verify/<pack-id>/` (git-ignored, `0700`/`0600`), written atomically by `ExportFolderWriter`, which gained one optional parameter, the folder name below the root (default `sim`; 1 to 20 lower-case letters). The format string is `xd-verify-pack-1`. The pack key is the SHA-256 of the seed, the sampler version, the count, the basket id (the run key does not cover it) and the sorted `strategy=run key` lines; it is the manifest's `run_key`. The pack id is the seed in lower case with every character other than a letter or digit written as a hyphen, a hyphen and the first 12 hex of the pack key (the writer's run-id rule is `a-z 0-9 -`; the hex part keeps two seeds that differ only in case apart). The manifest's extra lines carry the seed, the sampler, the count, each run, each row and each spot line, and no source.
9. **The files.** `pack.json` (identity and sources: the seed, the sampler, the runs with their parameters, keys, engine version, data range, labels, the bar-stream hash of every instrument that has a row, each row's sequence number, key, symbol, side, date and run key, the category table with `occurs`), `README.txt` (the banner of every run first, then the protocol in short), `index.html` and `cards/<nnn>-<ticker>-<side>-<date>.html` (the cards of P3-04c unchanged; the **name omits the exit reason**, and the index lists sequence, symbol, side and date only), `engine-values.csv` (the engine's side, with the source and categories of each row, **a file of its own on purpose**), `check-log.csv` (the owner's log: no source, category or engine column) and `manifest.json`. The cards show the engine's marks, so blindness is partly a convention; the first five (external) rows are done blind. The README and the index say so in a sentence. The console and `pack.json` print the total of rows only (no split into trades and edge picks, no `row_count`), and `pack.json` has `session_notes_cut`, true when an instrument's session notes hit the cap of 100, so a kind of spot check may not have been seen.
10. **The column map.** The owner can read exact levels off a chart and cannot read filled prices, so the tool compares `seen_entry_price`, `seen_stop`, `seen_target` and `seen_exit_price` with the exact prices to one tick, the times to the minute, `seen_exit_reason` exactly and `seen_r` (the owner's own arithmetic from the exact levels) to `r_exact` within 0.01. The filled prices, `r_filled` and the cost lines are a calculator check from the card's printed operands, compared by the tool only when the owner writes the optional `calc_*` columns. A pack of a gross run says `costs: none`; net R by hand waits for the owner's charge sheet and the bill check (ADR 0044).
11. **Determinism and overwrite.** Two roots, one seed and the same data give identical bytes. The manifest hashes `check-log.csv` as written, and the owner edits it afterwards, so a second run of the verb never reports `unchanged` over an edited log: it refuses and **names the file that differs from the manifest**. `--force` over a log the owner has started prints a loud warning, keeps the whole folder as `<pack-id>.previous` and writes a blank log, and is refused if `.previous` already exists (an earlier copy is never overwritten).

### The verb and the check mode (V4, V8)
12. **`verify-pack` is a verb of its own**, not `--trade-keys`/`--seed` on `simulate`: a pack spans several strategies, one run each, and a draw across strategies needs the runs side by side. It runs each named built-in strategy with its registered defaults and modelled fills (no `--param`, no `--fills`: a pack checks the rule set as it ships), pools the trades, draws, calls `BuildTradeCardsQuery` once per run with exactly the chosen keys and writes the pack. A stale second read is `simulation.cards_stale`, exit 1, nothing written. It never reads the result store: each `verify-pack` re-runs every strategy over the full window. The pooling and writing are in the verb host, not in a handler. Exit codes as `simulate`.
13. **`verify-pack --check <check-log.csv>`** (Part B) reads the filled log and `engine-values.csv` of the same folder, joined by `trade_key`, and prints per row and per field `ok`, `ok within tolerance`, `beyond tolerance`, `mismatch`, `missing` or `unsigned`, and then the pack lines. It writes nothing, **never prints that P3 passes**, prints no R value and no statistic, refuses a symbolic link, caps the sizes it reads and strips Control and Format characters from every owner text it echoes. The manifest is verified for every file except `check-log.csv`; a card, the index, `pack.json`, the README or `engine-values.csv` that differs from it is exit 2 with the file named. The conventions of the reading, which the protocol states: a finding is written `<class>: <text>` with class `data`, `model` or `implementation` (anything else is counted as unclassified); a finding without one of those classes keeps the pack incomplete; a model or implementation finding keeps it incomplete until its text says `closed <commit-or-ADR>` (the word `closed` as a whole word, not negated by `not`, `no` or `never`, then 7 to 40 hex characters or an ADR reference), because the exit rule needs zero open ones; more than 3 rows `not_decidable` (the limit is `CheckLogReader.MaxNotDecidable`, a recommendation for the owner to confirm under Q6) keeps it incomplete, and the count is printed; the external minute of a row must satisfy low <= open, close <= high; the check prints the SHA-256 of the log it read, and the first-pass log is to be copied or committed before it is edited again, because it is the evidence; the tolerance of a price is the instrument's tick printed in `engine-values.csv`, and the NSE default 0.05 when the engine has none; a row signed `agree` with a field beyond tolerance is `inconsistent` and counts as unexplained unless it also has a finding; `not_decidable` needs a note and a spot check skipped needs a reason; a trade row cannot be skipped. Only trade rows count toward the five against an external chart.

### The protocol (Q6) and the exit rule (recommended; the owner's to confirm)
14. **The protocol** is written in [`docs/research/p3-hand-check.md`](../research/p3-hand-check.md): the seed and the window first; a bar labelled by its start in India time; the 1-minute chart resampled to the strategy's timeframe with the first bucket at 09:15; per trade, levels read off the chart and written before the engine's values are opened; tolerances one tick, 0.01 R, one minute, the exit reason exact; at least 5 rows against an external chart, one a sell; the defect classes data, model and implementation, triaged in that order; after any model or implementation fix the pack is void and a new seed and a new pack are drawn.
15. **The exit rule.** The pack passes when every row (and every spot check not skipped with a reason) is signed `agree` or `disagree` with a closed or accepted finding; zero unexplained disagreements; zero open model or implementation defects (every one fixed and the check redone on a new pack with a new seed that has itself none); a data defect is a known class that does not change the trade's mechanics (ISS-20, ISS-21) or is accepted by the owner; at least 5 external rows, 20 random trades and one trade of each category that occurs; the record is committed; and the owner says in chat that P3 passes. **It is honest about its power:** 20 random trades with no disagreement give, informally, about 14 to 15 % as an upper bound on the share of *independent units* that would fail (the rule of three); that is not a bound on the share of trades, because the sample is equal-weight over cells and the trades of one instrument are not independent. The check says the mechanics checked match a chart, never that the engine is free of defects or that a strategy has an edge.
16. **Closing P3 on ORB alone is a change of the exit criterion and not a reading of it.** The roadmap lets only `BASE_BREAKOUT` slide to P4. If the owner takes that decision a record and an amendment of this ADR say that P3 passed on ORB only, that "balanced over strategies" was moot with one strategy and that nothing hand-checked `VWAP_PULLBACK` or the swing walk. Until then Round A is ORB and Round B the other two.
17. **The hold-out is decided before the draw.** If the last months are to be kept as a hold-out, the verb is given `--to` before them; looking at the trades of a month is looking at the month. If nothing is decided the record says that all the stored months were looked at.
18. **The committed record holds no price, no R value and no count of a group** (roadmap, *What a P3 number means*): the seed, the pack id, the run keys, the engine version, the data range, the bar-stream hashes, the trade keys with source and categories, the findings with class and closing reference, the date and the owner's statement.

## Consequences
- **No API, no table, no migration.** The pack is files; nothing reads them back but `--check`.
- **The owner's time is not in the size.** About 7.5 to 12 hours a pack (an estimate, to be corrected after the first three trades), and the same again after any fix. The agent work is about 3.45 days in two parts (the pack builder 2.85, the check mode 0.6), over the 2 of the roadmap row.
- **The first run is a full run of every named strategy** (not stored). The cost is the cost of the runs plus one read per instrument that has a row; the measured figures of the build are in the spec's wrap-up.
- **Coupling.** The first-evaluation category reads the interim definition text; the cards do not chart a swing trade's exit day (a Round B need).
