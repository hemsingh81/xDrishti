# 0163 - An account moves up the mode ladder one step, on stored evidence, by an owner act with a cooling-off, and down at any time; no mode built here can send

- **Status:** Accepted (decided in P12-07 under the owner's standing run instruction; spec [P12-07](../specs/P12-07-account-execution-modes-paper-manual-assisted.md)).
- **Date:** 2026-10-30.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** builds on ADR 0155 to 0162 (`IModeSource`, `AccountGate` and the closed defaults were the ports); follows ADR 0091 (an option behind an owner setting, database-checked), ADR 0148 and 0151 (the P11 ramp and the live phase check are read, not changed) and the owner-act pattern of ADR 0161 and 0162 (Live:Writes:Enabled before a fresh password, actor from the session, `xd_app` writes the owner row, a trigger judges). The xd-api allow-list and `Infrastructure/Brokers/BrokerEndpoints*` are not touched; no other project references Execution; `deploy/` is untouched.

## Context
The mode of an account decides whether the system may ever send an order for it. The ladder is paper, manual_live, assisted, semi_auto, auto_live. Only the first three exist in this build: semi_auto and auto_live need the one-tap confirm and the hard limits (P12-08, P12-09). The limits stand: no mode here may let anything send, live is refused everywhere, an `unavailable` input never passes, and every new switch defaults to closed.

## Decision
1. **Three rungs, the others are not values.** `LadderMode` is `paper`, `manual_live`, `assisted`. `semi_auto` and `auto_live` are not parsed anywhere (the API answers 400 `execution.values_invalid`, the database refuses them by a check constraint, the mode source reads an unknown stored name as `paper`). `TradingMode` is not extended: the configured mode stays `paper`/`manual_live`, the rest lives in the trail (spec D5).
2. **The effective mode is a trail, capped by the configuration.** Newest `execution.mode_trail` row in force, else the configured mode; the lower rank of the newest trail row and the configured mode wins, so a `manual_live` or `assisted` row on an account later configured `paper` reads `paper` (database function `execution.effective_mode`, mirrored in `ExecutionModes.Effective`). Fail to the lower side.
3. **Up is an owner act, two steps, on stored evidence.** `POST .../promotion-requests` (gate `Live:Writes:Enabled` first, then a fresh password, actor from the session, a reason) stores the evidence and its SHA-256 and a window: confirmable from the close of the second trading session after the request until the close of the fifth. `POST .../confirm` (gate, fresh password again) re-evaluates the gate and writes one `promotion` row that takes effect at 09:00 India time of the next trading day. A newer request supersedes the older. There is no automatic form (spec D1): the approvals-have-an-auto-option rule is about approvals that can be automated safely, and raising the system's own authority cannot.
4. **The gate is code, policy version 1.** `manual_live` to `assisted` needs: the global switch ON, the Trading role on, a connected Dhan connection, the live phase check (P11-06) `MET`, a sandbox suite result at most 30 days old (P12-02) and a static-IP attestation at most 30 days old (P12-11). Every check is listed with pass, fail or unavailable. **The last two have no stored record yet, so they are `unavailable` and the gate is closed; no promotion can happen until P12-11 and the suite record exist.** Any other pair is one failed check that says why.
5. **Down is always allowed and never needs a password.** The owner (session and CSRF, no gate, no password) or the system (`system:mode-guard`, the execution role) writes a row whose target is lower than the effective mode; a demotion is in force at once (the trigger stamps the time). The pure `DemotionPolicy` sends an account above paper to paper when its Trading role is off or it is archived, and an unreadable fact counts as the bad case. The guard writes at most one row and cannot raise.
6. **The database is the second wall.** Two insert-only tables (a trigger refuses UPDATE, DELETE, TRUNCATE even for the owner). `check_mode_trail` takes a per-account advisory lock (through a security-definer helper, because `xd_exec` may not call the advisory functions) and refuses: a from mode that is not the effective mode, a promotion that is not one step up or not to `assisted`, a promotion while the newest settings row has the switch OFF, a promotion without a matching, newest, unused request inside its window, a demotion that is not lower, a promotion written by `xd_exec`, a system demotion written by anyone but `xd_exec`, an owner demotion written by `xd_exec`. Clocks are `clock_timestamp()` and the effective-mode function is volatile: a waiter's transaction start can precede the row the winner just committed, and the first version of the concurrency test caught exactly that (two winners).
7. **Who writes what.** `xd_app` SELECT and INSERT on both tables (owner acts: it alone verifies the password); `xd_exec` SELECT on requests, SELECT and INSERT on the trail (system demotions). Nobody UPDATE or DELETE. `ExecGrantsTests` lists the exceptions. Like the rest of the execution schema the tables hold the account id without a foreign key to `accounts`.
8. **Composed nowhere.** `ModeTrailSource` (implements `IModeSource`), `ModeGuard` and `PgModeTrail` exist and are tested, but `IModeSource` stays `ClosedModeSource` in every host: replacing it is what would first give an account an answer, and that waits for the later modes and recovery. No mode of this build opens `AccountGate` (it accepts only semi_auto and auto_live), which a test pins for every stored name. `IBrokerOrders` stays registered nowhere; `IExitSendPolicy` and `IHaltSource` stay closed.
9. **Read model.** `GET /api/execution/modes` shows each account with a Trading role: configured and effective mode, a pending change, the open request, the next step with every check, the latest 20 trail rows. No token, secret, client id or balance. Logs (ids 6700 to 6704) carry modes and a short evidence hash, never an actor, e-mail, reason or password.

Limits of the trigger. The database cannot read the trading calendar, so it holds a floor, not the exact window: a request's `eligible_from` must be at least 47 hours after `requested_at` (the close of the 2nd trading session is never nearer) and a promotion may not take effect before the moment it is written; the exact close-of-session window is computed in the handler. `check_promotion_request` takes no advisory lock (a request and a confirm racing in the same millisecond is negligible for a single owner; the confirm trigger does lock). `GET /modes` reads per account (a shortcut for a handful of accounts, upgrade to batched queries past about 10). A PostgresException the store does not map deliberately propagates and answers 500: an unknown database failure is never turned into a refusal or a pass.

## Not built, owed (P12-07b and later)
The assisted order sheet and its match (criteria 14 to 17; `TriggerWatcher`, `AssistedSheet`, `SheetMatch`, `prepared_sheets`, `sheet_matches`, the advisory-run link); `ModeRouting` and `ActionClass` (their table names semi_auto and auto_live rows that cannot exist yet); the `ExecutionSettings` catalogue with risk direction, the settings snapshot trigger and `GET/PUT /settings` (so nothing can switch `execution_enabled` ON from the API yet: with no settings row it is OFF); the generalised `ExecSettingRegistry` move to Domain; the allocation check relaxation for assisted accounts (P11-04A's check stays as it is: an `assisted` account is not yet allocated differently from `manual_live`); the demotion triggers of the automated modes and the client-id-change trigger; the guard loop in xd-exec; the intents FK `mode_trail_id`; the 10-assisted-session and readiness gates (they belong to the assisted-to-semi_auto step, which is P12-08); the screen (P12-10). Shortcut: a promotion confirm and its trail row are two steps with the gate evaluated in the handler and the structural rules in the trigger; the trigger does not re-check the evidence itself (it cannot: the evidence lives in other modules). A compromised `xd_app` could write an `owner:` row; the limit is the same as ADR 0091.

## Consequences
The ladder exists as tested rows and a tested gate, and today no account can climb it because two pieces of evidence cannot exist yet, which is the intended state. Nothing new can send.
