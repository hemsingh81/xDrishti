# 0213 — The paper edge series gets a sequential failure test that flags a probable failure early with the odds stated, and never passes anything

- **Status:** Proposed (built in CF-09 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no** (a pure calculation over stored grades; it writes nothing, changes no setting, ticket or state, places no order, and the boundary only flags).
- **Date:** 2026-11-05.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** reads the edge series of [0211](0211-the-shadow-record-writes-every-nightly-candidate-before-the-open-forward-only-and-insert-only-chained-by-digest-as-a-count-separate-from-the-p10-paper-count-and-the-switch-is-the-owners-and-off-by-default.md) and [0212](0212-the-shadow-record-is-graded-after-the-close-through-the-one-replay-path-summarised-as-raw-and-effective-n-labelled-gross-unless-the-sheet-is-confirmed-and-switched-by-the-owner-with-a-fresh-password.md) (CF-08); uses the design effect and placeholder correlation of CF-08's edge count (CF-01's estimator is not wired to it yet); sits beside the P10 phase check ([0123](0123-the-paper-phase-check-judges-the-p10-exit-from-stored-records-with-its-caveats-in-the-label-and-reads-only.md); P10-06) and changes none of it. It follows the confidence programme rule of CF-00: a new test is report-only, `unavailable` is never a pass, the settings that produced a figure are stored with it.

## Context
The P10 exit is a fixed-sample rule: 60 counted trades, 84 elapsed days, 80 % session coverage, mean net R above 0 and not below the 5th percentile of the backtest band. At 60 trades with a spread of about 1.5 R the standard error is 0.19 R, so an edge of +0.2 R has its interval's lower end above zero only about 27 % of the time (P10-00 D7). Nothing was sequential and nothing spoke about power, so a strategy that is plainly failing waits for the calendar. A sequential test can tell the owner early that the evidence favours a mean near 0 over +0.2 R; it must never tell the owner early that a strategy is good, because stopping early on a good run inflates the error that the fixed gate controls.

## Decision
1. **A Gaussian-mean SPRT, used on the failure side only (`SequentialFailureTest`, pure, Domain.Confidence).** H0 mean net R per independent trade 0 against H1 `mu1` (0.2), `sigma` 1.5; the term of a trade is `llr(x) = (mu1 / sigma^2) * (x - mu1 / 2)` (0.088889 x (x - 0.1)). Boundaries come from the settings and are recomputed, never stored: caution `ln(beta_caution / (1 - alpha))` (-1.558145, evidence ratio 4.75), failure `ln(beta_fail / (1 - alpha))` (-2.944439, ratio 19), and an on-track level `ln((1 - beta_caution) / alpha)` (2.772589) at which the sum is reflected. The unit tests hold the spec's hand-worked numbers (steps, boundaries, 15/16/30/31 straight losses, the 7-wins series cautioning at trade 36 and failing at 50, the order-independence of 8 wins and 12 losses over 200 seeded shuffles, the 45 and 59 losses after the cap, 235 without reflection, the design effect, the sigma floor).
2. **Two lower levels.** At `beta = 0.2` a good +0.2 R strategy is wrongly stopped up to 21 % of the time (Ville, Gaussian), which is acceptable as a warning, not as a stop; the stop uses `beta = 0.05` (at most 5.3 %), then about 0.33 % for each later return to the cap, because the sum is reflected. Every response says so (the cumulative bound is in the statements).
3. **Reflection, state and what the flag is.** `S_n = min(U, S_{n-1} + llr_n / DEFF_n)`. A state is `monitoring`, `on_track`, `caution` or `failure`. **`failure` is latched for the epoch** (it is read from the series on every request, so it needs no stored state; the owner clears it by an epoch restart, P10-05, or by changing a setting, and a change of the cost basis of the series (a confirmation, a new or unconfirmed charge sheet, a grade without charges: each can flip it between gross and net R) can also change what is read). `caution` (sum at or below the caution boundary now) and `on_track` (sum at the cap now) describe the sum now and can revert; the first trade (and day) at which each level was crossed is reported. `on_track` is a label: its words claim nothing else, no member of any result type contains "pass", "proven", "ready" or "approv" (architecture test), and the report line says "report only, never a pass; the fixed P10 gates decide".
4. **The indifference point is `mu1 / 2` (0.1 R).** A strategy whose true mean is below 0.1 R drifts down and will eventually cross, and one at exactly 0.1 R can too, slowly. That is intended; the owner's lever is `mu1`. The wording never says "no edge": it says the evidence favours a mean near 0 over +0.2 R at the stated odds.
5. **Same-day candidates are discounted.** Each term is divided by the design effect `1 + (m - 1) * rho` of its day (`EdgeCount.DesignEffect`, `m` = the day's candidates that count for edge, graded or not, as the edge count counts them; rho the placeholder 0.3, said so on every answer, until CF-01 is wired to the edge count). Five same-day losses count as 2.27.
6. **Unknown variance, conservatively.** A trade's term uses `max(sigma, sample sd of the earlier trades)` once at least `SigmaFromN` (20) earlier trades exist; a larger sigma only shrinks a step, so it delays a flag, never hastens it, and each term is computed once with the sigma known at its step. **Reading of the spec:** "the first k trades with k >= 20" is read as the sd of the trades before the one being scored. Including the scored trade would make the 20-trade series of the spec's order-independence criterion (8 wins, 12 losses; sample sd 1.508) use a sigma above 1.5 on its last step in every order, so its final sum could not be +0.177778 as the criterion states; with the earlier trades only it is exact.
7. **The series.** Edge series: the shadow rows that count for edge, graded, written in the **current paper epoch** (a new reader column `edge_epoch`; with no epoch, the rows written when there was none), ordered by session date then trade key (ordinal). **The series is the longest run of complete sessions from the start of the epoch:** it stops at the first session in which any candidate that counts for edge is still `pending` (review fix). Grades are insert-only, so once every earlier session is complete a later grade can only add to the end of the series and never rewrite the path already shown: a failure seen is not lost to a late grade (a retried replay, a swing trade graded after its exit, a grader batch that reached the newest sessions first). The graded trades after that point are held back until it is graded, are counted in `heldBackTrades`, and the note names the session; a graded trade with no gross figure while the series is gross is left out and the note says how many (it still counts among its day's candidates for the design effect, as in the edge count). R is the gross R (before charges) unless every graded trade is net and every charge sheet is confirmed as the owner's, by the one rule shared with the shadow summary (`ShadowCostBasis`, extracted from `GetShadowSummaryHandler`, behaviour unchanged), and the basis is stated with the figures; the fills are modelled ("modelled fills; no queue, no latency"). The P10 behavioural series (the counted trades of the epoch, in exit order, grouped by entry day for the design effect) is run through the same test and shown beside it, never merged. An epoch restart gives an empty series that reads `monitoring`. Rows from earlier epochs are not read, so a restart clears a latched failure by construction.
8. **Scope.** `scope=system` (every edge trade, the default) or `scope=strategy` with `strategy=<id>`, the trades whose key starts with `<id>|` (the key is `Strategy|Symbol|Side|Date`; an id holding `|` is refused with 400 `paper.invalid`). Cell-level series and win-rate tests are out of scope.
9. **API, verb, line.** `GET /api/paper/sequential?scope=&strategy=` (session; `SequentialStatusView`: state, sum, boundaries and odds, trades counted, effective n, first crossing trade and date, rho and whether it is the placeholder, settings hash and text, a path summary (lowest sum and where, sigma of the last step), the held-back count, both series, the statements and one report line). 404 `paper.not_found` when no shadow edge candidate of the epoch exists in the scope (no series), 400 `paper.invalid` for a bad scope. The report line is printed apart from the check: a failing or unavailable sequential read becomes one stderr note ("sequential line unavailable") and never changes the exit code or the check's text (a cancellation still cancels). The verb `xd paper sequential [--scope system|strategy] [--strategy ID]` prints the same. **The report line beside the P10 phase check:** `xd paper phase-check` (without `--through` and `--epoch`) prints one extra line after the check's own text; the phase check handler, its view and its text are not touched (an architecture test: `Domain.Paper` and `Application.Paper` name no sequential type), so the P10-06 output is byte-identical. A screen is not built: the line is text for a later lazy tab.
10. **Settings.** `Confidence:Sequential` (`Mu1` 0.2, `Sigma` 1.5, `Alpha` 0.05, `BetaCaution` 0.20, `BetaFail` 0.05, `SigmaFromN` 20) in the configuration section of the confidence programme, validated at start (ranges in `SequentialOptions.Problems`); a key left out keeps the default (binding and start-up refusal are tested). There is no mode and no enforce switch: the test only flags, and what to do about a flag is CF-12's, behind the auto/manual setting. Every response carries the SHA-256 of the settings and their text. **For the owner:** the spec's `confidence.sequential.*` "versioned settings home" (ADR 0002) is implemented as the `Confidence:Sequential` configuration section, as CF-01 to CF-07 did, not as database settings versioned in the app; a change takes a restart. Say if the sequential settings should move to the versioned database home.
11. **Isolation.** No type of Planning, Selection or Autonomy (Domain or Application) names a sequential type; the handler, which reads shadow outcomes through `IShadowResultReader`, joins the summary as the second display use case (the CF-08 architecture test lists both); the handler names no store, setting, command, clock or logger; no log event is added, so no trade, level, symbol or strategy text can be logged.

## What is not built
- **The line on the shadow summary and the `/paper` tabs** (frontend; the data is `line` and the sequential view). The shadow summary view and verb are unchanged.
- **CF-11's hard fail and CF-12's kill rules** that read a `failure`; the demotion or any other action.
- **The mixture e-process for unknown variance** (the upgrade path) and tests on the win rate or on small cell series.
- **CF-01's estimated rho for the edge count**: the placeholder 0.3 is used, as the edge count uses it.

## Limits
- The Gaussian model fits fat-tailed R only roughly; the odds are approximate and stated as such. Using the edge series assumes shadow fills are close to what P11 will show. A marginal strategy near +0.1 R can be flagged by chance; the owner chooses `mu1`.
- Caution and on-track are not latched, only failure: a caution can clear if the sum recovers; the first-crossing fields keep the history.
- The latch lasts for the epoch's rows in the shadow record; a change of any setting, or a change of the cost basis of the series (a confirmation, a new or unconfirmed charge sheet, a grade without charges: each can flip it between gross and net R), changes the replay and can clear or set a flag (the settings hash and the basis say what produced a reading).
- A session whose candidate is never graded (a replay that keeps pending) holds back everything after it; the held-back count, the stuck session's date and the note say so, in the view and in the report line, whatever the number of trades in the series. **It may never release:** a pending row can be permanent (the replayer waits on a symbol in no basket now, the simulator refuses the ticket, or a replay keeps throwing), and then the series stays frozen at that session. `shortcut:` nothing ages or ends a pending row today; build one of (a) report how long the session has been pending, (b) let the owner mark the row `not_gradable`, when a freeze is first seen in practice.
- Every shadow row of every epoch is read and filtered in memory (a marked shortcut); the filter moves into SQL when the record grows.

## Consequences
One new read-only query and a pure calculator; nothing the plan, the tickets, the paper capture, the P10 count or the phase check reads changes. Sequential testing saves time on bad strategies, not on good ones: a +0.2 R strategy needs about 312 independent trades before the label reads on track, and the label is never a pass.
