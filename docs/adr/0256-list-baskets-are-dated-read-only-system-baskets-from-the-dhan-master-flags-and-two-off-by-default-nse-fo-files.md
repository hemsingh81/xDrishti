# 0256 — List baskets are dated, read-only system baskets from the Dhan master flags and two off-by-default NSE F&O files

- **Status:** Proposed (BK-05, Approved spec 2026-11-03). The two NSE file fetches become usable when the owner has read the NSE terms (the same gate as [ADR 0254](0254-index-baskets-are-stored-read-only-system-baskets-fed-by-an-off-by-default-guarded-fetch-of-nse-index-lists-with-forward-only-dated-membership.md)) and turned `Baskets:System:Lists:FetchEnabled` on. **Safety slice: YES** (two network fetches of NSE files, off by default; no order, no margin figure, no trading configuration touched).
- **Date:** 2026-11-11.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0254](0254-index-baskets-are-stored-read-only-system-baskets-fed-by-an-off-by-default-guarded-fetch-of-nse-index-lists-with-forward-only-dated-membership.md) (stored system baskets, the guarded fetcher, the fail-closed steps and the dated membership this reuses), [0258](0258-derived-baskets-are-formed-after-the-end-of-day-run-from-our-own-bars-as-immutable-dated-versions-with-scores-and-an-off-by-default-job.md) (basket 13 waited for these lists), [0255](0255-derived-baskets-are-pure-versioned-and-point-in-time.md) (a missing flag is not a pass), [0010](0010-instrument-master-sources-and-scope.md) (the master).

## Context
Scans, the plan and reports need "usable now / excluded now" lists: F&O stocks, ASM/GSM surveillance, MTF-eligible, SME and trade-for-trade series, the daily F&O ban list, and the F&O lot sizes. Four of these are fields the Dhan master already carries; the NSE surveillance pages need cookies and were rejected; only the ban list and the lot sizes need a file from NSE.

## Decision
1. **Four lists are read from the stored master, no network.** After every master refresh (the `instrument-master` service, a separate step in its own transaction: a failure there never rolls back the master) `RefreshFlagListsCommand` builds the sets with the pure `FlagListBuilder`: F&O stocks (`FnoEligible`, not retired), ASM/GSM (`AsmGsmFlag` is anything but blank or `N`; the category is the member `note`), MTF-eligible (`MtfLeverage > 0`; null and 0 are not eligible; no leverage figure is shown), SME / trade-for-trade (series `BE`, `BZ`, `SM`, `ST`). The changes are applied through the existing `IBasketMembershipApplier` as dated rows (`valid_from` = the India date of the refresh, `provenance = derived`, `source_kind = 'refresh'`, `source_ref` = the master refresh id; a flag turned off or a retired instrument closes the stay, never deletes). A failed master run records a failed check for each list and keeps the previous membership (`master_failed`). Each check writes one `basket_fetches` row (`unchanged` or `applied`), which is also how "recorded since" is known.
2. **MIS-eligible is not seeded.** The stored master has no intraday-leverage column; a basket would only be `unavailable`. Revisit when a column exists.
3. **The ban list is a `list` basket dated by the trade date in line 1.** The seeded source `fo_secban.csv` (`nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/fo/`) is asked at 19:00 and 08:30 IST (per-source due times inside the `system-baskets` window; the retry rule of ADR 0254 applies). A first line that is not the header or has no date is rejected (`bad_header`, `bad_date`); a first line only is a valid empty list (the previous day's list closes). The trade date must be within 5 days before to 7 days after today and not older than the source's last applied or confirmed trade date, `basket_sources.known_through` (`stale_trade_date`; an empty list day counts, because the date belongs to the source, not to a member). Row bounds 0..200 live in `basket_sources`. The index tripwire and change bound do not apply (a ban list may legitimately change entirely in a day). Symbols are matched to shares by symbol; an unmatched symbol is counted and listed in the fetch record.
4. **The lot file is a cross-check, not a table.** `fo_mktlots.csv` is parsed with every cell trimmed; the lot is the nearest expiry column (the first whose month-end is on or after the fetch IST date); rows whose symbol is not a share in the master (index rows) are skipped and counted; a non-numeric or non-positive lot, a wrong header, an empty file or a share-row count outside 100..400 (`basket_sources`) is `rejected`. Nothing is stored but the fetch record (outcome `unchanged`): `lots_summary:shares=..,nearest=..,mismatches=..`, `lot_size_mismatch:SYMBOL:master=..:nse=..` for each difference from the master's lot (the nearest unexpired stock future; the master is primary and never overwritten), and `fo_universe_diff:onlyMaster=n,onlyNse=m` (the basket is not corrected). **No `the lot table` table is built:** the spec review (question 5) recommends writing NSE lots into P14-01's `options.lot_sizes` instead; until the owner answers, nothing here stores lots. The source's fetch log needs a basket row: it is **archived** with no members and appears in no list and no screen.
5. **Two gates, one guard.** `Baskets:System:Fetch:Enabled` still gates the index lists; `Baskets:System:Lists:FetchEnabled` (default **false**) gates the two NSE files. With both off no HTTP client is registered; with only one on, the handler never requests a source whose gate is off (tested with a counting fetcher). The user agent is `Baskets:System:Fetch:UserAgent`. `BasketSourceEndpointGuard` additionally allows GET of exactly `/content/fo/fo_secban.csv` and `/content/fo/fo_mktlots.csv` on `nsearchives.nseindia.com` (https, default port, no query, no redirect, no cookies, 5 MB / 30 s). The master-derived sources are never requested whatever the settings. The master-derived catalogue rows carry `source_url = ''` (the column is NOT NULL and an applied schema is not altered; the spec said null); nothing reads the url of a source whose format is not an NSE file. Settings `Baskets:System:Lists:{FetchEnabled, BanRunTimes (19:00, 08:30), LotSizeRunAt (08:50)}` are validated at start-up and registered as an Internal section; `FoUnderlyingBounds` of the spec is dropped (the bounds are in `basket_sources`).
6. **Basket 13 is runnable, and a stale list is never a pass.** The universe reader builds the point-in-time `DayFlag`s from the three list baskets (ASM/GSM, ban, trade-for-trade). A flag exists only from the first date all three lists were recorded (the ban list, whose file is for the next trading day, from the day after its first check) and **only until the earliest of the lists' last good checks**: the India date of the last applied or unchanged check of the two master lists, and `known_through` of the ban list (the newest trade date of an applied or confirmed file, an empty list included). After that date the reader emits a terminal `DayFlag` with `Unknown = true`, and the engine does not count an unknown flag as a pass: a failed master run or a failed ban fetch drops the instrument from basket 13 on the later days. Until all three lists are recorded there are no flags and the refresh skips basket 13 (`flags_unavailable`); it cannot be backfilled (there is no flag history). The series of a date is EQ unless the trade-for-trade list holds the share that day or the master series is neither EQ nor one of the four restricted ones.
7. **No order, no margin figure.** The lists are inputs and exclusion filters; nothing here places or changes an order or a plan, and no margin multiplier or percentage is stored or computed.

## Consequences
- The four master lists appear with the first master refresh after the migration; the first membership is dated that day (no history before it; `membersKnownSince` says so).
- The two NSE files stay unrequested until the owner enables them; with `fetchEnabled` off the lists from the master still refresh.
- A basket row exists for the lot-size file's source (archived), so `GET /api/baskets` lists five list baskets and a copy out of the archived one is refused (tested).

## Known limits
- **Lot table deferred (open owner question 5 of the spec review):** whether to reuse `options.lot_sizes` (`source` widened with `nse_file`, unconfirmed). Until then the lots are only a cross-check in the fetch record.
- The series of a share that moved between EQ and a restricted series before the first recorded refresh is not known; the master series is the only source (a share with a series other than EQ or the four restricted ones is never EQ).
- A ban list that is not refreshed (a failed fetch) keeps its members for the members views, but basket 13 treats the days after `known_through` as unknown.
- NSE terms and the acceptance of a non-browser user agent for the `fo_*` files are UNVERIFIED; so is whether the files keep their layout.
