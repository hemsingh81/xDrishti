# 0205 — The Lab verdict shows a deflated Sharpe ratio from stored trial statistics and a session-level stationary block bootstrap beside the factor and the Monte Carlo in ReportOnly mode, and Gate is not offered

- **Status:** Proposed (built in CF-03 Part A from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no** (no network, no order; the verdict outcome, the six gates and their reasons, the factor, the run key and the seed of the old Monte Carlo are unchanged; nothing reads the new figures).
- **Date:** 2026-11-04.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** extends [0059](0059-the-test-ledger-and-the-verdict-evaluator.md) (the factor `1 + 0.25 log2 N` stays, "not a statistical claim"), [0062](0062-robustness-sensitivity-monte-carlo-by-year-and-a-sign-flip-baseline.md) (the Monte Carlo is untouched; the seed rule is reused with a salt) and [0203](0203-effective-n-is-the-smaller-of-an-entry-session-cluster-estimate-and-a-serial-estimate-shown-beside-n-in-report-only-mode-and-power-and-minimum-track-record-are-printed-with-it.md) (`T` is its effective n; `NormalDistribution` gives `Phi` and `Phi^-1`).

## Context
The factor `f = 1 + 0.25 log2 N` has no calibration, and the Monte Carlo treats trades as independent (its own documented weakness). The programme rule (CF-00): show a new figure beside the old, change nothing by default, say `unavailable: <reason>` rather than invent a number.

## Decision
1. **Pure Domain.** `Confidence/DeflatedSharpe` (`ExpectedMax(N, sigma_SR)`, `Probabilistic`, `Deflated`; `SR0 = sigma_SR [(1 - g) Phi^-1(1 - 1/N) + g Phi^-1(1 - 1/(N e))]`, `DSR = Phi((SR - SR0) sqrt(T - 1) / sqrt(1 - skew SR + (kurt - 1)/4 SR^2))`, `g` the Euler-Mascheroni constant, per-trade units, one trial gives `SR0 = 0`), `TradeMoments` (sample sd with n - 1, skewness and kurtosis from the population moments, as CF-01 already did), `SharpeProbability` (a value or `unavailable: <reason>`, never both). `Lab/StationaryBootstrap` (`Indices`, `Run`, `AutoBlockLength`) beside `MonteCarlo`; `SeededRandom` gains `NextDouble()` (top 53 bits) without changing `NextBits` or `Next`.
2. **Unavailable, never a pass.** `T` below 20, a Sharpe ratio that is NaN (no spread or fewer than 2 trades), skew or kurtosis not a number (fewer than 4 trades), the term `1 - skew SR + (kurt - 1)/4 SR^2` not positive, a spread of trial ratios that is not usable, fewer than 2 stored trials, or fewer than 80 % of the ledger's variants with stored statistics: the figure has no probability and its note starts `unavailable: ` with the reason.
3. **Trials come from a new table.** `lab.variant_stats` (migration `AddLabVariantStats`, insert-only for `xd_app`, granted table by table; the execution role and the MCP roles hold nothing): `(strategy_id, run_id, variant_hash, n, mean_r, sd_r, skew, kurt, sr_per_trade, method, created_at)`, unique `(run_id, variant_hash)` so a retry writes nothing twice (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`), checks `n >= 0`, a 64-hex hash, `sd_r >= 0`, `method` in `full|robust|sweep`, and every stored figure finite (a figure that cannot be computed is null). The spec's column list is kept; `strategy_id`, `id` and `created_at` are added so the family can be read and the latest row of a variant chosen. **This table is Part A**, not Part B: the spec lists it under Part A's scope and DSR needs it; Part B adds only the per-variant daily series to the existing jsonb document.
4. **What is stored, and when.** A run that ends in a result (`full` or `robust`) writes one row for the judged variant (its run id, its strategy hash, the walk-forward out-of-sample trades, `method` = the run's method) and, for `robust`, one row per sweep point, every point (the ledger row's derived run id and variant hash, the point's trades or none, `method` `sweep`). The rows are written after the last cancel check, after the sweep's ledger rows and before the result, so a cancel stores nothing and a retry stores nothing twice. They are written in every mode, also with `Deflation:Mode = Off`, so the history exists when the owner turns the figure on. Quick runs have no result and no row; they are in the ledger count, so a family with many quick runs reads `trial statistics stored for only X of Y variants` (the spec's review fix 2).
5. **N, sigma_SR, T.** N is the number of variants of the family with a finite stored Sharpe ratio (the latest row of each variant hash; this run's rows replace stored ones). The coverage denominator is the distinct hashes of the ledger plus this run's sweep points; every sweep point writes a row, also one that ran no trade or failed (n = 0, figures null), so a point without a ratio lowers the coverage instead of vanishing from it. `sigma_SR` is the sample standard deviation (n - 1) of those ratios. With `Confidence:Deflation:AssumedTrialSrSd` set, N is the ledger count and `sigma_SR` the assumed value (the note says so). `T` is CF-01's effective n of the pooled out-of-sample trades; `TBasis = Raw` uses the raw count with the effective number shown beside (review fix 3). Computed after the robustness stage so the sweep points are among the trials.
6. **Block bootstrap over entry sessions.** The unit is the session (all out-of-sample trades with the same entry date, in their order); a draw takes as many sessions as there are, each continuing to the next session with probability `1 - 1/L` or restarting at a random one with probability `1/L`, wrapping around; the draw's mean is the sum of R over the trades drawn. `L = round(k^(1/3))`, at least 2, unless `Confidence:Bootstrap:BlockLength` is set (2..60). Fewer than 2 sessions: `unavailable: fewer than 2 sessions with a trade`; fewer than 30: the interval is shown with "not trustworthy". The seed is the run's seed XOR a salt, so the stream differs from the Monte Carlo's and the sign-flip's. `monteCarloBlock` sits beside `monteCarlo` with `widthRatioToIid` (the 5th-to-95th width over the iid width); the old member and the run key are unchanged.
7. **Modes.** `Confidence:Deflation:Mode` and `Confidence:Bootstrap:Mode`, default `ReportOnly`; `Off` computes and shows nothing. **`Gate` is not offered** for either: `DeflationOptions.Problems` and `BootstrapOptions.Problems` refuse it and the host stops at start. In ReportOnly the outcome, the six gates and their reasons, the factor, the evidence, the limits and the robustness members other than the new one are byte for byte as before (a runner test serialises both and compares).
8. **Settings** (`Confidence:Deflation`: `Mode`, `MinProbability` 0.5..0.999 default 0.95, `AssumedTrialSrSd` 0.001..1 unset, `TBasis` NEff|Raw; `Confidence:Bootstrap`: `Mode`, `Draws` 100..20000 default `Robustness:McDraws`, `BlockLength` 0 or 2..60). Every figure carries the settings that produced it (`settings` on both views). The run key does not change.
9. **API.** `GET /api/lab/runs/{id}` verdict gains optional `deflatedSharpe`; `robustness` gains optional `monteCarloBlock`; the report (`/report`, its HTML) carries both. A result stored before reads null ("not computed"). No migration touches an existing row. The Lab screen (a lazy route) prints one line and the note under the gates and one line in the robustness block.

## Evidence: the known-null validation (acceptance criterion 6, with the power arm of review fix 1)
`DeflatedSharpeNullSimulationTests`, fixed seeds, under 2 s: 400 families of 16 variants of 100 iid standard-normal trades, `sigma_SR` from the 16 sample ratios, the family's best variant judged.

| Arm | DSR >= 0.95 | The heuristic expectancy gate (best variant's mean R >= 0.15 x f = 0.30) |
|---|---|---|
| Null (every variant mean 0) | **0.25 %** (bound 10 %: holds) | 2.75 % |
| Power (one variant with true per-trade SR 0.3 among 15 null) | **10.5 %** (bound 50 %: **fails**) | 55.25 % |

The false-pass rate is far below 5 %, but the power arm fails: a true per-trade Sharpe ratio of 0.3 at 100 trades is mostly below the luck bar of 16 trials, because the spread of the 16 sample ratios (about 0.1 per trade) already puts the bar near 0.2. The spec says Gate is offered only if both arms hold, so **Gate stays refused** (decision 7) and a test pins that fact. The analytic ceiling explains it: even with the spread of the trial ratios known (about 0.1 per trade, luck bar about 0.18), a true ratio of 0.3 at 100 trades clears 0.95 in only about a third of families, so the 50 % arm was out of reach at these sizes. Comparison with the heuristic is only the expectancy gate at its default adjusted threshold, not the whole six-gate verdict.

## Honest limits (printed with the figure)
- N counts neighbouring variants as independent: the bar is higher than strictly needed (conservative); unrecorded hand edits and strategies never logged make it too lenient. The formulas rest on normal-theory approximations; the paper's formulas were checked by hand and by script, not against its full text.
- The trial ratios mix the walk-forward out-of-sample variant with in-sample sweep points (plain runs of the default exit over the whole window); a family's older variants have a ratio only if they ended in a stored result (quick runs have none).
- The judged out-of-sample trades are the walk-forward winners of an exit selection whose candidate exits are not ledger variants, so they are not in N (lenient in that respect).
- T below 20 effective trades gives no probability, which will be most thin cells.
- The block bootstrap keeps same-day and serial dependence, not a regime shared across weeks; below about 30 sessions it is not trustworthy. A statistical test shows the block interval about 1.4 to 2.1 times wider than the iid one at a design effect of 3.

## Deviations from the spec, deliberately
- **Gate is refused for DSR** (decision 7): the power arm fails (above) and the task forbids switching a gate to DSR. The seventh gate is not built.
- `lab.variant_stats` has three columns beyond the spec's list (decision 3); `method` allows `full|robust|sweep`.
- N for SR0 is the number of variants with a stored ratio (review fix 2), not the ledger count, except with an assumed spread (decision 5).
- The DSR line is the `deflatedSharpe` member with its note; it is not appended to the verdict's `limits` list, so that list stays byte for byte.
- The AC 10 statistical test averages the width ratio over ten seeded datasets (one dataset of 20 sessions has its own noise; a single one gave 2.17 at the first seed); the band 1.4 to 2.1 is the spec's.
- Part B (PBO, the series, `Pbo:*` settings) is not built.

## Open questions for the owner
1. `MinProbability` 0.95 (the paper's convention) or 0.90? It only sets the reading level printed beside the figure while Gate is refused.
2. The power arm fails at 100 trades and a true per-trade ratio of 0.3. Does the owner want Gate considered at all (more trades per variant, a clustered N, or a different arm), or is DSR display-only for good?
3. Should quick runs also write a stored ratio, so older families are not mostly `trial statistics stored for only X of Y variants`? (They would be in-sample on a short window.)
4. Should trial clustering (the paper's effective number of independent trials) be built before Gate is ever offered?
