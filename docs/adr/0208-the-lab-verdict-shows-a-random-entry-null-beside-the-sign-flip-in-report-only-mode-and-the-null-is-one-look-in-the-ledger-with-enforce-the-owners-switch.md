## Context
P5-05's baseline is a sign-flip null: it keeps each out-of-sample trade's size and flips its sign. It says itself that it is lenient, because it tests the mean R against 0 given the trade sizes, and random entries with a 2R target lose money before costs. The question the owner needs answered is different: does the rule's timing add anything over entering anywhere in the same window with the same exits and costs? CF-07 D1 answers it with a seeded random-entry null. The programme rule (CF-00) holds: a new test is report-only beside PASS/MARGINAL/FAIL until the owner turns its enforce setting on; `unavailable` is never a pass; the settings that produced a figure are stored with it.

## Decision
1. **The seam is the candidate, not the walker.** The first task of the spec was a read of the walker. `TradeWalker.Walk(candidate, bars, rules)` already knows nothing of where an `EntryCandidate` came from: it reads the candidate and the minutes strictly after its own. So the entry-override seam (`EntryOverride`, in `XDrishti.Simulation`) is only the making of a forced candidate: the decision at the end of the minute `bars[i]`, that minute as the 1-minute trigger bar, and a stop at a given level or at a given fraction of the trigger close (below it for a buy, above for a sell). The walker is not changed. Tests: a forced entry at the minute and the stop the strategy itself used reproduces the replayed trade (prices, times, exit, R, MAE, MFE; only the trigger bar differs, because the strategy decided on a 15-minute bar); a forced entry gives the same trade whether the minutes after its exit are cut away or scrambled (leak test).
2. **The random trade.** `RandomEntryWalker` (pure, in `XDrishti.Simulation`) restates each real out-of-sample trade, for each seed, as a trade with the same instrument, session (India date), side, initial risk as a fraction of the trigger close (the price the stop is placed from: the real trade's `|trigger close - stop| / trigger close`, and the random trade places its stop the same way from the close of its own minute; a real trade whose stop is not on the loss side of its trigger close is not restated), fixed target multiple, square-off, fill rules and tick, entered at a minute drawn uniformly inside the strategy's own entry window (`first_entry` to `last_entry`, at the latest the minute before the square-off), and costs it with the same sheet at its own quantity (`TradeCostApplier`, the segment of the real trade). A draw that makes no trade (no fill bar, limit not reached, entry through the stop, quantity under one share, cost above the maximum) is drawn again up to `Redraws` (5) times, then dropped and counted. The bars of a session are adjusted on a copy exactly as the replay adjusts them and are read once per (instrument, session) for all seeds. Each draw comes from a generator seeded from the plan seed, the seed number and the trade's plan position only, so the order in which instruments are walked changes nothing. Same seed, same result; another seed differs.
3. **Statistics and the pass rule.** One seed is one full set of such trades; its value is the mean net R of its trades. `RandomEntryNull.Evaluate` (pure, Domain) reads the real mean against the seed means: empirical 5th, 50th, 95th and 99th percentile and mean, `count_ge` (seeds at or above the real mean), `p = (count_ge + 1) / (seeds + 1)`. The row passes if and only if `p <= 1 - percentile/100` (0.05); the 95th percentile is displayed and is not a second test (review fix 1 of the spec). Value, threshold, comparison and status (`pass`, `fail`, `unavailable`) are in the block (D5). Seed: first 8 bytes of the sha256 of `run key + ":random-entry"`. By hand: 38 of 1000 seeds at or above gives `p = 39/1001 = 0.0390` (pass), 50 gives `51/1001 = 0.0509` (fail); a real mean of 0.12 R against seed means of mean 0 and sd 0.04 passes, against mean 0.07 fails (unit tests on constructed series).
4. **Unavailable, never a pass.** Fewer than 30 real trades that could be restated; less than 80 % of the out-of-sample trades restated (the real mean and the seed means are always over the same restated trades, and the left-out count is printed); more than 20 % of the (trade, seed) draws dropped; a value that is not finite; a gross run, a swing strategy, an instrument the replay would skip, a session without bars or a failed walk: `unavailable: <reason>`, no p-value. These cut-offs are proposals (constants, not settings).
5. **What is restated.** Part A restates intraday trades with a fixed reward-to-risk exit: a target price, no legs, a candidate named `FIXED_RR{...}` without the `time_stop` overlay, a charge from the sheet. The default candidate exits of the Lab are exactly these. A swing strategy, a trail, a partial or a pyramid is not restated and the null says so; it is not approximated.
6. **Window and hold-out.** CF-06 shortens only a default window; an explicit window may end on or after the block start (it is refused only with `Confidence:HoldOut:Enforce` on). So the null does not trust the run's `To`: `LabRunner`, which holds the hold-out guard, gives the null a plain `developmentEnd` date, the earlier of the run's `To` and the day before the stored block start (the run's `To` when no block is kept). The request is sent with `To = developmentEnd`, so `RunSimulationHandler` reads bars up to the end of that day and not a bar further; `RandomEntryWalker` refuses a session after the end of the window; and a real out-of-sample trade after `developmentEnd` is not restated: it is left out of both sides of the comparison and counted in the printed left-out count, and the 80 % rule of decision 4 then decides (unavailable when too few remain). The block's `basis` says when the window was cut to stay clear of the hold-out block. The null names no hold-out type (an architecture test) and the hold-out pass is still reached from the unlock alone. Note that the full test itself, with an explicit window and enforcement off, can still choose and score winners on sessions inside the block (CF-06 warns in the report); the null only guarantees that it reads none.
7. **Where it runs.** `RunNullWalkQuery` is answered by `RunSimulationHandler` (the same preparation, the same instrument reading, the same cost sheet and tick), which hands each instrument that has a trade in the plan to the new port `IRandomEntryEngine` (adapter `SimulationEngineAdapter`, a second interface of the same class) instead of the replay, and builds no run record and stores nothing. `ISimulationEngine` is unchanged. `FullTestOutcome` carries the simulated trade behind each out-of-sample trade (matched by reference, not by value). A new stage `nulls` follows `robustness` in `LabRunner` for `robust` runs only; progress counts the trades walked (`IRandomEntryEngine` reports one per trade and checks the cancellation token before each seed of a trade); a cancel before the last check stores nothing; a crash is `lab.run_crashed`.
8. **The ledger.** One `trial_kind = 'null'` row (method `null`) per run, not per seed, appended with the sweep rows after the last cancel check, with an id derived from the run id so a retry writes nothing twice. The honest count of CF-06 (`TrialCounts.NullRows`) already adds it, so the next verdict's factor includes it. The migration `AddLabNullTrialKind` only widens the check constraint `ck_test_ledger_trial` to `('run','replan','null')`; Down deletes the null rows (the old constraint refuses them) and restores the old check. No new table, so no new grant.
9. **Modes and settings.** Section `Confidence:Nulls`: `Mode` (`Off` | `ReportOnly`, default `ReportOnly`), `Enforce` (default false: the owner's switch; `Enforce` on with `Mode` `Off` is refused at start, nothing would be computed to enforce), `Draws` 1000 (200..5000), `Percentile` 95 (90..99; it sets the level `1 - percentile/100`), `Redraws` 5 (0..20). A value out of range stops the host at start. Every block stores the text of the values and its SHA-256.
10. **Enforce.** With `Enforce` on and a PASS, a null that is a `fail` or `unavailable` caps the outcome at MARGINAL and adds a limit; a MARGINAL or FAIL is never raised or changed and the six gates are untouched. With it off nothing but the new member changes: a runner test serialises the verdict with and without the member and compares the bytes.
11. **API and report.** `GET /api/lab/runs/{id}` verdict and `/report` gain an optional `nulls` block (`mode`, `enforce`, `settings`, `settingsHash`, `randomEntry` with the real mean, the seed mean, p5/p50/p95/p99, `countAtLeast`, `pValue`, `threshold`, `comparison`, `status`, `reason`, `droppedShare`, `redraws`, `seed`; `capped`; `basis`; `notes`); the report warns `random_entry` on a fail or an unavailable null; the printed limits carry the basis and the notes; the HTML report gets a "Chance: random entry" section. An old stored document reads `nulls` as null. The block is added to the generated client (`schema.d.ts`); the Lab screen does not read it yet, so no frontend code was added.

## What is not built (parts B and C of CF-07)
- **Part B: the placebo set (D2)**: the 20 fixed shapes (`mirror`, `shift_+15/+30/+45/+60`, `date_shift_±1..5`, `instrument_roll_1..5`), the rank of the real strategy among 21, `p = rank / (k + 1)`, `inconclusive` below 19 comparable placebos; and the **mirror gate (D3)** (the mirror's net expectancy at most 0, "mostly drift" otherwise). The seam of this change (`EntryOverride` plus a restatement of the real trade) is the base; the shapes are new generators over the real signals. The `nulls` block has no `placebos` or `mirror` member yet.
- **Part C: the plateau gate (D4)** on the swept parameters (adjacent points keep 70 % of the base, median above 0, plateau centre reported), its settings `Confidence:Plateau:*` (neighbour retention 0.70, min points 3, `Enforce` false), and the "in-sample sweep" label of the stored block. The sweep itself is unchanged.
- **The `nulls` and `draws` fields of `POST /api/lab/runs`.** The null is on for robust runs by the `Confidence:Nulls` setting only.
- **The Lab screen block** "Chance and plateau" (null summary table, placebo ranking, parameter rows); the report HTML has the null section, the React screen does not.
- **Swing strategies and exits other than a fixed target** (trails, partials, pyramids, time-stop overlays) in the null (decision 5).
- **Timing on the owner's data.** The cost is the number of walks, about 320 trades x 1000 seeds; it is not measured here (the spec's 60 s target is unverified).
- **A separate setting for the minimum trades and the coverage** (constants for now), and the consumers of CF-11 rows 7 to 9.

## Open owner questions
1. The 95th percentile and p at most 0.05 as the bar, or looser while data are short? 2. 1000 seeds? 3. Are the unavailable cut-offs (30 trades, 80 % restated, 20 % dropped) acceptable? 4. When `Enforce` goes on (an owner act). 5. Should the null be restricted to the walk-forward test windows only? It is, since it restates the out-of-sample trades; the real trades are still winners of an exit selection, so selection bias is not nulled (printed in the limits).

## Consequences
Robust runs gain one stage and one more walk per seed and trade; progress counts it. The default is unchanged in every stored number: a fresh install shows the block, which reads `unavailable` until enough intraday fixed-target trades exist. The trades of one day are correlated inside a seed (not across seeds), so the null is not wider than reality (printed). Every robust run adds one `null` row to the ledger, so the honest count (when the owner has turned `CountInFactor` on) rises by one per run.
