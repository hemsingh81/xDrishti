- **Status:** Proposed (built in CF-07 part B from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03; the placebo set and the mirror gate only, see "What is not built"). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no** (research-side numbers; no order, no broker, no network; with `Enforce` off the verdict outcome, the six gates and their reasons, the limits, the evidence, the run key and the keys of the stored runs are unchanged).
- **Date:** 2026-11-05.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** extends [0208](0208-the-lab-verdict-shows-a-random-entry-null-beside-the-sign-flip-in-report-only-mode-and-the-null-is-one-look-in-the-ledger-with-enforce-the-owners-switch.md) (same block, same mode rule, same seam, same development window and same cancel rule), [0037](0037-simulation-conventions-and-the-two-stage-seam.md) and [0038](0038-fill-realism-the-entry-limit-gaps-slippage-ticks-and-the-order-inside-a-minute.md) (the walker is reused unchanged), [0042](0042-costs-and-sizing-the-charge-sheet-the-quantity-net-r-and-simtrade-schema-4.md) (a placebo trade is sized and charged by the same applier) and [0207](0207-a-strategy-version-is-pre-registered-the-ledger-counts-every-extra-look-and-a-locked-hold-out-is-fixed-at-the-first-post-and-read-once-by-an-owner-unlock-with-enforcement-and-the-honest-count-as-the-owners-switches.md) (the placebo set is a look; the window is the development window).

## Context
The random-entry null (ADR 0208) asks whether the rule's timing beats entering anywhere in the same window. Two questions remain in CF-07: does the strategy also earn as its own opposite, as a later copy, as a copy on another day or on another instrument (D2: a real edge must not), and is the whole result just drift (D3: the mirror must not earn)? Both are placebos of the real signals with no free parameter, so the owner cannot tune them until the strategy wins. The programme rule (CF-00) holds: a new test is report-only beside PASS/MARGINAL/FAIL until the owner turns an enforce setting on; `unavailable` is never a pass; the settings that produced a figure are stored with it.

## Decision
1. **The 20 shapes** (`PlaceboShapes`, pure, Domain). In this fixed order: `mirror` (opposite side, same risk fraction and target multiple, so the stop and target are mirrored about the decision price); `shift_+15`, `+30`, `+45`, `+60` (the same signal, the entry decision that many minutes after the real one in the same session; an earlier entry is look-ahead and is not offered); `date_shift_+1` to `+5` then `-1` to `-5` (the same clock-time decision on the session that many sessions of the same instrument away); `instrument_roll_1` to `_5` (the signal moved to the instrument that many places on in the fixed order, which is the ascending order of the ids of the instruments that have an out-of-sample trade, wrapping round); instrument rolls are one-to-one over the roll order, not a permutation within the day's instruments. `PlaceboShapes.Place` decides only where a signal goes (instrument, session offset, minute offset, side) and is a pure function of the shape, the signal and the roll order. A roll is a cyclic shift of the order, so for any roll the instruments of one day's signals are mapped one to one; a roll onto the signal's own instrument (two instruments and `roll_2`, `roll_4`; three and `roll_3`; or one instrument) has no placement and that shape then places no trade.
2. **The walk** (`PlaceboWalker`, pure, in `XDrishti.Simulation`). For each real signal (instrument, session, side, risk as a fraction of the trigger close, fixed target multiple, cost segment and the decision instant) and each shape placed on the instrument being walked, it finds the minute of the target session whose clock time is the real decision minute plus the shift (exactly that minute: a missing minute is "no minute at the shifted decision time"), forces an entry there through `EntryOverride` with the placement's side and a stop at the signal's risk fraction of that minute's close, walks it with the strategy's square-off, fills and tick, and costs it with the walked instrument's sheet in the real trade's segment (`TradeCostApplier`). The outcome is the net R or the reason there is none (no session, no minute, no fill, a walk refused, a swing strategy, a skipped instrument, a gross run). The shapes are not limited to the strategy's entry window: a placebo is the same signal moved, not a new entry rule. The bars of a session are adjusted on a copy as the replay does and read once per session for all shapes. Nothing is random, so the order in which instruments are walked changes no outcome. A session after the end of the window is never read.
3. **Where it runs.** `RunPlaceboWalkQuery` is answered by `RunSimulationHandler` as `RunNullWalkQuery` is (same preparation, the same instrument reading, sheet and tick), over the instruments of the roll order (those with a signal), through the new port `IPlaceboEngine` (the same stateless adapter, a third interface). `ISimulationEngine` and `IRandomEntryEngine` are unchanged; the handler's private walk record now carries a predicate (which instruments) and an action (what to do with each) instead of the random-entry plan. Progress counts the (signal, shape) pairs walked, after the trades of the random-entry walk (the stage total is the sum; the placebo walk's count is offset by the trades already done); the walk checks the cancellation token before each pair.
4. **The rank** (`PlaceboSet`, pure, Domain). A shape is comparable when it placed at least `max(30, ceil(0.8 x out-of-sample trades))` trades (the same cut-offs as ADR 0208, constants); its value is the mean net R of the trades it placed. The real value is the mean net R of the restated real trades that the random-entry walk compared (so the random-entry, placebo and mirror blocks show one real mean). A roll onto the signal's own instrument is reported on its row as "the roll lands on the signal's own instrument (only N instruments have a trade)". The real rank is 1 plus the comparable placebos whose mean is at or above the real mean (a tie goes against the strategy); `p = rank / (comparable + 1)`; the row passes if and only if `p <= 0.05` (`NullOptions.PlaceboAlpha`, a constant of the design (D2): it does not follow `Percentile`, which sets the level of the random-entry null only, because a level of 0.01 would make the set a certain fail and a level of 0.10 would let rank 2 of 21 pass, against "must rank first") and at least `MinPlacebos` (19) placebos are comparable. With 20 comparable and rank 1, `p = 1/21 = 0.0476` passes; rank 2, `p = 2/21 = 0.0952` fails; with 19 comparable, rank 1 is `1/20 = 0.05`, at the level, and passes. Fewer than `MinPlacebos` comparable, or fewer real trades than the minimum, or a value that is not finite: `unavailable: inconclusive ...`, no p-value, never a pass. The block lists the 20 rows (trades placed, mean net R, rank among the strategy and the comparable placebos, why a shape is not comparable).
5. **The mirror gate** (`MirrorGate`, pure, Domain). The `mirror` shape's mean net R is the value; the threshold is 0; the status is `pass` when it is at most 0 and `fail` when above, with the reason "mostly drift" (the edge is drift or beta, not timing). A mirror with fewer placed trades than the minimum, or a value that is not finite: `unavailable`, never a pass. The mirror also takes part in the rank as one of the 20.
6. **Window and hold-out.** As ADR 0208, decision 6: `LabRunner` gives both walks the same `developmentEnd` (the earlier of the run's end and the day before the stored hold-out block) and the request is sent with `To = developmentEnd`; `PlaceboWalker` reads no session after `InstrumentReplayInput.To`, so a date shift that would reach the block finds no session and places no trade. The new types name no hold-out type (an architecture test extends the null's).
7. **The ledger.** The placebo set is a family-level null of its own, so it is a look of its own: a second row of trial kind `null` with method `placebo` (the random-entry null keeps method `null`), written with the sweep rows after the last cancel check, with an id derived from the run id and the family (`placebo|<run id>`), so a retry writes nothing twice and the two rows of a run never collide. The honest count (`TrialCounts.NullRows`) already counts rows of kind `null`, so the next verdict's factor includes both. No migration: the check constraint of ADR 0208 already accepts the kind and `method` is free text. A migration test shows the row inserts and that Down removes every row of kind `null` whatever its method. The row is written even when the set is unavailable (the look was taken). When the setting `Placebos` is off no row is written.
8. **Modes and settings.** In `Confidence:Nulls` (ADR 0208): `Placebos` (default true; off computes neither the set nor the mirror, adds no walk and no row) and `MinPlacebos` (default 19, 19 to 20: with 18 or fewer the best rank, 1 of 19, is already above 0.05, so a lower minimum could only turn "inconclusive" into "fail"). `Enforce` stays the one switch of the family and `Enforce` with `Mode` `Off` is refused at start as before. A value out of range stops the host at start. The settings text and hash of the block include the two new values.
9. **Enforce.** With `Enforce` on and a PASS, a random-entry null, a placebo set or a mirror gate that is a `fail` or `unavailable` caps the outcome at MARGINAL and adds one limit naming which (for example "capped at MARGINAL by the mirror gate (fail) (enforced)"); a MARGINAL or FAIL is never raised or changed and the six gates are untouched. With it off nothing but the new members changes: the runner test that serialises the verdict with and without the block compares the bytes.
10. **API and report.** The optional `nulls` block of the verdict and of `/report` gains `placebos` (`shapes`, `comparable`, `minComparable`, `realMeanR`, `realRank`, `pValue`, `threshold`, `comparison`, `status`, `reason`, `rows`: `shape`, `trades`, `meanR`, `rank`, `comparable`, `reason`) and `mirror` (`trades`, `mirrorMeanR`, `realMeanR`, `threshold`, `comparison`, `status`, `reason`), both null in a document stored before it or with `Placebos` off. The report warns `placebo` on a fail or an unavailable set and `mirror` on a fail ("Mostly drift: ...") or an unavailable gate, and prints the limits of the figure in its notes; the HTML report gains "Chance: placebo set" and "Chance: mirror". The contract is regenerated (`scripts/gen-api.sh`); the Lab screen does not read the new members, so no frontend code was added.

## What is not built (part C of CF-07)
- **The plateau gate (D4)** on the swept parameters (adjacent points keep 70 % of the base, median above 0, plateau centre reported), its settings `Confidence:Plateau:*` and the "in-sample sweep" label. The sweep itself is unchanged.
- **The `nulls` and `draws` fields of `POST /api/lab/runs`** and **the Lab screen block** "Chance and plateau".
- **Swing strategies and exits other than a fixed target** in the placebo walk (as in the null).
- **A larger roll set.** The rolls go over the instruments that have an out-of-sample trade, not over the whole universe, so a run needs at least six of them for all five rolls to land on another instrument; with fewer, the missing rolls place no trade, the set can have only 19 comparable at best (three, four or five instruments) or is inconclusive (one or two). Rolling over the universe is a larger read and is left to the owner's decision.
- **Timing on the owner's data.** The cost is at most 20 walks per restated trade; it is small next to the 1000 seeds of the null and is not measured separately.

## Open owner questions
1. Is rank first of the comparable placebos at 0.05 the bar, or looser (for example top 2 of 21) while data are short? 2. Should the rolls go over the whole basket instead of the instruments with a trade? 3. Should a placebo that places fewer than 80 % of the trades be compared on the trades it placed (as now) or be left out? 4. When `Enforce` goes on (an owner act).

## Consequences
Robust runs gain at most 20 walks per restated trade after the random-entry seeds and one more ledger row, so the honest count (when the owner has turned `CountInFactor` on) rises by two per robust run instead of one. The defaults change no stored number besides the new members: a fresh install shows the two blocks, which read `unavailable` until enough intraday fixed-target trades and instruments exist. The real mean is over the restated real trades while each placebo is over the trades it placed, so a placebo that places few trades has a noisier mean (it is ranked only above the minimum); trades of one day are correlated, as in the null.
