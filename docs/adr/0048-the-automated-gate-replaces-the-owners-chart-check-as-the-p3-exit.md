# 0048 — The automated gate replaces the owner's chart check as the P3 exit

- **Status:** Accepted design, text unread by the owner (owner, 2026-10-21: chose "fully automated" in chat; the text of this ADR and of [spec P3-09d](../specs/P3-09d-the-automated-gate.md) has not been read by the owner). Amends item 15 (the exit rule) of [ADR 0046](0046-the-verification-pack-and-the-20-trade-check.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-21.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** ADR 0046 (pack, protocol, Amendment 1 the assisted check), ADR 0047 (the oracle and the rule tables), ADR 0037 and 0038 (the rules the reader and the spot checks follow).

## Context
ADR 0046 made the owner's hand check of 20 trades against charts, and the owner's word in chat, the exit of P3. The assisted check (Amendment 1) automated most of the reading but left the verdicts, the reading of the exceptions and the five chart rows to the owner. The owner decided that the project itself runs the verification, analyses the result by explicit rules and decides the next step.

## Decision
1. **The exit of P3** is the status `PASS` or `PASS_WITH_DATA_FINDINGS` of `verify-pack --gate <pack-folder>` on a pack drawn with the owner's seed, plus a green `scripts/check.sh`. The owner's chart check and signature are optional extra evidence and block nothing. Everything in ADR 0046 about the pack, the draw and the protocol stands; only item 15 changes. The rest of item 15's bookkeeping (a new seed and a new pack after any fix) stands.
2. **The rules** (spec P3-09d D2) are first-match, in this order, over categories computed from the rows and not from the assisted verdict (whose precedence hides a category behind a stronger one): `BLOCKED_DEFECT` (exit 12), `DATA_REPAIR_NEEDED` (11), `RERUN_NEEDED` (10), `PASS_WITH_DATA_FINDINGS` (0), `PASS` (0). Every status prints numbered next actions; every count is printed, so no category masks another. A pack with no trade row is `BLOCKED_DEFECT`, never a pass.
3. **Why the statuses divide this way.** The engine runs on the stored bars, so the reader on the stored bars tests the rules and the implementation only: any difference there, any trade it cannot read, or an engine row that is absent is a defect of the engine or of the rules (or of the reader), and blocks. A gross bar problem other than a hole (a price level not constant or outside the band, a gap above 1 % of price) makes the comparison meaningless for that row and calls for a data repair before anything is concluded. **A hole (a cited minute missing on the stored side or on the second source) is a data-quality finding, not evidence against the engine** (amended 2026-10-21 after the first real run: `p3-check-1` had a hole in KALYANKJIL 2024-04-23, 10:52 to 10:57, in the stored bars). It is listed with instrument, day, minutes, side and whether it lies between the trade's entry and exit minutes, and gives `PASS_WITH_DATA_FINDINGS` when nothing else is wrong; a defect still blocks. This is weaker than repairing the hole, and it is chosen because the existing gap backfill covers only the last `Data:Gaps:LookbackSessions` sessions (default 30, at most 250) and so can never reach a 2024 day, and because the owner does not want the stored history edited for the gate (ISS-29). A repair command for old holes is a later decision. A row with no second source has no second reading and must be run again. A difference only on the second source means the trade depends on which feed is right; it says nothing against the engine, so it is recorded as an open data-quality finding and does not block P3.
4. **The spot lines** are checked by machine from the stored bars and the pack's trades (spec P3-09d D3): a short session has fewer than 90 % of the 375 regular minutes, no trade of the pack lies on an excluded day, a weekend is not a regular session, a missing session has no bar, a flagged day shows a jump above 20 % on the stored bars. A contradiction blocks. What the stored bars cannot show is reported as `not_machine_checkable` and counted: a special session on a weekday and whether a weekday was a trading day (the calendar is in the database), a flagged day whose jump is not reproduced (the replay tests adjusted prices), a missing session on a weekend, and `open_at_end` (a fact of the run record, which is not in the pack). A "confirmed" flagged day means only that the raw stored jump exceeds 20 %; the replay judges adjusted prices, so it is not a check of the replay's own flag. An empty `spots` array is reported as one not-machine-checkable line and printed as "0 spot lines". These never pass silently and change no status; they remain for the optional chart check.
4b. **The pack must be complete.** `pack.json` must match its manifest entry, `pack_key` and `count` must equal the manifest's, the rows and spot lines must equal the manifest's `pack.row=` and `pack.spot=` lines, and the trades of `engine-values.csv` must equal the rows of `pack.json` both ways; any difference is a `BLOCKED_DEFECT` finding. A truncated or edited pack cannot pass.
4c. **One direction the gate cannot see.** The pack holds only trades the engine made, so "the engine skipped but the reader would have traded" cannot appear as a row. That direction is covered by the oracle of P3-08 (ADR 0047), not by the gate; the decision file says so.
5. **Thresholds** are named constants, none invented for the gate. `BarComparison.GrossGapPercent` = 1.0 % (ADR 0046 Amendment 1: the first real run's row-maximum gaps were 0.04 % to 0.35 %, a feed-noise cluster, with 1 % well above it). The completeness 0.90 and the discontinuity 0.20 are the registered defaults of `ReplayOptions`, which `verify-pack` runs with, read from that type and not copied. `GateRunner.LookbackDays` = 7 finds the previous session of a flagged day (it is longer than the usual weekend or holiday gap; a day with none is `not_machine_checkable`, not a pass).
6. **Files.** `gate-decision.json` and `gate-decision.md` in the pack folder, deterministic, with the SHA-256 of `pack.json`, `engine-values.csv`, `assist-log.csv`, `second-source-bars.csv` and `manifest.json`. The gate never reads or writes `check-log.csv` and never writes a verdict. It adds one log line, event 4424 (status and source name).

## The honest limit
The gate shows that the engine follows its written rules on an independent reading of the bars, stored and second source. **It is not a chart-by-eye check.** The reader, the oracle and the engine are written by the same model family from the same rule tables, so a misreading of the rules that all share stays possible and invisible to the gate; and a data feed that is wrong in the same way in both sources is invisible too. The gate says that the mechanics follow the rules, never that the rules describe the market, that the engine is free of defects or that a strategy has an edge. A third independent source (for example a TradingView export through `--second-source-folder`), or an optional owner's chart check of a handful of rows and of the not-machine-checkable spot lines, can be added later and makes the evidence stronger; neither is required.

## Consequences
- The 7.5 to 12 hours of the owner's check are no longer on the exit path. The power of the exit is lower than the original on the model (rules vs intent) and the same or higher on data and implementation, because every row is read, not a sample.
- A defect now found by the gate leads to a failing test, a fix and a new pack with a new seed, by the same rule as before.
- A gate on a strategy the reader cannot read (anything but ORB today) is `BLOCKED_DEFECT` through `cannot_read`: the machine does not claim what it cannot check.
- No API, table or migration.
