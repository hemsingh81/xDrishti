# 0151 - The ramp gates, breach rules and halts read the stored live record and write only insert-only rows; the evaluator moves no ticket; the exit report that unlocks S3 is not built

- **Status:** Accepted (decided in P11-04b under the owner's delegation of 2026-10-29; spec [P11-04](../specs/P11-04-scale-up-rules-and-risk-ramp.md), second half).
- **Date:** 2026-10-29.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0148](0148-the-ramp-and-the-live-switches-are-insert-only-owner-acts-live-accounts-are-sized-in-their-own-pool-at-the-step-scale-and-the-database-guards-the-live-allocation-and-the-live-auto-approval.md) (the ramp tables and the owner acts this completes), [0149](0149-the-live-capture-reads-the-days-trade-book-and-order-book-with-two-pinned-get-entries-stores-them-insert-only-and-gives-each-approved-ticket-one-final-outcome.md) and [0150](0150-the-execution-feedback-measures-each-live-entry-outcome-against-its-reference-in-delay-and-execution-estimates-slippage-per-liquidity-bucket-weekly-and-applies-nothing.md) (the evidence the gates read), [0147](0147-the-compliance-record-is-one-insert-only-owner-written-store-for-the-static-ip-sebi-and-dhan-read-policy-enquiry.md) (owner act, gate `Live:Writes:Enabled`).

## Context
04A built the step, the scale and the owner's moves, and left the evidence out: a raise was refused because no gate existed, and the live-ready checklist item could not pass because nothing reported the halt, capture and loss state. 04B supplies them: the evidence gates G1 to G7, the breach rules B1 to B7, the loss measure and the daily and weekly halts, the evaluator, the owner's `clear_hold` and `exit_confirmed`, the notices, the Today tile and `xd live ramp show`. Nothing here may place, change or cancel an order, and nothing here calls Dhan.

## Decision
1. **Pure core.** `RampGates` (G1 to G7), `BreachRules` (B1 to B7 and the conditions that still hold a hold), `DayLoss` (the first-in-first-out loss of a day's fills) and `HaltRules` live in Domain, take their numbers from `RampThresholds` (the spec's defaults, `Live:Ramp:*` options validated at start) and read no clock, file or database. A gate is `pass`, `fail`, `insufficient`, `unavailable`, `not_evaluable` or `info`; only `pass` counts; the step is eligible when G1 to G6 all pass. G7 is information.
2. **One reader.** `RampAssessor` turns the stored record (capture runs, outcomes, executions, fills, halts) into the evidence of the current step and applies the pure rules. A session counts when it is completed (before today) and after the India day on which the newest step-moving event (`set_step`, `auto_step_up`, `step_down`) was written. Capped at the last 120 sessions. Facts: sessions with a `healthy` capture; of the last five, those `gap` or `not_run`; settle runs of the last ten that were `partial` (the history disagreed with the trade book or could not be read: the settle now stores `partial` instead of `ok` when the history misses delivery fills of the trade book); closed round trips from `live_executions` (R = side times (exit VWAP minus entry VWAP) over the plan risk per share, `uncertain` when there is no risk or the exit quantity differs); G3 over the reference kind with the most measured entries (ticket level wins a tie), one-sided 95 % t bounds from the spec's table; discretionary trades = symbol-days of fills the matcher leaves discretionary; protection from the stored outcome (`unavailable` without order evidence blocks G4); drawdown = largest peak-to-trough of the running R sum.
3. **The evaluator writes rows only, once per change.** `EvaluateRampHandler` runs after every poll of `live-capture` (halts only) and after the settle and execution measure of `live-settle` (halts and ramp). Per manual-live account with a step: a hold by `rule:ramp` on any breach (when not on hold already); a `step_down` on B1 with two events, B6 or B7 (at S1 there is nothing lower, so it holds instead); and, only with `live.auto_ramp_up` ON, no hold and all gates passing at a step below 2, an `auto_step_up` carrying the frozen gate text and the settings snapshot id and hash. A `step_down` clears the hold flag; the owner's new `set_step` (gates applying) lifts it. The evidence of a step restarts at each step move, so the breach windows do too: stated, accepted.
4. **Halts.** The daily limit is the profile's R limit or percentage limit (the account's override), the weekly one the profile's weekly R limit with its action (`halve`, `stop_new`, `alert_only`); 1 R is the capital times the live risk percent at the current step. A halt is one insert-only row per account, kind and period (`trading.live_halts`, unique key makes it idempotent); the period decides how long it is in force (a day; for a weekly `stop_new` the rest of that ISO week; for `halve` the next ISO week). No `closed` row is ever written. A measure that is `unavailable` never opens a halt and never reads as "no loss"; a day with an unmarked open lot is `realised_only` and says so. The loss counts the fills of the session opened and closed or marked today, gross of charges; positions carried in from earlier days (a delivery sale with nothing bought today) and charges are not in it (stated on screen). Open lots are marked at the newest stored one-minute close not after now, never at cost.
5. **What a halt does here.** It is recorded, put in the bell and on the Today tile, fails `account_live_ready` for the planned session (a daily halt on its own day, a weekly stop for the rest of its week), takes the next week's scale down by half (`halve`) or to nothing (`stop_new`) in the plan context, and makes any later fill an `after_halt` entry for B1 and G5. **Not built, on purpose:** invalidating still-Approved or Armed tickets (`rule:live-halt`). The ticket lifecycle has no Armed to Invalidated move (and an Approved ticket is armed at the pre-open check, so an intraday halt finds none), and a ticket is shared by every account's allocation, so invalidating it would also cancel the paper accounts' tickets. The owner places every order; the halt is advice and friction (an approval at plan time cannot be made while it is in force), not a block. The approval-time check stays the plan-time checklist of ADR 0087.
6. **Owner acts.** `POST /api/live-trading/ramp/clear-hold` and `/ramp/exit-confirmed` use the order of ADR 0147: the capability gate `Live:Writes:Enabled` first, then a fresh password (`Elevate: false`), then the handler; insert-only, audited with numbers (never the reason), rate-limited like sign-in. `clear_hold` is refused with `live.hold_condition_active` while a breach condition holds on the evidence read now (the event rules B1 to B3 need `HoldCooldownSessions` (5) sessions without a new event; B4 and B5 stay while true; a step-down rule is not a hold condition). A raise by one step is judged on the evidence read at that moment and refused with `live.gates_not_met` listing every gate that is not `pass`; a hold refuses it (`live.hold_active`); S3 additionally needs `exit_confirmed` (`live.exit_not_confirmed`; the table refuses it too).
7. **`exit_confirmed` is closed until P11-06 exists.** It needs "the P11-06 report is MET at that moment: the owner reads it, the system does not decide". The report is not built, so `ILiveExitReport` is registered as `NoExitReport`, which can never say MET: the act is refused with 409 `live.exit_not_checked` and S3 stays unreachable. An unreadable report is never a pass. P11-06 replaces the registration.
8. **Plan context.** `StoredPlanContextSource` takes the live-ready facts and the scale factor from `ILiveReadyFactsSource`: no halt in force, the last completed session's capture `healthy`, its loss measurable. A session on or before the account's first step is not judged. The facts that cannot be read stay null (not a pass).
9. **Reads.** `GET /ramp` gains the gates, breaches, hold conditions, today's loss, halts and sessions; new `GET /api/live-trading/halts` and `GET /api/live-trading/notices` (derived: halt, hold, capture gap, step earned, compliance overdue; words and a route only). The bell merges the live notices (compliance is already there). `xd live ramp show` prints the same, read only. The Today `LiveTile` is a lazy chunk behind a wrapper of a few lines; it shows nothing without a manual-live account and a non-value is a word, never a digit.
10. **Every new setting defaults OFF or to the spec's number**; `live.auto_ramp_up` keeps its 04A row (OFF), auto step-up stops at S2, records `rule:ramp` with the settings snapshot, never counts an `unavailable` gate as a pass.

## Consequences
- With the capture and settle services running, an account's evidence builds from stored rows with no new job. The first raise needs 20 sessions at S1; until then every gate reads `insufficient` or `unavailable`, which is the safe state.
- A paused or failing capture shows up as `not_run`/`gap`: the loss is `unavailable`, the live-ready item fails, B5 holds the ramp after two bad sessions of five.
- `live_halts` is a fifth insert-only table of the live phase (`MakeAppendOnlyForAppRole`); the grants test lists it.
- Limits (stated on screen where they matter): the loss ignores carried positions, discretionary-only history and charges; the evaluation reads the sessions of the step (up to 120) with a few indexed reads each and was not timed against the 300 ms target; G3 pools all entries of one reference kind (not per bucket); real Dhan answers were never seen, so every figure is proved on hand-worked data.
- The weekly loss is the sum of each day's net (gains offset losses across the week), floored at 0; a day that cannot be measured is left out and the week reads `realised_only`.
- The 1 R of the daily and weekly halts uses the step scale only; the weekly-halve factor is not applied to it. So after a halve the halt limits are still measured in the full step's R, not the halved size.
- `LiveNotices` assesses every live account on each bell read (up to 120 sessions of indexed reads each); not timed. Upgrade to a short cache if the bell gets slow.
- Rejected: invalidating shared tickets on a halt; a hard block of fills (impossible: the owner places every order); a profit gate; closing halts by a second row; a system-only S3.

## Note 2026-10-29 (append-only, from P11-03 review)
`OneSidedT.Quantile95` used to take the next higher table row for a df between rows (df 10 gave 1.761, true 1.812), which made the G3 one-sided bound too narrow. It now takes the largest row at or below the df (df 19 is still 1.729, df 10 is 1.833, df 100 is 1.671, 1.645 from df 120): the bound is wider, so G3 is stricter. See ADR 0152.
