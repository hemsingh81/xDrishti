# 0166 - A confirm ticket is exact text the server wrote, decided once by a primary key and judged by the database clock, and a confirmation older than 30 seconds sends nothing

- **Status:** Accepted (decided in P12-08b under the owner's standing run instruction; spec [P12-08](../specs/P12-08-semi-auto-one-tap-confirm.md), criteria 1 to 12 and 17; the frontend, criteria 14 and 15, is a separate pass).
- **Date:** 2026-10-31.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** builds on ADR 0156 (schema `execution`, two runtime roles, insert-only by trigger), 0159 (P12-03's runs, Soft codes and `check_confirmations`), 0161 (halts; any active halt blocks), 0163 (the mode ladder: `semi_auto` is not storable, the mode source stays closed), 0164 (readiness; unavailable blocks like not ready) and 0165 (the intent creator and `ModeRouting`, composed nowhere). It weakens none of them.

## Context
In `semi_auto` the owner's tap is the manual approval of an order. A tap must confirm exactly what was shown, at most once, only while the card is fresh, and a confirmation must never lead to a send long after it was given. None of this may depend on the browser's clock or on a client-side debounce.

## Decision
1. **The server writes the text once.** `ConfirmText` (shared Domain, `XDrishti.Domain.Execution.Confirm`) renders the card from the stored parameters, culture-free, with Indian money grouping, prices at two to four decimals, the approval time to the minute and the expiry to the second (the parameters hold those resolutions), so the text is a one-to-one image of the parameters. `text_hash` is the SHA-256 of the text; a CHECK constraint recomputes it. The tap echoes the hash; a different hash stores nothing (`execution.text_changed`). While `exec.canary_pending` is ON the text says "canary: 1 share".
2. **The issuer** (`ConfirmTicketIssuer`, xd-exec, role xd_exec) takes a checking BUY intent of an account whose mode routes an entry to a tap, whose newest full P12-03 run passed every Hard check (cleared or Soft-only rejected), with a fresh last price as `ref_price`, the plan approval time and the entry's P12-01 client order id (attempt 1, also for a second ticket: nothing was sent for a refused one). `expires_at = issued_at + exec.confirm_ttl_s`. The password rule (`ConfirmPolicy.Password`): notional at least `exec.confirm_step_up_notional_pct` of a fixed capital, a Soft non-pass, or an unknown capital (only `capital_source = Fixed` counts as known; broker funds are not read). A second hand-over returns the current ticket; after a refusal, once, inside the entry window, a new ticket is issued (unique `(intent, attempt)`, attempt in 1..2, a trigger admits attempt 2 only after a refused 1). A fourth open ticket of an account is stored closed `refused` (`too_many_open`) in the same transaction. The issuer is composed by no host.
3. **One decision per ticket, by a primary key.** `ticket_outcomes.ticket_id` is the primary key: twenty parallel taps leave one row (the losers read it back and answer `alreadyDecided: true` with the same time), and a tap racing the expiry job leaves one row. The trigger `execution.check_ticket_outcome` stamps `decided_at` with `clock_timestamp()` and admits `confirmed` only from an `owner:` actor through the API role, before `expires_at`, with the switch ON, the effective mode `semi_auto` or `auto_live`, no active halt (system or account) and a valid day unlock, and with a reason when the ticket lists Soft codes; `skipped` only from the owner before expiry; `expired` only from the execution role at or after expiry; `refused` only from the execution role. A confirmed ticket with Soft codes writes P12-03's `check_confirmations` row (the codes, the reason, the actor) in the same transaction, through a SECURITY DEFINER trigger, so the runtime role gains no grant on the check record.
4. **The API judges first, the database again.** The tap reads the ticket, then one statement of facts (database clock, switch, effective mode, active halts, newest readiness of the session, the unlock, the last price, the intent's newest Soft set, the settings snapshot), then inserts. State refusals (`ticket_expired`, `disabled`, `mode_changed`, `halted`, `not_ready`, `not_unlocked`) leave the ticket open; a price refusal (`price_moved` beyond `exec.confirm_max_drift_pct` either way, `price_unavailable` when the price is missing or older than `exec.feed_max_age_s`) or a changed Soft set (`soft_set_changed`) leaves a `ticket_refuse` command row, and the execution service closes the ticket `refused` (the API may not write that outcome). The password, when the ticket asks for it, goes through the step-up handler without opening the elevation window and counts toward the lockout.
5. **The day unlock** is an owner act after a fresh password, for an account in `semi_auto` or `auto_live`, on a trading day by the database's India date; the trigger stamps the time, sets the expiry to 15:30 India time and refuses another date or a late unlock. It survives a locked session.
6. **Confirmed means sent within 30 seconds or not at all.** `IConfirmGate` (closed default `ClosedConfirmGate`, implementation `StoredConfirmGate`) opens only on a confirmed newest ticket whose decision is at most 30 seconds old by the database clock; `ConfirmHandOff` then moves the intent checking to submitting, or, for an older confirmation, checking to cancelled with reason `expired_after_confirm` (logged for the owner). Neither the stored gate nor the hand-off is composed; IConfirmGate is registered only as the closed default.
7. **What runs in xd-exec.** Only the safe direction: the expiry loop (every 2 s, `system:confirm-expiry`) and the `ticket_refuse` command (closing a ticket). Both write only `ticket_outcomes`.
8. **Grants.** xd_app: SELECT on the three tables, INSERT on `ticket_outcomes` and `day_unlocks` (its sequence). xd_exec: SELECT on the three, INSERT on `confirm_tickets` and `ticket_outcomes`, and SELECT on the three capital columns of `accounts.trading_settings`. No UPDATE, DELETE or TRUNCATE; the three tables are insert-only by trigger. `NOTIFY execution_ticket` (no payload) on every ticket and outcome.
9. **Settings.** `exec.confirm_ttl_s` 90 (15..300), `exec.confirm_max_drift_pct` 0.30 (0.05..1.00), `exec.confirm_step_up_notional_pct` 10 (1..100), all riskier when raised; the shared `ConfirmSettings` and the registry agree (a test pins it).
10. **Log ids.** 6716 issued, 6717 not issued, 6718 closed by the system, 6719 hand-off (what was left of 6710 to 6719 after P12-08a); the API and the host needed five more, taken from the next free block 6760 to 6769 (6760 expiry pass failed, 6761 decided, 6762 tap refused, 6763 unlocked, 6764 unlock refused). Hashes, codes and milliseconds only; never the password, the e-mail or the text.

## Deviations from the spec text
- The owner's reason for a Soft confirmation is stored on the outcome (`ticket_outcomes.note`, also the skip reason), not as `confirm_tickets.confirm_reason`: the ticket is insert-only and written before the tap. The confirm body gains an optional `reason`. `confirm_tickets` gains `check_run_id` (the run the Soft codes came from, for `check_confirmations`).
- The view gains `softCodes`, so the card can ask for the reason. New codes beyond the Contract: `execution.reason_required` (400), `execution.soft_set_changed` (409, named in the spec's risks), `execution.mode_not_executing`, `execution.not_trading_day`, `execution.unlock_closed` (409, day unlock), `execution.values_invalid` (400, including an Idempotency-Key that is not the ticket id).
- The tap and skip routes have their own limit of 60 per minute per client; the day unlock has the sign-in limit.
- Over HTTP only the refusal paths are tested: an intent references its account and the API suite's reset deletes accounts, while the execution record is insert-only. The tap's database path (twenty parallel taps, the boundary by the database clock, every trigger refusal) runs as the real roles in `ExecutionTicketsDatabaseTests`, on its own server, which writes one `semi_auto` trail row past the constraint as the owner and puts the constraint back.

## Not built, owed
The frontend card, hook and unlock dialog (criteria 14 and 15); composing the issuer, the stored gate and the hand-off (which waits for `semi_auto` to be storable and for P12-01's sender to be composed); P12-03 treating a Soft-only rejected run as non-final while a ticket is open; the poll fallback that finds confirmed tickets without a send beyond the hand-off itself.

## Consequences
A tap can only confirm the exact card the server wrote, once, while it is fresh by the database's clock, and only lead to a send within 30 seconds. Today no account can be `semi_auto`, the issuer and the hand-off are composed nowhere and the trigger refuses every confirmed row, so nothing new can send.

## Known limits (review 2026-10-31)
- The password is verified at the API only, after the state, text-hash and reason checks, so a wrong password counts toward the lockout only once those pass. The database trigger does not enforce `requires_password`; a client holding the `xd_app` credential could skip it. This matches the spec and is a known limit.
- A price or Soft-set refusal enqueues `ticket_refuse` before the ticket is re-read; a ticket skipped in a race gives a harmless failed refusal attempt (the primary key decides).
