# 0043 — The daily evaluation and the overnight entry order (`sim-5` and run-record schema 5)

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-18: "yes to all recommendations" on [P3-03e](../specs/P3-03e-the-daily-evaluation-and-the-overnight-entry-order.md), open questions 1 to 4 as recommended; the decisions the spec took on research when it was drafted — E8 for a 30m or 1h decision at 15:30 (open question 2), the four level stops lifted early (open question 3) and the pending order as an outcome of its own (open question 4) — are the owner's to revert). **Extends** [ADR 0038](0038-fill-realism-the-entry-limit-gaps-slippage-ticks-and-the-order-inside-a-minute.md) (the entry rows E0 to E7 gain E8 and E9) and [ADR 0040](0040-swing-positions-held-across-sessions-the-maximum-hold-the-position-gate-and-simtrade-schema-3.md) (whose decision 1 left a decision at the close of the session a `NoFillBar`); the rest of both stands. The replay rules of this slice are Amendment 7 of [ADR 0037](0037-simulation-conventions-and-the-two-stage-seam.md).
- **Relation to other ADRs:** builds on ADR 0037 (the two-stage seam), ADR 0038 (the entry limit E2 to E6, the five minutes), ADR 0040 (the swing walk, the gate, `SimTrade` schema 3) and [ADR 0042](0042-costs-and-sizing-the-charge-sheet-the-quantity-net-r-and-simtrade-schema-4.md) (costing after the window cut, which this slice leaves alone: the entry leg is charged on the fill date). It serves only P3-06 (`BASE_BREAKOUT`).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19 (built the same day). Written for the part B of the first draft of [P3-03d](../specs/P3-03d-swing-positions-held-across-sessions.md), split off by the coordinator.

## In short
- **An order can rest overnight (E8).** In a swing walk a decision at or after the end of its session (15:30, or the end of a special session, taken from the calendar) places its order for the **next trading session**. That is every decision of a 1D evaluation (taken at the step of the 15:29 minute) and the decision of the last 30m or 1h bucket. Under ADR 0040 such a candidate was a `NoFillBar`. An intraday walk never rests overnight.
- **It fills at the next session's open within the limit (E9).** The fill session is the next trading day by the calendar (the next weekday with none; an order rests over a holiday). The fill minute is the first minute that holds a trade on that date, at or after the session's open and at most `MaxFillDelayMinutes` (5) after it: 09:20 fills, 09:21 does not. E2 to E6 are the intraday rows with the trigger close of the daily bar: the limit is the close plus 0.1 % rounded down to the tick; an open at or inside it fills at the open (`AtOpen`), an open beyond it that trades back fills at the limit (`AtLimit`), one that does not is `LimitNotReached` (**a gap above the limit is a counted miss**), an open at or through the stop is `EntryThroughStop`. A minute of another date that is not the fill session ends the search with `NoFillBar`: a later session is never used.
- **An order still pending when the data ends is its own outcome (D3).** A candidate whose order rests overnight and that has no minute after its own is `NoTradeReason.OrderPendingAtEnd` (spelled `order_pending_at_end`), a fate (`RunFates.OrderPendingAtEnd`) and a counter (`WalkCounters.OrderPendingAtEnd`): a longer list may fill it, so a miss cannot be said. This keeps the last day of a window independent of how far the read goes (the prefix property of ADR 0040, D14).
- **The 1D evaluation is accepted; 1W and 1M stay refused.** `EntryScanner.Create` and `Simulator.Create` name only `1W` and `1M` (`simulation.scanner_timeframe_unsupported`). The frame closes the 1D bar at the step of the 15:29 minute; a bar closed late (the 15:29 minute missing) is stale, counted in `StaleBucketSteps` and never evaluated.
- **The four level stops run (D5).** `Simulator.Create` accepts `swing_low`, `swing_high`, `bar_low` and `bar_high` with a zero buffer on the evaluation timeframe, beside `or_opposite`. `fixed_pct`, `atr_mult`, a buffer and another stop timeframe stay refused by name (P3-05).
- **`SimTrade.Key` follows the decision date; the window cuts by it (D6).** Two trades of one instrument and side can have one fill date; the key uses the India date of the minute that closed the evaluation bar. For every intraday and every earlier swing trade the two dates are equal, so their key text is unchanged. `SimTrade` gains **no field**: its schema stays 4.
- **`sim-5` and run-record schema 5, `xd-run 5`.** `NoTradeReason`, `RunFates` and `WalkCounters` gain the pending order; `WalkCounters` gains `OvernightEntries`; the five golden files were regenerated a third time.

## Decisions
1. **E8 — when an order rests overnight.** `TradeWalker.Walk`, for a walk with a `SwingHold`: the minute of the day of the decision (the start of the candidate's own minute plus one) at or after `calendar.SessionOf(decision date).ToMinute` (15:30 with no calendar or for a date it does not know). The comparison uses the calendar the hold already carries; no new dependency.
2. **E9 — the fill session and the fill minute.** `SwingHold.NextSession` is the first trading day after the decision date within 30 calendar days (otherwise `simulation.rules_invalid`, like the due session). The five minutes are counted from the open of the fill session (`SessionOf(fill date).FromMinute`, 09:15 for a regular one), so a special session that opens at 10:00 allows 10:05. Minutes of the decision date that follow the candidate's own are ignored; the first minute of any other date that is not the fill date ends the search (`NoFillBar`); a minute with volume 0 is passed over and counted in `ZeroVolumeMinutes`. `EntriesAfterMissingMinutes` counts a fill later than the session's open; `OvernightEntries` counts every trade filled on a later date than its decision (`WalkOutcome.OvernightEntry`). `TradeDate` is the fill date and `DecisionEpochSeconds` stays on the decision date. The **due session of the hold is counted from the fill session** (session 1), so a hold of 2 sessions decided on Monday leaves at Wednesday's close. The 09:15 minute is the one with the auction price and the repaired range (ISS-18 (a), ISS-21): a daily strategy fills there by design.
3. **D3 — the pending order.** "Pending" means **the list ended while the order could still fill**: the search ran off the end of the list with no minute of another date, no minute past the five minutes and no fill (this includes a list that ends inside the fill session before any minute that holds a trade, so a list cut at 09:17 and one extended to 09:18 agree). The outcome is `OrderPendingAtEnd`, with `BarsExamined` 0. `NoFillBar` stays for a minute of another date that is not the fill session and for a minute past the five minutes (the mirror of N3 of ADR 0038 under an intraday walk). A pending order is not a trade and does not set the position gate. The P3-03d property D14 2(c) becomes: a trade of the second run whose decision date is on or before `To1` is, in the first run, a trade, an `OpenAtEnd` trade or an `OrderPendingAtEnd` candidate. The segment of the replay that ends before a flagged day hands the walker a list that ends at its last session: the order of that session is reported as **pending** although the next session exists in the data (the replay never lets a position or an order cross a flagged boundary). A special or Muhurat fill session is excluded from the bars the replay feeds, so in the replay its order is a `NoFillBar`; the walker unit tests cover special fill sessions.
4. **D4 — the daily evaluation.** The decision of a 1D strategy is taken at the step where the frame closes the 1D bar, the 15:29 minute, with the decision instant 15:30 and `DecisionBarIndex` the index of that minute. How often 15:29 is missing is UNVERIFIED (not measured) and each such day is a lost signal.
5. **D5 — the level stops** are lifted in `Simulator.Create` as the spec says (the scanner already read them); a high-level stop on an intraday SELL is accepted too, since the check is by kind, buffer and timeframe, not by style.
6. **D6 — `Key` and the cut.** `SimTrade.Key` is `StrategyId|Symbol|Side|` and the India date of `DecisionEpochSeconds − 60`. The replay cuts a trade by the same date (`CandidatesInWindow` and the suppression on a flagged day already used it), so `TradesInWindow ≤ CandidatesInWindow` at every window: a candidate decided on the Friday before `From` that fills on `From` is `TradesBeforeWindow`. `TradeDate` stays the fill date for costing (ADR 0042 charges the entry leg on it).
7. **D7 — `FillLimits.auction_open`** is "The first 1-minute open of the data can be the auction price. A daily strategy enters there, and a position held overnight that gaps through a stop or a target is filled in that minute (a stop at the open, a target at its level); a repaired opening bar has a wider range, so its slippage is larger, never smaller." Only this constant is edited; the codes are the ten of ADR 0040.
8. **D8 — versions and the third regeneration.** `SimulationEngine.Version` is `sim-5`; `SimulationRun.CurrentSchemaVersion` is 5 and the run key starts `xd-run 5`; `SimTrade.CurrentSchemaVersion` stays 4. `NoTradeReason.OrderPendingAtEnd`, `RunFates.OrderPendingAtEnd` (declared after `SkippedCost`), `WalkCounters.OvernightEntries` and `WalkCounters.OrderPendingAtEnd` are appended with default 0. The sim-4 renderings of the intraday fixtures (F, W, H, 500 seeded days) and of the rows of P3-03d (M, fixture R, Q1, G, J, Q2) were frozen **before the first change** (`Golden/*.sim-4.*`, never regenerated) and a test requires that they are byte-identical under `sim-5` in the sim-4 view (the engine label and the two new counters, when zero). Six golden files (walker, scanner, simulator, run record, costed and swing) were regenerated and the diff read: the `engine` label, the zero counters, in the run record the `schema=` number and the run-key hash, and in the simulator file one fixture: F22 (`STOP_NOT_READY_TEST`, a `swing_low` stop) was refused by name before and is run now.
9. **D9 — counters and fates.** `Candidates = Trades + NoFillBar + AfterSquareOff + EntryThroughStop + LimitNotReached + PositionOpen + OrderPendingAtEnd`; `OvernightEntries ≤ Trades`; `RunFates.Total` counts the new fate once (inside `NoTrade`); the reflection tests of the two `Add` methods cover the new properties. `ExportSpellings` spells the reason `order_pending_at_end`; the run JSON gains `overnight_entries` and `order_pending_at_end` in the walk counters and the fates (additive members: the format stays `xd-sim-export-1`).

## Decisions taken on research, not asked (the owner may revert any)
- **A 30m or 1h decision at 15:30 rests overnight** (E8), the same mechanism as the daily bar; the alternative was to leave the last bar's signals as misses.
- **The four level stops are lifted here**, not left to P3-05 (a daily strategy cannot use `or_opposite`).
- **A pending order is its own outcome**, not a `NoFillBar`.
- **A date before the fill date that has bars ends the search** (`NoFillBar`) instead of being skipped: the prefix property needs the answer not to depend on whether the fill session is present later.

## Consequences
- **Tests.** `XDrishti.Simulation.Tests` 917 (911 run, 6 explicit benchmarks), `XDrishti.Domain.Tests` 1,372, `XDrishti.SimExport.Tests` 177, `XDrishti.Application.Tests` 4,022, `XDrishti.Architecture.Tests` 228 (in Release: see below). The rows O1 to O9, O5b and the edge cases are in `OvernightEntryTests`; DF1 and DF2 in `DailyScannerTests`; the 12-session fixture, the window cut and the costing of a trade filled the day after its decision in `DailyReplayTests`; the seeded series, the prefix property and the look-ahead property in `DailySeededTests`; the frozen view in `Sim4ViewTests`.
- **The generator** (500 seeded daily series, 3,763 candidates) was measured on itself, not by a second implementation: 1,209 trades (1,198 at the open, 11 at the limit, 151 filled later than the open), 343 pending orders, 610 misses, 398 entries through the stop, 126 without a fill bar, 1,077 candidates blocked by the gate; the planted gap above the limit is in 93 series (spec 20 %), the gap through the stop in 44 (spec 10 %), a late first minute in 353 sessions. These replace the UNVERIFIED rates of the spec.
- **The properties and their controls.** The entry of an overnight order is settled by the minutes up to the fill minute and by nothing after it: over 3,263 settled candidates the entry-side fields are identical when the later minutes are removed and when they are replaced by extreme values (0 failures), while a wrapper that lets the limit use the high of the fill session fails on 114 of 500 series (the spec asks 10). The prefix property holds over 3,000 cuts, half of them in the middle of a session (10,409 comparisons, 899 of them pending orders; 0 violations) and the control that reads a pending order as a `NoFillBar` violates it on 452 cuts. The control is a relabelling of the pending outcome, not a second walker.
- **Architecture tests need a Release build.** In a Debug build they report violations for every type of every library (the compiler adds `System.Diagnostics` attributes); `scripts/check.sh` builds and tests in Release, where they pass. It also fails for the libraries this slice does not touch (Strategies, Indicators), so it is not caused by it.
- **Handed over.** To P3-04b1/c: `order_pending_at_end` is spelled and the run JSON has the two new members (the trades CSV has no new column: `SimTrade` is unchanged); the `Key` of a trade in `trades.csv` is not a column, but the run key it carries is the new one. To P3-05 and P3-06: the level stops run on any evaluation timeframe up to 1D; the fixture of criterion 10 (`DailyFixture`, `SWING_DAILY_TEST`) is what P3-06 extends; `rel_volume`, `tf("1W")` and a `swing_low` stop with `lookback = base_len` are read by the compiler and the scanner, not run by this slice (UNVERIFIED until its spec). To the roadmap and ISS-18 (a): the sentence about a daily strategy filling at 09:15 by design is in `FillLimits` and docs 07 and 08; the roadmap file is the coordinator's.
- **Limits that stay.** A missing 15:29 minute loses the day's signal (rate not measured). A limit of the close plus 0.1 % misses most gap-up opens: the number of misses is a counter, not a tuned figure. The fill is in the auction minute (ISS-18 (a)). Tickets that live across bars (`stop_above_high`, `limit_pullback`) are still refused. A read past `To` does not exist, so the last order of a window is pending, never a trade.

## Alternatives considered
- **Count the pending order as a `NoFillBar`.** Simpler, and the last day of every window would then depend on how far the read goes: a trade appears when the window grows. That is what window independence forbids.
- **Fill an overnight order at the open of whatever session comes next in the list.** A missing session would then shift the fill by days; a price of a day nobody asked about is not a fill.
- **Skip bars of a non-session date before the fill date.** Would make `NoFillBar` depend on a later date that may not be in the data yet.
- **Give `SimTrade` a `DecisionDate` field.** Would have raised the schema for a value that is computed from a field it already holds.
