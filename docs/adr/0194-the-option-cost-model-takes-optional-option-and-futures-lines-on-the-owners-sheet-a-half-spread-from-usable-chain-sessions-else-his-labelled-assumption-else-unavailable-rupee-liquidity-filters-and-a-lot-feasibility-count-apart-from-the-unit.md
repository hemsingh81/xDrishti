# 0194 — The option cost model takes optional option and futures lines on the owner's sheet, a half-spread from usable chain sessions (else his labelled assumption, else unavailable), rupee liquidity filters and a lot-feasibility count apart from the unit

- **Status:** Proposed (built in P14-07 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: yes** in the plan's sense: it asks the owner for his rates and assumptions (never invented in `src/`) and it adds a second reader of `options.chain_snapshots`. No network, no order, no setting that sends.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0042](0042-costs-and-sizing-the-charge-sheet-the-quantity-net-r-and-simtrade-schema-4.md) and [0044](0044-the-cost-choice-the-stored-charge-sheet-the-cost-sheet-verbs-and-the-bill-check.md) (the sheet, its canonical text and hashes; "nothing in `src/` holds a rate"), [0176](0176-the-option-chain-recorder-stores-five-minute-index-chains-and-a-daily-stock-pass-from-two-new-allow-listed-data-api-endpoints-behind-three-gates-its-own-cap-and-no-reader.md) (consequence: P14-07 reads half-spreads only after 60 sessions and only through a reader slice of its own), [0178](0178-the-option-archive-quality-checks-read-the-recorded-chains-and-rolling-bars-in-bounded-ranges-and-store-insert-only-verdicts-with-an-honest-unavailable-and-no-trading-use.md) (usable chain sessions; its per-table reader pins are changed here by exactly one file), [0191](0191-the-option-pricing-core-is-a-pure-black-76-library-with-fixed-units-a-closed-list-of-unavailable-reasons-and-no-reader-yet.md) (the delta that the lot count reads is the caller's; this slice does not reference the pricing core).

## Decision
1. **Optional segments on the existing sheet.** `CostSegment` gains `Options` and `Futures`; `ChargeSchedule` gains optional `Options` and `Futures` rates (`null` = the owner gave none); the canonical text writes `"options"` then `"futures"` after `"delivery_equity"` only when present, so the canonical text and `RatesSha256` of every sheet without them are byte-identical (a golden test pins the TEST and fixture sheets). A JSON `null` for either is refused (never read as "none" or as zero rates). `CostModel.Leg` for a segment with no rates fails with `costs.costs_unavailable`; `OptionLegCost` then leaves the leg gross, labelled `costs: none (no option lines)`. Rejected: a second "derivatives sheet" (two effective-date chains to keep in step).
2. **Consequences for existing code, stated.** (a) `CostSegment` is also the type of the stored `cost_segment` column of `research.sim_trades`; its CHECK is generated from the enum, so the migration widens it to the two new words (the replay writes neither: a cash trade is `IntradayEquity` or `Delivery`). (b) **Recorded deviation from acceptance criterion 1 ("every existing cost test is green unmodified")**: growing the enum and the generated check changed what some existing tests pin, so these were edited, each only for that reason: `SimTradeSchemaTests` (the enum-members pin lists four members), `CostModelTests` (one property loop restricted to the two cash segments; the TEST sheet has no option lines), `RunStoreReadTests` (a row that used `futures` as an invalid segment now uses `swing`), and the lists extended for the new tables and namespace: `ResearchStoreMigrationTests`, `ResearchGrantsTests` (table count 22 to 24), `DomainResearchSurfaceTests`, `ResultStoreArchitectureTests`, `ExecGrantsTests`, `ReadOnlyRolesTests`. No existing assertion about a cash segment, a hash or a canonical text was changed; `CostSheetGoldenTests` pins those. `ExportSpellings` names the new members `options` and `futures`.
3. **Half-spread, measured.** For an underlying with at least `Options:Costs:MinUsableSessions` (60) usable chain sessions (coverage `pass`, parity `pass` or `warn`, [0178](0178-the-option-archive-quality-checks-read-the-recorded-chains-and-rolling-bars-in-bounded-ranges-and-store-insert-only-verdicts-with-an-honest-unavailable-and-no-trading-use.md)), the model holds the median of `(ask - bid) / 2 / mid` in percent over two-sided, uncrossed quotes, per bucket: moneyness (strike steps from the snapshot's ATM: 0, 1-2, 3-5, 6-10), days to expiry (0-2, 3-7, 8-30, 31+), India time of day (09:20-10:00, 10:00-14:30, 14:30-15:30). The ATM of a snapshot (one instant and expiry) is the strike nearest the underlying's price (the lower on a tie) and a step is the smallest gap between that snapshot's strikes; a snapshot with one strike, a quote outside every bucket, a one-sided, crossed or locked (bid equal to ask: it would read as a zero spread) quote are counted as left out, never used. A bucket with fewer than `MinQuotesPerBucket` (200) quotes uses the pool with the time-of-day split merged (stored with `tod = all`), then the pool with the moneyness split merged too (`moneyness = all, tod = all`); still short, it has **no row**: absent is `unavailable`, never a number. The median of an even count is the mean of the two middle values.
4. **Content address.** `model_id` is the SHA-256 of `model_version` (1; also stored in the settings JSON, so a later fix to the algorithm raises it and the same sessions are rebuilt under a new id), `underlying id`, the sorted session dates used, `MinUsableSessions` and `MinQuotesPerBucket`. The handler looks the id up **before** reading any quote: a rebuild with the same inputs reads nothing and writes nothing; a new usable session is a new model (the old ones stay; the newest by last session is the one a lookup loads).
5. **Reader discipline (the deliberate change to [0178](0178-the-option-archive-quality-checks-read-the-recorded-chains-and-rolling-bars-in-bounded-ranges-and-store-insert-only-verdicts-with-an-honest-unavailable-and-no-trading-use.md) decision 8).** `IChainSpreadReader.QuotesAsync(underlyingId, session)` is the one method of the second reader of `options.chain_snapshots`: one `(underlying_id, ts)` range of one India day, six columns (`ts`, `expiry`, `strike_px`, `underlying_px`, `bid_px`, `ask_px`), no IV, Greek, open interest, volume or flag, no write. Only `BuildSpreadModelHandler` calls it, and only for sessions that `SessionUsability.IsUsable` accepted from the quality results (a fake reader that records the dates asked proves no unusable session is read). The per-table pins of `OptionQualityArchitectureTests` and `OptionChainRecordingArchitectureTests` change by exactly `ChainSpreadReader.cs` in the `chain_snapshots` lists; the quality namespaces' "no other type names a quality type" rule gets one name, `BuildSpreadModelHandler`, which asks the quality results which sessions are usable. Nothing else changes in those tests.
6. **Half-spread, assumed.** Before 60 usable sessions, or for an unavailable bucket, the half-spread is the owner's `Options:Costs:AssumedHalfSpreadPct` for the underlying (keyed by the short symbol, `NIFTY`: the exchange prefix `NSE:` would break the configuration path), labelled `spread: assumed x % by owner`; else `unavailable` (`spread_unavailable`). The setting is empty by default and no shipped settings file holds it; bounds 0.01 to 20 percent.
7. **Past dates.** A measured model used for a date before its first session is flagged `spread_from_recent_archive` (the field `FromRecentArchive` of the value; P14-09 counts it per trade). The label of a measured spread is `spread: measured (n sessions, from <first> to <last>)`.
8. **Cost of a leg.** `OptionLegCost.Evaluate` returns the eight charge lines when the sheet has the segment (else none, with the reason code and the gross label: `costs: none (no option lines)`, or `costs: none (no schedule in force on the leg's date)` when the sheet has option lines but the date is before its first schedule), the spread cost `round(turnover x half-spread / 100, 2)` when the spread is available (else none), and a total **only when the spread is available**: an unavailable spread is never added as zero. The charges of a leg without option lines count as gross in that total, and the label says so; P14-09 compares like with like.
9. **Liquidity.** Open interest x spot at least `Options:Liquidity:MinOiNotionalCr` (5 crore) and the entry bar's volume x spot at least `MinBarTurnoverLakh` (25 lakh), both UNVERIFIED starting values. A null open interest or volume, or a spot not above zero, is `liquidity_unknown`; `default` is `Unknown`, never a pass.
10. **Lot feasibility.** `loss_per_lot = lot x |delta| x |entry - stop|` against the cash trade's risk `qty x |entry - stop|` (fractional quantity, computed whatever the verdict): above it is `not_sizable`; a lot that `LotSizeLookup` cannot give is `lot_unknown`; a delta that is not a number or whose absolute value exceeds 1 is `delta_unavailable` (no overflow on an absurd value); a stop at the entry or a quantity not above zero is `risk_invalid` (two additions to the spec's closed list, both "never sizable"). A lot that is not confirmed carries `lot: unconfirmed`.
11. **Storage.** Migration `AddOptionSpreadModels`: `research.option_spread_models` and `research.option_spread_buckets` (raw SQL; nothing maps them in the EF model), granted per table with `GrantTableToAppRole` then `MakeAppendOnlyForAppRole` (never `GrantSchemaUsageToAppRole`, which would give back UPDATE and DELETE on the append-only tables of `research`); `xd_exec` and the read-only roles hold nothing. `Down` drops both and narrows the `sim_trades` CHECK again. `half_spread_pct` is stored for a bucket that reached its count only.
12. **No invented rate.** No source file builds `ChargeRates` from literals (only `CostSheetJson` calls `ChargeRates.Create`); the option cost types hold no fractional literal but the percent scale of the accumulator; the settings default of `AssumedHalfSpreadPct` is an empty dictionary and no `appsettings*.json` names it. An architecture test pins all of that, and that no decision, live, recorder, execution or other assembly references a cost type.
13. **Verb.** `xd options spread-model build [--underlying SYMBOL]` (the symbol is normalised as the coverage route does, via the shared `UnderlyingSymbol.Normalize`: `nifty` and `NIFTY` mean `NSE:NIFTY`): for each underlying with recorded chains (or the one named) it prints one line, `built`, `already_built` or `not_built` with the reason (`sessions 59 of 60`), exit 0 in all three; exit 2 for a bad command line or an unknown symbol; 3 on failure; 130 on cancel. It is not a scheduled service.

## Consequences
- P14-08, P14-10 and P14-15 can cost an option leg on its own date with one tested path and read its labels; none of them may reference the cost types until their own slice loosens the architecture test with an exact allow-list.
- Without the owner's option lines every option result is gross and says so; without his assumption or 60 usable sessions the option verdict is `NOT_EVALUABLE` (`spread_unavailable`).

## Known limits
- `shortcut:` the stored text of a sheet is bounded at 8,000 characters (`CostErrors.MaxCanonicalLength`, equal to `AppSetting.MaxValueLength` and the column width of every app setting). A schedule with the option and futures lines is about 940 characters, so such a sheet holds about seven schedules, not twelve; `CostSheet.Create` refuses a larger one with `costs.sheet_too_long` (a test pins it). The bound is not raised here: it would need a column change for every setting, and an existing sheet's text must not move. Enter only the schedules that differ.
- A measured spread used for a date before the model's first session carries `; spread_from_recent_archive` in its label as well as the flag.
- The spread is measured in 2026-27 and applied to 2021-26 (flagged). Vendor bid and ask are top-of-book snapshots every five minutes; a fill larger than the top quantity costs more (not modelled).
- The ATM step is the smallest gap among the snapshot's strikes; a snapshot whose neighbouring strikes are missing may overstate the step (the bucket then reads nearer the ATM than it is).
- The build holds the half-spreads of the sessions in memory (a few million numbers for a full set of index sessions); the 2-minute build time for 250 sessions is UNVERIFIED and ungated.
- Rate sources named in P14-00 (STT on premium, futures STT) are secondary and appear only as examples in the design text.
