# 0040 — Swing positions: held across sessions, the maximum hold, the position gate and `SimTrade` schema 3

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-18: "yes to all recommendations" on [P3-03d](../specs/P3-03d-swing-positions-held-across-sessions.md), open questions 1 to 5 as recommended; the decisions the spec took on research when it was drafted — *Decisions taken on research* below — are the owner's to revert). **Supersedes decision 8 of [ADR 0038](0038-fill-realism-the-entry-limit-gaps-slippage-ticks-and-the-order-inside-a-minute.md)** (`SimTrade` schema 2) and **extends its decisions 2 (the exit table), 9 (the counters) and 11 (`FillLimits`)**; the rest of ADR 0038 and all of [ADR 0037](0037-simulation-conventions-and-the-two-stage-seam.md) stand. ADR 0038 carries a dated pointer to this one and its decisions are not edited. The replay rules of this slice are Amendment 4 of ADR 0037.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** builds on ADR 0037 (the two-stage seam: the scanner makes candidates without an exit, the walker walks one), ADR 0038 (the rule table X1 to X6 and S1 to S4, which a swing walk reuses row by row) and [ADR 0036](0036-the-typed-strategy-model-and-the-compile-of-its-sections.md) (the compile already holds `max_hold_sessions` and refuses swing SELL).
- **Date:** 2026-10-18 (built the same day)
- **Pointer (2026-10-19):** the entry of a candidate decided at or after the end of its session (a `NoFillBar` here) is changed by [ADR 0043](0043-the-daily-evaluation-and-the-overnight-entry-order-sim-5-and-run-record-schema-5.md): the order rests overnight and fills at the next session's open within the limit (E8, E9). The decisions above are not edited.

## In short
- **A swing walk is the ADR 0038 walk without the end of the day.** A `WalkRules` that carries a `SwingHold` (the longest hold in sessions, 1 to 5, and the trading calendar that counts them) walks the minutes of as many sessions as it takes. The rows X2 to X5 (stop, target, stop first, the fill minute of a limit entry) and S1 to S4 apply to every minute that holds a trade on every date, written once in one routine that the intraday loop and the swing loop both call. There is no square-off (X7).
- **The maximum hold leaves at a close (X8).** The entry session is session 1; the due session is N − 1 trading days later by the calendar (a special session counts, a holiday does not; with no calendar every weekday is a session). A due minute that holds a trade and reached neither level leaves at its **close** with reason `MaxHold`; a due minute that holds no trade leaves at the close of the last minute that does (`MaxHoldAtLastClose`, counted). The data ending first is `EndOfData` and means exactly that the list ended (X9).
- **The overnight gap is the rule of ADR 0038, not a new one.** A stop that the market opens through fills at the open; a target fills at the target and never better. Both are counted (`OvernightStopGaps`, `OvernightTargetGaps`).
- **One open position per instrument and side (D11).** A small internal `PositionGate` sits between the scanner and the walks: a candidate decided at or before the hold end of the last accepted trade, or after a trade that ended with the data, is not walked and is counted `PositionOpen`. The candidates stay exit-independent, so the same candidates can be walked under any exit policy.
- **`SimTrade` schema 3 and `sim-3`.** Two fields appended (`SessionsHeld`, `SessionsUnseen`), `ExitReason.MaxHold` and `NoTradeReason.PositionOpen` appended, seven counters and two fates appended, run-record schema 3, run key `xd-run 3`. An intraday trade does not change by one byte in the schema-2 view.

## Decisions
1. **D1 and X7 — the swing walk.** A walk is a swing walk exactly when `WalkRules.Hold` is set. Its entry rows E0 to E7 are those of the intraday walk, on the decision's date: a decision at the close of the session (15:30) is a `NoFillBar` (the daily evaluation and the overnight entry order are P3-03e). `stop` and `target` are fixed at the entry, never rounded, never moved; MAE and MFE are cumulative over every minute held, from the exact entry; a minute with volume 0 holds no trade on every date. `Hold` together with a square-off other than `WalkRules.NoSquareOff` (0), or no `Hold` with a square-off outside 1 to 1439, is `simulation.rules_invalid`. **Structure:** `TradeWalker` keeps the entry and the intraday loop; the per-minute decision is `MinuteRule.Decide`; the swing loop is the internal `SwingWalk`; `WalkResults` builds the trade and the no-trade. The frozen schema-2 view (D9) is the net that proves the extraction changed no intraday byte.
2. **D3 — counting sessions.** With a calendar a session is a date for which `IsTradingDay` is true (a special session or a Saturday session included); the due session `T` is reached from the entry date by moving forward N − 1 trading days; `SessionsHeld` is `1 + TradingDaysBetween(entry date, exit date)`; the window of the due session is its own (`ToMinute − 1` is the due minute). With no calendar the default week is used: a holiday is counted in N and is unseen, and the result differs from the one with the calendar (K3 against K4). A search that finds no trading day in 30 calendar days is `simulation.rules_invalid`; `SwingHold.Create` refuses N outside 1 to 5 as a second net behind the compile.
3. **D4 and X8 — `MaxHold`.** The stop and the target are checked in the due minute first (a stop touched at 15:29 is a `Stop`). Otherwise the exit is the close of the due minute, a market-type fill that pays the base slippage with no extra ticks (**S5**: `(Ticks × tick) + BarRangeFrac × range` of the exit minute, rounded `tickDown` for a long). A due minute that holds no trade, or a due session with no minute at all, makes the position leave at the close of the last minute that holds a trade on the last date that has one, on or before `T`, counted in `MaxHoldAtLastClose`; this is decided the moment the walker reads the start of the first minute of a later date (or of a minute of `T` after the due minute) and reads nothing else of it. A special session that the replay never feeds counts toward N, so a position whose due session is one leaves one session short, counted. A maximum is a maximum: leaving at a known close is the safe reading; waiting for the next open would hold through a session nobody can see.
4. **D5 and X9 — the end of the data.** A list that ends while the position is open and before the due minute exits at the close of the last minute that holds a trade, `EndOfData`, a market-type fill. For a swing trade this means exactly that the list ended (a list that ends at 15:29 with a minute that holds a trade is a `MaxHold`; with an empty 15:29 it is `EndOfData`). It is data and not a rule: the replay lists it in `OpenAtEnd`.
5. **D6 — the overnight gap.** A stop (BUY) is `low ≤ stop`, exit `min(stop, open)`: a session that opens at or below the stop exits at the open, the whole gap paid. A target exits at the target, never at a better price, also when the session opens beyond it. This keeps `R` of a `Target` exit exactly `k`, keeps the model from being more generous than the market, and is measured instead of guessed (`OvernightTargetGaps`); a real limit order that is gapped over fills at the open, so a swing result is somewhat **low** on gap-up winners (a decision taken on research; changing it is a rule change, a new engine version and a regenerated golden file). `OvernightStopGaps` and `OvernightTargetGaps` count the gap exits whose exit minute is the first that holds a trade on a date later than the entry date.
6. **D7 — sessions the walker never sees.** An *unseen* session is a calendar session between the entry date and the exit date on which the walker read no minute that holds a trade (a halt, or a session the replay excluded as special, short or missing). The stop and the target were not tested in it; the first minute after it is. It is **counted, not excluded**: `SessionsUnseen = max(0, SessionsHeld − sessions seen)` on the trade and its sum in the counters. A split or bonus in the applied actions makes the series continuous; one that is not, and moves less than 20 %, is a gap like any other. **Dividends are neither adjusted nor received** (a limit, below).
7. **D8 — R, MAE, MFE and the time in the trade.** As ADR 0038 D5b, with the exit rules per exit: after a `Stop` the exit's own distance counts as adverse, after a `Target` MFE is `k`, a minute that opens beyond the level it exits at contributes no range, after `MaxHold` and `EndOfData` the last minute counts in full; `−MAE ≤ RExact ≤ MFE`. A gap through a stop gives `RExact < −1`. `SessionsHeld` is the time in the trade: 1 for an intraday trade and for a swing trade that leaves on its entry date.
8. **D9 — `SimTrade` schema 3 (supersedes decision 8 of ADR 0038).** `SimTrade.CurrentSchemaVersion` is 3 and the 30 positional properties of schema 2 keep their order and meaning; appended: #31 `SessionsHeld` (`int`, ≥ 1) and #32 `SessionsUnseen` (`int`, ≥ 0). `ExitReason` gains `MaxHold` and `NoTradeReason` gains `PositionOpen`, both **at the end**; enum members are stored by name. For every trade of a walk without `Hold`: `SessionsHeld` 1, `SessionsUnseen` 0. `SimulationEngine.Version` is `sim-3`; the roadmap's merge order is P3-03d, then P3-03c (schema 4, `sim-4`), then P3-03e (the next free numbers). **Regeneration:** the schema-2 renderings were frozen **before any code changed** (`walker.sim-2.frozen.txt`, `hrows.sim-2.frozen.txt`, `seeded-intraday.sim-2.sha256.txt`, never regenerated and not read by `GoldenFile.Check`), then the golden files were rewritten (four changed: walker, scanner, simulator and run record; the definition text of ORB is unchanged) and the diff read: the first line, the `schema=` number, the `engine=` label, the run-key hash, the two fields, the new counters and fates, nothing else. A test renders the intraday fixtures (F1 to F17b, W1 to W14, H1 to H25 with and without slippage, 500 seeded days) in the **schema-2 view** (a formatter that prints `schema=2` and the 30 fields and 20 counters of schema 2 and nothing else) and compares them with the frozen files: an intraday trade is byte-identical under `sim-3`.
9. **D10 — counters (extends decision 9 of ADR 0038).** `WalkCounters` gains, appended with default 0: `PositionOpen`, `ExitsMaxHold`, `MaxHoldAtLastClose`, `OvernightStopGaps`, `OvernightTargetGaps`, `SessionsHeld` (the sum over the trades), `SessionsUnseen` (the sum); `WalkOutcome` gains `MaxHoldAtLastClose`, `OvernightStopGap`, `OvernightTargetGap` and `HoldEndEpochSeconds`. Identities: `Candidates = Trades + NoFillBar + AfterSquareOff + EntryThroughStop + LimitNotReached + PositionOpen`; `Trades = ExitsStop + ExitsTarget + ExitsSquareOff + ExitsMaxHold + ExitsEndOfData`; the overnight gaps are subsets of the gap exits; `MaxHoldAtLastClose ≤ ExitsMaxHold`; `SessionsHeld ≥ Trades`; for an intraday run `SessionsHeld = Trades`, `SessionsUnseen = 0` and every other new counter is 0. A gate-blocked candidate adds `Candidates` 1 and `PositionOpen` 1 and nothing else. A reflection test checks that `Add` of both records sums every property.
10. **D11 — the position gate.** In `Simulator.Run`, per side, over the candidates in decision order, after the scanner and between two walks (the row said "after walking"; the gate needs the previous exit, so the walks are sequential and a blocked candidate is simply not walked, which gives the same result). Let `E` be the *hold end* of the last accepted trade: its exit instant, **except** for a `MaxHoldAtLastClose` trade, whose `E` is the **end of its due session** (`ToMinute(T)`); a candidate with decision instant `D` is **blocked** when `D ≤ E`, or when the last accepted trade is `EndOfData`. Why: a short run and a longer one must block the same candidates (a candidate that is `PositionOpen` in one run cannot be a trade in the other: the property of D14 2(d)), and a position whose due minute is missing is `EndOfData` in the short run and `MaxHoldAtLastClose` in the long one. The gate comes before the walk, so a candidate that would have been a `LimitNotReached` is a `PositionOpen`. It applies to **swing walks only** (`Simulator.HoldsOvernight`); intraday is not gated and the test pins `PositionOpen` 0 on every intraday run. A segment restart starts an empty gate. **Named limit 1 — the gate models the signal's position, not the account's:** a swing trade that P3-03c skips for quantity or cost still blocks the next candidates (the coordinator's decision, reversible; the pinning test G6 is written by the slice that merges second). **Named limit 3 — a candidate suppressed by a flagged day still occupies the gate of its segment (a decision of the coordinator after the review, the owner may reverse it):** the new segment's scanner makes a candidate on the flagged day itself, the swing walk enters it, the replay drops its outcome as `EntriesSuppressed`, and the gate has already accepted it, so the next N − 1 sessions' candidates of that segment are `PositionOpen` although the position was never taken (same family as limit 1: the gate models the strategy's position, and the strategy's candidate counts). Pinned by a replay test: Monday flat at 100, Tuesday opens at 60 (flagged) and breaks out at 11:15, Wednesday breaks out at 11:15 (N = 3): suppressed 1, position open 1, trades 0, open at the end 0; without Tuesday's breakout: position open 0, open at the end 1. The gate is not changed.  **Named limit 2 — a blocked signal spends the day:** the scanner makes at most one candidate per date and side and does not know the gate, so a blocked candidate still spends the date and a valid signal later that day is a `LaterTrigger`, never a candidate (pinned by a test).
11. **D12 — an intraday trade is flat at square-off and nothing about it changes.** `TradeWalker.Walk` with no `Hold` runs the rules of ADR 0038 as they stand; a property over 500 seeded days pins that every exit is on the entry date and none is `MaxHold`.
12. **D13 — the replay (Amendment 4 of ADR 0037).** For a swing strategy (`Simulator.HoldsOvernight`), an `EndOfData` trade of any segment that a flagged day follows is **excluded and counted** (`RunFates.ExcludedAcrossDiscontinuity`, the totals, and `DiscontinuityNote.ExcludedPositions` for that day); it appears in neither `Trades` nor `OpenAtEnd`. The `EndOfData` trade of the last segment stays `OpenAtEnd`. A swing trade belongs to the window by the date of its entry, as an intraday trade; **a trade still open when the read ends at `To` is `EndOfData` and goes to `OpenAtEnd`** (strict at `To`: the last N − 1 sessions of a window cannot show trades that need longer than the data). Fates in order: dropped by a flagged day, a trade before the window, a swing trade excluded across a flagged boundary, an `EndOfData` trade, another trade, a walker no-trade by reason (`PositionOpen` included); the fields are appended at the end of `RunFates` with default 0. The record is schema 3 and the run key starts `xd-run 3`; the key already carries the engine version and the calendar's exceptions, so a run that counted a holiday differently has another key.
13. **D14 — window independence for multi-day trades.** For any two windows over the same stored bars: (1) `From1 < From2` — the trades, fates and counters of the second are those of the first with the trades dated before `From2` moved into `TradesBeforeWindow`, every other trade byte-identical, every `PositionOpen` the same; (2) `To1 < To2` — every trade of the first run is a trade of the second, byte-identical; every open trade of the first is in the second as a trade, as open, or as excluded, with the same entry-side fields and an exit not before; a trade of the second dated up to `To1` is in the first as a trade or open; a candidate that is `PositionOpen` in the first run is `PositionOpen` in the second. Nothing after `To` decides a trade closed before it, and a trade that needs the future is reported as open, not guessed.
14. **D15 — `FillLimits` (extends decision 11 of ADR 0038).** The text of `auction_open` is corrected ("A position held overnight that gaps through a stop or a target is filled in that minute (a stop at the open, a target at its level), and a repaired opening bar has a wider range, so its slippage is larger, never smaller"); one code is added at the end, `dividends_not_modelled` ("Cash dividends are neither adjusted in the prices nor received: a position held over an ex-date shows the price drop without the dividend"). `FillLimits.NotModelled` holds ten codes in that order. This slice edits `auction_open` and adds the code; the text of `touch_is_fill` is P3-04b1's alone. ISS-18 (a) and (e) are reworded and the new limit is added.
15. **`Simulator.Create`.** The swing style is run (it was refused by `simulation.unsupported_style`, which is retired), for the evaluation timeframes 30m and 1h, BUY only (swing SELL is refused by the compile). Refused by name as before: an overlay, a filter, a market-context or expiry read, `stop_above_high`, `limit_pullback`, every stop but `or_opposite` with no buffer read on the evaluation timeframe, several `rr` values, 1D and longer (P3-03e). A side that cannot be built always gives a problem: the path in which a side with a null square-off yielded no simulator and no problem is closed.

## Decisions taken on research, not asked (the owner may revert any)
- **A position open across a flagged day is excluded and counted** (already decided by P3-04); the cost is the owner's to know: a real crash beyond 20 % is left out of the results (SIEMENS 2025-04-07, TMPV 2025-10-14, VEDL 2026-04-30 of ISS-20, none checked for a missing adjustment).
- **The `MaxHold` exit is the close of the session's last 1-minute bar**, the idealisation of ISS-18 (e), with the base slippage.
- **An `EndOfData` swing trade blocks every later candidate in its list, and a `MaxHoldAtLastClose` trade holds its instrument until the end of its due session.**
- **The gate models the signals' positions; a sizing or cost skip still blocks, and a blocked signal spends the day.**
- **Dividends are not modelled.**

## Consequences
- **Tests** (`XDrishti.Simulation.Tests`, 811 in all after this slice, `XDrishti.Domain.Tests`, `XDrishti.Architecture.Tests`). The hand rows M1 to M7b, K1 to K7, G1 to G6 (G2b included), J1 to J4, Q1 and Q2 equal the table of the spec to the figure; the overnight shift invariance holds on 17 H rows; the schema-2 view equals the three frozen files; the gate equals a plain reference loop on 500 seeded multi-day series; a closed trade does not depend on the minutes after it (500 series, 2,000 and more cuts) while three negative controls fail (a walker that peeks at the next session, caught on 233 of 500 series; a calendar read off the list, 113 of 500; a gate that ends a position at its last print instead of its due session, 18 of 500); the window properties of D14 hold over 100 seeded multi-day instruments with flagged nights, and their two controls fail (a scanner that starts at `From`, 57 of 100; a gate that starts empty at `From`, 75 of 100). The generator reaches every counter of criterion 24 well above the 10 asked (over 500 series and 1,058 candidates: 309 `MaxHold` exits of which 101 at the last close, 83 overnight stop gaps, 80 overnight target gaps, 283 end-of-data exits, 32 ambiguous minutes, 343 position-open candidates through the gate, 32 trades with unseen sessions). The generator was checked by measuring the code itself, not by a second implementation: that remains a limit.
- **Performance.** The explicit benchmarks `Swing_walk_throughput_for_holds_of_one_and_five_sessions_is_reported` and `Walk_throughput_over_250_sessions_is_reported` now warm up for one second and report the median of nine timed runs (Apple M4 Pro, Release): about 10.8 million visited minutes per second for a hold of 1 session and 10.5 million for a hold of 5 (41 walks), against 13.7 million (exact) and 13.6 million (modelled) for the intraday walker in the same run. An earlier cold figure of 2.0 and 4.0 million was a JIT artefact and is withdrawn; the independent review measured, warm, 13.1 million intraday against 12.9 million on the base code (no regression). A walk allocates the same number of bytes for a hold of 1 and of 5 sessions (asserted); the gate skips the walks of blocked candidates.
- **Handed over.** To P3-03c (merges next): `SimTrade` schema 3 is its base (it appends `Costing` as the 33rd field and takes schema 4 and `sim-4`); it can rely on `SessionsHeld`, `TradeDate` (the entry date) and the date of `ExitEpochSeconds`, and on `Simulator.HoldsOvernight` for the cost segment; it appends `SkippedQuantity` and `SkippedCost` to `RunFates` and writes the test G6. To P3-03e: the next free engine and run-record numbers (expected `sim-5` and 5); its `SimTrade` gains no field; it changes the `Key` to the decision date and adds the order that rests overnight. To P3-04b1, P3-04b2 and P3-04c: `ExportSpellings` needs `max_hold` for `ExitReason.MaxHold` and `position_open` for `NoTradeReason.PositionOpen` (their reflection and enum-spelling tests go red on purpose until they are written); `trades.csv` needs `sessions_held` and `sessions_unseen`; P3-04c derives its strata from the `ExitReason` enum, so `MaxHold` appears in its selection without an edit. To P3-08: the rows M, K and G of the spec are the input of the oracle, with the swing categories (a `MaxHold` exit, a gap exit across a night, a halted session, a position excluded at a flagged day).
- **Limits that stay** (ISS-27 and ISS-18): a gap through a target is not improved (swing results are somewhat low on gap-up winners); unseen sessions are counted, not excluded; a gap under the 20 % flag threshold is filled as a gap (a 3.4 % stop and a 19.9 % unflagged gap is −5.8 R, so the mean R of a swing run can be set by a handful of trades: read the median and the count of gaps beside it); a flagged boundary excludes the position; the window edge is strict; dividends are not modelled; a due minute that is missing exits at an earlier close; the circuit limit of a stock held overnight is not modelled.

## Alternatives considered
- **Fill a target at the open when the session opens beyond it.** Closer to a real resting limit, but it makes `R` of a `Target` exit depend on the gap, breaks the comparison of `FIXED_RR` values across P4 and is more generous than the market where the doubt is. Kept as a measurement (`OvernightTargetGaps`) the owner can ask to act on.
- **Wait for the next open when the due minute is missing.** Holds through a session nobody can see; leaving at the last known close is the safe reading.
- **Count only regular sessions.** Needs a second notion of session for no gain; the trade says how many were never seen.
- **Apply the gate in the scanner.** The scanner sees no position, and the candidates would depend on an exit; a gate between walks keeps the seam of ADR 0037.
- **Walk the candidates first and drop the overlapping ones afterwards.** The same result, with a walk per blocked candidate and counters that include minutes nobody traded; the gate before the walk is cheaper and its counters are clean.
- **Read N − 1 sessions past `To` so that a position can finish.** A changed read in P3-04a, a rule for the data after `To` in the bars hash and the key and a restated window property; do it when the owner asks for the last weeks of the data to count.
