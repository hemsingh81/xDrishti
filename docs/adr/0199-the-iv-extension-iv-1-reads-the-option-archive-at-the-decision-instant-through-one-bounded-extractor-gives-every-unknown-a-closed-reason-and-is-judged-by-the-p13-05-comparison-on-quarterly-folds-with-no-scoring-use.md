## Decision
1. **Where the code lives.** Pure types in `XDrishti.Domain.Options.Iv`: `IvFeatures` (the eight features of one decision), `IvFeatureInput`, `IvSeries` and `IvSeriesPlan` (which series), `IvBarBook` and `IvSlice` (the last bar that has ended), `DailyAtmIv` and `DailySideIv` (the daily series), `IvRank` and `IvRankResult`, `RealisedVolatility`, `IvLabels`. In `Learning.Extensions`: `IvFeatureCatalog` (`iv-1`, the eight names in order, family `ext:iv-1`), `IvReasons` (the closed reasons), `IvSourceNotes` (what the extraction depended on), `ExtensionComparisonOptions.ForOptions()`, `ProtocolX.OptionsFor`, `ExtensionCatalogs.GroupOf("iv-1")`. The use case stays `ExtractExtensionFeaturesHandler` (verb `xd learn features --run <key> --extension iv-1`); it reaches the archive through the port `IIvFeatureExtractor`, implemented by `IvFeatureExtractor` in `Application.OptionIv` (outside `Application.Learning` because the architecture rules keep archive and quality types out of it). The evaluation is `ComparisonEvaluation` with family `ext:iv-1`. An architecture test pins the exact sets that name each, that nothing in selection, planning, paper, live trading, the execution service or the P15 phase check does, and that the pure types name no pricing, quality or application type.
2. **The eight features, in this order, all at the decision instant `t`.** `iv_atm`, `iv_rank_252`, `iv_pct_252`, `iv_term_slope`, `iv_minus_rv20`, `nifty_iv_rank_252`, `pcr_oi`, `results_before_expiry`, with the definitions and closed reasons of the spec. The IV is the vendor value read as percent and divided by 100 (label `percent_assumed`, UNVERIFIED until the P14-02 pilot; a wrong unit scales every value but not the ranks); a value that is not a finite number above zero and at most 500 percent is missing. The label is stored in the extraction record's sources and in the evaluation record's inputs.
3. **The series.** NIFTY is read on the weekly series (codes 1 and 2), BANKNIFTY on the monthly series (codes 1 and 2), every share on the monthly series (code 1 only). The front series is the nearest whose expiry is at least three trading sessions after the trade date; the back series (the term slope) is the next code when the archive holds it, so a share has `no_term_series` and NIFTY has it only while W1 is the front series. The expiry dates are those of `ExpiryCalendar` (stored contracts, else the derived and unconfirmed weekday rule of ADR 0197). When no held series has three sessions to expiry, or an expiry date cannot be given, the features of the option series are `no_eligible_series` (an option underlying of the archive with nothing to read today); an instrument that is not an option underlying at all is `no_options`. BANKNIFTY is read on its monthly series throughout (its weeklies ended in 2024), which makes the spec's "BANKNIFTY M2 - M1" the term slope at every date.
4. **Only what had ended, only the same day.** A bar is stamped with its start and has ended at start plus its width. `IvBarBook.Last` returns the slice with the latest start that has ended at or before `t` on the India date of `t`; an earlier session's last bar is never carried forward (`no_bar_before_t`). `iv_atm` and `pcr_oi` take the latest timestamp that both the CE and the PE side have (a CE bar paired with an earlier PE bar is not a pair; sides with bars but never at one timestamp give `iv_missing` and `oi_missing`). The reasons are ordered: no bar on either side (`no_bar_before_t`), then a bar whose key-session did not pass the quality checks or that carries the suspect flag (`archive_unusable`), then a missing side, offset-0 bar or invalid IV (`iv_missing`). A key-session with no stored verdict is unusable (an `unavailable` check never counts as a pass, ADR 0178). `archive_unusable` derives from verdicts of whole sessions, which are computed after the session closed and so include data after `t`; it is a data-quality filter on the input, not a signal, and is the same read as in P14-08.
5. **Daily ranks from the previous session.** `DailyAtmIv` holds one value per session: the mean of the CE and PE offset-0 IV of the last bar of the session on the front series of that date, only when both sides are usable and valid (one side only gives no value, never a half value). `iv_rank_252` and `iv_pct_252` take the values of the 252 sessions before the trade date and `x` = the previous session's value; fewer than 200 values, or no value for the previous session, is `short_history` (a missing previous value is not replaced by a neighbour); a flat window is `flat_history`. The trade date's own value and anything later are never read. `nifty_iv_rank_252` is the same rank for NIFTY for every trade, so an instrument without options still gets it.
6. **Realised volatility.** `iv_minus_rv20` is `iv_atm` minus the sample standard deviation (n - 1) of the 20 daily log returns of the 21 sessions before the trade date, times the square root of 252; closes are the raw ones with a split or bonus inside the window put on one basis by the factor of the actions ex-dated after the earlier close (as `results_car3`); a missing close for any of the 21 sessions is `short_history`.
7. **Open interest and the results flag.** `pcr_oi` is the sum of PE open interest over the sum of CE open interest at offsets -3 to +3 of the front series at the last ended bar; any offset or side without open interest is `oi_missing`, a call sum of zero is `zero_call_oi`, a measured zero put sum over a positive call sum is a known 0. `pcr_oi` also answers `no_bar_before_t` and `archive_unusable` (the same closed reasons as `iv_atm`; the spec lists neither, they would otherwise hide inside `oi_missing`). `results_before_expiry` is 1 when the next results session of the P15 calendar (visible at `t`) is on or before the front series' expiry, 0 when a visible entry falls after it, and `event_unavailable` for every fail-closed reason of ADR 0182.
8. **One bounded extractor, filtered in the query.** For each underlying with trades (and NIFTY): one grouped query per window of at most 2,299 days for the daily last bars (`DISTINCT ON` over the offset-0 rows; the windows are consecutive and concatenated, so a run of any span is read), one filtered query for the quality verdicts of the 28 fetch keys it needs (`fetch_key = ANY(keys)`, with the run id of each verdict), and the bars of the trade dates in 31-day stretches at the seven offsets only (`atm_offset = ANY(offsets)`); an existence query decides whether an instrument is an option underlying at all (`SELECT EXISTS`). The three reads are added to the rolling-bar reader of ADR 0197 (`BarsAsync` with offsets, `DailyLastBarsAsync`, `HasSeriesAsync`) and to a new interface `IRollingQualityRuns` implemented by the quality store; both are SELECT only, UTC bounds are passed to the database, and the runtime role needs no new grant. Daily closes are the 1D aggregate, never the minute bars. 50,000 trades in 2 minutes and the daily series for 22 underlyings in a minute are UNVERIFIED.
9. **The extraction record.** `research.extension_extractions` gets `iv` / `iv-1`, `lag_minutes` 0 (the features read no filing time and a calendar row counts from its intimation; the configured lag of the event filter is not applied, an open question), `parser_version` and `filings_watermark` null, the calendar SHA-256 of the snapshot used, and the NaN counts per feature and closed reason. The counts text gains an optional `sources` object after them (the format without it is byte-identical to the P15 one, and the gate reads only `parser_unverified`): the archive watermark (the highest finished fetch-log id over the ranges read), the ids of the quality runs whose verdicts decided usability (every run that wrote a newest verdict for a key-session it looked at), and the IV unit. An extraction whose archive watermark is 0, or with no NIFTY series, is refused (`learning.iv_archive_empty`) and stores nothing, because the rows are insert-only and a frozen all-NaN set would be judged; an evaluation whose records carry watermark 0 or none is NOT_EVALUABLE `features_stale` before any trial is counted. A later watermark than the stored one is how a later review marks the record stale (P14-17); this slice stores the fact and compares nothing.
10. **The evaluation.** `xd learn extension-eval --family ext:iv-1 [--holdout]` is protocol X with the `ForOptions()` preset: the P13 depth preset (day blocks with the 5-session sensitivity, at least 2,000 paired candidates, 300 flips, log loss and Brier required, 6 evaluable folds) on calendar-quarter folds with a 12-month first train window and a 5-session embargo. A trial is appended to the ledger before any number; N is the family's distinct trial hashes; the hold-out is read once, only after a development KEEP, as the P15 families do (the last four seasons, cut by dates before any displayed number). The extraction records gate the verdict first: a missing record or rows of another run size are `features_stale`, a lag other than 0 is `features_stale`. The evaluation record's inputs name the extension version, the lag (0), the calendar SHA-256, the archive watermark (the highest over the runs), the quality run ids (their union) and the IV unit. A planted feature is KEEP at 7 test quarters and NOT_EVALUABLE at 6 (`folds 5 of 6`: the first fold has no earlier scores to choose a threshold); placebo features are not KEEP at N = 1 and 16.
11. **Contract.** No migration and no new table. `GET /api/research/extensions?family=ext:iv-1` lists the records (the summary text and the generated contract name the family); the family is **not** in `ExtensionFamilies.All`, which the P15 phase check iterates, and a test pins the check's output unchanged with an `ext:iv-1` KEEP stored. Verbs: `xd learn features --run <key> --extension iv-1` (the existing option), `xd learn extension-eval --family ext:iv-1 [--holdout]`. Log events are those of the shared handlers.

## Honest limits (printed in every record through the shared limits)
- The IV unit and the vendor's model are UNVERIFIED (P14-02 pilot, P14-03 probe). The ATM series switches strikes as the spot moves: `iv_atm` is a moving-strike value by design. The derived expiry weekdays are unconfirmed (ADR 0197).
- NOT_EVALUABLE until five years of cash trades exist (CF-04 Part B), and even then likely INCONCLUSIVE. Gross R unless the owner's checked charge sheet exists (ISS-25): a gross KEEP is a record and nothing more.
- Most tracked shares have no option series in the archive: for them only `nifty_iv_rank_252` is known.

## Open questions for the owner
1. **The hold-out window.** The hold-out is inherited from the P15 protocols: the last four seasons. Is a window of its own for an options feature set wanted (for example the last six months, as protocol O)? Default: inherited; no threshold is invented.
2. **Lag of the results calendar.** The spec says the extraction's lag is 0; the event filter's calendar uses the configured lag (`Filings:LagMinutes`). Should `results_before_expiry` use the configured lag too? Default: 0, as specified.
3. **Gross KEEP.** Protocol X (P15) allows a KEEP on gross R and says so in its limits (P15-10 open question 3, a KEEP is a record only). Protocol S and O forbid a gross KEEP. Should `ext:iv-1` follow them? Default: as protocol X; no scoring switch exists, so a KEEP changes nothing. Note that a gross development KEEP unlocks the single hold-out read, so a gross KEEP spends it.
4. **The term series for NIFTY.** The back series is the code after the front series, so NIFTY's slope is NaN whenever W1 is too close to its expiry (W2 is then the front). Is a fixed W2 - W1 wanted instead? Default: as built.
5. **The spec's reason lists** do not name a reason for a missing previous-session value (`short_history` is used), for `pcr_oi` without a bar or on an unusable session (`no_bar_before_t`, `archive_unusable` are used) or for a share with no eligible series (`no_eligible_series`). Confirm or name others.
