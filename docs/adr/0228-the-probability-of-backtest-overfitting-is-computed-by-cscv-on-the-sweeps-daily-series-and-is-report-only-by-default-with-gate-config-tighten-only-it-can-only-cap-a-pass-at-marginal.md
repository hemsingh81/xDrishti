# 0228 — The probability of backtest overfitting is computed by CSCV on the sweep's daily series and is report-only by default; `Gate` is config, tighten-only (it can only cap a PASS at MARGINAL)

- **Status:** Proposed (built as CF-03 part B, 2026-11-08). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no.** By default nothing changes a verdict; with `Gate` (config, tighten-only) a PASS can only be lowered to MARGINAL (a tightening), and no order is placed.
- **Date:** 2026-11-08.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** completes CF-03 after part A ([0205](0205-the-lab-verdict-shows-a-deflated-sharpe-ratio-from-stored-trial-statistics-and-a-session-level-stationary-block-bootstrap-beside-the-factor-and-the-monte-carlo-in-report-only-mode-and-gate-is-not-offered.md)); same mode pattern (`Off|ReportOnly|Gate`) as the other `Confidence` blocks.

## Context
The Lab sweep varies one parameter at a time. CF-03 asks how likely it is that the in-sample best variant falls to or below the median out of sample (Bailey, Borwein, Lopez de Prado and Zhu; combinatorially symmetric cross-validation). Formulas were checked by hand on the spec's cases only, not against the full paper.

## Decision
1. **Series:** the base run and each sweep point that ran a trade are variants. Each is the daily sum of net R by exit date, aligned on the union of those dates (0 on a day with no exit), stored once in the `robustness` member as `variantSeries` (values rounded to 4 decimals). A non-finite value makes PBO `unavailable`.
2. **`Cscv.Pbo` (pure, Domain):** S contiguous blocks (`Pbo:Blocks`, default 8, even 4..16), all `C(S, S/2)` splits, in-sample best by the mean of daily R (or Sharpe by `Pbo:Metric`), out-of-sample rank ascending with ties at the middle rank, `omega = rank/(N+1)`, `lambda = ln(omega/(1-omega))`, PBO = share of splits with `lambda <= 0`. Identical variants and days fewer than blocks are refused.
3. **Unavailable, never a number:** fewer than `Pbo:MinVariants` (default 10) variants, unaligned or non-finite series. The common single-parameter sweep has fewer than 10 points, so PBO reads `unavailable` there; it needs CF-06/CF-07 grids to say anything. The outcome is unchanged when it is unavailable.
4. **Modes:** `Off` no figure and no series; `ReportOnly` (default) figure and series, the outcome untouched; `Gate` (config, tighten-only: it can only cap a PASS at MARGINAL) a PASS with PBO above `Pbo:MaxPbo` (default 0.5, equal allowed) becomes MARGINAL with the reason, a MARGINAL or FAIL is untouched, an unavailable PBO moves nothing. The gate runs after the other stages and cannot raise an outcome. Settings are validated at start; the figure records the settings text it was produced with.
5. **Power check (the counterpart of 0205's refusal for DSR):** 200 seeded families, 17 variants, 520 sessions, 8 blocks (`PboSimulationTests`). With pure noise PBO was above 0.5 in **54.5 %** of families (the gate would catch about half of a noise PASS); with one variant of a true +0.15 R a day among 16 noise ones a real edge was capped by mistake in **5.5 %** (limit set at 10 %). The false-cap rate is within the limit, so `Gate` is offered, still config, tighten-only, and default off. A weaker edge (+0.05 R a day) was capped in **45.5 %**, close to the noise rate: PBO does not tell a weak edge from noise. The figure is diagnostic: no variant is selected by it.

## Consequences
The API contract gains `PboView` and `VariantSeriesView` on the robustness block (null on old documents, which read "not computed"). One-parameter neighbours are not independent strategies, so PBO says whether the best of these holds, not whether the family has an edge; the report prints that. A window of about two years gives few sessions per block, so the figure is noisy. Cost is milliseconds. Tests: Domain (hand cases 4/6 and 28/70, refusals, extremes, options, verdict cap, power simulation), Application (series, unavailable cases, size, gate through the runner).
