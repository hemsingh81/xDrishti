# 0197 — The option expression simulator re-marks each stored cash trade as one long option on a fixed strike read from the offsets, with an expiry calendar of labelled derived dates, a counted model-price fallback, and every unpaired trade counted by reason

- **Status:** Proposed (built in P14-08 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no** (research re-marking of stored trades; it writes only its own insert-only rows, adds one bounded reader of `options.rolling_bars`, and nothing selects, plans or orders; the simulator engine `sim-9` is not changed).
- **Date:** 2026-11-04.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** 0053 (same entries: one row per cash entry under every candidate), 0038 (the worse outcome inside a bar), 0043 (fills), 0045 (insert-only research rows, open-at-end excluded and counted), 0058 (run identity), [0177](0177-the-expired-options-backfill-fetches-rolling-atm-offset-bars-from-one-new-allow-listed-data-api-endpoint-at-night-behind-four-gates-after-an-owner-run-probe-its-own-cap-and-no-reader.md) (consequence: P14-08 reads bars through a reader slice of its own), [0178](0178-the-option-archive-quality-checks-read-the-recorded-chains-and-rolling-bars-in-bounded-ranges-and-store-insert-only-verdicts-with-an-honest-unavailable-and-no-trading-use.md) (usable key-sessions; the bar timestamp meaning is UNVERIFIED), [0191](0191-the-option-pricing-core-is-a-pure-black-76-library-with-fixed-units-a-closed-list-of-unavailable-reasons-and-no-reader-yet.md) (delta and the model price), [0193](0193-the-cell-key-gains-a-reserved-universe-part-and-an-expression-part-written-with-the-fewest-trailing-parts-so-every-stored-key-stays-byte-identical-and-only-cash-cells-reach-a-decision.md) (the expression and its profile), [0194](0194-the-option-cost-model-takes-optional-option-and-futures-lines-on-the-owners-sheet-a-half-spread-from-usable-chain-sessions-else-his-labelled-assumption-else-unavailable-rupee-liquidity-filters-and-a-lot-feasibility-count-apart-from-the-unit.md) (charges, half-spread, liquidity, lot feasibility).

## Decision
1. **Where the code lives.** Pure types in `XDrishti.Domain.Options.Remark`: `OptionRemark` (the re-mark of one trade), `RemarkMoney`, `BarGrid` and `HeldStrikeSeries`, `ExpiryCalendar` with `ExpiryRules`, `SeriesPlanner`, `OptionProfileParams`, `RemarkRunId` and their value types. They are the only Domain code that names both the pricing core and the cost model. The use case `RemarkRunHandler` and its ports are in `XDrishti.Application.OptionExpression` and `.Abstractions.OptionExpression` (not `OptionRemark`, which would hide the Domain class of that name); the adapters in `Infrastructure/OptionExpression`. An architecture test pins the exact set of types that name each. Nothing in selection, planning, paper, live trading, the execution service, the simulator or any other assembly names them.
2. **One row per cash trade.** Every trade of the stored run, closed or open at the end, gets exactly one row in `research.expression_trades`: `paired`, or unpaired with one reason. The closed list is the spec's ten (`no_series`, `no_series_for_expiry`, `expiry_unknown`, `strike_left_window`, `archive_unusable`, `no_iv`, `illiquid`, `liquidity_unknown`, `basis_mismatch`, `open_at_end`) **plus two**: `spread_unavailable` (a net-basis trade whose half-spread P14-07 cannot give: no honest net figure, never costed as zero) and `risk_invalid` (a cash stop at the entry: no rupee risk to scale). The counts per reason are in the run row; an unpaired row carries no premium, no units, no rupees and no R (a check constraint says so). A trade whose instrument is unknown or has no bars is `no_series`.
3. **The held strike.** A BUY is a call, an intraday SELL a put. The strike is read once, from the entry bar: `atm` is offset 0, `itm1` offset -1 for a call and +1 for a put. It is then fixed: the premium at any later bar is the bar of whichever offset has that `strike_px` at that bar time. A strike outside the window has no bar (`unavailable`: never a zero, never the last price); a bar flagged `suspect` is unavailable; a bar of a key-session the quality checks did not mark usable (coverage `pass` and stitching `pass` or `warn`, ADR 0178) is `archive_unusable`. With no quality verdict at all, nothing is usable.
4. **Fills.** Entry at the open of the first option bar whose start is at or after the cash entry minute (and within one bar width of it); the exit the same way at the cash exit minute; a cash exit at or after the start of the session's regular last bar (the session end from the trading calendar less the bar width; a square-off) at the close of that last bar. An exit minute before that with no bar at or just after it (a hole, or a truncated session) is **not** moved to another bar: the exit is priced by the fallback at the exit instant, else the trade is `strike_left_window`. The option is never filled earlier than the cash fill; a row records the option bar time (or the exit instant) as its exit time. **The bar timestamp is taken as the bar start (UNVERIFIED until the P14-03 probe is accepted, ADR 0178); every row is labelled `bar_ts: start (unverified)` and a test pins the convention.**
5. **Exits.** The cash exit maps to the option exit at the same minute. The optional premium stop (`premium_stop_pct`, a percent of the entry premium; default none) exits at `entry x (1 - p)` rounded to the paisa when a bar's low reaches it, at the bar's open when it opens at or below it. When the premium stop and the cash exit fall in the same option bar the **lower premium** is taken (ADR 0038). A position still open at the close of the last session before its expiry leaves at that close, reason `expiry_exit` (D6). A bar needed for a stop check or an exit that is missing (the exit bar, or the last bar of the expiry session, which also is never replaced by an earlier bar) is priced by the fallback (decision 6) or the trade is `strike_left_window`, never `no_series`. Without a premium stop every bar of the held strike between the entry and the exit is still checked for usability (a bar of an unusable key-session is `archive_unusable`); an absent or suspect bar there is simply not needed.
6. **The fallback and its label.** A missing held-strike bar is priced by Black-76 on the **latest vendor IV of that strike strictly before the bar** and no older than `Options:Remark:MaxIvAgeMinutes` (default 60, 5 to 390, measured from the instant being priced), on a forward equal to the underlying's one-minute close that ended at the priced instant, **with no carry** (no rate is set anywhere in the repository: a labelled approximation, `forward = spot`, discount 1). **Size of the no-carry bias:** a rate of about 6.5 % and a week to expiry move an at-the-money weekly premium by roughly 5 to 10 % against forward = spot (UNVERIFIED rule of thumb; no rate is set in the repository), so a model price is biased and its share is the figure to read. Without such an IV or such a close the trade is `strike_left_window`. Every paired row also carries the IV unit label (`iv_unit: percent_assumed`). A row that used a model price carries the label `bs_fallback`; the run row holds the share of paired trades that did (P14-09 caps it) and the verb prints the `strike_left_window` share and the bias toward short trades.
7. **Units and R.** The cash trade's rupee risk is `qty x |entry - stop|` (the charged trade's `RiskTaken`; a gross trade has no quantity, so the risk is per share and the row says `qty: per share`). The delta is Black-76's on the entry bar's vendor IV, the bar's spot and the expiry (label `delta: bs_on_vendor_iv`; no IV, or a delta that is not a usable number, is `no_iv`). `units = risk / (|delta| x |entry - stop|)`, fractional. Gross rupees = `(exit - entry premium) x units`; net rupees = gross less the charges of the buy and of the sell leg on the owner's sheet, each on its own date, less the half-spread of each leg (P14-07, in a bucket of the leg's own strike steps from the money, days to expiry and time of day). `R_equiv = rupees / risk`. Hand example (O.2): risk 1,000, premium 40, delta 0.5 gives 200 units; exit at 50 gives +2,000 gross and `R_equiv` 2.0; with the TEST sheet of the tests and a 1 % half-spread the net is 1,756.17 (26.68 + 37.15 charges, 180.00 spread). **The basis is the cash run's:** a charged run is re-marked net on the same stored sheet, a gross run gross; a stored sheet whose `RatesSha256` is no longer the run's, a sheet with no option lines for a leg, or a cash trade on the other basis than the option side is `basis_mismatch` (both gross or both net, never mixed). The cash R of a row is `RNet` (charged) or `RFilled` (gross).
8. **Liquidity and lots.** The rupee liquidity filter of P14-07 on the entry bar's open interest and volume; a missing value is `liquidity_unknown`, a filtered one `illiquid`. Lot feasibility is a column (`sizable`, `not_sizable`, `lot_unknown`, and `delta_unavailable`, `risk_invalid` for the two other answers of the count), never a fate; the lot in force on the entry date is recorded with `lot: unconfirmed` where it is.
9. **The expiry calendar (data, UNVERIFIED).** The rolling bars carry no expiry date and `options.contracts` starts on 2026-11-01. An expiry date is a contract date when `options.contracts` holds one of that kind in that week or month (label `expiry: contract`), else **derived** (label `expiry: derived`): the nominal weekday of the week, or the last such weekday of the month, moved to the previous trading day while it is not one. The weekday table is seeded below from secondary sources; **the build agent had no way to open the NSE circulars, so none of it is checked**; the two epoch dates are taken from `options.market_epochs` when present. A date the rule cannot give is `expiry_unknown`.

   | Underlying | Kind | Rule | Era (nominal date) | Source |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | NIFTY | weekly | Thursday | until 2025-08-31 | secondary |
   | NIFTY | weekly | Tuesday | from 2025-09-01 (epoch `nifty_expiry_tuesday`) | secondary |
   | NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, shares | monthly | last Thursday | until 2025-08-31 | secondary |
   | NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, shares | monthly | last Tuesday | from 2025-09-01 (same epoch) | secondary |
   | BANKNIFTY | weekly | Thursday | until 2023-09-05 | secondary (added beyond the spec: the backfill reaches five years back) |
   | BANKNIFTY | weekly | Wednesday | 2023-09-06 until the day before 2024-11-20 (epoch `weekly_index_one_per_exchange`) | secondary |
   | shares | weekly | none | - | - |

   **Series choice** (`nearest_safe`): NIFTY on `W`; BANKNIFTY on `W` only while a weekly expiry exists inside a weekly era of the derived rule (so not in the last days before the withdrawal, 2024-11-14 to 2024-11-20, which would otherwise be `expiry_unknown`) and `M` otherwise; shares on `M`; always code 1, unless the trade's planned last session (the trade date for any sell and for a buy with `max_hold_sessions` 1; for a buy with more, the entry session counted as 1 plus `max_hold_sessions` - 1). **The stored run does not say whether its strategy is intraday or swing, so the profile's `max_hold_sessions` is the plan: it must be 1 for an intraday run.** Every row is labelled `planned_hold_sessions: N (1 = intraday)` and the verb's usage says so is on or after the last session before that expiry, then code 2. The backfill holds NIFTY `W1`, `W2`, `M1`, BANKNIFTY `M1`, `M2` and `W1` (until the withdrawal) and shares `M1` only (15-minute bars, +-3), so a hold that reaches a share's expiry, or a BANKNIFTY weekly's, is `no_series_for_expiry`.
10. **The profile.** An `option_buy` `ExpressionProfile` (P14-06) with exactly four parameters, always written so one choice has one hash: `strike` (`atm`, `itm1`), `expiry` (`nearest_safe`), `premium_stop_pct` (`none`, or above 0 and below 100) and `max_hold_sessions` (1 to 20). A `future` profile is refused (`option_profile.no_futures_bars`): no futures bars exist. The profile is part of the identity of a re-mark and of a ledger trial, never of a strategy version.
11. **The reader.** `IRollingBarReader` has two methods: the bars of one series (underlying, kind, code, side, bar width) over one time range, all offsets (a range over 366 days is refused before any query), and the highest finished `options.fetch_log` id of the underlying over a date range (the archive watermark). `IContractExpiryReader` reads the distinct expiry dates and kinds of one underlying's contracts in a date range. The bars are read in stretches of at most a month of entries per series and shared by the trades on them; the one-minute closes of the underlying are read once per stretch. The per-table pins of ADR 0177 and 0178 gain exactly `RollingBarReader.cs` (for `rolling_bars` and `fetch_log`) and `ContractExpiryReader.cs` (for `options.contracts`).
12. **Run identity.** The run id is the SHA-256 of `expr-remark/1`, the cash run key, the profile hash, the re-mark version `expr-1`, the archive watermark, a hash of the usable key-sessions read (the quality verdicts carry no run id, so their content stands for it), the spread model ids and the owner's assumptions, the cost sheet's `RatesSha256` (or `gross`), hashes of the lot rows, the epoch rows and the contract expiries read, and the settings the figures depend on (the IV age, the liquidity thresholds). **Shortcuts, recorded as limits:** the trading calendar's holidays and the one-minute closes used by the fallback are not hashed into the id (a changed calendar or a corrected bar re-marks to the same id; change `remark_version` to force a new one). The same inputs give the same id (rows are sorted by trade key, inputs are sorted), the store is asked first, and a second run writes nothing.
13. **Tables** (`AddExpressionTrades`, raw SQL, insert-only for `xd_app` through the per-table helpers, nothing for `xd_exec` or the read roles): `research.expression_runs(run_id, cash_run_key, profile_hash, remark_version, inputs, trades, paired, unpaired, bs_fallback_share, labels, created_at)` and `research.expression_trades(run_id, trade_key, fate, side, expiry_flag, expiry_code, expiry, expiry_source, strike_px, entry_ts, exit_ts, entry_premium_px, exit_premium_px, units, gross_inr, net_inr, r_equiv, cash_r, exit_reason, lot, sizable, labels)`. Differences from the spec text: `expiry_flag` and `expiry_code` are nullable (a trade with no series has none), `sizable` is `varchar(24)` (the longest word is `delta_unavailable`), and `fate = 'paired'` holds exactly when the strike, the expiry, both premiums, the units, the gross rupees, `r_equiv` and `cash_r` are present; an unpaired row has none of them (also `net_inr`). `Down` drops both.
14. **Settings and verb.** `Options:Remark:MaxIvAgeMinutes` (5 to 390; default 60). `xd options remark --run <run-key> --profile <hash>`: exit 0 done (also when nothing new was written), 1 an unknown run or profile or a profile that cannot be re-marked, 2 a bad line, 3 a failure, 130 cancelled.

- **Writes** are one transaction of row-by-row `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, as the sibling research stores (`PairResearchStore`, `SpreadModelStore`) do; a binary import was not used. Quality verdicts are read once per underlying for all its trades' dates.

## Honest limits (printed with every run and stored in every row)
- With **no recorded data** every trade is `no_series` (or `archive_unusable` where bars exist without a passing verdict); the run says so and writes its counts. Whether any stored run trades an index is UNVERIFIED: if none does, the index archive (most of the backfill) pairs with nothing and the index profiles are NOT_EVALUABLE `no_paired_trades` in P14-09.
- The expiry weekdays are secondary-source data (decision 9); the bar timestamp meaning, the IV unit (percent, `percent_assumed`) and the open-interest unit are UNVERIFIED until the probe is accepted.
- The fallback is Black-76 with **no carry** on an observed IV: a labelled approximation, counted, capped later by P14-09.
- The fill is the next option bar's open: a delay of up to one bar (5 or 15 minutes) that P14-09 prints as a limit; the delta and the units use the entry bar's own IV and spot (as the spec says), which are known only at the bar's end.
- Stock series are 15-minute bars, +-3 offsets and code 1 only: many share trades are `strike_left_window` or `no_series_for_expiry`, and the paired remainder is biased toward short trades (the share is printed).
- A gross cash trade has no quantity: its risk is per share, which leaves `R_equiv` unchanged but makes `units` per share; lot feasibility is then against a risk of one share.
- 10,000 paired trades within 5 minutes is UNVERIFIED and ungated.
