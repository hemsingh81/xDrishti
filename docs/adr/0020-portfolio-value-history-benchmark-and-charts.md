# 0020 — Portfolio value history and benchmark computed from lots and stored daily bars; ECharts for charts

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-13: "approved as written") — builds on [ADR 0012](0012-holdings-acquisition-dates-and-returns-model.md) (lots, returns, XIRR), [ADR 0016](0016-market-bars-csv-load-dhan-fetch-and-app-managed-aggregates.md) (stored 1D bars) and [ADR 0017](0017-trading-calendar-eod-schedule-quality-adjustments-and-csv-undo.md) §5 (adjusted prices on read)
- **Date:** 2026-10-13

## Context
The Portfolio screen shows today's figures only. Design doc 12 §3 and §10 promise a **value history**, the **benchmark** ("the same cash flows invested in Nifty 50": benchmark XIRR, alpha) and the **max drawdown** of the history, and doc 12 §9 sketches a `valuations_daily` snapshot table. The system now stores daily bars for tracked instruments (CSV history and Dhan), adjusts them for splits/bonuses on read, has dated lots per holding (acquired and closed dates, quantity, unit cost), and can already compute XIRR from any set of cash flows. No chart library exists in the frontend yet; the design names ECharts for reports and Lightweight Charts for price charts.

## Decisions
1. **Compute on read, no snapshot table in this slice.** The value of a scope (all, account, bucket) on date *d* is Σ over its lots held on *d* of `quantity × adjusted daily close(d)` where a lot is held on *d* when `acquired_on ≤ d` and (`closed_on` is null or `> d`); invested on *d* is Σ `quantity × unit cost` of the same lots. Quantities are the lots' (the broker's, current basis), so the **corporate-action-adjusted** close of ADR 0017 §5 is the matching price; the series therefore follows later edits at once — a lot date the owner edits, a split the owner confirms, bars a new fetch adds. The date `d` runs over the trading days of the calendar from the scope's earliest lot to the last stored close, plus **today's point at the current price** (quote or sync) while the market is open. A price missing on a day carries the last earlier close forward (halts); a holding with **no stored daily bars at all** is left out of the history and counted (`unpricedHoldings`), the series is marked `partial`; it is never valued at zero. The pure calculator lives in `Application/Portfolio/Returns` next to XIRR. A `valuations_daily` snapshot is **not** built now: it would freeze numbers the owner can still correct, and the read is cheap (a few hundred holdings × a few hundred days of 1D bars). Official end-of-day snapshots stay a later option (doc 12 §6).
2. **Benchmark = the same cash flows in the index.** For every cash flow of the scope (lot buys, closed-lot sells) the equivalent amount buys or sells **units of NIFTY 50** at that day's close; the benchmark value on *d* is units × close(d). **Benchmark XIRR** is the XIRR of the same flows with the benchmark's value as the closing inflow; **alpha** = XIRR − benchmark XIRR (percentage points). The index must be a **tracked instrument with daily bars** (it is fetched like any other: Dhan `IDX_I`, ADR 0016); it is the master-list index NIFTY 50, set once in settings as the benchmark (not hard-coded in the calculator), and the Portfolio screen offers a one-click **Track the benchmark** when it has no bars. Until it has bars from the scope's first flow the benchmark figures are `unavailable` with the reason (never zero).
3. **Holdings need bars to appear in the history.** Holdings are not tracked automatically today. The history card says how many holdings it covers ("41 of 43 holdings have price history") and offers **Track my holdings** (tracks the instruments of the scope's open holdings that have none, through the existing tracking command, bulk) so the next *Update data* builds their bars; the first Dhan fetch goes back `Data:Dhan:InitialDays` (365 by default) — older acquisition dates use the CSV history where it exists, otherwise the history starts at the first bar and says so.
4. **Max drawdown** is computed on a **unit-value (NAV) series** so cash flows do not look like gains or losses: when a lot is bought, units are issued at the NAV of that day (the first day starts at 100); the NAV on *d* is value(d) ÷ units(d); drawdown is the largest peak-to-trough fall of the NAV series, in percent, with the dates. The same method gives the benchmark's drawdown for comparison.
5. **Charts: Apache ECharts**, pinned exactly, **tree-shaken** (line chart, grid, tooltip, legend, data zoom, canvas renderer) and **lazy-loaded** behind a small `shared/ui` wrapper (`TimeSeriesChart`: series with colours from the theme tokens, tooltip, range zoom, light/dark, `aria` description and a data-table alternative for screen readers, reduced-motion aware, resizes with its container). It is the library the reports of later phases will use; Lightweight Charts is reserved for price/candle charts. The chart chunk loads only when a chart card scrolls into view or opens, so the initial bundle stays ≤ 200 KB gzip; the chart chunk has its own budget (≤ 170 KB gzip).
6. **API** (read-only, no step-up): `GET /api/portfolio/history?accountId&bucketId&from&to&resolution=day|week|month` returns points `{date, value, invested, benchmarkValue|null, nav, benchmarkNav|null}` and the facts `{coverage {holdings, covered, unpriced}, partial, benchmark {state, symbol, reason}, maxDrawdown {pct, from, to}, benchmarkMaxDrawdown, firstDate, lastPriceAt}`; `GET /api/holdings/summary` gains `benchmarkXirr`, `alpha`, `benchmarkState` (`ok|unavailable|insufficient|n_m`) for the same scope. Week/month resolution takes the last trading day of the period; at most 2,000 points are returned.

## Consequences
- The portfolio's history is as right as the lots' dates and the stored bars: **lots on the default date (1 Jan 2025) make the history start there** and the card says "N lots use the default date" like the summary does.
- No migration and no scheduled job: the story adds read models, an API, a chart and an action; consistency after edits is automatic.
- A stored history of the owner's own valuations (what the screen showed on a past day) is not kept; if reports later need it, a snapshot table is added then with its own ADR.
- Adding ECharts is the first heavy frontend dependency; the lazy chunk and the wrapper keep it out of the initial load and give later stories one place to look.
- The benchmark is only comparable when the index has bars for the same period; the card says when it does not.
- **Live day limitation.** The index has no live quote source. On a trading day before its bar is fetched, the portfolio's last point is valued at today's prices while the benchmark is valued at the index's previous close, so a naive comparison would lag by the index's move of the day. The API therefore ends the benchmark lines at the index's last close (`benchmark.asOf`; the live point has no benchmark value and the benchmark NAV and drawdown are computed on the days the index has), reports the summary's `benchmarkAsOf`, and labels the alpha with `alphaBasis`: `last_close` (both sides as of their last close) or `live_vs_last_close` (the portfolio at prices of a later trading day than the index close, so the alpha holds the portfolio's move of the day but not the index's). Recomputing the portfolio at the index's as-of close was not done: it needs the bars of every holding for the summary, which is heavy for a tile.
- Chart and XIRR use one rule for the benchmark: a cash flow buys or sells index units at the close of the first trading day on or after its date (a lot dated on a weekend uses the next session's close; a lot bought and sold before a trading day comes leaves no flows), and the XIRR flows keep the lots' own dates.
- The maximum drawdown is measured within the requested range (the peak must lie inside it); the response gives the range's first and last day. A request that would read more than 2,000,000 daily prices is refused (`portfolio.invalid`); the adjusted closes can be cached per instrument if the scope grows. Server time is typically about 100 ms (measured about 90 ms for 300 holdings over 521 days); the tests bound it generously on purpose.
