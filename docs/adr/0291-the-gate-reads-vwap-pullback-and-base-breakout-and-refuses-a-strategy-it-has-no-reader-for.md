# 0291 — The gate reads VWAP_PULLBACK and BASE_BREAKOUT, and refuses a strategy it has no reader for (P3-09e)

- **Status:** Accepted (owner delegated, 2026-11-14). Amends [ADR 0048](0048-the-automated-gate-replaces-the-owners-chart-check-as-the-p3-exit.md) (the exit-code table and the "cannot read" consequence).
- **Date:** 2026-11-14.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** ADR 0037, 0038 (fills), 0040 (swing hold, X8), 0043 (the resting order, E8, E9), 0047 (the oracle); specs P3-05 and P3-06 (the rule tables).

## Context
The first Round B gate (`p3-roundb-1`, 25 rows) was `BLOCKED_DEFECT` on every row with "reader: no trade". `XDrishti.SimReader` read only ORB; the machine claimed a defect where it simply could not read. That is a tooling gap, and the 0048 consequence "a gate on a strategy the reader cannot read is BLOCKED_DEFECT" was wrong for a strategy the reader will never know.

## Decision
1. **Two readers** in `XDrishti.SimReader`, written from the rule tables of the specs only, with no engine, strategy or indicator code (project references and the architecture tests unchanged). The fill and exit walk of ORB (ADR 0038 E2 to E6, X1 to X5) moved into shared internal helpers; the ORB reading is unchanged (its 35 tests are green).
   - `VwapPullbackReader`: 15-minute bars anchored at 09:15; VWAP of the bars' typical prices, volume-weighted, per session; ATR(14) Wilder over all sessions in a row, seeded with the mean of the first 14 true ranges; setup `tf("1h").ema(20) > ema(50)` (hourly buckets 09:15, 10:15, ... and the 15:15 stub, so 7 a session; a bucket closed at the very step of the decision counts) and `tf("1D").close > ema(20)` on the previous session; trigger items per P3-05 D3; window 10:00 to 14:30; stop = lowest low of the dip and trigger bars, used as given; limit +0.1 %, 5 minutes, square-off 15:15; the first candidate of the day is the only one.
   - `BaseBreakoutReader`: daily bars from the 1-minute bars; setup and trigger per P3-06 D1 (weekly gate, 50-day average, base span, relative volume, closing high with a tie); stop = lowest low of the last `base_len` sessions; the order rests overnight and fills in the first five minutes of the next session (09:20 fills, a later minute does not); a hold of at most 5 sessions, the fill session being the first, leaving at the close of the due session's last minute with a trade, after the stop and the target of that minute.
2. **History and dates.** The readers need sessions before the trade date (and, for the swing trade, after it). The assisted read loads them from the stored bars (130 calendar days back; BASE_BREAKOUT also 14 days forward) with one range query per row (`IStoredDays.GetRangeAsync`).
   - **Two dates.** `pack.json`'s `date` is the ENTRY (fill) date; the decision date is the last segment of `trade_key` (ADR 0043 D6) and is parsed into `PackedTrade.DecisionDate`. They are equal for ORB and VWAP_PULLBACK (intraday) and differ for BASE_BREAKOUT (the order rests overnight; `BASE_BREAKOUT|NSE:AMBER|Buy|2025-06-18` has date 2025-06-19). BASE_BREAKOUT is read around the decision date.
   - **What the second source replaces.** ORB and VWAP_PULLBACK: the row's day. BASE_BREAKOUT: the decision day and the entry day (the two days whose bars decide the entry: the signal's close, low and volume, and the fill minute); the days of the hold and all history stay stored. The second source is therefore asked for both days (the cache key list is aligned), a row is `source_unavailable` if either is missing, and the bar comparison cites the decision day's last minutes and the entry day's first minutes, each day on its own price ratio. A difference on a history day or a hold day cannot show as `data_sensitive`.
   - **Parameters are not defaulted.** The reader's parameters (`pull_atr`, `rr`; `base_len`, `base_pct`, `vol_x`, `rr`) are read from the pack's strings; a missing or misspelled one refuses the pack with exit 2 and names it (the row and the parameter), because a silent v1 default would hide a pack that was run with other values.
3. **Unsupported strategy.** `AssistRunner` (so `--assist` and `--gate`) refuses a pack with a strategy outside ORB, VWAP_PULLBACK and BASE_BREAKOUT **before any read or write**: exit **2** (unusable input), the text "unsupported strategy: the gate has no independent reader for X", no gate file. A future strategy gets a reader first; it is never `BLOCKED_DEFECT` (12).
4. **Exit codes of `verify-pack --gate`:** 0 PASS or PASS_WITH_DATA_FINDINGS; 10 RERUN_NEEDED; 11 DATA_REPAIR_NEEDED; 12 BLOCKED_DEFECT; **2 unusable input, now also an unsupported strategy**.

## Interpretations taken where the documents are ambiguous (not resolved from the engine code)
- **Daily bar:** open of the first minute, high and low over the session, close of the last minute that holds a trade (P3-06 D7), volume the sum.
- **Weekly bar (P3-06 D6):** the week of the signal is closed, and counts as the last closed week, only when the signal session is the last of its week, taken as "the next session in the data is in a later ISO week" (a holiday on the Friday makes the Thursday the last). Fewer than 10 closed weeks, 50 sessions, or 21 sessions for the volume mean: no trade.
- **`rel_volume`:** today's volume against the mean of the 20 sessions before; a mean of 0 is no trade.
- **Calendar:** a session is a date with bars. A weekday with no stored bars is not counted in the five-session hold (the engine counts the calendar's sessions; a stored hole could differ, and would show as a difference to be judged).
- **Hourly buckets and the VWAP bars:** built from the minutes present, closing at the first minute present at or after the bucket's last minute (the ORB rule), the 15:15 stub closing at the 15:29 step. One rule for the hourly trend, today and history: every bucket that has closed is a bar (a series), whereas the 15-minute decision merges buckets that close at the same minute into the last one; a prior session's bucket that never closed (its 15:29 minute missing) is not a bar.
- **Stale daily bar:** a signal session without its 15:29 minute loses the signal (P3-06 D5).
- **Position gate** (a candidate while a position is open): not modelled; the rows are trades the engine made.
- **Raw history (not machine-checkable).** The history is the raw stored bars; the engine adjusts for corporate actions and, when the run admitted them (CF-04d), reads pre-seam bars source-aware. The reader does neither and is not switched to the engine's adjusted read path. A row carries a **history caveat** when
  - an applied action's ex-date lies after the first loaded date and up to the last date the reader uses (`factors.FactorAt(from) != factors.FactorAt(lastUsed)`; `lastUsed` is the trade day for VWAP_PULLBACK, the end of the hold window for BASE_BREAKOUT); or
  - the RUN admitted pre-seam bars for that instrument from a seam date after the first loaded date (`seam > from`). The reader drops the bars dated before the seam and an admitting run keeps them, so the two differ exactly then, wherever the seam falls, after the range included. This is read from the run record's `seam=` line, carried into `pack.json` as `instruments[].pre_seam` (written only for such an instrument, so every other pack is byte-identical); it is not read from today's switch. With admission off the engine and the reader see the same cut bars, so a seam alone is no caveat and a mismatch stays a defect. A trade day before the admitted seam (ORB and VWAP_PULLBACK read the stored day, which is cut and empty there) is caveated too, so it gets the hand check and not a false `BLOCKED_DEFECT`.
  - **Old packs.** A pack drawn before `pre_seam` existed has no such field and is read as admitting none. If its run did admit pre-seam bars, the gate fails closed: a resulting difference shows as a defect, never as a pass. Such a pack must be redrawn with `--force`, because its pack id is unchanged.
  - shortcut: an action after the last used date scales the whole range evenly and is not flagged (the same gap as ORB's `adjusted_instrument`).
  The gate (fail-safe, ADR 0048 amended): a caveated row that **agrees** with the engine is a data finding (`PASS_WITH_DATA_FINDINGS`, "history not machine-checkable"); one that **differs**, or where the reader reads no trade, is `RERUN_NEEDED` (exit 10) with the text "not machine-checkable: hand check needed" and its own next action, never exit 0 and never `BLOCKED_DEFECT`.
- **Pack integrity of the dates (exit 2).** The trade key must end in a `yyyy-MM-dd` decision date; an intraday strategy (ORB, VWAP_PULLBACK) needs decision date = date, BASE_BREAKOUT needs decision date before its entry date. Otherwise the pack is refused with the row and the key named, before any read.
- **A second-source day with no bars** is `source_unavailable` (reason "the second source returned no bars for the day"), never an empty session in the splice.
- **EMA seed:** SMA-seeded, started at the first session loaded (about 90 sessions), so the averages have converged for the sign tests used; the engine starts at the first stored day.

## The honest limit
As ADR 0048: the readers, the oracle and the engine come from the same rule tables and the same model family. A shared misreading stays invisible; a history day wrong in the stored bars is invisible to the second source (point 2).

## Consequences
- The Round B pack can be gated; a difference between a reader and the engine is now a real finding to be judged against the specs.
- `IStoredDays` gains `GetRangeAsync` (the host and the test fakes); `ReaderExitReason.MaxHold` is new.
- No API, table or migration.
