# 0165 - An intent is made only from an approved ticket for an account whose mode routes an entry, and a trigger it cannot act on closes it with a reason, never later

- **Status:** Accepted (decided in P12-08a under the owner's standing run instruction; spec [P12-08](../specs/P12-08-semi-auto-one-tap-confirm.md), criteria 13 (routing), 16 and 17).
- **Date:** 2026-10-31.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** builds on ADR 0155 and 0156 (the intent state machine, `execution.intents` and the event chain, written only by `xd_exec`), 0159 (P12-03's collar `exec.price_band_pct`, the entry window and `canary_cap`), 0161 (the halt record; an unreadable record is halted), 0163 (the mode ladder: `semi_auto` and `auto_live` are not storable, the mode source stays closed) and 0164 (readiness; unavailable blocks like not ready). P12-00 D11 holds: no `trading.*` row is written. No migration, no API, no deploy file and no allow-list entry is touched.

## Context
P12-03 judges an intent and P12-01 sends it, but nothing turned an owner-approved plan ticket into an intent. The intent is the first record that names an order the system may later send, so it must be made only where a mode can act on it, from numbers the owner approved, and it must never wait for a better moment after the moment passed.

## Decision
1. **Routing is one total table (`ModeRouting`, P12-07 criterion 12).** `paper` and `manual_live` route nothing; `assisted` prepares a sheet for an entry only; `semi_auto` waits for the owner's tap on an entry and acts on its own for `StopTighten`, `ExitReduce` and `CancelPending`; `auto_live` acts on its own for an entry and the reducing classes. `StopLoosen`, `AddToPosition` and `ReEntry` are refused in every mode with `execution.not_supported` until P12-13; `OutsidePlan` is refused in every mode with `execution.outside_plan`; a SELL entry is refused (`execution.not_supported`, entries are BUY only). No answer or an unknown mode routes nothing; an unknown class is refused. The table lives in `XDrishti.Execution` over `ExecMode`, which already has the five values; the shared `LadderMode`, the database check and every parser stay as ADR 0163 left them. **`semi_auto` is not opened anywhere**: no row can hold it, `ModeTrailSource` still reads it as `paper`, and the creator is tested with a fake mode source.
2. **An intent comes only from an approved ticket with an allocation for an account that may act.** One pass reads the allocations of tickets that went through an owner approval for the sessions from today to a week ahead (read only). It creates an intent only when the ticket is `approved` or `armed`, the account gate is open (an answer, `execution_enabled` ON, a sending mode, an environment), the mode routes a BUY entry to a tap or to the system, the ticket carries P7-19's numeric entry, stop and target, and the symbol has exactly one Dhan NSE equity id. The intent stores the entry reference as entry and trigger, the stop and target levels, `structure = super`, and the plan, ticket, approval and newest mode trail row in force. The unique key `(account_id, plan_item_id)` makes a second pass a no-op (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`).
3. **While the canary is pending the intent holds one share.** P12-03's `canary_cap` refuses more than one share while `exec.canary_pending` is ON (the default), so the creator stores `min(allocation, 1)` then; the allocation itself is not changed. The ticket text of P12-08 says so.
4. **Planned, then armed, then checking once.** A planned intent is armed when its ticket is armed by the pre-open check, from 09:10 India time of its session. An armed intent whose trigger a fresh live price (younger than `exec.feed_max_age_s`) reaches (`TriggerWatcher`: BUY at or above) moves to `checking` once, if no halt is active and the halt record is readable, the account may still act, the newest readiness check of this session is `ready` and at most 16 minutes old (the loop writes at least every 15), and the time is inside the entry window (09:20 to 15:00 by default, both ends included). Otherwise it is cancelled with the strongest reason: `halted`, then `mode_changed`, then `missed_not_ready`, then `window`. There is no catch-up: a crossing outside the window or while not ready closes the intent for the day, and an armed intent that never crossed is cancelled `window` after the window closes. A ticket invalidated or expired cancels its open intent (`plan_invalidated`, `plan_expired`).
5. **A trigger already run past is void.** If, when the intent is created on its session day, a fresh price is beyond the entry on the entry's side by more than `exec.price_band_pct` (measured as `ltp_collar` measures it, against the last price), the intent is cancelled `void` at once: the entry could only be chased.
6. **The day mark does not gate.** The 09:10:30 `day_states` row is a record of the morning (ADR 0164: "trading may still start when ready"); the creator reads only the newest readiness check, so a late login can still trade a later crossing but never a missed one.
7. **What it can touch.** It writes `execution.intents` and `execution.events` as `system:intent-creator` through the existing event chain (the database judges every move again) and reads `trading.plans`, `plan_items`, `plan_item_allocations`, `plan_events`, `workflow.approvals`, `market.instruments`, `instrument_broker_ids`, `live_quotes` and `execution.mode_trail`, all already granted to `xd_exec` by ADR 0156 and 0163: no migration. It receives no broker, credential or sender port (an architecture test pins its constructor) and is composed by no host. Logs 6710 to 6715 carry intent and account hashes, a ticket id, quantities and reason words, never a price, a name or an e-mail.

## Not built, owed (P12-08b and later)
The ticket, its text and hash, outcomes, the day unlock, the confirm gate and API, the expiry loop and the frontend (criteria 1 to 12, 14, 15); composing the creator in `xd-exec` (with a real mode source, which waits for `semi_auto` to be storable); the ticket-state bridge of P12-00 finding 1 (`trading` ticket `triggered`/`closed`); the trading-day calendar check (the creator trusts the plan's date). A crossing is seen only at pass time, so a price that touches the trigger between two passes and falls back is missed (the pass interval is the composer's choice). A security id is taken from the instrument master at intent time; a later remap is P12-03's `instrument_tradable` to catch.

## Consequences
An approved ticket can become an intent the moment an account can act, and the intent can only move toward a send through the checks and, in `semi_auto`, the owner's tap. Today no account can act, so no intent is made, which is the intended state. Nothing new can send.
