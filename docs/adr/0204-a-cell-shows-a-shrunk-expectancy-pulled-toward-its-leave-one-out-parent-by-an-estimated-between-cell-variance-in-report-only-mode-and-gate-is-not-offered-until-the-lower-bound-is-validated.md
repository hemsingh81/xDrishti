# 0204 — A cell shows a shrunk expectancy pulled toward its leave-one-out parent by an estimated between-cell variance in ReportOnly mode, and Gate is not offered until the lower bound is validated

- **Status:** Proposed (built in CF-02 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no** (no network, no order, no stored verdict or label changes; nothing reads the new figures; the snapshot key gains `|shrinkage=` in ReportOnly, decision 7).
- **Date:** 2026-11-04.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** extends [0064](0064-cell-statistics-bayesian-shrinkage-recency-weights-and-point-in-time-snapshots.md) (the Beta win-rate posterior stays exactly as it was, strength 20, its limit "the parent includes the cell" is now answered by a second, leave-one-out column) and [0203](0203-effective-n-is-the-smaller-of-an-entry-session-cluster-estimate-and-a-serial-estimate-shown-beside-n-in-report-only-mode-and-power-and-minimum-track-record-are-printed-with-it.md) (effective n and `NormalDistribution`); keeps the cash-only guard of [0193](0193-the-cell-key-gains-a-reserved-universe-part-and-an-expression-part-written-with-the-fewest-trailing-parts-so-every-stored-key-stays-byte-identical-and-only-cash-cells-reach-a-decision.md) (same rows, same filter).

## Context
A 10-trade cell at +0.40 R outranks a 40-trade cell at +0.25 R on the raw mean, and `min_trades` hides the thin cell instead of weighing it. The programme rule (CF-00): show a new figure beside the old, change nothing by default, say `unavailable: <reason>` rather than invent a number.

## Decision
1. **Pure Domain, `BetaShrinkage` untouched.** `Domain/Learning/NormalShrinkage` (the posterior `parent + B (m - parent)`, `B = tau^2 / (tau^2 + sigma^2/n)`, variance `tau^2 sigma^2 / (n tau^2 + sigma^2)` which equals `B sigma^2/n` and is `tau^2` for n = 0; lower bound = mean minus `z` times the sd at `QuantileLevel`, 0.05, the same 5 % quantile the Beta lower bound is), `ShrinkageEstimators` (DerSimonian-Laird tau^2 with the floor, estimated Beta strength, leave-one-out parent), `CellShrinker` (the second pass) and `ShrinkageAssessment` (the row member). `ShrinkageOptions` is in `Domain/Confidence` beside `ConfidenceOptions`.
2. **n is the smaller of CF-01's effective n and the Kish weight n** `(sum w)^2 / sum w^2`. Without CF-01's figure (its mode Off, fewer than 5 trades, no entry dates) the weight n alone is used and the row note says so. The sigma^2 of a cell is the sample variance of its trades' R; from 5 trades its sd is floored at `Confidence:Power:SigmaFloorR`; from 5 trades up to `SigmaPoolStrength` (30) it is blended with the group's pooled variance with weight `n / (n + 30)`; below 5 trades the group's pooled variance alone (review fix 4).
3. **tau^2 by DerSimonian-Laird once per parent group over all its siblings** (cells with at least `MinTradesForSibling`, 5, trades), floored at `TauSquaredFloor` (0.01 R^2); fewer than 3 siblings use the floor and say so. The row's `tauSource` is `estimated` when the estimate reached the floor, else `floor`. The estimate includes the cell while the parent mean excludes it (a stated, small inconsistency; the leave-one-out tau^2 is a documented alternative, not built). The pooled variance of the group likewise includes the cell.
4. **The Beta strength is estimated by the same moment method** (rate noise `pbar (1 - pbar)/n`, `rho = tau_p^2/(pbar (1 - pbar))`, strength `(1 - rho)/rho` clamped to 5..200); with fewer than 3 siblings, a pooled rate of 0 or 1 or no spread it is the configured 20 and `strengthSource` reads `default`. The second win-rate column `shrunkWinRate` is the Beta posterior with that strength toward the leave-one-out recency-weighted parent rate; the P6-01 columns are unchanged.
5. **Parent chain and level names.** A regime cell is pulled toward its own `*` cell without itself (level `star`; the trades of that `*` cell that are in no regime cell, the unlabelled ones, stay in the parent), then toward the other `*` cells of its strategy (`strategy`), then toward every other `*` cell (`system`). A `*` cell starts at `strategy`. The first level whose trades, the cell excluded, reach `MinParentTrades` (30) is used; none gives `unavailable: no parent with 30 trades` and all shrunk figures null. The spec's list `regime,star,strategy,system` is kept in the stored check; the value `regime` is reserved and never written (the spec's "regime parent" is the `*` cell, which is called `star`). The parent mean is the n-weighted leave-one-out mean of the other members, n being the same smaller-of figure (the unlabelled remainder of a `*` cell has no CF-01 figure and uses the weight n alone). No hysteresis: the level can change between snapshots as trades cross 30, visible in `parentLevel`.
6. **Modes, `Confidence:Shrinkage:Mode`, default `ReportOnly`.** `Off`: nothing computed, rows and key exactly as before. `ReportOnly`: the figures are stored and shown; no other field of any row changes (a test compares every field with and without). **`Gate` is not offered**: the spec's review fix 1 says the lower bound ignores the uncertainty in tau^2, the parent mean and sigma^2 and must not feed `Gate` until a seeded coverage simulation validates it. The value is accepted by the enum but refused by `ShrinkageOptions.Problems` (the host stops at start) and by the calculator. No evidence label changes in any mode.
7. **Snapshot key.** `|shrinkage=<settings>/<sigma floor>` is appended (the floor is CF-01's `Power:SigmaFloorR`, which the figures read, so it must be in the key even when CF-01 is Off) only when the mode is not Off, after the CF-01 `|confidence=` part, so a key computed with shrinkage Off is byte-identical to the one before; with ReportOnly (the default) the key is new, so the snapshot with the figures is a new snapshot and a changed setting is another (the same rule as ADR 0203). Every older snapshot stays as it is.
8. **Data.** Migration `AddCellStatsShrinkage`: nullable `parent_level`, `parent_mean_r`, `tau_squared`, `blend`, `shrunk_mean_r`, `shrunk_lower_r`, `estimated_strength`, `shrunk_win_rate`, `tau_source`, `strength_source` as in the spec, plus three beyond it: `shrink_n` (the n the precision rests on), `shrink_mode` and `shrink_note` (a stored row says its mode and why a figure is unavailable). One check `ck_cell_stats_shrinkage` on the word columns. The table stays insert-only for `xd_app` (no grant changed; `ResearchGrantsTests`). A row written before the migration reads "not computed" (`shrinkage` null).
9. **API and screen.** `GET /api/cells` and `GET /api/cells/{key}` rows gain optional `shrinkage` (the spec's members plus `mode` and `nUsed`; null for a row stored before, all figures null with `note` when unavailable); the row's `limits` text gains the shrinkage limits. The cell drawer table (a lazy chunk) gets two lines, "Shrunk R" and its lower bound, with the reason when unavailable.
10. **The floor report** (review fix 2): the compute outcome carries `ShrunkRows`, `TauFloorRows` and `TauFloorShare`, and the log (event 4473) and the `xd learn cells` line print the counts only. It is not on the list endpoint (a filtered list would give a share of a part).
11. **Settings** are the configuration section `Confidence:Shrinkage` (no stored editor exists; "owner-tunable" is a configuration change as in ADR 0203): `Mode`, `TauSquaredFloor` 0.0001..1 (above 0, or the lower bound would not exist), `MinTradesForSibling` 3..30, `MinParentTrades` 10..200, `StrengthMin`, `StrengthMax` 1..1000 (min not above max), `QuantileLevel` 0.01..0.25, `SigmaPoolStrength` 1..1000 (the last is from review fix 4). A value out of range stops the host at start.
12. **Where it is not used.** `PointInTimeCells`, `WhatWorksBuilder`, `MetaPolicyEvaluator`, the report engine and every other `CellStatsCalculator` caller pass no shrinkage options: byte-identical rows. Instrument-level pooling is out of scope.

## Deviations from the spec, deliberately
- **Gate is refused, not built** (decision 6), because review fix 1 forbids it before the coverage simulation. The spec's AC 9 "except the evidence label in Gate" therefore has nothing to exercise; the test asserts the refusal.
- Three extra columns (decision 8); `mode` and `nUsed` on the view; the floor report on the outcome and log, not on the list (decision 10).
- The `Quantile` helper is CF-01's `NormalDistribution` (the spec's UNVERIFIED point). `CellStatsRow` had no stored SD: it is computed in the shrinker from the window's trades, not added to the row.
- The regime cells of P6-02 exist (`RegimeCellBuilder`), so the `star` level is exercised by the real split, not only by a synthetic cell. The parent mean of a `*` cell is taken over the cells of the strategy, each weighted by its n, not over the strategy's trades pooled.
- Tests use hand-built trades, so the spec's AC 1 to 5 numbers are checked on the pure functions (they take sigma^2 as given) and the calculator tests check structure and the leave-one-out arithmetic.
- **No stored real snapshot** is available for the AC 9 / review-fix-6 regression fixture; the tests compare every field of the rows computed with and without the options, with the old key text, and a legacy row simulated by nulling the new columns (integration).

## Open questions for the owner
1. `TauSquaredFloor` 0.01 R^2 (a prior sd of 0.1 R between cells) is the spec's proposal. Read the first reports: if most cells sit at the floor (`TauFloorShare` near 1), the floor and not the data sets the shrinkage.
2. May `Gate` later make the evidence label use the shrunk lower bound? It needs the seeded coverage simulation first (review fix 1), a separate step not built here.
3. Is instrument-level pooling wanted (needs a cluster key beyond the session)? Left out.
4. The `|shrinkage=` key part makes the default ReportOnly snapshot a new snapshot; set `Confidence:Shrinkage:Mode` to `Off` to keep recomputing the older snapshots idempotently.

## Consequences
- API: rows gain `shrinkage`; contract and the frontend types regenerated. Nothing reads it for a decision.
- Honest limits printed with the figures: shrunk R is an estimate of the past edge pulled toward its group, not a forecast; the lower bound ignores the uncertainty in the pooling parameters and is optimistic; tau^2 comes from a handful of siblings that share a market period (shrinkage is a little too weak, not too strong) and its floor is an assumption; the normal model for R is an approximation; with few regime cells the regime level is untested on real data.
