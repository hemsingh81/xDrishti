# 0269 — Opening-minute bars are recorded from the live feed on their own connection behind three gates, and baskets 4 and 12 are frozen from complete minutes only, or not formed

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [BK-03b](../specs/BK-03b-opening-minute-bars-from-the-live-feed.md), approved 2026-11-11; built 2026-11-11). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: yes** (a new broker socket and a writer; read-only market data, no order).
- **Date:** 2026-11-11.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0258](0258-derived-baskets-are-formed-after-the-end-of-day-run-from-our-own-bars-as-immutable-dated-versions-with-scores-and-an-off-by-default-job.md) (the derived job; this closes its "Owner decision 2026-11-11" for baskets 4 and 12), [0255](0255-derived-baskets-are-pure-versioned-and-point-in-time.md) (the pure engine, no look-ahead), [0173](0173-the-trade-tick-recorder-stores-last-trade-updates-of-the-depth-set-when-cumulative-volume-rises-on-a-second-quote-socket-behind-three-gates-its-own-cap-and-no-reader.md) (the three-gate pattern and the second connection this copies), [0024](0024-live-feed-xd-feed-and-live-prices.md) (the live feed, whose subscription list is untouched).

## Context
Gappers (basket 4, formed 09:20) and Opening-range wide (basket 12, formed 09:30) need the first minutes of the day. Nothing recorded 1-minute bars from the live feed: it keeps the latest price every 2 seconds, and the stored 1-minute history arrives by import.

## Decision
1. **A pure `MinuteBarBuilder`** turns quote packets into 1-minute bars on the packet's **exchange time** (IST minute), never the receive time. A bar is complete when a later minute's packet arrives or when its minute is over by `OpeningBars:GraceSeconds`. A packet before the instrument's current minute, or for a minute already written, is dropped and counted `late`. A minute with no packet gives **no bar** (nothing is filled forward).
2. **Its own connection and loop.** `OpeningBarsRecorder` and `OpeningBarsSupervisor` run in `xd-feed` as a fifth background loop with their own try/catch, connector (`OpeningBarsFeedConnector`, a separate `DhanFeedConnector` with its own packet counts, the same allow-listed endpoint) and run record `opening-bars`. The live feed's subscription list and cap are not touched. The tick and depth recorders already open their own sockets, so a second connection is within what the plan carries; a 805 disconnect ends the session as refused. The fallback of sharing the live feed's connection was therefore not needed.
3. **Three gates, all OFF:** the deployment flag `OpeningBars:Enabled`, the owner's stored switch `baskets.opening_bars` (insert-only `market.opening_bars_switch`, owner actors only, no row = OFF) and the service `opening-bars` (window 09:10 to 09:32, paused). With any gate closed nothing reads a credential, opens a socket or writes a row; a gate that cannot be read is closed.
4. **The store is insert-only.** `market.bars_1m_live (instrument_id, minute_start, o, h, l, c, v, packets, recorded_at)`, primary key `(instrument_id, minute_start)`, written one statement a second with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` (a second write is a counted `duplicate`). `market.opening_bar_events` (`started`, `gap`, `low_disk`) tells the derived job what the connection did; the runtime role cannot update or delete either table.
5. **The set** is the derived universe of the previous session, only while a frozen intraday basket is switched on, capped to `OpeningBars:MaxInstruments` (500) by ADV20; the shares cut are named `not_subscribed` in the version, not dropped silently. The recorder and the freeze use the same function.
6. **The freeze.** `FreezeOpeningBasketsHandler` forms each enabled frozen intraday basket once per day at its formation time (09:20 and 09:30) plus the grace and a 3 s settle; the feed host's loop asks for it from 09:20 once the deployment flag is on, whatever the other gates say. Only bars whose minute starts **before** the formation time are read. A share with a missing opening minute is excluded `opening_minute_missing`; the whole day is `not_formed` with `no_live_bars` (recorder not started), `feed_gap` (a start after 09:15:00, a restart, or a drop or silence overlapping the window), `low_disk` or `late` (not formed by `FormBy`, 2 minutes). A missing input is never a pass. Formed and not-formed versions are frozen: a later call that day writes nothing. The engine itself reads a window only when every minute has a bar (an incomplete session is insufficient history).
7. **Stop** at `RecordUntil` (09:31): unsubscribe and close; and when free disk is under the shared `Depth:MinFreeGb`.

## Consequences
On a trading day with all three gates on, baskets 4 and 12 are formed once from complete bars or say plainly why not. With everything OFF (the default) the system is unchanged, and no basket 4 or 12 version appears. No order, account or plan code is touched; an architecture test keeps the recorder away from the live session, the other recorders and the trading code.

## Deviations and interpretations (see the spec's build notes)
- The freeze is asked by the feed host's loop (it owns the clock at 09:20), not by the `derived-baskets` service, which stays an after-EOD service in `xd-api`; the freeze handler is the same use case and is registered in the application layer.
- The switch has an API route and a Services-screen card (owner decision 2026-11-11): fresh password per change, an audit event, insert-only rows.
- Retention (`OpeningBars:KeepDays`, 60) is a SECURITY DEFINER function `market.prune_opening_bars` owned by the migration role and called once a day by the recorder loop only while all three gates are open; the runtime role stays insert-only on the table.
