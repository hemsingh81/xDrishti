# 0248 — The simulate-everything job is a resumable, table-driven queue of training chunks that yields to market hours, disk and the broker

- **Status:** Accepted by delegation (owner, 2026-11-09: unsettled decisions are researched, the best chosen, recorded here).
- **Date:** 2026-11-10.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** Builds on [0243](0243-simulate-everything-is-training-data-fenced-from-the-hold-out-and-from-scorecard-evidence.md) (entry conditions, points 3 and 7). Does not change 0207, 0217, 0220 or 0221.

## Context
AU-12 runs every strategy over every tracked stock across all usable history. It takes hours or days, shares one 10 GiB VM with the live recorders, and must survive a restart, an owner pause and a cancel.

## Decision
1. **Where it runs.** A hosted loop in `xd-api` (like the replay job), not the services framework: a service run has a timeout and one outcome, a multi-day job has neither. Off by default in the sense that matters: with no job row the loop only reads the table every 5 seconds. A job exists only because the owner started it (`POST /api/simulate-all`, session and CSRF, audited as `SimulateAllStarted|Paused|Resumed|Cancelled`).
2. **The tables are the queue and the memory.** `research.sim_all_jobs` (at most one running or paused job, by a partial unique index) and `research.sim_all_chunks` (unique per job, strategy, version, instrument). A chunk is claimed with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, and only while its job is running, so pause and cancel stop the claims at once. At start the host puts chunks left `running` back to `pending` (acceptance 3). A final job is final (trigger); the runtime role cannot delete.
3. **Chunk = one strategy version over one instrument**, built through the one request value (`SimulationRequest.Purpose = Training`, which becomes `RunHeader.Purpose` and `SimulationRun.IsTraining`) and stored through `StoreRunCommand`, so the store gate of 0243 point 6 applies unchanged. The run key makes a repeat a no-op. "Active strategy version" is decided as the latest stored version of each family: older versions are history, and every extra version multiplies the work by the instrument count. A failed chunk keeps its code and a short reason; the job ends `completed_with_failures` when any failed. A chunk past its time limit is failed (`simulate_all.chunk_timeout`); the token reaches the scan loop of the engine (looked at every 4096 minutes), so a CPU-bound replay stops and the worker is freed. A throw outside the executor fails the chunk (`simulate_all.chunk_crashed`); a result that cannot be recorded puts the chunk back to pending; and each tick takes back any chunk running longer than the time limit plus 5 minutes (a worker that died inside a live host). A chunk that keeps failing to record is retried at every tick; the failure is logged each time (no retry counter, to keep the schema small).
4. **Entry conditions (0243 point 7).** The start fails closed, creating nothing, unless the backfill is complete: no bar-history job running, at least one tracked instrument, and every instrument the history phase fetches has stored bars. The same check runs at every tick and at resume. Then the training window must resolve (it ends the embargo before the hold-out block; no determinable block means no job). One `training_sweep` ledger row per strategy version is written before the first chunk of a job runs, with an id derived from job and version so a restart writes nothing twice.
5. **The non-evidence reader.** `ITrainingRunReader` (headers of `purpose = 'training'` runs) is the only way to read training runs. Every statement in it carries `purpose = 'training'`, the mirror of the evidence fence; `EvidenceReaderFenceArchitectureTests` requires that of the file, allows no other file to read training runs, allows only the port, its implementation and its registration to name the reader (the job's own folder may not), and the AU-13 learning folder is added to that list with its own test. Evidence readers cannot reach it: the run store still leaves training runs out.
6. **Yield rules (research, best default chosen).** Market hours: the real session of the trading calendar plus 15 minutes either side (a regular day gives 09:00 to 15:45 IST, acceptance 5; a special session or a holiday is respected). No chunk is claimed when less than `ChunkTimeoutMinutes` remain before the window opens, and the chunk in flight carries a deadline at the window start: it is cancelled and put back, never left to run into the session. A running bar-history job (the end-of-day fetch is one) or a missed-day recovery pass (the broker and the database are busy); free disk under the floor, and an unreadable disk counts as too low; an incomplete backfill. The backfill is asked at most once a minute. System pauses lift by themselves when the condition clears; an owner pause does not. The owner's pause takes over a system pause.
7. **Bounded resources, validated settings (`Simulate:*`, Internal tier, out of range stops start-up).** `Workers` 1..16, default 2: one chunk holds one instrument's bars of the window in memory and the engine is CPU bound, so the worker count is the bound on both, and 2 leaves cores and the 10 GiB VM's memory to the recorders and the database. `MinFreeGb` 10..500, default 40, above the recorders' floor of 20 so the recorders stop last. `RunInMarketHours` default false. `ChunkTimeoutMinutes` 1..240, default 30. `RunWorker` (host switch) default true. Disk: a chunk stores one run (record text plus trades); the floor pauses the job before the disk is full. Broker: the job reads stored bars only and makes no broker call at all (an architecture test pins that the job names no broker type); it also waits while a bar-history job runs.
8. **Progress and estimate.** `GET /api/simulate-all/current` is a plain read of the tables, usable while the job runs (AU-14, AU-15). The estimate (time and disk) is shown once 1 % of the chunks (at least one) are done, from the measured mean, with its basis stated; storage per trade (400 bytes) is a named assumption, not a measurement, and the disk estimate counts the run text and trades only: it excludes indexes and dead rows, so the real growth is larger. Accepted contention: the job also shares CPU with the live feed and the database; the two workers and the disk floor are the bound, and no yield to other services is added beyond the bar-history job and the recovery pass. The job does not stop to wait for the owner at 1 %: the owner can pause or cancel from the estimate, and it runs unattended otherwise.
9. **Not in this slice.** The job stores which versions a sweep covered but does not link a tuned strategy to its sweep (follow-up in AU-13); trades are not yet in the training reader (the first reader that needs them adds them behind the same fence).

## Consequences
A crash costs at most the chunks in flight. A cancel is not instant: a chunk in flight (seconds, bounded by the time limit) finishes and its result is recorded; nothing later starts. Two workers on this VM mean a full run takes days, which the market-hours pause lengthens on purpose.
