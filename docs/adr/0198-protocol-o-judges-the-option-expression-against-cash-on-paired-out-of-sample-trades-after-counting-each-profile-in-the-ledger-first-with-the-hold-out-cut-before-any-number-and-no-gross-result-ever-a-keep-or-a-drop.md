# 0198 — Protocol O judges the option expression against cash on paired out-of-sample trades, after counting each profile in the ledger first, with the hold-out cut before any number and no gross result ever a KEEP or a DROP

- **Status:** Proposed (built in P14-09 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no** (read-only over stored expression runs; it writes ledger rows and its own insert-only records; a KEEP enables research and paper discussion only, `expression` stays `cash` on every decision path and no live option path exists).
- **Date:** 2026-11-04.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0197](0197-the-option-expression-simulator-re-marks-each-stored-cash-trade-as-one-long-option-on-a-fixed-strike-read-from-the-offsets-with-an-expiry-calendar-of-labelled-derived-dates-a-counted-model-price-fallback-and-every-unpaired-trade-counted-by-reason.md) (the rows judged here; the open items it left for this slice are closed in decisions 4 and 7), 0059 (the test ledger, `VerdictEvaluator`, `MultipleTesting`), 0080 (verdict words), 0183 (the comparison core: block bootstrap, hold-out once), 0186 (protocols F, X, P, L in the same table), [0196](0196-the-pairs-study-counts-every-candidate-in-the-ledger-before-any-test-walks-pairs-in-a-separate-pair-1-walker-on-the-simulators-fills-and-judges-them-by-protocol-s-where-a-gross-drop-is-valid-and-a-gross-keep-impossible.md) (protocol S, the pattern followed: ledger first, hold-out cut before any displayed number, Lab gates = `QualificationGates.Default`), 0193 (the profile), 0194 (charges, half-spread, lot feasibility).

## Decision
1. **Where the code lives.** Pure types in `XDrishti.Domain.Options.Evaluation` (`ProtocolO`, `ExpressionStudyOptions`, `ExpressionPair`, `ExpressionTrial`, `ExpressionWindows`, `ExpressionEpochs`, `ExpressionProfiles` and their value types); the family list `ExpressionFamilies` (`expr:option_buy`, protocol letter `O`, version key `expr-1`) beside `PairFamilies` in `Learning/Extensions`, **not** in `ExtensionFamilies.All` (the P15 phase check iterates that list and a test pins that it never asks for this family). The namespace is under `Domain.Options` because it names the Remark types (the archive rule keeps option types inside that folder). The use case `EvaluateExpressionHandler` and its port `IExpressionReader` are in the `OptionExpression` namespaces of Application; the select-only adapter `ExpressionReader` in Infrastructure; the verb `xd options expression-eval` in the host. An architecture test pins the exact sets that name each, that nothing in selection, planning, paper, live trading, the execution service or any other assembly does, and the call order of the handler.
2. **Population: the stored cash run's out-of-sample windows, by geometry.** `--run` is the key of a cash simulation run stored by a **full** Lab test (`created_by = lab:full`; another origin is refused, `expression.run_not_full`) that `xd options remark` has re-marked under the profile. The test windows are those of the Lab's walk-forward (`ExitSelector.Windows` with the default options the full test uses: first window nine months after the first entry, three-month windows), per option side (`CE` for the buys, `PE` for the intraday sells), from the first and last entry date of the side's closed trades (a trade open at the end of the data is left out, as the full test's trades are closed ones). A trade counts when its entry date is inside a window of its side. **The winner of each fold is not re-derived** (that needs every candidate's trades, which only the in-memory full test has; the Lab does not store OOS membership): the run is taken as the cell's chosen exit and every record says so (`oos_windows: geometry ...`). Open question 1.
3. **Ledger first.** The order is fixed in the handler and pinned by a test: a spent hold-out is refused; the profile, the run and the re-mark are looked up (a lookup of a name, no date or figure); the trial (family `expr:option_buy`; text = the profile hash and the population, `expr-trial/1`; hash = SHA-256 of family and text; ledger run id over the run's data window with the prefix `p14-ledger/1`) is appended and **its hash is read back** (`extensions.trial_not_in_ledger` otherwise, nothing computed); only then are the dates, the unpaired counts and the paired rows read. N is the family's distinct trial hashes; a re-run appends nothing and stores the same evaluation id; another profile on the same run (or the same on another run) is another trial and widens the margin. The pre-registered primary profile (`atm`, `nearest_safe`, no premium stop, `max_hold_sessions` 1 for an intraday run) is judged when `--profile` is omitted; every other is secondary and counted.
4. **The unit and the folds.** Per paired trade `d = R_equiv(option) - R_equiv(cash)` (P14-08: same rupee risk, same cost basis). Folds are calendar quarters of the entry date; a quarter with 20 or more paired trades is evaluable (no model is fitted, so no train window, embargo or purge applies inside a fold; the embargo and purge apply at the hold-out edge). `CalendarFolds` is not called: it cuts train and test rows for a fitted comparison, and here the fold is only a grouping, as in protocol S. The shortfalls count the pooled trades of the evaluable folds (`folds 5 of 6`, `paired trades 350 of 400`). The two extra unpaired reasons of ADR 0197 (`spread_unavailable`, `risk_invalid`) are **confirmed**: both are counted fates, never dropped trades, printed by reason with their share; when nothing is paired, `no_paired_trades` is named (for example no stored run trades an index) and, when unpaired trades lost their half-spread, `spread_unavailable` with its count.
5. **The verdict table** (thresholds are the spec's, all in `ExpressionStudyOptions`, hashed into the record): NOT_EVALUABLE with each missing quantity named (folds under 6, pooled paired under 400, no paired trade, a bound unavailable); KEEP needs all of (a) the option's own `R_equiv` per fold as `OosEvidence` (method `walk_forward`, calibration gate 0 as not applicable) passing `VerdictEvaluator` with N and `QualificationGates.Default`, (b) both day-block and 5-session-block lower bounds of mean `d` at or above `0.05 R x Factor(N)` (10,000 draws, seed 1337, one-sided 90 %), (c) mean `d` above 0 in `ceil(2/3 x folds)` folds and in the latest epoch (30 or more pairs, else `latest epoch n 12 of 30`, which is not met), (d) net, (e) a Black-76 fallback share of 5 % or less of the pooled pairs; DROP needs the minima, not KEEP, net, and the larger upper bound under `0.05 R` (not widened); INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. An unavailable input fails a rule and never supports DROP. The record also prints the unpaired shares by reason (a limit above 30 %), the sizable-only line, the `spread_from_recent_archive` and derived-expiry shares, the SD of `d`, the design effect (bootstrap SE over the naive SE) and the minimum detectable effect (2.5 x the naive SE: SD 1.5 R and n = 400 give about 0.19 R). The futures baseline is printed as `NOT_EVALUABLE no_futures_bars`.
6. **Gross results are INCONCLUSIVE, whichever way they point.** Cash charges in R can exceed option charges in R (cash turnover per rupee of risk is far larger), so a gross `d` can be biased either way: it can neither keep nor drop. A gross result whose upper bound is under the margin says so in its limits (`gross upper bound below margin`). The result is gross when any pooled pair has no net figure (P14-08 pairs both sides on one basis, so a run is wholly net or wholly gross).
7. **The fallback cap (P14-08 open item).** The cap is 5 % of the pooled out-of-sample pairs, read from the rows (label `bs_fallback`). The `bs_fallback_share` stored in `expression_runs` counts the whole run (in-sample and hold-out trades included) and is **not** printed or used.
8. **The hold-out is cut before any number is built.** From dates only (the last entry date of the closed trades of any fate, capped by the last window's end: the cut never depends on which trades were paired), the hold-out starts six months earlier plus a day (the cut of `HoldOut.Reserve`). Everything stored or printed for the development verdict is read with the hold-out cut off **in the query** (windows clipped before the start, UTC bounds from the India dates): the paired rows, the unpaired counts, the shares, the epochs. The development pairs of the last five trade dates before the start (embargo) and any that leave on or after it (purge) are dropped and counted. `--holdout` reads it once and only after a development KEEP, as a second record (`holdout_read = true`): at least 100 net paired trades, mean `d` above 0 and the day-block lower bound above 0 confirm (KEEP); fewer than 100 is NOT_EVALUABLE; a gross hold-out confirms nothing. The partial unique index on `(family, version_key)` refuses a second read (`extensions.holdout_used`, checked before anything is looked up). The version key `expr-1` is shared by every profile of the family: **one reading of the hold-out for the whole family** (open question 3); because of that, `--holdout` is accepted for the primary profile only (`expression.holdout_primary_only` otherwise), so a secondary profile cannot consume the family's one reading. When every paired trade lies in the hold-out, the record says `paired trades 0 of 400 (every paired trade lies in the hold-out from <date>)` instead of `no_paired_trades`. A shortcut: the embargo and the 5-session sensitivity blocks count trade dates of the paired trades, not exchange sessions.
9. **The latest epoch is judged on development data only.** From 2025-09-01 (the stored `nifty_expiry_tuesday` epoch when present). With a six-month hold-out most of a one-year epoch can lie in the hold-out, so even with five years of bars the latest epoch holds few development pairs and the first answers are mostly INCONCLUSIVE (P14-00 risk 1).
10. **Contract.** `xd options expression-eval --family expr:option_buy --run RUNKEY [--profile HASH] [--holdout]`: exit 0 whatever the verdict, 1 refused (unknown run, profile or family, a run not from a full test, a run not re-marked, a trial not in the ledger, a spent hold-out), 2 a bad line, 3 failure, 130 cancelled. `GET /api/research/extensions?family=expr:option_buy` and `/{id}` list the records (the summary text names the family; contract regenerated). Log events `expression.evaluated` and `expression.refused` (ids 7070, 7071) carry the family, the verdict word, ids and counts only. No migration: the records are `research.extension_evaluations` rows of protocol `O`, fold unit `quarter`.

## Honest limits (printed in every record)
- The OOS windows are the Lab's geometry; which exit won each fold is not re-derived (decision 2). A run that is not the cell's chosen exit is still judged on those windows.
- Survivorship (ISS-22) and the recent-spread assumption flatter both sides unequally; the bar timestamp, the IV unit, the open-interest unit and the derived expiry weekdays are UNVERIFIED (ADR 0197); the fill is the next option bar's open.
- Expected first answer: NOT_EVALUABLE (`folds 0 of 6` until five years of bars exist, CF-04 Part B; `spread_unavailable` until 60 usable sessions or an owner assumption; gross and at best INCONCLUSIVE until the owner loads the option and cash charge lines).
- Only a large edge (about 0.2 R per trade at 400 pairs) is detectable.
- 10,000 paired trades with two block lengths in 30 s is UNVERIFIED.

## Open questions for the owner
1. **Out-of-sample membership.** Should the Lab store which trades were out of sample (the winner's test-window trades) so that protocol O can use the exact set instead of the windows' geometry? Default: no; the geometry is printed as a limit.
2. **The Lab's gates as the bar for an expression.** (a) judges the option's own `R_equiv` on `QualificationGates.Default` (30 trades, 0.15 R, profit factor 1.2, 60 % positive folds, 10 R drawdown) scaled by the factor of N for trades, expectancy and profit factor, as for pairs (ADR 0196). Is that the right bar, or should the expression be judged only by the paired difference (b) and (c)? Default: keep the Lab's gates; none is invented.
3. **One hold-out reading per family or per profile.** Default: per family (a second profile cannot read it), because each profile read is another look at the same months.
4. **The 2.5 multiple of the standard error** for the printed minimum detectable effect is the spec's power note, not a tuned value.
