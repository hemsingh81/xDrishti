# 0280 — The assistant reads the stored replay, readiness and shadow results through one tool over six more read-only GETs of the same service token, and the labels travel unchanged

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [AI-01](../specs/AI-01-assistant-reads-replay-and-readiness.md), approved 2026-11-12; built 2026-11-12). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-11-12.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** extends [ADR 0267](0267-the-tool-server-reads-the-two-basket-reports-from-the-api-with-its-own-read-only-token-accepted-on-exactly-those-two-routes-and-the-eval-reads-its-own-api-over-the-fixture.md) (the token and its route list: now 2 + 6); reads what [ADR 0272](0272-the-system-replay-is-research-only-fenced-from-evidence-and-hindsight-labelled-and-runs-the-live-pieces-over-as-of-facts-in-memory.md) (replay), ADR 0221 (readiness) and ADR 0212 (shadow summary) already compute; the eval stays as [ADR 0143](0143-the-eval-grades-the-assistant-by-code-on-a-synthetic-fixture-read-only-with-exact-numbers-intervals-and-an-owner-accepted-baseline.md) made it.

## Context
The owner can ask "how did the last replay do?", "is strategy X ready?", "what does the shadow record say?" and the assistant had no tool for any of them. The labels (hindsight, gross or net, `unavailable`) are computed by the API; a second reader of the tables would have to repeat them.

## Decision
1. **Through the API, same token, a second narrow policy.** `AssistantEvidenceRead` (schemes: the owner session first, then `xd-service-read`) is on exactly six GETs: `replay/runs`, `replay/runs/{id}`, `replay/runs/{id}/equity`, `confidence/scorecards`, `confidence/scorecards/{strategyId}/versions/{version}` and `paper/shadow/summary`. Every other route, every write and the replay sessions and trades still need the owner's session. `ServiceReadTokenTests` pins the route count at 2 + 6 and the policy by name; revoking the token closes all eight.
2. **One tool, `get_evidence(kind, id?, strategyId?, version?)`**, kind `replay_list | replay_run | readiness_list | readiness_card | shadow_summary` (the equity route is open to the token but the tool does not use it). The tool takes only `IAiEvidenceReader`; `ApiEvidenceReader` over `EvidenceApiClient` (folder `Infrastructure/Ai/ReadOnly/EvidenceApi`, the same `Mcp:Api` settings and token) is its implementation. Its allow-list is a delegating handler: only a GET of the five paths on the configured host.
3. **The labels are part of the answer.** The API's body is handed on unchanged under `body` (hindsight labels with their sentences, costs label, states, the shadow summary's "separate count" statement). Every replay answer also carries a fixed fence, "Replay is research only, not evidence: ...", in `fence` and in the notes; a readiness answer carries "unavailable is never a pass". A test fails if a label is dropped.
4. **Fail closed** as ADR 0267 section 4: no address or token, a transport error, a timeout, a status other than 200, 404 or 422, a body over 2 MiB or not JSON is `tool.unavailable` ("stored results are not reachable now") and nothing else is called; 404 is `evidence.not_found`, 422 `tool.invalid_arguments`.
5. **The eval API serves the six GETs.** `xd-api-eval` (`Api:ReportsOnly`) now also maps the replay, readiness and shadow modules, behind a gate that answers 404 to every route of those modules that is not one of the six, before any authentication. The fixture gains one completed replay run (3 sessions, 2 closed gross trades, labels `filters_without_history`, `survivorship`, before costs), version 1 of its two strategies (their scorecards are derived and honestly Not ready with the unavailable inputs named) and one shadow session of 12 graded candidates. The seeder switches the shadow tables' user triggers off for its one transaction (they stamp their own clock and refuse a row after the open of its session) and on again before the commit; it runs only as the owner into `xdrishti_eval`.
6. **Four eval questions** (`eval/sets/evidence-questions.jsonl`, EQ-01 to EQ-04, an addendum like the basket questions: its own file, not in the locked 30 and their threshold); a question whose `get_evidence` says `tool.unavailable` is `tool_not_evaluable`, never a pass. `EvalEvidenceGoldenTests` holds their numbers (return 1.2 %, mean R 37/120 with the 90 % interval up to 49/60).

## Consequences
- The fixture hash changes: the accepted eval baseline reads stale until **the owner re-seeds (`scripts/eval-db.sh reset`), re-runs the eval and accepts a new baseline**. Nothing here runs or accepts one.
- The schema budget (2,300 tokens) is kept: the new tool costs about 150 tokens, bought by shortening the descriptions of `calc`, `describe_table`, `get_learning`, `get_plan`, `lab_validate`, `list_runs`, `list_tables` and `run_sql` (no word a test or the assistant relied on). The golden of the tool list is regenerated.
- No migration, no new secret, no new network, no write route reachable with the token; `McpComposeRulesTests` and `EvalComposeRulesTests` are unchanged.
- Two architecture rules about the replay tables (`ReplayArchitectureTests` writers, `EvidenceReaderFenceArchitectureTests` namers) name `FixtureSeeder.cs` as one explicit, commented exemption: it INSERTs the synthetic replay run of `xdrishti_eval` and nothing else, and reads only the emptiness check of the eval database. The rules are otherwise unchanged (a seeder test asserts it only inserts into the three tables).
