# 0260 — Correlated names share one risk cap by average-linkage clusters, and the gate can read a basket's own breadth; both are off by default and every change is an owner act

- **Status:** Accepted (spec BK-08, approved by the owner 2026-11-03). **Safety slice: YES** (it changes which candidates survive the fill and which breadth the market gate reads; it can only add a limit; it places no order).
- **Date:** 2026-11-11.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0084](0084-portfolio-fill-greedy-with-every-limit-rechecked-and-unavailable-blocking.md) (the fill this adds one check to), [0085](0085-smart-rules-in-the-plan-gate-and-throttle-and-same-bet-with-unavailable-never-a-pass.md) (the gate; unavailable is never a pass), [0033](0033-weekly-monthly-timeframes-calendar-features-and-the-market-context.md) (the tracked-set breadth), [0091](0091-auto-approval-is-an-option-behind-an-owner-setting-default-off-a-new-approval-kind-the-database-checks.md) (owner acts), [0259](0259-position-sizing-only-scales-down-by-volatility-capacity-and-basket-budgets-and-every-setting-is-an-owner-act-with-a-fresh-password.md) (the precedent for the audit and the OFF-is-identical test), [0242](0242-few-visible-settings-and-learned-changes-tighten-automatically-while-loosening-needs-the-owners-password.md) and [0247](0247-learned-setting-changes-are-bounded-steps-on-a-short-allow-list-stored-in-an-insert-only-log-and-applied-at-the-next-start.md) (descriptors, allow-list).

## Context
The fill counted five banking longs as five independent bets (it has a pair rule and a sector count, not a bet count), and the market gate read the breadth of the owner's own tracked set, whatever that happened to be. The spec asks for a cap on open risk per correlation cluster and for a gate that can read the breadth of a stated, liquid basket, with that basket's breadth, dispersion and average correlation stored point in time each day.

## Decision
1. **Average linkage on 1 - rho, cut at `ClusterRho` (0.70, the design's pair limit).** `CorrelationClusters` (pure, `Domain/Selection`) merges the two groups of the highest average pair correlation while that average is at least the cut. Single linkage would chain: one 0.72 pair would make four unrelated names one cap. A pair with no figure is unknown and is left out of the average (never 0 or 1); a symbol with no known pair at all (among several) is a singleton flagged `correlation_unknown`. Cluster ids are the smallest member symbol, so they do not depend on the input order. Pairs with too few common sessions are already absent from the `CorrelationTable` (30 of 60), so no separate `MinCommonSessions` exists.
2. **The cap is a fill limit, OFF by default.** `FillConstraints.MaxClusterRiskPct` (percent of capital, null = OFF) adds one check, `cluster_cap`, after the pair rule and the sector, mix and basket counts: open risk of the cluster (book, earlier picks and this ticket, absolute risks) is at most the cap ("not above"). A cluster, a ticket risk, or an open position's risk or cluster that is not known is `unavailable` and blocks, unless the known total already fails. With the cap OFF the check is not added, no clusters are built, no text is written and the plan is byte-identical to before (the hash included; tested).
3. **A cap broken against the open book alone is a rejection, not a reserve.** The fill's rule (ADR 0084) is that a reserve is admissible on its own against the book; the cluster cap follows it. A cap broken only by this plan's earlier picks is a reserve with code `cluster_cap`. (The spec's example, a candidate in a cluster already full from the book, says reserve; the built semantics say rejected, and one behaviour for every limit was the spec's own decision 3. Owner question.)
4. **The ticket says what the cap did, only when it is ON.** The reasons carry `cluster {A, B}: open risk 1.1% of cap 1%` (percent of capital: the plan has several accounts, so there is no single rupee capital) or `unknown correlation`. No checklist check is added (the 13 stay).
5. **Only the plan-day symbols are clustered:** the candidates that reach the fill plus the open book, from the correlations the plan already computes. No nightly whole-set clustering (labels would flip between runs).
6. **The gate's breadth source** is a setting, `tracked` (default, exactly the P3-01e value) or `basket:<id>`. With a basket the gate reads the 20-day breadth (`b20`) of the newest stored row dated before the planned day; no row, a row not before the day, a row older than `GateMaxAgeDays` or an `insufficient` row is `unavailable`, never a pass and never the tracked figure (a fall-back would hide the change of definition on a bad data day). The note names the basket: "breadth of Nifty 50: 0.62". The VIX is read as before.
7. **`market.basket_context_daily`** (insert-once, rows never updated, `xd_app` insert and select only) is written after the context step of the end-of-day post-steps for the gate basket only (the owner's choice), for each of the latest five closed sessions without a row, by the BK-06 kernels (`BasketBreadth`, `Dispersion`, `PairwiseCorrelation`): the tracked-set breadth is that function called with another member set, so the two cannot define breadth differently. The row of a day uses closes up to that day and the members as of it (property-tested: appending later bars changes nothing). The last closed session waits until every member has its bar (as the tracked breadth does). `membership_basis` is `point_in_time` only when the basket's dated membership reaches the day, else `single_snapshot`.
8. **Every change is an owner act with a fresh password, in both directions.** `PUT /api/selection/basket-settings` takes the session, the CSRF token and the password (checked on every change, the elevation window untouched); the actor is the session's owner; `BasketSettingChanges` refuses a non-owner before the store and the table checks `changed_by LIKE 'owner:%'` again. The table `selection.basket_settings_changes` is insert-only (the P7-12 store is fixed-column, so a sibling table); each key that changes is a row with old and new value, direction, actor, database time and hash; the newest row per key wins; each accepted change also writes one account event `BasketSettingsChanged` (no password). Direction: a lower cap or a cap set from OFF, a lower cut: `tighten`; a higher or cleared cap, a higher cut, any change of the breadth source: `loosen`; a cut changed while the cap is OFF before and after: `neutral`.
9. **No auto path.** The three keys are `Stored` descriptors (Advanced, safer `none`, not learnable) and on no allow-list, so neither Auto nor learning changes them (ADR 0247). The owner rule of an auto option (a setting, default OFF, recorded as auto) has nothing to apply to here: the cap and the source apply when the owner turns them on, and nothing else ever does. `unavailable` inputs never count as a pass. No setting allows an order.

## Consequences
- OFF is the old behaviour everywhere: no cap, the tracked breadth, a plan with the same hash.
- One more read of the settings table per plan build and per gate read (a few rows).
- The cluster cut and cap are estimates on 60 sessions of daily returns: clusters near the cut flip between nights, and correlation rises in a sell-off. This is a risk reducer, not a guarantee.

## Known limits
- The banner "gate source changed until dismissed" (criterion 11) is moved to BK-10b with the sizing banner; the settings read shows the newest loosening.
- Shortcut: the basket context uses raw closes (no corporate-action adjustment) and **no ex-date exclusion** (the tracked-set breadth excludes a share with an ex-date in its window), so a split inside a window can make one member vote wrongly. Upgrade when the adjusted close series and the ex-dates are wired into the basket step.
- Only the latest five closed sessions are caught up; an older backfill is a separate run.
- The gate source is logged by the Infrastructure source (event 7395: basket, row state, available, reason code); the plan note carries it too. The age check uses the configured `GateMaxAgeDays`, and the gate input is stamped with the older of the VIX day and the row day.
