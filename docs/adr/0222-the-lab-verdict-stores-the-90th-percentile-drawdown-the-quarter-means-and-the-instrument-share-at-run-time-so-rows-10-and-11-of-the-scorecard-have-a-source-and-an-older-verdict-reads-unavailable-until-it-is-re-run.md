# 0222 — The Lab verdict stores the 90th percentile drawdown, the quarter means and the instrument share at run time, so rows 10 and 11 of the scorecard have a source, and an older verdict reads `unavailable` until it is re-run

- **Status:** Proposed (built in scorecard-sources slice S1, from the owner-Approved CF-11 spec). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no.** Report only: nothing places an order, sizes a trade, changes a verdict or moves a cell.
- **Date:** 2026-11-07.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** narrows the port of [0221](0221-the-readiness-scorecard-is-derived-and-read-only-unavailable-is-a-fail-and-the-owners-enforce-switch-off-by-default-makes-the-two-lifecycle-gates-refuse-instead-of-warn.md) (nine inputs without a producer become six); adds members to the robustness block of [0062](0062-robustness-sensitivity-monte-carlo-by-year-and-a-sign-flip-baseline.md); the block bootstrap of [0205](0205-the-lab-verdict-shows-a-deflated-sharpe-ratio-from-stored-trial-statistics-and-a-session-level-stationary-block-bootstrap-beside-the-factor-and-the-monte-carlo-in-report-only-mode-and-gate-is-not-offered.md) is left as it is.

## Context
CF-11 row 10 wants the share of calendar quarters at or above -0.05 R, no year over 60 % and no instrument over 15 % of the total R; row 11 wants the observed drawdown within the Monte Carlo 90th percentile and the 95th under the budget. The Lab verdict stored the by-year table, the 50th and the 95th percentile of the drawdown, and no quarters and no instruments, so ADR 0221 put the three missing figures behind a port that says `unavailable`. The out-of-sample trades themselves are **not stored** (`lab.lab_results.result` holds the verdict JSON only), so the figures cannot be derived later from a stored verdict: they have to be computed when the Lab runs and be added to the verdict.

## Decision
1. **Three figures are computed in the robustness stage and stored in the verdict (jsonb, no migration).** `MonteCarloResult.DrawdownP90` (the 90th percentile of the bootstrap's largest fall, nearest rank, the same rule as the stored 50th and 95th and as `BacktestBand`'s 90th), `RobustnessView.ByQuarter` (a row per calendar quarter that has at least one out-of-sample trade: year, quarter, trades, mean R) and `RobustnessView.MaxInstrumentShare` (the largest instrument's total R over the overall total). All three are **optional members in the last position**, so a document stored before them deserialises with them null.
2. **Pure Domain types.** `QuarterBreakdown` (beside `YearBreakdown`; the floor is a parameter, so it names nothing of the readiness slice) and `InstrumentShare`. No clock, I/O or randomness (an architecture test, which also pins that the `VerdictEvaluator` source names none of them). The draws, the seeds and the stored 50th and 95th are untouched: the 90th is one more read of the sorted array the bootstrap already has (hand-worked goldens at seed 0 pin all three).
3. **The scorecard reads them from the verdict, not from the port.** `LabEvidenceReader` fills `McDrawdownP90`, `QuarterShare` (the share of the stored quarter means at or above `EdgeChecklist.QuarterFloorR`, -0.05) and `MaxInstrumentShare`. The three methods leave `IReadinessGaps` (nine to six); `UnavailableReadinessGaps` and the fixtures follow; the architecture test keeps one implementation, unavailable-only answers and only-the-reader-reads-the-port.
4. **The verdict does not read them.** `VerdictEvaluator` reads neither the robustness block nor the new members: PASS, MARGINAL and FAIL and every gate are unchanged (a test over the source and the existing verdict goldens). Row 10 and 11 are scorecard rows only.
5. **An older verdict reads `unavailable: produced before the scorecard sources existed; re-run`** beside rows 10 and 11 (and so counts as a fail, like every unavailable input). Nothing re-runs automatically.

## Interpretations (proposals, for the owner)
- **Quarters with at least one trade.** A calendar quarter with no trade is not a row and is not a quarter below the floor: it is no observation. The quarter is that of the entry date. A mean of exactly -0.05 R passes (compared to nine decimals, so the last bit of a sum does not decide). With no trade there is no share (unavailable).
- **Instrument share when the total is not above 0 is 1.0**, the reading the by-year share already takes in `LabEvidenceReader`, so a strategy that does not make money cannot pass the concentration limit by having a negative total. When another instrument lost, the largest one can have made more than the total; the share is capped at 1.0 ("all of it").
- **The 90th percentile is that of the plain Monte Carlo only.** The block bootstrap (ADR 0205) stores a 50th and a 95th and no 90th. Adding one would change the block's stored shape for no figure the scorecard needs, and the choice between its 90th and the plain one is ambiguous: "observed within" is looser with the larger 90th and stricter with the smaller. The block's 95th already enters row 11 as the larger of the two 95ths (as before). Owner question 2.
- **Per-instrument figure is by instrument id** (not symbol), on the out-of-sample trades paired in order with the simulated trades the full test keeps (`OosSources`); if they were not kept, or their count differs from the trades' (never a guess at which R belongs to which instrument), the share is null and the row says so.

## Owner questions
1. **May an existing version be re-run to fill the new figures?** A re-run is the only way to fill rows 10 and 11 for a version already tested, and it is one more look at the same version: a new `robust` run adds a null look to the trial ledger (the random-entry null, the placebo set and the sweep points are rows), so the honest trial count N rises and **Factor(N) rises, which makes rows 3 and 6 stricter.** Nothing here runs it; it is yours to ask for.
2. **Plain or block 90th for "observed within"?** This slice uses the plain Monte Carlo. If you want the block bootstrap's 90th (the ADR 0205 interval accounts for same-day clustering), say whether the larger or the smaller should decide.

## Consequences
For a version run after this slice, rows 10 and 11 of the scorecard are judged from the Lab's own out-of-sample trades and can pass, fail or be unavailable on their merits; for an older version they read `unavailable` with the instruction to re-run. The verdicts, the gates and the draws are unchanged. The roadmap's remaining gaps are rows 13, 14 and 15, the integrity and tier-C hard fails and the risk share.
