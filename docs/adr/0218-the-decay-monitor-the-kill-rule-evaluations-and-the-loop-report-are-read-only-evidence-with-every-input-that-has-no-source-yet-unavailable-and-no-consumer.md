# 0218 — The decay monitor, the kill-rule evaluations and the loop report are read-only evidence, with every input that has no source yet unavailable and no consumer

Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no** in this part (pure calculators and two GET reads; nothing is proposed, stored, paused, changed or executed, and no order exists). - **Relation to other ADRs:** reads the edge series of [0211](0211-the-shadow-record-writes-every-nightly-candidate-before-the-open-forward-only-and-insert-only-chained-by-digest-as-a-count-separate-from-the-p10-paper-count-and-the-switch-is-the-owners-and-off-by-default.md) and [0212](0212-the-shadow-record-is-graded-after-the-close-through-the-one-replay-path-summarised-as-raw-and-effective-n-labelled-gross-unless-the-sheet-is-confirmed-and-switched-by-the-owner-with-a-fresh-password.md) (CF-08) by the same rule as the sequential failure test of [0213](0213-the-paper-edge-series-gets-a-sequential-failure-test-that-flags-a-probable-failure-early-with-the-odds-stated-and-never-passes-anything.md) (CF-09, whose failure boundary is the fourth kill input); reuses the P6-09 detectors (ADR 0074, unchanged), the paired day-cluster bootstrap of [0216](0216-the-probability-that-a-model-beats-the-rung-below-comes-from-a-paired-day-cluster-bootstrap-with-a-fold-sign-test-and-sample-uniqueness-as-evidence-only.md) (CF-10c-1) and the point-in-time backtest reference of P10-04 (ADR 0122); follows the pattern of [0217](0217-a-model-may-influence-a-decision-only-through-one-gate-whose-predicate-needs-an-unspent-hold-out-forward-evidence-no-calibration-alarm-and-the-owners-switch.md) (an evidence port with one source that says nothing is available, consumers pinned empty).

## Context
Spec CF-12 asks that the owner be told when a live-forward edge is wearing out, with the rule and the numbers, that a failing strategy can be stopped, and that the owner can see whether the learning loop is earning its keep. Part 12a is the read-only evidence half: the calculators and two reads. Proposals, the auto setting, the re-enable act, the lifecycle change, the daily service, the migration, the verbs and the screens are 12b and 12c.

## Decision
1. **Pure Domain calculators (`XDrishti.Domain.Learning`; no I/O, no clock, seeded randomness only).** They live beside the P6-09 and P6-12 code they read (the confidence namespace may not depend on Learning, an existing rule), and each is pinned by the spec's hand table in a unit test.
   - `DecayMonitor` (D1): non-overlapping windows of 60 trades (the trades ordered by the instant they were known, then key); a window alerts when its mean R is **strictly below** `mu_ref - z sigma sqrt(DEFF / 60 + 1 / N_ref)` (`DecayMonitor.Threshold`: -0.0910 with DEFF 1, -0.1975 with DEFF 2.5, and -0.0736 if the reference error were ignored); DEFF is the mean design effect of the window's days (`1 + (m - 1) rho`, rho the placeholder 0.3 of CF-08 and CF-09). The reference is the P6-09 `ExpectancyBand` (mean, sd, trade count from `RollingBandDetector.MakeBand`, which needs 30 trades and a spread). "Twice in a row" is the streak of completed windows below their level counted back from the latest (a window above resets it); the report also keeps the window at which the streak first reached 2. The **P6-09 CUSUM** is run on the same series by the existing `CusumDetector` (reused, not copied) against the same reference; a **by-quarter table** gives trades, mean R and the share of quarters strictly below the floor.
   - `KillRules` (D2): evaluation functions that return a verdict with its numbers (`KillRuleVerdict`: rule, state, value, limit, trades, text) and nothing else. (a) **cost stress**: on the last 100 trades `net(1.5) = net(1) - 0.5 c` strictly below zero fires (a value within 1e-12 of zero is zero: `0.05` with `c = 0.12` gives -0.01 fires, `0.07` gives +0.01 holds, `0.06` gives 0.00 holds); fewer than 100 trades is `not_evaluated`; a rule that fired needs **20 new trades since it last fired** before it fires again (review fix 3), else the state is `held`. (b) **drawdown**: the series' largest fall strictly above 2 times the 95th percentile of the matched draws at the series' own n (percentile 8 R: 17 R fires, 16.0 R holds); the factor is stated in the text (review fix 4). (c) **decay twice in a row**. (d) the **CF-09 failure boundary**, marked `ProposalOnly`: it already hard-fails the scorecard, so it may only ever create a proposal and the auto setting (12b) must never execute it. A missing or short input is `not_evaluated`, which is neither a pass nor a fail. The numbers 100, 1.5, 2 and 20 are arguments with the spec's defaults; the `confidence.kill.*` settings are 12b.
   - `ThreeArmLoopTest` (D4): arm A frozen champion, B champion now, C no model (take all); A and B take a candidate when its score is at or above that arm's own train base rate; an arm's value is the candidate's R when taken and 0 when skipped; the paired differences are resampled by whole trade day with the reused `PairedDayBootstrap`. **Retrained versus frozen** and **retrained versus no model** are reported separately (review fix 2), each with the mean difference, the 90 % interval, P(first arm better) and P(first arm worse). The reading: P(better) at or above `HelpsAt` (0.95) **helps**; P(worse) at or above a **fixed** bar of 0.95 **hurts**; else **no measurable difference** (an answer); fewer than 20 distinct days **inconclusive**. The common set is the candidates the retrained arm scored; arm A is compared only if it scored every one of them. The exact oracle (+10, -5, +20 on three days: 23 of 27 resamples better, 0.8519, no measurable difference) is a unit test against the bootstrap.
   - `LearningCurve` (D5): OLS over the last 3 to 5 gains; with 4 or more points rising if `slope - 1.645 SE > 0`, flat if `slope + 1.645 SE < min_slope` (0.002 per 50 rows), else unclear; with 3 points unclear (no standard error); when the slope is above zero the rows at which the line reaches the target gain 0.01 are projected (`-0.004, 0.001, 0.005` at 50, 100, 150: slope 0.00009 per row, 203.7 rows, 54 more; a projection from a curve not shown to be rising is labelled "not a finding"). The refit loop that produces the gains is **not** built.
   - `ReliabilitySlope` (D6): the logistic regression of the outcome on `logit(p_hat)` (intercept and slope, Newton from (0, 1), probabilities held in [1e-6, 1 - 1e-6]) over the trailing 200 outcomes; alarm when the slope is below 0.8 or above 1.2 **and** more than 2 standard errors from 1; with fewer than 200 outcomes it is `shown_only` and never alarms; a fit that cannot be made (one kind of outcome, equal predictions, separation) is `unavailable`. Oracle: predictions 0.2 and 0.8 with observed 0.35 and 0.65 give 0.4465 and an alarm, observed 0.2 and 0.8 give 1.0. The P6-09 rolling ECE and gap test are unchanged (a test pins 12 pp: no breach at 100 outcomes, a breach at 200).
   - `DecisionLift` (D7): within each night the scored candidates are ranked by score (ties by key), the top and bottom `m / 2` are the halves (the middle one of an odd night and unscored candidates are in neither); mean R of the top half against the bottom with a pooled standard error and z, and the share of approved tickets in the top half against 50 % (`z = (p - 0.5) / sqrt(0.25 / m)`); a figure under 1.96 is said to be not significant. Hand tables: lift 0.30, SE 0.3795, z 0.79; 21 of 30, z 2.19.
2. **Two read handlers (`GetDecayReportHandler`, `GetLoopReportHandler`).** One use case each, internal and sealed, registered explicitly, derived on every request, nothing stored. The edge series is assembled by shared helpers used by **both** this handler and the sequential handler, so the two cannot read different series: `EdgeSeries.Select` (rows of the epoch and scope, cut at the first session with an ungraded candidate), `EdgeSeries.Figures` and `EdgeSeries.Basis` (cost basis, the figure, the order by session then trade key, the trades left out) and `BehaviouralSeries.Select` (the counted, finite P10 trades with their entry-day candidates and exit day); the sequential handler's output is unchanged and its tests are untouched. A graded shadow trade is taken as known at its session's date at 00:00 India time (Unix seconds, the order of sessions and not a market time). The decay handler judges the edge series (graded shadow trades that count for edge, current epoch) and the P10 behavioural series beside it, and evaluates the four kill inputs on the edge series. The loop handler asks one evidence port (`ILoopEvidence`) and hands each present section to its calculator.
3. **`ILoopEvidence` has one source and it says nothing is available (`NoLoopEvidence`).** The frozen champion's scoring of old features, stored scores per candidate, the epoch's frozen artifact, the refit loop and outcomes stored with their probabilities have no source (`// shortcut:` in the code); each section of the loop report says `unavailable` with its reason. Tests pin that this is the only implementation (found by reflection over every project, with a sentinel) and that only the loop handler reads the port; the calculators and the handler are tested with a fake that supplies data.
4. **Routes (read only).** `GET /api/confidence/decay?strategy=` (400 `paper.invalid` for a missing strategy or one holding `|`; 404 `paper.not_found` when the strategy has **neither** a shadow edge candidate **nor** a counted paper trade in the current epoch) and `GET /api/confidence/loop`. A test pins that the endpoint file maps no other verb. `scripts/gen-api.sh` was run (contract and `frontend/src/shared/api/schema.d.ts`).
5. **Settings `Confidence:Decay` (`WindowTrades` 60, `Sigmas` 1.5, `QuarterFloorR` -0.05) and `Confidence:Loop` (`MinDays` 20, `Draws` 2000, `Level` 0.90, `HelpsAt` 0.95, `MinSlopePer50Rows` 0.002, `TargetGain` 0.01, `SlopeLow` 0.8, `SlopeHigh` 1.2, `SlopeSe` 2).** They are the spec's `confidence.decay.*` and `confidence.loop.*` keys as configuration sections like `Confidence:Sequential` and `Confidence:PairedBootstrap`, **not in the versioned home of ADR 0002** (a stored, owner-edited version is a later part). Bound, range-validated at start, and **configuration can only tighten them** where a looser value would weaken a bar: window at most 60 (20 to 60), `Sigmas` at most 1.5, quarter floor -0.05 up to 0, at least 2000 draws, an interval of at least 0.90, a "helps" bar of at least 0.95, the band no wider than 0.8 to 1.2 and the standard-error test no wider than 2. Three things are deliberately **not** tighten-only settings: the reliability window is fixed at 200 outcomes (a longer window would only delay the alarm, so it is not a setting) and the "hurts" bar is fixed at P(worse) 0.95 (a lower bar would flag harm on thin evidence, a higher one would follow `HelpsAt` up), and `MinDays` (20 to 250) is a trade-off, not a tightening, since more days make every verdict rarer and later. There are no `kill` settings and no mode or switch. The hash of the settings is part of every response.
6. **Enforcement (`DecayLoopArchitectureTests`), each scan with a positive control and a sentinel.** The calculators are pure Domain (no I/O, clock, system random, cryptography or Application types; a source scan for `DateTime` and `Random`); Planning, Selection and Autonomy (Domain and Application) name nothing of the slice; the lifecycle (cell state and move types and the Application learning namespace) names nothing of it; **no other type reads the slice (the consumers are pinned empty)** and a first consumer must be added to the test in review; the P6-09 and P6-12 modules, the bootstrap and the backtest band name none of the new types; the handlers name no store, writer, clock, logger or command; and no proposal, apply, re-enable type, kill setting or migration exists. `Cf12GoldenTests` pin the SHA-256 of the serialised output of the P6-09 band, CUSUM and calibration detectors and of the P6-12 counterfactual report over fixed inputs, taken from the code as it stood before this slice (AC12).

## What is available today, and so what the reports say
- **Decay report:** needs a strategy with graded shadow edge trades in the current epoch and at least 30 backtest trades of the strategy (with a spread) before the epoch's start for the reference; otherwise the series is `unavailable` with the reason. The edge series is judged only against a reference of the same cost basis (net with net, gross with gross). Cost stress needs a net series (the charges are gross R minus net R) and 100 trades; drawdown is always `not_evaluated` (below); decay twice needs two completed windows; the failure boundary needs a series.
- **Loop report:** every section is `unavailable` today (item 3).

## UNVERIFIED answered
- **Can the P10-04 band code give the 95th percentile of the max drawdown at an arbitrary n?** Yes for a series whose trades carry a cell: `BacktestBand.Compute` draws at the paper set's own n (`paper.Count`) from the backtest trades of each trade's cell and returns `DrawdownP95`. It cannot be asked for the **edge series**, because a shadow row records no cell (only the trade key `strategy|symbol|side|date`), so rule (b) is `not_evaluated` there with that reason (`// shortcut:`). Recording the cell on the shadow row (or a strategy-level unmatched draw, which is a different, weaker comparison) is a later step.
- **Does `Paused` for all cells of a strategy mean "paper size zero"?** Not touched in 12a (no lifecycle change); open for 12b.
- **How the epoch's frozen artifact is loaded for scoring old features:** it is not; there is no source (item 3).
- **Learning-curve refit cost:** not measured; the refit loop is not built.

## Deviations and interpretations (proposals, for the owner)
- **Reference source.** The spec takes `mu_ref`, `sigma` and `N_ref` from the out-of-sample plus hold-out trades of the Lab result. There is no per-strategy port for those; the reference is the strategy's backtest trades before the epoch started as read for P10-04 (`BacktestReferenceReader`), which include in-sample trades. In-sample trades typically raise `mu_ref`, so this makes an alert earlier, not later. The response says so.
- **Namespace.** The calculators are in `XDrishti.Domain.Learning`, not `Confidence`: an existing architecture rule forbids the confidence namespace from depending on Learning (the CUSUM, the bootstrap and the calibration records live there). The two options records stay in `Confidence`.
- **Direction of the three-arm labels.** "First arm" is the retrained arm: `retrained_vs_frozen` (is retraining helping) and `retrained_vs_no_ml` (is the model helping). "Hurts" is read as P(worse) at or above the bar, which equals the spec's "P(better) at or below 0.05" except where resamples tie at exactly zero (two arms that took the same candidates), which is never harm; the bootstrap counts a tie against "better".
- **Twenty new trades.** Read as "since the rule last fired", not since the last evaluation of any kind (the latter would stop it from firing again for twenty trades after every quiet evaluation). There is no record of when a rule fired until 12b, so the handler does not apply the wait; the function does and is tested.
- **Cost c.** `c` is the charges in R (gross R minus net R of the shadow row); a trade with a negative cost (gross below net) makes the rule `not_evaluated`. The slippage of the modelled fill is inside both and is not recorded apart, so `c` is understated and the stress is lighter than CF-05's (`// shortcut:`, and said in the response text). A gross series has no charges to stress and is `not_evaluated`.
- **The 3-point curve** shows its projection with "not a finding" although the verdict is unclear; the spec's hand table asks for the numbers.
- **`HelpsAt` is a setting and the "hurts" bar is fixed at 0.95**; the spec's `curve_step` (50) is not a setting yet because the refit loop that uses it is not built.
- **Days are not resampled in the lift** (a plain standard error), so it is a description and not a gate.
- **Seeds** are made from the seed key the caller gives (sha256 inside the bootstrap); the loop handler takes the key from the evidence.
- **Not built (12b and 12c):** kill proposals and their table, the apply and the auto setting, re-enable, the lifecycle change, the decay-monitor service, the `auto` changelog use, the migration `AddDecayAndKill`, the verbs `xd learn decay|loop|curve`, the Learning-screen tabs, the learning-curve refit loop.
