# 0212 — The shadow record is graded after the close through the one replay path, summarised as raw and effective n, labelled gross unless the sheet is confirmed, and switched by the owner with a fresh password

- **Status:** Proposed (built in CF-08 part B from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: yes (light)** (a second automatic service, `shadow-grade`; it starts paused, grades nothing until `paper.shadow.enabled` is ON, writes only `paper.shadow_grades`, places no order, calls no broker and moves no ticket).
- **Date:** 2026-11-05.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** completes [0211](0211-the-shadow-record-writes-every-nightly-candidate-before-the-open-forward-only-and-insert-only-chained-by-digest-as-a-count-separate-from-the-p10-paper-count-and-the-switch-is-the-owners-and-off-by-default.md) (part A, the write side); follows [0090](0090-grading-replays-each-ticket-through-the-simulator-on-its-own-session-and-stores-one-insert-only-grade.md) and [0116](0116-paper-fills-are-the-grading-replay-captured-per-armed-allocation.md) (one replay path), [0091](0091-auto-approval-is-an-option-behind-an-owner-setting-default-off-a-new-approval-kind-the-database-checks.md) (the fresh-password switch and the id drawn under a lock) and [0127](0127-the-owners-confirmation-of-the-charge-sheet-is-an-insert-only-log-written-only-by-the-owners-endpoint-after-a-fresh-password-bound-to-the-rates-hash-and-off-by-default.md) (the owner's confirmation of the sheet, P10-00 D5). It changes none of them.

## Context
Part A records every candidate of a nightly plan before the open. Part B turns the record into evidence: it grades the candidates after the close with the same replay as paper, summarises them honestly (raw n and effective n, the interval, the label of the costs), and gives the owner the switch.

## Decision
1. **Grading goes through `TicketReplayer` and nothing else.** `GradeShadowHandler` takes the shadow entries that count for edge and have no grade (newest session first, a bounded batch of 300), finds the plan item of each, replays it with `TicketReplayer.ReplayAsync` and the configured charge sheet, and inserts one grade per entry into `paper.shadow_grades`: `graded` with `r_sim` (the grade's R: net when the replay charged the trade, else gross; the same R as the P7-13 grade and as the P10 paper exit), the entry and exit minutes, the exit reason, the engine version and the sheet id; `not_triggered`; or `not_gradable`. No other code builds a `RunSimulationQuery` for a ticket (the architecture test of the paper capture now also scans `Application/Shadow`, and `GradeShadowHandler` is pinned to name the replayer and not the simulation use case). A pending replay is not stored and is tried again by the next run; a graded row is never rewritten (unique on `entry_id`; the adapter skips a unique violation and has no update). A configured sheet that is not stored fails the run (as paper capture does), never a silent gross fallback. Only candidates with `counts_for_edge` are graded: a set-aside candidate (held, not eligible, merged duplicate, conflict) is recorded and is not edge evidence, so a replay of it would be wasted.
2. **`shadow-grade`**, a scheduled service like `paper-capture`: daily 17:30 after the `eod` run (which stores the bars of the session; paper capture runs at 17:00), paused until the owner enables it, and idle when `paper.shadow.enabled` is OFF (the handler reads nothing and writes nothing). A candidate that failed unexpectedly makes the run `failed` (with counts only); the next run tries it again.
3. **The summary** (`GET /api/paper/shadow/summary`, `xd paper shadow summary`) is read only and computed on read from `IShadowResultReader` (the one read port, named by one use case, `GetShadowSummaryHandler`; no Planning, Selection or Autonomy type may name it, and the Domain/Application Paper types still name no shadow type). It gives: raw n; effective n (the design effect `1 + (m - 1) * rho` per day; rho 0.3 and the sentence that it is a placeholder until CF-01); the pace (sessions to 60 effective trades at the rate seen so far, only once 10 trades are graded, else the reason; counted over sessions that have at least one final grade, so a session not graded yet does not lower the rate); the mean R with a 90 % percentile-bootstrap interval (the paper programme's own seeded bootstrap, `BacktestBand.PaperInterval`, 10,000 draws) and the hit rate, for all edge trades and by group (primary, reserve, rejected); fewer than 10 graded trades give a provisional mean and no interval; the behavioural count (P10's `CountedSet` of the newest epoch) beside the edge count, never added; the candidates still pending, not triggered and not gradable; the chain head (sessions, gaps, last digest); and the line "modelled fills; no queue, no latency" and the separate-count sentence on every answer. With no row it says so, and says that the switch is OFF when it is.
4. **The figures are gross unless the sheet is confirmed, and the numbers follow the label.** A grade row stores two figures of the same trade: `r_sim` (what the replay returned: net of the stored sheet when it charged the trade, else gross; it equals the P7-13 grade and the P10 paper exit) and `r_gross`, the replay's filled R (`SimTrade.RFilled`, the figure `TicketGrader` itself uses when a trade is not charged; the replay does give it separately). `r_gross` is a nullable column added by the migration `AddShadowGradeGross` (the table was empty and the switch OFF, so nothing is back-filled; part A's migration is not edited). The summary shows the **net** figures (`r_sim`) only when every graded trade is net and every charge sheet is confirmed as the owner's (`ICostConfirmationFact`, ADR 0127); in every other case (no sheet, a sheet not confirmed, or a mix) it shows the **gross** figures (`r_gross`) and `costs.gross` is true, with the reason in the label. The flag, the label and the numbers always agree (P10-00 D5: never a net figure under an unconfirmed sheet). Tests assert the number itself. The paper ledger's wording (ADR 0116, `PaperWording.AfterStoredSheet`) is the precedent for not calling an unconfirmed result net.
5. **The switch** `PUT /api/paper/shadow/settings` (owner session, CSRF, **a fresh password for this change**, ON and OFF alike, `OwnerAct.AuthorizeAsync`: the step-up window is neither opened nor used up; the actor is the session's owner, never the body) appends one row to `paper.shadow_settings` (insert-only) and records one `ShadowRecordSwitched` account event (id and on/off, never the reason). Problems: 401 `auth.invalid_credentials`, 423 `auth.locked`, 403 `paper.shadow_owner_only`, 400 `paper.shadow_reason_invalid`, 429. **Migration `StampShadowSettingInCommitOrder`:** part A's trigger set only the time; the new function takes an exclusive advisory lock and draws the id after it (the P7-12 pattern), so two changes cannot commit in the other order and the highest id is always the last change. Part A's migration is not edited. There is no auto option: nothing here is an approval.
6. **`xd paper shadow verify`** recomputes every row hash and digest from the stored rows and prints the first broken session (exit 4) or the last digest to keep (exit 0). `xd paper shadow summary` prints the summary.
7. **The digest binds the date and the status (part A's carry-over).** `ShadowDigest.Next` is now `sha256(prev|date|status|sorted row hashes)`. The first draft hashed only the previous digest and the row hashes, so a session moved to another date, or an ok session turned into a gap, would not have been found. The format was changed before any session existed (the switch has been OFF and no row has been written), so nothing was re-hashed, and ADR 0211 is amended in place for it.
8. **Gaps are complete (part A's carry-over).** A run records a gap row for every trading session after the newest stored one that has opened, up to the session asked for or the furthest stored plan that has not opened, and **stops at the first session that cannot be written** (an unopened session with no plan): writing a later session first would leave it, still unopened and so not a gap, without a row for ever. A first run starts at the first session that has not opened (no history to be a gap in). The `WriteShadowCommand.Manual` flag and `ShadowWording.GapMeaning`, which nothing used, are removed.
9. **Provenance of the probability (part A's carry-over).** The advisory note is written and read by one pair of functions in `SelectionPlanBuilder` (`AdvisoryNote`, `TryReadAdvisoryNote`); the shadow record parses against the builder's own formatter, and a test feeds the builder's output through `AdvisoryPWin`. A note with more than three decimals or other words is not read.
10. **Logs and security.** Counts only (ids 5402 to 5405: ints and the logger); no level, feature, symbol or strategy text; a failed replay is counted and its message is not kept. No secret is involved.

## What is not built
- **The weekly export of the chain digest** into the Reports export (P8-20, review fix 4) and **to a file outside the database.** The Reports export is a capability-gated, research-table exporter with its own architecture rules (it may not touch the paper namespaces and every kind calls the very handler of a screen), so a new kind there is not a small change. Until it exists the digest is printed by `xd paper shadow verify` and `summary` and returned by the summary endpoint (`chain.lastDigest`); the owner copies it outside the database by hand. **Open follow-up** (owner question below).
- **The Shadow tab of `/paper`** (the spec's own follow-up) and a history read of the switch (the table is the history).
- **A 409 `paper.shadow_gap`.** The spec's contract lists it without saying when it is raised. Nothing refuses a switch change: re-enabling after a pause simply records a gap row for each night missed, and the summary shows the number of gaps.
- **Features, the model id and the feature version** in a row, until the plan record holds them (ADR 0211).

## Limits
- **The fills are modelled** (touch is fill, no queue, no latency); the record is about the rules' edge.
- **n_eff rests on a placeholder rho of 0.3** until CF-01; the interval ignores the same-day correlation, so it is somewhat too narrow.
- **A grade is stamped by the database clock** and refused before 09:00 of its session; a candidate whose session closed but whose bars are not stored stays pending and is retried.
- **The edge and behavioural counts can cover different periods.** The summary reads the shadow rows of every epoch (there is no epoch filter yet, a `shortcut:` in `GetShadowSummaryHandler`), while the behavioural count is P10's newest epoch. Filtering by the rows' edge epoch belongs with the `/paper` tab.
- **Plan dates more than 10 days after today are ignored** when the writer chooses the sessions to look at (`MaxDaysAhead`, a `shortcut:` in `WriteShadowHandler`): a guard against listing years of days for a mistaken plan date; real plans are for the next session.
- **The batch is 300 candidates, newest session first**; an old candidate that stays pending (a symbol that left every basket) is passed over when newer ones fill the batch.

## Consequences
A second insert-only record and one more paused service; with the switch OFF (the default) both services read one setting and stop. Nothing the plan, the tickets, the paper capture or the P10 count reads changes (the golden test now seeds a captured armed primary entry, so the counted set and the band are not empty, and compares them byte for byte with and without shadow rows).
