# 0121 — The weekly paper review and the discipline report are deterministic texts over stored records, stored once per week and epoch, and apply nothing

- **Status:** Proposed (spec [P10-03](../specs/P10-03-weekly-review-and-discipline-report.md), built 2026-10-26; the spec is still Draft). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it.
- **Date:** 2026-10-26.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0094](0094-the-weekly-digest-lists-stored-proposals-in-one-insert-only-record-per-iso-week-and-the-weekly-service-applies-nothing.md) (the digest pattern and its storing rule), [0116](0116-paper-fills-are-the-grading-replay-captured-per-armed-allocation.md) (paper entries, exits, skips), [0118](0118-the-paper-ledger-is-derived-from-entries-and-exits-and-the-plan-may-read-it-behind-an-off-by-default-setting.md) (the ledger), [0119](0119-a-measurement-epoch-freezes-the-measured-configuration-changes-are-detected-classed-and-labelled-and-nothing-is-applied-or-blocked.md) (epochs), [0090](0090-grading-replays-each-ticket-through-the-simulator-on-its-own-session-and-stores-one-insert-only-grade.md) (grades), [0093](0093-auto-journal-reads-the-stored-fills-matches-them-to-approved-tickets-by-account-instrument-side-and-day-and-stores-a-fill-grade-beside-the-plan-grade.md) (discretionary fills).

## Context
R19 asks whether the owner follows the plan and what deviating cost. In a paper phase no real order exists to skip, there is no discipline score defined (P8-00 D10), and a skipped ticket has no fill. The owner needs one weekly read, every number with its n and state.

## Decision
1. **Seven exclusive categories** for the primary tickets of a window (`DisciplineClassifier`): `followed`, `sized_down`, `approved_not_entered`, `pending`, `rejected`, `lapsed`, `invalidated_preopen`; they add up to the primary count (a property test over every ticket state and trail shape). `confirmed_despite`, `auto_approved` and `reserve_approved` are counted beside. A ticket is decided by its newest state, its stored paper trades and skips per allocation: any allocation with neither a trade nor a skip, or an open trade, is `pending`; an undecided (Proposed or Modified) ticket is `pending` with the reason `undecided`; an Invalidated ticket is `invalidated_preopen` whatever its earlier approval, because the system, not the owner, dropped it. A ticket for several accounts is one ticket and one R (the lowest account id's); only rupee lines are per account.
2. **Skipped is the plan-price grade** (P7-13). Rejected and lapsed tickets with a graded R make the owner-skipped line; not triggered and not gradable or missing grades are counted apart, never R 0. Invalidated tickets are shown on their own line and are not in the owner's deviation sum. A mean R needs 15 graded tickets (`insufficient`), is `provisional` below 30; sums are facts and are always shown with n.
3. **Size effect** `(allocated - quantity) / allocated x r_sim` per closed sized-down ticket (summed over its accounts); open or never-entered size-downs add nothing and are counted apart. **R not taken** is skipped plus size effect, each ticket once, printed with its n and an `insufficient` mean.
4. **Added trades** are the journal's discretionary fills, one `DiscretionaryAsync` call per closed session of the window; counted, R `unavailable: no plan levels`, rupees only from finished round trips, `empty` (not zero) without fills.
5. **Execution gap for paper**: plan R (mean `r_exact`), slippage (`r_gross - r_exact`), charges (`r_net - r_gross`, `unavailable` without a stored sheet, `provisional` when only some trades carry one), net; missed triggers and real slippage are `not_evaluable: paper has no real fills (P11)`.
6. **The review text is a pure function** (`PaperReviewBuilder`, `ReviewText`): the two pinned opening lines, a built-at line (with the "State as of" sentence for an ended week), sections each ending with a limits sentence. A source that cannot be read (paper record, journal, comparison) is shown as `unavailable: <exception type>` in its section and the rest is produced; reasons carry no message text. The backtest comparison is one line from `IReviewComparisonLine`, which says "not built" until P10-04 replaces the registration.
7. **Pace**: counted trades are closed, primary, measured, once per ticket across the whole epoch; days elapsed of 84; a weekly pace; an ETA to 60 only from 10 counted trades and 21 elapsed days, else `unavailable`, always called a straight-line guess.
8. **Storing** reuses the digest's rule through `PaperReviewWeek` (current week from Saturday 10:00 IST, or the most recent completed week; older weeks are dry runs; a future week is `paper.invalid`). `paper.weekly_reviews` is insert-only for `xd_app` (granted on its own table), unique per ISO week and epoch, with a check that the text opens with the pinned line. A second build returns the stored review.
9. **Read-only seam**: the handlers hold `IPaperReviewSource` (read members only, implemented over the plan, grade, journal, governance and paper reads) and `IPaperReviewStore`; no plan move, approval, setting, hypothesis or broker port is reachable (tests check the constructors and the calls). The `paper-review` service (Saturday 10:30, after `weekly`) starts paused.
10. **Surface**: `GET /api/paper/reviews`, `/reviews/{isoWeek}`, `/review` (dry run), `/discipline`; `xd paper review [--week] [--store]`, `xd paper discipline [--from] [--to]`; one lazy `Review` tab in `/paper` holding the review sections, its closed-trades table and the discipline tables (a second tab was not added to keep the first-load budget). Log ids 5214 to 5218.

## Consequences
- Switched on, a review is stored every Saturday, also for an empty week; an empty week is an answer.
- A session captured after the review was built shows as pending in the stored text; the stored text is not rewritten.
- The discipline lines of a skipped ticket are modelled, so at 10 to 20 skipped tickets they carry counts and sums only.

## Alternatives considered
- **An LLM-written text**: rejected; numbers come from tools only. P9-06 may phrase these numbers later.
- **A discipline score**: rejected; none is defined.
- **An owner "mark reviewed" write**: rejected for this slice; a missing review shows as a gap in the weekly routine.
