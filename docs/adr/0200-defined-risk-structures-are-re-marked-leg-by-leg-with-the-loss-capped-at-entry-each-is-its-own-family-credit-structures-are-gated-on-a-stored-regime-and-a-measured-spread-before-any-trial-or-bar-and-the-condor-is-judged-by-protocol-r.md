# 0200 — Defined-risk structures are re-marked leg by leg with the loss capped at entry, each is its own family, credit structures are gated on a stored regime and a measured spread before any trial or bar, and the condor is judged by protocol R

- **Status:** Proposed (built in P14-10 from the owner-Approved spec, 2026-11-03). Becomes Accepted when the owner has read it. **Safety slice: no** (research re-marking and simulation of option structures from stored bars; it writes only its own insert-only rows and records, no margin is computed, nothing selects, plans, sizes or orders, and no multi-leg order path exists: that needs P12 order groups, which this plan does not build).
- **Date:** 2026-11-04.
- **Relation to other ADRs:** [0197](0197-the-option-expression-simulator-re-marks-each-stored-cash-trade-as-one-long-option-on-a-fixed-strike-read-from-the-offsets-with-an-expiry-calendar-of-labelled-derived-dates-a-counted-model-price-fallback-and-every-unpaired-trade-counted-by-reason.md) (the machinery reused: held strike series, expiry calendar, exit mapping, fallback, unpaired reasons, the two tables), [0198](0198-protocol-o-judges-the-option-expression-against-cash-on-paired-out-of-sample-trades-after-counting-each-profile-in-the-ledger-first-with-the-hold-out-cut-before-any-number-and-no-gross-result-ever-a-keep-or-a-drop.md) (protocol O, the core protocol R shares), 0194 (charges, the measured half-spread, lot), 0183 and 0186 (the records), 0059 (the ledger and `VerdictEvaluator`), 0038 (the worse outcome inside a bar), 0193 (the profile kinds).

## Decision
1. **Where the code lives.** Pure types in `XDrishti.Domain.Options.Structures`: `StructureRemark` (the re-mark of one structure), `SpreadParams` and `CondorParams` (the profile parameters), `CondorSchedule` and `CondorEntry` (the range family's entries), `StructureRow`, `StructureLegRow`, `StructureNames`. The use cases are `RemarkStructureHandler` (debit and credit spreads, one cash run), `SimulateCondorsHandler` (the condor, no cash run) and `RemarkDispatchHandler`, which lets the one verb `xd options remark` reach `RemarkRunHandler` (option_buy) or `RemarkStructureHandler` by the kind of the registered profile; the shared parts are in `RemarkShared`. In `Domain.Options.Evaluation`: `ProtocolR`, `StructureGate`, `ProtocolWording` and the per-family primary profile in `ExpressionProfiles`. A new port `IRegimeLabels` (an existence query and the range sessions) has the adapter `NoRegimeLabels`; `IExpressionTradeStore` gains the overload that stores the legs. Architecture tests pin the exact sets that name each type and that nothing in selection, planning, paper, live trading, the execution service, the simulator or any other assembly does.
2. **A leg is a held strike, priced as in ADR 0197.** Every leg is read at the open of the first bar at or after the event minute and held on its strike. The first leg follows the P14-08 strike rule; the second is `width` steps further (a debit call spread above, a put spread below; a credit spread's long wing beyond its short leg): `width` 2 or 4 for an index, 2 for a share (the window is +-3: a share spread of width 4 is refused at validation, `width_outside_window`, before a bar is read; a credit spread on a share is refused, `structure_profile.index_only`). A leg that cannot be read at entry is `strike_left_window` (the second) or `no_series` (the first); a bar of an unusable key-session is `archive_unusable`; every leg must pass the P14-07 liquidity filter. A leg that is missing later is priced per leg by the counted Black-76 fallback (the trade carries `bs_fallback`, so does the leg row) or the structure is `strike_left_window`: a hole in the bars never moves the exit earlier or later than the cash exit.
3. **The loss is capped at entry and stored.** The structure's value per unit is the long legs less the short legs (a debit) or the short legs less the long legs (a credit), checked against `[0, width]` at the exit bar and at a stop-trigger bar: a raw value outside it means the two legs' vendor bars disagree and the trade is the counted fate `structure_inconsistent` (a further closed reason, in the check constraint, `StructureNames.Unpaired` and the evaluation's unpaired counts), never a price and never clamped into a full loss. So a paired structure cannot lose more than its stored maximum loss (a debit: the debit; a credit: the width less the credit; a condor: the wider wing less the credit) or gain more than its maximum gain. An entry value at zero or below, or at or above the width, is `structure_invalid` (no defined risk to size on). Both maxima are labels of the trade row (`max_loss_px`, `max_gain_px`, `entry_debit_px` or `entry_credit_px`). There is no naked short leg: a short leg is only built beside a long leg beyond it (a test pins it); no margin is computed.
4. **Units from the maximum loss.** For a spread that expresses a cash trade `units = risk / (max loss per unit in rupees)`, so the structure's worst case equals the cash trade's rupee risk; `R_equiv` is the rupees over that risk, on the cash run's basis (both gross or both net, else `basis_mismatch`). A condor has no cash risk: it is one lot (`lot_unknown` when the lot in force is unknown) and `R` is the net rupees over `max loss x lot`.
5. **The worse outcome and the stop.** A debit spread may carry `structure_stop_pct` (a percent of the entry debit; `none` is the primary); a credit spread has none (its width is its stop); the condor's stop is a loss of `m` times its credit. A stop inside a bar takes the worse leg combination of ADR 0038: for each short leg the bar high, for each long leg the bar low, at the same time; a bar that opens through the stop fills at the gapped open; where the stop and the cash exit fall in the same bar the worse value is taken. The exit is the cash exit mapped as in ADR 0197 and the D6 exit at the close of the last session before the expiry.
6. **Costs: one half-spread per fill, four for a spread round trip, eight for a condor.** `RemarkMoney.NetLegs` charges every fill on the owner's sheet on its own date and one half-spread each; a missing sheet line is `basis_mismatch` and a missing spread `spread_unavailable`, never zero. **A credit structure is costed only on a measured half-spread** (the model's bucket, `Source = Measured`): a fill whose spread came from an owner assumption makes the row `spread_unavailable` with the label `spread: not measured`. A debit spread runs on the owner's assumption, labelled.
7. **Gates before anything is read.** (a) **Regime.** `IRegimeLabels.AnyAsync` false (P6-02 and P3-01d are not built, so today always): `xd options remark` on a credit profile and `xd options condors` are refused, `structure.regime_unavailable`, before the cash trades, the calendar, the quality verdicts or a bar are read (a test with fakes counts zero reads); the evaluation of `expr:credit_spread` and `expr:iron_condor` stores a NOT_EVALUABLE record `regime_unavailable`. (b) **Measured spread.** A model of fewer than `MinUsableSessions` (60) sessions, or none (an owner assumption is not a model), is `structure.spread_not_measured` for the verbs (the credit re-mark refuses when no underlying of the run has a measured model, before any archive read; a run with some such underlying marks the others' trades `spread_unavailable`) and a NOT_EVALUABLE record `spread_not_measured` for the evaluation, which takes the best measured model of an index underlying. Both gates are named together when both fail. **A failed gate appends no trial to the ledger and reads no date or figure** (it is not a look at data); N in the record is the family's current count. A credit spread trades only a session whose previous session was labelled `range`; every other trade is `not_range` (counted). The first answer for both credit families is NOT_EVALUABLE `regime_unavailable`.
8. **The condor entries (the range family).** On the first trading session of each Monday-based week, at 09:30, when the previous session was `range`: the nearest weekly expiry at least two sessions away (so the post-2025-09-01 Tuesday expiry is not taken from the Monday: the entry takes the next week's, series code 2), the shorts `k` steps from the money, the wings `wing_steps` (2) beyond, the exit at the close of the session before the expiry or the stop. The series are the ones the backfill holds (NIFTY `W1`, `W2`; BANKNIFTY `W1` until its weeklies were withdrawn): a week that needs another is counted `no_series`, a week without a weekly `no_weekly`, a week after a non-range session `not_range`; the counts are printed and stored in the run's inputs. The call and put series are read in stretches of at most a month of entries; the call series is the clock. The pre-registered primary is `k` 6, `m` 1; the counted variants `k` 4 or 6 times `m` 1 or 2 make N = 4 (factor 1.5).
9. **Families, identity and the shared tables.** `expr:debit_spread`, `expr:credit_spread` and `expr:iron_condor` are in `ExpressionFamilies.All` (and so in the shared route; the P15 phase check does not list them). The trial text names the profile and the population, and the trial hash is the SHA-256 of the family and the text, so a trial of one family never counts in another (a test pins that the same profile and run give four hashes and N = 1 in each). A profile of another kind than the family's is refused (`expression.profile_kind_mismatch`). The condor has no cash run: `xd options condors` stores a run whose `cash_run_key` is the population key (a SHA-256 of the underlying and the two dates; printed) and whose inputs carry `from`, `to`, `underlying` and the week counts; that key is the `--run` of the evaluation. The hold-out is read once per family (the family and `expr-1`).
10. **Protocol O for the spreads, protocol R for the condor.** The debit and credit families use protocol O unchanged (ADR 0198; the credit family is judged only after the gates). Protocol R is protocol O's core with the condor's words and options (`ExpressionStudyOptions.Condor`): the unit is the condor's own R (the cash R of every pair is taken as 0, so `d` is the R), nothing is fitted so every range-regime entry before the hold-out is out of sample, NOT_EVALUABLE below 6 evaluable quarters or 200 pooled condors, KEEP needs the Lab's gates with N, both block lower bounds (days and 5 sessions, 10,000 draws, seed 1337) at `0.05 R x Factor(N)`, two thirds of the quarters positive and the latest epoch (30 or more) positive, net costs and a fallback share of 5 % or less; a gross result is INCONCLUSIVE whichever way it points; DROP needs the minima, net and the larger upper bound under 0.05 R. The hold-out (last six months) is cut by date before any figure and read once with `--holdout`. The records are `research.extension_evaluations` rows of protocol `O` or `R`, fold unit `quarter`.
11. **Migration `AddExpressionLegs`.** `research.expression_legs(run_id, trade_key, leg int2, side, position char(5), strike_px, entry_premium_px, exit_premium_px, labels)`, primary key `(run_id, trade_key, leg)`, a foreign key to the trade row, insert-only for `xd_app` by the per-table helpers, nothing for `xd_exec` or the read roles. **Differences from the spec text:** the migration also replaces four checks of `research.expression_trades` (constraints only, no row changes): the fate list gains `not_range`, `structure_invalid`, `lot_unknown`, `structure_inconsistent`; the side list gains `IC` (the condor); a structure may be worth nothing at the exit (`exit_premium_px >= 0`); a paired row may have no `cash_r` only when its labels say `no_cash_twin`. `Down` drops the legs and puts the original checks back as `NOT VALID` (they bind every later row; no stored row is deleted). Raw SQL on purpose.
12. **Contract.** `xd options remark --run RUNKEY --profile HASH` (debit_spread and credit_spread profiles added), `xd options condors --profile HASH --from DATE --to DATE [--underlying NSE:NIFTY|NSE:BANKNIFTY]` (exit 0 done, 1 refused, 2 bad line, 3 failure, 130 cancelled), `xd options expression-eval --family expr:option_buy|expr:debit_spread|expr:credit_spread|expr:iron_condor --run RUNKEY [--profile HASH] [--holdout]`. The condor is costed on the sheet named by `Options:Remark:CondorCostSheetId` (empty: gross, which can neither keep nor drop). The shared `GET /api/research/extensions?family=` lists the three new families.

## Honest limits (printed with every run and stored in every row)
- With no regime labels (today) and without a measured spread model the credit spread and the condor answer NOT_EVALUABLE; only the debit spread can be evaluated, and only when the cash run has a re-mark, the option archive has bars and the owner's charge lines are loaded. About 52 weekly condor entries a year in a range regime only: 200 condors need most of five years.
- The structure inherits every ADR 0197 limit (the bar timestamp, the IV unit, the open-interest unit and the derived expiry weekdays are UNVERIFIED; the fill is the next option bar's open; the fallback has no carry).
- Wider wings than the window holds cannot be priced (a share: width 2 only; an index: the wings of a condor reach 8 of the 10 steps).
- A structure whose two legs' bars disagree at its exit bar or stop-trigger bar is `structure_inconsistent` and unpaired; the share is in the printed unpaired counts. A stop exit's value is the stop level (or the gapped open value) while its stored and costed leg prices are the gapped opens or the bar's worst (long low, short high); they need not sum to that value, and the row says so (`exit: stop; ...`).
- Short legs carry the expiry-day margin and pin risk that D6 avoids by leaving a session early; gaps through both legs are capped by the structure, not by a stop. Nothing computes a margin.
- The condor's liquidity filter is applied to all four legs, including the far wings (conservative: an illiquid wing cannot be filled); it may unpair most entries. The call series is the condor's clock; a put bar at a time the call series lacks is not a stop-check time.

## Open questions for the owner
1. **Per-quarter minimum of protocol R.** The spec names 6 evaluable quarters and 200 condors but not what makes a quarter evaluable. Protocol O's 20 per quarter cannot be met by a weekly range-regime family (at most 13 entries a quarter), so `ExpressionStudyOptions.Condor` uses 10 (hashed into every record). Default: keep 10; another number is another protocol.
2. **The condor parameters** (`k` 6, `m` 1, wings 2 steps) as drafted and pre-registered; the four counted variants make N = 4.
3. **Liquidity of the wings.** Should the P14-07 liquidity filter apply to the long wings? Default: yes, all legs.
4. **Build before the regime tagger.** As the spec's default: the credit and condor code and their gates are built; they evaluate when regimes are stored. The regime port is global (one label per session, not per underlying); P6-02 may need it keyed by underlying.
5. **Which index models count for the evaluation gate.** The gate takes the best measured model of NIFTY and BANKNIFTY; a row of an underlying without one is `spread_unavailable`, counted. Default: as built.
