# 0042 — Costs and sizing: the charge sheet, the quantity, net R and `SimTrade` schema 4

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-18: "yes to all recommendations" on [P3-03c](../specs/P3-03c-costs-and-sizing-the-charge-sheet-the-quantity-and-net-r.md), the split, the named limit of D17 and the DP charge field as recommended; the decisions the spec took on research when it was drafted — D1 (where costs are applied), D3 (decimal money), D6 (rounding per line and per leg), D11 (the quantity from the exact entry, floor), D13 (net R from filled R) and the `test-` id prefix — are the owner's to revert).
- **Relation to other ADRs:** builds on [ADR 0037](0037-simulation-conventions-and-the-two-stage-seam.md) (the two-stage seam; its D7 and Amendment 1 said that every trade carries `Costs = None` and that the third label is the constant `costs: none`: **this ADR supersedes those two statements for a run that was costed**, and Amendment 6 of ADR 0037 points here; a gross run still says both), [ADR 0038](0038-fill-realism-the-entry-limit-gaps-slippage-ticks-and-the-order-inside-a-minute.md) (the unit of R, `RExact` and `RFilled`, the filled prices the charge is computed on), [ADR 0040](0040-swing-positions-held-across-sessions-the-maximum-hold-the-position-gate-and-simtrade-schema-3.md) (schema 3, `HoldsOvernight`, the position gate) and [ADR 0026](0026-dividends-entered-by-the-owner-and-charges-in-net-returns.md) (charges in net returns, account-level fees outside the charge).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19 (built the same day). Slice P3-03c is the **pure half**: the sheet, the model, the sizing, the costing of a trade, the record and the labels. It has no request field: every run through the handler is gross and says `costs: none`. The request's cost choice, the store, the migration, the verbs and the bill check are [P3-03f](../specs/P3-03f-the-cost-choice-the-stored-charge-sheet-the-verbs-and-the-bill-check.md).

## In short
- **A trade can say what it cost.** A charge sheet (effective-dated schedules of ten rates per segment) charges the two orders of a trade, line by line, on the **filled** prices and on the rates in force on each order's own India date. The trade carries `RExact`, `RFilled` and `RNet = RFilled − CostR` side by side, and the 16 lines of the charge (eight per leg), so that a figure can be recomputed by hand to the paisa.
- **Pure Domain code, applied by the replay.** `TradeCostApplier.Apply(trade, context, segment)` sizes the trade and charges it; `InstrumentReplayer` calls it after the window cut and the exclusion across a flagged day and before the fates are counted. `TradeWalker`, `Simulator` and the scanner are not changed and do not know a cost.
- **A skip is counted, never hidden.** A quantity under one share is `SkippedQuantity`, a charge in R above `max_cost_R` is `SkippedCost` (the quantity is checked first); both are fates of `RunFates`, appended at the end with default 0. A charge equal to the limit is kept.
- **Money is `decimal`, rounding is per line and per leg.** Each line is computed from the exact turnover and rounded to the paisa once by the schedule's rule (`half_away_from_zero`, `half_even` or `down`); GST is computed from the rounded brokerage, exchange and SEBI lines of the same leg. Measured: a `double` rounds the exit SEBI of W1 (1.275) to 1.27 and the entry SEBI of W7 (1.005) to 1.00, where the decimal gives 1.28 and 1.01.
- **Fail closed.** A rate that is missing, null, negative, not a number, too large, too precise or repeated makes a sheet impossible to build; a brokerage cap of 0 means no brokerage (never "no cap"); a date before the first schedule is `costs.no_rates_for_date` and the replay throws, never a zero-cost trade. Nothing is invented: the only numbers in the repository besides doc 16's are the TEST sheet's, and that lives in the test projects.
- **`SimTrade` schema 4, `sim-4`, run-record schema 4.** One new last positional field `Costing` (the 33rd; a default value), `CostBasis.Charged`, `RunFates.SkippedQuantity` and `SkippedCost`, `RunHeader.Costs` / `SimulationRun.Costs` (`CostRecord`), the run key's `costs=` line. Nothing of schema 3 is byte-compatible with schema 4.

## Decisions
1. **D1 — where costs are applied.** In the replayer, by a pure Domain function, after the walk (not in the walker: its golden file and the two-stage seam would carry costs it must not know; not a post-pass in `Simulator.Run`: it has no window, so it would cost trades before `From`, which a sheet effective from `From` cannot price). A gross replay (no cost context) calls nothing. `WalkCounters` are the walker's and still count the skipped trades: **the totals of `Walk` are not the kept trades; `Fates` are.**
2. **D2 — the seam to the request.** `InstrumentReplayInput.Costs` (last member), `RunHeader.Costs` and `SimulationRun.Costs` exist; the handler, `SimulationRequest`, `ISimulationEngine` and the adapter are unchanged, so a run through the handler is gross, `Costs` is null, the key line is `costs=none` and the label says `costs: none`.
3. **D3 — types and units.** Money is `decimal` rupees; ratios (`CostR`, `RNet`) are `double`; `Quantity` is `long`; a percent or per-crore rate has at most 6 decimal places. The costing reads the trade's own fields, prices converted with the conversion of ADR 0038 T3 (`Math.Round((decimal)x, 8)`), so it is a function of the stored record.
4. **D4 — the sheet.** `CostSheet(Id, Label, Source, Checked, Schedules)`; id `[a-z0-9][a-z0-9._-]{0,39}`, an id starting with `test-` is a TEST sheet; 1 to 12 schedules, strictly ascending, years 2000 to 2099; per segment ten explicit fields (`brokerage_pct`, `brokerage_cap`, `brokerage_min`, `stt_buy_pct`, `stt_sell_pct`, `exchange_txn_pct`, `sebi_per_crore`, `stamp_buy_pct`, `other_pct`, `dp_charge_per_sell`); the brokerage rule is closed (cap 0 means brokerage 0, the percent and the minimum must be 0 too; with a cap the minimum is at most the cap; a flat fee is percent 0 with minimum = cap). Bounds are nets against a slipped unit, not guards (percent lines 5, GST 100, cap and minimum 10,000, SEBI per crore 1,000, DP 1,000). The input is at most 16,000 characters and the canonical text 8,000. A property name repeated in one object is refused; a number that underflows to zero (`1e-29`) is `too_precise`, never a zero rate; the names echoed in error paths are cut at 80 characters and control characters are escaped.
5. **D5 — canonical forms and hashes.** `ToCanonicalJson()` (snake_case, no white space, numbers by `CanonicalText.Number(decimal)`, dates `yyyy-MM-dd`); `RatesJson()` is the same text restricted to `id` and `schedules` and `RatesSha256` its SHA-256, so that the label, the source and the note `checked` change neither the hash nor the run key.
6. **D6 — rounding.** Each line to the paisa, once, by the schedule's rule; GST from the rounded lines of the leg; a leg's total is the sum of its eight rounded lines. Whether the broker rounds per order, per trade or per bill is UNVERIFIED: only the mode is a field of the sheet.
7. **D7 — dates, per leg.** The entry leg uses the schedule in force on the trade date, the exit leg the one in force on the date of the exit minute. A swing trade may straddle a change (L1).
8. **D8 and D9 — the charge of a leg, and what a charge is not.** Brokerage `min(cap, max(min, T × pct / 100))` (0 when the cap is 0), STT on the side the sheet says, exchange and SEBI on both sides, stamp duty on buy orders only, "other" a share of turnover, GST on brokerage + exchange + SEBI of the leg, the DP charge once on a delivery sell. It is not a tax computation, an account fee, margin or funding.
9. **D10 and D11 — the sizing profile and the quantity.** `standard` (risk 0.5 %, at most 25 % of capital) and `conservative` (0.25 %, 25 %) are code defaults equal to the seeded rows of `accounts.risk_profiles` (a database test compares them). `quantity = floor(min(capital × risk% / risk, capital × cap% / entry))` with `entry` the **exact** entry and `risk = |entry − stop|` in decimal: the quantity must not depend on slippage or the grid; `floor`, never rounding, so the risk taken never exceeds the budget; below 1 share is a counted skip. Capital is fixed (no compounding). 1R of the trade is `RiskTaken = quantity × risk`.
10. **D12 — the segment.** `IntradayEquity` for a strategy that does not hold overnight, `Delivery` for one that does (`PreparedStrategy.Simulator.HoldsOvernight`, decided in the replay because the Domain cannot name a strategy style); a swing trade that exits on its entry day is still `Delivery`.
11. **D13 — costing a trade and net R.** Quantity (below 1: skip), the two turnovers on the **filled** prices, the two legs, `TotalCost`, `CostR = TotalCost / RiskTaken` in decimal, `CostR > MaxCostR` compared in decimal (equal is kept), then the trade with `Costs = Charged` and `Costing`; every other field is the walker's, byte for byte. `RNet = RFilled − CostR` (slippage is already in `RFilled`, so it is never charged twice): `RNet ≤ RFilled ≤ RExact`, with `RNet = RFilled` only when the total is 0.
12. **D14 — the record.** `SimTrade` schema 4 (`Costing` last, `Costs == Charged` iff `Costing` is not null), `TradeCosting` (segment, sheet id, quantity, risk taken, both turnovers, both legs, total, `CostR`, `RNet`), `RunFates` +`SkippedQuantity`, +`SkippedCost`, `CostRecord` (sheet id, rates hash, canonical text without `checked`, the note, capital, profile, `max_cost_R`), the key line `costs=none` or `costs=<id>|<rates sha256>|<capital>|<profile>|<risk %>|<max capital %>|<max cost R>` after `fills=`, `SimulationEngine.Version` `sim-4`, run-record schema 4 (`xd-run 4`). The identity: the trades the walker gave in the window = `ExcludedAcrossDiscontinuity + OpenAtEnd + Trades + SkippedQuantity + SkippedCost`; `Fates.Total` still equals the scanner's candidates.
13. **D15 — labels, limits and wording.** `CostDescription.Label` (`costs: none`; `costs: sheet <id>` with the TEST note and the "not checked against bills" note), `CostDescription.SizingLabel`, `SimulationHeader.LabelsOf(rules, costs)`, `CostLimits.NotModelled` (ten codes). No string the slice produces holds a whole word of a statistic or of the console scan.
14. **D16 — the TEST sheet** is `tests/Shared/TestCostSheet.cs`, compiled into the test projects only (an architecture test looks in the `src/` assemblies and files): id `test-synthetic-1`, brokerage 0.03 % capped at 20, delivery brokerage 0 and GST 18 % from doc 16, every other number invented; two schedules (from 2026-01-01 and 2026-03-03).
15. **D17 — the named limit `swing_gate_before_costing`.** The position gate of ADR 0040 runs in `Simulator.Run` before the walk; costing runs afterwards. A swing trade that costing skips still counts as the strategy's position, and the later candidates it blocks stay `PositionOpen`. Kept so that the replay stays window-independent (a trade before `From` cannot be costed) and printed in every costed banner; reversible by a later slice.

## Decided in the build
- **A strict JSON reader of its own for the sheet** (`MiniJson`, internal to `Domain.Research`): the research namespace may not use `System.Text.Json` (an architecture rule: its canonical texts are hand-written), and the framework reader keeps the last of a repeated name silently. The reader keeps every member of an object (so a repeated name is `duplicate_property`), keeps a number as its text (so a rate is a decimal and never passes through a double), reads `NaN` and `Infinity` as a token (so the field is named in the error) and limits nesting to 8. This settles the point the spec marked UNVERIFIED.
- **Four codes beyond the list of D4** for field errors: `invalid_json` (the text is not JSON), `invalid_value` (a value of the wrong kind or outside its list: an id outside the pattern, an unknown rounding, a date that is not `yyyy-MM-dd`), `out_of_range` (a date outside 2000 to 2099) and `too_long` (a text over its bound). `SizingProfile.Create` reports `costs.sizing_input_invalid` with the field named, not a fifth error code.
- **`RunLabels.Of` takes the third label from `CostDescription.Label(run.Costs)`** (a gross run is unchanged: the same eleven lines and `FillLimits.NotModelled`), and for a costed run adds the sizing line after the costs line and appends `CostLimits.NotModelled` to the limits. The export (`XDrishti.SimExport`) writes `costs` as `none` or `charged`, flattens `Costing` into 17 columns (`costing_segment`, `costing_sheet`, `costing_quantity`, `costing_risk_taken`, `costing_entry_turnover`, `costing_exit_turnover`, the eight line groups summed over the two legs, `costing_total_cost`, `cost_r`, `r_net`; empty for a gross trade) and writes the 16 lines of every trade and the cost record into `run.json`; `CostSegment` has the spellings `intraday_equity` and `delivery_equity`. The export format string `xd-sim-export-1` was not raised: no export had been written by a release.
- **The unit of R, exactly.** `RiskTaken / Quantity` equals the walker's risk per share for entry and stop (both are 8-place values in the walker). The exit of a target is `entry + rr × risk` and can have a ninth decimal, so a recomputation of `RExact` must read the exit as the double holds it, not rounded to 8 places (rounding it moves R by up to 5 × 10⁻⁹ / risk, 1.7 × 10⁻⁹ on one seeded trade); the property test reads it that way.

## Consequences
- **Tests.** 1,359 in `XDrishti.Domain.Tests`, 844 in `XDrishti.Simulation.Tests` (6 explicit), 176 in `XDrishti.SimExport.Tests`, 222 in `XDrishti.Architecture.Tests`, 4,022 in `XDrishti.Application.Tests`, and one database test for the seeded profiles. The worked examples W1 to W8 and L1 are written as data from the spec and were re-checked with an independent tool (Python 3 `decimal`) before the code was compared with them; the costed golden file (`costed.golden.txt`, F2 and F1 under the TEST sheet) is paired with W8 and W7 and the test asserts the hand figures before it compares the file. Five negative controls (stamp duty on sells, GST on STT, the cap ignored, a rounded quantity, the total rounded once) and three more each fail the worked examples; an unbroken reference written separately from the production code agrees with it on 2,000 seeded legs under all three roundings. **The negative controls are mutants of that reference (`ReferenceCosting`), not of `CostModel` or `Sizing`**, and only the worked figures are run against them: the properties half of criterion 23 is not run against the mutants.
- **Golden files.** Four files (walker, scanner, simulator, run record) and the swing file were regenerated on purpose with `engine sim-4`; the diff was read: the engine and schema labels, the run-key hash, the two new fate fields in the lines that print `RunFates`, and nothing else. The SHA-256 of the run-record file by engine version has its `sim-4` row (computed with `shasum`, then checked with Python).
- **Performance.** Costing is O(1) per trade; the explicit benchmark `Costing_throughput_is_reported` costs about 4.4 lakh trades per second in Release (Apple M4 Pro, single thread, cold, seeded intraday trades). No number is asserted.
- **Handed over.** To P3-03f: the request's cost choice, the sheet store and its migration (the canonical text limit of 8,000 characters is fixed here), the verbs, the bill check (`GtrRow` must expose the seven charge lines), the refusal of a `test-` id, the owner's sheet, rounding level, the GST base, the DP figure and the dates. To P3-08: the oracle recomputes net R from the printed lines with its own D8 and D9. To P3-09: net R of 20 trades by hand from the printed lines. To the banner and the cards (P3-04b2, P3-04c): the sizing line (`CostDescription.SizingLabel`) is a label of a costed run; it reads `... whose charge is above 0.1 of its risk taken ...` so that the full `ForbiddenWords` scan, digit followed by `R` included, passes (the first wording, `above 0.1 R of cost`, did not; changed after the review).
- **Limits that stay** (`CostLimits.NotModelled`): fixed capital and unconstrained overlap, no margin or leverage (the quantity uses the exact entry, so the filled entry may exceed the cap by a few ticks), no liquidity cap, one order per leg, cash equity only, no trading tax or account fee, the figures are exactly as right as the sheet (unchecked until P3-03f), one flat DP figure, no flat per-order fee, and the gate that runs before costing.

## Alternatives considered
- **Costs in the walker.** Its golden file and hand-calculated cases would change and the seam of ADR 0037 (P4 replays the same entries under other exits) would carry costs it must not know.
- **A post-pass in `Simulator.Run`.** No window there, so trades before `From` would be costed and the counters would no longer add up.
- **Rounding once per trade or per bill.** Unknown which level the broker uses; per line and per leg is the finest level and the one place the owner's bills can overturn without changing a column.
- **Money in `double`.** Rounds an exact half the wrong way (the measured cases above); a sum of eight rounded lines misrounds in binary.
- **The quantity from the filled entry.** It would make the size depend on slippage and the grid, and a limit order is sized before its fill price is known.
- **Net R from `RExact`.** Would charge slippage twice or not at all; `RFilled` is the result at the prices an order would have got.
- **The gate after costing (D17).** Changes the `Simulator.Run` of ADR 0040 and the replay's window rule, since trades before `From` cannot be costed; left to a later slice if the owner wants it.
- **`System.Text.Json` for the sheet.** Forbidden in this namespace by an architecture rule and silent about repeated names; a 300-line strict reader is cheaper than relaxing the rule.
