# Execution runbook: daily login, token and static IP

Operating notes for the owner (spec [P12-11](../specs/P12-11-daily-login-token-and-static-ip-operations.md), [ADR 0164](../adr/0164-readiness-is-judged-from-six-checks-recorded-insert-only-and-marked-at-09-10-30-and-the-daily-login-stays-the-owners.md)). The app never logs in for you, never renews a token and never holds a Dhan PIN or TOTP secret. Execution can act only when the readiness card on Execution, Today says **Ready**; anything else, including *unavailable*, means it will not act. Times are India time.

1. **Every trading morning (target 08:30, window 16:45 yesterday to 09:00).** Open xDrishti, Accounts, Connect (or Reconnect) the trading account, sign in to Dhan with its 2FA, return to the app. Open Execution, Today: the card must say Ready. Unlock today (password) if the account is in `semi_auto` or `auto_live`. Approve the plan before 09:05.
2. **If the card says token short.** The login was made between 09:15 and 16:45 yesterday and ends before today's close. Connect again now; the old token is replaced.
3. **If it is 09:10 and the card says not ready.** Nothing will be sent today for triggers that already fired. Connect now; trading resumes only for triggers that fire after Ready and inside their windows. Open positions keep their stops at the broker; open Dhan to see them; the system cannot see them until Ready.
4. **If an order was rejected with an invalid-IP code (DH-905).** The account is stopped for new entries. Check the router's WAN address and your ISP line; compare with the address saved at Dhan (Profile, Get Trading & Data APIs). If you added or changed the IP after your last login, Connect again once (Dhan: a token made before the IP was added may not reflect it). In Dhan's web app use the sandbox tool (IP Setup, Get Static IP) and confirm Orders Allowed is true; record the attestation in the app. If the address really changed, you cannot change it at Dhan for 7 days after the last change (the card shows the date); use the secondary slot if one exists; otherwise the account stays stopped.
5. **Setting up or changing the IP (planned).** Ask the ISP for a fixed public IPv4 that is not shared (P11-05). In Dhan web add it as primary (and a second fixed address as secondary if you have one); note the 7-day lock. Connect again after adding it. Use the sandbox tool to confirm; save the address in `exec.expected_static_ip` and record the attestation.
6. **Every quarter, or on P11-05's `nextReviewOn`.** Read the latest SEBI/NSE circulars on retail algo trading and Dhan's API terms and release notes; record the re-check on the Compliance tab of `/live` (P11-05); stop and decide before going on if anything says a personal algo needs registration or that the rules for your account changed.

## What the card checks

`connected`, `token_covers_session` (the token must last past `exec.ready_token_until`, default 16:45, and at least `exec.token_min_remaining_min` from now), `token_live` (the broker answered the profile in the last 5 minutes and agrees), `ip_configured` (the broker lists `exec.expected_static_ip`, read in the last 15 minutes), `ip_attested` (your attestation of that address, at most `exec.attestation_max_age_days` old, orders allowed) and `no_blocking_halt`. Any check that cannot be read shows *unavailable* and blocks like a failure. A rejected IP or token (`ip_rejected`, `token_rejected`) holds until a fresh attestation or a good profile read after it, never by time.

## Where the broker reads are real (ADR 0170)

The `token_live` and `ip_configured` checks read Dhan's profile and `getIP` only in an `xd-exec` started with `Execution:Environment` = `sandbox` **and** `Execution:SendEnabled` = `true`, using the sandbox secret files `Execution__Sandbox__ClientId` and `Execution__Sandbox__Token`; without those files the reads are refused locally and the card says *unavailable*. In every other environment, `live` included, the reads are closed and the card is always *unavailable*: the live token hand-over exists (below) but `xd-exec` has no route to Dhan yet, so the live reads are refused and no live account can be Ready. In a sandbox process the two reads are those of the one sandbox login and are applied to every account the loop checks, so do not run a sandbox `xd-exec` against accounts whose readiness record you mean as live evidence. Each read leaves log event 6616 (call, path, status, reason code; never a token, client id or address).

## The live token hand-over (ADR 0171)

Nothing new to do at login: when you connect an account, the API also seals the day's access token with a second key into `accounts.exec_token_handover` (one row per account, in the same save as the connection; if that write fails the connect fails). Disconnect, a new client id and archiving delete the row; a new login replaces it. The key is the Keychain item `xdrishti/exec/handover-key`, created by `deploy/xd-up.sh` on its first run like the vault key and given to `xd-api` and `xd-exec` only (file `exec_handover_key`, `ExecHandover__Key`); the API refuses to start without it. If the key is changed, every hand-over is unreadable until the next login. `xd-exec` reads the row (never the vault) only in `live` with `Execution:ReadinessLiveEnabled` = `true` (`XD_EXEC_READINESS_LIVE`, default off), and only while the connection is Connected and unexpired. Even then the live reads stay refused by the guard table, because `xd-exec` has no route to Dhan until the separate egress decision (ADR 0171 step 5); the card stays *unavailable* in live.

## What is recorded

`execution.readiness_checks` when the verdict or a reason changes and at least every 15 minutes from 08:00 to 16:30 on trading days; `execution.day_states` once per account and session at 09:10:30 (if the service was not running then, the first run afterwards stores it late and *unavailable*); `execution.ip_attestations` when you attest. All three are insert-only.

## The confirm ticket (`semi_auto`, P12-08b)

Spec [P12-08](../specs/P12-08-semi-auto-one-tap-confirm.md), [ADR 0166](../adr/0166-a-confirm-ticket-is-exact-text-the-server-wrote-decided-once-by-a-primary-key-and-judged-by-the-database-clock-and-a-confirmation-older-than-30-seconds-sends-nothing.md). No account can be `semi_auto` yet, so no ticket exists; this is what will happen once one can.

- **Unlock the day first** (`POST /api/execution/day-unlocks`, your password, once per account and trading day, valid until 15:30). Without it every tap answers *not unlocked*.
- **Read the card, then tap once.** The text is the order; it expires 90 seconds after it was issued (`exec.confirm_ttl_s`) by the server's clock. Large tickets (at least 10 % of the account's fixed capital), tickets with a Soft check not passed (give a reason) or an account without a fixed capital need your password with the tap.
- **A refusal sends nothing and says why:** expired, mode changed, execution switched off, halted, not ready, not unlocked, price moved more than 0.30 % from the card (or no fresh price), Soft checks changed. A price or Soft-set refusal closes the card; one new card may follow while the entry window is open.
- **A confirmed tap that is not sent within 30 seconds is cancelled** (`expired_after_confirm`), for example after a restart: nothing stale is sent. Skipping is a decision, not an error.

## The limits sheet and `auto_live` (P12-09)

Spec [P12-09](../specs/P12-09-auto-live-within-hard-limits.md), [ADR 0167](../adr/0167-auto-live-sends-only-inside-an-owner-chosen-limits-sheet-that-the-database-proves-for-every-entry-an-empty-sheet-allows-nothing-and-no-action-happens-without-its-row.md). No account can be `auto_live` yet and `auto_live_enabled` is OFF; this is what will hold once one can.

- **An empty sheet allows nothing.** Each account needs a complete sheet (`PUT /api/execution/limits/{accountId}`): orders a day (at most 20), notional per order and per day, the day's loss cap, open positions (at most 10), the allow-list of trading symbols exactly as the instrument master spells them (at most 50), the window (09:16 to 15:00 at the widest) and `EQ_MIS`. The same caps bind `semi_auto` taps.
- **Raising waits a full session.** Any loosening (a cap up, a wider window, a symbol added, exit-all switched off) needs your password and takes effect at 09:00 India time of the next trading day after one full closed session; lowering is in force at once and needs only the session.
- **What refuses an automatic entry,** each with a code on the Today feed: the caps, the window, the instrument, the product, the worst-case day loss, an approval that is not yours, a provisional plan, a quantity above the plan, an open position or order in the symbol, a strategy's first live use (it becomes a ticket instead), pre-trade checks, no day unlock, no owner activity in the last 10 minutes, not ready, a feed price older than 15 s, a reconciliation break, a halt.
- **Every automatic action is one row** of `execution.action_log`, written in the same transaction as the entry; nothing is handed to the sender without its row.
