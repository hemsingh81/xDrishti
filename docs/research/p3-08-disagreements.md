## P4-04 (2026-10-22): the oracle extended for `PYRAMID`

[P4-04](../specs/P4-04-the-pyramid.md) extends the reference walker (`WalkRun`) with `OraclePyramid` and a `PyramidStep`, written from the rules D1 to D10 of the spec and [ADR 0052](../adr/0052-the-pyramid.md), not from the engine code: the bars that closed at or before the start of a minute are read in order and ask for the next level when a close is at or beyond `entry + level × R0`; the add that waits is made, missed or skipped at the open of the minute that sees it, except in a minute that ends the position anyway; the legs in are a list and the average entry is recomputed from it; the stop is the tighter of the stop in force and the level that holds the open risk to one risk; the excursions are taken minute by minute against the average entry at that minute (the engine folds segments instead, so the two formulations of D9 are compared); the trail is the oracle's own chandelier with no activation.

- **Compared:** at least 18,500 series (O's generator at 500 series in each of its 16 categories, then the engine's intraday, daily and swing generators at 500 each, every one in both fill modes), with the ladder (four shapes, two to four levels), `max_adds`, the ATR multiple, the period, the full size (unknown, 3, 5, 40 or 100 shares) and the time stop (every third seed) chosen by the seed, on every field of every trade, the legs, the counters (four new), the no-trade reason, the hold end and the gate.
- **First run:** no disagreement. Reported as it is and not as a result: the oracle's author and the engine's author are the same, and the readings recorded in ADR 0052 (decision 3 (a) and (b), decision 6) are shared by both.
- **Finding, not a disagreement:** `AddsSkippedRisk` is 0 in every series of both implementations, because the skip cannot happen (ADR 0052, decision 4: the open risk at the stop in force is at most one risk and an add is made only above it). The spec asked for an oracle case and a hand row for it; neither can be built, and a property holds the zero instead.
- **Dry read of the new fields:** `AddsTaken`, `AddsMissed`, `AddsSkippedRisk` and `AddsSkippedQuantity` have a row each in [the field definitions](p3-08-field-definitions.md), status `SPEC ONLY` (the text is the spec P4-04 D3, D4, D12 and ADR 0052); no field has the status `NONE`. The oracle's list of the named limits of the costing has a new last member, `pyramid_capital_cap`.
- **Leak property** ([P4-00](../specs/P4-00-exits-and-exit-learning-plan.md) D9) over 11,100 seeded series (`PyramidPropertyTests`): truncating the minutes after a closed bar changes nothing the position did at or before the first minute after it, the legs in included. The negative control, a rule that moves the stop to the close of the next bar, fails it on more than ten series.

## P4-05 (2026-10-22): the oracle extended for the comparison arithmetic

[P4-05](../specs/P4-05-same-entries-comparison-and-mae-mfe-diagnostics.md) adds `OracleComparison` (with `OracleComparisonTrade`, `OracleComparisonCandidate`, `OracleSample` and `OracleComparisonRow`) to the oracle. It is written from D5, D6 and D7 of the spec and [ADR 0053](../adr/0053-the-same-entries-comparison-and-the-mae-mfe-diagnostics.md), not from the engine's `ComparisonReport`, and is given seven numbers of each closed trade: the common set, the table (win rate, mean, median, downside deviation, total, profit factor, average hold, the count by exit reason), the mean difference from the first candidate and the figures of the MAE/MFE table. The walk rules are not in question here; they keep the oracles of P4-01 to P4-04.

- **Compared:** 300 seeded instruments (`SeededSeries`), the seven policies as seven candidates of ORB v1, gross and costed (TEST sheet): every figure above for each candidate, on its own trades and on the common set, to 1e-9 (`ComparisonStatsOracleTests`). The honest comparison reports no difference; a second test shows that the same oracle with one trade removed does differ.
- **First run:** no disagreement. As before the oracle and the engine have one author, so the readings of the spec that both share are recorded in the ADR: a winner is `RExact > 0`; the median of an even count is the mean of the two middle values; the downside deviation is taken over all trades of the sample; the adverse excursion is the trade's `MaeR` (the whole trade, not "before the best point").
- **Dry read of the new fields:** none: no field of `SimTrade`, no counter and no record changed in this slice.
