**Step 2 (2026-10-19): the oracle is imported and compared. The comparison found no disagreement between the oracle and the engine; the independent review added one OPEN class B entry (Q3-MFE), a defect that both share.** O's walker and gate, O's 100 hand rows, O's 16 categories (`OracleSeries`) and the engine's four generator families (`SeededDays`, `SeededDaily`, `SwingSeries`, the seeded replay series) were compared on every compared field (33 `SimTrade` fields with the echoes, 29 counters, the no-trade reason, the hold end and the blocked set), in two fill modes, by `scripts/check.sh`'s suite (`WalkComparisonTests`). There is no class C or D entry and the one class B entry (Q3-MFE) is a rule defect that both implementations have, not a difference between them: the engine, the oracle and the adapter agree everywhere they were compared, and the five ratio fields were bit-equal in every one of the pairs (no tolerance was used; see ADR 0047, *Measurements*). ### Follow-ups (recorded, not built)
The generators' parameters are narrow: the oracle's series use reward-to-risk 2.0, an entry limit of 0.1 %, a square-off of 15:15 and slippage at the default or none; the engine's seeded days vary the reward-to-risk and square-off but not the limit or the slippage. Widening them (other limits, other slippage figures) is a follow-up of P3-08b or P3-09.

### Replay level (acceptance criterion 7), restated

The oracle's author was not given the replay (P3-04: the window cut of ADR 0043 D6, the flagged-night exclusion, the fates of the run record), so the comparison is at the **walk and gate level through the real `Simulator`**: for 100 seeded replay series and three strategies (an intraday ORB, a swing on the 1h bar, a swing on the daily bar, which places overnight orders), the engine's scanned candidates and outcomes (gate included, `EndOfData` trades and pending orders included) are read from the replay's segments, and the oracle walks and gates the same candidates over the same minutes; trade for trade and fate for fate they are equal. The window cut, the exclusion and `RunFates` remain pinned by the existing replay tests (`WindowReplayTests`, `SwingWindowPropertyTests`, `ReplayLookAheadPropertyTests`, `RunAssemblyTests`); an oracle for them is not part of P3-08.
