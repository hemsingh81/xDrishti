<summary>what the fills do not model</summary>
<ul>
<li>not modelled: auction_open: The first 1-minute open of the data can be the auction price. A daily strategy enters there, and a position held overnight that gaps through a stop or a target is filled in that minute (a stop at the open, a target at its level); a repaired opening bar has a wider range, so its slippage is larger, never smaller.</li>
<li>not modelled: circuit_locks: Locked circuits are not modelled: there is no price-band data, so a fill at a locked price is not refused.</li>
<li>not modelled: touch_is_fill: A resting limit fills when the price touches it; real queues fill less often, so a fill on a target is optimistic, and the base slippage charged on it is only an approximation of what a resting order costs.</li>
<li>not modelled: no_queue_or_impact: There is no queue position, partial fill, market impact of the order size or liquidity cap.</li>
<li>not modelled: grid_approximate: The tick is today&#39;s tick from the instrument master; old prices can lie on a finer grid or off the grid, so rounding to the tick is approximate.</li>
<li>not modelled: minute_path: Inside one minute only the open, high, low and close are known; a minute that reaches both stop and target is taken as the stop.</li>
<li>not modelled: hole_is_no_trade: An absent minute and a minute with no volume are read as a minute without a trade; the order rests through it, up to the 5-minute bound.</li>
<li>not modelled: one_minute_entry: The entry order lives for one minute; a miss is never retried.</li>
<li>not modelled: no_latency: The order is live at the instant the trigger bar closes and fills at the first print of the next minute; decision and network latency are zero.</li>
<li>not modelled: dividends_not_modelled: Cash dividends are neither adjusted in the prices nor received: a position held over an ex-date shows the price drop without the dividend.</li>
</ul>
